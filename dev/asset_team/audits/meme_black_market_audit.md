## Q1, how well does it represent the location? 4/5
The doc's "exterior" is a threshold: the hidden VHS shelf inside the Internet History Museum, opened by a GeoCities kiosk, no external signage. The threshold plate has the shelf, a kiosk and a CRT, and a second state with the shelf swung open on green light, so the mechanism reads. The interior is the strongest of the three buildings: Windows-98-style monitor altar ringed by candles (AIM-away-message shrine, l.42), a bleached-tips wig on a stand with a Guy Fieri visor (l.48), ASCII cats in the ductwork (l.45), Funcoland bags, lava lamps, Baja Blast green vape cloud (l.49), brown carpet, stacked consoles, a desktop showing a blue screen (l.39). It looks like someone's sacred rot den. Missing: the "gutted Blockbuster with dial-up modem shrine" is not visible anywhere in the threshold, no MSN Messenger terminal, no corrupted gif wall art actually flickering (posters are tidy synthwave prints), no "DO NOT LOOK AT THE BABY".

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? 3/5
Palette: doc says Primary CRT Blue #4169E1, Accent #00FF00, Background Carpet Burn Brown #8B4513. Interior brown and warm amber dominate, green appears as mist and strips (fine as accent), but blue is only a few small screens, so the "primary" is not primary. The threshold is purple/pink synthwave and warm lantern amber, almost no #4169E1 and no green until the door opens. That reads as a boutique museum corner (velvet ropes, brass posts, vines, plants, a cosy rug), not a cursed meme shrine; it is the same museum language as the approved museum ref. Satire: the interior is dense and specific; the threshold is respectable.

## Verdict: REDO exterior (threshold); interior KEEP with a palette re-grade
Q2 is under 4, so the building does not pass as a set. The threshold is the failure. The interior is close to over the top; a blue/brown regrade (CRT blue spill, less amber) would satisfy the doc.
