**Files read:** design/worlds/debaucheryville/three_crown_keghaus.md (whole); PNG_Assets/Three_Crown_Keg_Haus_Complete_PNG_Asset_Requirements.md; design/items/brewery_tour/{beer,opener,stamp}_three_crown_keg_haus_item.md; worlds/debaucheryville/environment/three_crown_keg_haus_environment.json (grep); Quests/Location Specific/Debaucheryville/brewerytourdebaucheryville.md (grep); debaucheryville_locations_index.md (grep); dev/asset_team/TEAM_BRIEF.md s2; dev/asset_team/reviews/three_crown_keg_haus_{ext,int}_review.jpg.

## Q1, how well does it represent the location? 2/5
- Doc: "medieval dive bar reimagined by someone who failed architecture", "300-year-old castle turned frat basement", health-code violation. Painted exterior is a charming storybook stone cottage on a clean cobbled lane; the interior is a handsome, candle-warm medieval tavern. That is "respectable", which the author rejected.
- Hits: backlit crown-over-stein sign (small), boarded shutters, patched roof, keg chandelier, throne behind the bar, crown heraldry. These are decoration, not decay.

## Q2, interior and exterior requirements? 2/5
- Palette (#CC5500 rust primary, #6A0DAD faded purple accent, #5C4033 dive brown): amber and brown are present, but the surfaces are polished and the purple is small. Rust and grime are not dominant.
- Missing required elements: sticky floor outside, mustard "art" on walls, gaffer tape on booths, mismatched furniture at wrong heights (furniture here is a coordinated set), tracksuit-wearing nobles slumped in booths, passed-out patrons (zero bodies; the stealth puzzle needs them), foam-shaped sconces (plain sconces), vomit, mold, creaky/worn tiles for the puzzle.
- No satire is readable. Nothing says failed architect, tracksuit nobility or health-code violation.

## Verdict: REDO, both
Not over the top; reads as a tidy fantasy inn.

## Redo plan
- **Hook:** "Noble by Name, Not by Health Code": castle turned frat basement.
- **Palette:** #CC5500 rust on everything metal and signage, #5C4033 filthy ground, #6A0DAD as faded velvet only.
- **Exterior:** lopsided, wrongly-extended building (failed-architect additions), rusted crowns sign over a stein, sticky stained alley, cabbage-steam vent, bulging bin of kegs.
- **Interior:** one cramped room; foam-shaped sconces; chandelier made of mini-kegs; gaffer-taped moldy booths; mismatched furniture heights; mustard-drawn art; heraldry rotting; worn vs intact floorboards; velvet keg throne.
- **Satire beats:** tracksuit nobles slumped in booths (Interior Visuals); mustard "art" (Decorative Elements); "Obvious signs they ignore: moldy booths, passed-out patrons"; heritage claimed on a health-code violator (Primary Vice).
- **Not in the file, needs approval:** are the passed-out nobles painted into the plate or placed as separate sprites? How many?
