# Audit: euro_ink_4_less (debaucheryville)

**Q1, represents the location: 3/5.** The file's hook is confident incompetence ("Mostly No Regrets", duck for skeleton). Painted: neon sign with dead "4", WALK-INS WELCOME, HYGENIC/LICENSED/CHEAP FAST GOOD stickers, "NO REGRETS Mostly", duck on the door. Those are small text checks. The building is a mossy, ivy-covered brick box, a tasteful ruin, not a seedy tourist-trap den. Missing: Viktor the Barker, the kebab shop and currency exchange neighbours, the cobble-to-concrete alley.

**Q2, leans into requirements: 3/5.** Palette: pink (#FF1493) is only the sign and a puddle; Dingy Gray (#696969) and brown dominate; Clinical White (#F5F5F5) and harsh fluorescent are absent; green (#7FFF00) is one small bottle sign. Interior is stronger (flash wall, pink skull and green bottle neon, sheet station, beaded curtain with mattress, clock, PAIN IS TEMPORARY poster, duct-taped chairs, beanbag) but dim and moody; the geese-dragons, certificate and uncertain Wall of Fame gags don't read. Satire lives in text, not the building. Respectable grunge, not over the top.

**Verdict: REDO** (exterior mainly; interior repaint for palette and gags).
