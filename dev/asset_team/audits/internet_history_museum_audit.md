# Audit: Internet History Museum (debaucheryville)

**Q1, represents the location: 4/5.** Nearly every doc element is painted. Exterior: floppy-disk boards, "AOL USERS ONLY" tape, Comic Sans banner, 404 Error sign with pigeons, PalmPilot, "ZOMG LOL WTF M8?" (all Exterior Description). Interior: modem pyramid shrine, GeoCities diorama, Angelfire flames, Habbo smiley with knife and chalk outline, MSN graveyard with green lights and webcams, iMac G3 altar with CDs, Chuck E. animatronic with Furbies, QR banners. The interior is a faithful, dense "cursed yearbook". Misses: no printer-paper Comic Sans exhibit banners, no CRT-glow-as-main-light.

**Q2, design requirements: 3/5.**
- Palette: Primary CRT Blue #4169E1 appears only on the exterior roof. Interior is dominated by brown, tan and grey stone; Dusty Grey #A9A9A9 is the floor but pink #FF6EC7 is confined to small screens and signage. Neither plate is "blue-and-pink vaporwave".
- Exterior: reads as a derelict corner shop, ivy and cobbles, one storey, tasteful decay. It is "Prague with a sign on it", not a Blockbuster shell gone feral. The doc's joke (museum treating garbage as sacred) is not in the building's shape, scale or signage.
- Interior: over the top, funny, strong. Not the problem.

**Verdict: REDO (exterior only; keep interior, re-tint palette only if the author wants).** The interior is clearly over the top. The exterior is respectable and brown.

## Redo plan (exterior)
**Hook:** Former Blockbuster preserved as a sacred shrine to garbage ("Preserving Our Digital Past (In Comic Sans)").
**Palette:** #4169E1 dominant on roof, awning and facade wash (Blockbuster-blue, not weathered grey); #FF6EC7 on glitching banner, neon edging, graffiti; #A9A9A9 for walls and pavement.
**Exterior:** Taller Blockbuster silhouette with oversized ticket-stub canopy, blue facade end to end, doorway entirely tiled in floppies and jewel cases, "AOL USERS ONLY" tape in several layers, dead PalmPilot dangling as a camera, sagging 404 sign dominating the roofline with pigeon colony, banner in lurid pink. Less ivy and cobble, more cable spaghetti and CRT glow in windows.
**Interior:** Keep as painted; confirm all six exhibits plus animatronic stay.
**Satire beats:** (1) floppy/jewel-case doorway, "boarded up with floppy disks"; (2) "AOL USERS ONLY" tape; (3) Comic Sans banner "PRESERVING OUR DIGITAL PAST"; (4) pigeons on the 404 sign.
