## Q1, how well does it represent the location? 4 / 5
- Signage matches the doc line by line (l.34-38): "THE PARTICIPATION TROPHY" in faded carnival letters, "EVERYONE'S A WINNER!" banner, "REDEEM YOUR TICKETS HERE" with arrow, "All Prizes Are Final. No Refunds. No Complaints. No Hope.", handwritten "Yes, these are the GOOD prizes".
- Prize wall tiers are legible: top shelf grand prizes (golden hamster, championship belt, trophies), middle novelty sword and cane, bottom sad stuffed animals, balloon dog, glitter hat (l.45-48). Cages and boxes of junk on the floor stand in for "Participation Prizes".
- Peeling stripes, a propped leaning beam for "questionable structural integrity" (l.30), rigged-game stalls in the background (l.41).
- Weak: the doc says "deliberately underwhelming compared to the flashy games" (l.31). This booth is lush and charming, so the sad energy is a little diluted. No "Smile! You 'Won'!" sign. Interior (counter, warehouse of sadness, l.51-60) not painted, but not required by the spec.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? 4 / 5
- Palette: Faded Carnival Red #CD5C5C, Depressing Yellow #F0E68C, Worn Canvas #D2B48C, Regret Purple #9370DB. Painted: red/cream stripes dominate, canvas big top as ground, purple only on a sword, plush and belt. Good fit; the purple accent could be stronger.
- Satire is visible: garbage-as-prestige, hoarded junk, grand prizes dusty on display.
- Over the top: moderately. It is a dense, funny booth with a sagging, patched look, not a respectable building. A little too pretty for the doc's "childhood disappointment, concentrated" harsh lighting.

## Verdict: KEEP
Both scores are 4 and the booth is a visible joke. Optional polish later: harsher lighting, more Regret Purple.
