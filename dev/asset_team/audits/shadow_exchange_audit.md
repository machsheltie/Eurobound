## Q1, how well does it represent the location? 4/5
Doc exterior: damp narrow alley, crooked sign reading "SHÆDOW EXCHÆNGE — Best Rates If You Don't Ask", hidden cobblestone hinge revealing a stair lit by cigarette-burned tealights. The plate delivers nearly all of it: wet alley, the hinged paving hatch, tealights, cigarette butts, the exact sign, purple graffiti, steam. The hatch-in-the-ground is a good gag. Interior doc: vaulted wine-cellar arches as stalls with iron lattice, split-flap rate board, glass currency counters, CRT market wall, desk lamp, UV glow, counterfeit handbags on lattice. All present, including barrels in the arches and a UV-lit counter. Missing from both: the "cash-only candle marker" the alley is meant to be found by (PNG spec item 1), the Shadow Ledger duct-taped on the desk, stamped paperwork with mismatched logos, handbags with deactivated tags are small and hard to read.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? 3/5
Palette: #708090 stone grey dominates and #1C1C1C shadows are right, but #9400D3 UV purple is a faint graffiti smear on the exterior and two small wand glows inside (the night plate does better). The doc's #FFD700 gold and #DC143C red ticks are mostly warm amber lamp light, not currency gold. The satire is not on the walls: "clerks with too many calculators", "receipts from three different businesses", "WEDNESDAY" wristbands, "rates that change based on how loudly you ask" are invisible. Both plates read as a tasteful moody dungeon shop: tidy floors, no clutter, no clerks' mess, no sense of "officially unofficial". The exterior alley is a handsome empty brick corridor; nothing there is scandalous. Respectable.

## Verdict: REDO, both (a light, additive redo)
Q1 is 4, but Q2 is under 4 and neither plate is clearly over the top. The bones are right; keep the hatch, arches and board and add the comedy layer.

## Redo plan
- **Hook:** "Best Rates If You Don't Ask": an old bank vault run as a post-Soviet flea market where everything feels officially unofficial.
- **Palette:** #708090 arches and coats; #9400D3 UV washing the counters, a UV-lit stair and wand beams; #1C1C1C shadow; #FFD700 on the rate board and trays; #00FF00 CRT market wall; #DC143C red ticks and crash charts.
- **Exterior:** crooked sign hung by one bolt, stacked mismatched tags and stickers, a lit cash-only candle marker on a stoop, tealight line down the hatch, more butts and puddles, purple UV graffiti arrow. Add one smug lookout silhouette-free prop, such as a folding chair with a calculator.
- **Interior:** every counter with several calculators, receipts and stamps spilling off the desk, handbags hung thick on the lattice with security tags, split-flap board with visible red and green arrows, Shadow Ledger duct-taped to the desk lamp, vault door ajar behind the main counter for Premium Stock. No people.
- **Satire beats made visible:** (1) "clerks with too many calculators" (l.59, 82); (2) receipts from three different businesses, mismatched logos (l.45); (3) counterfeit handbags and deactivated tags (l.44); (4) rates move with loudness (l.82, 121).
- **Not in the file, needs approval:** Does Mrs. Rata's counter get a hand-painted rule sign, and in what wording? Should the "WEDNESDAY" wristbands appear as a visible stack? May the Premium Stock gate be shown already built behind the counter, locked?
