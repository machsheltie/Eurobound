## Q1, how well does it represent the location? 4 / 5
- Interior is on target: black velvet seat (l.39), chipped wall champagne holder with bottle (l.40), selfie-cam mirror lit pink (l.41), champagne-flute chandelier (l.37), graffiti "ROI = Regret On Investment / I BLEW IT... literally / Buy Low, Cry High" (l.44), QR code on the floor, RGB strip lights, scribbled handles and doodles.
- Exterior: STAFF ONLY curtain with cracked-looking gold embroidery, glitter and wristband trail (l.29-30), rope. Missing: the faux-brass plaque "generously sponsored by Tech Bros Who Can't Hold Their Liquor" (l.31), the main exterior joke. Bouncers are people, so rightly absent, but no stand-in prop.
- Interior misses the plaque too, and the "Mod Podge glitter marble" reads as real marble.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? 3 / 5
- Palette: Gold #FFD700, Black Velvet #1C1C1C, Champagne #F7E7CE. Interior matches well: gold and black dominate. Exterior is crimson and brass, with red dominant over gold and black.
- Satire: the doc's joke is cheap fakery pretending to be luxury ("toilet-papered luxury", glitter Mod Podge, chipped holder). The exterior is genuinely opulent: damask, tassels, lanterns, brass stanchions. It is a real VIP lounge, handsome and respectable. The interior is gaudy enough to work (gold toilet, neon edges) but nothing looks fake or cheap.
- Floor is the glossy checkerboard used in many rooms; doc says cracked tile.

## Verdict: REDO (exterior). Interior KEEP.
Interior scores 4/4 and is over the top. The exterior is too tasteful.

## Redo plan (exterior)
**Hook:** A stall dressed as the Vatican of VIP, sponsored by tech bros who cannot hold their liquor.
**Palette:** #FFD700 trim, tassels and plaque rim; #1C1C1C velvet bulk of the curtain; #F7E7CE bottle-service foam and glitter; RGB shimmer glow from the edges.
**Exterior:** staff curtain visibly cheap (shiny velvet, cracked embroidery, dangling hem), huge faux-brass plaque with the doc's full text, glitter and lip-gloss smear trail, expired wristbands on the floor, RGB LED strip flickering around the frame, bouncers represented only by two velvet-rope podiums.
**Interior:** keep.
**Satire beats made visible:** sponsorship plaque (l.31, l.82); glitter trail and wristbands (l.29); "STAFF ONLY" cracked embroidery (l.30); RGB shimmer (l.33).

- Should a floor-standing "Mod Podge" glitter-marble tell be painted into the exterior walls?
