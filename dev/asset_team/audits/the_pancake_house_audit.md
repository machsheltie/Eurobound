## Q1, how well does it represent the location? Score 2/5
Doc: "Charming canal house with outdoor terrace, windmill decorations, tourist magnetism maximum", blue door with Delft tile frame, golden pancake sign, folksy font, windmill logo, chalkboard at 17 SOVS, heaters. All present, literally. But the doc's joke is a tourist trap selling a hubcap-sized crepe at canal prices, and nothing is exaggerated into that. It looks like a lovely real canal restaurant: flower boxes, ivy, lanterns, bikes, calm water. The roofline is the same stepped gable as the other Shamsterdam plates.

## Q2, does it lean into the design requirements? Score 3/5
Most required elements are there: Delft frame, golden sign, windmill, chalkboard, heater posts, blue-checkered tables, copper pans, Delft tiles, clogs, open kitchen pass, steep stairs. Palette: Dutch Blue #1E90FF is only an accent (door, cloths, sign); brick red-brown dominates the exterior, so the palette fails. Missing: begging ducks, the laminated menu, "As Featured In" clippings, windmill miniatures on tables, price signage. The interior is a faithful cosy restaurant with no satire. Not over the top; a respectable twee Dutch cafe, which the author rejected.

## Verdict: REDO (both)
Pretty and literal; the tourist-trap parody is absent.

## Redo plan
- **Hook:** "It's Basically A Crepe, But We Charge For The View" (Satirical Subtitle): authenticity theatre priced by the canal.
- **Palette:** Dutch Blue #1E90FF (facade, door, tiles, cloths; dominant), Butter Yellow #FFD700 (giant pancake sign, glow), Delft White #F5F5F5 (tile ground), Wood Brown #8B4513, Copper Warm #B87333, Canal Gray-Green #5F7A6E (water).
- **Exterior:** Blue Delft-tile-clad front, oversized golden pancake sign, an outsized windmill bolted on, every surface labelled ("AUTHENTIC DUTCH PANCAKES Since 1967", "FAMILIES WELCOME"), heater posts with price tags, ducks lined up begging.
- **Interior:** Delft-tile overload, windmill miniature on every table, never-used copper pans, laminated menu on a lectern, press clippings stopping at 2014, assembly-line griddle pass.
- **Satire beats made visible:** (1) windmill miniatures "15 SOVS if broken" (Furniture & Props); (2) heaters 2 SOVS/hour (Environmental Context); (3) "As Featured In" clippings 2009-2014 (Decorative Elements); (4) stock photos of "traditional pancake making".
