# Audit: Midnight Munchies General Store (debaucheryville)

Sources: `design/worlds/debaucheryville/midnight_munchies_general_store.md`, its PNG asset spec, reviews `_ext_review.jpg` / `_int_review.jpg`.

## Q1, represents the location? Score 2/5
- Doc l.30-31 "beacon for the desperate", l.9 "3 AM Prices": the exterior is a tidy brick corner house with flower boxes, ivy, a lamppost and a rooftop AC unit. It reads as a quaint village shop, not a desperation depot.
- Doc l.32 "Cluttered" window: the display is neat shelves.
- Signs painted: name, "Open When You Need Us", hours. Missing: "Prices reflect the hour" (l.38) and "You could have bought this cheaper earlier. But here we are." (l.39), the best joke in the file.
- Interior: orderly straight aisles, empty floor. Doc l.45 "Cramped aisles, maximum product density", "Entry Chaos". The heat lamp station (l.80-84) is a small case at far right. The four named sections are not told apart. No 2 AM price shift, no Desperation Tax signage, no Hostel Row seediness.

## Q2, leans into design requirements? Score 2/5
Palette (l.93): White #F5F5F5 primary, Green #00FF00 accent, Late Night Blue #191970 background, Warning Red #FF6347.
- Exterior: dominant colours are tan brick and cobble. Green appears only in the neon sign and glow. No blue ground, and no red anywhere.
- Interior: grey-brown grime and dark green fridge glow. Some blue on the back wall, but no fluorescent white, no red.
- Satire: the building itself tells no joke. It is a respectable, historically pretty shop; the "Prague it up" failure the brief bans. Boring.

## Verdict: REDO

## Redo plan
**Hook:** The shop is a fluorescent lifeline that charges you for your own bad planning (l.101, l.230).
**Palette:** #F5F5F5 blown-out fluorescent walls and fridge glare dominate; #00FF00 neon and energy-drink glow draw the eye; #191970 night sky and street ground; #FF6347 on price/warning signs.
**Exterior:** Squat corner box, over-lit like a beacon (l.30-31), fascia crammed with signs, window packed floor to ceiling (l.32), fluorescent spill across the street, flickering neon (l.34). Lit signs carry all six doc lines (l.34-39), the "Prices reflect the hour" and "cheaper earlier" ones in red. Clearly unlike the placeholder neighbours.
**Interior:** Cramped aisles at maximum density (l.46); four labelled sections (Recovery, Enhancement, "Just In Case", Practical); big glowing heat lamp station (l.80); Věra's counter with newspaper (l.86-90); lottery tickets; hard fluorescent top light; no people.
**Satire beats made visible:**
- Desperation Tax price board with a 2 AM line (l.20, l.197).
- "Honest addition" sign (l.39).
- Heat lamp with mystery meat (l.82-83).
- "Brass knuckles" keychains and party-popper "flash grenades" (l.67-68).
- Fake VIP wristbands and fake club stamps (l.62, l.75).
**Not in the file, needs approval:**
- Should the interior show a visible clock reading 2 AM or later?
- How crowded should the heat lamp case look (can the 10% "why did you do this" item be shown)?
- Is a rooftop or awning gag wanted for the exterior, or keep the façade to doc signs only?
