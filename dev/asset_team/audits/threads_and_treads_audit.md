## Q1, how well does it represent the location? Score 3/5
Doc: "Narrow storefront, dark facade, aggressive aesthetic", "leather and metal everywhere", window "LEATHER • CHAINS • BOOTS • ATTITUDE", honest line "Looking Dangerous Sold Separately (It's Inside You)", 24/7 hours (ext). Painted: a narrow black front with the sign, tagline, window text, honest line and a 24/7 plaque. The text is right. But skull-and-wings banners, red and green ivy and a brass lantern make it a charming heritage shop, not "Edgelord Armory" and "Illusion of Danger". The ivy actively undercuts "aggressive".

## Q2, interior and exterior requirements? Score 2/5
Palette: Leather Black #1A1A1A primary, Chrome #C0C0C0 accent, Dark Red #8B0000 background, Industrial Gray #4A4A4A. Painted: warm brown wood, amber lanterns and Persian-style rugs dominate; red is small banners. Chrome and gray are barely there. Interior misses: the Entry Gauntlet of jackets on both walls (partly), Secret Soft Section, Mirror Wall (not visible), "industrial lighting". It looks like a cosy Victorian tailor. Over the top: no. The satire (buying an identity at 2 AM, "genuine" vs "genuine") is not visible anywhere.

## Verdict: REDO (both)

## Redo plan
**Hook:** The all-night store where you buy a new personality at 2 AM: "Leather, Chains, and the Illusion of Danger."
**Palette:** #1A1A1A walls and facade; #C0C0C0 chains, studs, fixtures; #8B0000 red glow, curtains, lining; #4A4A4A floors, pipes.
**Exterior:** Narrow black slab, chain-swagged, spiked trim, chrome lettering sign, red glow through windows, mannequins in leather and boots, 24/7 plaque, "Looking Dangerous Sold Separately (It's Inside You)". No ivy.
**Interior:** Jacket gauntlet down both walls, Boots section (steel toe), Accessories wall of chains and spiked bracelets, a small cosy Secret Soft Section of band tees tucked behind, and a full Mirror Wall with flattering light; industrial fixtures.
**Satire beats made visible:** "genuine" vs "genuine" tags (Section 1); the Soft Section hidden behind a curtain (Section 4); flattering mirror lighting "because commerce"; the 2 AM clientele.
