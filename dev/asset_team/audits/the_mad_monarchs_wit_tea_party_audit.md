## Q1, how well does it represent the location? Score: 3/5
Doc: "Everything is orange: the beer, the walls, the urinal cakes, and anyone who arrives in another colour, because the bouncer has a paint sprayer." Painted: orange cobbles, orange awning, bunting, the "HAPPY BIRTHDAY YOUR MAJESTY" banner, a bike, overspray on the neighbours, a chalkboard. Good. Missing: the bouncer (only a small orange tank by the door), brass band, visible paint mist, the "TOMORROW IS CANCELLED" plaque. The walls are brown brick, so "everything is orange" is untrue of the building itself. Interior has the long table and clocks, but no orange chairs, no orange teacups (white linen), no copper urns behind glass, no dais with the cake.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? Score: 3/5
Palette: Party Orange #FF7A00 shows in awning, cobbles and glow, but dark brick and navy dilute it; orange is a tint on a normal street, not dominant. The exterior is another stepped-gable canal house with railing and trees (the repeated staging flagged in the finished-art check). It is warm and festive, not a 28-year binge: no hungover lifers, no bunting layered like tree rings, no stopped clock on the facade. Nothing says seedy or "mandatory fun".

## Verdict: REDO (both)
