# Audit: Hostel Midnight Checkout (debaucheryville)

**Verdict: REDO**

## Q1, represents the location? 2/5
- Doc: "four stories, questionable structural choices", "faded grandeur meets budget renovation". Painted: a tidy, sound brick townhouse beside near-identical neighbours. Reads as a nice old building, not a hostel for "the same desperate clientele".
- Doc signs are present (two dead neon letters, 4.2 Stars, 24 HOUR asterisk, Quiet Hours, window ACs) but small and tidy. Nothing is the joke.
- Interior is ground floor only. No bunks, dorm floors, basement, Wi-Fi corner crowd, or the 40 strangers the doc calls the core concept.

## Q2, leans into requirements? 2/5
- Palette: Budget Beige #D4C4A8 should dominate; the face is orange-brown brick and grey stone. Hostel Blue #4169E1 is nearly absent. Neon is hot pink, not Warning Red #FF6347. Green #8FBC8F appears only as interior wainscot.
- Satire (note museum, 60% working ladders, wrong-bed syndrome) is not visible from the street. Respectable and moody-pretty, not seedy, not over the top.

## Redo plan
**Hook**: Hostel chaos held together by passive-aggressive notes ("12 SOVS/Night. Your Dignity Costs Extra.").

**Palette**: Beige #D4C4A8 facade and walls; Blue #4169E1 bunk frames, sign trim; Green #8FBC8F corridors; Red #FF6347 neon and warning notes; Faded Wood #8B4513 doorframe.

**Exterior**: Four storeys visibly patched and listing, ornate doorframe beside plastic signage, ACs at random intervals, flickering neon, plain neighbours.

**Interior**: Cutaway storeys plus basement: reception (half-empty key hooks), common room, kitchen note museum, Wi-Fi corner, bunk floors, basement lockers and washers.

**Satire beats made visible**:
1. Neon "two letters flickering"; "4.2 Stars, 'It was an experience'" (Signage).
2. Kitchen notes "LABEL YOUR FOOD", "WHO TOOK MY CHEESE" (Kitchen).
3. Bunks with 60% working ladders (Bunk Bed Experience).
4. "Quiet Hours 11PM-7AM" ignored (Signage).
