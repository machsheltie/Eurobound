## Q1, how well does it represent the location? Score 2/5
Doc: faded green door, 60%-working buzzer, brass "TOLERANCE" sign, hand-painted sign, TripAdvisor sticker 3.4 stars, "PARTY HOSTEL" badge, door note "RING TWICE. WAIT.", weed smoke from upper windows, EDM, a guy on the stoop, between a cheese shop and a "coffee" shop. The plate has a cheese shop and a green-lit leaf shop beside it (good), a green door, small stickers and a little smoke. But the building is a clean sandstone gable with a neat sign and flower boxes. It looks like a quaint boutique inn, not a decaying party hostel.

## Q2, does it lean into the design requirements? Score 3/5
Exterior: no stoop guy, no door note, no passive-aggressive notes, no laundry, no grime. Beige #D4C5A9 is present as pleasant stone; Budget Blue #4169E1 is nearly absent and Notice Yellow #E8D44D is absent. Interior: only the lobby was painted. It has the plexiglass desk, vending machine, 2009 TV, old PC, world map and three couches, which is well observed, but the dorm, THE BUNK, kitchen and bathroom (the doc's best comedy) are not shown. The lobby is clean and tidy with an empty centre rug, far from "three couches that tell three different stories of human suffering". Neither over the top nor seedy.

## Verdict: REDO (both)
Exterior is a respectable inn; interior covers one of four required rooms and is too tidy.
