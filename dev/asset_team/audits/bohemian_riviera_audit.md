## Q1, how well does it represent the location? Score: 4/5
A lot of the doc is painted: three port-o-potties on a pneumatic arm with hazard tape, the "BEATDOWN - LETO - SEZONA 22" banner (l.74), the EMTV camera crane, 2003 pallet, "NO DIVING (SERIOUSLY, IT'S 40cm)" on the tower, space heaters, inflatable palms, mismatched loungers, and the container / tanning-bed / tiki bar strip. Missing: Petra's sign-up table, the prize podium, Zdenka's shower cart, Rosta's boat-hook station, the "40 TONS - NOT FOR HUMAN CONSUMPTION" receipt stapled to the signpost (l.81), the damp stain under the arm (l.99). The palms stand straight, but the doc says they sag at 15 degrees (l.82). The river is a pretty green, not murky.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? Score: 3/5
Palette (l.119-125): Sand #E8D4A0 dominates, as it should. Palm green #32CD32 is present. River #5E7350 is rendered as a pleasant sparkle, not murk. EMTV Magenta #FF1493 is a tiny logo and a neon bar, not the production brand it should be on crane, banner and signage. Heater Orange appears only in small glows. Satire is carried by props, but the whole plate reads as a polished tropical-resort diorama: clean blue potties, tidy tiki bar, no filth. It lacks the doc's seediness (flies, stain, broken latch swinging open, space heaters in July heat shimmer).

## Verdict: REDO (exterior; interior not built)
Strong bones, but too pretty and missing three of the six zones.

## Redo plan
- **Hook:** landlocked beach resort with a catapult show, "a beach resort experience for a country with no beach, no resort, and no shame" (l.3).
- **Palette:** #E8D4A0 sand, #5E7350 murky river, #FF1493 on crane, banner and signage, #708090 steel arm, #32CD32 listing palms, #FF7F32 heaters, #FFC966 string lights.
- **Exterior:** keep the layout. Add the signpost with its stapled sand receipt, murkier water, the sign-up table with fanned release forms, the prize podium with a trapdoor and creased banner, Zdenka's cart, and Rosta's stand with a stacked port-o-potty monument.
- **Interior:** none built. Needs an approved interior per bar (Zha Zha container, The Tan Line, Kokos Loco).
- **Satire beats made visible:** sign "NO DIVING (SERIOUSLY, IT'S 40cm)" (l.74); the center potty's latch swinging open (l.96); the 2003 pallet (l.98); plastic coconuts (l.92).
- **Not in the file, needs approval:**
  - Is an interior wanted for the Riviera, given the doc defines zones outdoors?
  - Should the bar strip get its own plate set?
  - Is a pass at the murkier, grubbier look wanted before any added zones?
