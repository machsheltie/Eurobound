## Q1, how well does it represent the location? 3/5
Doc: a BASEMENT den "beneath Hostel Row", narrow staircase down, flickering INTERNET neon, KAVARNA in smaller Cyrillic, three warning signs. The exterior plate has the neon, the Cyrillic line and all three signs, but the building is a tidy three-storey brick townhouse with shutters, window flower boxes, an air conditioner and a potted shrub. It reads as a pleasant guesthouse, not a basement between hostels. Nothing says seedy, 24-hour or scammer. Interior is much closer: CRT maze, mismatched desks, ethernet snaking, ashtrays, posters, red crypto rigs, striped knockoff-Adidas banner, stairs down.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? 3/5
Exterior 2: flower boxes and warm glowing windows are the opposite of "constant ventilation hum trying to clear cigarette smoke and desperation". No smoke vent, no wall-of-stickers, no hostel neighbours in its shadow. Palette: #00FF41 green is only a small sign (about 3% of the frame); the street is beige and terracotta. #DC143C appears on the signs only. Interior 3: green CRTs and BSOD blue are present, and #696969 grey is right, but the floor is a nice red-and-cream checker, the room is tidy and well lit, and only two of about nine screens show Windows 98 blue screens. Missing: the "bootleg movie posters covering walls" are a few framed pinups, no printed ICQ lists taped to monitors, no overflowing ashtray pile reading as despair, no Terminal #7/#13 marking. Satire (Matrix-wannabe tourist, "your data is not safe") is not visible in either plate. It is respectable.

## Verdict: REDO (exterior fully; interior second pass)
Both scores are under 4 and neither plate is over the top. The exterior has to be rebuilt as a hole in the ground; the interior needs a punch-up, not a new room.

## Redo plan
- **Hook:** "Your Data Is Not Safe (Cash Only)": a Matrix-wannabe basement run by people who learned computers from bootleg CDs.
- **Palette:** #00FF41 floods the stairwell and spills up onto the pavement and neighbouring hostel walls; #DC143C on every warning sign and the Adidas banner; #696969 smoke-stained concrete ground; #E8E8D0 beige CRT casings; #0000FF blue screens in a cascade.
- **Exterior:** not a townhouse. Plain, shabby hostel flank walls pinch a sunken stair (basement entrance "partially hidden between hostels"). One huge flickering INTERNET neon bolted askew over the stair rail, KAVARNA in Cyrillic below it, the three warnings stencilled on. Ventilation pipe belching smoke at street level, cables strung hostel to hostel, green glow pooling on cobbles. Different shape from the existing contact sheet: one low storey of street, mostly stair and sunken yard.
- **Interior:** keep layout and camera. Swap the checker floor for stained concrete; add ethernet "like digital ivy" duct-taped up the walls; many more BSOD screens; printed ICQ lists on every monitor; walls papered in bootleg posters and knockoff Adidas banners; denser smoke haze; Terminal #7 with its error message and #13 marked; hidden metro back door; fluorescent strips plus green glow, sickly.
- **Satire beats made visible:** (1) "NO TORRENTING / CASH ONLY / YOUR DATA IS NOT SAFE" signage (doc l.31); (2) printed scammer ICQ lists taped to monitors, Windows 98 machines (l.46, 83); (3) crypto rigs humming like shrines (l.45, 143); (4) a bro-bait "HACK THE PLANET" feel against plain Windows 98 (l.60, 150-152).
- **Not in the file, needs approval:** Should a hostel signage name appear on the flanking walls? How much of the stairwell may be shown below street level in the elevation? Is the poster art a spoof film (Hackers is real and keeps its real name) or generic?
