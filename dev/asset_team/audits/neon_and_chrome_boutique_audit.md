Doc: "club entrance that sells things", "Mannequins in full club regalia, bathed in purple and pink neon", sign in "glowing LED script", small honest line "Actual belonging sold separately" (ext, Signage). ## Verdict: REDO (both exterior and interior)

## Redo plan
**Hook:** A club entrance that sells things: the shop itself is a fake VIP door ("Dress for the Club You Want to Be In.").
**Palette:** #FF1493 floods the facade and sign; #4B0082 walls and floor; #C0C0C0 chrome frame, rails, mirrors; #00BFFF LED strips and glasses.
**Exterior:** Glass-and-chrome front built as a nightclub entrance: velvet rope and stanchions, a wedge of pink neon light leaking onto the street, chrome-framed automatic door, window mannequins in full club regalia, LED-script sign, tagline "Equip Your Night", categories strip, "Look Like You Belong" with the small "Actual belonging sold separately" (doc Signage).
**Interior:** Showroom in "club preview" mode: Eyewear Station with mirror, Platform wall with the tallest boots highest, Access Accessories with an empty wristband holder, Glow section in UV, locked Premium Stock behind rope, the Club Simulation booth (strobe, UV, speakers, fog), 3/4 high view, no people.
**Satire beats made visible:** wristband holder holding nothing ("Theme & Vibe"); "The higher the platform, the higher the price" (Section 2); "Premium Stock (Locked Until Boss Victory)"; bass-lit "Club preview" look.
**Not in the file, needs approval:** Is a visible doorman/queue prop allowed in the plate (no people rule)? Should rope signage use the Velvet Curtain name? How tall may the platform pyramid be?
