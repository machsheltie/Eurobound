## Q1, how well does it represent the location? Score: 3/5
Doc "Structure": a 1.5 m, six-storey brick house leaning 7 degrees, hook at the top, FIRE EXIT signs pointing at the hook. All painted, and the sign board is correct. But the premise is the net, the boats and the lawyer. Painted: no cargo net on the pavement (only a rope coil), no tour boat, no phone-wielding tourists, and the "narrower house, 1.2 m, man in a suit at a fifth-floor desk" is a normal-width house with a lit window. The lean reads as a slight slump, not "three beers in". No framed 1689 fire certificate visible.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? Score: 2/5
Palette: Canal Brick #8C3B2A is present on the gable, but Canal Green #3E5B4A is mostly replaced by two huge yellow-green trees that swallow a third of the frame, and Hoist Rope #C8A165 is a thin thread. Satire is whispered: this is a pretty canal postcard, the very thing the building is meant to mock. It shares the stepped gable, railing and tree staging flagged in the finished-art check. Interior (content and tone only): a cosy lit cutaway, copper kettle and bathtub present, but no bobsled stools, no bucket on a hook, no 212 letters, no 7 degree tilt. Respectable and boring.

## Verdict: REDO (exterior; interior needs the same content fixes, its layout stays parked)

## Redo plan
- **Hook:** "Leaning since 1689. Load-bearing since never": a fire hazard selling itself as charm.
- **Palette:** Canal Brick #8C3B2A dominates the whole gable; Hoist Rope #C8A165 as a fat visible net and rope; Canal Green #3E5B4A for water and ground only; Gable White #F2EDE4 for sign and certificate. Cut the trees.
- **Exterior:** The skinniest silhouette on the street, visibly lurching 7 degrees. Cargo net spread on the pavement under the hook. Green FIRE EXIT signs on every floor pointing at the hook. Fire certificate in the window. Tour boat below with a forest of phones; the 1.2 m house beside it with the lawyer at his fifth-floor desk. Bike racks.
- **Interior:** Each floor 1.5 m wide and tilted 7 degrees; bobsled bar stools, bathtub fermenter on wedges, bucket on the second hook, tally on floor 4, extinguisher on floor 6.
- **Satire beats (doc lines):** FIRE EXIT arrows "pointing out the window at the hook"; "SPARE ROPE (1974)"; net weight limit "SOME"; lawyer "taking notes".
