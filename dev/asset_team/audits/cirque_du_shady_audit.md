## Q1, how well does it represent the location? Score: 4/5
The exterior is faithful and funny. Painted: arch "THE CIRQUE DU SHADY / EST." with the date scratched out and repainted (doc l.86), the "ADMISSION 5 SOVS. EVERYTHING ELSE EXTRA." booth with the "(THIS INCLUDES THE BATHROOM.)" addendum, flyer corkboard, Vance's tent with fogged flap and "RINGMASTER - BY APPOINTMENT (NO APPOINTMENTS NEEDED)", the ICQ flap with a CRT glow at night, rust-ringed pole, stitched canvas. Missing: the grift pipeline the doc calls the whole point (l.131). No game alley rig, no Shady Productions fax table and 2003 pallet (l.106-110), no prophecy glow, no meat cart, no concrete-sunk stakes. The interior is a generic circus ring with a target board, and none of the doc's zones appear in it.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? Score: 3/5
Palette (l.120): Big-Top Red #B22222 and Canvas Cream #F5F0DC dominate the canvas, correctly. Neon Marquee #FF6EC7 is only a thin sign outline. Vape-Cloud Gray #C0C0C8 appears as one small puff, so it is not dominant. Satire: the signs carry the jokes, but the architecture does not. It reads as a lovingly distressed vintage circus, handsome and tidy, not a vertically integrated scam. The interior has no satire at all: no fax, no flyer, no "2003" pallet, no jammed ticket dispenser, no knife-scarred board joke, no Bubbles.

## Verdict: REDO (interior; exterior pushes harder)
Exterior is close to a keep but too pretty. The interior is a hard redo.

## Redo plan
- **Hook:** a circus that has not traveled since 1997 and runs a full-stack scam ecosystem (l.3, l.131).
- **Palette:** #B22222 canvas and gate trim, #F5F0DC canvas base and folding-table cloth, #FF6EC7 fat marquee as the eye-catcher, #C0C0C8 vape fog filling Vance's tent, #6A0DAD / #D4AF37 tent-seam glow.
- **Exterior:** keep gate and booth. Add concrete tent-stake footings, a rust-ringed wheel pole, a jammed ticket dispenser, game alley with concrete milk bottles, the prophecy tent glowing through its seams, and an overflowing fog bank from Vance's tent.
- **Interior:** the Shady Productions office behind the canvas wall: folding card table, one-wheeled office chair, fax machine, drifts of identical flyers, shrink-wrapped pallet stamped 2003 (l.107-110). Add the performance circle with mismatched folding chairs and a scarred target board, and the ICQ CRT corner. Hay and the Bubbles enclosure at the edge.
- **Satire beats made visible:** "(THIS INCLUDES THE BATHROOM.)" (l.86); the identical flyer stack reading "WANTED: ATHLETIC-INTELLECTUAL HYBRID" (l.108); the 2003 pallet (l.109); the tent that has "clearly not moved" (l.88).
