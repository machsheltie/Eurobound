## Q1, how well does it represent the location? Score: 2/5
Doc: "Instagram magnet", "Respect The Grain. Fear The Spin.", a building that spins, tourists comparing tasting notes. Painted: a handsome, serene windmill at golden hour over green fields with a neat sign set and a ramp. That is a calendar picture, not the parody. Missing: any tourist or photo queue, the train platform joke, any wobble or lean (the name is Wobbly), grain dust haze, the liability comedy. The interior shows only the ground floor as a tasteful wine shop with a compass floor; the doc's gift shop is "EVERYTHING" branded, "I SURVIVED THE SPIN" shirts, 18 SOV glasses. Shelves here are sparse and elegant. No grinding floor, tasting room, vibrating vats, gears or blade level.

## Q2, how well does it lean into the design requirements? Score: 2/5
Palette: Grain Gold #DAA520, Copper #B87333, Windmill Wood #8B4513 are partly present (copper pipes, wood), but sunset cloud, blue sky and countryside green #6B8E23 dominate the exterior; gold does not lead. The satire target is "authentic experience tourism, paying premium for discomfort", and nothing looks like a marketed trap. Same heritage-postcard register as the other Shamsterdam plates. Respectable and boring.

## Verdict: REDO (both)

## Redo plan
- **Hook:** a real 1680 windmill monetised as a dizzying photo prop.
- **Palette:** Grain Gold #DAA520 on sails, signage and beer; Copper #B87333 on vats and pipes; Windmill Wood #8B4513 on the tower; Flour White #FFFAF0 for dust haze; countryside only as a thin strip.
- **Exterior:** Tower visibly tilted and spinning, thick grain-dust haze, fairy lights and banners on the sails, the modern sign glued over the ancient door, the ramp, WATCH HEAD and "dizziness is not our fault" signs at readable scale, a photo-op frame, a queue of phone tourists.
- **Interior:** Gift shop crammed to the ceiling (glasses, souvenirs, book, SURVIVED THE SPIN shirts); grinding floor with vibrating vats, open grain bins, five-language DO NOT TOUCH signs; tasting room with barrel tables and flight boards; blade-level selfie platform.
- **Satire beats (doc lines):** "The spinning is intentional. The dizziness is not our fault."; history panels "5% windmill, 95% beer"; "Brewmaster's throne"; blade level "Danger Level: theoretical (lawyers insisted)".
