## Hook
Trijntje hand-writes every shelf card in ballpoint on yellow card: the filthiest text in Shamsterdam, delivered like a woman selling tea towels.

## Exterior
Zone 2, on the square (line 460). Proposal: a squat, cheerful seaside-souvenir kiosk in red, not a canal gable, bursting past its own doorway. A rack of ponchos and tea towels on the pavement, a postcard spinner showing only the bin, `I DIDN'T GO IN` shirts hanging from the awning like bunting, an oversized keyring on the sign. Yellow cards taped to the window, unreadable from afar, legible at zoom. Fountain and rubber duck sit in front (lines 104-105). Plain brick neighbours. Differs from the stepped-gable plates by being a tourist-trap stall made of merchandise.

## Interior
One small, overstocked room. Wall of shelves and a till counter with Trijntje's chain-glasses spot, a mirror (the ponchos go on in front of it, line 481). Stock from the table (line 464): passports, `I DIDN'T GO IN` and `I WENT IN TWICE` shirts, keyring, candle, bonbon box, district tea towel, bin postcard, guilder, ponchos, pen. Every item carries a yellow shelf card in her hand, verbatim. Lino, strip light warmed by red spill. High three-quarter camera, no people.
