Roofline: flat parapet carrying a giant wall-clock face. At the door: platform scales set into the pavement, a brass gong on a stand in the window. Signs, doc text exactly: "STEP ON THE SCALES. I CANNOT HELP YOU UNTIL YOU STEP ON THE SCALES.", "THE CHART IS RIGHT. YOU ARE NOT THE EXCEPTION. THERE HAS NEVER BEEN AN EXCEPTION.", "THE WATER IS FREE. THAT IS NOT A TECHNIQUE. THAT IS WATER.", "DO NOT TOUCH THE GONG." Staging: a water cooler on the kerb, no railing or tree. Differs from the gable plates by being a lopsided data-sheet tower, and from Erotiek & Zo (wide white box) and the bar (low dark corner) by height and green.
