## Hook
"The Wall Provides. The Wall Judges Not." A coin-op wall of tiny lit doors dispensing hot fried mystery at 3 AM; the building is a shrine to bad decisions.

## Palette
- Frietomat Yellow #FFD700: whole facade slab, letters, uniforms (dominant)

- Fluorescent White #F5F5F5: interior wash, pavement light spill (ground)

## Exterior
Flat-fronted, two-storey modern-infill slab, fully yellow, with a flat cornice and a glass shopfront. No stepped gable, so it differs from all five Shamsterdam plates. Signs, spelled exactly: "FRIETOMAT" (huge, lit), "AUTOMATIEK", "SINCE 1941", "24 UUR", window food photos "that look better than reality". Glass doors always open, steam escaping, fluorescent light spilling across the pavement. Staging proposal: street-corner angle, no canal railing, no tree. Plain unnamed neighbours stay dark so it burns like a beacon.

## Interior
Single room. THE WALL (4 rows x 6 chrome-framed glass doors, red occupied lights) fills the left two-thirds: Kroket/Frikandel/Bamischijf; Kaassouflé/Berehap/Mexicano; Hamburger/Cheeseburger/Satéschotel; Mystery Row. Counter (right), kitchen serving window (rear, fryer glow, steam), standing ledges, two sad stools, "COIN CHANGE" machine (taped note), full trash bin, empty napkin dispenser, wall clock, menu board. Floor: not stated (PNG lists none), so not checkerboard; proposal below. Light: harsh fluorescent plus warm door glow.
