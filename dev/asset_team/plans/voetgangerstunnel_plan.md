**Hook**: "The Underground Labyrinth of Wrong Turns": signage built to filter tourists, with an "accidental" exit between two red-lit windows.

**Palette**: Tunnel White #F0F0F0 (wall tile); Stripe Blue #003DA5 and Stripe Orange #FF6600 (meaningless stripes, distinct end-caps); Floor Speckle #8B8B8B (terrazzo, worn real routes); Fluorescent Yellow #FFFACD (overhead cast, flicker); Emergency Green #00FF00 (Nooduitgang signs, glowing, the honest ones); Warning Red #FF0000 (Uitgang signs near the red-window exit).

**Area layout**: Plate A, Entrance Hall into the Intersection of Doubt: stairs down from the Bike Crossing, wall map with a wrong "You Are Here" dot, Marloes's kiosk built into the wall, bicycle parking spilling underground, Henk's booth where five corridors meet, signs "Centraal Station", "Museum Plein", "Centrum" all pointing the same vague way. Exits in the nodes: up to the surface, long corridor, wrong turn zone, back to entrance, two "mystery" corridors. Plate B, Wrong Turn Zone: graffiti "DON'T FOLLOW HENK", "Exit 7 = Red Light LOL", "Willem knows"; exits to Centraal Chaos, Roodelicht Row, Coffeeshop Quarter, canal, start, hidden Frietomat. Willem (accordion, 247 SOVS in his hat) plays in the Long Corridor, which has adverts for businesses closed in 2003.
