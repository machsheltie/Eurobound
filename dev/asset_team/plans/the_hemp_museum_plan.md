## Hook
"It's About The PLANT, Actually": a museum of 500 years of history that everyone speed-walks past to reach the live plants, then buys a grinder.

## Palette
Educational Green #228B22 door, signage, hemp imagery. Museum Cream #F5F5DC plaques, panels. Historic Brown #8B4513 timber, floors, frames, facade. Grow Light Purple #8B008B top-floor wash. Gift Shop Bright #FFE9A8 shop lighting. Dust Gray #9A9186 grime.

## Exterior
Tall narrow canal house in Historic Brown, green door, real brass plaque, tacky neon "OPEN" beside it. Signs: "HASH MARIHUANA & HEMP MUSEUM" in respectable serif; "500 Years of History"; window "EDUCATIONAL EXHIBITS" huge and "Gift Shop" tiny; door "No Smoking Inside". Purple grow-light glow leaks from the top-floor windows (the real attraction). Gift-shop annex bulging beside the front, bigger than the museum (needs approval). Neighbours stay plain and unnamed: peep show one side, coffeeshop the other. Roofline: bell gable with hoist beam, not stepped (needs approval). Differs from existing plates: green door and purple glow, no canal railing or tree row.

## Interior
Entry Hall (ticket counter, audio-guide rack, 10,000 BCE timeline, coat hooks, donation box); Industrial Hemp (ground; rope cases, sailor's quarters, "Feel the Hemp!"); Historical Usage (first; pipes, vintage ads, Prohibition Timeline); Growing and Processing (second; observation window, smell jars, video loop); Gift Shop (six zones, larger than any exhibit, tea station); Hemp Garden Courtyard (Henrik's bench). Floor: worn canal-house boards, no checkerboard. Light: "dim with spots", purple on floor two, #FFE9A8 in the shop, daylight in the courtyard.

## Satire beats made visible
1. Spotless empty Industrial room against fingerprinted Growing Room glass (PNG Sheet 4 notes).
2. Gift shop larger than every exhibit ("The gift shop is 40% of the floor space").
3. Queen Victoria panel, readable at gameplay zoom ("everyone remembers this one").
4. "Seeds: legal to sell, illegal to grow" cabinet, with the Growing Guide on the shelf behind.
5. Free hemp tea placed between stairs and shop ("loss leader").
