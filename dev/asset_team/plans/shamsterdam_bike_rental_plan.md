## Hook
"The shop sells the illusion of belonging": orange rental bikes that mark tourists as prey, insurance tiers rising from theft to dignity, and a family profiting from every crash.

## Palette
Rental Orange #FF6600 bikes, signage, uniforms, ground-floor cladding. Tourist Target / Warning Yellow #FFD700 caution highlights, Platinum border. Canal Blue #003DA5 canal wall, old blue rental bikes. Asphalt/Grease Grey #4A4A4A floor, stands, repair station. Damage Red #CC0000 insurance warnings. Receipt White #F5F5F5 walls, forms; Receipt Cream #FFF8DC paper and workshop light.

## Exterior
Converted canal-house ground floor wrapped solid orange, giant "BIKE RENTAL" sign in four languages, "Fiets Verhuur" small and ignored. 53 identical orange bikes in rows on stands spilling across the pavement, each marked "TOURIST". Orange brand stripe at eye level. Staging: south sidewalk beside the crossing, one bent bike, a selfie spot. Neighbours plain. Differs from existing plates: orange and yellow, a wall of bikes instead of canal railing and trees; roofline above the orange base stays plain (needs approval).

Floor: commercial tile, worn tile, bare concrete in the workshop, "Fiets Verhuur" mat, no checkerboard. Light: fluorescent #F5F5DC, workbench spot #FFF8DC.
