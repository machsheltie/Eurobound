## Hook
A four-metre free chocolate-phallus fountain in a public square that everyone double-dips, in front of a museum that treats all of it with total sincerity (md Theme & Vibe).

## Palette

## Exterior
Wide paved forecourt on a plaza, NOT the canal-gable street: a grand four-storey nineteenth-century civic frontage, too grand for its contents. The fountain is the hero: a four-metre bronze-finish stainless phallus pouring dark couverture over four tiers, steaming, in front of the door. Gunter's green-and-white cart four metres off. Queue snaking. Signs, spelled exactly: "THE DONG PALACE" (art deco, crooked by two degrees), "DE CACAOGALERIJ — Nu Geopend", "ADULTS ONLY", "Educational Exhibits" plaque, pink neon Venus outline, plinth plaque "FONTEIN VAN DE GULLE GEVER — 2011", "EEN KEER DOPEN A.U.B. / ONE DIP PLEASE" road-sized sign, HYGIËNESCORE card. Opposite the empty "I SHAMSTERDAM" plinth. Unlike the five existing plates: stone civic hall plus giant landmark, no brick stepped gable, no railing-and-tree staging.

## Interior
Entrance Hall: marble, ticket desk, Giggling Figure, bench, framed fine. Ground, De Cacaogalerij: cold 17 degree gallery, vitrines, De Oervorm, National Styles Wall, Scale Progression, glass Tempering Room, Failures Case. First, De Mechanische Vleugel: red/pink wash, mutoscopes, Marilyn statue, sketches, chastity belts, 1970s section, worn board floor. Second, Ervaar de Tempel: Throne, Mirror Maze, Costume Corner, Swing, wax figures, green screen, flash light. Exit Gift Shop: sparse, lit shelves. Floors per docs (marble, pale gallery tile, worn board); no checkerboard.
