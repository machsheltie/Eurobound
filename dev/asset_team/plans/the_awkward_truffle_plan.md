## Hook
Complete boutique dignity applied without blinking to chocolate genitals; the shop's layout is the customer's slide from "I'll just look" to "I need the party platter" (md Theme).

## Palette
Chocolate Brown #3C1414 (product bodies, shadows), Belgian Gold #CFB53B (script, case trim, award plaques), Cream Display #FFF8DC (walls, case interiors, wrapping), Anatomical Pink #FFB6C1 (packaging, Zone 3 ribbon), PNG spec adds Boutique Warm #E8C87A (light) and Velvet Shadow #2A1A2E (Zone 3 drapes).

## Exterior
The md wants a "charming", "high-end boutique" face hiding the chaos. To keep it from reading as a tidy canal postcard, push the pretension to parody: a narrow, over-gilded luxury-chocolatier front, gold-leaf script fascia, award plaques stacked like medals, a velvet rope and tiny doorman-style awning, and a chocolate-dipped-looking brown-and-gold shopfront. The window front shows only "regular" chocolates, but a pink-ribboned, velvet-curtained gap reveals anatomical hints beyond. Signs, spelled exactly: "THE AWKWARD TRUFFLE", "Artisan Chocolate — Est. 2008", "Belgian Master Chocolatier — Award Winning", "All Shapes Made With Equal Love", tiny "Yes, those are what you think they are". Not a stepped gable with tree and railing; flat-fronted, sandwiched between plain neighbours, so it pops by being the only shiny one.

## Interior
One long shop in three zones, walked left to right. Zone 1, The Respectable Entry: brightest cream, normal truffles and pralines, counter and wrapping station near the front. Zone 2, The Transition: warmer, ambiguous shapes. Zone 3, The Commitment: velvet drapes (#2A1A2E), pink ribbon coding, party platters. Six cases: Classics, Suggestives, Anatomicals, Other Anatomicals, Couples' Collection, Party Platters. The Crafting Window with Sophie visible. Floor: polished cream-and-brown boutique floor, fading to dark velvet carpet in Zone 3 (md names no floor; no checkerboard). Warm boutique spots, glowing crafting window.
