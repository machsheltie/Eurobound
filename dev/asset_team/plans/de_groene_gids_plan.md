## Exterior
Two-storey flat-roofed corner box on two streets. No gable, no stepped roofline, no canal railing, no trees. One wide glass shopfront, a billboard-scale neon on a steel rooftop frame, vents on the roof. Differs from the sheet's brick-gable Shamsterdam row (all stepped gables and trees) and the cream stucco blocks. Neighbours: plain unnamed grey flat-roofed boxes, no signs. The two souvenir shops (l.37) are plain shopfronts with hanging shirt racks, no text.
