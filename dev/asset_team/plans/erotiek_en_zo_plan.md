## Exterior
Not another stepped gable. A bright, enormous, flat-fronted big-box shop crammed into the brick row: three storeys of blank retail slab with a corrugated-steel loading-bay door open at the side, pallet racking visible, and an outsized price-tag sign bolted across the roofline. Fluorescent-lit like a DIY megastore, the one daylight-bright frontage on a red street. Doc signs, spelled exactly: "NO FILMING.", "PLEASE DO NOT TEST THE DISPLAY UNITS.", "RETURNS: 14 DAYS WITH RECEIPT." plus a name board "Erotiek & Zo". Props: a very large plain crate on a pallet by the door, one discreet logo, stencilled `DEZE KANT BOVEN`. Staging: no canal rail or tree; a pallet jack and a queue ribbon on cobbles. Differs from the five gable plates by shape (box, not gable) and light (white, not warm brick); from its neighbours by being the brightest and biggest.
