# The Standing Ovation: art plan

**Files read:** the_standing_ovation.md; PNG_Assets/The_Standing_Ovation_PNG_Assets.md; npcs/The_Standing_Ovation/max_vex.md; items/brewery_tour beer, opener, stamp; sinfonia.md. **MISSING:** core, environment, interactions, items, location_nodes, sprite_mapping JSON; events; separate NPC profiles for Herr Dramatisch, Critics, Stagehands.

**Hook:** Drinking turned into opera. Every pour is a performance; the beer is excellent and the drama "completely unnecessary".

**Palette:** Curtain Red #8B0000 carpet, curtains, marquee trim. Spotlight Gold #FFD700 bulbs, gilding, spots. Theater Black #1A1A1A walls, ground. Foam White #FFFAF0 foam, playbills, footlights. Copper #B87333 kettle, organ-pipe taps. Ruby #9B111E velvet depths. Gilded Brass #B8860B rails. Backstage Grey #4A4A4A Green Room only.

**Exterior:** Three-bay opera-house annex, three storeys, tower each side, gilded cornice, pilasters, carved arch lintel "THE STANDING OVATION". A bulb-ringed marquee reads "NOW POURING:" with changeable beer letters. Signs: "Critics Welcome — We'll Change Their Minds"; "BEER OFFICE" (ticket booth relabelled); board "★★★★★ — The Management". Red carpet flaring at the curb to a bar, worn threadbare down the middle; brass stanchions with decorative velvet rope; mask-shaped door knockers; street lamp angled like a follow-spot; gold light spilling from open doors. Night, bulbs chasing. Unlike every plate: none is a theatre; Stoutworks is copper brick.

**Interior:** Orchestra Pit: sunken bar as stage, organ-pipe taps, footlights, painted backdrop (clouds, cherubs, hops), proscenium arch, chandelier. Balcony: gilded rail, self-appointed critics' box. Green Room: kettle, tank, barrels chalked "TEARS (ALLEGEDLY)", stamp podium, door "PERFORMERS ONLY (EVERYONE IS A PERFORMER)". Wings: recovery booth, "YOU'LL GET THEM NEXT TIME" sampler, tasting nook, "EXIT (DRAMATICALLY)". Velvet curtain exit. Floors: scuffed oak stage boards, red carpet with gold border, balcony parquet, honest grey concrete in the Green Room. No checkerboard. Light: spots and footlights with per-beer colour gels; Green Room flat fluorescent.

**Satire beats made visible:**
1. "Applause signs (used ironically, then unironically)": lit APPLAUSE sign.
2. "'Reviews' framed on walls (all self-written, all glowing)": sandwich board, framed notices by "The Management".
3. "velvet ropes (decorative, not exclusive)": ropes around nothing.
4. "Taps arranged like organ pipes": copper pipe-organ bar.
5. "Where the actual brewing happens (less dramatic)": grey Green Room against gilt front.
