## Hook
A cash register wearing a museum as a costume: 90% gift shop, three floors of tourist bait, every exit through the Chocolate Anatomy Emporium (md Theme; PNG spec overview).

## Palette
Tourist Trap Red #CC3333 (banners, fascia, carpet), Gift Shop Gold #FFD700 (price tags, case trim, egg foil), Questionable Cream #F5E6D3 (walls), Chocolate Brown #5D3A1A (stock, shelving). PNG spec adds Red Bulb Glow #8B1A1A (upper floors) and Fluorescent Wash #F8F8E8 (ground floor).

## Exterior
Narrow and tall, squeezed between a coffeeshop and a souvenir emporium, but NOT another stepped gable on a tree-lined railing. Four storeys of shouting: red fascia, cream upper walls, a flat gaudy roof billboard. Signs, spelled exactly: neon "MUSEUM" (letters wobbling, uncertain, quotation marks per the md's own tell), "SHAMSTERDAM MUSEUM OF QUESTIONABLE ART — Est. 1996", "Adults Only (But We Don't Really Check)", rotating banner "NEW: Chocolate Anatomy Exhibit — PERFECT FOR BACHELOR PARTIES". Glass doors papered with 3.2-star ratings and the "Featured in BuzzFeed's '27 Weird European Museums'" sticker. Street promoters with 2-for-1 coupons. Differs from all five Shamsterdam plates: signage-wall dominates, no brick-gable postcard, no canal staging.

## Interior
Ground floor, Gift Shop and Entrance: fluorescent wash, 60% shop, spinning "I ❤️ SHAMSTERDAM" racks, Daan's register, Chocolate Anatomy Emporium (tiered, glistening, four-language signage), egg case, NFT QR terminal. First floor, "The Erotic Arts Through History": peep shows, window photo booth, red bulbs. Second floor, "Contemporary Expressions": tulip canvases, three pedestals (one broken), looping video, toilet-seat mirror "Masterpiece". Third floor, "The Vault": roped-off storage, motion-sensor lights, spot on the egg case, "PLEASE TAKE ONE" stack. Floor: creaky wooden boards and gift-shop carpet in red accents; no checkerboard (doc names none). Stairs creak between floors.
