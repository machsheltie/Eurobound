## Hook
"Old money, hung at eye level": a museum of other people's inheritances, where art is a tax instrument and the bros paid more than their flight to see proof they will never be rich.

## Palette (doc, PROPOSED)
Vault Gilt #C9A227 lettering, frames, donor plaque. Old Money Green #1F3A2D gallery walls and facade. Marble Hush #E7E2D6 floors, columns. Velvet Rope Red #7A1E2B ropes, carpet runner. Relic Spotlight #FFF3C4 document hall.

## Exterior
PROPOSED, different from The Dong Palace (tall, warm grey stone #9C9184, four storeys, fountain forecourt): a wide, low, symmetrical temple, two storeys, deep green walls, a gilt pediment on six marble columns, set behind iron railings and a velvet rope. A brass donor plaque bigger than the door, text illegible (not in canon). Gilt fascia "THE GALLERY OF GENERATIONAL WEALTH". Horizontal and green against the Dong Palace's vertical stone. Not a canal gable, no brick.

## Interior (four plates, all PROPOSED names)
1. **Portrait Gallery**: portraits of men who never worked, green walls, gilt frames, marble floor, velvet rope.
2. **Yacht Wing**: inherited yachts in miniature in glass cases, blue-lit carpet and brass rails.
3. **Trust-Fund Document Hall**: paperwork in vitrines under Relic Spotlight beams, like relics; diamond-pattern marble.
4. **Nepo Baby Hall of Fame**: side room, descendants' portraits, one plaque each.
