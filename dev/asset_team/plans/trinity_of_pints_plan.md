**Hook:** "Cum Laude in Lager": a pub cosplaying as a prestigious university, where drinking is coursework and every fake credential is party-store quality.

**Palette:** Academic Green #355E3B banners, robes, trim. Scholarly Gold #CFB53B crest, medallions, faux-Latin lettering. Old Parchment #F5DEB3 diplomas, walls, catalog. Wood Brown #8B4513 tables, shelves, podium. Plastic Ivy #4CBB17 ivy, deliberately too bright. Navy #191970 stamp ink, department flags.

**Exterior:** Wide, symmetrical, grand Georgian-pastiche frontage with 5 percent wrong proportions, set behind ornate iron gates scrolled "TRINITY OF PINTS" in faux-Latin. Three storeys, central pediment, one oversized pointless bell lamppost (recorded bell, visible speaker). Plastic ivy with visible zip ties climbs the wall. Signs: "TRINITY OF PINTS — Est. MCMXCVII", motto "In Vino Veritas, In Cervisia Felicitas", "NOW ACCEPTING STUDENTS — No Prerequisites Required", daily chalk course board, tiny "Academic standards may vary". A drunk student silhouette sags in a window. Differs: the only gated, wide, symmetrical campus front; others are narrow street fronts.

**Interior:** Entrance Hall (enrollment desk, fake alumni portraits, trophy case of empties), Great Hall (long tables, head table of robed bartenders, department banners, LED stained glass with pint-glass saint), Library (hollow books, flask compartment, cocktail "research"), Lecture Halls (stadium seating, chalk formulas, graduation podium), the Quad beer garden with lawn games and graduation backdrop. Floor: long plank hall with parchment-toned stone flags in the entrance, no checkerboard. Light: LED faux candles, stained-glass colour wash.
