# Collectible sets and what completing them earns

Research for bead Eurobound-7lys, 2026-11-14. Author's request: "look at games like the Batman Arkham ones with its collectibles (Harley Quinn statues, joker items etc) to see what completion earns. Because we have things like our brewery tour items."

This report describes how other games do it, lists what Eurobound's own sets earn today, and sets out choices. It makes no decisions and invents no Eurobound content, names or numbers.

**How to read the sourcing.** A claim followed by a link was checked against that source this session. A claim marked *(recalled)* is from general knowledge of the game and was not checked against a source this session; the fan wikis that would confirm it refused automated reading. Treat recalled counts as approximate.

## Short version

- Arkham pays out all the way along, and the pay is mostly not power. Every few pickups unlock a character bio, a 3D figurine, concept art, a story tape or a challenge map. Power (upgrade points) comes from the XP each pickup gives.
- Arkham's 100% reward is a scene with the man who set the puzzles: you catch the Riddler. Players liked that in Asylum and City. In Arkham Knight the full ending of the whole game was put behind all 243 Riddler challenges, and critics called it tedious.
- The games players praise make each pickup worth having on its own (a joke, a memory, a line of dialogue), keep sets small, and help you find the last few. The games players resent have huge counts, no help, and a final reward that either is nothing or is something everybody wanted and few will reach.
- Eurobound's brewery tour already has a full ladder: per brewery, per city, and all 16. The gaps are elsewhere: the 12-NFT reward is pending, the openers have no reward of their own, the Chronicles title has two different names in canon, and the Vinyl Hunt has no count.

## 1. Batman Arkham

### Arkham Asylum (2009)

The whole collection is one list, "Riddler challenges", 240 in total, shown as a count per area on the map screen. The Riddler taunts Batman over the radio as the count rises. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Asylum))

| Set | What you collect | How it is tracked | What it earns along the way |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riddler trophies | Green question-mark trophies hidden in vents, behind weak walls, on ledges. The largest share of the 240 ([SegmentNext](https://segmentnext.com/batman-arkham-asylum-riddler-challenges)). | Counted per area. | XP, plus unlocks from the shared pool below. |
| Riddles | One written riddle per room or area. You answer by finding the object and scanning it. Most answers are nods to villains who are not in the game. | Listed per area, solved or unsolved. | XP and a character bio for the villain the riddle points at. |
| Joker teeth | Wind-up chattering teeth, in groups, that you smash. A group counts as one challenge ([TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/a35474/arkham-analyst-achievement)). | Counted per area. | XP. |
| Interview tapes | Patient interview recordings, five per villain *(recalled)*. | Stored in a menu; play back any time. | Each tape is its own reward: a scene of a villain with their doctor. Five tapes together tell one short story. |
| Chronicles of Arkham | Stone tablets carrying the asylum founder's hidden diary. | Menu list, in order. | Each is a chapter of a ghost story. The last one opens a hidden room and reveals who has been writing them ([TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/BatmanArkhamAsylum)). |
| Secrets maps | One map per area. | Once picked up, every unsolved challenge in that area is marked on the map *(recalled)*. | This is the help system, sold as a collectible. |

Shared rewards: XP (which buys Batman's upgrades), character biographies, in-game statues of the characters, and challenge maps ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Asylum)). Achievements fire at percentages of the 240, starting at 5% ([TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/a35474/arkham-analyst-achievement)).

**100%:** Batman traces the Riddler's signal and you hear the police arrest him over the radio. No fight, no item. *(recalled)* The payoff is that the smug voice that has insulted you for the whole game panics.

### Arkham City (2011)

400 challenges as Batman, 440 with Catwoman's ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_City), [PSNProfiles guide](https://psnprofiles.com/guide/427-batman-arkham-city-trophy-guide)).

| Physical challenges | Do a named move or trick. | Checklist. | Squares on the grid. | |
| Riddler hostages | Not picked up. A new hostage room opens each time you pass a set number of challenges ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_City)). Each room is a death-trap puzzle. | Side mission in the mission list. | A puzzle room and a rescued person. | The last hostage opens at 400. Help for the last few: thugs glowing green are Riddler informants. Leave one for last in a fight, interrogate him, and trophy locations appear on your map ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_City)). One guide warns that even all informants do not reveal everything ([PSNProfiles guide](https://psnprofiles.com/guide/427-batman-arkham-city-trophy-guide)).

What critics said: The Guardian called the Riddler missions satisfying; Joystiq called them frustrating when you lack the gadget a trophy needs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_City)).

### Arkham Knight (2015)

243 Riddler challenges: trophies, riddles, breakable objects and "bomb rioters" (thugs with bombs strapped on, to be disarmed) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Knight), [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/guide/batman-arkham-knight-all-riddler-collectibles-locations-trophies-riddles-bomb-rioters-objects.html)). Informants return. Partial progress earns upgrade points and Gotham stories *(recalled)*.

**100%:** you fight the Riddler in a robot suit and lock him in a police cell. And the game's full ending, the "Knightfall Protocol", needs every side mission finished, which includes all 243. A shorter ending plays once about half the side missions are done ([PowerPyx trophy guide](https://powerpyx.com/guides/batman-arkham-knight-trophy-guide.html)).

This was the part players resented. Wikipedia's summary of the reviews lists "the requirement for 100% completion for the ending" among the criticisms. Destructoid found many of the challenges tedious and noted that smashing objects is not a riddle ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Knight)).

### What Arkham teaches

1. One collector, one voice. Every set belongs to the Riddler, so the count is a running argument with a character.
2. A drip of small unlocks. Something opens every few pickups, and the menu shows which pickup opened it.
3. Different kinds of pickup under one total: find it, solve it, smash it, listen to it. No one activity runs to 240.
4. The help is in the world (maps, informants), so asking for help is still playing.
5. The end is a scene with a person. It worked twice. Tying the main story's ending to it did not.

## 3. Patterns

### Kinds of completion reward

| Kind | What it is | Example | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | A stat, skill, slot or item that makes you stronger | Ghost of Tsushima springs; Korok seeds up to 441 | If it is strong, players who dislike collecting feel forced. If it arrives at 100%, there is nothing left to use it on. |
| Cosmetic | A look, costume, badge or change to something on screen | Spider-Man's suit; Arkham's figurines | Low. Needs art. |
| Lore | A story told in pieces | Arkham tapes; Psychonauts vaults | Low, if each piece is good alone. |
| Joke or anti-reward | The prize is deliberately worthless, or a curse | Hestu's Gift; the Auditore cape | Lands only if the useful rewards ended earlier and the player can see the joke is on purpose. |
| Access | A boss, a room, a scene or an ending | Riddler's capture; Yakuza's secret boss | Good when the thing is extra. Resented when it is the real ending (Arkham Knight). |
| Title or tracker | A name, a diploma, a filled book | Pokémon diploma | Cheap, and thin by itself. |
| More collecting | A tool that helps the next hunt | Shiny Charm | Only works if there is a next hunt. |

### Pacing

- Pay per pickup, per small group and at the end. The games people praise have all three.
- Group by place. A district or a city is a set you can finish in one sitting. Arkham, Ghost of Tsushima and Assassin's Creed II all count per area.
- Put the useful rewards early and the bragging rewards late. Korok seeds stop being useful at 441 of 900. Red Dead asks for part of each set.
- Small sets of authored things beat large sets of identical things. 100 substories and 55 backpacks are loved; 420 flags and thousands of figments are not.

### Helping players find the last few

- A count per area, so the player knows where to look.
- A map bought or found in the world (Arkham's secrets maps).
- A character who tells you, at a price (Arkham's informants).
- A sense that points at the nearest one (Ghost of Tsushima's wind; the Korok detector).
- Nothing missable. If a set can be locked out for good, players resent the set.
- A list that names what is missing. Psychonauts 2 shows a number and no more, and that drew the complaints.

## 5. Options for the author

Each option is a pattern from section 3, with what choosing it would mean. Nothing here is a recommendation of content. Every option needs an author ruling.

### Brewery Tour (stamps, openers, selfies, beers)

- **Leave it.** The ladder is complete. The only open items are the ones already tracked: the Hangover interactions and units.
- **Give the openers their own payoff, or rule that they have none.** At present the opener is a second trophy for the same act. Patterns that could fit: cosmetic only (the keychain's look and jingle, already proposed); a joke reward; or a use for the full ring. Ruling "none, the ring is the reward" is also an answer, and matches Arkham's figurines.
- **Stagger the two rewards at 16, or keep them together.** Continental Beer Master and Photogenic Drinker land at the same moment. Other games separate a power reward from a bragging reward so each gets its own beat.
- **A person at the end.** Arkham's lesson is that 100% lands best as a scene with a character. Whether the tour's end has a face (a brewmaster, a ceremony, anyone) is a story question for the author alone.
- **Help with the last few.** The passport shows empty and locked slots. Whether anything in the world points at an unfound brewery (the Arkham informant or secrets-map pattern) is undecided. With 16 named venues it may not be needed.

### NFTs

- **The 12-NFT reward.** The neighbours are a cosmetic at 4, a small stat trinket at 8 and a scene at 16. The kinds of reward not yet used on this ladder are lore, joke or anti-reward, access, and a finding aid.
- **A per-city beat, or none.** `fratbronft.md:95` promises city completion rewards but defines none. The milestones are by total count (4 / 8 / 12 / 16), which is not the same as finishing a city. The choice is count only (as now), per city, or both.
- **The card as the reward.** As with Arkham's tapes and Spider-Man's backpacks, the per-item payoff is the copy on the card, which is reserved for the author.

### Couchsurfing Chronicles

- **Which title is canon.** "Slept Around, Found Out" (master file) or "Cultural Ambassador" (city files), or one of each for different thresholds. This is a contradiction to settle, not a design gap.
- **City milestones, or none.** There are 5 or 6 hosts per city and no per-city reward. Per-area milestones are the commonest pattern in the games above. Leaving it flat is also defensible, since each host already pays out.
- **Correct the count line.** "1-2 opportunities… per city" no longer matches the city files.

### Restroom graffiti and Vinyl Hunt

- **Graffiti: a tracker.** Nine items, three titles with conditions, and no described way to see which are done. The options are a list in a menu, a physical object in the bros' inventory (as the passport is for breweries), or nothing.
- **Vinyl Hunt: count and ladder.** It needs a number per city, a tracker, and a decision on whether the remix is the only reward or the last of several. Every one of those is new content.

### Junk and one-off sets

- **Keep junk outside any set.** The ruling makes each inspect line the reward. Counting junk toward a total would turn a surprise into a checklist, which is how figments became a chore. A "junk seen" count would be the Pokédex pattern; it would need a ruling and may work against the joke.
- **Arcade prizes.** Whether winning all 13 earns anything is open. The patterns are the same seven kinds as in section 3.

### One question across all sets

Arkham puts every set under one total and one collector. Eurobound's sets each have their own book: passport, keychain, wallet, Crash Gallery. Whether there should be one place that shows all of them (the Yakuza completion-list pattern) or whether they stay separate objects is a design question with a cost either way. One list is clearer. Separate objects are funnier, and nothing in canon asks for a master list.
