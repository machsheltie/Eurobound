## 3. Options

All three keep the ruled order (Publandia → Debaucheryville → Sinfonia → Shamsterdam), keep visited cities open (`NEXT_WORK.md:27`), keep the plane for arriving on and leaving the continent (`opening_step1.md`, `NEXT_WORK.md:27`), and charge in SOVS. Any number or place marked **[author]** stays blank until ruled.

### Option A: The train, with the departure board (recommended)

The train is the transport the docs name most often between cities, and the approved L2 load screen already shows its board with all four cities.

1. Each city has a train station the bros walk to. **[author: where it is in each city and what it is called; none is named yet.]**
2. At the station the board lists the cities. The next city in the ruled order opens when that city's story allows it **[author: which story beat opens it]**; cities already visited are always on the board.
3. A ticket is paid in SOVS **[author: the price, and whether it differs by leg]**. No SOVS, no ticket.
4. The trip itself is a short transition, the way the Shamsterdam countryside train already is (`Wobbly_Windmill_Brewery_Complete_PNG_Asset_Requirements.md:136`), with room for party banter on board (as at `nothingisgettingthrough.md:439`). Whether the trip costs game-clock time **[author]**.
5. The home flight from Shamsterdam stays a story scene (`NEXT_WORK.md:27`); the customs checkpoint stays "if implemented" for the author.

Fits: `economy_system.md:25`, the L2 board, the Nothing Is Getting Through train line, the Retelling's "train out".

### Option B: Story crossings forward, the train for going back

The first trip into each new city is a scripted story scene that the author writes beat by beat (as the opening flight and the Beat 5 limp already are). Going back to a city already visited uses the train from Option A.

1. Leaving for the next city: a story scene, no ticket, no menu. What the scene is (train, limp, or something else) is the author's for each leg **[author]**.
2. Returning to a visited city: the station and SOVS ticket as Option A, points 1-4.

Fits: story order belongs to the author (`.claude/CLAUDE.md`, "The story belongs to the author alone"); Nothing Is Getting Through already scripts the Debaucheryville → Sinfonia crossing as Beats 4-5, which a free ticket menu could contradict. Cost: one authored scene per forward leg (three).

### Option C: The cartoon map

Travel uses the opening's own device: "the plane's shadow crossing a cartoon map. A dotted line. A little plane icon. The map is labeled EUROMEMEIA" (`opening_step1.md:321`), with the train in place of the plane between cities (or the plane, **[author]**).

1. The bros use a departure point in each city **[author: where]**, pay in SOVS **[author: price]**, and the screen shows the EUROMEMEIA map with a dotted line from city to city.
2. The map shows only the four cities, in the ruled order; visited ones can be picked again.
3. The map art already exists as an idea in an approved scene, so the same asset serves the opening and every trip.

Fits: one consistent travel picture from the first minute of the game. It needs a map art asset and a ruling on the vehicle icon; it shows less of the bros on the trip than A.

## 4. Not proposed

- **Ferry legs.** The ferry is named only as a ticket type (`economy_system.md:25`). No route, port or boat exists, so no option uses one. If the author wants a ferry leg, it is a new place and needs a ruling.
- **Plane hops between cities.** No airport is named in Debaucheryville, Sinfonia or Shamsterdam, and the L2 board shows trains; the docs use the plane only for arriving and leaving the continent.
- **Free walking between cities.** The "limp" is a scripted quest beat, not a map; an overworld between cities would be new places.
