# Cyberspace Kavarna Location Package Design

## Intent and Identity

Define Cyberspace Kavarna as the Hostel Row basement internet den, ICQ quest hub, terminal-rental business, and gateway into Debaucheryville's digital underground.

- Location ID: `debaucheryville_hostelrow_cyberspace_kavarna_01`
- Slug: `cyberspace_kavarna`
- NPC folder: `Cyberspace_Kavarna`
- Primary source: `cyberspacekarvana.md`

## Visual and Spatial Design

A narrow stair below Hostel Row carries flickering `INTERNET / KAVARNA` neon and blunt warnings into a maze of CRTs, mismatched Soviet-era desks, duct-taped Ethernet, ashtrays, harsh fluorescent light, and sickly monitor glow. The main landmarks are stair/entry desk, rental terminal rows, Dmitri's control position, CyberGoth Katka's node, ICQ quest terminals, ventilation/smoke storytelling, restroom clue point, and return stair.

The maze should feel cluttered without obscuring the primary route. Broken-screen, occupied, hacked, and degraded equipment states need modular mapping. Peak scammer hours from 03:00-06:00 alter patrons and offers rather than changing the floor plan.

## Gameplay, Quests, and State

The café is always accessible and charges for terminal time. It hosts ICQ coordination, sting operations, questionable digital services, technology-degradation hazards, timed pricing, and cross-location data handoffs. `icqscammersting.md` owns its quest dialogue and outcome state. Dmitri, Katka, and rotating patrons retain documented roles and schedules.

As an approved normalization decision inferred from both briefs, Backroom Concessions is treated as a separately packaged child room reached through the café's clue and concealed transition. Cyberspace owns the front-side graffiti/clue and sets `cyberspace_backroom_concessions_entrance_discovered`; Backroom Concessions owns everything beyond its doorway.

## Package Directives

Interactions need readable terminal selection, rental/payment, login, quest-terminal, service, clue, and failure flows. Items separate rental/service purchases from carried hardware or quest data. Environment supplies smoke and CRT effects with clean-air and reduced-flicker accessibility variants. PNG and mapping cover the stair façade, desk maze modules, CRT states, cables, counter, characters, patrons, UI screens, clue art, and the Backroom transition exterior.

## Validation

- The café and Backroom use distinct location IDs and interior ownership.
- Terminal rates, hours, peak events, and failure probabilities agree across files.
- ICQ dialogue remains quest-owned.
- Reduced-flicker and smoke-accessibility states exist.
- Nodes remain navigable despite the intentionally tangled visual language.
