# Meme Black Market Location Package Design

## Intent and Identity

Define Meme Black Market as the Internet History Museum's hidden 03:33 vendor, sidequest, and meme-economy room.

- Location ID: `debaucheryville_museum_meme_black_market_01`
- Slug: `meme_black_market`
- NPC folder: `Meme_Black_Market`
- Primary source: `memeblackmarket.md`

## Visual and Spatial Design

The museum transition opens through a secret VHS shelf into an RGB-lit den crowded with retro computers, corrupted GIF displays, Funcoland bags, AIM away-message icons, ASCII-cat guardians, and a Windows 98 crash altar. The compact graph is shelf threshold, MemeLord.exe counter, summon point, trinket display, mastery/lore terminal, atmospheric altar, and return shelf.

The room must feel illicit and improvised, not like a second full museum. Screen-heavy visuals use painterly environment layers with selective pixel content inside displays. Reduced-flash and static-free states remain visually legible.

## Gameplay, Quests, and State

Entry requires `meme_black_market_graffiti_clue_read`, the museum's quest-unlatched after-hours route, the 03:33 GeoCities interaction, and the documented MSN password. The destination consumes `meme_black_market_entrance_discovered` and `meme_black_market_entrance_unlocked`. The room owns meme summons, meme trinket sales or rewards, MemeMastery progression, vendor interaction, ICQ darkweb linkage, and relevant Fistø/Lord Pilsner lore bindings.

The museum owns access-puzzle staging; this package consumes its unlocked transition flag and owns the return state. MemeLord.exe dialogue is referenced if present elsewhere.

## Package Directives

Interactions separate summon, purchase, equip/use, mastery inspection, lore, and exit. Items define each trinket's actual mechanical effect and risk without treating pure references as inventory. NPCs center MemeLord.exe and only documented ambient entities. PNG and mapping reconcile older market asset documents into room layers, screens, shelf interior, vendor, summon effects, trinket icons, UI, and accessibility states.

## Validation

- All four entry prerequisites use the same flags as the museum.
- The password is stored once and referenced safely by both packages.
- MemeMastery changes have deterministic thresholds.
- Pixel visuals remain screen content, not a conflicting whole-scene pipeline.
- The only normal exit returns through the museum shelf.
