# The Bohemian Trap Location Package Design

## Intent and Identity

Define The Bohemian Trap as a mobile-looking but node-stable absinthe street cart in the neon quarter.

- Location ID: `debaucheryville_vendor_bohemian_trap_01`
- Slug: `bohemian_trap`
- NPC folder: `Bohemian_Trap`
- Primary source: `the_bohemian_trap.md`

## Visual and Spatial Design

An artificially aged wooden cart performs Belle Époque Paris through deep green paint, gold trim, black iron, Art Nouveau Green Fairy labels, backlit bottles, sugar cubes, spoons, and theatrical ritual equipment. Its nightly position shifts within documented neon-quarter anchor points, but it is not a room inside Absinthe Arcade.

As an open-air vendor, its interior graph is the service footprint: approach, bottle display, ritual station, performer node, customer reaction space, and district exit. Cart relocation changes placement metadata and backdrop, not location identity.

## Gameplay, Quests, and State

The cart operates 18:00-04:00. It sells tiered absinthe products and a purchasable ritual, applies documented intoxication/confusion and control-reversal effects, supports Bravado responses, and tracks the `Was That Worth It?` consequence. The Absinthe Arcade network is thematic/economic; purchases may affect later interactions without making the cart part of the Arcade package.

## Package Directives

Items are bottles, servings, ritual purchases, and resulting status references with authoritative prices. Interactions separate browse, buy, perform ritual, consume, recover, and examine. NPCs center the performer and documented customers. Environment includes green emission, flame or drip effects, street ambience, and safe non-flashing alternatives. PNG and mapping cover cart faces, awning/signs, displays, ritual props/states, vendor animation, product icons, control-reversal UI, and backdrop-compatible shadows.

## Validation

- Nightly relocation uses approved anchor nodes only.
- Every absinthe tier has one price/effect definition.
- Control reversal has duration, accessibility notice, and recovery state.
- The cart remains a standalone exterior location.
- The Arcade is referenced without duplicating its assets or interactions.
