**The pattern the three share:**
- **P1. Buff band:** +15 to +20 all stats, for one whole battle.
- **P2. Penalty ratio:** the penalty is two-thirds to three-quarters of the buff (10/15, 15/20, 12/18).
- **P3. Status length:** about 3 minutes per point of penalty (30 for −10, 45 for −15, 40 for −12).
- **P4. Price:** follows the status length, at about 1.5-1.7 SOVS per minute (50/30, 75/45, 60/40).
- **P5. Theme:** one city-themed battle special (none for Publandia). The status adds city-themed extras: one that costs control (Neon Nightmare's input reversal, Imperial's automatic bows), one sensory cue, and one social gag.
- **P6. Cure:** a local food or drink, sleep, or waiting it out.
- **P7. Stacking:** the after-battle status never stacks; one instance at a time (`brewerytourpublandia.md:326-331`).

Shamsterdam is the final city (`NEXT_WORK.md:27`: "the crescendo before the airport").

## 3. Units and scale: issues shared with the other three cities (not resolved here)

The three ruled hangovers predate two later approvals, so Canal Courage inherits both issues. Bead **Eurobound-picf** (discovered from c24) puts them to the author. Canal Courage moves with whatever is ruled there and keeps its place at the top of the band.

1. **Minutes vs turns and steps.** The approved status model counts battle turns and overworld steps (`2026-11-12-status-effect-data-shape-proposal.md:250-253`). Under the approved anchors (Embarrassed: 60 game-minutes ↔ 2 turns, `NEXT_WORK.md:783`; 40 steps a turn), 45 minutes is 1.5 turns or 60 steps, about 19 seconds of walking. Read as game-clock minutes (screen change 10, fight 20, `NEXT_WORK.md:685`), it is about one fight and two screens.
2. **"+N all stats" vs the battle scale.** The approved party stats are single and low double digits (Pilsner: Offense 8, Defense 6, Speed 5, Grit 3; `design/mechanics/Battle/battle_numbers_proposal.md:36`). +20 more than triples Pilsner's Offense (8 → 28) and quintuples his Speed (5 → 25), and −15 drives several stats below zero.

## 5. Intoxication-law 10-point review

1. **Source:** the Canal Courage consumable. It unlocks with all 4 Shamsterdam stamps and can be bought again for 75 SOVS at any stamped Shamsterdam location. `alcohol: true`.
2. **Immediate effect:** +20 all stats and −25% Bravado loss for the entire battle.
3. **Escalation:** It counts as 1 drink on the alcohol ladder. A second one does not stack the Hungover (P7). Drinking it ends Sober.
4. **Player-facing cues:** the Hungover status row on the Status card with an explicit "−15 all stats" readout. The Tunnel Confusion question marks show while Canal Confusion runs. In reduced-motion mode the question marks are static, and the readout always stands alone.
5. **Player control:** Bounded. Wrong tunnel exits come more often, but the 10% floor means the right exit is never impossible. No dialogue or movement takeover is added.
6. **Duration and recovery:** 45 minutes (unit per Eurobound-picf). Herring, sleep or waiting it out ends it. It is saved in `FieldStatus`.
7. **Comedic consequence:** He fights like a local, then can't find his way out of a tunnel built to filter out tourists, and comes up in Roodelicht Row.
8. **Status interactions:** See §4. Sober is lost, Canal Confusion adds to Tunnel Confusion and Grist, Postponed Hangover delays it, and the stacking rule matches the other three.
9. **Technical dependencies (proposed engineering, not built):** a battle-scoped "+N all stats" buff and a Bravado-loss multiplier in `BattleCore`; a post-battle status hook (shared with the other three hangovers); the Navigation roll and Canal Confusion (Eurobound-76gz and its engine bead); the Sober flag (eb8).
10. **Further author approval:** Everything in §2 and §4, the status name, the social-gag slot, and the flavor text. The unit and scale issues are in Eurobound-picf.
