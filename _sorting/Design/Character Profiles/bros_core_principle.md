# CORE PRINCIPLE: Who the Bros Actually Are

**Status:** Canon — author ruling (2026-08-01). **Binding on all content authoring in every city.**
**Read this before writing any scene, location, quest, or NPC that the bros react to.**

---

## 🚨 THE PRINCIPLE

> **"The bros shouldn't be horrified. The subversiveness is WHY they came to Europe. They knew what they were getting into. They chose the cities they're in for these reasons. They're no innocent little lambs."**
> — Author, 2026-08-01

> **"The bros fucking went to Europe seeking out debauchery. They went there FOR the sex, drugs, drinking, partying, whores, sex clubs, etc. They aren't innocent tourists. They aren't afraid. They're there specifically to act out on all fronts as much as possible and these cities are known to give that in spades."**
> — Author, 2026-08-01

Bradley, Chadwick and Lord Pilsner are **not** naive tourists who stumble into vice and recoil. They are grown men who booked this trip **on purpose**, picked these specific cities **for exactly what those cities are known for**, and arrived with a list.

**The itinerary is: sex, drugs, drinking, partying, sex clubs, sex workers — the whole menu, on every front, as much as possible.** That is why they came. It is not implied, hinted at, or coyly gestured toward. It is what they are doing.

They are entitled, enthusiastic, unembarrassed, unrestrained, and completely unshockable. **And the cities deliver in spades** — nothing is withheld, rationed, or disappointing.

---

## What They Are NOT

Do not write them as any of these. Every one is a wrong note:

| ❌ Wrong | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Horrified / scandalised by adult content | They came for it. Being shocked makes no sense and makes them sympathetic. |
| Frightened, flinching, cowering | Fear makes them victims. They are never victims. |
| Innocent, naive, or corrupted-by-Europe | Nobody corrupted them. They arrived pre-corrupted and delighted. |
| Ashamed afterwards | They brag. Loudly. Inaccurately. Forever. |
| Learning a lesson | They never learn anything. Ever. |

---

## What They ARE Deluded About

The delusion is **about themselves, never about what Europe is.** They know exactly what they came for. What they are wrong about:

- **That they are worldly.** They are not. They are a demographic.
- **That they are transgressive rebels.** They are line item #4,000 this month, with a predictable average spend.
- **That they are unique.** Every person serving them has met ten thousand identical men doing the identical confident routine.
- **That they are sophisticated.** They think a museum visit and a foreign phrase make them cultured.
- **That the locals find them interesting.** The locals find them tedious, and are professionally polite about it.

**This is the real buffoon delusion** and it refines the framework in `_sorting/Templates/location_validation_template.md`: what the bros THINK is happening is that they are having a wild, dangerous, singular experience. What is ACTUALLY happening is a well-run business processing a routine customer.

## The Comedic Engine

> **⚠️ THE GAME IS NOT SAFE, NOT BORING, AND NOT CORPORATE. IT IS SUBVERSIVE.**
> **The bros get exactly what they came for — and it has ridiculous results.**
> — Author ruling, 2026-08-01

**They came for debauchery. They GET debauchery. Their forty-year-old bodies cannot survive it.**

This is an adult-only MA game and it delivers on that. The comedy is **not** anticlimax. Do not write scenes whose punchline is "it turned out to be administrative," "the wild thing was actually boring," or "they were disappointed." That is the opposite of this game.

The structure is **escalation**:
1. The bros want something outrageous.
2. They get it — immediately, fully, and more than they asked for.
3. It compounds into something more ridiculous.
4. The consequences land on them, absurdly and physically.
5. They learn nothing and brag about it forever.

**The emotional beat is delighted, in-over-their-heads chaos — never fear, never deflation.**

### Satirizing the industry — the RIGHT way
Vice industries in this game are **hyper-competent at excess**, not dull. The joke is that they are gleefully, professionally, absurdly good at delivering more than anyone asked for and charging for all of it. Bureaucratic details (a loyalty stamp, an upsell, a laminated warning nobody reads) work as **garnish on genuine mayhem** — never as the main event.

## 🚨 WHAT ACTUALLY BREAKS THEM: THEIR BODIES AND THE WOMEN

> **"What breaks them is their aging bodies and the women. Because they think they're still as good as college age men. They go and try to live that life at nearly 40 and find their stomachs can't handle the constant eating of greasy fatty foods, their bodies can't keep up with the all night drugs, drinking and partying and their bodies can't hold up to the onslaught of STDs. They think they can still pull beautiful younger women and instead often get turned down for younger hotter models."**
> — Author, 2026-08-01

**They are nearly 40 and trying to live a 20-year-old's life. That gap is the engine of the entire game.**

There is no terror. Nothing shocks them, frightens them, or shames them. The cities never withhold. **The only thing that ever defeats them is themselves.**

### Engine 1 — THE BODY
Their appetite is exactly what it was in college. Their bodies are not.

| It fails at | Because |
|---|---|
| **The food** | Constant greasy, fatty, late-night eating — döner, kapsalon, kebab, chips, fried everything. It goes in enthusiastically and comes out catastrophically. |
| **The stamina** | All-night drugs, drinking and partying that used to be routine now flattens them. They cannot recover. They keep booking a 21-year-old's schedule in a 39-year-old's body. |
| **The STDs** | Their bodies cannot hold up to the onslaught. Established canon — lean in. Consequences cartoonish and comedic, never grim. |

### Engine 2 — THE WOMEN
They are certain they can still pull beautiful younger women. They cannot.

- They get **turned down**, constantly — and frequently **for younger, hotter men, in front of them.**
- They never conclude they are too old. It was the timing, the lighting, she was intimidated, she had a boyfriend, Chadwick ruined it.
- This is the sharpest recurring humiliation in the game and it never stops being funny.

### The rule
Every escalation should tax a body that cannot take it, or land a rejection they instantly rationalise. **The world says yes to everything. Their own bodies are the only thing that says no.** None of them ever once identifies age as the cause.

## The Retelling Rule

They did it, they are not ashamed, and **they inflate it**. Each bro's version escalates independently over the rest of the game until the three accounts are mutually incompatible. Nobody ever fact-checks them. Nobody ever admits doubt.

---

## Hard Rules for Writers

1. **Never make them sympathetic through fear.** Sympathy is earned elsewhere (see Jiří, Gustav) and never from them being scared of adults doing legal work.
2. **Never let them achieve self-awareness.** They never realise they were a transaction.
3. **Everyone is ridiculous — there is no straight man.** Vendors, workers, operators and locals are as absurd as the bros: outsized personalities, insane specialisms, total confidence. Do NOT write any group as the sober realistic anchor while everyone else is a joke — that is its own condescension. Nobody is a victim because nothing here is played straight enough for victimhood to exist.
4. **Alcohol makes them louder, more confident, and more expensive — never braver or more frightened.**
5. **They are affectionately drawn.** Players root for them the way you root for Galavant: incompetent, vain, and somehow charming. Contemptible, not hateable.
