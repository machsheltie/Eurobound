# NPC Profile: Miss Morphine

*"ur digital footprint is fascinating. mind if i study it?"*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_miss_morphine_01`
- **Display Name**: Miss Morphine
- **Internal Name**: Miss Morphine — the only name attached to the handle, the note, and the USB. No other name has ever been established anywhere in the game, and none ever will be
- **Role**: Darkweb ICQ Contact / Couchsurf Trigger / Mystery Item Source / Cross-City Riddle Dispenser
- **Associated Quest**: Couchsurfing Chronicles — Debaucheryville entry #5; Darkweb Dossier crossover events; `debaucheryville_sidequest_nothing_is_getting_through_01` (rejection-system participant); an optional Sinfonia chain hooked to the USB (pointer only — not specced in this file)
- **Primary Location**: The ICQ client. That is the location. She has no map node, no walk cycle, and no pathing
- **Secondary Appearances**: The Cyberspace Kavarna's "Miss Morphine's Digital Trail" investigation board (as a subject, never as a presence); a neon-lit room, once, which nobody has ever been able to find again

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing

An ICQ handle sends the party cryptic riddles wrapped in customer-service phrasing, decorated with excessive ASCII art, always implying it knows more than it is saying. It offers "premium experiences." One of those offers — `💊Wanna vibe IRL?💊` — resolves into a couchsurf scene, after which the party wakes in a neon-lit room full of Y2K tech, VHS tapes, and expired party pills, with a typed note and a USB stick. The account keeps messaging afterward. The Cyberspace Kavarna maintains an open file on the question of who she is; per that location's own canon, the answer is "it's complicated."

**Nothing in this game — this profile, the questline, the item, the ICQ logs, an examine string, a placard, a footnote, or a post-credits stinger — ever establishes whether Miss Morphine is a person, several people, an automated system, or something else.** That is not a gap to be filled later. It is the character.

### What Bros Believe

That they had, in Chadwick's canonical phrasing, a cyberpunk experience. That this was next-level European culture. That Pilsner is a pioneer of digital-age dating protocols FlagMan never covered. That "Had authentic Y2K experience with mysterious European" is Tinder gold. They do not find the question interesting, because to them there is no question — they were obviously incredible, and she was obviously overwhelmed, and that is the end of the analysis.

### The Comedy Gap

The single most unsettling thing that happens to these men on the entire trip is also the thing they are least curious about. An anonymous account studied them, filed them, left them a receipt calling them beta testers, and vanished — and their takeaway is a bio line. The player is left holding the entire mystery alone while three grown men rate it five stars and move on. The satire targets frat-bro incuriosity and Y2K-era internet trust, never the woman on the other end of the handle, if there is one.

### Clothing

Not applicable — no rendered body exists to dress. The visual grammar of the character lives entirely in her interface presence:

- **The profile picture (canonical)**: An anime girl in sunglasses with a question mark overlaid on top. Low-res, over-compressed, and never updated. The question mark is not subtle and is not meant to be
- **The ASCII**: Every message is over-decorated with ASCII art, occasionally so wide it breaks the client's message box. This is her only reliable stylistic fingerprint
- **The window chrome**: Her ICQ window opens fractionally larger than any other contact's and always in the same screen position, regardless of where the player last dragged it
- **The presence dot**: Green. Always green. Including during the Cyberspace Kavarna's posted service-maintenance windows, when the client is provably offline for everyone else. This is documented, never explained, and never brought up by any NPC

### Distinguishing Features
- Replies arrive faster than a human could type and slower than a script would fire, and the distribution of those delays has been mapped by exactly one NPC at the Kavarna, who reached no conclusion
- Never uses the party's names. Uses "u," "ur," and — occasionally, chillingly — the party's ICQ numbers
- Every message contains at least one piece of information she should not have
- Signs off, when she signs off at all, as **"- M"**

### Sprite Notes

**Miss Morphine has no character sprite and must never be given one.** Not a silhouette in a doorway, not a shape behind frosted glass, not a shadow across a wall, not an off-screen footstep with a body implied, not a cameo in a crowd. Artists asking for a Miss Morphine sprite should be pointed at this line. Her entire art footprint is: the profile-picture icon, the ICQ window, the ASCII, and the room she was already gone from. If a future artist wants to "just do a concept," the answer is no — one leaked concept resolves the character permanently and there is no undo.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype

**The Open File** — a presence assembled entirely from message logs, whose personality can be described in detail and whose existence cannot be described at all. Everything below is inferred from text, and the profile is deliberately written so that every trait reads identically whether it belongs to a person, a team, or a very good script.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|--------------|
| Cryptic-Plus-Corporate | Riddles delivered in customer-service register: "thank you for your interest in this experience" |
| Implies More Than She Says | Every message hints at a larger apparatus behind it, and never confirms one |
| ASCII-Maximalist | The art is bigger than the text roughly half the time; occasionally it is the entire message |
| Studies, Does Not Judge | She logs behavior with genuine interest and no visible opinion about it, which is far worse than contempt |
| Absolutely Consistent | The voice never slips, never gets tired, never has an off day. Draw whatever conclusion you like; the game will not confirm it |

### Internal Monologue

*Deliberately withheld.* This section is the one place in the NPC template where a writer would ordinarily resolve exactly the question this character must never resolve — an interior voice would prove there is an interior. In place of a monologue, the file offers the last thing she sent before going quiet, which reveals precisely as much as it is ever going to:

> "ur digital footprint is fascinating. mind if i study it?"

### Motivation

Stated motive, from the note left behind: **"Thanks for beta testing my social experiment. - M"** — that is the entirety of the on-record explanation, and it is one sentence long, unsigned beyond an initial, and typed. Whether the experiment is social research, a long grift, a data harvest, a bored person's hobby, or an artifact of the Kavarna's own unresolved file is not established. What is established is the pattern: contact is made, the party is observed, a receipt is left, and the contact string never varies by so much as a character — the same eleven-symbol pop-up, every single time, which is either a person with a very fixed habit or something that was never typed by a person at all. The file does not say. The file will never say.

*Neon-lit room. Y2K tech stacked to the ceiling. VHS tapes. Expired party pills. She is gone. On the table: a note, typed except for the sign-off, and a USB stick.*

> **"Thanks for beta testing my social experiment. - M"**

*No bro can afterwards agree on whether anyone else was in the room. Chadwick is certain. Bradley is certain of a different thing. Pilsner did not look up. The room cannot be found again on any subsequent night — the door is there, the building is there, the room is not. Nobody in the party finds this worth discussing.*

*(If a later encounter occurs — see Post-Cure Callback — it takes place in **a** neon-lit room full of Y2K tech, VHS tapes, and expired party pills. Whether it is **the** room is not established, is not investigable, and is never answered.)*

```
💬 Miss Morphine: "thank u for participating. results pending. results are always pending."
```

*Declining the `💊Wanna vibe IRL?💊` pop-up produces no response at all. The window closes itself after four seconds. She messages again two in-game nights later with identical text, as though the first message had not been sent — or as though the decline was itself the data point. ## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent

**She has no spoken lines and should be given none.** All of her dialogue is rendered text in an ICQ window and must stay that way — a voice is a body, and a body is an answer.

### Tone
- Written register: lowercase, clipped, customer-service politeness bolted onto riddles. "thank u for continuing to beta test" is the exact tone target
- ASCII: over-decorated to the point of intrusion; the art is part of the register, not a garnish
- Never menacing, never flirtatious, never warm. Neutral interest is the whole performance, and neutral interest is what makes it land

### Key Delivery Notes
- **If a VO pass is ever attempted over player objections, the read must be constructed so that it cannot be identified as human or synthetic** — layered, processed, and ambiguous in both directions. The safe answer is still no VO
- Text-reveal timing carries the performance: the ASCII draws in row by row, the reply body appears instantly. Do not smooth this out
- The auto-replies are not "cold" — they are *administrative*. Direct any UI sound design accordingly: a notification chime, not a sting
- No sound effect, no music cue, and no camera move may ever imply a presence in the room during her scene
