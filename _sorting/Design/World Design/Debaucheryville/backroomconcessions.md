## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Digital underground food culture where hacker aesthetics meet questionable nutrition. The kind of place where you consume mystery meat while consuming conspiracy content, all while breathing the ozone of overworked mining rigs.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Gamer food culture and digital underground pretensions commercialized into sketchy concession operation
- **Secondary Targets**: Cryptocurrency culture, hacker aesthetic commodification, internet café food safety standards
- **Cultural Specificity**: Eastern European internet café culture mixed with American gamer fuel stereotypes
- **Humor Approach**: Digital underground meets health code violations with cryptocurrency seasoning
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets pretentious digital culture and questionable food practices, not legitimate internet café businesses

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Serving questionable nutrition disguised as authentic digital underground experience
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Back-room food operation exploiting gamer culture stereotypes for tourist money
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros think they're experiencing authentic hacker culture while consuming obvious health violations
- **Comedy of Errors**: Digital underground aesthetic masking basic food safety incompetence
- **Consequence System**: Food items provide beneficial stats with hilarious negative side effects

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Accessing authentic digital underground culture through alternative nutrition and hacker aesthetics

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Paying premium prices for questionable food in sketchy back-room operation with borrowed cyberpunk aesthetic

**How impaired are they**: Hungry and curious enough to follow bathroom graffiti to hidden food vendors

**Obvious signs they ignore**: Health code violations, mystery meat origins, thermal paste vending machine, pop-up ad infected computers as dining atmosphere

**How locals play along**: Donny.exe treats obvious food safety violations as authentic underground culture while maximizing profit from digital tourism
