# NPC Profile: CryptoBro69's "Ex"

*"You're better looking than him, but dumber."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_cryptobro69s_ex_01`
- **Display Name**: CryptoBro69's "Ex"
- **Internal Name**: The "Ex" — the quotation marks are load-bearing and permanent. He calls her his ex. She calls him "a guy I blocked." Both statements are on the record; only one of them is hers
- **Role**: Couchsurf Host / Ambient Antagonist-of-the-Counter / Item Source
- **Associated Quest**: Couchsurfing Chronicles — Debaucheryville entry #3; `debaucheryville_sidequest_nothing_is_getting_through_01` (rejection-system participant); alternate acquisition route for NFT #2
- **Primary Location**: Front of the main counter, The Shadow Exchange, Debaucheryville
- **Secondary Appearances**: Her flat (couchsurf interior — futon under buzzing CRTs and LED strips); she does not relocate anywhere else, ever

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing

She is at the Shadow Exchange to argue about slippage fees, and she is winning. She holds an account she is trying to unwind, a burner phone full of messages she is deliberately not answering, and a completely clear-eyed read on everyone in the building including herself. She is not heartbroken. She was never his girlfriend. She was, briefly, adjacent to a man who introduced her to people as his girlfriend, and she has spent the intervening months methodically extracting herself from the paperwork that created. Taking a tourist home is not therapy and is not a rebound arc — it is a Tuesday, and she says so out loud.

### What Bros Believe

That they were selected as the rebound. That "you're better looking than him, but dumber" was a compliment with a small caveat attached rather than a complete and accurate two-part assessment. Pilsner believes they demonstrated what a real man looks like. Chadwick believes they are now crypto influencers. Bradley is drafting "helped someone heal from a toxic crypto relationship." She healed from nothing, because nothing happened to her.

### The Comedy Gap

She told him the truth, to his face, in eight words, and he wrote it down as a win. Everything about the encounter is documented — the scratched NFT with its "MINTED: 03:17 AM — GAS FEE: 'Don't Worry About It.'" metadata, the missing cab voucher, the twenty-five gold — and the bros' account of it is a completely different story from the one the evidence tells. The comedy target is the frat-bro rebound fantasy and the crypto-adjacent men who generate ex-girlfriends they never had. It is never her.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype

**The Unbothered Auditor** — she catalogues people accurately and out loud, considers this a neutral act, and has never once softened a finding for anyone's comfort. She is not bitter and she is not wounded; those readings would both require her to have cared more than she did. What she has is receipts, an excellent memory, and the specific patience of a person who intends to see a dispute through to the end.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|--------------|
| Brutally Precise | "Better looking than him, but dumber" is not a barb — it is a complete assessment delivered at the correct length |
| Selectively Unreachable | The phone buzzes all night. She answers exactly none of it and does not explain the policy |
| Transactional Without Malice | She took the cab voucher because she needed a cab. There is no revenge in it. That is somehow worse |
| Genuinely Amused | She finds the bros funny. Actually funny. She is not performing amusement for anyone |
| Done Explaining | She has corrected the "ex" framing enough times that she now just lets it stand and moves on |

### Internal Monologue

> "He's going to tell his friends about this. He's going to tell them a version where he did something. And in fairness — he did carry the bag up four flights. That's the sentence I'd write if I were being generous, and I have never once been asked to be."

### Motivation

She wants out — of the account, of the paperwork, of the group chat where a man she barely dated keeps referring to her in the possessive. Everything she does at the Shadow Exchange is aimed at closing a position she should never have been in. The couchsurf is not part of that plan. It is a night off from the plan, and she treats it exactly that lightly.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent

Local Debaucheryville, fluent and impatient English, no exaggeration. She code-switches into the counter argument at a faster clip than she uses with the bros — the bros do not merit the fast version.

### Tone
- Counter argument: fast, precise, genuinely engaged; she is enjoying this
- Assessments of the bros: flat, brief, and completely without cruelty in the delivery — the cruelty is entirely in the accuracy
- Herp rejection laugh: real, from the chest, not a sneer; she is not mocking a stranger, she is reacting to something that is objectively funny
- Post-cure callback: the only time in her whole track where she sounds impressed, and it is about an object

### Key Delivery Notes
- "You're better looking than him, but dumber" must be one sentence, no pause before "but" — pausing turns an assessment into an insult and loses the joke
- "He's a guy I blocked. Next." is verbatim and identical every time; never reinflect it
- Direct the actor away from any "bitter ex" read. She is not hurt, she is busy
- She never raises her voice at a bro. She raises it exclusively at the rate board
