*"Secret show at midnight. Very few know. You seem... worthy."*

- **Display Name**: Mysterious Local
- **Internal Name**: Ondřej "Midnight" Mráz
- **Role**: Time-Waster / Path B Enabler
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_astronomical_cockup_01` (Path B only)
- **Primary Location**: Astronomical Cock-Up Square, Debaucheryville (Night Only)
- **Secondary Appearances**: Same location only

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Convincing gullible tourists to wait for a "secret midnight show" that doesn't exist. When midnight passes and nothing happens, he claims they "weren't spiritually ready" and the REAL show is at 3 AM. He does this purely for entertainment - he doesn't even charge money.

### What Bros Believe
A mysterious insider is sharing forbidden local knowledge about a secret astronomical clock show that only the truly cultured can witness. This is exactly the kind of authentic experience they came to Europe for.

### The Comedy Gap
There is no secret show. There has never been a secret show. Ondřej has been doing this for years purely because he finds it funny. The bros wait until 3 AM, see nothing, and conclude they still "weren't spiritually ready."

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Theatrical Troll** - Ondřej doesn't want money. He wants entertainment. Watching tourists wait for hours for something that will never happen brings him genuine joy. He's committed to the bit with the dedication of a performance artist.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Theatrical | Every word is delivered for maximum mystery |
| Patient | Will spend hours maintaining the illusion |
| Amused | Secretly delighted by tourist gullibility |
| Committed | Never breaks character, never admits truth |
| Philosophical | Has developed genuine theories about "tourist faith" |

### Internal Monologue
> "They believe it. They always believe it. The desperate ones, the ones who need to feel special - they'll wait all night. And when nothing happens? They blame themselves. Beautiful. Art, really."

### Motivation
Pure entertainment. He's retired, has insomnia, and discovered years ago that tourists will believe ANYTHING if delivered mysteriously enough. The clock square is his theater, and gullible tourists are his audience.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Initial Approach (Night Only)
*Emerges from shadows, speaks in low conspiratorial tone:*

> "You. You have watched the clock show, yes? The... public show?"

**Lord Pilsner**: "Yeah, it was amazing. Life-changing."

> *knowing nod*
> "That was the show for tourists. There is... another show."

### The Pitch
> "Secret show at midnight. Very few know. The clock, it reveals its TRUE face. The apostles... they speak. Very sacred. Very ancient."

**Bradley**: "That doesn't sound historically accurate—"

**Chadwick**: "Bro, let the man speak."

> "Not all can see it. You must be... worthy. Spiritually prepared. I sense in you... potential."

**Lord Pilsner**: "I KNEW there was more to it."

### Pre-Midnight
> "Wait here. Watch the clock. At midnight exactly, if you are ready... you will see."

*Disappears into shadows*

### Post-Midnight (Nothing Happened)
*Reappears from different direction:*

> "You did not see it? Hmm. Perhaps... you were not ready. The clock, it judges."

**Chadwick**: "We waited the whole time!"

> "Patience is not enough. You must be... spiritually aligned. The REAL show is at 3 AM. For those who prove themselves."

### Post-3 AM (Still Nothing)
*If bros actually wait until 3 AM:*

> "Still nothing? The clock has spoken. You are close. So close. Perhaps... next visit. Perhaps."

*Walks away*

**Lord Pilsner**: "We need to come back. We're SO CLOSE."

### If Pressed for Details
> "I cannot say more. The clock knows who is ready. It has shown itself to me... once. Many years ago. I still remember."

*Takes long drag of cigarette*

> "Some things cannot be explained. Only... experienced."
