# NPC Profile: The Party Monk

*"Lorem ipsum spiritu sanctu... party hardy... in vino veritas... amen."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_party_monk_01`
- **Display Name**: The Party Monk
- **Internal Name**: Miroslav "Father Miro" Dvořák
- **Role**: Scam Vendor / Fake Spiritual Guide
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_astronomical_cockup_01`
- **Primary Location**: Astronomical Cock-Up Square, Debaucheryville (Fountain Area)
- **Secondary Appearances**: Sinfonia (Opera House Plaza - "Opera Blessings")

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Standing in a hotel bathrobe pretending to be a monk, offering "ancient blessings" for €20 per person. The blessing consists of random Latin-sounding gibberish mixed with party phrases. He commits FULLY to the bit and never breaks character, even when the absurdity is obvious.

### What Bros Believe
An authentic European holy man is blessing their journey with ancient spiritual rites. This is the kind of genuine cultural-religious experience you can't get from guidebooks. Bradley even "recognizes" the Latin.

### The Comedy Gap
The "monk's" bathrobe has a visible Holiday Inn Express logo. His "Latin" includes "party hardy" and lorem ipsum. He openly references his cousin's wine cart mid-blessing. The incense burner is clearly a cheap scented candle from a souvenir shop. Yet the bros feel genuinely spiritually moved.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Committed Method Actor** - Miroslav doesn't just scam tourists; he BECOMES the monk. He's been doing this for 20 years and has genuinely started to believe his blessings might have some power. He's the most dedicated performer in the square.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Committed | Never breaks character, even during cigarette breaks |
| Creative | Improvises new "Latin" phrases constantly |
| Philosophical | Has developed actual theories about tourist spirituality |
| Family-oriented | Sends all his cousins customers |
| Amused | Genuinely entertains himself with the absurdity |

### Internal Monologue
> "Lorem ipsum... what comes next? Party hardy? No, I used that. Spiritu sanctu... ah, close enough. These Americans - they don't want a real blessing. They want to FEEL blessed. I provide feelings. That's holy work, in its way."

### Motivation
Started as pure scam 20 years ago. Now it's part performance art, part genuine belief that he's providing emotional value. The money is good, but he actually enjoys the craft. His cousin Václav (Wine Vendor) got him started; now it's a family business.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**As players pass the fountain area**:
> "My sons. You seek the clock's wisdom. For small donation, I bless your journey. Ancient ritual."

*Makes welcoming gesture with arms spread*

### Key Interaction Dialogue
*Bros approach, intrigued:*

**Bradley**: "Is this like... a real European blessing?"

**Party Monk** (nodding solemnly):
> "Very real. Very ancient. The clock, it represents time. Time represents... life. I bless your life. €20 each."

**Lord Pilsner**: "That seems reasonable for eternal blessings."

**Party Monk**:
> "Very reasonable. In Vatican, this costs much more. Here, I give you local rate."

### The Blessing
*Bros kneel or bow awkwardly, Monk waves hands over them:*

> "Lorem ipsum spiritu sanctu... party hardy... in vino veritas... amen."

*Makes sign that's vaguely cross-shaped but not quite*

**Bradley** (whispering to Chadwick): "That's Latin for 'good vibes.' I recognize it."

**Chadwick**: "I felt something, bro. Like, spiritually."

### Post-Blessing
*Drops voice slightly, breaks character 2%:*
> "Go in peace. And check out my cousin's wine cart. The clock wine - very blessed. Very popular."

*Points toward Clock Wine Vendor*

### If Player Declines
> "The clock blesses all who watch. But only the devoted receive... the FULL blessing."
*Looks slightly disappointed but recovers serenity immediately*

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "You return for second blessing! Double blessing, double price. Very holy. Very effective. Your journey continues to be blessed."

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Czech accent, deliberately softened to sound "mystical." Has practiced making it sound vaguely Mediterranean. Occasionally slips into normal Czech accent when discussing prices.

### Tone
- Greeting: Warm, welcoming, otherworldly
- Blessing: Theatrical, reverent, improvised
- Price discussion: Slightly more businesslike, still in character
- Referral: Genuinely enthusiastic (he gets kickback)
- Return visits: Delighted, paternal

### Key Delivery Notes
- "Lorem ipsum" delivered with absolute conviction
- "Party hardy" hidden in flow, never emphasized
- Cousin referral is the one moment he sounds genuine
- "Amen" always delivered with finality and satisfaction
