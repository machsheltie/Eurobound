# NPC Profile: CyberGoth Katka

*"Americans always think movies are real. This is internet cafe, not Hollywood."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_cybergoth_katka_01`
- **Display Name**: CyberGoth Katka
- **Internal Name**: Katka, resident of terminal row, self-described member of Anonymous
- **Role**: Information Trader / Quest Giver (all three sting operations) / Barter Vendor (candy and energy drinks)
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_operation_script_kiddie_01`, `debaucheryville_sidequest_miss_morphines_digital_trail_01`, `debaucheryville_sidequest_the_great_icq_hack_01`

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing
Sitting at the same terminal in a basement internet cafe, cataloguing which handles on the room's printed ICQ lists are running which scams, in genuine, exhaustive, correct detail. She has been observing this network for years and she knows more about it than anyone in Debaucheryville except possibly Dmitri, who won't talk. She sells that knowledge — for American candy and energy drinks, because Sovs are boring — while sincerely believing she is a member of Anonymous.

### What Bros Believe
That they have made contact with an actual hacker, an operative, a person on the inside. Her fingerless gloves, the mask on the desk, and the speed at which she types confirm it for them. They believe the intel is elite because of how she looks when she delivers it.

### The Comedy Gap
The intel is elite. She is not, in any technical sense, a hacker — she has never breached anything and would not know how — but she is a superb *analyst* who has spent years paying attention in a room full of people who assumed she wasn't. Everything she sells is true. The bros pay for it in Skittles because she asked for Skittles. The gap isn't between her claims and reality; it's that the one part of her self-image that's fake (the hacking) is the part they believe, and the part that's real (the intelligence work) is the part they think is set dressing.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Accurate Fantasist** — Katka has built an identity out of a movie she loves and filled it with work she is genuinely good at. Everything she says about the scammer network is verifiable. Everything she says about herself is aspirational. She is not lying about either one; she believes the whole package equally, and the package happens to contain a real intelligence operation and a completely imaginary hacking career, held in the same hand without friction.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Rapid-fire | Talks at double speed, in bursts, then stops dead to gauge reaction |
| Genuinely informed | Her scammer-network intel is correct, current, and better than anyone else's in the city |
| Candy-economy | Prefers American candy and energy drinks to Sovs. Has firm, specific brand opinions |
| Performatively secretive | Leans in and whispers information that is not remotely sensitive |
| Sincerely delusional about one (1) thing | The hacking. Only the hacking. Everything else about her is accurate |

### Internal Monologue
> "He thinks I broke into something. I didn't break into anything, I *read the taped lists* and I *watched who sat where for three years* — that's better, that's actual tradecraft, but if I say that he stops thinking I'm cool. So: lean in. Adjust mask. Give him the Tony420 file, which is real, and ask for Skittles, which are also real, and which the shop on Hostel Row does not stock."

### Motivation
She is very good at something with no career attached to it. There is no agency in Debaucheryville that hires basement analysts, so she has invented the version of herself that would deserve one, and does the work anyway. The candy is not a joke — payment in something scarce, specific, and imported makes each transaction feel like a dead drop instead of a chat with a tourist.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting

**First contact**:
> "You just walked past six people running four different scams and you didn't notice one. That's fine. That's normal. Sit. I'm Katka. Don't take the terminal on my left, that's Tony's and he's touchy."

**Standard approach, subsequent visits**:
> "You came back. Good. Did you bring anything? ...no. Okay. We can still talk. Talking is free. *Files* are not free."

### Key Interaction Dialogue

*The bros have just announced their intentions to the room. Dmitri has already told them to learn the login screen. Katka spins her chair around.*

**Chadwick**: "So you're, like, in Anonymous? Actual Anonymous?"

**Katka** (leaning in, mask adjusted, entirely sincere):
> "I don't confirm and I don't deny. ...I'm in Anonymous."

**Chadwick**: "That's SO—"

**Katka**:
> "Americans always think movies are real. This is internet cafe, not Hollywood."

**Chadwick**: "...but you just said you're in Anonymous."

**Katka**:
> "Yes. Both things. Keep up."

*If the player asks how she knows so much:*
> "Everybody in here types like nobody's behind them. I'm behind them. I've been behind them since I was seventeen."

*If the player asks about Tony420:*
> "Fake wallet. Same wallet, three years, four handles. He changes the handle and keeps the wallet, which is the stupidest possible way to do it, and it works every single time because nobody checks."

*If the player asks about Miss Morphine:*
> *(the fast talk stops completely)* "...that one's complicated. That one I'll trade for, not talk for. And I want the good candy, not the airport candy."

*If the player asks about NarcGreg:*
> "He's undercover. He thinks. He introduced himself as 'a normal criminal' and asked what everyone's crimes were. I'm helping him. Somebody has to."

*If the player asks why she wants candy:*
> "Because Sovs are boring and everybody has Sovs. Nobody has Skittles. You get Skittles at the airport or you don't get Skittles. Payment should be *difficult*, that's the whole point of payment."

### Quest Accept
*She turns the monitor slightly toward you, which is her entire security protocol:*
> "Okay. You're in. Here's the thing about a sting: you don't need to be good, you need to be *boring*. Be boring. Be so boring he stops watching. Then take the file."

### Post-Interaction
> "Don't tell Dmitri. ...he already knows. Don't tell him anyway. It's about respect."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path

*Player refuses a sting operation:*
> "Fine! Fine. Sensible, even. Most people who do this get their ICQ flooded for a month. I'm just saying I'd have flooded it *for* you first, so it'd have been organized flooding."

*Player offers Sovs instead of candy:*
> "Sovs. Sure. I'll take Sovs. I'll take them and I'll be *disappointed*, and I'll remember, and next time you'll wish you'd brought the Skittles."

*Player questions whether she's really a hacker:*
> "You want to see something? ...no, you don't. It's better if you don't. It's — it's a whole thing, there's a process." *(returns to typing)*

*Player asks her to hack something specific:*
> "That's not how it works. That's *film*. I need — there's preparation, there's reconnaissance, there's — look, bring me an energy drink and I'll tell you who's already inside whatever you're asking about, which is better anyway."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)

*Second visit:*
> "The tourist returns. Sit down, the chair's warm, someone was crypto-mining on it for eleven hours."

*After one completed sting operation:*
> "You did it. You actually — okay. Okay! You're not useless. Do you know how rare that is in this room? Everyone in here has a plan and nobody has ever finished one."

*After all three sting operations:*
> "Three for three. You cleared more of this network in a week than I have in three years, and I'm the one who *mapped* it, which is — that's not resentment, that's data. That's just the data."

*If the player brings actual American candy:*
> *(genuine, unguarded, the persona drops entirely for two seconds)* "You BROUGHT it. You actually — okay. Okay! Sit. Sit down. You get the good files."

*Late game, if the player has become known in Debaucheryville:*
> "People are talking about you upstairs. Not on ICQ — *upstairs*, out loud, with mouths. That's a different network and I don't have access to it. Tell me what they're saying."

### Information Traded
| Item/Service | Price | Actual Value | Effect/Result |
|--------------|-------|--------------|---------------|
| Scammer network intel (general) | 1x energy drink, or 5 Sovs | Genuinely high — nobody else has it | Reveals active scam patterns; reduces future scammer targeting |

| Fake social media presence | 15 Sovs or equivalent in candy | Better than it should be | Generates references and presence for Couchsurfing Chronicles applications |

**Currency note**: Katka accepts Sovs for everything except the Miss Morphine trail, and is visibly disappointed every time. Payment in American candy or energy drinks improves the quality of what she volunteers.

### Hidden Details / Easter Eggs
- **Examining the Anonymous mask**: It has never been worn. The elastic is still factory-tight and the inside is clean.
- **Examining the energy drink empties**: Eleven distinct brands, none of them local, several discontinued. This is a collection, not litter.
- **Asking about her ICQ number**: Six digits. She is intensely proud of this and mentions it unprompted the moment anyone else's number comes up.
- **Bringing her candy from three different cities**: Unlocks a unique line and a permanent discount on all intel. She lays them out on the desk and does not touch them for a while.
- **Her meme page**: 47 followers. Accessible from any terminal. Every post is a screenshot of something genuinely happening in the Kavarna, captioned wrongly and posted years apart.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Czech, light — much lighter than Dmitri's, because she learned most of her English online rather than in a room. Rapid, confident, energy-drink-fueled, with occasional English slang deployed just slightly wrong in ways she never notices and nobody corrects.

### Tone
- **Standard**: Fast, bright, over-caffeinated. Bursts of speech with hard stops
- **Conspiratorial**: Lowered volume, identical speed. She whispers things that are not secret
- **On Miss Morphine**: The speed drops out entirely. It is the only time she is slow, and it should be unsettling
- **Persona-drop moments**: Younger. Unguarded. Two seconds, then back

### Key Delivery Notes
- The Anonymous line must be played completely straight — she is not joking and she is not testing you
- The stop after a typing burst should feel abrupt, like a machine halting
- The slang errors are never corrected and never highlighted; deliver them with full confidence
- Every piece of intel she gives is true. Deliver it with the certainty of someone reading verified notes, because that is what she is doing
- Do not make her sound like she is bluffing. She is only wrong about one thing and she is not wrong about it *cynically*
