## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Threads & Treads is the store where people go to literally buy a new identity at 2 AM. The leather jacket as armor against the world, the combat boots as commitment to a new direction, the chain wallet as... actually practical pickpocket defense, but ALSO a statement. The satire isn't that wanting to look cool is silly — it's that the store understands and supports this desire while gently acknowledging the performance of it all.

Zdeněk, the owner, has watched thousands of people walk in looking one way and walk out looking different. He knows what they're really buying: confidence, protection (literal and emotional), a story to tell themselves.

### The Zdeněk Philosophy
"People come here at 2 AM because something happened. Maybe they saw someone who looked how they want to look. Maybe they lost something and want to become someone else. Maybe they just want to feel powerful for one night. I sell them leather. What they actually buy is permission."

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: The performance of "edginess," buying aesthetic as identity
- **Secondary Targets**: Aging attempts to look young/cool, 2 AM life decisions
- **Cultural Specificity**: Prague's rock/goth/alternative scene, Eastern European leather culture
- **Humor Approach**: Affectionate understanding of why people need to transform
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Validates the desire while seeing through it lovingly

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling people the "them they want to be" at markup
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: All-night for the 2 AM transformation market
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think leather jackets make them rock stars
- **Comedy of Errors**: The "this isn't me but I want it to be" purchase, the morning-after mirror
- **Consequence System**: Equipment provides real stats, style affects NPC interactions

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Buying some cool clothes for the trip

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Participating in retail-assisted identity performance

**How impaired are they**: Often somewhat (2 AM shopping has contexts)

**The transformation fantasy**:
- Put on leather jacket = become cooler
- Put on combat boots = become tougher
- Add chain wallet = become "that guy" (positive in their mind)
- Look in mirror = become protagonist of own movie

**The reality**:
- They DO look different
- Whether "better" is subjective
- The confidence boost IS real
- The morning light is less forgiving

**How locals interact**: Zdeněk has seen this 10,000 times, supports the journey

### The "Cool Factor" Stat
- **Function**: Affects NPC interactions in club/scene contexts
- **Thresholds**:
  - 0-10: Ignored by scene NPCs
  - 11-25: Acknowledged, basic respect
  - 26-50: Scene acceptance, better treatment
  - 51+: Scene status, special interactions
- **Context-Dependent**: High Cool Factor in clubs, less relevant in daytime
- **The Catch**: GENUINE scene knowledge matters too (wearing the shirt isn't enough)

### The Mirror Assessment
- **Trigger**: Approach mirror with items equipped
- **Display**: Full character view with current stats
- **Comparison**: Before/after outfit comparison
- **Zdeněk Commentary** (if requested): Honest assessment of the look
- **Purchase Confidence**: Seeing yourself helps commit to purchase

### The 2 AM Transformation Discount
- **Time**: 2 AM - 5 AM
- **Effect**: 10% discount on "dramatic transformation" purchases
- **Qualification**: Buying 3+ items that significantly change appearance
- **Zdeněk's Take**: "You're committing. I respect commitment."

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Commit to full new look, walk out transformed, wear outfit confidently
- **Lose Bravado**: Buy leather jacket but stick with regular shoes (half-commit), ask "does this look stupid?"
- **Neutral**: Standard purchase, standard equipment

### The "Who Do You Want To Be?" Choice
- **Trigger**: First significant purchase
- **Options**:
  - "I want to look tougher" — Zdeněk suggests defense-focused gear
  - "I want to fit in here" — Zdeněk suggests scene-appropriate gear
  - "I don't know, I just want to change" — Zdeněk has thoughts
- **Effect**: Shapes recommendations, minor dialogue changes
