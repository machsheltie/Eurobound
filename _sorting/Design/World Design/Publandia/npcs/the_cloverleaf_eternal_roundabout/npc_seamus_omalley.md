## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Running a car rental business at the edge of Publandia, directly adjacent to The Cloverleaf—Ireland's most inescapable roundabout. He's been warning tourists for 40 years. None of them listen. He warns them anyway.

### What Bros Believe
A colorful local character with a flair for dramatic storytelling. His warnings about "The Cloverleaf" are clearly exaggerated Irish folklore meant to add atmosphere to their authentic European road trip.

### The Comedy Gap
Seamus has a wall full of Polaroids of abandoned rental cars. A counter showing "DAYS SINCE LAST CLOVERLEAF INCIDENT" that has been stuck on 0 since 1984. A map with "HERE BE DRAGONS" written over the roundabout. The bros see all of this and think: "What a quirky character."

### The Hidden Tragedy
In 1992, Seamus's wife Mary went for "a quick drive to see the cliffs." She entered The Cloverleaf. The car came back eventually. She didn't. Seamus still wears his wedding ring. He tells people she's "still circling." He might not be joking.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Cassandra** - Seamus sees the future. He knows what will happen. He warns everyone. No one believes him. He warns them anyway because someone has to try.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Resigned fatalism | Knows warnings won't be heeded, gives them anyway |
| Dark humor | Copes through gallows comedy |
| Caring despite defeat | Genuinely wants to save people |
| Thousand-yard stare | Has seen too many enter, too few return |
| Professional persistence | Still runs the business, still maintains hope |

### Internal Monologue
> "Here come three more. Americans, by the look of them. They'll want a car. They'll ignore the warnings. They'll enter The Cloverleaf. And I'll be here when they come back. If they come back. They always come back eventually. The roundabout spits most of them out. Eventually."

### Motivation
Once: Make an honest living renting cars.
Now: Bear witness. Warn those he can. Maintain the Board of Shame. Wait for Mary.

### About The Cloverleaf

**If Asked to Explain:**
> "The Cloverleaf? It's... it's where the roads meet. And don't meet. And meet again. Forever. Some say it's a roundabout. I say it's a test of the soul. Ye'll understand. Everyone understands. Eventually."

**About the Counter:**
> "Days since last incident? *glances at 0* ...We've stopped resetting it. Seemed cruel to the numbers."

**About the Board of Shame:**
> "Every photo is a car that went in. Not all of them came out. The cars, I mean. The people... some say they're still circling. I don't say that. But some say it."

**About the Map:**
> "*Here be dragons?* I didn't write that. *pause* Well, I wrote it. But I meant it."

---

### About His Wife

**If Photo is Examined:**
> *touching wedding ring* "Mary. My wife. She went for a quick drive in '92. 'Just to see the cliffs,' she said."

**If Asked What Happened:**
> "The car came back. Eventually. Three weeks later. Found on the outer lane, petrol empty, wipers still going. Mary wasn't in it."

**If Asked If She's Okay:**
> "They say she's still circling. I like to think she's happy. Found peace in the endless loop. That's what I tell myself. That's what I have to tell myself."

**About the Wedding Ring:**
> "She'll want to see it when she comes back. When. Not if. When."

---

### Rental Transaction

**Offering Vehicle Options:**
> "We've got The Shamrock—economy. The Lucky Charm—standard. The Emerald Dream—for those who think size matters. They're all the same in The Cloverleaf."

**Offering GPS:**
> "GPS? *dark laugh* Aye, we've got GPS. €20 a day. It'll say 'recalculating' until ye lose the will to live. But sure, take it. Everyone takes it."

**Offering Insurance:**
> "Premium insurance. €35 a day. Covers everything. *pause* Except incidents in circular traffic patterns. That's in the fine print. Page 47."

**Offering Rosary Beads:**
> "*places beads on counter* €5. For the dashboard. Ye'll want them. Trust me on this one."

**Finalizing Rental:**
> "Sign here. And here. And... here. That one's the liability waiver. Don't read it. Nobody reads it. Just sign."

### Post-Cloverleaf Return

**If Bros Survived (Path A):**
> "Ye made it out. *genuine surprise* Most don't, ye know. At least not with their sanity intact. Ye've earned my respect. Doesn't mean much. But ye've earned it."

**If Bros Adapted (Path B):**
> "Three days in The Cloverleaf? *whistle* Ye've seen things. Changed things. The roundabout leaves its mark on everyone. Welcome back to the world of straight roads."

**Offering "Seamus's Respect" Discount:**
> "For ye? 10% off. Survivor's discount. Not that ye'll want to rent again. Nobody rents twice."

**If Bros Ask About Others:**
> "Patrick and Margaret? Still circling. 37 years now. The tour bus? They've got a lovely community going. Elected officials and everything. The farmer? Waiting for a gap. Still waiting."
