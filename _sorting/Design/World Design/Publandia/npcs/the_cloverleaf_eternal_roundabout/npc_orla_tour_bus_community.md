## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing
Running a fully functioning democratic community inside a tour bus that's been circling The Cloverleaf for 12 years. She's organized 30 former tourists into a society with elected officials, waste management, food coordination, and regular social events.

### What Bros Believe
An overly organized lady on a bus who takes her tour guide role WAY too seriously. Probably some kind of bureaucratic overachiever.

### The Comedy Gap
The Cloverleaf has trapped 30 people for over a decade, and Orla's response was to create a functioning micro-democracy with elections, committees, and orientation sessions. The absurdity is that it works. The community is genuinely thriving.

### The Beautiful Absurdity
What could be a Lord of the Flies situation has instead become a model of cooperative society. They have waste management. They plan movie nights. They throw anniversary parties for Patrick and Margaret. Orla's organizational skills turned a disaster into... a life.
