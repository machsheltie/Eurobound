# The Quiet Man

*Where trivia night is a contact sport and "pub quiz" is a euphemism for organized violence*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: publandia_seedy_quiet_man_01
- **Display Name**: The Quiet Man
- **Irish Alias**: "An Fear Ciúin"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "It's Not Fighting If You Call It Trivia"
- **World Location**: Publandia
- **Area Zone**: Basement below a "closed" pub off the Quay of Last Resort (Seedy Underbelly)
- **Location Type**: Underground Fight Club / Disguised Trivia Night / Rumor Trading Post / Unlicensed Violence Venue

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 9 PM - 4 AM (when "trivia" happens)
- **Stat Requirements**: None to watch; Bravado 30+ to participate
- **Item Requirements**: €10 "participation fee" OR answer the entry "trivia question"
- **Previous Location Requirements**: Hear about it from NPC at The Blarney Trap or Paddy's Book
- **Special Conditions**: Must give correct answer to "trivia question" (actually a test of discretion)

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Up the stairs, through the "closed" pub
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (violence is ongoing)
- **Failure States**: Lose a fight = "Humbled" status, but can return
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Contender" status after winning 3 fights

### The Entry Ritual
- **Location**: "Closed" pub with lights off, knock on cellar door
- **The Trivia Question**: Actually a discretion test
  - Question: "Who won the 1952 Best Picture Oscar?"
  - Correct Answer: "The Quiet Man" (the film — also the password)
  - Wrong Answer: Anything else, and you're "not trivia material"
  - Meta Joke: The film is about an American boxer who moves to Ireland and tries NOT to fight
- **The Bouncer**: Donal, who "hosts" the trivia

**Donal's Entry Script**:
- "Trivia night, is it? Grand. One question to enter."
- "Who won the 1952 Best Picture Oscar?"
- *Correct*: "A man of culture. Down the stairs. Mind your step and your teeth."
- *Wrong*: "That's not it, friend. Trivia's cancelled tonight. Come back when you've studied."

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Quiet Man is Fight Club if Fight Club had to pretend to be a pub quiz to avoid legal trouble. The satire operates on the ridiculous fiction that everyone maintains — calling brawls "trivia," punches "answers," and knockouts "wins" — while CLEARLY engaging in organized violence. It's commentary on how we rename things to make them acceptable, how "entertainment" justifies almost anything, and how tourists will participate in sketchy activities if they seem "authentic" and "underground."

The name is perfect irony: the 1952 film "The Quiet Man" is about a boxer who REFUSES to fight. This venue is about nothing BUT fighting. And it's not quiet at all.

### The Ciaran Philosophy
"Quizmaster" Ciaran used to run actual pub quizzes. They were boring. One night, a dispute over an answer became physical. The crowd loved it. Ciaran realized people don't come to trivia for the questions — they come for the competition. He just... removed the middleman. Now every question is answered with fists, and everyone's happier. Except the losers. They're unconscious.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Fight clubs, underground violence tourism, euphemisms for brutality
- **Secondary Targets**: Pub quiz culture taken too far, tourist thrill-seeking
- **Cultural Specificity**: Irish fighting reputation, "The Quiet Man" film irony
- **Humor Approach**: Absurd commitment to the trivia fiction
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Violence is cartoonish, consequences are real but not graphic

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Unlicensed fighting, organized violence
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Hidden beneath closed pub, Irish fighting stereotypes subverted/confirmed
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think they can fight because they've seen UFC
- **Comedy of Errors**: Overconfidence meeting reality, losing to "trivia"
- **Consequence System**: "Humbled" status, physical damage, but also respect if you lose well

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Underground fight club, they're tough enough

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Getting beaten up by people who actually know how to fight

**How impaired are they**: Varies (some fights require sobriety, most don't)

**The fighter's journey**:
1. "I've been in fights before" (bar scuffles don't count)
2. "That guy doesn't look so tough" (looks deceive)
3. "I'll just use my reach advantage" (no formal training)
4. "Why is the floor coming toward my face so fast?"
5. *wakes up in Recovery Room*
6. "...I want to try again"

**The delusion enablers**:
- Alcohol (confidence without skill)
- Crowd cheering (feels like support, it's entertainment)
- "Trivia" framing (seems less serious than "fight")
- Other tourists fighting (normalized)
- Occasional tourist wins (rare, celebrated, keeps hope alive)

**How locals interact**: Real fighters recognize tourists immediately, go easy (usually)

## Gameplay Mechanics
### The "Trivia" System

**Entry Fee**: €10 to watch, €20 to participate
**Fight Sign-Up**: Tell Ciaran you want to "answer a question"
**Matching**: Ciaran matches you based on size and "experience" (how beat up you look)

### Fight Mechanics

**Standard Combat with Modifiers**:
- Uses base game combat system
- Environment modifiers apply (Pit bonuses/penalties)
- Crowd reaction affects Bravado mid-fight
- "Category" determines special rules

**Fight Categories**:
| Category | Rules | Effect |
|----------|-------|--------|
| "Geography" | Ground game allowed | Grappling moves unlocked |
| "History" | Anything goes | No restrictions |
| "Literature" | No groin shots | Guaranteed minimum dignity |
| "Science" | Improvised weapons | Can grab items |
| "Sports" | Boxing rules | Only punches, standing only |
| "Sudden Death" | First knockdown wins | One-round maximum |

**The Crowd Meter**:
- Builds with exciting moves
- Full meter = "Crowd Favorite" buff (+damage, +defense)
- Empty meter = "Boring" debuff (crowd turns, -morale)
- Maintained by varied attacks, taunts, dramatic moments

### Fight Outcomes

**Victory**:
- €50 prize (winner's purse)
- +30 Bravado
- "Contender" progress (3 wins = status)
- Crowd respect, free drinks
- Rumor Trading Post access

**Defeat**:
- No prize (loser pays nothing extra)
- -15 Bravado
- "Humbled" status (temporary, actually respected for trying)
- Wake up in Recovery Room
- Nurse Brigid patches you up (free HP restore)

**Knockout Loss**:
- Additional -10 Bravado
- "Knocked Senseless" status (confusion effect, 30 min)
- Longer Recovery Room scene
- Brigid's disappointed head shake

**Technical Win (Opponent Can't Continue)**:
- Same as victory, but less Bravado (+20 instead of +30)
- Crowd slightly disappointed
- "Won ugly" acknowledgment

### The "Contender" Path

**Progression**:
1. First Fight: "Participant" — you tried
2. First Win: "Competitor" — you can actually fight
3. Three Wins: "Contender" — taken seriously
4. Five Wins: "Champion Material" — boss fight eligible
5. Beat the Champion: "Quiz Champion" — title holder

**Champion Fight (Optional Boss)**:
- **The Champion**: "Big" Seamus O'Malley
- **Title**: "Quiz Champion" (undefeated in 47 "questions")
- **Stats**: Significantly higher than regular opponents
- **Reward**: Title, permanent respect, €200 purse, The Champion's Belt (equipment)

### Rumor Trading Post

**Access**: After first fight (win or lose) OR €50 payment
**Function**: Buy and sell information

**Available Rumors** (Rotating):
| Rumor | Price | Content |
|-------|-------|---------|
| "Craic Tax Tip" | €20 | Location of rare item in the Craic Tax |
| "Paddy's Fix" | €30 | Which fight is fixed tonight at Paddy's |
| "Stoutworks Secret" | €25 | Hidden room in brewery dungeon |
| "Craic Baron's Weakness" | €50 | Hint for city boss fight |
| "Rival Trio Location" | €15 | Where rivals will be tomorrow |

**Selling Rumors**:
- Share information you've learned
- Payment based on value (€10-40)
- Creates economy of secrets

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Win fights, lose gracefully, work crowd, achieve Contender status
- **Lose Bravado**: Get knocked out badly, quit mid-fight, cry
- **Neutral**: Lose but fight well (respected, minimal Bravado loss)
- **Special**: Beat the Champion = massive Bravado boost, temporary "Legend" status

### Status Effects (Location-Specific)
- **"Fight Ready"**: Signed up, waiting for bout (+anticipation)
- **"In the Pit"**: Currently fighting (focus mode)
- **"Victorious"**: Won recent fight (+confidence, +crowd love)
- **"Humbled"**: Lost recent fight (temporary, respected for trying)
- **"Knocked Senseless"**: KO'd badly (confusion, -all stats, 30 min)
- **"Contender"**: 3+ wins (permanent respect)
- **"Quiz Champion"**: Beat the champion (title, equipment, permanent status)
