## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Knot Your Problem is a pretzel stand run by a man who has achieved complete emotional detachment from his customers' lives. Eoin sells pretzels. He does not sell conversation, sympathy, advice, or validation. The name is both a pretzel pun AND a statement of philosophy: your problems? Knot his.

The satire targets the drunk need to overshare with food vendors, the parasocial relationships people form with service workers, and the beautiful efficiency of someone who has simply stopped caring about anything except the quality of their product. Eoin's pretzels are excellent. Eoin's emotional availability is zero. Both are features.

### The Eoin Philosophy
Eoin Reilly has been selling pretzels for 8 years. In that time, he's heard approximately 50,000 drunk stories, relationship updates, life crises, and unsolicited confessions. He used to engage. He used to care. Now he sells pretzels. The pretzels are still excellent. Everything else has been optimized out of the interaction. 

He's not rude — he's efficient. He's not cold — he's professional. He's not uncaring — he cares deeply about pretzel quality. Everything else? Knot his problem.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Drunk oversharing, emotional labor expectations on service workers
- **Secondary Targets**: Late-night food desperation, the need for human connection (misdirected at vendors)
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dublin drunk food culture, the 3 AM confession booth that is any food cart
- **Humor Approach**: Aggressive indifference played completely straight
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes customer behavior, not service workers' actual struggles

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Enabling continued drinking (salt = thirst = more alcohol)
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: The sodium conspiracy — positioned near pubs deliberately
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think the pretzel guy wants to hear about their night
- **Comedy of Errors**: Attempts at emotional connection meeting a wall of professional indifference
- **Consequence System**: Light HP restoration, zero emotional support

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Making a friend, sharing their experience, bonding with a local

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: A man is selling them a pretzel and would like them to leave

**How impaired are they**: Usually very (that's when the oversharing starts)

**The pretzel interaction**:
1. "PRETZELS! I need carbs! Hey man, you would not BELIEVE the night I've had—"
2. *Eoin stares, waiting for order*
3. "—so anyway she was like— wait, what do you have?"
4. "Pretzels."
5. "Right, uh, I'll take a large one. So ANYWAY—"
6. "5 Sovs. Next."
7. "...don't you want to know what happened?"
8. "No. Mustard's on the left."

**The transformation**:
- Approach seeking food AND emotional outlet
- Begin unsolicited story
- Get pretzel
- Story remains unfinished
- Leave confused but fed
- Actually kind of respect it?

**How locals interact**: Locals know the deal, order quickly, leave quickly, everyone's happy

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Transaction System

**The Eoin Experience**:
1. Approach cart
2. Eoin: "What do you want?"
3. Select item (no browsing encouraged)
4. Pay (exact change preferred)
5. Receive pretzel
6. Eoin: "Next." (even if no one's behind you)
7. Leave

**Attempted Conversation Responses**:
If player tries to share information, tell stories, or seek emotional support:

| Player Attempt | Eoin's Response |
|----------------|-----------------|
| "You won't believe my night—" | "Probably not. What pretzel?" |
| "Can I get some advice?" | "Get the cheese stuffed. Next." |
| "I'm having relationship problems—" | "Mustard helps. The yellow one." |
| "Do you ever feel like—" | "No. 5 Sovs." |
| "What's your story, man?" | "I sell pretzels. That's the story." |
| "You seem like a good listener—" | "I'm not. Classic or large?" |
| "I just need someone to talk to—" | "Café's down the street. This is a pretzel stand." |
| *crying* | "Napkins are free. Pretzel?" |

### The Efficiency Bonus
Eoin rewards customers who respect the transaction:

**Quick Order Bonus**:
- Order immediately without preamble: "Good. Here." (+2 HP bonus)
- Exact change: Nod of respect (+1 HP bonus)
- No attempted conversation: Slightly larger pretzel portion

**Slow Order Penalty**:
- Browsing too long: *impatient stare*
- Attempting stories: *dead-eyed waiting*
- Asking for recommendations: "They're all good. Pick one."

### The Sodium Conspiracy (Hidden Mechanic)
Eoin's pretzels are positioned near pubs deliberately:
- Salt increases thirst
- Thirst increases drinking
- Drinking increases pretzel need
- The circle continues

**Conspiracy Bonus**: If player drinks alcohol within 30 minutes of pretzel, +5% Bravado (the synergy is intentional)

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Quick efficient transaction (respect), accepting the no-sympathy policy gracefully
- **Lose Bravado**: Getting visibly rejected emotionally by a pretzel vendor, crying at the cart
- **Neutral**: Standard transaction
- **Special**: "The Perfect Transaction" (order, pay exact change, leave without speaking beyond necessary) = +10 Bravado, Eoin's single approving nod

### Status Effects (Location-Specific)
- **"Carbed Up"**: Pretzel consumed, +HP, slight thirst increase
- **"Emotionally Unchanged"**: Attempted oversharing, received pretzel instead of validation
- **"Efficient Customer"**: Quick transaction, Eoin remembers (doesn't care, but remembers)

## NPCs & Characters
### The Vendor

**Eoin Reilly**
- **Role**: Pretzel vendor, emotional void, efficiency incarnate
- **Appearance**: 30s Irish man, neutral expression, clean apron, no wasted movements
- **Background**: Former bartender (too much emotional labor), found peace in pretzels
- **Demeanor**: Professional indifference elevated to art form
- **The Eoin Method**: Quality product, zero engagement, maximum throughput
- **Philosophy**: "I make good pretzels. That's my contribution to their night. Everything else they're going through? Knot my problem."

**Eoin's Dialogue Categories**:

*The Transaction:*
- "What do you want?"
- "5 Sovs. Mustard's there."
- "Next."
- "Here."
- "Exact change next time."

*Deflecting Conversation:*
- "That's a story. This is a pretzel stand."
- "Sounds rough. Classic or large?"
- "I believe you. Cheese stuffed?"
- "Uh huh. Napkins are free."
- "Don't know, don't need to. 3 Sovs."

*Rare Moments of... Something:*
- *to quick, efficient customer* "Good." *single nod*
- *to regular who respects the system* "Usual?" *already preparing it*
- *to someone having genuine crisis* "...café's two blocks down. They have chairs. And people who are paid to listen. Pretzel's on me. Go."

*On His Philosophy:*
- "I used to bartend. You know how many problems people told me? Thousands. You know how many I solved? None. Pretzels, though. Pretzels I can do."
- "They want me to care. I want them to have a good pretzel. We compromise."
- "Some people think I'm rude. I'm not rude. I'm efficient. There's a difference."
- "Eight years of drunk confessions. You know what I learned? Everyone's got problems. Pretzels don't fix problems. But they're still good pretzels."
