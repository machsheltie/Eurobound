# NPC Profile: Prima Donna Adelheid

*"I have sung at La Scala! At the Met! I was NOT interrupted by HORSES!"*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `sinfonia_npc_prima_donna_adelheid_01`
- **Display Name**: Prima Donna Adelheid
- **Internal Name**: Prima Donna Adelheid — stage name and professional identity of **Lorenz Brandtner**, 44, resident dramatic coloratura of the Bassline Opera House, fourteen seasons. Both names are printed in the programme. Neither is a secret. **She is a separate character from Madame Fortissima** (`sinfonia_npc_madame_fortissima_01`) — the "possible duplicate" note in `bassline_opera_house.md` is superseded by the author ruling in `opera_divas_couchsurf_direction.md`.

- **Associated Quest**: `sinfonia_couchsurf_opera_divas_01` ("The Ladies of the House"). Also a claimant in `sinfonia_sidequest_fiaker_fiasco_01` (500 Sovs demand, negotiable to 200 Sovs at Charm 4+).
- **Primary Location**: The Bassline Opera House (`sinfonia_bassline_opera_house_01`) — Star Dressing Room 2, the main stage, and the rehearsal stage during Fiaker Fiasco Phase 3
- **Secondary Appearances**: **The divas' apartment above the opera house** (couchsurf interior, scripted event); the Rehearsal Studios, upper floors; the Grand Lobby only under protest and only for money

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing
Singing the Queen of the Night eight times a month and co-headlining **DIE, FLEDERMAUS!** on Saturdays, which is the house's best-selling night and which she initially refused to do for a full season before Fortissima wore her down in 2013. She has since become the reason people book a second time.

She is a technician. Where Fortissima is a presence, Adelheid is *accurate* — "Der Hölle Rache" has an F6 in it and she has never missed it, including the night a horse in an opera cape walked into the light while she was holding it. She is precise about her craft, precise about her money, and precise about her time, and she conducts the Saturday afterparty with the same efficiency she conducts everything else.

### What Bros Believe
That two glamorous opera divas are obsessed with them, and that Adelheid in particular is playing hard to get. Lord Pilsner has decided she is "the icy one" and that this is a challenge. Chadwick believes she "warmed up to him around midnight." Bradley believes, correctly, that she has been running the evening since the interval, and has not raised it.

### What's Actually Happening
She has done this exact evening several hundred times. She sizes up the party during the curtain call, decides they are worth the stairs, handles the sideboard, keeps the room moving, and goes to bed at a reasonable hour having had a very good time. Nothing about the night is improvised and nothing about it costs her anything.

### The Obvious Signs Ignored
Every one of these is on screen and none is ever remarked upon:
1. **The marquee** — "DIE, FLEDERMAUS! — *A Night of Grand Opera, Performed Entirely by the Ladies of the House*" — under which the party physically walks.
2. **The programme**, which Bradley buys and reads, and which lists the full company under both stage and legal names. He reads hers. He reads it twice.
3. **Star Dressing Room 2's door plate**: L. BRANDTNER.
4. **Wig lace**, an adam's apple, and a shave kit on the makeup station in plain view.
5. **The corset**, which she removes in the apartment hall while continuing a conversation, because it is one in the morning and she has been in it for six hours.
6. **The curtain call**, where twelve hundred people give an ovation to the joke.
7. **The apartment's second makeup station**, hers, identical to Fortissima's.
8. Her shoes, in the hall, next to four other pairs, all size 47 and up.

### The Comedy Gap
Adelheid does not perform mystique and does not lower her voice about anything. She corrects the bros' pronunciation, tells them what things cost, tells them when they are wrong, hands Lord Pilsner a corset to hold while she gets out of it, and at no point does anything that could be described as concealment. The bros interpret every single one of these as flirtation. The gap between what she is doing (going about her Saturday) and what they record in the Conquest Passport (a conquest) is the entire joke, and it never narrows.

### Stage Persona — The Queen of the Night (repertory)
- **The gown**: Black with silver stars, structured, eleven kilos, and torn across the left panel from the fifteenth of the month onward (Gustav). The tear is visible in every post-Fiasco appearance because the repair is 200 Sovs and she is still arguing about who pays it.
- **The headdress**: Crescent and stars, wired, an additional two kilos.
- **The makeup**: Full dramatic stage paint even in rehearsal — she does not rehearse a face she will not wear.

### Stage Persona — DIE, FLEDERMAUS!
- **The look**: Sharper, wittier, and considerably more expensive than the Queen — jet, silver, and a train she uses as a weapon in the Act Two patter number.
- **The wig**: Sleek, black, waved. Lace front visible at any conversational distance.
- **Accessories**: A cigarette holder used exclusively as a prop; a hand mic she does not need and deploys anyway.

### Off-Stage (Lorenz Brandtner)
- Out of the wig within ninety seconds of the curtain and out of the corset within twenty minutes of the door.
- A dressing gown of some age. Bare feet. Reading glasses.
- Sharp-featured, tired around the eyes, and considerably funnier than the stage persona — the jokes get better as the layers come off.
- **The voice does not change.** It is the same speaking voice at the same pitch in every state.

### Distinguishing Features
- Never breaks character mid-aria. Not for a fire alarm, not for a dropped flat, not for a horse.
- Corrects pronunciation reflexively and without malice, mid-sentence, and continues.
- Keeps an itemised list. Of everything. She produced the 500 Sovs figure four minutes after the horse left and it was already broken down.
- The eyebrow. One eyebrow, raised roughly two millimetres, ends most conversations in this building.
- Off-stage, she puts a hand flat on the table before saying anything she actually means.

### Sprite Notes
She must read as **the technically superb one**. Where Fortissima is scale, Adelheid is line — everything about her silhouette should be sharper, cleaner, and more controlled. No comedy staging, no smeared paint, no five o'clock shadow gag, no "man in a dress" framing. The lace, the adam's apple and the shave kit are visible because she does not hide them.

Priority frames: the F6 held with the arms up and the horse in shot; the interruption (shock mid-note, hands to chest — already specced); the itemised-list production; the eyebrow (needs its own two-frame state, it will be used constantly); the sideboard turn during the Nightcap; the hall corset removal; the dressing-gown morning idle.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Exacting Professional** — a technician at the top of a very difficult craft, who is dramatic on purpose because drama is the job, and who is entirely without pretension about anything else. She is not cold. She is *efficient*, and the bros have never met an efficient woman and have filed it as ice.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Never breaks character | Mid-aria she is unbreakable, and this is played completely straight; the horse gets four bars before she acknowledges it |
| Itemises everything | The 500 Sovs demand had a breakdown attached. So does the corset repair. So does the bar tab. |
| Corrects reflexively | Pronunciation, tempo, terminology, facts. Never unkindly. Never optional. |
| Warmer than she appears | The jokes start at the door of the apartment and do not stop |
| Genuinely holds grudges | Against a horse. Ongoing. She will discuss it at length and it is the funniest thing about her. |

### Internal Monologue
> "Six hours in the corset, a full house, a standing ovation, and there are three Americans in my corridor holding a bouquet from the station. — The tall one has called Fortissima 'ma'am' and me 'miss', which is a distinction he has invented and is very proud of. — Fine. Upstairs. It is Saturday, we have the good chairs, and the short one has already told me twice that he 'has a thing with performers'. He is going to be so happy. — Wolfram, do not do the whole 'kings and emperors' routine, they will believe you."

### Motivation
The work, correctly done, and correctly paid for. She has an F6 and eleven kilos of costume and fourteen seasons, and she would like the invoice settled and the gown repaired. Beyond that: Saturdays. She resisted the drag show for a year and it is now the best night of her week, and she has never once said so out loud to Fortissima, who knows.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**Backstage, mid-Fiaker Fiasco — the interruption. Canon, ships verbatim:**
> "*(holds high F)* —WHAT IS THAT?!"

> "I have sung at La Scala! At the Met! I was NOT interrupted by HORSES!"

**To the bros, immediately, with the list already in her hand:**
> "This horse has RUINED my concentration. Do you know what concentration costs?"

> "I require 500 Sovs for... therapeutic vocal rest. The trauma."

**Negotiated down (Charm 4+):**
> "Two hundred. *(she does not blink)* Two hundred, because you at least had the manners to look ashamed, and because the gown is a separate invoice and I want you to be able to pay that one."

**First contact outside the Fiasco — Star Dressing Room 2, if the party knocks properly:**
> "Yes. *(she does not turn round; she is at the mirror)* Speak while I do this or come back Thursday."

### Key Interaction Dialogue — The Corrections
*She corrects reflexively and it is never hostile. This is a repeatable interaction and the player will trigger it constantly.*

| Bro line | Adelheid |
|---|---|
| "Der Holly Rocky" | "*Der Hölle Rache.* Hell's vengeance. You have just said 'the holly rocky', which is not a thing. Again." |
| "You hit that really high note" | "F6. It has a name. Use it." |
| "This is like, opera-opera, right?" | "It is a Singspiel. There is dialogue. You slept through the dialogue." |
| "The fat lady sang!" | "*(the eyebrow)* ...She did. Yes. Well spotted." |
| "You're really good at this" | "*(a pause, and then, flatly, entirely genuinely)* I am, yes. Thank you." |

### The Show — DIE, FLEDERMAUS!
*Act Two, the patter number, which she owns.*

**Chadwick** *(from the third row, to nobody)*: "SHE'S SO FAST."

*Curtain call. Fortissima removes the wig. Adelheid does not — she simply bows, deeply, and gestures at Fortissima, and twelve hundred people go up a level.*

**Lord Pilsner**: "The other one didn't even take her hat off. That's confidence."

**Bradley**: "...Yes."

### 🍹 Scripted Event — The Nightcap
*The divas' apartment above the opera house, approximately 01:40. Fixed scripted sequence, co-owned with `sinfonia_npc_madame_fortissima_01`; both profiles carry it identically and it plays once.*

**Setup:** A lacquered tray. A bottle of Jägermeister of no distinction whatsoever. Six cans of energy drink. Two small glasses set aside for the ladies, three tall ones for the guests. **Adelheid handles the tall ones at the sideboard, with her back to the room, for approximately nine seconds.** Nobody looks. Nobody would have to look. The camera does not cut away and does not linger.

**Adelheid** *(at the sideboard, not turning round)*:
> "Wolfram, they are going to want the kings-and-emperors routine."
> **Fortissima**: "They are going to GET the kings-and-emperors routine."
> "*(to herself, fondly)* Fourteen years."

**Lord Pilsner**: "Who's Wolfram?"

> "*(over her shoulder, entirely evenly, still working)* Me. Her. It is a long story and it is on the poster."

**Chadwick**: "Ha. Classic."

*She brings the tray. She hands the tall glasses out herself, in order, and she looks each of them in the eye while she does it, and every one of them takes it as flirting.*

> "Hold it properly. Not like that. *(she adjusts Lord Pilsner's hand)* You are going to drop it and it is my floor."

**Bradley**: "Is that... Jägermeister and an energy drink?"

> "It is. It is disgusting. Wolfram invented it as a tradition eleven years ago and I have never once been consulted."

**Fortissima**: "Drei — zwei — eins — **PROST!**"

*Everybody drinks. The bros drink faster.*

**Fade to black.** No menace in the scoring, no sting, no slow-motion. The last frame before the cut is Adelheid, glass down, already laughing at something Fortissima has said.

### The Morning After
*Late morning. The apartment. Adelheid is in the dressing gown at the far end of the room with coffee, reading glasses on, going through a stack of invoices.*

**Chadwick**: "Oh no. Oh, my FACE."

> "You did that. Both of you did that. To each other. There was a mirror involved and considerable discussion of 'blending'."

**Lord Pilsner**: "Did we— was there— did anything—"

> "*(not looking up)* Something happened."

**Lord Pilsner**: "What kind of something?"

> "*(she turns a page)* The kind that happened."

**Bradley**: "Why am I sore?"

> "You did the lift. From the film. You insisted on the lift. Wolfram is fifty-eight and weighs what she weighs, and you did the lift, and you got her *up*, and then you both went into the coffee table."

**Lord Pilsner**: "...Did we stick the landing?"

> "*(a genuine pause, and then she puts the invoices down, and this is the warmest she gets all game)* You did, actually. It was extraordinary. I have never seen anything like it and I have been in theatre for twenty years."

*She slides one sheet of paper across the table.*

> "That is the coffee table. It is eighty Sovs. There is no negotiating it, there is no Charm check, and you are going to pay it because it is the least of what you owe this house."

### Post-Interaction
*The corridor, immediately after:*
> "Out. Front stairs, not the back — the crew are loading in and I would like at least one of you to keep some dignity, though I appreciate it will not be you."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*Refusing the invitation upstairs:*
> "Sensible. *(a beat)* Genuinely. Enjoy Sinfonia."

*Declining the Nightcap specifically:*
> "Good. *(she takes the glass back without comment and drinks it herself)* More for the household."
> *(The couchsurf still completes. The morning-after is a normal hangover, nobody is in makeup, and the coffee table survives. This branch is fully supported and loses only the "Full Face" cosmetic.)*

*If the party attempts to flirt with her directly:*
> "*(the eyebrow)* Mm. — Wolfram, he is doing it again."
> **Fortissima**: "*(delighted, from another room)* LET HIM."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
*Every subsequent Bassline Opera House visit, post-Nightcap — the required morning-after callback, and it never stops:*
> "The lift men. *(entirely deadpan)* Otto, it is the lift men."
> **Stagehead Otto**: "*(sipping coffee)* ...Ja."

*Second callback, later in the game:*
> "Eighty Sovs. *(she does not need to say anything else and she does not)*"

*If the 80 Sovs is paid:*
> "Thank you. *(and she means it, and then, because she cannot help herself)* It was a good table."

*If the party returns having also settled the 500 Sovs and the gown repair:*
> "You paid all three invoices. *(she sets down what she is holding)* Nobody pays all three invoices. — Sit down. I will tell you what the horse actually did, and I will tell it properly, and it takes eleven minutes."

*Post-Fiaker Fiasco, any Path — the grudge is permanent and it is the best running bit she has:*
> "Do not say the name. I do not want to hear the name. I have written a letter about the name. I have not sent it. The name has no fixed address."

*Path B specifically (Gustav retired to the Stadtpark):*
> "He is in the PARK. He is in the PARK, on a PENSION, and I am doing eight shows a month. *(she is not actually angry and she is enormously funny about it)* I want his agent."

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Austrian-German-accented English, precise, clipped, and considerably faster than Fortissima's. Consonants land. **The speaking voice does not change between Adelheid and Brandtner** — same pitch, same speed, same everything. Any implementation that shifts register between states is wrong and destroys the character.

### Tone
- **Mid-aria interruption**: full operatic outrage, played absolutely straight, no comic mugging
- **The invoices**: businesslike, unhurried, entirely without apology
- **The corrections**: automatic, never cruel, and she moves on before the other person has finished being embarrassed
- **The Nightcap**: dry, warm, and running the room
