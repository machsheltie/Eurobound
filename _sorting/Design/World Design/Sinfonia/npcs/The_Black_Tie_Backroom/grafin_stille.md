## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Gräfin Stille arrives at eleven, sits at the mid-stakes table, plays for four hours, and leaves. In that time she will not say a word. Not a greeting, not a thank you, not a request for a card. Everything she needs to communicate at a poker table she communicates by betting, and it turns out that is everything, because she is the second-best player in the room and possibly the best.

Her play is calculated and patient. She bluffs occasionally, precisely, and always for a reason that becomes obvious one hand later. She punishes hesitation. She has one tell and it is real: **she sips her champagne more often when she is strong.** A player who notices can beat her. She has never adjusted it, in four years, and nobody knows whether that is because she cannot or because she has decided the trade is acceptable.

She has never been heard to speak in this building. No staff member has heard her voice. Whether she can, whether she chooses not to, and why, are **never established, never hinted at, and never resolved by any content in this game.**

### What Bros Believe
The bros find her deeply unnerving and immediately construct several explanations, all of them wrong and none of them ever corrected. Chadwick decides she is a spy. Lord Pilsner decides she is "the house's real weapon" and attempts, at length, to get a reaction out of her — table talk, staredowns, one truly disastrous attempt at flirtation delivered to a woman who does not blink. She raises. He folds. He describes the exchange afterward as "a conversation," and in fairness to him, it was.

### The Comedy Gap
She is the only person at the table producing no information whatsoever, and she is therefore the only person the bros feel they truly understand. Everything they believe about her is invented by them, out loud, in her presence, at length, while she takes their chips a hundred euro at a time.

**Binding:** her silence is never explained. Not by her, not by Madame Karten, not by a dealer, not by an examine string, not by an achievement description, not by a late-game reveal. The mystery is permanent canon and any content that resolves it is a defect.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | Indeterminate — somewhere in a twenty-year band, and nobody has narrowed it |
| Build | Straight-backed, still, economical; moves once per action and not at all otherwise |
| Height | Average |
| Energy | Absolute composure |

### Clothing
- **Black evening gown**: The same silhouette every night, in what may or may not be the same gown. Immaculate. Nothing about it dates it to any particular decade.
- **Long gloves**: Removed for play, folded once, placed to the left of her chips, and never touched again until she leaves.
- **Accessories**: A single champagne coupe, refilled by the servers without instruction; earrings that catch the chandelier when she turns her head, which is rarely.
- **Footwear**: Never visible. She is seated before anyone arrives at the table and stands only to leave.

### Distinguishing Features
- A face that does nothing. Not a mask, not a scowl — simply a face at rest, for four hours, through wins, losses, and one gentleman's attempt at flirtation.
- **The tell**: the coupe comes up more often when she is strong. Real, learnable, and never confirmed by any in-game text or UI.
- Chips pushed forward with two fingers, exactly the same distance every time, so that the size of a bet is legible only from the stack and never from the gesture.
- A single small nod, delivered to precisely three people in this building, at most once per evening each.

### Sprite Notes
Stillness is the entire character and the sprite must sell it — she should be the only figure on the main floor with no idle fidget at all beyond the coupe. Keep her elegant, adult, and formidable; she is never drawn as spooky, sinister, or ghostly, and never as decorative. Priority frames: the champagne lift (the tell — identical every time, no exaggeration on the strong version), the two-finger chip push, and the single nod. Her leaving animation — gloves back on, one at a time, unhurried, while a table waits — should be strong enough to end a scene on. Reference energy: the most composed person in any room she has ever been in.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**Speaks Only in Bets** — a woman who has reduced an entire social interface to chips and finds it sufficient. She is not cold and not hostile; she is simply complete. People who spend an evening at her table generally come away feeling they were treated fairly and told nothing, which is exactly what happened.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Silent | Not one word, ever, in any scene, on any branch, in any language |
| Legible Anyway | Her play communicates approval, contempt, patience, and interest with total clarity, and every regular in the room can read it |
| Patient | Will sit through forty hands without a significant pot and consider the evening well spent |
| Exact | Same seat, same coupe, same two-finger push, same arrival time, four years running |
| Quietly Generous | Tips Siegfried in chips, folded into the coat, never handed over — an amount he has never disclosed to anybody |

### Internal Monologue
> *(None is authored, and none may be authored. Gräfin Stille has no interior monologue in any document in this repository. What she is thinking is not available to the player, the narrator, or the design, and the absence is the character.)*

### Motivation
Unestablished by design. What is observable: she comes four nights a week, she plays well, she leaves at three, and she has never once chased a loss. Anything beyond that is not written here and is not written anywhere else.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
*She does not greet. A player sitting down at her table receives one glance, of ordinary length, and then the dealer's attention returns to the deck.*

**Her entire spoken vocabulary in the game:**
> *(Sips champagne. Raises €100. No other communication necessary.)* — canon, ships verbatim as a stage direction; it is **never voiced and never subtitled as speech.**

### Key Interaction Dialogue — The Bet Language
*She has one channel and uses it fluently. The player learns to read this by playing against her; nothing in the game ever translates it.*

| What she does | When |
|---|---|
| Immediate raise, two fingers | Somebody hesitated |
| Flat call, no movement afterward | She is not finished with this hand |
| Coupe lifted, then a check | Strong. This is the tell, and it is real. |
| Fold delivered face-down, edge-on, without a glance | The hand was over three streets ago |
| A single nod across the table after a hand | The rarest thing she does. Three people have received one. |
| Gloves back on, one at a time | The evening is over, regardless of what anyone else at the table was planning |

### To the Bros
*Lord Pilsner has been talking at her for eleven minutes. He has tried table talk, a staredown, and a compliment that did not survive contact with the air.*

**Lord Pilsner**: "Silent treatment. Classic. That's a POWER move and I RESPECT it."

*She raises €100.*

**Lord Pilsner** *(folding instantly)*: "...See, that's a conversation. That's two people having a conversation."

**Chadwick** *(reverent, and audible across two tables)*: "Bro. She's a SPY."

*She does not look up. Somewhere behind them, Graf von Zittrig greets her by title, warmly, for the nineteen-hundredth time, and receives nothing, and is not remotely discouraged.*

### Post-Interaction
*After taking a significant pot from a player, she does exactly one thing, and only if the hand was played well: the single nod. There is no dialogue attached, no follow-up, and no way to earn a second one in the same evening.*

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*Player leaves her table mid-session, or declines the seat:*
*(She does not react. The chair is re-filled within ten minutes by somebody else and she does not appear to notice that either.)*

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
*On any subsequent visit, if the party has previously beaten her in a session, she is already seated when they arrive and the chair opposite her is empty. It was not empty before they walked in. Nothing else about her behaviour changes, and no character comments on the chair.*

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
**None. She has no voice lines and will never have voice lines.** No breath sounds, no vocalisations, no hum, no sigh. Any future pass proposing dialogue for this character is a defect.

### Tone
- **Audio design in place of voice**: the coupe against the table, the chip push, the fold, the single glove-snap on departure. These four sounds are her entire audio presence and should be mixed with more clarity than the room around them.
- **Nothing sinister.** No stinger when she raises, no low drone under her table, no horror-adjacent scoring anywhere near her.

### Key Delivery Notes
- The canon stage direction *(Sips champagne. Raises €100. No other communication necessary.)* ships verbatim **as a stage direction only** — it is design text, never a subtitle, never spoken, never rendered as dialogue in-game.
- Her stillness must be maintained by the animation and audio teams as strictly as a line reading would be. One unmotivated fidget breaks the character.
- She is dignified, formidable, and adult in every frame. She is never played as decorative, never as a femme fatale, and never as an object of the bros' pursuit — Lord Pilsner's attempt lands on a woman who is at work, and the scene belongs to her.
