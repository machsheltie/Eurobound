## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Dancing Stein satirizes Vienna's waltz obsession — the city where three-quarter time is practically a religion. The pub demands everyone participate in the cultural tradition, turning a simple drink order into a mandatory dance performance.

The deeper joke: Nobody here is actually a good dancer. The waltz "requirement" is enforced loosely by staff who are also mediocre. But everyone pretends it's elegant because admitting the shuffling isn't a real waltz would break the spell.

### The Mandatory Waltz
To get a drink, you must:
1. Enter through the rotating door (sets the tempo)
2. Waltz across the floor to the bar (doesn't have to be good)
3. Order while still moving (bartenders are used to this)
4. Waltz back to a table (drink in hand)
5. Only THEN can you sit

Staff enforces this with friendly insistence, not hostility.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Waltz culture as mandatory tradition
- **Secondary Targets**: Dance snobbery, rhythmic gatekeeping, "authentic" Viennese experience
- **Cultural Specificity**: Vienna's ballroom culture applied to casual drinking
- **Humor Approach**: Gentle absurdity, everyone's in on the joke (sort of)

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Brewery Tour Integration

**Stamp Unlock Challenge: "The Stein Waltz"**

**Challenge Type**: Rhythm Dance (With Full Beer)

**Mechanics**:
1. Receive full stein from bartender
2. Music starts (waltz tempo)
3. Complete full circuit of dance floor
4. Maintain waltz rhythm (QTE)
5. Don't spill below the "acceptable loss" line
6. Return to bar, drink what remains

**Difficulty Levels** (Self-Selected):
| Level | Beer Fullness | Timing Window | Reward Multiplier |
|-------|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Beginner | 50% full | Generous | 1x |
| Standard | 75% full | Normal | 1.5x |
| Master | 100% full | Tight | 2x |

**Spill Mechanics**:
- Some spilling allowed (line marked on stein)
- Major stumbles = splash animation
- Total spill = failure (but funny)
- Perfect run = not a drop lost

**Success**: Stamp + signature beer unlocked + "Graceful" buff (+10% Dance, 1 hour)
**Failure**: "Two Left Feet" status (50% off next beer, must retry to stamp)

- The Spinning Brewery handles production
- The Dancing Stein handles the "experience"
- Both share the waltz obsession
- The signature beer was co-developed
