# SHAMSTERDAM (Amsterdam Parody)
## Theme: Permission & Regret — "What happens here, you'll remember forever. Whether you want to or not."

The city that promises total freedom but delivers total accountability. A labyrinth of canals, coffeeshops, and poor decisions where tourists come expecting anything goes and leave realizing everything has consequences. The locals have seen it all, judged it all, and learned to profit from it all.

**Satirical Core**: The gap between what tourists THINK Amsterdam is (a lawless playground of vice) versus what it ACTUALLY is (a heavily regulated, deeply judgmental city that's exhausted by your bachelor party). The bros arrive expecting "European Vegas" and discover a city that's actively campaigning for them to stay away.

## STREETS & DISTRICTS

**The Infamous Window District**

- Strict "NO PHOTOS" signs the bros keep violating (automatic fine encounters)

**Scenic Waterway District**
- Picture-perfect canal houses with leaning architecture
- Houseboats converted to questionable businesses
- Bicycle traffic that does NOT stop for confused pedestrians
- Bridge buskers playing Dutch folk songs ironically
- The canal water: murky, mysterious, and home to 12,000 discarded bicycles

**Haze-Filled Tourist Trap Zone**
- Green neon signs promising "authentic" experiences at tourist prices

### Cheese & Clog Corner
**Dutch Stereotype District**
- Giant wheels of Gouda in every window
- Wooden shoe shops targeting photo-op tourism

**Cultural Alibi District**

## DEEPER MECHANICS & SYSTEMS

### Bravado System (Shamsterdam Specific)
- **Gain Bravado**: Successfully navigating coffeeshop menus, not getting hit by bikes, surviving bachelor party encounters, eating herring
- **Lose Bravado**: Getting fined for public smoking, asking locals "is this legal?", mispronouncing everything, wearing matching stag party costumes
- **City-Specific Thresholds**:
    - **High Bravado**: Locals mistake you for a returning expat, better prices, access to "local only" spots
    - **Medium Bravado**: Standard tourist treatment, standard overcharging
    - **Low Bravado**: Actively targeted by "Stay Away" campaign NPCs, price gouging, public shaming

### Shamsterdam-Specific Status Effects
- **Contact High**: Confused state from secondhand exposure (+Relaxed, -Coordination)
- **Bicycle Trauma**: PTSD from near-miss collisions (-Speed in all traffic situations)
- **Space Cake Gamble**: Delayed effect (nothing for 2 hours, then EVERYTHING)
- **Red Light Shame**: -Charm, +Embarrassed, dialogue options reveal too much
- **Tourist Trapped**: Automatically pay 40% more for everything
- **Cheese Sweats**: +HP from dairy, -Speed from fullness
- **The Giggles**: Random laughter interrupts dialogue, -Seriousness
- **Dutch Directness**: NPCs tell you exactly what they think (often painful)
- **Canal Confusion**: All streets look the same, navigation penalties
- **Stay Away Targeted**: City's "discouragement campaign" NPCs follow you

### Permission Paradox System
The central irony of Shamsterdam: everything is "legal" but nothing is without consequences
- **Coffeeshop Entry**: Legal, but public smoking = €100 fine
- **Window Shopping**: Legal, but photography = phone confiscation + shame
- **Bachelor Parties**: Legal, but matching costumes = targeted enforcement
- **Being Loud**: Legal, but neighbors WILL call the police
- **Existing as Tourist**: Legal, but the city literally paid for ads telling you not to come

### Fine & Penalty Mechanics
- **Public Consumption Fine**: €100 gold, triggered by outdoor smoking
- **Photography Violation**: -50 gold + "Marked Tourist" debuff
- **Noise Complaint**: Random police encounter, -Bravado
- **Bicycle Lane Walking**: Getting yelled at by EVERYONE
- **Canal Littering**: Instant NPC mob judgment

### Time-Based Elements
- **Pre-10pm**: Museums, cheese shops, cultural alibi opportunities
- **10pm-2am**: Peak chaos in Roodelicht Row, coffeeshop crowds
- **2am-4am**: FEBO automat pilgrimage, canal-side regret
- **Post-4am**: The "where are we and how did we get here" phase
- **Dawn**: Walk of shame past jogging locals, maximum judgment
