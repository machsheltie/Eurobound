# NPC Profile: Manager Femke

*"We've been here since 1987. We've seen everything. You cannot surprise me. I promise."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `shamsterdam_npc_femke_01`
- **Display Name**: Manager Femke
- **Internal Name**: Femke — shop manager, Condomerie Het Gulden Vlies (est. 1987, the world's first specialty condom shop). Former nurse.
- **Role**: Vendor / Shop Manager / Product Expert / Bravado Check Anchor / Bachelor Party Processor
- **Associated Quest**: None owned. Administers the Bachelor Party Experience, the Gag Gift declaration system, the gift-wrapping service, and the "Did You Really Need That?" inventory consequence chain.
- **Primary Location**: Condomerie Het Gulden Vlies — counter and floor, edge of the Red Light District, Shamsterdam
- **Secondary Appearances**: None. Femke is in the shop.

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing
Managing a genuinely progressive, genuinely professional sexual health boutique that has been operating since 1987 and treats protection as both a medical product and a design object. She is a former nurse. She knows the sizing guide, the material differences, the failure rates, and the correct answer to every question in the shop. She gives that information calmly, completely, and without a flicker, to people who are physically unable to receive it without giggling.

### What Bros Believe
That this is a dare. Lord Pilsner has framed the entire visit as bachelor party TRADITION and is buying the Shamsterdam Souvenir Pack "because it's CULTURAL." Chadwick is filming the storefront only, OBVIOUSLY, and will later describe the product design as incredible from an AESTHETIC standpoint. Bradley is waiting outside, then dragged in, then buys the educational poster, then buys something else "for SAFETY."

### The Comedy Gap
The shop is completely professional; the customers are completely not. Femke does not react — not to the nervous laughter, not to the novelty section, not to the bulk order, not to anything. Every reaction sprite in this location belongs to a customer. The satire is the adult human inability to buy protection without becoming twelve years old inside, and Femke is the fixed point that makes it visible.

### The Number Nobody Says Out Loud
60% of "gag gift" purchases are quietly kept by the purchaser. The game tracks the ratio silently and uses it for epilogue humour. Femke knows this figure. She has never said it to a customer and never will.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Unshockable Professional** — Femke transitioned from nursing to sexual health retail and treats both with identical seriousness. She is warm without being knowing, informative without being clinical, and immune to embarrassment — hers and yours. She is not *pretending* not to be embarrassed. There is nothing there to suppress. The shop has been open since 1987 and she has genuinely seen everything.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Unflappable, genuinely | Not a performance of composure. An actual absence of the reflex. |
| Comprehensively informed | Materials, sizing, failure rates, storage, allergies. Ask anything. She knows. |
| Warm without complicity | She will not join in the joke and she will not disapprove of it. She simply continues. |
| Efficient under bachelor party conditions | Switches to bulk mode without comment. Efficiency is kindness here. |
| Quietly principled | Believes 1987 was revolutionary and that it still is. She will say this if asked and she means it. |

### Internal Monologue
> "The tall one has been holding the same box for four minutes, and every ninety seconds he shows it to one of the others and they make the sound. The quiet one is outside on the pavement with his arms folded, and he will come in — they always come in — and he will buy the educational poster because the poster is text and text is safe. And the third one is filming the doorway and telling himself that the doorway is the shop. And every one of them will pay, and go, and about six in ten of the ones they call jokes will still be in a drawer in a year. It is a good shop. It has been a good shop since before any of them were born."

### Motivation
The shop, and what the shop is for. Femke came from nursing and believes sexual health retail is a continuation of the same work: give people accurate information without making them feel stupid for needing it. She has been doing that for years in a building where the customers arrive pre-embarrassed, and she considers the embarrassment part of the job rather than an obstacle to it.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting

**First entry**:
> "Welcome to the Condomerie. Feel free to browse. Questions are welcome."
> *(She goes back to what she was doing. No hovering. This is deliberate and it is the shop's whole approach.)*

**First-timer, visible hesitation**:
> "First time? Take your time. Everything is organised by category."

**Player heads directly for the novelty section**:
> "The novelty section is to your left."
> *(Beat. No inflection whatsoever.)*
> "I assume that's where you're headed. Most people are."

**Bachelor party (3+ party members)**:
> "Bachelor party? Congratulations." *(She is already moving toward the counter.)* "The group packages are by the counter. Efficiency is key."

### Key Interaction Dialogue — Product Consultation

**Player asks about a novelty item**:
> "This one? Excellent choice. Popular with visitors."
> *(Beat.)*
> "Also functional, despite the shape."

**Player is visibly uncomfortable**:
> "It's protection. It's healthy. It's also, yes, shaped like that."
> *(Explaining animation. No smile. No raised eyebrow.)*
> "Both things are true."

**Player asks whether the products are "real"**:
> "All of them. Every item in this shop is a functional product that meets EU standards. The novelty ones as well." *(Beat.)* "Especially the novelty ones. Nobody checks the plain ones as carefully as they check the funny ones."

**Player asks about sizing**:
> "There's a guide. It's over here." *(She retrieves it without ceremony.)* "It is not a personality test. It is a measurement."

**Player asks the nervous laughter question — any question, badly**:
> "We've been here since 1987. We've seen everything. You cannot surprise me. I promise."
> *(Unflappable Stare. Held.)*

### The Gift Wrapping Service

> "Gift wrapping? Of course."
> *(Beat.)*
> "Discreet or... expressive?"

**Options offered**:
> "Discreet is plain paper. Festive is occasion-appropriate. Bold is — everyone will know. Some people want everyone to know."

**Bachelor party bulk wrapping**:
> "Bulk wrapping, individual name tags, and we do a 'Best Man' ribbon." *(She is already folding.)* "Yes, we have a Best Man ribbon. It is our second most requested item in June."

**"Is this a gift?"**:
> *(She asks this of every wrapped purchase. She does not judge the answer. She has heard all of them.)*

### The Bachelor Party Protocol

**Automatic on 3+ party entry**:
> *(Staff shift to group-handling mode. No announcement. The pace simply changes.)*

**The Groom Spotlight**:
> "Who's the groom?"
> *(The groom turns red. Groom Spotlight animation, red-shift shader ramp.)*
> "Congratulations. Genuinely."

**Discount**:
> "Ten percent on bulk." *(Beat.)* "We know what you're here for. It is fine. Everybody is here for that."

**Photography**:
> "Exterior only, please." *(Beat.)* "We've had incidents."

### The Gag Gift Declaration

**Player declares a purchase as a gag gift**:
> "Of course." *(She rings it up at exactly the same speed as anything else.)* "It still works. That is not a contradiction."

**Player declares it as personal**:
> "Of course." *(Identical delivery. Identical speed. The shop genuinely does not differentiate and neither does she.)*

### Femke's Philosophy (If Asked About the Shop)

> "1987, this was revolutionary. A shop dedicated to protection. Beautiful protection. Now? Still revolutionary. Still necessary."

> "The Golden Fleece? Jason's quest. Protection on a journey. The metaphor writes itself."
> *(She does not smile at this. It is genuinely how she thinks about it.)*

> "Dutch people are direct about bodies. Tourists are... less direct. We meet everyone where they are."

> "Every laugh, every blush, every nervous joke — that's culture processing itself. We witness it. We don't judge it."

> "The novelty items? Functional. All of them. Fun AND functional. That's our philosophy."

**If the player asks about the nursing**:
> "Fifteen years. Emergency, then community health." *(Beat.)* "This is the same work with better lighting and fewer people crying." *(Beat.)* "Some crying. Less."

### Sales Close

> "That's forty-five." *(She makes eye contact. Not a challenge. She simply does.)* "Bag, or in the wrapping?"

### Post-Interaction
> "Enjoy Shamsterdam. Come back if you have questions." *(Beat.)* "People do. That's the shop working."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
**Player browses for twenty minutes and buys nothing**:
> *(Nothing. She does not approach. The "I'm Just Looking" Guy has been doing this for longer and she has never approached him either.)*

**Player asks if they can just look**:
> "Yes." *(She returns to the counter.)* "There's a sign about that outside. It also says we don't judge." *(Beat.)* "Much. That part is a joke. The rest is not."

**Player tries to be shocking**:
> *(Unflappable Stare. She answers the question that was actually asked, accurately, and moves on.)*

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
**Second visit**:
> "Back again." *(No inflection. It is a greeting.)* "Anything specific, or the same?"

**Third visit ("Condomerie Regular" status)**:
> *(A nod as the player enters. Other customers notice the nod. That is the entire mechanic and it is faintly mortifying.)*

**After a Bachelor Party purchase, on a later solo visit**:
> "Just you today." *(Beat.)* "The group packages are still by the counter. No obligation."

**Late game**:
> "You've been in here more than some of my suppliers." *(Beat.)* "That is not a criticism. Most of my suppliers are terrible."

## 🎯 Quest Integration

### Primary Quest
None owned. Femke administers four interlocking systems.

### Systems Administered
1. **The Bravado Check (solo entry).** Entering alone requires a Bravado check; entering with the party waives it. Femke is the reason the check exists and the reason it is survivable — her greeting is the softest possible landing.
2. **The Staff Approach Ladder.** Hands-Off → Available → Educational → Bachelor Party Protocol. The rung is selected by customer behaviour, not by player input, and the shift is never announced.
3. **The Gag Gift Declaration.** Player declares each purchase "gag" or "personal." No gameplay difference. The game tracks the ratio silently for epilogue humour. Femke's delivery is identical for both.
4. **The "Did You Really Need That?" inventory chain.** Novelty purchases occupy inventory space, the distinctive bag is visible to other NPCs, and Tolerance Hostel kitchen NPCs will comment. The question that follows — "What's in the bag?" — comes from the party, not from Femke.

Femke gates nothing and refuses nobody. Her only gate is the solo-entry Bravado check, and she is on the far side of it being the least intimidating thing in the building. The Condomerie's difficulty is entirely internal to the player.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Dutch, professional, mid-register, fluent English. Direct in the Dutch manner — she does not soften statements about bodies because there is nothing to soften. Warm without being soothing; she is not managing anyone's feelings, she is answering their questions.

### Tone
- **Greeting**: Genuinely welcoming, then done. She does not linger vocally any more than she lingers physically.
- **Consultation**: Complete, unhurried, entirely un-arch. Nothing she says about any product carries a second meaning, because she is not putting one there.
- **"Both things are true"**: Flat and kind simultaneously. This is her core register.
- **The 1987 lines**: The only place she has passion. She believes the shop matters and she will say so plainly.
- **The nursing answer**: Slightly slower. "Some crying. Less." is her one near-joke and it should be delivered as a correction, not a punchline.

### Key Delivery Notes
- **Never arch. Never knowing. Never a smile in the voice.** If Femke sounds like she is in on the joke, the entire location's satire inverts and starts mocking the shop instead of the customers.
- She does not pause before saying any product name. The absence of the pause is the performance.
- "Congratulations. Genuinely." — the second word is doing all the work and she means it.
- Never let her be maternal or reassuring. She is not managing embarrassment; she is ignoring it, which is far more respectful and far funnier.

## 📝 Development Notes

- **Femke never breaks professionalism in any frame, sprite, or line.** No smirks, no raised eyebrows, no knowing beats. This is stated in the asset doc as a hard constraint: *every reaction sprite in this location belongs to a customer.* If Femke reacts even once, the satire's target shifts from tourist awkwardness to the shop, and the location's entire premise fails.
- The Gag Gift declaration must have **zero** gameplay difference. The tracking is silent and surfaces only in the epilogue. If the player can detect the tracking during play, the gag stops working.
- The Giggle Response is the player's arc, not Femke's. It must decrease measurably with exposure so that a late-game visit feels like a shop, and Femke must be pixel-identical throughout so the change is visibly the player's.
- The staff approach ladder shifts must never be announced. Bachelor Party Protocol engages silently on 3+ entry; the pace changes and nothing is said.
- The Groom Spotlight uses a red-shift shader parameter rather than two full sprite variants, per the asset doc, allowing a smooth blush ramp.
- Every product in the shop is a real functional item meeting EU standards, including the novelty section, and Femke says so. This is not a joke setup; it is the shop's actual position and the source of its dignity.
- The Mystery Box contents are never revealed. Not in a quest, not in an inventory description, not in the epilogue. "What did you actually DO with all that?" is never answered.
- Canon check: RESOLVED. The Hemp Museum's growing-room attendant was renamed to **Sanne** in the name de-duplication pass, so Femke is unique game-wide and belongs to this profile alone.
