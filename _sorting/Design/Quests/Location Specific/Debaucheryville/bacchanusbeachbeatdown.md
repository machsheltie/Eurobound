# Sidequest: The Bacchanus Beach Beatdown

## 📋 Quest Identity
- **Quest ID:** `debaucheryville_sidequest_bacchanus_beach_beatdown_01`
- **Display Name:** "The Bacchanus Beach Beatdown"
- **Satirical Subtitle:** "Three strikes and you're airborne"
- **Quest Giver:** Petra the Production Assistant @ Fauxst Beach sign-up table, The Bohemian Riviera
- **City:** Debaucheryville
- **Recommended Level:** 3-5
- **Estimated Duration:** ~25 minutes

---

## 🎭 Core Premise

### The Setup

**What the bros THINK is happening:**
> They have been scouted. Not approached — *scouted*. An international television production walked the entire Bohemian Riviera, looked at nine hundred college-age vacationers, and picked the three guys in cargo shorts. Clearly the producers were searching for a rare and specific specimen: the athletic-intellectual hybrid. The complete package. Body of a competitor, mind of a scholar, availability of a man with no return flight booked. This is the comeback moment. This is the tape that gets shown at the reunion.

**What is ACTUALLY happening:**
> Shady Productions runs a cheap catapult show on a fake beach and needs bodies. Contestants are strapped into port-o-potties bolted to a pneumatic arm. Three wrong answers launches you into The Bacchanus. Every question is asked in Czech, which nobody has hidden, because nobody has been asked. The fourth contestant is a 22-year-old national-team rower who was walking past with a gym bag and got waved over because the show needs someone who can actually answer. The bros were not scouted. They were standing near the sign-up table.

**Why it's funny:**
> Petra discloses every single thing. The language, the toilet, the catapult, the river, the water quality, the perpetual media waiver. She offers — twice — to read the release forms aloud in English. The bros sign them unread while flexing at a monitor they think is a camera, because a man who believes he's been scouted cannot hear a woman explaining the terms of his own launch. Nothing is hidden from the bros. Everything is hidden from the bros.

---

## 🎯 Satirical Targets
- **Primary Target:** Reality-TV exploitation formats — the MTV summer-special / *The Challenge* economy, where humiliation is the product, the contestant is the raw material, and the release form is the only honest document on set.
- **Secondary Targets:** Aging bros measuring themselves against college-age vacationers and grading on a curve only they can see; the belief that being *filmed* is the same as being *chosen*; sponsorship desperation (a host contractually drinking from three competing energy drinks at once).
- **Cultural Specificity:** The language arrogance of the monolingual tourist — nine questions in a language the bros have not attempted one word of in six days, decoded by vibes and hand gestures. Plus "beach culture" in a landlocked country: trucked-in sand, inflatable palms, space heaters, a lifeguard tower over water you could stand up in, and beach-resort pricing on a river embankment. The locals running it are not villains — they are entrepreneurs who correctly identified that tourists will pay to be launched.
- **Y2K/Era References:** EMTV camera cranes and the taped-VHS episode airing; Chadwick's ICQ chat as a live cheat channel staffed by trolls; the entire prize pool sourced from a defective 2003 merch pallet.

### Bro Select (Path Choice)

The player picks **ONE** bro to answer for. That bro is the one you actually play. The other two answer on autopilot — confidently, catastrophically — accumulate strikes without player input, and are launched at staggered intervals (see below). **Minimum two of three bros are always launched.** The quest cannot resolve without at least two airborne port-o-potties. This is not a difficulty setting. This is the format.

#### PATH A: Lord Pilsner — "The Legend Answers"

**What bros think they're doing:**
> Fielding their strongest intellectual asset. Lord Pilsner has an encyclopedic command of European history, culture, and geography, assembled over two decades from bar conversations, one documentary he fell asleep during, and pure conviction. He does not "guess." He *recalls*. The others are stepping aside out of respect.

**What they're actually doing:**
> Sending a 39-year-old man who thinks the Habsburgs invented the funnel into a nine-round quiz in a language he has not attempted a single word of in six days, because he shouted "I got this" fastest.

**Execution Steps:**
1. **Sign-up.** Pilsner signs all three release forms — his, and both of theirs — "to save time," having read none of them. Petra notes for the record that this is not legally how that works. He gives her a thumbs up.
2. **Strap-in.** He insists on being loaded into the center port-o-potty because "the center guy is the guy the show is about." The center unit is the center unit because its door latch is broken.
3. **Phase 1 — The Gauntlet.** Nine questions, three strikes. Pilsner shouts a confidently wrong answer first every round; the player then decodes and locks in the real one. *(See `minigame_beatdown_trivia_gauntlet`.)*
4. **Janek's thrown answer.** Around Q6, Janek deliberately misses one out of boredom. Pilsner interprets this as the tide turning.
5. **Phase 2 — Sudden Death.** Survive Phase 1 with fewer than three strikes and it's a head-to-head buzzer duel against Janek. *(See `minigame_beatdown_sudden_death`.)*

**Hard Mode Flavor — Drunk-Confidence Answers:**

Pilsner's automatic wrong answer each round is delivered as a **toast**. He raises an imaginary glass, waits for a crowd response that does not come, and then commits fully.

> "To the Emperors — who INVENTED the funnel — twelve hundred, final answer, and I'll fight the man who says otherwise."

> "Gonna go ahead and say Sinfonia. Not because of the question. Because it's always Sinfonia and nobody has the guts to say it."

> "The answer is 'the Old Empire.' Bro, I don't even know what the question was, but statistically? The Old Empire."

**Key Dialogue/Moments:**

*The port-o-potty door swings open mid-question because the latch is broken. Pilsner catches it with his foot without breaking eye contact with the camera.*

**Lord Pilsner:** "That was intentional. That's a signature move. Put that in the edit."

*Jaxson Vane consults his cue card, then Pilsner, then his cue card again.*

**Jaxson Vane:** "That is... not close. That is not in the same *category* as close."

**Lord Pilsner:** *to the crowd* "He's rattled."

**NPC Reactions:**
- **Jaxson Vane:** *lowering three cans simultaneously* "Ladies and gentlemen, the American has selected 'the Old Empire' for the second consecutive question, and I want to stress that neither question was about the Old Empire."
- **Janek Sýkora:** *quietly, to Petra, in Czech* "Should someone tell the sirs?" — **Petra:** "I told the sirs."
- **Vacationer in the crowd:** *filming on a flip phone* "Is he okay? Is this a bit? Is that man okay?"

#### PATH B: Chadwick — "Going Live"

**What bros think they're doing:**
> Deploying their audience. Chadwick's followers are, in his professional assessment, "a distributed intelligence network." Any one of them might know the answer. Collectively, they are unbeatable. This is not cheating. This is *leveraging a platform*, which is a thing real professionals do, and if the producers didn't want him to do it they shouldn't have given him a seat with signal.

**What they're actually doing:**
> Feeding a nine-round quiz to an ICQ room of eleven people, nine of whom are bots he paid for in 2004 and two of whom are actively hostile, while a host in indoor sunglasses watches him do it from forty feet away with nothing obstructing his view.

**Execution Steps:**
1. **Sign-up.** Chadwick signs while livestreaming the signing. Petra offers to read the form aloud. He asks her to say her name again "for the intro."
2. **Strap-in.** He asks the crew to mount his phone inside the port-o-potty. A grip does it. It is, genuinely, the best-mounted camera on the entire production.
3. **Phase 1 — The Gauntlet.** Identical nine questions, three strikes — plus the **"📡 GO LIVE"** button, visible on every question. *(See cheat spec below.)*
4. **Janek's thrown answer.** Chadwick posts about it in real time and gets ratioed by his own bots.
5. **Phase 2 — Sudden Death.** Same buzzer duel. **The GO LIVE button is disabled in Phase 2** — Jaxson personally unplugs the extension cord, on camera, without commentary.

**The Cheat: "📡 GO LIVE" (Chadwick exclusive)**
- Available on any Phase 1 question. Opens the ICQ overlay for 6 seconds.
- Chat returns crowd-sourced answers at **~70% reliability**. The other 30% is trolling, and the trolling is *confident*.
- Each activation ticks **Jaxson's Suspicion Meter** by one — **regardless of whether the answer was right.** He is not suspicious of cheating. He is suspicious of *the phone*.
- **3 activations = DISQUALIFICATION.** Immediate launch. **No consolation prize.** No Hamagotchi. Nothing.

**Suspicion Meter (UI):** rendered as Jaxson's Oakleys in the corner of the screen.
- **Tick 1:** He tilts them down a quarter inch. Says nothing.
- **Tick 2:** He lowers them to the tip of his nose. Still says nothing. Drinks from all three cans.
- **Tick 3:** He takes them off completely. His eyes are exactly as unimpressed as everyone expected. He walks to the lever.

**Sample ICQ Feed (Q1):**
```

dialup_deb: twelve. its always twelve
Tony420: say "banana" they LOVE that over there

```

**Key Dialogue/Moments:**

**Chadwick:** *whispering into the mounted phone* "Chat, chat, chat — I need this. Nobody troll. I'm serious. This is my LIVELIHOOD."

*Six seconds pass. Four of the eleven type "BANANA."*

**Chadwick:** *out loud, to a national broadcast* "BANANA."

**Jaxson Vane:** *long pause* "...Unfortunately, bro."

**On disqualification:**

**Jaxson Vane:** *takes the Oakleys off, sets them on the podium, walks to the lever* "House rules. No devices in the toilet."

**Chadwick:** "It's not a device, it's a PRESS CREDENTIAL —"

**Jaxson Vane:** "Unfortunately, bro."

*LAUNCH. No prize podium. No Hamstradamus. Hamstradamus is visibly holding a Hamagotchi and visibly puts it back in the box.*

**NPC Reactions:**
- **Petra:** "I did tell him about the devices rule. It is on page two. I offered to read page two."
- **Jaxson Vane:** *to the crane camera, warmly, in his imported-American voice* "That's why we love this show, folks. Everybody gets a swim."
- **Janek Sýkora:** *watching the launch, mildly* "He was talking to his telephone the entire time. I assumed it was a medical thing."

## 🎮 Minigame Specifications

### `minigame_beatdown_trivia_gauntlet`

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Questions** | 9, sequential, no skips |
| **Contestants** | 4, concurrent: the selected bro, the two non-selected bros, and Janek Sýkora. All four face the **same 9 questions on one shared clock** — this is a single broadcast round, not four parallel runs. |
| **Turn Structure** | Round-robin within each question. Jaxson reads the question once; the three bros answer in podium order (left → center → right) as a scripted stack of confidently wrong shouts; Janek answers last, briefly, without looking up. The player's answer window opens on the selected bro's turn and only on his turn. Once a non-selected bro is launched (Q4 or Q7), he is removed from the round-robin for all remaining questions — podium order simply skips his slot for the rest of the gauntlet. |
| **Non-Selected Bros** | Fully scripted, zero player input, no on-screen strike counter. Their strikes accrue invisibly and are **hard-timed, not simulated** — the first non-selected bro strikes out at **Q4**, the second at **Q7** (mid-sentence). Their answers are never correct on any question, on any playthrough. |
| **Janek** | Answers all 9 correctly **except one scripted thrown answer at ~Q6**, and **cannot be struck out** under any circumstance — he has no strike counter, no fail state, and no exit from the show before Phase 2. He is the wall, not an opponent who can be outlasted. |
| **Strikes** | 3 — third strike fires the catapult immediately, mid-question if necessary. **Only the selected bro's strike counter is player-affected**; it is the only one displayed. |
| **Language** | All questions delivered and displayed in Czech-parody. No subtitles. No translation toggle. |
| **Decode Channels** | (1) **Cognates** in the on-screen text; (2) **Props** Jaxson physically holds up; (3) **Host gestures** — he points, mimes, and counts on his fingers without realizing he's helping; (4) **Crowd reaction** — the vacationers audibly react to how absurd a question is, which narrows the category |
| **Answer Flow** | The selected bro shouts his confidently wrong answer first, unprompted, every round. THEN the player picks from four options. The bro's shout is always one of the four. |
| **Timer** | 12 seconds per question. Timeout = strike. |
| **Fail State** | 3 strikes → launch → LOSS branch |
| **Pass State** | ≤2 strikes after Q9 → Phase 2 |

> **Design note:** the bro's wrong shout being *one of the four selectable options* is the core joke of the minigame. Every round the player must actively decline to be their own character.

#### Sample Question 1 — Q1, the softball

**AS DISPLAYED (Czech-parody, no translation shown):**
> **"Kolik hvězdiček má vlajka Evropské Unie?"**

**Visual Clue:** Jaxson unfurls a small EU flag prop with one hand while holding all three energy drink cans in the other. He does not point at the stars. He does, however, absent-mindedly tap the flag twelve times against the podium while waiting.

**Decode Channels:** Cognate — *"Evropské Unie."* Prop — the flag itself. Gesture — the twelve taps, which are not intentional and which the crowd notices before the bros do.

**The bro's confidently-wrong shout:**
- **Lord Pilsner:** "FIFTY. Same as ours. That's how flags work, bro, that's the whole system."
- **Chadwick:** "Chat says twelve but chat also says banana, so — FIFTY."
- **Bradley:** "Fifty. Europe's got fifty states, they just don't advertise it."

**Correct Answer:** **Dvanáct.** (Twelve.)

**On correct:** *The crowd applauds. Pilsner assumes the applause is for him having been overruled gracefully.*
**On incorrect:** **Jaxson Vane:** "Strike one, and folks, that was the free one."

#### Sample Question 2 — Q3, the metric trap

**AS DISPLAYED:**
> **"Kolik decilitrů má velké pivo?"**

**Visual Clue:** Jaxson raises an empty half-liter glass to the crane camera and holds it there. The crowd, without being asked, begins a slow rhythmic chant of *"pět… pět… pět…"* — not to help, but because a crowd on a beach will chant anything.

**Decode Channels:** Cognate — *"decilitrů."* Prop — the glass, which is the exact volume being asked about. Crowd — the chant is literally the answer said aloud repeatedly.

**The bro's confidently-wrong shout:**
- **Lord Pilsner:** "A liter and a HALF. I know because I've had nine of 'em and I can still spell my name."
- **Chadwick:** "Whatever's bigger. Go bigger. Big numbers do numbers."
- **Bradley:** "It's a gallon. Metric gallon. Slightly smaller than a regular gallon, which is why you gotta drink more of 'em."

**Correct Answer:** **Pět.** (Five deciliters — half a liter.)

**On correct:** *The chant becomes a cheer. The bro raises both arms inside a portable toilet, which limits the gesture considerably.* **Bro:** "See, I let the crowd think they helped. That's leadership."
**On incorrect:** *The chanting stops. The silence is worse. Somewhere in the crowd, one person says "he was told."*

#### Sample Question 3 — Q8, the one that is a warning

**AS DISPLAYED:**
> **"Jak se jmenuje řeka, do které za chvíli spadneš?"**

**Visual Clue:** Jaxson gestures, in order, at: the river, the port-o-potty the bro is currently strapped inside, and then the river again. He holds the last gesture. The crowd is already laughing before the question is fully out, which is the loudest crowd reaction of the entire gauntlet — because the question is not a question, it is a courtesy.

**Decode Channels:** Gesture — the full sequence spells the whole sentence. Crowd — reacting to content, not difficulty. Cognate — *"řeka."* Prop — the river. The river is right there. The river has been right there the entire time.

**The bro's confidently-wrong shout:**
- **Lord Pilsner:** "The PUBLANDIA. Bro, all of them are the Publandia at some point, it's a whole system, it's like a subway."
- **Chadwick:** "THE SINFONIA. Every river in Europe is the Sinfonia, they just rename it per country for tourism reasons."
- **Bradley:** "There's no river. There's no river in this question. He's testing whether we'll invent a river."

### Catapult Animation
- The pneumatic arm needs **three independently-triggerable units** with staggered fire timing — the comedy depends on Launch One arriving as an interruption and Launch Two arriving mid-syllable. Do not build this as a single three-unit animation.
- **Jaxson never watches a launch.** His idle during every fire is "looking at his cue card." This is a rig constraint, not a suggestion — if his head turns toward the river the joke inverts and he becomes a participant instead of an employee.
- The center unit's **broken door latch** needs its own swing-open state, usable during Path A dialogue and at any point thereafter.

### Launch Physics Gag
- The parabola must be **too long and too graceful.** Real ballistics reads as an accident; this needs to read as engineering. The arc should visibly clear the inflatable palms and the lifeguard tower before the water.
- **Audio:** the impact needs a specific wet, hollow, resonant note — brief the sound designer that this is the show's theme music and it is reused, unchanged, every season. It should be recognizable on the bar TVs later at low volume through a CRT filter.
- **Speech-bubble cutoff at Q7 is frame-critical.** The bubble must terminate on a partial syllable and the audio must clip hard, then continue faintly over the water for ~1s at falling volume. Do not fade — the sentence keeps going, it just leaves.
- The screenshot button must remain live through the entire launch animation (see Mobile UX).

### Czech-Parody Question Rendering
- All nine questions display **untranslated, with no subtitle track and no translation toggle** — the decode is the minigame. Do not add an accessibility "reveal answer" option that shows the English; if an accessibility path is required, extend the timer instead.
- Diacritics must render correctly at mobile text sizes (**ě š č ř ž ý á í é ů**). Verify the UI font has full coverage before content lock; a missing glyph turns a joke into a bug report.
- The **prop, gesture, and crowd-reaction channels each need to independently carry enough signal** to solve the question — a player who mutes the game must still be able to win off props and gestures, and a player who isn't looking must still get the crowd chant.
- **The bro's wrong shout must always be one of the four selectable options.** This is the load-bearing joke of the whole minigame. Never present it as flavor-only text.
- All Czech-parody strings in this document are drafted for comic rhythm and cognate legibility. Flag for a **native-speaker pass before voice recording** — the humor targets the bros' failure to engage, never the language or its speakers, and a clumsy string would put the joke on the wrong side.

### Suspicion Meter UI (Path B only)
- Rendered as **Jaxson's Oakleys**, top-right, three states: tilted → lowered to nose-tip → removed. No numeric counter, no bar fill, no tooltip. The player should feel the third tick coming before the UI confirms it.
- The meter **ticks on activation, not on outcome.** A correct crowd-sourced answer costs exactly as much suspicion as "BANANA."
- Chadwick's viewer count reads **11** at all times, in all conditions. It never moves. The "(9 bots)" tag appears only in the closing frame of each ICQ overlay, small, unremarked.
- **GO LIVE is disabled in Phase 2** via the on-screen unplugging of an extension cord — animate the unplug, do not gray out the button. Jaxson does it himself and says nothing.
- Disqualification must fire the launch **immediately** on the third activation, before the question resolves. The player never learns whether the third answer was right.
