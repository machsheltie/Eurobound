# Sidequest: Club Flyers - "Peak Y2K Marketing Genius"

## 📋 Quest Identity
- **Quest ID:** `debaucheryville_sidequest_club_flyers_01`
- **Display Name:** "Peak Y2K Marketing Genius"
- **Satirical Subtitle:** "Synergizing the Paradigm One WordArt at a Time"
- **Quest Giver:** Vape Witch Vanessa @ Velvet Curtain Club
- **City:** Debaucheryville
- **Recommended Level:** 3
- **Estimated Duration:** 30-40 minutes

---

## 🎭 Core Premise

### The Setup
**What the bros THINK is happening:**
> Finally, someone recognizes their dormant marketing brilliance from their college days. Time to leverage their "dot-com era business knowledge" and show these Europeans what American hustle looks like.

**What is ACTUALLY happening:**
> Vanessa needs cheap labor to poster the city. The bros create flyers that look like they were designed in 1999 using GeoCities, Microsoft Publisher, and whatever clipart came free with Windows 98.

**Why it's funny:**
> Three 35+ year old men genuinely believe their understanding of marketing, technology, and "going viral" hasn't changed since they had Hotmail addresses. Every design choice screams 2001 and they think it's cutting-edge.

---

## 🎯 Satirical Targets
- **Primary Target:** People who peaked in the early 2000s and never updated their skills
- **Secondary Targets:** Outdated slang, Y2K aesthetic nostalgia, "guru" marketing speak, nu-metal era masculinity
- **Cultural Specificity:** American tourists assuming their approach translates everywhere
- **Y2K/Era References:** GeoCities, WordArt, Hotmail, AIM, Friendster, Kazaa, "synergy"
