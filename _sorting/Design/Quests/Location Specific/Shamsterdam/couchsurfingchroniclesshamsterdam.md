# Enhanced Couchsurfing Chronicles — Shamsterdam Edition

**Premise:** Meet the five most ridiculous women in Shamsterdam, none of whom work anywhere the bros went, all of whom are running something insane, and each of whom offers a "crash spot."
**Optional interiors** where the bros "crash" in a shipping container full of canal, on a stop of a commercial walking tour, on both sides of one canal at once, below decks on a vessel that has not moved since 2011, and on a fourth floor reached on foot behind a bicycle.
**Player must navigate awkward morning-after conversations** to get stat boosts (Charisma, Bravado) or hilariously bad debuffs (Hangover, Bicycle Trauma).
**1-2 opportunities to crash with NPCs per city** — Shamsterdam runs five.

> ## ⚠️ BINDING — WHAT A CHRONICLES HOST IS
> **Author ruling.** Couchsurf hosts are **purpose-built characters invented for this system**. They are personas, epithets and self-appointed ranks — *Miss Morphine*, *QUEEN Riviéra Renata*, *Vape Witch Vanessa*, *Backpack Girl Who Never Left*, *CryptoBro69's "Ex"*, *The Eternal Student*. They are not ordinary people with ordinary names and ordinary flats, and they are **never** repurposed city staff.
>
> **A rope hostess, a hostel day manager, a merchandise seller, a café server and a ticket-desk clerk previously occupied these five slots. All five were struck by author ruling.** They remain perfectly good NPCs in their own location files and **must not be re-promoted into this file by anybody, ever.** Chronicles hosts are written to make the bros look stupid; that is the entire reason each one exists.
>
> ### 🎮 TONE — THE EARTHBOUND REGISTER *(binding on every host in this file)*
> Author, verbatim: *"Our game is based off of EarthBound... We are operating within that type of spectrum where it's really out there. None of their characters were just average characters."*
> 1. **Mundane-modern setting, total absurdity played completely straight.** Nobody in Shamsterdam finds any of these five women strange. That is what makes them funny.
> 2. **One weird premise per character, committed to absolutely.** The premise IS the person — never a quirk bolted onto a normal person.
> 3. **Nothing explains itself.** No lore justification, no origin story, no wink.
> 4. **Deadpan delivery.** The stranger the character, the more matter-of-fact the presentation. Dutch bluntness is *flat*, never cruel.
> 5. **Each one has one inexplicable rule she will not bend** — nothing dries indoors, the tour never crosses a bridge, they have never been in the same room, life jackets below decks on a moored vessel, and she does not go through the tunnel — and **none of the five rules is ever explained to anybody, in any state, ever.**
> 6. **Memorable on sight.** If a host can be described as "a plausible person with an interesting job," she is wrong and must be reworked.

**House rule for this city:** in Publandia the bros are told the truth to their faces. In Debaucheryville they are let into places. In Sinfonia they are *received*. **In Shamsterdam they are processed.** Every host in this file is running a system that was working perfectly before they arrived and will be working perfectly after they leave. Nobody in this city is impressed, nobody in this city is offended, and nobody in this city has any reaction left to give them. It is not coldness. **The reflex is simply gone.**

> **Shamsterdam is the last stop on the itinerary — it is not the end of anything.** The player can leave and go back to any city at any time. See the routing ruling below. Nothing here is terminal, no state here is unreachable, and no Passport page is permanently blank.

## **Enhanced Gameplay Mechanics**

### **Trigger:** Accept an invitation to "crash" from NPCs encountered on a wharf at three in the morning, at stop eleven of a paid walking tour, on both sides of a canal simultaneously, on a boat that is not going anywhere, or at 18 km/h.

### **Mechanic:** Triggers a morning-after micro-scene with enhanced bro delusion dialogue and choice/random outcomes.

### **Enhanced Payoff Types:**
- **Positive:** +Charm, +Bravado, or minor items
- **Negative:** Status effects (Hungover, Bicycle Trauma, Canal Confusion, Contact High, Dutch Directness), minor Sovs loss, fines, or gag items
- **Mixed:** Flavor collectibles, achievement progress, or FlagMan reference items

### **Shamsterdam-Specific Rule — Everything Is Permitted and Everything Is Logged**
Publandia hosts say things. Debaucheryville hosts take things. Sinfonia hosts invoice things. **Shamsterdam hosts do all three and then write it in a book.** No Shamsterdam couchsurf is ever refused on moral grounds by anybody — there are no moral grounds left in this city, they were used up in 1987 — but every single one generates **a ledger number, a logbook line, a route amendment, a fee, or an exhibit reference**, and every one of those is correct and permanent.

### **Shamsterdam-Specific Rule — The Bikes Do Not Stop**
Every walk of shame in this file crosses at least one bike lane. **Bicycle Trauma** is applied on every morning-after exit, all five entries, no exceptions, and it stacks. Standing in a bike lane is 95 Sovs. Officer van den Berg does not care whose flat you just left, or whose container, or whose boat.

## **🎯 The Enhanced Satirical Hook**
