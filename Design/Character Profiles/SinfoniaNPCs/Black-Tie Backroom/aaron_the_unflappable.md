# NPC Profile: Aaron the Unflappable

*"The kings were humming, man. They wanted to stretch their legs."*

- **Internal Name**: Aaron — "the Unflappable" is what the room started calling him after last year's hand, and it stuck to him the way a nickname sticks to a man who has never once asked for one
- **Role**: Ambient Legend / Scripted Encounter NPC (spectator cinematic only — **never a playable opponent**)
- **Associated Quest**: `sinfonia_sidequest_the_kings_were_humming_01`

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Aaron is the best player in a room full of people who dress better than he does. He sits at the felt-covered corner table with a double White Russian in a crystal tumbler, a worn carton of candy cigarettes at his elbow, and a chip stack that goes up over the course of a night the way a tide goes up — not dramatically, not in a way anyone can point at, just relentlessly and in the correct direction. He plays entirely on feel. He does not count outs, he does not track ranges, and if you handed him a pot-odds chart he would use it as a coaster. He watches the table, decides the hand has a mood, and acts on the mood, and the mood is right often enough that the Backroom stopped questioning it a long time ago and simply built its evening around him.

Last year he took the biggest pot the room has recorded on pocket kings, called instantly, with a candy cigarette in the corner of his mouth. Nobody who was in the room that night has been able to describe it satisfactorily to anyone who wasn't. The house has stopped trying. Madame Karten refers to it as "the kings," with the definite article, as if there were only one pair of them ever dealt in Sinfonia.

He recently quit smoking. The candy cigarettes are the substitute, and he has built a full liturgy around them without noticing he was doing it: stir the drink with one, take a long dramatic drag off it, then bite the end off with a loud CRUNCH that lands in whatever silence the table happens to have, then draw a fresh one from the carton. The carton never seems to empty. He is a genuine connoisseur of junk food, holds strong and detailed opinions about wafer-to-chocolate ratios, and expresses all of them in a room that serves canapés on silver. He is kind to people after he cleans them out, unprompted and without a trace of performance, because that is simply what he does with the ninety seconds after a hand ends.

### What Bros Believe
The bros see a man in a slightly rumpled velvet dinner jacket drinking something with milk in it and eating candy at a poker table, and they diagnose him instantly and unanimously. **Chadwick**: *"Look at this guy. He's drinking melted ice cream and chewing on sugar sticks. He's weak."* From that read, everything follows: he is the soft seat, the tourist, the one the whole room has been feeding on and nobody has had the nerve to finish. Lord Pilsner identifies himself as the man with the nerve. They have seen Casino Royale. They know what a tell is. They will explain, at volume, exactly which tells they are reading off a man who is not producing any. When he calls their all-in without pausing to think, they read it as tilt. When he turns over pocket kings, they read it as luck. When he is kind to them afterward, they read it as respect — one operator to another, an acknowledgment that they came in heavy and made him sweat. They leave the Backroom broke, holding candy and a token, having concluded they were unlucky in a soft game and will correct it next time.

### The Comedy Gap
Everything the bros see is true and every conclusion they draw from it is wrong. He *is* drinking a White Russian. He *is* eating candy cigarettes. He *is* playing on nothing but feel, and he will tell them so, plainly, in advance, at conversational volume, before he takes their entire stake — and they will hear it as a man talking himself into a mistake. Aaron never bluffs them about who he is; he is the most honest person at that table by a distance, and the honesty is what buries them.

**Binding:** the room never explains him. No dealer mutters an aside, no local leans over to tip the player off, no narrator lands a verdict, no achievement string clarifies what happened. The felt speaks to Aaron and does not speak to anybody else, and the game holds that line permanently. The player watches the hand, watches the bros describe it wrong, and is trusted to see it. Any line of content that accounts for Aaron's success — a hidden skill stat, a wink, a "he's actually a pro" reveal, a Madame Karten explainer, a post-hand narrator summary — is a defect and must be cut on sight.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | Late 30s to mid 40s; nobody in the room has established which and nobody has asked |
| Build | Loose-limbed and restless — a man who is never fully still in the chair, shifting weight, bouncing a heel, rotating the tumbler a quarter turn at a time; not nervous, just running at a different frame rate than the room |
| Height | Average |
| Energy | Squirrely, warm, entirely unbothered |

### Clothing
- **Slightly rumpled velvet dinner jacket**: Deep burgundy, genuinely good, genuinely old, and worn like a bathrobe. It has been sat in. It will be sat in again. He meets the Backroom's dress code by a comfortable margin and observes it by none of its spirit, and Viktor has never once said a word about it.
- **Open collar, tie in the jacket pocket**: The tie is present. The tie has been present every night for years. It has never been around his neck.
- **Accessories**: A crystal tumbler that is never empty of a double White Russian; a worn carton of candy cigarettes with a soft, furred edge from being opened and closed several thousand times; a small drift of snack wrappers at his right elbow that the servers clear and that regenerates within the hour.
- **Footwear**: Formal shoes, correct, unpolished, and one of them is always tapping.

### Distinguishing Features
- **The ritual**: stir the drink with the candy cigarette → long dramatic drag → bite the end off with a loud CRUNCH → fresh one from the carton. Roughly every ninety seconds, in every state, including mid-hand and mid-sentence.
- The CRUNCH lands in silences. It has ended at least two arguments at other tables and interrupted one marriage proposal, none of which he registered.
- Chalky white ring at the corner of the mouth, permanent, refreshed constantly, never wiped away.
- A heel that bounces continuously and stops dead — completely, instantly — for about two seconds before he acts on a hand. Nobody has connected this to anything. It is not a tell, it is not a hint system, it is not decoded by any character or any mechanic, and it must never be labelled in-game.
- Total facial serenity at all times, in a room where everyone else's face is a full-time job.

### Sprite Notes
Read him as the most relaxed man in a room where relaxation costs €500. The velvet must be *good* and *rumpled* — never shabby, never a joke costume; he is not underdressed, he is comfortable, and the difference has to be legible at 64px. Keep the tumbler in frame in every state; keep the wrapper drift on the table. Priority frames are the ritual cycle (stir / drag / **crunch** / fresh draw) — the crunch frame is the character's signature and should be the strongest single pose in the sheet, mouth closing on the candy, eyes calm, chips untouched. Reference energy: the one person at a formal event who is having a completely normal evening. He is never drawn as a punchline, never slumped, never sloppy, never sweaty. Dignity is a rendering requirement.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Unbothered Local Legend** — a man who long ago stopped experiencing a poker table as an adversarial environment and now experiences it as a place he likes to sit. He is not hiding a system, not running a persona, not managing a table image. His attention moves quickly and lands wherever it wants: the hand, the snack, the light through the chandelier, the guy across from him who seems like he's having a rough week. He gives every one of those things the same full, brief, sincere consideration. The chips accumulate on their own, as far as he is concerned.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Vibes-Native | Plays entirely on feel and says so freely; describes hands in terms of temperature, mood, and sound, and means it literally, and is right |
| Dopamine-Forward | Attention arrives fast, leaves fast, and comes back; three conversations, one hand, and a chocolate wafer all held simultaneously without visible effort |
| Genuinely Kind | Checks on people after busting them, every single time, unprompted; the ninety seconds after he takes a stack are the warmest ninety seconds in the building |
| Immune to Provocation | Volume, insults, staredowns, and table talk land on him like weather; he doesn't rise, doesn't cool, doesn't respond in kind, and there is no version of him that gets rattled |
| Uncurated | Drinks a milk cocktail, eats gas-station candy, and wears his tie in a pocket in a room built entirely out of curation, and has never once framed any of it as a statement |

### Internal Monologue
> "Okay — okay, this one's warm. This one's got a hum on it. — Did I eat? I don't think I ate. There's the wafer ones behind the bar, Siegfried put two aside. — Loud guy's having a night. Loud guy's *having a night*, man. I should say something nice to him after. — Yeah. Yeah, the kings are up and they don't want to sit down. Call it."

*(Binding: this is the ceiling of his interiority. There is no second layer underneath it. He is not privately calculating, not privately amused at anyone's expense, and not privately anything other than exactly what he appears to be. Any pass that gives him a hidden analytical inner voice destroys the character.)*

### Motivation
Aaron likes the room. He likes the felt, the chandelier light, the four-in-the-morning quiet after the loud tables clear out, and the way a hand feels right before it goes his way. He is not building a bankroll toward anything, not chasing last year's pot, and not trying to be a legend — the legend is something the room did to him while he was sitting there. What he actively wants, most nights, is a good hand, a fresh drink, something crunchy, and for the person he just cleaned out to leave feeling okay about it. He gets all four with some regularity, which is why he keeps coming back.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**Player approaches the corner table for the first time, entry conditions met — this triggers `blacktie_event_kings_were_humming_01`:**
> "Hey. Hey — pull up, there's chairs. There's always chairs over here." *(stirs the tumbler with a candy cigarette)* "You guys want anything? They'll bring you anything. It's all built into the thing."

**If the party lingers before the hand begins:**
> "This corner's the good corner, man. The lights are softer. You can hear yourself think over here — well. You can hear *something* over here."

### Key Interaction Dialogue — The Read
*The bros arrive at the corner table and conduct their assessment out loud, six feet from the man they are assessing. Aaron is stirring his drink with a candy cigarette and is entirely aware they are talking about him.*

**Chadwick**: "Look at this guy. He's drinking melted ice cream and chewing on sugar sticks. He's weak."

**Aaron the Unflappable** *(taking a long dramatic drag off the candy cigarette; not remotely offended; genuinely offering)*:
> "It's a double. You want one? They do 'em right here, they don't skimp on the Kahlúa."

*He bites the end off. The CRUNCH lands in the exact gap where Chadwick expected a reaction. Chadwick reads this as fear.*

**Lord Pilsner** *(to the dealer, to the table, to Sinfonia)*: "Make the bet big. Show him what American capital looks like."

### The Vibes Speech
*Lord Pilsner has just narrated, at full volume, the three tells he believes he has read off a man who has not moved. Aaron listens to all of it, all the way through, with real interest, the way you'd listen to someone describe a dream they had.*

**Aaron the Unflappable** *(warm, unhurried, sincerely trying to be helpful)*:
> "You guys are calculating numbers. You're looking at the board like it's a math test. Poker isn't math, bro. You gotta FEEL the turn. You gotta let the felt speak to you."

*He draws a fresh candy cigarette from the carton. The bros have just heard a man announce that he has no idea what he is doing, and they have believed him, and they are correct, and it is going to cost them everything.*

**Chadwick** *(delighted, under his breath, not nearly quietly enough)*: "He's TILTING."

### The Call and the Reveal
*The 7-2 offsuit goes in. Every chip the party posted at the door goes with it. There is a pause of the length that television has taught the bros to expect.*

*There is no pause. Aaron calls before Lord Pilsner's hand has left the chips. He does not look at his cards again. He does not look at the pot.*

**Lord Pilsner** *(to Chadwick, thrilled)*: "He panicked. He PANIC-CALLED."

*Aaron turns over pocket kings.*

**Aaron the Unflappable** *(mild, explanatory, entirely satisfied that this constitutes an explanation)*:
> "The kings were humming, man. They wanted to stretch their legs."

*The dealer sweeps. The room does not react, because the room has seen this, and because the room stopped reacting some time last year.*

### The Sympathy Beat
*Three men are standing very still around an empty patch of felt. Aaron looks up from his stack and takes them in properly for the first time all night, and something in his face actually changes — this is the only moment in the encounter where he leads with anything.*

**Aaron the Unflappable** *(genuinely, gently, with no edge in it anywhere)*:
> "Aw, man. Aw — you guys look like a couple of doggies caught in the rain."

*Nobody at the table has ever been talked to like this after losing money and nobody knows what to do with it.*

### The Grants
*He slides the worn carton across the felt with two fingers.*

**Aaron the Unflappable**:
> "Here. Take one. It helps with the hand jitters when you lose your shirt. Don't smoke it, just bite the end off. You want a hit of my White Russian to wash it down? No? Suit yourselves."

*[ACQUIRED: `item_candy_smokes` — party, permanent]*

*Then he digs in the velvet pocket, past the tie, and comes out with a scratched aluminum token. He presents it the way you present something you believe will genuinely help.*

**Aaron the Unflappable**:
> "It's on the chain. Digital land, baby. She Thinks My Tractor's Sexy. Limited drop. Mint #42. Hold onto that—that's your ticket back to the top."

*[ACQUIRED: `item_tractor_nft` — party, permanent]*

**Chadwick** *(turning the token over, already filming it)*: "Bro. He's cutting us IN."

### Post-Interaction — The Exit
*The bros head for the stairs, reframing hard and out loud, already asset-rich. Aaron calls after them, warmly, without getting up, already stirring the next drink.*

**Aaron the Unflappable**:
> "No cab vouchers, bro. But if you walk fast enough, you stay warm. Good luck with the felt next time!"

*He means every word of this. It is the single most useful thing anybody says to them in Sinfonia.*

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*If the player walks away from the corner table before the scene arms, or leaves the Backroom without approaching:*
> "Yeah, no, all good. I'm here." *(bounces the heel, gestures at the room with the tumbler)* "I'm gonna be here."

*If the party is bounced at the door for insufficient collateral, Aaron is never seen at all — Viktor's refusal happens two floors and one staircase away from him, and Aaron never learns that anyone was turned away, because nobody has ever told him anything about the door.*

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Loose, warm, unhurried conversational English with no regional pin on it — the voice of somebody who has been in this room long enough that nobody asks where he's from and he has never volunteered it. "Bro," "man," and "baby" are load-bearing and land as affection, not affect. Never slick, never lounge-lizard, never a hustler's cadence. He is not doing a voice.

### Tone
