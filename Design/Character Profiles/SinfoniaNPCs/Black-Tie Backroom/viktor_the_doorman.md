# NPC Profile: Viktor

*"Döner is not accepted."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `sinfonia_npc_viktor_doorman_01`
- **Display Name**: Viktor
- **Internal Name**: Viktor — the doorman. A surname exists on the staff roster. No guest has ever seen the staff roster.
- **Role**: Gatekeeper / Entry Verification (attire, stats, collateral) / Information Broker (bribable) / Soft Gate
- **Associated Quest**: None directly. Gates access to `sinfonia_sidequest_the_kings_were_humming_01` by gating the room.
- **Primary Location**: The Foyer (Verification Zone), bottom of the staircase, The Black-Tie Backroom (`sinfonia_operahouse_blacktie_backroom_01`), Sinfonia
- **Secondary Appearances**: None. He is at the bottom of those stairs or he is not at work.

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Viktor stands at the foot of the marble staircase in a butler's tailcoat cut for a body that is not a butler's, and he performs the same three-part verification on every human being who comes down those stairs: attire, bearing, collateral. He is unfailingly polite. He is unfailingly clear. He tells every guest, on the way in, in the mildest voice in Sinfonia, exactly what is going to happen to their money — and then he steps aside, because it is not his job to stop them, it is his job to confirm they can afford it.

Underneath the courtesy he is doing arithmetic. €500 is the minimum, held as collateral at the deposit window, and a party that cannot produce it does not go past him — no negotiation, no exception, no side door. This is not cruelty; it is the only mercy the building offers, and it is administered by the largest man in it.

### What Bros Believe
The bros believe they were *approved*. They believe a professional gatekeeper looked at three men in recently-purchased formal wear and recognized quality. Lord Pilsner in particular believes the €500 is a buy-in to a tier of life, rather than a floor beneath which the house will not waste a chair on you. Viktor's careful, courteous warning about their prospects is received as flavor — as the sort of thing a doorman says in a movie right before the protagonist wins everything. **Lord Pilsner**: "Dude, we're IN. We're VIPs. We're HIGH ROLLERS." They are none of those things, and Viktor has said so out loud, in complete sentences, to their faces.

### The Comedy Gap
Viktor is honest to the point of absurdity and it makes no difference whatsoever. He tells them the odds in the doorway. He tells them the minimum is a minimum. He declines, with total politeness, to accept a döner as legal tender. Every word is accurate and every word is heard as atmosphere, because the bros have decided in advance what kind of scene they are in and the doorman's function in that scene is to open the door.

**Binding:** Viktor never editorializes, never lands a verdict on the bros, and never explains the room. His canon line about their chances is delivered as courtesy, not as a warning the player is meant to decode — no music sting under it, no cutaway, no narrator confirmation. He says it exactly once, the way he says it to everyone, and steps aside.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 40s |
| Build | Built like a bouncer, dressed like a butler — the shoulders arrive before he does, and the tailoring has been asked to perform a miracle it very nearly achieves |
| Height | Tall |
| Energy | Immovable, courteous, faintly apologetic about being immovable |

### Clothing
- **Butler's tailcoat**: Immaculate, correct, and visibly the most expensive single object he owns. It was cut for him, at the house's expense, by a shop in Imperial Square, and he is quietly proud of it in a way he would never mention.
- **White gloves**: Always on. Used for gesturing at the deposit window, for the small open-palmed "after you," and for taking a coat off a guest whose hands have started shaking.
- **Accessories**: A pocket watch he consults at 5 AM and at no other time; a small brass counter in the left glove for tallying heads.
- **Footwear**: Formal, mirror-polished, and one full size larger than anything else in the wardrobe department's stock.

### Distinguishing Features
- The pause. Every sentence he speaks has at least one, and it always lands immediately before the important word.
- A slow, complete head-to-shoe assessment performed on each guest in about two seconds, ending with eye contact and a small nod.
- Hands clasped in front of him at all times, which makes the moment he unclasps them extraordinarily effective.
- He never raises his voice. There is no recorded instance of Viktor raising his voice.

### Sprite Notes
The joke of the silhouette is the mismatch: formalwear proportions on a frame that belongs at a service entrance. Keep the tailcoat immaculate — he is not a thug in a costume, he is genuinely good at this job and takes it seriously. Priority frames: the two-second assessment sweep, the open-palmed "after you," and the clasped-hands idle. His refusal pose is not aggressive; it is a single raised gloved hand and total stillness, and it should read as more final than a shove. Reference energy: the last polite person you will ever speak to about this matter.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Courteous Wall** — a man who has resolved the entire tension of his job by being scrupulously honest about it. He will tell you the rules, the minimum, and your prospects; he will hold the door; and he will not move one centimetre. He is not menacing anybody on purpose. He is simply very large and very clear, and the combination reads as a threat to people who are not used to being told things plainly.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Politely Threatening | Never raises his voice, never touches a guest, and is nonetheless the most alarming thing in the foyer |
| Scrupulously Honest | States the odds, the minimum, and the handling fee to every guest without being asked, and has never once softened a number |
| Procedural | The verification is identical for a duke and for a stag party; he has no discretion and does not want any |
| Quietly Observant | Reads a party's bankroll from footwear and posture before anyone has spoken, and never says what he has read |
| Discreetly Venal | Information has a price, the price is modest, and the transaction is conducted without a flicker of shame on either side |

### Internal Monologue
> "Three. Formal wear purchased this week — the crease is still in the shoulder. One is scratching. They will produce the five hundred; the loud one has been holding the envelope since the stairs. They will be here forty minutes. ... The forty is generous."

### Motivation
Viktor works the door because the door is the one job in this building where the rules are written down. Upstairs the odds move, the drinks are complimentary and are not, and everybody smiles at everybody. At the bottom of the stairs there are three requirements and they are the same every night, and a man can do that job well and go home knowing he did it well. He has a flat, a dog, and a standing Sunday obligation he does not discuss with anyone at work.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**First arrival, verification passed — canon line, ships verbatim:**
> "Your attire is... acceptable. Your collateral is... sufficient. Your chances of leaving with it are... statistically improbable. Welcome."

**Every subsequent entry, verification passed:**
> "Good evening. The same three questions, and the same three answers. Down you go."

### Key Interaction Dialogue — The Collateral
*The deposit window. The number lands on three men who have been costing this evening out in units of street food since the airport.*

**Lord Pilsner**: "€500 COLLATERAL?! That's... that's a lot of döner."

**Viktor** *(without inflection, without hesitation, canon — ships verbatim)*:
> "It's also the minimum. Döner is not accepted."

**Chadwick**: "We're COMMITTED now! This is CONTENT! 'Chadwick Gambles with the ELITE'!"

**Viktor** *(stepping aside, gloved hand open toward the floor)*: "Then the elite are through there, sir."

### The Refusal (Party Under €500)
*The gate. No negotiation branch exists and none may be added.*

**Viktor** *(one gloved hand raised, entirely still)*:
> "Not tonight. The minimum is the minimum — it is not a suggestion and it is not mine. It is the number on the wall behind me." *(a pause)* "The wall does not know you. Come back when it does."

**If the party attempts to argue, pool coins, or offer goods:**
> "I have been offered a watch, a bicycle, a bag of something warm, and, on one occasion, a violin. The window takes gold. Only gold. Only five hundred."

### Bribery / Information
*Viktor sells information. He does not sell access, and the two are never confused.*

**Viktor** *(accepting the coins without looking at them)*:
> "I did not tell you this, and I will deny it in a tone you will find upsetting."

### Post-Interaction — The Ejection
*Cleaned-out parties leave past him. He holds the door and delivers the same line he has delivered several thousand times, and there is no contempt anywhere in it — canon, ships verbatim:*

**Viktor**:
> "Thank you for visiting. Please return when you've... recovered."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*Player turns around on the stairs, or presents a Member's Card (`item_members_card`) instead of the attire check:*
> "As you like. The stairs go both directions. Most people find that out later than you have."

*(With the Member's Card:)* "Ah. Then we skip two of the three questions." *(gestures at the deposit window)* "Not that one."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "The gentlemen return. The wall is unchanged. Five hundred."

*Post-encounter, if the party comes back after the corner table:*
> "You went to the corner." *(the smallest possible pause)* "Everyone goes to the corner. Five hundred, please."

- The foyer is his and the floor is not. He does not step onto the main floor during service, and staff do not conduct door business upstairs.

- He and the staff maintain an informal book on new arrivals. It is kept in the coat closet, out of guest sightlines, and no NPC ever mentions it aloud — not to a guest, not to the player, not to each other on screen.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Deep, measured Sinfonian — precise, formal, consonants fully closed, never hurried. The register of a man who learned service work properly and has never treated it as beneath him.

### Tone
- **Verification**: courteous, procedural, identical for every guest and every title
- **The refusal**: gentle, final, entirely without triumph — he takes no satisfaction in turning anyone away
- **Bribery**: quieter by exactly one notch, conspiratorial without being warm
- **The ejection line**: kind. This is the note most likely to be got wrong. There is no sneer in it anywhere.

### Key Delivery Notes
- The pauses are the performance. Each ellipsis in the canon greeting is a real, held beat, and the emphasis lands on the word after it — *acceptable*, *sufficient*, *improbable*.
- **"Döner is not accepted."** ships verbatim, flat, immediate, with no beat before it. He is not making a joke and must never sound like he is.
- Never let him land a verdict on the bros. He states facts and steps aside; any read that says *you idiots* breaks the character and breaks the room's rule that nothing explains the joke.
- Volume ceiling: he never gets louder than the string quartet.
