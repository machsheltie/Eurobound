# NPC Profile: Madame Karten

*"Darling, you seem TENSE. Another drink? The cards favor the relaxed player."*

## 📋 NPC Identity

- **Display Name**: Madame Karten

- **Role**: Floor Manager / Game Oversight / Rule Enforcement / Ambient Encouragement (upsell)
- **Associated Quest**: None owned. Present on the floor during `sinfonia_sidequest_the_kings_were_humming_01`; oversees the cheating system and the Herr Falsch escalation.

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Madame Karten runs the floor. She knows the house edge on every table to the decimal, she knows which of tonight's guests are on a run and which are on a slide, and she knows — usually within a hand or two — which of them is about to make the decision that costs them the evening. Her job is to make sure that decision is comfortable. She brings the drink. She admires the run of luck. She mentions, warmly, that there is a table with a bit more room at it.

She is not lying to anybody. She never states a false fact about the odds; she simply arranges the temperature of the room so that people bet more, and she is superb at it. She also genuinely likes most of her guests, remembers their grandchildren's names, and has personally walked three regulars to the door over the years and quietly told them not to come back. Both of those things are true of her at the same time, permanently, and neither cancels the other.

### What Bros Believe
The bros believe Madame Karten has taken a shine to them. She is the first person in Sinfonia to be openly, uncomplicatedly delighted by their existence, and they read that delight as recognition. **Chadwick**: "WE'RE UP! WE'RE ACTUALLY UP!" — and there she is, thrilled for him, suggesting a larger table, because the cards seem to favor him tonight. Lord Pilsner concludes he has an ally in management. Chadwick concludes he is being scouted. Both concepts are load-bearing for the rest of their night.

### The Comedy Gap
Every single thing she says to them is warm, sincere in its warmth, and precisely calibrated to increase the size of their next bet — and none of those three facts is in tension with the others. She is a grandmother and she is a professional, and the bros can only process one of those at a time, so they process the grandmother.

**Binding:** the house's business model is visible to any player who looks at the room, and no character ever articulates it. Madame Karten never confesses, never winks, never has a rueful moment at the bar, and no aside of hers is ever heard by a bro or by the player as a confession. The italic gloss in the location file is a design annotation and is **not** a spoken line — it is never voiced, subtitled, or rendered in-game.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 60s |
| Build | Small, upright, and completely undiminished; moves through a crowded floor without ever appearing to hurry or to be blocked |
| Height | Short |
| Energy | Warm, delighted, and absolutely nothing gets past her |

### Clothing
- **Floor-length evening gown, midnight blue**: One of four, rotated by night of the week. Immaculate. Twenty years old and cared for like a violin.
- **Pearls**: Real, single strand, worn every night, touched exactly once per shift and never at a random moment.
- **Accessories**: Reading glasses on a fine chain, used for looking at chips and never at cards; a small black notebook nobody has ever seen opened.
- **Footwear**: Low, silent, and chosen for eleven hours on marble.

### Distinguishing Features
- The eyes. She is looking at your face, and she has already counted your stack, your tells, and the two chips your friend palmed forty minutes ago.
- Touches a guest's forearm when she arrives at a table — light, brief, grandmotherly, and it stops conversation every time.
- Never turns her back on the room; she circulates in a way that always keeps the maximum number of tables in her sightline.
- A specific, radiant smile that appears the instant a guest wins something, and it is not fake, and it is also not free.

### Sprite Notes
Elegant older woman, small in frame and enormous in authority. She must never be drawn as sinister — no shadowed eyes, no predatory posture, no cackle. The whole effect depends on her looking exactly like a delighted grandmother in an expensive gown, so that the player's growing awareness of what she is doing comes entirely from watching her work rather than from the art. Priority frames: the forearm touch, the radiant win-smile, and the two-second stillness she does when she has spotted a cheat and has not yet decided when to act. Reference energy: the most welcome person at the party, who is also the reason the party is going to cost you money.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**Grandmother's Warmth, Shark's Instincts** — a woman who has spent forty years on gambling floors and has genuinely never stopped enjoying the company. The affection is real. The arithmetic underneath it is also real, and runs continuously, and has never once made an error. She would tell you the house edge if you asked her directly; nobody ever asks her directly.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Genuinely Warm | Remembers names, illnesses, grandchildren, and which guest cannot have shellfish; none of this is a technique, and all of it is useful |
| Total Table Awareness | Tracks every stack, every tell, and every hand on her floor simultaneously, and has never been observed to check |
| Never False | Does not state incorrect facts about odds or games; steers by temperature, drink, and suggestion, which is entirely different and entirely legal |
| Immovable on Rules | Catches cheats early, watches them for a while, and enforces the escalation exactly as written, warmly, with no anger in it |
| Privately Merciful | Has, a handful of times in twenty years, ended somebody's membership for their own sake, at the door, quietly, and never told anyone why |

### Internal Monologue
> "The loud one is up two hundred and has already decided it is skill. He will go to the bigger table if I ask him twice, and I will only need to ask once. — The young man at four is palming. Poorly. Let him do it twice more so there is no argument about it later. — Somebody bring Herr Zittrig a water; he is going to need one and he will not ask."

### Motivation
She loves the floor. Forty years in and the room still turns her on — the noise, the felt, the moment a table changes mood all at once. She runs it beautifully because running it beautifully is the thing she is best at in the world, and she intends to be doing it until the night she cannot. She is well paid, unsentimental about the money, and has no ambition to own the place; owning it would mean leaving the floor.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**First arrival on the main floor:**
> "New faces! Oh, how lovely. Come in, come in — the far tables are friendlier, the near tables are faster, and the champagne is complimentary, which means somebody has already paid for it. Enjoy yourselves."

**To a guest who is losing quietly, arriving at their shoulder with a fresh glass — canon line, ships verbatim:**
> "Darling, you seem TENSE. Another drink? The cards favor the relaxed player."

### Key Interaction Dialogue — The Early Win
*Chadwick has won a hand and has begun making an announcement about it.*

**Chadwick**: "WE'RE UP! WE'RE ACTUALLY UP!"

**Madame Karten** *(arriving instantly, radiant, entirely sincere in her delight — canon, ships verbatim)*:
> "How wonderful! Perhaps a larger table? The cards seem to favor you tonight..."

**Lord Pilsner** *(to Chadwick, out of the side of his mouth, thrilled)*: "Management's WATCHING us."

**Madame Karten** *(who is, and who says so without a flicker)*: "I watch everyone, sir. It is the whole of the job."

### The Advice
*She gives advice constantly and it is always, technically, true.*

- **On a cold table**: "Tables have moods. That one is sulking. There is a warmer one by the pillar." *(There is not a warmer one. There is a table by the pillar.)*
- **On a hot streak**: "You must never argue with an evening that has decided to be kind to you."
- **On a bad beat**: "Oh, that was cruel. That was genuinely cruel." *(She means it. She has seen ten thousand of them and this one was cruel.)*
- **On the complimentary drinks**: "Take it, take it. Everything in this room is complimentary except the chips."

### Rule Enforcement — Catching a Cheat
*She has known for two hands. She arrives at the shoulder, hand light on the forearm, smile unchanged, voice pitched for exactly one listener.*

**First offense**:
> "Darling. Your sleeve." *(a pause of perfect length)* "Play the hand you were dealt, and we will both pretend I was somewhere else this evening."

**Second offense** *(same warmth, no anger anywhere in it)*:
> "That is the second time, and the second time is not mine to forgive. Twenty-four hours. Your chips stay with the house. Go home, sleep, and come back Thursday — you are not a bad man, you are simply a very poor cheat."

**Third offense** *(she does not raise her voice; she simply stops walking)*:
> "No. Not tonight and not again. Viktor will see you out. Do not make him do it properly."

### Post-Interaction — The Cleanup
*The party is broke. She is at the rail, and she is not gloating, because she has never once gloated at anybody.*

> "Oh, my dears. Sit for a moment before the stairs — the stairs are worse when you take them fast." *(she signals for water, not champagne)* "There. Now. You will not believe me tonight, but this was an inexpensive evening."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*Player refuses her table suggestion or leaves early with winnings intact:*
> "Sensible. Genuinely — I say that to about four people a year and I have said it to you." *(and she means it, and she will not mention it again, and she will be equally pleased to see you back)*

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "There you are. The pillar table missed you." *(She remembers your name from here on. She remembers everyone's name from here on.)*

*Post-encounter, if the party returns after the corner table:*
> "Ah. You met Aaron." *(a small, fond smile at nothing in particular)* "Yes. The kings." *(She says nothing further about it, in this or any later conversation, and no branch exists in which she elaborates.)*

*High Roller status achieved:*
> "The rope comes down for you now. Do try to remember that the rope is the only thing that changes."

### Territory / Social Dynamics
- The floor is hers absolutely: dealers, servers, tempo, and the escalation ladder for cheats all run through her, with no appeal.
- Servers report guests' tells to her, not to the dealers, and the reporting is invisible — a tray angle, a glance at the bar.
- She never overrules Viktor at the door and Viktor never overrules her on the floor. In twenty years this arrangement has been tested twice and held both times.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Warm, cultivated Sinfonian with a slight old-fashioned formality in the sentence shapes — she says "my dears" and "darling" constantly, and both land as genuine. Never arch, never theatrical.

### Tone
- **Greeting and circulating**: delighted, hostess-warm, faster than the room
- **The upsell**: identical to the greeting. There must be no audible gear-change, ever — this is the single most important note on the character.
- **Enforcement**: the same warmth at half the volume, pitched for one person, with no anger in it at any stage
- **Late shift**: softer, slower, and kind; the water beat is played completely straight

### Key Delivery Notes
- **"Darling, you seem TENSE. Another drink? The cards favor the relaxed player."** ships verbatim, with the emphasis exactly where the canon puts it, and the italic gloss in the location file is **never spoken**.
- **"How wonderful! Perhaps a larger table? The cards seem to favor you tonight..."** ships verbatim, delivered as pure delight.
- She has no sinister register. There is no line in this profile that should be read with a predatory edge on the voice; the edge lives entirely in what she does, never in how she sounds.
- Casting note: cast for genuine warmth and cast an actress who can hold a smile through the enforcement lines without curdling it.

- **The italic gloss is not dialogue.** `the_black_tie_backroom.md` annotates her canon line with a design aside; that aside is never voiced, subtitled, captioned, or rendered in any form. Her spoken text is the spoken text.
