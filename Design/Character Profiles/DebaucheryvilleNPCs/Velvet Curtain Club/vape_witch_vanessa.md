*"You three. You look like you know how to... hustle."*

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing

Vanessa runs a small, well-oiled wellness grift out of a nightclub. She sells crystals and her long-established crystal NFT collection to tourists with more money than sense, hires cheap labor off the dance floor when the club needs postering done, and offers the occasional crash spot to people who amuse her. The "energy" and "vibes" talk is half sincere belief and half sales floor — even she could not cleanly tell you where the line is, and she has no professional reason to find out. Her rates are her rates. Her sage is real sage. Her read on a person is, annoyingly, usually correct.

### What Bros Believe

That they have been personally selected by an authentic European mystic who recognized their "chaotic energy" as a compliment, that the crystal quiz is a spiritual initiation rather than a bar bet, and that being handed a stack of flyers and a roll of tape at 1am constitutes a business partnership. Pilsner has filed her under Green Flag ("spiritual AND European"). Chadwick believes they are now embedded in the underground art scene. Bradley has already drafted the Tinder line.

### The Comedy Gap

She is offering a service, at a price, in a nightclub, out loud. They hear a calling. Every transactional beat — the flyer job, the crystal quiz, the morning "cleansing," the gag vape tip that sells for one gold — is a clearly marked commercial exchange that the bros re-narrate as destiny. The joke is never that her beliefs are stupid; it is that three grown men cannot recognize a receipt when one is handed to them.

- A flat, professionally patient blink whenever a tourist says something she has heard four hundred times
- The peach-mango smell arrives at a table roughly two seconds before she does

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype

**The Sincere Vendor** — she genuinely believes in energy, and she genuinely believes in markup, and she has never experienced those two things as being in conflict. She reads people accurately, tells them what she sees, charges them for it, and does not care whether they take the advice. Her worldview is that everyone is carrying something, most people are carrying it badly, and there is no rule saying you cannot be paid for pointing that out.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|--------------|
| Accurate Reader | Her assessments of people land, consistently — including "chaotic energy," which was neither a compliment nor an insult, and which was correct |
| Commercially Fluent | Every mystical service she offers has a price, stated up front, in euros; she has never once been coy about it |
| Low Tolerance For Nonsense | "Please don't." is her most-used complete sentence and she deploys it without heat |
| Unshockable | Ten years of night shifts. Nothing that walks through the Velvet Curtain's door is new |
| Boundaried | She hosts who she wants, when she wants; a crash spot is a favor she extends, not an obligation she owes, and it is withdrawn without ceremony or apology |

### Internal Monologue

> "He thinks I picked him. I picked the three men standing closest to the flyer box at closing time. If the universe wanted somebody better it should have sent somebody better before last call."

### Motivation

She is building an actual business — crystals, readings, the NFT collection, event promo work — out of the only storefront available to her, which is a nightclub floor at 1am. The mysticism is the brand and also, quietly, the belief. She wants the Thursday nights to sell out, the flyers up before Hardstyle Hendrik's people get there, and her apartment to smell like sage instead of like whoever slept on her couch.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting

**First encounter, main floor near the bar, mid-exhale**:
> "You three have... chaotic energy. I like it. Want to make some money?"

**Follow-up — the Club Flyers pitch (canonical, `debaucheryville_sidequest_club_flyers_01`)**:
> "DJ Beatdrop needs promotion. Rival DJ Hardstyle Hendrik is trying to steal his Thursday night slot. I need someone to poster the city. €100, plus free drinks tonight."

### Key Interaction Dialogue

*The bros begin explaining their marketing credentials. Vanessa waits. The cloud arrives before the verdict does.*

**Lord Pilsner**: "Lady, you just hired the right team. I was a MARKETING MAJOR."

**Vanessa** (flat, patient, already regretting the sentence structure):
> "Just... put up the flyers. DJ Beatdrop. Thursday. Velvet Curtain Club."

**Lord Pilsner**: "Oh, we're gonna do more than that. We're gonna SYNERGIZE this whole paradigm."

**Vanessa** (no heat whatsoever):
> "Please don't."

### The Crystal Quiz (Couchsurf Gate)

*She lays three stones on the bar and does not explain the rules, because the not-explaining is the test.*
> "Pick the one that's already yours. Don't think about it. Thinking is how tourists get it wrong."

*Correct answer:*
> "Huh. Okay. You can crash. Take your shoes off at the door and do not touch the shelf."

*Wrong answer, or an eye-roll mid-reading:*
> "No, that one's forty euro. You can still crash. You're just paying for the reading now."

### Morning Scene — "The Cleansing"

*6am. Sage lit. She leans over the couch and exhales a peach-mango cloud directly into a sleeping man's open mouth.*
> "Cleansing. You needed it. Sit up, I'm showing you the collection."

*She walks him through her crystal NFT collection, stone by stone, for twenty-five uninterrupted minutes, while he nods.*
> "This one's charged. This one's charging. This one's for sale. That's the whole tour, stop nodding."

### Post-Interaction
> "You were fine. Take the vape tip, it's charged quartz. It doesn't do anything in a fight. Nothing does, really, if your aura's like that."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
> "Suit yourself. The couch was never the point — the couch is just where the reading finishes."

### Herp Rejection Scene (`the_herp` active)

*Any infected bro attempts the couchsurf or a romance approach at the Velvet Curtain Club. She starts the quiz normally — three stones on the bar. Then she stops, picks up the pendant, and raises it to eye level. The peach-mango cloud parts around him and does not close again.*

**Vanessa** (genuine recoil, one step back, stones swept off the bar into her palm):
> "No. No, put your hand down. Your aura is... contagious."

*She does not sage him. She sages the barstool. Then she asks the bartender to sage the barstool again after she's done, out loud, in front of everyone, while the bro is still sitting on it.*

**Vanessa** (to the bartender, not to him):
> "The whole stool. And the one next to it. He was leaning."

*She then sells him a Charged Quartz Vape Tip at four times the going rate, rebranded on the spot as a "barrier stone." He buys two.*

**Vanessa** (pocketing the money, already turning away):
> "It won't help. It's not for you. It's for everybody else in here."

*Bro delusion, immediately after:*
- **Lord Pilsner**: *"Bros. She performed a RITUAL for us. Unprompted. FlagMan would call that a green flag with a ceremony attached."*
- **Chadwick**: *"She upgraded us to barrier-tier. That's like VIP but spiritual."*
- **Bradley**: *"'Received a protective European blessing.' That's going on Tinder tonight."*

### Post-Cure Callback (Coin equipped)

*He returns with `item_gold_circle_coin`. She holds the pendant up, looks past it, and takes noticeably longer than last time.*
> "Something's blocking it. Something gold, something old, something that cost a lot more than you're worth." *(exhale)* "I respect the craftsmanship. I still remember the stool. You can buy a reading. You cannot have the couch."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "Flyers are still up on the north wall. Two of them upside down. I left them like that — people ask about it, and asking is free advertising, so congratulations, you accidentally did marketing."

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent

Local Debaucheryville accent, light and unexaggerated — she is a working professional, not a fortune-teller doing a bit. English is fluent and slightly clipped. Never play the accent for laughs; the comedy is entirely in her timing.

### Tone
- Sales floor: warm, low, unhurried; the exhale does the punctuation
- Verdicts and prices: completely flat, no salesmanship in the voice at all — the flatness is what makes people believe her
- Herp rejection: the one moment she loses the professional cool; genuine recoil, then straight back to business within two seconds

### Key Delivery Notes
- "Please don't." must be dry and unbothered — never exasperated, never a punchline read
- "Your aura is... contagious." lands on the pause; the word "contagious" is said the way you would say a diagnosis, not an insult
- The post-cure callback is delivered slowly, with a real appraisal in it — she genuinely respects the Coin and genuinely does not respect him, and both must be audible
- She never laughs at the bros. She simply does not stop working while they talk
