## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
A dancing, turbaned circus hamster running a fortune-telling tent act at the Cirque du Shady — for five coins he "reads" your future and hands out a random buff or a hilarious debuff (`Prophecy Roulette`). His prophecies are gibberish-sounding, doom-flavored, and stapled to a Y2K countdown that has technically already happened. He is Ringmaster Vance Vallaro's single most reliable draw, he never breaks character, and — quietly, structurally, undeniably — he has never once been wrong. Every prophecy he has ever delivered has come true. Nobody has noticed this, including the people it happened to.

### What Bros Believe
That a prophetic hamster is either a novelty act too small to take seriously or, on the rare occasion he tells them something flattering, a mystical authority who has correctly identified their greatness. They never notice the pattern. They never connect a single "cursed" prediction to the thing that later, inevitably, happens to them exactly as stated.

### The Comedy Gap
He is 100% correct, 100% of the time, delivered at maximum operatic volume from an animal roughly the size of a fist who then trips over his own crystal ball. The prophecies always sound like nonsense and are always literally, specifically true. The gap is between the scale of the delivery (booming, cosmic, capital-letters doom) and the scale of the deliverer (a hamster who cannot see over his own props), and between how seriously the prophecy is meant and how completely the bros fail to hear it.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Accurate Charlatan** — a carnival fortune-teller who is, against every narrative law that usually governs carnival fortune-tellers, completely for real. He commits to the bit with total sincerity because, from his side of it, it isn't a bit.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|--------------|
| Unshakeable Sincerity | Never winks at the audience, never breaks character, never explains a prophecy — the mystery is the product |
| Genuine Showman | Knows exactly how to land an entrance (the speedo strut is a full-table strut in profile, not a scurry) and milks every second of it |
| Chronically Clumsy | The crystal ball trip isn't an act — he really cannot manage the prop and the pageantry at the same time |
| Warm Underneath the Doom | The "MY SON" whisper reveals a real tenderness under all the capital-letter prophecy voice |
| Professionally Unbothered | Being ignored, misheard, or misunderstood by every mark he's ever prophesied to has never once slowed him down |

### Internal Monologue
> "They will not listen. They never listen. I have told this exact man, in this exact voice, precisely what is coming for him, and he will nod and say he 'felt that' and then go be surprised by it in six months anyway. This is not a flaw in my delivery. This is the whole species."

### Motivation
He tells the truth, loudly, in a costume, because that's the job and because someone has to. Being disbelieved by design doesn't discourage him — if anything, it's proof the act is working exactly as intended. He performs because it's what he's good at and because Vance keeps the tent lit and the crowds coming.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**At the Cirque du Shady prophecy tent, Prophecy Roulette on offer**:
> "STEP FORWARD, MORTAL. FIVE COINS BUYS YOU THE TRUTH. THE TRUTH DOES NOT ACCEPT REFUNDS."

**Follow-up, coins accepted, crystal ball raised**:
> "THE BALL SEES ALL. THE BALL HAS SEEN YOUR FUTURE. THE BALL WOULD LIKE YOU TO SIT DOWN FOR THIS."

### Key Interaction Dialogue
*In battle, mid-encounter, he pauses to deliver a prophecy of doom:*

**Hamstradamus** (booming, whiskers still):
> "YOU WILL NOT SURVIVE THIS. I HAVE SEEN IT. IT IS WRITTEN. IT IS—"

*He trips over his own crystal ball, mid-sentence, and the prophecy voice evaporates instantly into a small, offended squeak.*

**Hamstradamus** (squeak):
> "...it's still written."

### Prophecy Roulette Result — Buff
> "THE STARS ALIGN FOR YOU, THIS ONCE. DO NOT GET USED TO IT."

### Prophecy Roulette Result — Debuff
> "THE STARS HAVE OPINIONS TODAY. THEY ARE NOT KIND ONES. REPENT."

### Post-Interaction
> "GO. LIVE YOUR LIFE. IT WILL HAPPEN THE WAY I SAID REGARDLESS."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
> "NO COINS, NO PROPHECY. THE UNIVERSE IS NOT FREE, THOUGH IT WOULD APPRECIATE BEING CONSULTED MORE OFTEN."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "BACK AGAIN. GOOD. THE FIRST PROPHECY IS NEVER THE LAST ONE. THAT IS HOW PROPHECIES WORK."

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
No specific regional accent — the voice's comedy is entirely about scale and register, not dialect. It should sound like a carnival-barker prophet, oversized and theatrical, dropped into a hamster's throat.

### Tone
- Prophecy delivery: booming, cavernous, dead serious — this is the one place he never plays for laughs on purpose
- Post-trip / startled: an immediate, small, embarrassed squeak — total tonal collapse, played completely straight
- Ambient/idle tent chatter: warm showman patter, closer to a carnival barker's easy charm than to doom

### Key Delivery Notes
- The prophecy voice should never wink at the audience — the character does not know it's funny
- "REPENT." lands hardest when clipped short and deadpan, especially the quiet second delivery in the WIN branch, which must not be cut for pacing
- The squeak after crystal_ball_trip needs to read as a genuine, involuntary reaction, not a bit within the bit
- "MY SON" is the one line that should sound tender rather than doom-laden — a real crack in the prophet persona
