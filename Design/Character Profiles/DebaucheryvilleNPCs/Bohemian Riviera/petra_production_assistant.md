## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Petra runs sign-up, forms, and headset comms for Shady Productions' *Bacchanus Beach Beatdown*. She has done this for enough seasons that the disclosure script comes out of her at the same flat, complete cadence every single time — the toilet, the catapult, the river, the water quality, the language, the waiver, the offer to read all four pages aloud in English. She says all of it, every time, to everyone, because saying it once and having it ignored taught her that saying it well doesn't matter; saying it *completely* is the only part of the job that's actually hers. She is bilingual, unhurried, and has never once needed to raise her voice to be heard over a man who isn't listening.

### What Bros Believe
The bros believe Petra is a formality — the paperwork lady standing between them and the crane camera, a bureaucratic speed bump on the way to the moment the universe was clearly building toward. They do not register her as the person actually explaining, in full, exactly what is about to happen to them. They register her as background noise with a clipboard.

### The Comedy Gap
Petra never lies, not once, not in any line, in any branch, including barks. Every scam beat in the entire questline — the language, the toilet, the river, the water quality, the waiver, the fact that the monitor isn't a camera — is disclosed by her, in full, in English, before anyone signs anything. The joke is never that she's hiding something. The joke is that she's the only completely honest document on set, and the bros treat her like the least important person there.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Honest Broker** — a working professional who has made peace with the fact that her job is to tell the whole truth to people who have already decided not to hear it. She isn't cynical about the bros specifically; she's seen this exact pattern with hundreds of contestants and has stopped expecting it to break.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|--------------|
| Total, Compulsive Disclosure | States every material fact of the show, every time, unprompted, whether or not anyone is listening |
| Deadpan Clipboard Energy | Delivers catapults, rivers, and contamination waivers in the exact same register she'd use for a lunch order |
| Quietly Keeping Score | Runs a private mental tally of "seconds until they mention college" for every new sign-up group — an old habit that keeps the repetition bearable |
| Procedurally Precise | Corrects factual errors on the spot ("That is not a camera. That is a monitor for the camera.") without editorializing further |
| Unbothered by Being Ignored | Has fully accepted that being heard and having disclosed something are two separate jobs, and only one of them is hers |

### Internal Monologue
> "Three minutes, this time. New record. Usually it's ninety seconds before one of them says 'back at school' like it's a place I've heard of. I have told them about the catapult. I have told them about the river. I have offered, twice, to read four pages of Czech legalese aloud in English, which takes eleven minutes I do not have. They will sign before I finish the sentence. They always do. I will still say the sentence."

### Motivation
Petra keeps this job because it's steady, it pays better than most production work in Debaucheryville, and because being scrupulously honest with people who won't listen is, in a strange way, less exhausting than lying would be — she never has to remember what she told anyone, because she told them everything. She isn't performing integrity for the camera. Nobody's filming her. She's just not willing to be the reason anyone can say they weren't warned.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**When the bros first stop at the sign-up table**:
> "Hi. Yes. Sign-up is here. The show is called The Bacchanus Beach Beatdown. It is a trivia contest. The trivia is in Bohemian. All of it. Every question. There is no translator — we cut that from the budget in 2011."

**Continuing over any interruption, same flat pace**:
> "You will be strapped into a portable toilet. The portable toilet is attached to a pneumatic catapult. Three incorrect answers and the catapult fires you into the river. The river is The Bacchanus. It is not clean. It has never been clean. I want to be clear that I am not exaggerating for the show."

### Key Interaction Dialogue
*When a bro reframes her disclosure as something friendlier than it is:*

**Bradley**: "So it's an obstacle course."

**Petra** (flat):
> "It is not an obstacle course. There are no obstacles. There is a toilet, and then there is the river."

**Chadwick** *(nodding slowly, filming)*: "She's downplaying it. That's how you KNOW it's big."

**Petra**:
> "I am not downplaying it. I am describing it."

### The Release-Form Beat
*She produces three release forms — four pages each, single-spaced, entirely in Czech-parody legalese.*

**Petra**:
> "These are the release forms. They are in Bohemian. By signing, you waive the right to pursue us for injury, drowning, contamination, and 'reputational consequence in perpetuity across all media, known and not yet invented.' Would you like me to read them aloud in English? I am happy to. It takes eleven minutes."

**Lord Pilsner** *(already signing)*: "We're good."

**Petra**:
> "You did not let me finish."

*If a bro signs on behalf of the others ("to save time"):*
> "I want it noted for the record that this is not legally how that works."
*(She notes it. She says it out loud. Nobody asks her to stop noting it.)*

*On the monitor Chadwick flexes at:*
> "That is not a camera. That is a monitor for the camera. It is showing you the beach behind you."

### Sign-Up Complete / Quest Handoff
*Once all three forms are signed and she's tucked them under the clipboard clip:*
> "You're signed up. Strap-in is at the catapult. Jaxson will call your names — well, he will call 'contestants.' He does not have your names either."

### Post-Interaction
*Into her headset, evenly, regardless of how sign-up went:*
> "Confirm three contestants. Confirm three forms. Confirm zero of the eleven minutes used."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*If the party lingers at the table without signing anyone up:*
> "No rush. The forms don't expire. The tide — there is no tide, it's a river, but the offer doesn't expire either."

### The Post-Launch Checkout Line
*At the hostel door on the LOSS branch. A Shady Productions runner — the one who "does not make eye contact and does not stop moving" — is already extending `item_hamagotchi_consolation` toward the bro and walking past at the same time. Petra is beside him, clipboard out, item never touching her hands, timing the line to land exactly as the runner's hand does:*
> "Please sign here to confirm receipt of one [1] second-place prize."

*If asked what "second place" means when there were four contestants:*
> *(checking the clipboard)* "Second place is everyone who did not win. That's — however many of you are standing here. You're all tied for it."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
*If the party returns to Fauxst Beach on a later filming day:*
> "Back again. New form, same river. Would you like me to read this one aloud too? Still eleven minutes."
