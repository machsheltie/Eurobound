# NPC Profile: QUEEN Riviéra Renata

*"That's QUEEN Riviéra Renata to you!"*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_queen_riviera_renata_01`
- **Display Name**: QUEEN Riviéra Renata
- **Internal Name**: Renata (her surname is on file with the strip's bar owners, the lifeguard station, and nobody else — she has been "Riviéra Renata" since before most of the current bar staff were born, and she promoted herself to QUEEN somewhere in the mid-nineties. The strip ratified it without a vote.)
- **Role**: Local Legend / Ambient Party Fixture / Scripted Quest Catalyst (origin point of the outbreak scene) / mandatory Couchsurfing Chronicles entry
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_nothing_is_getting_through_01`
- **Primary Location**: `night_party_zone`, Fauxst Beach, The Bohemian Riviera, Debaucheryville

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Renata is fifty-five years old and has been coming to this stretch of riverbank since before anyone trucked sand onto it. She remembers when it was mud. She has spent three and a half decades in devoted, unbroken sun worship — the tan is not a phase, it is a practice, and the result is decades-deep bronze, high gloss, and total ease in her own body. She looks *incredible*, and she is entirely aware of it, in the flat, unexcited way a person is aware of their own address. She has a ridiculous, undimmed libido, she loves a good party more than she loves almost anything else, and she spreads the love amongst all beachgoers equally — it's only fair. She arrives when the sound system starts, she dances until the string lights go amber, she gets exactly what she came for, and she goes home, or she doesn't, and either way she is up at dawn jogging the promenade in enormous sunglasses. Nobody on the strip finds any of this remarkable. The bartenders keep her stool clear. The lifeguard waves. When the sound-system guy takes requests, he takes hers first. Locals speak her name with genuine respect and a little awe, the way you'd say the name of a long-serving mayor who has never once lost an election.

### What Bros Believe
Each bro, independently and without consulting the other two, believes he has personally landed a twenty-two-year-old at the peak of his own considerable powers. The game renders this literally: through the beer goggles her sprite is replaced by the same woman rendered at twenty-two — unmistakably her face, her posture, her presence, with about thirty years subtracted and a coat of music-video airbrush the bros' imaginations supply for free. **The only thing the goggles lie about is the number.** They do not make her better-looking; she was never a downgrade, and there was nothing to upgrade. The identical render appears three times, for three men standing in three different parts of the same party, none of whom is aware the other two are having the identical thought about the identical woman. Every one of them privately credits his own charm. Every one of them plans to tell the story later. None of them ever tells it.

### The Comedy Gap
Renata knows exactly who she is, exactly what she wants, and exactly how the night is going to end; she is the only person in the scene with an accurate picture of the evening. The bros are wearing their own arithmetic on their faces and calling it a conquest. The gap isn't between what she is and what they think she is — that framing would make her the punchline, and she is not the punchline. The gap is that three pushing-forty men in crusted board shorts believe they were *selected*, by a woman who came to her own party, had a nice time, and treated all three of them with the exact same easy, uncomplicated warmth.

The satire target is the double standard itself. A man collecting younger partners into his fifties is a legend with a nickname; Renata is doing precisely that, at precisely that level, and the bros' morning horror is not that she isn't beautiful — she is visibly, unarguably a queen, better-dressed at 7 a.m. than they have been at any point in their adult lives — it's that she is fifteen years older than them, she is the legend the locals were name-dropping all night, and she has just patted them on the cheek the way their mothers do. She visibly is the queen. They visibly were the haul. Every one of them understood that instantly, in front of the others, and said nothing.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|--------------|
| Age | 55 |
| Build | Genuinely stunning and built by use, not by a mirror — the frame of a woman who walks the length of this strip twice a day, swims in water the lifeguard will honestly tell you not to swim in, and has never once in her life sucked anything in. Moves like she has never entered a room she wasn't invited to |
| Height | Average — tall enough to look each bro directly in the eye without adjusting, which she does |
| Energy | Sovereign, sunlit, entirely unbothered |

### Clothing
- **Night: the good dress**: Party-ready and expertly chosen, gold at the shoulders — not a costume and not a bid for anything; she picked it because she likes it and because it moves well when the sound system is good
- **Morning: impeccable**: This is the load-bearing costume beat of the entire questline. She redressed before she slept and she redressed *properly* — linen crisp, wrap skirt tied straight, gold sandals refastened, hair handled, everything squared away and camera-ready before she stands up. In the low gold morning light she looks like the last frame of a perfume advertisement while three men decompose in the sand beside her
- **Accessories**: Enormous dark sunglasses (on before she is fully upright, and not removed before noon for any reason); a thin gold chain worn since the mid-eighties; a woven beach tote containing sunscreen, a lighter, a spare hair tie, and a bottle of water she will hand to a stranger who needs it more
- **Footwear**: Flat gold sandals at night; the same sandals, refastened and spotless, in the morning; running shoes at dawn

### Distinguishing Features
- Decades-deep, even, high-gloss bronze she regards as a completed body of work — she will tell you the year she started, unprompted, with the pride of a craftsman. **Texture, never decay**
- Sunglasses go on before she stands, every time, without exception — a fixed continuity detail
- Pats people on the cheek. Everyone. Bar staff, the lifeguard, strangers, all three bros. It is the single most affectionate and most annihilating gesture in the questline
- Laughs first and loudest at her own good time; never at anyone else's expense

### Sprite Notes
Draw her with the same craftsmanship and dignity as Janek Sýkora, and then dress her better than anyone else in Debaucheryville. Her true sprite is the canonical asset — warm, grounded, glossy, lit to match the scene's palette exactly, with the strip's amber string-light bounce falling on her the same way it falls on every other local. She is the most self-possessed *and* the best-looking person in the frame at all times, and she should read that way in every pose, especially the morning ones. **The morning-exit pose is a glamour pose:** put-together, striking, morning-light gorgeous, staged directly against three sprites of crusted wreckage. If a reviewer looks at the morning frame and does not immediately think "she is out of their league," the art has missed.

**Never render her as withered, leathery, sagging, gross, or a cautionary tale.** Sun worship is texture — burnished, healthy, decades-deep — not damage. No comedy squash-and-stretch, no unflattering angles, no "reveal lighting."

The second sprite, `renata_goggles_render`, is **a beer-goggles overlay, not a second character and not a compliment.** It is unmistakably *her* — same face, same posture, same presence — rendered at twenty-two, with a coat of over-airbrushed music-video gloss and lighting that sits *slightly wrong for the scene*, so an attentive player clocks it as an overlay before the morning reveal ever happens. **The lie is her age and only her age.** Do not make the overlay prettier than her true sprite; if the two are compared side by side, the true sprite should win on craft, presence, and wardrobe every time. Ship the identical overlay asset for all three goggle moments — the sameness is the joke.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Unbothered Sovereign** — a woman who worked out what she enjoys somewhere around 1991 and has spent every summer since doing exactly that, in public, with no apology, no explanation, and no interest in whether anyone approves. She isn't defiant; defiance would require an audience she's arguing with, and she doesn't have one. She's warm, she's generous, she remembers the name of every bartender's kid, and she has the settled confidence of someone who has never once in her adult life needed to be chosen.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Entirely at Peace | With her age, her skin, her appetites, and the entire body of work — she volunteers the year she started tanning the way a sommelier volunteers a vintage, because it is a boast |
| Impeccable On Principle | Leaves nowhere, ever, less than fully put together — she has never in thirty-five summers been seen in daylight looking like anything other than the best-dressed person on the sand |
| Egalitarian by Principle | "I spread the love amongst all beachgoers equally — it's only fair." She means it structurally; she has genuinely never ranked anybody |
| Maternal Without Warning | Pats cheeks, fixes collars, hands over water bottles, tells strangers to drink something that isn't beer — the same warmth to a bro, a bartender, or a lost vacationer |
| Constitutionally Cheerful | Nothing that happens to her registers as an indignity, because she has never conceded that any of it is one |
| Already Somewhere Else | By the time a bro has finished composing his opening line, she has heard a better song start two bars down and is halfway there |

### Internal Monologue
> *(in the local language, translated)* "Good song. Good night. The tall one with the necklace has been building up to say something for forty minutes, poor thing — somebody should tell him the trick is to just say it. Ah, he's said it. It was not good. That's all right." *(a beat, warmly)* "I have been coming to this beach since it was mud. I have earned this."

### Motivation
Renata wants a good night, a good song, good company, and the sun on her shoulders in the morning. That is the entire engine. She is not lonely, not on a comeback, not proving anything to an ex-husband, and not in need of rescue by a man in cargo shorts — every one of those framings was considered and rejected, because every one of them would make her smaller than she is. She likes her life. She built it deliberately. The bros happened to be standing in it for about nine hours.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**Ambient, `night_party_zone`, to anyone she passes on the way to the good speaker**:
> "You're not dancing. Why are you not dancing? Come, come — nobody is watching, and if they are watching, so what."

**To a bro who has just delivered an opening line he workshopped for forty minutes**:
> "Mm. Yes. Buy me nothing, I have a drink. Can you dance, or only talk?"

### Locals' Background Name-Drops (Party Night)
*Overheard crowd dialogue during the party montage — three separate passes, none of which any bro processes. This staging belongs to the quest file; the line is canon and must be delivered verbatim.*

**Locals, in passing, with easy familiarity**:
> "Renata's here somewhere tonight—"

*The bros hear a name. They do not attach it to anyone. It goes in and it goes out.*

### Key Interaction Dialogue
*Morning. Seagulls. **Chadwick and Bradley** are upright and destroyed — crusted, sunburnt, sand in every seam. **Lord Pilsner** is cocooned in a sleeping bag wearing only a leopard-print male thong that is not his.*

**Chadwick**: "Bro. Whose is this."

**Lord Pilsner**: "...Mine now?"

*Renata rises from the other side of the sleeping bag — fully, flawlessly dressed, because she redressed before she slept and she redressed properly. Linen crisp. Skirt straight. Sandals fastened. Sunglasses on before she is fully upright. In the low gold light she looks like the closing frame of an advertisement for something expensive. Three men in the sand do not. She stretches once, checks the sky the way farmers check the sky, and shoulders her tote.*

**QUEEN Riviéra Renata** *(bright, unhurried, entirely at ease)*:
> "Ah — good, the sun's up. Drink water, all of you. Not beer. Water."

*She crosses to Chadwick and Bradley and pats each one on the cheek — exactly, identically, unmistakably the way their mothers do. Neither of them moves. Neither of them looks at the other.*

*She turns to Pilsner in the sleeping bag, throws both arms around his neck, and plants a big kiss on his mouth. It is affectionate, it is unhurried, and it is the last thing that happens to him for the rest of the questline.*

*Then she jogs off down the beach, tote over one shoulder, calling back over it:*

**QUEEN Riviéra Renata**:
> "You're such nice boys!"

### The Correction (Mid-Exit — The Name Finally Lands)
*Somewhere behind her, in three separate skulls, the name from last night finishes loading.*

**A bro** *(hoarse, delayed, genuinely uncertain)*: "Wait— *you're* Riviéra Renata?"

*She stops. Turns. Lowers the enormous sunglasses about half an inch. Winks. And delivers it with full, earned, unhurried pride — a woman stating her title, not correcting a mistake:*

**QUEEN Riviéra Renata** *(swagger, warmth, absolute certainty)*:
> "That's QUEEN Riviéra Renata to you!"

*Sunglasses back up. She resumes jogging. She does not look back a second time.*

**Narrator**:
> "Nobody said it out loud."

*All three bros begin, in unison, to itch. The verdict arrives from inside the sleeping bag, delivered with total relief by a man who has not moved and does not intend to:*

**Lord Pilsner**: "POISON IVY. Thank God. Boys, that's poison ivy."

*(Bradley's "there was DEFINITELY foliage at that beach party" and his CRABS line follow here; both belong to the quest file's staging, not to this profile.)*

### The Rejection Scene (Any Bro Attempts a Re-Approach While `the_herp` Is Active)
*Triggered anywhere on the strip. She is not disgusted, not cruel, and not informed — nobody has told her anything, and the bros would sooner die than raise the subject. She is simply busy. Her rejection is the only one in the questline that isn't a rejection at all, which is worse.*

**QUEEN Riviéra Renata** *(delighted to see them, already walking)*:
> "The nice boys! Look at you. No, no — I can't tonight, there's a thing at the Cirque and Marek is bringing the big speaker. Go, enjoy yourselves! Drink water!"

*She pats the nearest one on the cheek. It happens again. They flinch this time, and she does not notice, because she is already forty feet away and waving without turning around.*

**QUEEN Riviéra Renata** *(calling back, cheerfully, from a distance)*:
> "You know where the beach is!"

*She has moved on to the next party. There was never a queue. There was never a competition. The bros stand there with a status effect and a theory about foliage.*

### Post-Interaction
*Ambient line the next time the party passes her lounger, whatever the party's condition — she treats them exactly as she treats everyone, forever:*
> "Boys! Sit, sit — no, not on the towel, the towel is for the towel. There, the sand is fine."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*If the player walks the bros past her without engaging — the only outcome in the entire questline in which nobody loses anything:*
> "Mm. Off they go." *(to the bartender, without heat, already looking at the water)* "Nice boys. Terrible dancers."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits — Post-Coin)
*After `item_gold_circle_coin` is equipped and the party returns to the Bohemian Riviera. She is exactly the same, because nothing that happened was ever a chapter in her life. She notices the Coin, because she notices everything.*

**QUEEN Riviéra Renata** *(lifting the sunglasses half an inch, entirely approving)*:
> "What is that around your neck? Gold? Ooh — very fancy. Very fancy!" *(patting the cheek of whichever bro is wearing it)* "See? You clean up. I always said it."

*She never said it. She is being kind. She goes back to her lounger.*

Renata is not a vendor and not a service provider. **N/A is deliberate here:** any transactional framing of this character would collapse the entire tone rule — she is a local having her own life, not an offering. ### Hidden Details / Easter Eggs
- Examining her lounger at any time of day shows her name scrawled on the frame in marker, in three different hands, in three different decades — the strip has re-labeled it for her twice as the frames wore out
- Examining her stool at the bar strip while she is not on it: it is empty, wiped, and unclaimed, in a bar where nothing is ever unclaimed

This profile neither claims it nor denies it, and no line of dialogue anywhere in the game resolves the question. It was at the party. Many things were at the party

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Local, warm, and lived-in — a smoke-and-honey voice with thirty-five summers in it, unhurried, consonants intact. Never exaggerated for comedy and never played as a "character voice." She is the most naturalistic performance in the scene; everyone around her is louder than she is.

### Tone
- **At the party**: relaxed, amused, genuinely enjoying herself — an easy alto that never has to compete with the music because she never raises it
- **The morning exit**: brisk and maternal, the tone of someone with a full day ahead who is being nice about the delay
- **The QUEEN correction**: pride, not correction. Warm, delighted, absolutely certain — she is stating her title, not scolding a stranger. A wink is audible in it
- **The rejection**: sunny, affectionate, and already in motion — nothing withheld, nothing weaponized; she just has somewhere to be
- **The post-Coin callback**: approving and a little tickled, the way you'd compliment a nephew's first suit

### Key Delivery Notes
- **"That's QUEEN Riviéra Renata to you!"** ships verbatim, with swagger, and must never be delivered as offense or as a joke at her own expense. The line is a coronation, not a punchline
- **"You're such nice boys!"** ships verbatim and must be entirely sincere — no irony, no smirk, no knowing lilt. She means it. It is devastating precisely because she means it
- Never let her voice acknowledge anything unflattering about the situation, her age, or her night. She has nothing to acknowledge
- The three cheek-pat beats must be scored identically — same warmth, same rhythm, same length. The identical treatment of all three men *is* the reveal; any variation in delivery kills it
- Emotional range: narrow and sunny by design. She never gets angry, never gets embarrassed, and never gets wistful. If a take sounds wistful, cut it

## 📝 Development Notes

- **SHE IS NOT THE JOKE. This is binding and non-negotiable.** The comedy lands 100% on the bros' beer goggles and their morning horror. No line, sprite, animation, sound cue, or ambient NPC anywhere in the game mocks her age, her body, her tan, or her appetites. If a later pass adds one, it is a defect, not a punch-up.
- **Appearance rule (author-locked).** Renata is genuinely hot at 55 and impeccably dressed the morning after — put-together, striking, morning-light glamorous while the bros are crusty wreckage. Sun worship reads as decades-deep bronze and total ease: **texture, never decay.** She is never written or rendered as withered, gross, or a cautionary tale. She is the confident older woman doing exactly what celebrated men do, collecting younger men without shame, and the satire target is the double standard itself — she visibly IS a queen; they visibly were the haul.
- **The goggles lied about her AGE, not her looks.** `renata_goggles_render` de-ages her to 22 and changes nothing else — she was never a downgrade and there was nothing to upgrade. The overlay must never be drawn more attractive than her true sprite. It renders only in first-person bro POV inside `night_event_outbreak_01`, never in a neutral camera, never again after the morning cut, and is never registered as her sprite in any data file. Ship the identical asset for all three beats; the sameness is the gag.
- **The morning horror is the age-and-legend reveal plus The Herp — never her appearance.** What lands on the bros is that she is fifteen years their senior, that she is the local legend whose name was in the air all night, and that she just mothered two of them. Nothing about how she looks is available as a joke, and the wide shot makes clear she is the best-looking person in the frame.
- **No horror sting on her sprite.** When the goggles drop at the morning cut, the reveal music, the zoom, and the reaction frames land on the *bros' faces and on the thong*. The camera treats Renata exactly as it treats any other local: normally, and flatteringly. Any "shock reveal" framing pointed at her is a tone break.
- **No bro ever comments on her appearance in the morning.** Not one line, not a mutter, not a lip-flap — and not because there's anything to be diplomatic about. "Nobody said it out loud." is canonical, load-bearing, and the reason the scene works: none of them will be the first to admit what happened, and the silence is entirely about *them*.
- **Cast assignments are author-locked.** Lord Pilsner is in the sleeping bag, owns "...Mine now?", receives the neck-hug and the kiss, and delivers "POISON IVY. Thank God. Boys, that's poison ivy." from inside the bag. Chadwick and Bradley are the standing bros who receive the identical maternal cheek-pats; Chadwick opens with "Bro. Whose is this."; Bradley keeps both the "DEFINITELY foliage" line and the CRABS line. Do not reshuffle in a later pass.
- **The thong is a permanent equippable item** (`item_leopard_print_thong`), granted to Lord Pilsner at the outbreak morning; its item file is owned by the items lane and is not specced here. **Its original owner is never established** — this profile must never claim it for Renata and must never deny it on her behalf. The question is not open for a later "clarification" pass.
- **The transmission chain is never litigated.** The game never confirms, discusses, or dramatizes where The Herp came from; nobody accuses her, nobody warns anyone about her, and no NPC ever raises her health. The bros blame foliage. The bros will always blame foliage.
- **Beer goggles are already in-game canon** — Lord Pilsner's Ultimate ("Mid-Life Crisis," Vintage upgrade) applies a literal Beer Goggles debuff. The outbreak's rendering trick should reuse that established visual language so the effect reads as a known game system, not a one-off cheat.
- **The cheek-pat is the reveal, and it must be identical all three times** — same animation, same timing, same warmth, no cross-referencing dialogue between the bros. Do not add a reaction line "clarifying" the moment in a later pass; the clarification is what would ruin it.
- **Her rejection scene must never be cruel or informed.** Nobody tells her anything. She isn't recoiling — she's busy. Of the six rejection scenes in this questline, hers is the only one that isn't a rejection, and that contrast is deliberate.
- **She never leaves Debaucheryville.** No cross-city appearances, ever. Traveling would imply she wanted something from them.
- Voice acting priority: lock the sincere, unhurried baseline first, then the QUEEN correction — every other line calibrates against those two.
