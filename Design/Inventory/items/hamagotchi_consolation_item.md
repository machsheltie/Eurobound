# Item Definition: Hamagotchi™ Virtual Hamster Pal

*"It's basically an antique, bro." - Bradley, regarding a dead pixel screen that has never once been alive*

## 📋 Item Identity
- **Item ID**: `item_hamagotchi_consolation`
- **Display Name**: Hamagotchi™ Virtual Hamster Pal
- **Internal Name**: Waterlogged Knockoff Tamagotchi, Hamstradamus-Branded, Same 2003 Pallet
- **Item Type**: Junk / Key Item (Non-Removable Curiosity)
- **Rarity**: Common (bros think it's Priceless)
- **Source**: Delivered off-site, at the hostel door, by an unnamed Shady Productions runner — after the walk of shame, at the end of The Bacchanus Beach Beatdown LOSS branch. Not handed over by Hamstradamus, and not delivered at the Fauxst Beach prize podium.

---

## 🎭 The Deception

### What The Item Actually Is
A knockoff Tamagotchi from the same defective 2003 merch pallet as the stein and badge, branded with a crude printed decal of Hamstradamus's face on the shell. Years of beach storage got it thoroughly waterlogged — the buttons are corroded solid and do nothing no matter how hard or how often they're pressed, and the tiny LCD screen has been permanently stuck for over two decades on a single flickering frame: a faint, pixelated outline of a hamster ghost, floating in place, going nowhere. This is the consolation prize handed to whichever bro loses The Bacchanus Beach Beatdown — the "you got launched into the river but at least here's something" prize.

### What The Bros Believe It Is
"Bro, this is VINTAGE tech. Like, pre-smartphone. This is the OLDEST piece of technology any of us has ever owned. You can't put a price on that. This basically makes me a collector now."

---

## 📊 Stats & Effects

### Direct Stats
None. The Hamagotchi has no stats, no active ability, and no cooldown — it is purely a curiosity item the losing bro carries for the rest of the game.

### Passive Effects
| Effect | Description |
|--------|--------------|
| **Permanently Treasured** | Bros refuse to ever discard, sell, or trade it, regardless of its junk classification |
| **Screen Never Changes** | The hamster ghost frame never advances, animates, or resolves into anything else, no matter how long it's carried or "checked on" |

### What It Does NOT Do
- ❌ Turn on, respond to button presses, or display anything besides the stuck hamster ghost frame
- ❌ Grant any stat, ability, or gameplay bonus
- ❌ Actually function as a Tamagotchi-style pet in any capacity
- ❌ Become more valuable or "vintage" through any in-game action
