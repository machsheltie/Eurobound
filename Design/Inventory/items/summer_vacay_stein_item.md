# Item Definition: Souvenir Champion Stein

*"To the champion!" - Lord Pilsner, toasting with a stein that has never once held a beverage worth toasting*

## 📋 Item Identity
- **Item ID**: `item_summer_vacay_stein`
- **Display Name**: Souvenir Champion Stein
- **Internal Name**: Hollow Yellowing Plastic Stein, Same 2003 Pallet as the Badge
- **Item Type**: Removable Inventory Item (Consumable-Use Trinket)
- **Rarity**: Common (bros think it's One-Of-A-Kind)
- **Source**: Hamstradamus @ Fauxst Beach prize podium — win The Bacchanus Beach Beatdown

---

## 🎭 The Deception

### What The Item Actually Is
A hollow plastic beer stein from the exact same defective 2003 merch pallet as the trivia champ badge, handed over alongside it the moment a bro wins The Bacchanus Beach Beatdown. Decades of storage have yellowed the plastic from "cheerful gold" to "concerning urine tone," and the seam where the two mold halves were fused together was never sealed properly — anything poured in seeps back out along a thin, constant drip line down the side. It says "CHAMPION" on the front in the same flaking glitter print as the badge, because Shady Productions bought both off the same lot and never bothered sourcing anything separately.

### What The Bros Believe It Is
"This isn't a cup, bro, this is a TROPHY you can DRINK OUT OF. That's next-level. Do you know how many guys have a trophy AND a badge? We basically have a whole championship RIG now."

---

## 📊 Stats & Effects

### Active Ability: "Take a Swig"
- **Cooldown**: 60 seconds
- **Effect**: Restores +5 HP
- **Risk**: 20% chance to trigger **Glitter Gullet** — a minor 10-second debuff (see below)
- **Flavor Line**: "There wasn't even anything in it. Somehow you swallowed glitter anyway."

### Glitter Gullet (Minor Debuff, 10 Seconds)
A brief, self-contained queasiness spike from swallowing a stray fleck of the stein's flaking glitter print along with whatever the bro poured in. Not a recognized battle status effect — just a short overworld nuisance: -1 Speed for the duration, a faint green tint on the portrait, and a small on-screen belch icon. Wears off on its own; no cure item needed, no lasting consequence.

### What It Does NOT Do
- ❌ Contain, dispense, or have ever contained anything worth drinking
- ❌ Hold liquid without leaking from the seam
- ❌ Count as an actual trophy for any in-game achievement purposes
- ❌ Get cleaner or less yellow no matter how many times it's "sanitized"

- **Quest Name**: "The Bacchanus Beach Beatdown"
- **Quest Giver**: Hamstradamus
- **Location**: Fauxst Beach, The Bohemian Riviera

### Gates/Unlocks
| What It Unlocks | How |
|------------------|-----|
| "Take a Swig" active ability in the item menu | Stein added to inventory on Beatdown WIN outcome |
| Toast dialogue barks between bros | Stein present in party inventory, triggers opportunistically in hub areas |

Unlike the trivia champ badge, the stein is a normal removable inventory item — it can be dropped, sold, or otherwise disposed of like any other consumable-use trinket, it just never actually gets used up.
