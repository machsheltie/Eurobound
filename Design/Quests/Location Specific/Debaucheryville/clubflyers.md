# Club Flyers: "Peak Y2K Marketing Genius" 
**Enhanced Satirical Sidequest - 90s-00s Cringe Edition**

## Core Premise (Y2K Delusion)
**"DJ Beatdrop vs. DJ Hardstyle Hendrik"** - Two rival DJs need promotion, and our aging bros think their **"college marketing expertise"** from 2001 will totally dominate the European club scene. **Vape Witch Vanessa** pays them to poster the city, not realizing she's hired three walking time capsules.

---

## Binary Choice (90s-00s Cringe Overload)

### Path 1: "Web 1.0 Marketing Mastery"
**What They Think They're Doing:** Leveraging their "dot-com era business knowledge"
**What They're Actually Doing:** Creating flyers that look like GeoCities pages printed on paper

**Execution:**
- **Pilsner** insists on "synergy-driven content" with WordArt fonts and clipart flames
- **Chadwick** adds his ICQ number and promises to "viral market" via his 47 contacts
- **Bradley** draws terrible ASCII art borders and adds "optimized for Netscape Navigator"

**Y2K Cringe Details:**
- Flyers feature rainbow gradients, Comic Sans, and "Under Construction" GIFs
- Include phrases like "All That and a Bag of Chips!" and "Don't Have a Cow, Man!"
- QR codes that just link to their dead Friendster profiles
- **Pilsner** adds his Hotmail address: "brewmaster69@hotmail.com"

**NPC Reactions:**
- **Young Debaucheryville Local:** *"Is this performance art? Are you mocking the early internet?"*
- **Club DJ:** *"Dude, did you make this on Microsoft Publisher? My mom used this font in 2002."*
- **Art Student:** *"This is brilliant commentary on Y2K aesthetic nostalgia... wait, you're serious?"*

**Rewards:**
- **"GeoCities Business Card"** - Locals treat you like a living museum exhibit
- **+2 Bravado** - Bros think they've "disrupted the paradigm"
- **"Dial-Up Nostalgia"** trinket - NPCs ask if you need help using modern technology

### Path 2: "Alpha Male Vandalism 2001" 
**What They Think They're Doing:** "Guerrilla marketing" like they learned from watching Fight Club
**What They're Actually Doing:** Defacing flyers with the artistic skill of a middle schooler from 1999

**Execution:**
- **Chadwick** draws flame decals and writes "PWNED!" on rival flyers
- **Pilsner** adds beer bottle stickers and "WASSSUUUUP!" speech bubbles
- **Bradley** just crosses out names and writes "THIS SUCKS" in block letters

**Y2K Cringe Details:**
- Use gel pens and correction fluid like weapons of mass destruction
- Add tribal tattoo designs copied from nu-metal album covers
- Write "All Your Base Are Belong to Us" thinking it's current slang
- **Bradley** keeps saying "That's off the hook!" unironically

**NPC Reactions:**
- **Local Graffiti Artist:** *"Yo, did some American tourists escape from a time machine?"*
- **Security Guard:** *"Are you the guys who've been writing 'BOOYAH!' on everything?"*
- **Club Regular:** *"My little brother drew better stuff than this... in 2003."*

**Rewards:**
- **"Frosted Tips Vandal"** trinket (+2 Bravado, -2 Charm, NPCs call you "nu-metal tourist")
- **"Y2K Villain"** status - Bouncers remember you as "those Limp Bizkit guys"
- **"Peak Alpha Achievement"** - Narrator: *"Truly, the savage marketing wisdom of a Blockbuster Video employee"*
