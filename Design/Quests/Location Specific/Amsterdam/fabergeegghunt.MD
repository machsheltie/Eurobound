## Quest Giver: Agent Havok

### Character Profile

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **NPC ID** | `shamsterdam_npc_agent_havok` |
| **Display Name** | "Mysterious Local Contact" (revealed as Agent Havok post-quest) |
| **True Identity** | Havok the Sheltie — Chaos Agent from *Sunday Brunch with Giselle* podcast/recipe blog |
| **Cover Identity** | A "local" sitting at an outdoor café, wearing an obviously tourist-purchased "SHAMSTERDAM" hoodie, knock-off Wayfarer sunglasses, and trying WAY too hard to look casual |
| **Voice Style** | Military movie briefing delivery — think Tommy Lee Jones in *Small Soldiers* meets Sgt. Bilko giving recon reports, but slightly altered/disguised for the "undercover local" role |
| **Sprite Design** | Anthropomorphized Sheltie in human clothes, fur visible around collar, tail occasionally wagging when excited about the "mission" |

### Hidden "Tell" Easter Eggs (Bros Miss All of These)

The bros are too oblivious to notice, but players familiar with the podcast/blog will recognize:

1. **Café Order**: Orders a "Giselle Special" — the barista has no idea what that is, serves him regular coffee
2. **Newspaper Prop**: Holding a newspaper upside down, headline reads "BRUNCH CHAOS: LOCAL DOG SUSPECTED"
3. **Catchphrase Slip**: Occasionally mutters "This is NOT a drill, this is BRUNCH" under his breath
4. **Menu Consultation**: Has a laminated "MISSION DOSSIER" that's clearly a recipe card for eggs Benedict
5. **Background Detail**: His "briefcase" has a *Sunday Brunch with Giselle* sticker half-scratched off
6. **Name Slip**: Almost introduces himself as "Agent Ha—" before catching himself: "Agent... Haaaa...ns. Hans. Local name. Very Shamsterdam."

### Agent Havok's Speaking Style

**Briefing Mode** (military report voice, overly serious):
```
"Listen up, civilians. Intel suggests a high-value asset is being held 
in the Shamsterdam Museum of Questionable Art. Codename: THE EGG. 
Fabergé. Priceless. The kind of score that separates tourists... 
from LEGENDS.

*adjusts sunglasses that are clearly too big*

Security is... *checks laminated recipe card* ...TIGHT. 
Multiple hostiles. Laser grids. The works.

*tail wags slightly*

But I know a way in. For operatives of your... *looks at the bros* 
...caliber."
```

**"Local" Accent Attempts** (slips constantly):
- "Ja, ja, very Shamsterdam, this plan. Very... *checks hand* ...gezellig."
- "As we say here in Debaucheryville— I mean, Shamsterdam— I mean, MY HOME WHERE I LIVE AND AM FROM..."
- "The tulips are... blooming? Is that a thing locals say? It feels right."

**Location**: Café de Schaamte, outdoor seating

**Trigger**: Bros walk past the café after visiting 2+ Shamsterdam locations

**Cutscene Text**:
> A figure at an outdoor café table gestures urgently. He's wearing an "SHAMSTERDAM" hoodie with the tags still on and sunglasses that are clearly from a tourist shop. His newspaper is upside down.

**Agent Havok (Initial Contact)**:
```
*speaks in exaggerated whisper*

"Psst. You three. Yeah, you — the ones who look like you peaked 
in 2007. Come. Sit. Order something. Act natural.

*glances around suspiciously at pigeons*

I have a proposition. A job. The kind of score that makes careers.

*slides laminated card across table — it's clearly a brunch recipe*

There's an egg. THE egg. Fabergé. Priceless beyond measure. 
Currently sitting in the Shamsterdam Museum of Questionable Art, 
guarded by... 

*dramatic pause* 

...forces you cannot imagine.

*a pigeon lands nearby, he flinches*

We don't have much time. You in?"
```

### Stage 3: The Heist

**Location**: Shamsterdam Museum of Questionable Art (Gift Shop)

#### Part A: The Entry

**Scene Description**:
> The building is obviously a tourist gift shop. Neon signs advertise "SOUVENIRS" and "CHOCOLATE" and "THINGS YOUR MOTHER WOULDN'T APPROVE OF." The bros see none of this. They see a FORTRESS.

**Cutscene Text**:
> Lord Pilsner presses himself flat against the wall, breathing heavily. 
> 
> **Pilsner**: "This is it, boys. The Shamsterdam Museum of Questionable Art. One of the most secure facilities in Europe."
> 
> *The door has a "WELCOME! PUSH TO ENTER :)" sign on it.*
> 
> **Pilsner**: "Chadwick, kill the lights."
> 
> **Chadwick**: *turns ON his ring light, illuminating everything*
> 
> **Pilsner**: "PERFECT. Bradley, breach the entrance."
> 
> *Bradley pushes the door. It opens with a cheerful jingle.*
> 
> **Bradley**: "I'm in. Repeat: I'm IN."
> 
> **Pilsner**: "Outstanding. Execute Operation Golden Yolk."

**Interior — The Chocolate Anatomy Emporium**

As the bros enter in tactical formation (unnecessary crouching, hand signals no one understands), they pass directly through the gift shop's most prominent display:

**THE CHOCOLATE ANATOMY SECTION**

A massive floor display featuring:
- Chocolate penises in white, milk, and dark chocolate (€8.99-€24.99)
- White chocolate specimens marbled with wild neon colors for maximum tackiness
- A size range from "novelty keychain" to "bachelorette party centerpiece"
- Artisanal decorated versions with sprinkles, edible glitter, and fondant bow ties
- A helpful sign: "PERFECT BACHELOR/BACHELORETTE GIFT! ASK FOR GIFT WRAPPING!"

**The Bros' Reaction**: 

They notice NOTHING. They are in Mission Mode.

**Lord Pilsner**: *tactical crouch-walking directly past the display* "Eyes on the prize, gentlemen. No distractions."

**Bradley**: *accidentally knocks into the display, a chocolate penis rolls across the floor* "Contact! We have contact!"

**Chadwick**: *picks it up without looking, pockets it* "Evidence secured. Keep moving."

**Lord Pilsner**: "European art installations. Probably priceless. DON'T TOUCH ANYTHING."

*They continue past walls of tulip magnets, wooden shoe keychains, and a rotating rack of "I SHAMSTERDAM" t-shirts, seeing none of it.*

**Optional Player Interaction — Examine Display**:

If player chooses to interact with the chocolate display:

> **Chadwick**: "Huh. These chocolate... sculptures... are really... abstract."
> 
> **Bradley**: "Europeans are so cultured, bro. This is like... modern art."
> 
> **Lord Pilsner**: "FOCUS. We're not here to admire the local... artistry. We're here for THE EGG."
> 
> *Bradley quietly pockets two more "sculptures" for "the guys back home."*

**Item Acquired (if examined)**: "Modern Art Chocolate (x2)" — Consumable, restores 15 HP, description: "Bradley insists these are 'abstract sculptures.' They are not."

#### Part B: The Guard

**The "Security Guard"**: 
- **Name**: Daan (pronounced "Don")
- **Description**: 19-year-old art student working the night shift, completely uninterested, wearing headphones, scrolling TikTok
- **Threat Level**: Zero
- **What He Actually Says**: "Hmm? Oh. Museum's closed but gift shop's whatever. Don't touch the expensive stuff."
- **What The Bros Hear**: "HALT. STATE YOUR BUSINESS. THIS FACILITY IS UNDER MY PROTECTION."

**The "Neutralization"**:

**Cutscene Text**:
> Lord Pilsner approaches the counter with MAXIMUM gravitas. Daan does not look up from his phone.

**Lord Pilsner**: *in his most commanding voice* "Good evening. I am... a collector. Of rare antiquities. I believe you have something I seek."

**Daan**: *still scrolling* "Mm."

**Lord Pilsner**: "I'm prepared to make this worth your while."

*Pilsner slides a €20 note across the counter. Daan glances at it, shrugs, pockets it, returns to phone.*

**Daan**: "Cool. Eggs are in the back. By the clogs."

**Lord Pilsner**: *intense whisper to the others* "I've negotiated access. He's been... compensated. We have exactly twelve minutes before he raises the alarm."

**Bradley**: *awed* "Bro. You bribed a European official. That's so BOURNE."

**Chadwick**: "Should I have filmed that? Was that a bribe? Can I post a bribe?"

**Lord Pilsner**: "No evidence. Move. NOW."

*Daan puts in his other earbud. He will not think about them again.*

**[GUARD: "NEUTRALIZED"]** — *The bros believe this completely.*

#### Part C: The "Laser Grid"

**Scene Description**:
> The bros enter the main gift shop area. Motion-sensor lights flicker on as they walk. This is a standard energy-saving feature in European retail.

**The Bros' Interpretation**:

**Bradley**: *freezes mid-step* "LASERS. Nobody. Move."

**Chadwick**: *drops to the floor* "I KNEW IT. I KNEW they'd have lasers!"

**Lord Pilsner**: "This is military-grade security. One wrong step and we trigger the silent alarm."

*The lights are just lights. They turn on when you walk by. This is how lights work.*

**Bradley**: "Okay. Okay. I trained for this. I watched the documentary."

**Chadwick**: "What documentary?"

**Bradley**: "The one with the... the acrobat guy... who does the... *mimes contorting* ...through the lasers."

**Lord Pilsner**: "Don't mention the acrobat. Focus. Bradley — you're up."

**The "Navigation"**:

**Cutscene Text**:
> Bradley begins an elaborate series of unnecessary movements. He rolls. He crawls. He does a pose that might be yoga. The lights turn on and off normally as he moves because that's what motion-sensor lights do.

**Bradley**: *contorted in an uncomfortable position* "I'm... through... the first... grid..."

**Chadwick**: *filming* "This is INCREDIBLE content."

**Lord Pilsner**: "He's a natural. Look at that form."

*Bradley army-crawls past a display of decorative windmills. A light turns on. He freezes for thirty seconds. The light turns off because he stopped moving. He interprets this as success.*

**Bradley**: *sweating* "Sector... clear. You guys... come through... exactly like I did."

*Lord Pilsner and Chadwick copy Bradley's ridiculous movements exactly. The lights behave completely normally the entire time.*

**Lord Pilsner**: *on the other side, breathing heavily* "Outstanding work, soldier. That was the most dangerous twelve meters of my life."

**Bradley**: "I think I pulled something. Worth it."

**[LASER GRID: "DEFEATED"]** — *The motion-sensor lights continue operating normally.*

**Easter Egg**: Bradley unlocks the passive ability "Tactical Awareness" — does absolutely nothing, but he believes it makes him better at stealth.

#### Part D: The Vault

**Scene Description**:
> They reach Section C: "Decorative Ovoid Objects & Seasonal Chocolates." A small glass display case sits on a pedestal next to a rack of tulip-shaped lollipops. Inside: a single ornate egg with a €3.50 price tag clearly visible.

**The Bros' Interpretation**:

**Lord Pilsner**: *reverent whisper* "There it is. The Fabergé Egg. One of the most valuable objects in Europe."

**Chadwick**: "It's... beautiful. It's glowing."

**Bradley**: "That's the overhead fluorescent lights, bro."

**Lord Pilsner**: "It's the glow of HISTORY, Bradley."

*The egg is a limited-edition Kinder Surprise decorated to look fancy. The tag reads: "FABERGÉ-STYLE COLLECTOR'S EGG — Milk Chocolate with Surprise Toy Inside! €3.50 or 2 for €6"*

*The bros do not read the tag.*

**The Display Case**:
- **Actual Security Level**: A simple latch. Not locked. There's a "PLEASE TAKE ONE" sign next to a stack of identical eggs.
- **What The Bros See**: A reinforced vault requiring expert skills to breach.

**Lord Pilsner**: "Chadwick. Assessment."

**Chadwick**: *examining the glass case with intense scrutiny* "Pressure-sensitive glass. Probably alarmed. One wrong move and this whole place locks down."

*It is a standard gift shop display case from IKEA.*

**Lord Pilsner**: "Bradley. Can you crack it?"

**Bradley**: *flexing fingers dramatically* "Stand back. I've got this."

*Bradley reaches for the latch. He pauses. He takes a deep breath. He opens the latch. It opens immediately because it was never locked.*

**Bradley**: "...I'm in. Holy shit. I'M IN."

**Chadwick**: "How did you DO that?!"

**Bradley**: "Honestly? I don't know. It's like... my hands just KNEW."

**Lord Pilsner**: "Natural talent. Some men are born for this."

*Lord Pilsner reverently lifts the egg from the case. It's surprisingly light. The wrapper crinkles.*

**Lord Pilsner**: "We did it. We actually did it. This is the greatest heist in European history."

*From across the shop, Daan's voice, not even looking up:*

**Daan**: "Dat is drie vijftig." *(That's three fifty.)*

**Lord Pilsner**: "HE'S CALLING FOR BACKUP. MOVE MOVE MOVE!"

*The bros sprint out of the gift shop. Daan sighs and marks "1x Fabergé egg" on the inventory shortage list. This happens at least twice a week.*

**[THE VAULT: "CRACKED"]** — *They did not pay for the egg.*

**Agent Havok (Debrief Mode)**:
```

"Operatives. Status report. Asset... acquired?"

*sees the egg, ears perk up*

"Outstanding. OUTSTANDING. Mission success. 
I knew you had it in you. Mostly.

*checks laminated card*

Now. The moment of truth. Open it."
```

**The Unwrapping**:

**Cutscene Text**:

*Long silence*

**Bradley**: "...I got a hippo."

**Chadwick**: "Is this... is this a Kinder egg?"

**Lord Pilsner**: *long pause* "This is... the ORIGINAL. Don't you see? The Fabergé family INVENTED the Kinder egg. This is the prototype. The ur-egg. It's PRICELESS."

**Chadwick**: "Wait. These are ILLEGAL in America. We can't bring this back. We're holding CONTRABAND."

**Bradley**: "Bro. We just smuggled an illegal egg out of a European museum. We're basically international criminals now."

**Lord Pilsner**: "EXACTLY. We're LEGENDS."

**Agent Havok's Reaction**:
```
*stares at the chocolate and plastic*

*long pause*

*tail wags slowly*

"...Mission accomplished."

*completely serious voice*

"You have done what few operatives have ever achieved. 
You have acquired... THE egg. 

The chocolate will fuel your journey. 
The toy... will remind you of this moment forever.

*sniffs*

I'm proud of you, soldiers. Truly.

*accidentally knocks over briefcase, recipe cards spill out*

I must go. My people need me. And by my people I mean... 
Shamsterdammers. Of which I am one. Goodbye."

*exits dramatically, trips on tail*
```

## Satirical Notes

### What This Quest Is Really About

The Fabergé Egg Hunt satirizes:

1. **Tourist Delusions of Grandeur**: The bros think they're in an Ocean's movie when they're in a gift shop
2. **American Ignorance Abroad**: They don't know Kinder eggs are sold everywhere (and banned at home)
3. **The Gap Between Expectation and Reality**: Every "obstacle" is mundane
4. **Male Friend Group Mythology**: They'll tell this story for decades, completely wrong
5. **The Commodification of "Authentic Experiences"**: Even their heist is just consumer tourism

### What Makes It EarthBound

- Mundane setting (gift shop) treated as epic location
- Absurd NPCs (bored teenager, disguised dog) played completely straight
- Items that are worthless but kept anyway (the hippo)
- The joke is on the protagonists, but affectionately
- Escalating commitment to a bit that pays off emotionally
