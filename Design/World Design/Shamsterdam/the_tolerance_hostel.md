# The Tolerance Hostel

*Where budget travel meets boundary testing — 12 strangers, one bathroom, zero privacy*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: shamsterdam_canalring_tolerance_hostel_01
- **Display Name**: The Tolerance Hostel
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "We Tolerate Everything (Except Quiet Hours Violations)"
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: Canal Ring Promenade, tucked behind more respectable establishments
- **Location Type**: Budget Accommodation Hub / Social Disaster Zone / Morning-After Confessional

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 24-hour reception (theoretically), night staff is one guy who's "around somewhere"
- **Stat Requirements**: None for check-in (dignity check to survive the stay)
- **Item Requirements**: €35/night for 12-bed dorm, €55 for "private" 4-bed, €85 for actual private (always "fully booked")
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (often first or last stop in Shamsterdam)
- **Special Conditions**: €20 key deposit, €50 "damage deposit" that you won't see again

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through lobby, past judgment of day staff
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (survive each night, technically "complete")
- **Failure States**: Can be banned for noise violations (3 strikes), but enforcement is... flexible
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Regular" status after 3+ nights, staff learns your name (this feels like failure)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The universal hostel experience — a place that "tolerates" everything except being comfortable, quiet, or dignified. It's where travel dreams meet travel reality: the €35/night savings cost you in sleep, sanity, and faith in humanity. Everyone here has a story, nobody wants to hear it at 3 AM, and the communal kitchen is a war zone of passive-aggressive notes and stolen yogurt.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Budget travel romanticism vs. reality, "meeting people" means "enduring people"
- **Secondary Targets**: Hostel rating inflation, "party hostel" culture, the lie of "cultural exchange" at 4 AM
- **Cultural Specificity**: Amsterdam hostels specifically overrun with bachelor parties and cannabis tourists
- **Humor Approach**: The gap between hostel brochure ("make lifelong friends!") and hostel reality ("which one of you snores")
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets hostel economics and tourist behavior, not budget travelers or hospitality workers

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling the illusion of community while profiting from discomfort
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Amsterdam hostels deal with specific clientele (high, drunk, or both)
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros could afford better but chose "the experience," now experiencing regret
- **Comedy of Errors**: Every hostel cliché manifested — the snorer, the thief, the 5 AM alarm, the couple who forgot it's a dorm
- **Consequence System**: Sleep quality affects next-day stats, kitchen drama affects Bravado

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Authentic backpacker experience, meeting fellow travelers, living like locals

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Paying €35 to not sleep while someone's alarm goes off every hour

**How impaired are they**: Usually very (this is Shamsterdam), impairment doesn't help the bunk situation

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- "Private room" means 4 strangers instead of 12
- 3.4 TripAdvisor stars is NOT good
- "Party hostel" means "you will not sleep"
- The €20 deposit is a fee, not a deposit
- Lockers require their own lock (sold at reception for 3x market price)
- The "free breakfast" is bread and whatever spread hasn't been stolen

**How locals play along**: No locals are here — staff are fellow travelers working for accommodation, equally exhausted

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Check-In System
- **Reception Interaction**: Approach desk, wait for staff to finish phone call (personal, not business)
- **Room Assignment**: Choice of 12-bed (€35), 4-bed "private" (€55), or told actual private is "fully booked" (€85)
- **Deposit Collection**: €20 key + €50 damage, neither of which you'll see again
- **Key Acquisition**: Physical key on keychain large enough to be embarrassing in public
- **Orientation**: "Kitchen's there, bathroom's there, quiet hours are 11 PM, nobody follows quiet hours"

### Room Selection Consequences
| Room Type | Price | Effects | Notes |
|-----------|-------|---------|-------|
| 12-Bed Dorm | €35 | -Sleep quality, +Random encounter chance, +Story opportunity | THE authentic experience |
| 4-Bed "Private" | €55 | -Sleep quality (less), +Awkward intimacy with 3 strangers | Private between air quotes |
| Actual Private | €85 | "Fully booked" (always), redirected to 4-bed | The lie that keeps on lying |
| THE BUNK | €35 | Worst position in 12-bed, -HP overnight, +Bravado for surviving | Bottom corner, draft, stain |

### Sleep Quality System
Each night's rest affects next-day performance:
- **Restful Night** (rare): Full HP/SP restore, +5% all stats
- **Disturbed Sleep** (common): 75% HP/SP restore, no bonus
- **What Sleep?** (frequent): 50% HP/SP restore, -5% Speed
- **The Snorer Night** (random): 25% HP/SP restore, -10% all stats, +Exhausted status

**Sleep Disruption Events** (random chance each night):
- Someone's 5 AM alarm (high probability)
- The Snorer activates (very high probability)
- The Couple forgets where they are (moderate probability)
- Locker thief attempt (low probability, lose random item if no lock purchased)
- Fire alarm (false, someone smoked inside) (moderate probability)
- Your own bunkmate returns at 4 AM, drunk (very high probability)

### The Communal Kitchen (Morning-After System)
- **Trigger**: Visit kitchen between 7-10 AM after any night out
- **Function**: NPCs share stories from previous night, gossip exchange, exposition dump
- **Mechanics**: 
  - Overhear conversations about locations you haven't visited (hints)
  - Fellow guests ask about YOUR night (Bravado check for exaggeration vs. truth)
  - Passive-aggressive note discovery (environmental storytelling)
  - Fridge drama witness (someone's yogurt was taken, investigation ongoing)

### Fridge Politics Mini-System
- **Your Shelf**: One designated shelf, items placed here are "safe" (85% of the time)
- **Communal Shelf**: Items here are fair game, expect loss
- **Unlabeled Items**: 50% chance another guest takes them overnight
- **Passive-Aggressive Labeling**: Writing your name + date + threat increases safety to 95%
- **The Eternal Peanut Butter**: Cannot be taken, cannot be discarded, exists outside time

### Bulletin Board System
- **Job Postings**: "Need someone to share taxi to airport" "Looking for travel buddy to [city]"
- **Event Flyers**: Pub crawls, walking tours, "meditation sessions" (code for other activities)
- **Quest Hooks**: Occasionally contains legitimate side quest triggers
- **Lost & Found Notices**: "Lost: Passport (American). If found, I'm the crying one in room 4."
- **Warnings**: "BEWARE: Guy in room 6 sets alarm for 5 AM EVERY DAY and NEVER WAKES UP"

### Bathroom Line Mechanics
- **Morning Rush**: 7-9 AM, line forms, patience tested
- **Wait Time**: 5-15 minutes real-time (can skip with Bravado check — cutting line)
- **Shower Quality**: Random hot water availability (first 3 guests get hot water, rest get "character building")
- **The Good Shower**: One shower has consistent hot water, location learned after 2+ nights
- **Privacy Incidents**: Small chance of lock failure, awkward encounter, -Bravado

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Secure THE BUNK and survive, win fridge territory dispute, tell impressive (exaggerated) night story in kitchen, help someone with directions
- **Lose Bravado**: Complain about snoring (everyone snores), ask to change rooms, get caught cutting bathroom line, have your food stolen and react poorly
