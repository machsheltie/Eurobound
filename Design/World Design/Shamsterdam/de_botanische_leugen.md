## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Tulips are legitimately Holland's most iconic export, and the Bloemenmarkt is a genuinely beautiful place. The satire isn't that tulips are fake — they're real. The satire is the elaborate fiction that American/Australian/Asian tourists will successfully grow Dutch tulips from bulbs purchased at a tourist market. The vendor knows. The tourist hopes. The customs agent waits. The bulb sits in a drawer for three years before being thrown away, never planted.

The stall's name, "The Botanical Lie," refers not to fraud but to the polite fiction everyone participates in: "These will DEFINITELY grow in my climate."

### Historical Callback
- **The Date "1637"**: The year of Tulip Mania, when tulip bulbs briefly cost more than houses
- **The Implication**: The Dutch have been selling tulip dreams to gullible people for 400 years
- **The Reality**: It worked then, it works now, the bulbs are real, the dreams are the product

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Tourist souvenir logic, buying something you can't use
- **Secondary Targets**: Import/export agricultural ignorance, "grow your own" optimism
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dutch flower market tradition, international customs realities
- **Humor Approach**: The gap between purchase joy and planting reality
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Respects Dutch flower culture, satirizes tourist purchasing psychology

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling agricultural products to people who can't legally import or successfully grow them
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Customs will probably confiscate, but the stall doesn't mention that
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros buying tulip bulbs they will DEFINITELY plant (they won't)
- **Comedy of Errors**: No one checks import regulations, everyone believes in their future garden
- **Consequence System**: "Bulb Burden" inventory item with uncertain future

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Purchasing authentic Dutch tulip bulbs to grow at home

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Buying pretty bags that will be confiscated, lost, or forgotten

**How impaired are they**: Sober but drunk on aesthetic beauty and souvenir logic

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- They don't have a garden
- They've never grown anything
- They don't know their country's agricultural import regulations
- The "growing instructions" are optimized for Dutch climate
- This is a purchase made for the IDEA, not the execution

**The math**: 90% of purchased bulbs are never planted
- Of the 10% planted: 50% die immediately (wrong climate)
- Of the survivors: 70% produce disappointing results
- Success rate: ~1.5% of purchases result in actual Dutch tulips
- Customer satisfaction: 95% (they don't track outcomes)

**How locals interact**: Polite transaction, no eye contact during the customs question dodge

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Bulb Purchasing System
- **Approach**: Enter stall, immediately surrounded by color
- **Browsing**: Each bin has variety name, color preview, price
- **Selection**: Choose individual bulbs or "Value Bags"
- **The Question That Isn't Asked**: "Where are you taking these?"
- **The Answer That Isn't Given**: Import regulations for your country
- **Transaction**: Money exchanged for hope in paper bag

|------|-------|------|-------------|-------|
| Single Tulip Bulb | €3 | Souvenir | 2% grow success | Individual hope |
| "Rainbow Mix" (10) | €15 | Value Bag | 1% grow success | Variety pack of dreams |
| "Garden Starter" (20) | €25 | Bulk Purchase | 0.5% grow success | Ambition exceeded capacity |

### The "Bulb Burden" Inventory Item
- **Type**: Quest Item / Souvenir / Guilt
- **Weight**: Light (physically), heavy (existentially)
- **Effect**: Occupies inventory slot for remainder of game
- **Fate Determination**: Resolved at customs checkpoint (if implemented) or epilogue
- **Bros' Relationship**: Increasingly abstract discussions about "when we plant them"

### The Customs Reality (Optional Encounter)
If game includes airport/customs checkpoint:
- **Trigger**: Attempting to leave Shamsterdam with bulbs
- **Outcomes**:
  - **Declared (Honest)**: 50% confiscation, 50% allowed (depends on destination)
  - **Hidden (Risky)**: 20% caught (lose bulbs + fine), 80% successful smuggling
  - **Forgot About Them**: They're in the luggage, who knows
- **Bros' Approach**: Genuinely forget they have them (most likely)

### The Planting Epilogue (If Included)
- **Trigger**: Post-credits or future game reference
- **Reveal**: What happened to the bulbs
- **Possibilities**:
  - Never removed from bag (85%)
  - Planted, died immediately (10%)
  - Planted, produced something (4%)
  - Planted, actual Dutch tulip (1%)
- **Bros' Reaction**: Complete non-acknowledgment of tulip existence

### The Vendor's Dance
**The Information That Is Offered:**
- Color of the flower
- Height of the flower
- "Easy to grow!"
- Price

**The Information That Is Not Offered:**
- Your country's agricultural import regulations
- Climate requirements
- Soil composition needs
- The fact that you need to plant them within weeks
- Professional cultivation requirements

**The Questions That Are Deflected:**
- "Can I take these through customs?"
- "Will these grow in Arizona?"
- "Do I need special soil?"
- Response: "Wonderful choice. Would you like a bag?"

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Negotiate "bulk discount," speak Dutch plant names confidently, resist wooden tulip alternative
- **Lose Bravado**: Ask about customs (vendor's expression will haunt you), admit you don't have a garden, buy wooden tulip only
- **Neutral**: Standard purchase, standard delusion

## NPCs & Vendors
### Stall Staff

**Vendor Margriet**
- **Role**: Bulb saleswoman, dream merchant, eye contact avoider during customs questions
- **Appearance**: Middle-aged Dutch woman, floral apron (of course), patient smile
- **Background**: Family stall since 1962, has sold millions of bulbs, tracks no outcomes
- **Demeanor**: Warm, helpful, professionally deaf to certain questions
- **The Margriet Method**: Answer the question you wish they'd asked, not the one they asked
- **Dialogue**:
  - "Welcome! What colors do you love? We have every color. EVERY color."
  - "These are Queen of Night. Very dramatic. Very Shamsterdam. You have a garden?"
  - *if they say no* "A pot works too! A windowsill! A dream!"
  - "The growing instructions are in the bag. Very clear. Mostly clear."
  - *customs question* "They travel very well! Would you like the protective bag? Two euros extra."
  - "My grandmother started this stall. Different bulbs, same joy. Same hope."
  - *large purchase* "You're going to have a BEAUTIFUL garden. I can see it already."
  - "The Rainbow Mix is our most popular. Everyone wants a rainbow."

**Assistant Piet**
- **Role**: Bag filling, heavy lifting, silent accomplice
- **Appearance**: Young Dutch man, dirt under fingernails (credibility), quiet presence
- **Background**: Margriet's nephew, works summers, studies something unrelated
- **Demeanor**: Helpful, silent about the awkward parts
- **Dialogue**:
  - "I'll put them in the bag. Careful, they're fragile. Like dreams."
  - "Growing instructions inside. Read them when you get home. Before you plant. Definitely before."
  - *customs question directed at him* "My aunt handles questions. I handle bulbs."

### Customer NPCs (Atmospheric)

**The Experienced Gardener (Rare)**
- **Composition**: Older person, knows plants, here for specific varieties
- **Behavior**: Asks actual botanical questions, knows import rules, has done this before
- **Dialogue**: "I need Rembrandt tulips. No, not the bag. Individual. I have the CITES permit."
- **Function**: Shows what informed purchasing looks like (bros won't notice)

**The Instagram Tourist**
- **Composition**: Ring light, posing with tulips, not actually buying
- **Behavior**: 50 photos, 0 purchases
- **Dialogue**: "Can I hold like, ALL of them? For the shot? I'll put them back."
- **Function**: Shows the bulbs as aesthetic objects, not plants

**The Honeymooners**
- **Composition**: Newly married couple, buying "their future garden"
- **Behavior**: Choosing varieties together, naming future tulips, very sweet
- **Dialogue**: "We'll plant these at our house. When we have a house. These are our HOUSE tulips."
- **Function**: Heightens the hope/reality gap, makes satire bittersweet

**The Customs-Aware Tourist (Very Rare)**
- **Composition**: Solo traveler, practical shoes, actually read regulations
- **Behavior**: Buys wooden tulips only, no regrets
- **Dialogue**: "I checked. I can't bring bulbs into Australia. So I'm getting these instead."
- **Function**: The rational alternative, ignored by bros

**The Return Customer (Annual)**
- **Composition**: Someone who's been here before, recognizes Margriet
- **Behavior**: Buying again despite unclear success record
- **Dialogue**: "Last year's didn't grow, but maybe THIS year. Different varieties."
- **Function**: Shows the cycle of hope, eternal purchase
