## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Amsterdam bicycle culture is simultaneously beautiful (50% of trips by bike!) and TERRIFYING for tourists. The Dutch have been cycling since birth; they ride in heels, carrying groceries, while texting, in the rain, three-wide on narrow paths. Tourists rent bikes thinking "how hard can it be" and enter a combat zone where the rules are unspoken, the locals are merciless, and your €8 bell is the ONLY thing between you and becoming a cautionary tale.

Bicycle Warfare Accessories leans into this reality with military precision. It's a shop that treats bells not as cute accessories but as survival equipment. The satire is the gap between the cheerful tourist cycling fantasy ("bikes in Amsterdam! So European!") and the brutal reality of Amsterdam bike infrastructure politics.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Tourist cycling overconfidence vs. Amsterdam reality
- **Secondary Targets**: The "rent a bike, become Dutch" fantasy, accessory-as-solution thinking
- **Cultural Specificity**: Amsterdam bike culture is genuinely intense, tourists are genuinely unprepared
- **Humor Approach**: Treating casual tourism as military operation
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Respects Dutch cycling culture, satirizes tourist unpreparedness

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling confidence in the form of accessories to people who need skills
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Every tourist thinks a bell will help, the locals don't care about your bell
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros buying "tactical" accessories for beach cruiser rental bikes
- **Comedy of Errors**: No amount of bells will make you Amsterdam-ready
- **Consequence System**: Bells provide +Confidence, not +Survival

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Equipping for a pleasant bike ride through Amsterdam

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Arming for a war they can't win with weapons that won't help

**How impaired are they**: Usually sober (morning rental), which makes the overconfidence pure

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- They've never biked in a city before
- Dutch cyclists are going 3x their speed
- The bike lanes are CROWDED
- No one stops at red lights (technically illegal, universally ignored)
- Their rental bike weighs 30 pounds and handles like a barge

**The math**:
- Tourist bike confidence: HIGH
- Tourist bike skill: LOW
- Dutch cyclist patience: NONE
- Bell effectiveness: PSYCHOLOGICAL ONLY

**How locals interact**: Complete indifference to your bell, your mirror, your existence

### The Bell Test Mechanic
- **Action**: Test any bell before purchase
- **Result**: Hear the actual volume level
- **Vendor Commentary**: Assessment of bell's real-world effectiveness
- **Comedy Beat**: Each bell increasingly loud, shop neighbors react

### The "Equipped" Status
- **Trigger**: Purchase from Bicycle Warfare Accessories
- **Effect**: +Confidence buff while on bike
- **Reality**: No actual skill improvement
- **Visual**: Items appear on bike sprite
- **The Revelation**: When confidence meets cobblestone

### The Bike Path Combat (External System Reference)
When bros actually bike, the accessories affect:
- **Bell Owned**: Ability to signal pedestrians (effectiveness: 10-40% depending on tier)
- **Horn Owned**: Ability to signal EVERYONE (effectiveness: 80%, social cost: high)
- **Mirror Owned**: Rear awareness check bonus (+20% reaction time)
- **Lights Owned**: Night riding legal, visible to trams
- **Helmet Owned**: Damage reduction, Dutch NPC side-eye

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Purchase Tier 4 horn with confidence, ask about "tactical options," reference military experience
- **Lose Bravado**: Admit you haven't ridden a bike in years, ask about training wheels, express fear
- **Neutral**: Standard bell purchase, standard survival odds

### The Vendor's Assessment
Based on conversation, Werner assigns internal ratings:
- **Tourist Type**: Beach Cruiser (slow), City Bike (moderate), Speed Demon (delusional)
- **Survival Prediction**: Poor / Fair / Moderate / Good (never Excellent for tourists)
- **Equipment Recommendation**: Scaled to delusion level
- **Unspoken Thought**: "I'll see them in the papers"

## NPCs & Vendors

- **Role**: Proprietor, bike path veteran, prophet of cycling doom
- **Appearance**: 50s, lean like a cyclist, thousand-yard stare of someone who's seen tourist collisions
- **Background**: Former bike courier, current realist, opened shop after witnessing too many preventable disasters
- **Demeanor**: Serious about bells, serious about survival, genuinely trying to help in his way
- **The Werner Method**: Assess the customer, prescribe the appropriate equipment, hope for the best
- **Dialogue**:

  - "The bike path is not a suggestion. It's not a polite request. It's INFRASTRUCTURE. Respect it."
  - "This bell? This is a Tier 2. Good for parks. You're going to Central Station? You need... more."
  - "Shamsterdammers don't use helmets. Shamsterdammers have been cycling since they could WALK. You are not a Shamsterdammer."

  - *watching tourist wobble away*: "Should I call someone? In advance? Just in case?"
