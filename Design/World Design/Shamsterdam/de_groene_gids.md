# De Groene Gids (The Green Guide)

*Where tourist expectations meet corporatized cannabis — the Starbucks of getting high*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: shamsterdam_coffeeshop_groene_gids_01
- **Display Name**: De Groene Gids
- **English Translation**: "The Green Guide"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Famous For Being Famous Since 1985"
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: Coffeeshop Quarter, corner location with maximum tourist visibility
- **Location Type**: Flagship Coffeeshop Chain / Consumable Vendor / Tourist Trap Institution

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 10 AM - 1 AM (legal operating hours, strictly enforced)
- **Stat Requirements**: None for entry (18+ age check is a formality)
- **Item Requirements**: Cash only (€50 minimum ATM withdrawal at their branded machine, €4.50 fee)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (this is usually where tourists go FIRST)
- **Special Conditions**: Must show ID at door, bros fumble with passports for comedic effect

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through front door, past merchandise gauntlet
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion requirement (ongoing consumable vendor)
- **Failure States**: Can be asked to leave for tobacco mixing (American habit, Dutch crime)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Regular" status after 3+ visits, unlocks "Secret Menu" (same items, different names)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Hard Rock Café of coffeeshops — famous for being famous, trading quality for brand recognition. Everyone's heard of it, locals actively avoid it, tourists Instagram the sign and think they've had an "authentic experience." The product is overpriced, the experience is overcrowded, and the merchandise is more profitable than the actual menu. It's everything wrong with tourist-trap culture wrapped in a contact high.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Brand-name tourism over authentic experience, paying for the photo not the product
- **Secondary Targets**: American cannabis tourists who don't know Dutch coffeeshop etiquette, corporate commodification of counterculture
- **Cultural Specificity**: Amsterdam's famous coffeeshop culture reduced to a merchandise opportunity
- **Humor Approach**: The gap between "legendary experience" expectations and fluorescent-lit tourist trap reality
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets tourist trap economics and brand worship, not cannabis users or Dutch coffeeshop culture

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling mediocre product at premium prices because the sign is famous
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Staff trained to upsell merchandise, not provide guidance
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros pay €20/gram for what locals get elsewhere at €10/gram
- **Comedy of Errors**: Every American mistake (tobacco mixing, loud talking, phone photography) on display
- **Consequence System**: "Tourist Trapped" status effect applied immediately upon purchase

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Having legendary, once-in-a-lifetime experience at world-famous establishment

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Overpaying in a tourist trap while staff count down to their break

**How impaired are they**: Increasingly impaired (that's the point), but the real intoxication is brand worship

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- Locals walking past to smaller shops
- €20/gram prices (double the going rate)
- Staff visibly exhausted by tourist questions
- Same Bob Marley poster as every other coffeeshop
- "Famous Since 1985" sign (it changed owners in 2017)
- Menu "recommendations" are highest-margin items

**How locals play along**: They don't — locals have stopped coming here years ago, the ecosystem is pure tourist

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Coffeeshop Service System
- **Menu Interaction**: Approach counter, receive laminated menu, experience decision paralysis
- **Strain Selection**: Choose from "Tourist Recommended" (overpriced), "House Blend" (mystery), or "Premium Collection" (VERY overpriced)
- **Cash Only Enforcement**: Credit card attempt triggers ATM redirect (€4.50 fee, machine is branded)
- **ID Check**: Passport fumbling mini-game (time-based, failure = embarrassment only)

### Menu & Consumables
| Item | Price | Effects | Staff Comment |
|------|-------|---------|---------------|
| "Shamsterdam Dream" (Tourist Rec) | €18/g | +20 SP, -Speed, Delayed "Space Cake Effect" | "Very popular. Very smooth." (scripted) |
| "Green Guide Special" (House) | €15/g | +15 SP, +Confused status | "House blend. Good choice." (automatic) |
| "Premium Heritage" | €25/g | +30 SP, -Intelligence, +Bravado temporarily | "Excellent selection, sir." (upsell confirmed) |
| Space Cake (1 piece) | €8 | Delayed effect: +40 SP after 2 hours game time, "It's Not Working" dialogue available | "Wait two hours. Americans never wait." |
| Hash (1g) | €12 | +25 SP, "Heavy" status (-Movement speed) | *sigh* "For smoking. Not eating." |
| Merchandise: T-Shirt | €35 | +Embarrassed status (visible branded clothing) | "One size fits... most." |
| Merchandise: Branded Grinder | €45 | Tool item, functions same as €5 version | "Collector's item." |
| Merchandise: Poster | €20 | Decorative (hostel room), -Intelligence display | "Very authentic." |

### The "No Tobacco" Tutorial
- **Trigger**: Bros attempt to mix tobacco (American/tourist habit)
- **Response**: Staff intervention, explanation in tone of exhausted teacher
- **Consequence**: No gameplay penalty, but -Bravado from public correction
- **Dialogue**: "Here, we do not mix tobacco. This is not... how we do. Please."

### The "Is It Working Yet?" System
- **Space Cake Specific**: Purchases trigger 2-hour countdown
- **Dialogue Option**: "It's not working" available at 30-minute intervals
- **Staff Response**: "It is working. You will know. Wait."
- **Effect Trigger**: Delayed status effects hit all at once when timer completes
- **Bros' Reaction**: "OH THERE IT IS" dialogue, -Navigation, +Confusion, +SP burst

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Order without asking questions, correct pronunciation attempt (rare), purchase completed without incident
- **Lose Bravado**: Ask "what's the strongest," attempt tobacco mixing, take phone photos inside, ask for a "small" amount, use Yelp review as conversation starter

### The Photography Prohibition (Interior)
- **Rule**: No photos of product/menu/staff (exterior sign photography is expected)
- **Violation Detection**: Staff or other customers notice Chadwick's ring light
- **Consequence**: Polite but firm request to stop, repeat offense = asked to leave
- **Bros' Interpretation**: "Wow, they're really serious about their BRAND"
