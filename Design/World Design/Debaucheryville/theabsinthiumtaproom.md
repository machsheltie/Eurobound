## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Old-world bank vault meets post-Soviet flea market. Everything feels officially unofficial: stamped paperwork that doesn't match, clerks with too many calculators, transactions that exist in bureaucratic grey areas.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Post-communist economic grey markets exploiting Western tourist currency confusion
- **Secondary Targets**: Cryptocurrency culture, underground economy legitimization, financial system workarounds
- **Cultural Specificity**: Eastern European informal economy commercialized for tourist money-changing needs
- **Humor Approach**: GTA-style financial satire with Eurotrip tourist exploitation comedy
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets exploitative currency practices and tourist confusion, not legitimate local commerce

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Currency manipulation and black-market trading exploiting tourist financial desperation
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Underground bazaar treating illegal transactions as legitimate business
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros think they're getting "insider rates" while being obviously manipulated
- **Comedy of Errors**: Complex financial schemes designed to confuse and overcharge tourists
- **Consequence System**: Risk/reward meter tracks notoriety with escalating "verification fees"

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Getting exclusive access to underground financial network with insider rates

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Being systematically fleeced by professional currency manipulators in fake-legitimate operation

**How impaired are they**: Financially desperate enough to trust obvious black-market operation with official-looking paperwork

**Obvious signs they ignore**: Mismatched receipts, "brokers" with too many calculators, rates that change based on how loudly you ask

**How locals play along**: Professional fixers treat obvious scams as legitimate financial services while maximizing tourist exploitation

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Currency Conversion System (Original)
- **Split-Flap Rate Board Mini-Game**: Interact during "green ticks" for improved rates, avoid "red ticks"
- **Street Rep/Charm Influence**: Stats can nudge board for small temporary rate improvements
- **Dynamic Rate Fluctuation**: Exchange rates change based on player timing and negotiation

### Barter Window System (Original)
- **Non-Currency Trading**: Concert wristbands, metro day-passes, gag items for rare gear
- **UV-Scan Timing Checks**: Identify counterfeits via quick reaction mini-game
- **Consumables and Vouchers**: Trade for forged vouchers and specialized consumables

### Shady Contracts (Original Black-Market Services)
- **"Buy a Favor"**: Pay to reduce area enemy aggro for 10 minutes
- **"Sell Your Dignity"**: Exchange Embarrassed status for one-time rate boost
- **Risk/Reward Tracking**: Each transaction affects Notoriety meter

### Premium Stock System (Post-Boss Content)
**Unlocked after Count Velvet Absinthe defeat:**
- **Saint of Low Sodium Medal**: Alternative acquisition source
- **Velvet Rope Clip**: Duplicate for build crafting options  
- **Forged Duty-Free Receipt**: Shop discounts in designated area
- **Hidden Gate Access**: Back counter reveals premium inventory

### NPCs & Vendors (Original Cast)
- **Mrs. Rata (Main Clerk)**: "Cash speaks. Whining does not." Sells practical consumables
- **The UV Man**: Tests items for fee, sells Pocket UV Wand tool
- **Token Goblin**: Arcade tokens, transit cards, queue-skip chips specialist
- **Rate Sharks**: Grey-coated fixers with nicotine fingers and immaculate ledgers
- **Expat Flippers**: Travel cards and concert wristband specialists
- **Counterfeit Connoisseurs**: UV wand bill testing like wine sommeliers
- **Influencer Resellers**: "Limited" VIP wristbands mysteriously always say WEDNESDAY

### Original NPC Dialogue
