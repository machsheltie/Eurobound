# Jakub the Local Observer

*"Američané."*
*(Americans.)*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_local_observer_jakub_01`
- **Display Name**: Jakub
- **Internal Name**: Jakub Novotný (no relation to tattoo artist)
- **Role**: Ambient NPC / Local Color / Czech Commentary
- **Associated Quest**: None (background atmosphere)
- **Primary Location**: Astronomical Cock-Up Square, Debaucheryville
- **Secondary Appearances**: Same location only

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Having a cigarette break with a friend, watching tourists, and providing Czech commentary on the absurdity of American behavior. He's not a scammer - just a local enjoying free entertainment.

### Function in Scene
Provides Czech dialogue (with subtitles) that lets players know how locals perceive the bros' behavior. His commentary is deadpan, bemused, and represents the "normal person" perspective.

---

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 30-40 |
| Build | Average |
| Energy | Relaxed, observational |

### Clothing
- **Style**: Normal casual clothes (not tourist, not scammer)
- **Accessories**: Cigarette, coffee cup

### Distinguishing Features
- Clearly NOT a tourist or vendor
- Standing to the side, watching
- Has a friend nearby for dialogue

---

## 💬 Dialogue (All Czech with Subtitles)

> "Podívej. Ti Američané brečí."
> *(Look. The Americans are crying.)*

**Friend Response (Czech, subtitled):**
> "Kvůli hodinám?"
> *(Over the clock?)*

**Jakub (Czech, subtitled):**
> "Američané."
> *(Americans.)*

### Watching Bros Get Blessed
> "Ten vysoký chce požehnání od toho chlapa v županu."
> *(The tall one wants a blessing from the guy in the bathrobe.)*

### Watching Bros Buy Wine
> "Zaplatili šedesát euro za krabicové víno."
> *(They paid sixty euros for boxed wine.)*

### Watching Tattoo Decision
> "Ten vysoký chce tetování."
> *(The tall one wants a tattoo.)*

**Friend:**
> "Z Euro Ink?"
> *(From Euro Ink?)*

**Jakub:**
> "Američané."
> *(Americans.)*

# Disappointed Tourist

*"...that's it?"*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_disappointed_tourist_01`
- **Display Name**: Disappointed Tourist
- **Internal Name**: Generic Tourist
- **Role**: Ambient NPC / Reaction Marker
- **Associated Quest**: None (atmosphere)
- **Primary Location**: Astronomical Cock-Up Square, Debaucheryville
- **Secondary Appearances**: Same location only

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Doing
Standing in the crowd after the 38-second clock show, expressing the disappointment that everyone except the bros feels.

### Function in Scene
Provides the "normal" reaction to contrast with the bros' spiritual awakening. Their single line - "...that's it?" - punctuates the clock show's anticlimax.

---

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | Any |
| Build | Any |
| Energy | Confused, let down |

### Clothing
- **Style**: Tourist casual (identifiable but not stereotypical)
- **Accessories**: Camera, phone

### Distinguishing Features
- Clearly confused expression
- Looking around to see if they missed something
- Checking guidebook/phone

---

## 💬 Dialogue

### Post-Clock Show
**After the 38-second show ends:**
> "...that's it?"

*Looks around at crowd*
*Checks guidebook*
*Shrugs*

---

## 🎬 Animation States

### Idle Animations
| Animation | Description | Frequency |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| confused_looking | Scanning for more show | 50% |
| guidebook_check | Consulting materials | 30% |
| disappointed_shrug | Accepting reality | 20% |

---

## 📝 Development Notes

- Single line character, but crucial timing
- "...that's it?" must land in the silence after clock show
- Contrasts perfectly with bros' tearful reaction
- Can be different tourists on different visits
- Universal experience of clock disappointment
