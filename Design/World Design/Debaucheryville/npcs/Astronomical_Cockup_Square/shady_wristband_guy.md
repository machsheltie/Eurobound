# NPC Profile: Shady Wristband Guy

*"Psst. Americans, yes? You want... the VIP experience?"*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_shady_wristband_guy_01`
- **Display Name**: Shady Wristband Guy
- **Internal Name**: Martin the VIP Specialist
- **Role**: Scammer / Quest Giver
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_vip_wristbands_01`
- **Primary Location**: Astronomical Cock-Up Square, Debaucheryville
- **Secondary Appearances**: Various nightlife areas (selling to other tourists)

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Selling children's birthday party wristbands (bulk purchased for €0.003 each) as "VIP All-Access" passes for €30 each. The wristbands say "Je mi 7!" in Czech, which translates to "I'm 7!" - as in "I am seven years old."

### What Bros Believe
A connected local is hooking them up with exclusive Level Seven VIP wristbands that grant elite status at every club in Debaucheryville. This is the kind of insider connection that separates REAL travelers from tourists.

### Profit Margin
**999,900%** - He paid approximately €3 for 1,000 wristbands. At €30 each, he's living his best life.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**Hustle Culture Personified** - He sees tourists as walking ATMs and feels zero guilt about it. In his mind, he's providing a SERVICE. The wristbands make people FEEL VIP, and isn't that what really matters?

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Entrepreneurial | Always looking for the next mark |
| Quick-talking | Closes deals before buyers can think |
| Shameless | Zero guilt about the scam |
| Observant | Spots Americans from 50 meters |
| Adaptable | Adjusts pitch based on target |

### Internal Monologue
> "Level Seven! I can't believe I said that and they BOUGHT it. Americans are incredible. They WANT to believe they're special. I'm basically a therapist who takes cash."

### Motivation
Simple economics. The wristbands cost nothing. The tourists have money. The clubs are free to enter anyway. He's just... facilitating an experience. A premium experience. Worth every euro.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach Sequence
**Initial Contact** (conspiratorial whisper, looking around dramatically):
> "Psst. Americans, yes? You want... the VIP experience? I have something special. Very exclusive. Not for regular tourists."

**The Pitch**:
> "These wristbands... they open ALL doors. Velvet Curtain Club. Absinthe Arcade VIP section. Even places tourists don't know exist. Very exclusive. Only €30 each."

### The Critical Lie
*When Bradley asks about the Czech text:*

**Bradley**: "What does the writing say?"

**Shady Wristband Guy** (without hesitation, total confidence):
> "It says 'Level Seven Access.' Highest tier. Only for VIPs."

### Sales Close
*Pulls wristbands from coat with flourish*:
> "Special material. See? Very official. The bouncers, they see this, they KNOW. No waiting. No cover. Pure VIP."

### Post-Sale
> "You are now Level Seven. Very exclusive. Enjoy your VIP lifestyle."
*Already scanning for next marks*

### If Player Declines (Rare Path)
> "Your loss. Level Seven doesn't wait. Maybe I see you later... in the regular line."
*Shrugs, immediately approaches another tourist group*
