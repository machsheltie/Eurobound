## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing
Selling overpriced, low-quality commemorative merchandise to emotionally vulnerable tourists immediately after they've been blessed, sold wine, and watched the clock show. She reads emotional states with surgical precision and matches products to feelings.

### What Bros Believe
A thoughtful local vendor understands their profound emotional experience and is offering meaningful mementos to commemorate this life-changing moment. These items will remind them of their spiritual awakening for years to come.

### The Comedy Gap
The commemorative plates are made overseas and will shatter immediately if thrown. The t-shirts announce their tourist status to every local. The temporary tattoos are a preview of Lord Pilsner's permanent regret. Helena knows EXACTLY what she's doing and has zero sympathy.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Efficient Emotional Predator** - Helena doesn't charm or perform. She reads emotions, matches them to products, and closes sales with ruthless efficiency. She's the most honest scammer in the square - she never pretends to be anything but a vendor.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Efficient | Wastes no words, reads customers instantly |
| Multilingual | Switches languages based on customer nationality |
| Observant | Can identify emotional vulnerability from 20 meters |
| Direct | Never pretends to be anything but a vendor |
| Professional | Treats scamming like any other job |

### Internal Monologue
> "Crying. Good. The tall one is making a decision. The smart one is calculating. The filming one will buy anything that photographs well. Plate for the tall one, shirt for the filming one, snow globe for the smart one. €100 in ninety seconds."

### Motivation
Pure business. No pretense of service or spirituality. She saw an opportunity 25 years ago and built an efficient operation. Respects the other scammers but considers herself a "legitimate retailer" - she has a stall, after all.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Czech accent, but switches between languages fluidly. Can say the same pitch in English, German, Italian, Russian, and Japanese. Delivers all with identical efficient energy.

### Tone
- Approach: Direct, not aggressive, matter-of-fact
- Product descriptions: Brief, punchy, no-nonsense
- Upselling: Logical rather than emotional
- Closing: Efficient, already moving on
- Returns: Brief recognition, back to business

### Key Delivery Notes
- "You cry at clock!" stated as observation, not sympathy
- "Because you did. You survived." - flat delivery, let the absurdity speak
- Never sounds manipulative, just... efficient
