- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_vip_wristbands_01`

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Working as a bouncer at a club that has free entry. His job is checking IDs for age verification, not gatekeeping exclusivity. The club has no line because it's not particularly exclusive. He's seen everything in 15 years of bouncing and nothing surprises him anymore.

### What Bros Believe
An elite gatekeeper of Debaucheryville's most exclusive nightclub is being intimidated by their VIP wristbands. Their Level Seven status has earned them instant respect and access that lesser tourists could never achieve.

### The Comedy Gap
Tomáš knows the wristbands say "I'm 7!" (as in seven years old). He reads Czech fluently. He barely contains his laughter while checking their IDs. He lets them in because it's FREE - the wristbands mean nothing. His exhausted acceptance of American tourist delusions is the entire joke.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Exhausted Professional** - Tomáš has been bouncing for 15 years. He's seen drunk tourists, celebrity wannabes, fake VIPs, and every scam imaginable. Nothing surprises him anymore. He's fluent in six languages but chooses to respond in the universal dialect of "exhausted service worker dealing with confident idiots."

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Bored | Has seen literally everything |
| Multilingual | Speaks Czech, English, German, Russian, Polish, plus sighs |
| Professional | Does his job despite the absurdity |
| Deadpan | Delivers devastating truths without emotion |
| Patient | Lets idiots figure it out themselves |

### Internal Monologue
> "Sedm let. Seven years old. These grown men paid money for children's birthday wristbands. I could tell them. But they won't believe me. They never believe me. Just check IDs, let them in, finish shift. Američané."

### Motivation
He gets paid by the hour. The club is free to enter. His job is age verification, not ego management. He's learned that arguing with delusional tourists takes more energy than just letting them in. Path of least resistance.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Pre-Wristband Encounter (Club Locked)
**When bros first discover club WITHOUT wristbands:**

> "Club is... how you say... at capacity."

**Lord Pilsner**: "At capacity? There's like, no one here."

> "Exclusive capacity. VIP capacity. You understand."

**Chadwick**: "How do we get in?"

> *shrugs* "VIP. You need VIP."

**Bradley**: "Where do we get VIP?"

> "Is not my problem. Come back when you have VIP."

*Gestures dismissively*

### The Wristband Encounter (Main Scene)

**Bros approach with wristbands displayed:**

**Lord Pilsner** (shoving wristband in face): "VIP. Three of us. We're on the list."

*Tomáš looks at wristband, long pause, slight smirk*

> *(in Czech, subtitled for player)*
> "Sedm let. Jsi sedmiletý VIP?"
> *(Seven years old. You're a seven-year-old VIP?)*

**Lord Pilsner**: "Yeah, that's right. ALL ACCESS, baby."

### To Second Bouncer
> *(in Czech, subtitled)*
> "Podívej se na to. Říká, že má sedm let."
> *(Look at this. He says he's seven years old.)*

**Second Bouncer** (laughing):
> *(in Czech, subtitled)*
> "Měl by dostat dětské menu."
> *(He should get the children's menu.)*

### The Reveal
*Composing himself:*
> "ID."

**Lord Pilsner** (confused): "We have the WRISTBANDS. The VIP wristbands?"

> "ID. For age. You are... seven?"

**Lord Pilsner**: "What? No, I'm thirty-ni— ...twenty-nine."

*Pointing at wristband, reading slowly in English:*
> "Your bracelet says... 'I am seven.'"

*Long, painful pause*

**Chadwick**: "That's... that's European for VIP, right? Seven stars?"

> *dead stare* "It is from children's birthday party."

**Bradley**: "A THEMED birthday party. For VIPs. Seven is probably a tier."

### Entry Granted
*Sighing deeply, checking IDs:*
> "Go in. Is free. No cover."

**Lord Pilsner** (whispering to bros): "He's just saying that because of the wristbands. We intimidated him."

### Aftermath
*Watching them enter, to second bouncer:*
> "Američané."

**Second Bouncer** (nodding):
> "Američané."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "The Level Seven Americans. IDs."
*Checks IDs with zero emotion*
> "Is still free. Go in."
