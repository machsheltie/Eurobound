## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Connoisseur's Cache satirizes "elevated" retail — the way ordinary items become special through presentation, narrative, and the implication that buying them makes you sophisticated. It's a gift shop pretending to be a gallery pretending to be essential.

The deeper joke: Most items are genuinely useful. The "artisanal pain relief" works as well as regular medicine. But you're paying for the STORY, the EXPERIENCE, the feeling that your headache is more cultured than other headaches.

### The Provenance Problem
Every purchase comes with a speech:
- Where the item was sourced
- Who crafted it (and their philosophy)
- Why it's superior to alternatives
- How it will enhance your journey
- This speech cannot be skipped (until Discerning Customer status)

The speeches are accurate but unnecessary. The shopkeeper genuinely believes this information enriches your life.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Premium retail narrative, "curated" culture
- **Secondary Targets**: Gift shop pretension, unnecessary certification
- **Cultural Specificity**: Viennese museum shop traditions, Habsburg souvenir culture
- **Humor Approach**: Shopkeeper's sincerity makes the absurdity funnier
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes retail practices, not cultural appreciation

### Buffoon Exploitation
The shop sees opportunity in confusion:
- Bros won't understand what they're buying
- They'll nod along to provenance speeches
- They'll pay extra because it SEEMS important
- They'll leave with certificates they'll never read

### The Provenance System

**Standard Purchase**:
1. Select item
2. Shopkeeper retrieves item reverently
3. Provenance speech begins (30-60 seconds)
4. Cannot interrupt without offense
5. Purchase completes with certificate

**Provenance Speech Example**:
*Selecting "Heritage Herbal Tea"*
> "Ah, excellent choice. This blend dates to 1847, when the Müller family of the high valleys began cultivating these specific herbs. The lavender is from their original garden — the same plants, maintained for generations. The chamomile is wild-harvested by trained herbalists who understand the proper lunar cycle for maximum potency. The blend ratio has never been altered, except once in 1923 when a flood required temporary substitution, which the family considers 'the lost year.' You're not just buying tea. You're buying continuity."

**Discerning Customer** (€200 spent):
- Can politely skip speeches: "The usual, please."
- Shopkeeper nods approvingly: "You already understand."
- Transaction completes in 5 seconds
- Still get certificate (can't escape that)

### Certificate System

Every purchase includes a certificate:
- Item name and description
- Date of purchase
- Authenticity guarantee
- Serial number (yes, for tea)
- Shopkeeper's signature

**Certificate Comedy**:
- Accumulate enough and inventory fills with paper
- Some NPCs impressed by certificates
- Others confused: "You have a certificate... for pain medicine?"
- Can be sold back for €1 each (insulting but possible)

- **Role**: Primary vendor, provenance deliverer, certificate provider
- **Appearance**: Wire-rim glasses, vest with watch chain, perpetually holding something carefully
- **Personality**: Genuinely passionate about the stories behind objects
- **Function**: Shop interface, provenance speeches, specialty access
- **Dialogue**:

  - "This isn't mere commerce. This is... curation."
