# The Gilded Suite

*A "historic palace" converted to luxury lodging where the five-star reviews are suspiciously uniform, the host is unsettlingly attentive, and checkout time is... negotiable*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: sinfonia_imperialdistrict_gilded_suite_01
- **Display Name**: The Gilded Suite
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Rest Well. We're Watching."
- **World Location**: Sinfonia
- **Area Zone**: Imperial District, near Imperial Square
- **Location Type**: Luxury Accommodation / Mystery Location / Optional Dungeon / Couchsurfing Alternative

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: Check-in 3 PM - 11 PM, Check-out by 11 AM (strictly enforced)
- **Stat Requirements**: None to book, but +2 INT recommended to notice red flags
- **Item Requirements**: €150/night (suspiciously reasonable for a "palace")
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (advertised on bulletin boards citywide)
- **Special Conditions**: Must agree to "House Rules" (lengthy, concerning document)

### Booking System
Unlike couchsurfing, Das Schlafgemach requires payment:
- **Standard Room**: €150/night (HP/SP restore, but...)
- **Deluxe Suite**: €300/night (better restore, more surveillance)
- **The Royal Chamber**: €500/night (full restore, maximum creepy)
- **All bookings**: Require signing the "Guest Agreement" (nobody reads it)

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Check out by 11 AM (front door unlocks)
- **Early Exit**: Possible but requires finding the "emergency release" (puzzle)
- **Overstay**: Host becomes... insistent
- **Completion Requirements**: Survive one night OR uncover the truth
- **Failure States**: "Overstayed Welcome" — trapped until you solve the exit puzzle

## Visual Design
### Exterior Description
- **Approach**: Grand Habsburg-era palace facade, immaculately maintained
- **Entrance**: Ornate iron gates, manicured gardens, fountain that watches you (sculptural eyes)
- **Signage**: 
  - Brass plaque: "Das Schlafgemach — Est. 1784 — ★★★★★"
  - Smaller sign: "As Featured in 'Authentic Palace Living' Magazine" (magazine doesn't exist)
- **Atmosphere**: Too perfect, too clean, too quiet
- **Environmental Context**: Neighboring buildings seem to lean away from it

### Interior Layout — The Gilded Trap

**The Grand Foyer (Check-In)**
- Crystal chandeliers, marble floors, classical music playing
- Reception desk staffed by impeccably dressed host
- Guest book with centuries of signatures (handwriting suspiciously similar)
- "House Rules" framed on wall (27 rules, increasingly specific)
- Elevator that only goes to YOUR floor (how does it know?)

**The Guest Rooms (Varying Tiers)**

*Standard Room*:
- Comfortable but dated furnishings
- Portrait whose eyes definitely follow you
- Bathroom with excellent water pressure and no lock
- Window that doesn't quite open all the way
- "Complimentary" fruit basket (fruit never ripens or rots)

*Deluxe Suite*:
- Larger space, better view
- Multiple portraits (all watching)
- Bathroom WITH lock (but lock doesn't work)
- Balcony with beautiful view (door sticks)
- More fruit (still eternal)

*The Royal Chamber*:
- Palatial space, canopy bed, the works
- Wall of portraits (they're having meetings when you're not looking)
- Bathroom like a spa (cameras shaped like fixtures)
- Private terrace (iron bars painted as decoration)
- So much fruit

**The Hallways (Liminal Spaces)**
- Identical doors in every direction
- Carpet patterns that shift subtly
- Sconces that dim when you look at them
- Other guests occasionally glimpsed (never the same ones twice)

**The Breakfast Room (Morning Trap)**
- Lavish continental breakfast included
- Other "guests" present (NPCs who speak in reviews)
- Host presides, watching you eat
- Food is excellent (suspiciously so)

**The Basement (Secret Area)**
- Accessed via hidden passage OR getting lost in hallways
- Contains: Old guest records, previous "hosts," the truth
- Optional dungeon content for curious players
- Explains why checkout is "strictly enforced"

### The Uncanny Details
Every element is slightly wrong:
- Clocks all show different times (but none show the CURRENT time)
- Room numbers change between visits
- Minibar restocks itself while you watch
- The "Do Not Disturb" sign is never quite where you left it
- Wi-Fi password is your grandmother's maiden name (you never told them)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Das Schlafgemach satirizes luxury short-term rentals — the suspiciously glowing reviews, the hosts who are "just SO passionate about hospitality," and the underlying creepiness of staying in a stranger's heavily monitored "historic" property.

The deeper joke: Every weird Airbnb fear is real here, just slightly exaggerated. The surveillance, the fake reviews, the host who's TOO available — Das Schlafgemach takes the subtext and makes it text.

### The Host — Herr Gastfreund
The heart of the horror (comedy horror):
- **Appearance**: Impeccable period-appropriate suit, unnaturally white smile, never blinks
- **Availability**: Always appears when needed (and when not needed)
- **Hospitality**: Aggressive, smothering, inescapable
- **Mystery**: Has he always been the host? How old IS this listing?
- **Philosophy**: "A guest who leaves is a failure of hospitality."

He's not malicious. He genuinely believes eternal guests are happy guests.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Short-term rental culture, professional hosting, review manipulation
- **Secondary Targets**: Historic property tourism, Habsburg nostalgia, surveillance hospitality
- **Cultural Specificity**: Viennese palace hotels, Austrian "gemütlichkeit" (coziness) pushed to extreme
- **Humor Approach**: Horror-comedy, the politest possible menace
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes hosting culture, not actual housing issues

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Obsessive hospitality masking control issues
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: "Historic" claims exaggerated, reviews all fake, surveillance as "service"
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who don't read agreements or notice obvious red flags
- **Comedy of Errors**: Ignoring every warning sign, praising the prison
- **Consequence System**: Overstay = puzzle trap, discovery = boss encounter option

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Accommodation System

**Booking Options**:
| Room | Price | Rest Quality | Surveillance Level | Special |
|------|-------|-------------|-------------------|---------|
| Standard | €150 | 80% HP/SP | Medium | Basic creepy |
| Deluxe | €300 | 100% HP/SP | High | Extra portraits |
| Royal | €500 | 120% HP/SP + buff | Maximum | Full experience |

**Rest Effects**:
- HP/SP restoration as listed
- "Well-Rested" buff (varies by room)
- BUT: "Being Watched" debuff until leaving (-5% to stealth)
- Random "night events" may interrupt rest

### Night Events (Random Selection)

**The Visit** (Common):
- 3 AM knock on door
- Herr Gastfreund "just checking in"
- Options: Answer (social encounter), Ignore (he checks anyway), Investigate (clue found)

**The Rearrangement** (Uncommon):
- Wake to find furniture moved
- Your belongings "organized" helpfully
- Nothing missing, somehow worse
- Clue to basement hidden in arrangement

**The Other Guests** (Rare):
- Glimpse of previous guests in hallway
- They speak in five-star review phrases
- Following them leads to basement
- "The breakfast buffet is EXCEPTIONAL" *empty eyes*

**The Portrait Party** (Very Rare — Royal Chamber Only):
- Wake to find all portraits facing your bed
- They weren't like that when you slept
- One portrait now includes YOUR face
- Major clue to boss encounter

### The 27 House Rules

Selected rules from the agreement (comedy document):

1. Checkout is at 11 AM. This is non-negotiable.
2. Guests may not rearrange furniture. It knows where it belongs.
3. The third-floor east wing is for staff only. There is no third floor.
4. Do not speak to other guests about your room number.
5. The portraits are original artwork. Do not make eye contact for more than 4 seconds.
7. Rule 6 has been removed for your comfort.
8. The fruit is complimentary. The fruit is eternal.
12. If you hear your name at night, it is probably the house settling.
15. The basement does not exist. Do not look for it.
19. Herr Gastfreund has always been the host.
27. By signing this agreement, you acknowledge that checkout is at 11 AM.

### The Basement Discovery (Optional Dungeon)

**Finding the Entrance**:
- Follow clues from night events
- Notice the hallway inconsistencies
- Check behind the eternal fruit painting
- OR simply get lost enough times

**Basement Contents**:
- Guest records dating to 1784 (same handwriting)
- Rooms full of "lost" items from previous guests
- The original Herr Gastfreund portrait (looks identical to current)
- "The Hospitality Engine" — the truth about the palace
- Optional boss: The Previous Host

### The Previous Host (Optional Boss)

**Boss Profile**:
- **Name**: The First Guest (Never Checked Out)
- **Appearance**: Guest from 1784, formal attire, confused expression frozen forever
- **Backstory**: The first guest who loved it so much he never left. Literally.
- **Status**: Not malicious, just unable to check out

**Encounter Type**: Puzzle boss, not combat
- Must help him find the original checkout desk
- Navigate shifting hallways
- Solve "guest agreement" riddles
- Convince him it's okay to leave

**Victory**:
- He finally checks out (tearful, grateful)
- Herr Gastfreund appears, mildly distressed
- Basement secrets revealed
- **Reward**: "Eternal Guest Card" — free stays, no surveillance, immunity to house rules

### Herr Gastfreund Confrontation (Alternative Boss)

**Triggered By**: Attempting to leave after discovering basement secrets

**Encounter Type**: Social combat / chase sequence
- He wants you to stay. Forever.
- Not violent, just VERY INSISTENT
- Must navigate his hospitality attacks
- Escape through puzzle sequence

**His Attacks**:
- "One More Night": Time manipulation, extends stay
- "Five-Star Service": Overwhelming kindness, decision paralysis
- "The Guest Book": Previous guests testify to excellence
- "Checkout Denied": Locks all exits

**Escape Methods**:
- Complete previous host quest (he lets you go, confused)
- Find the original deed (proves property violations)
- Leave a ONE-STAR review (his weakness)
- Convince him to take a vacation (existential crisis)

**Reward**: "Honest Review" badge (immunity to fake reviews citywide), €500 refund

- Costs money (significant)
- Professional rather than personal
- Better amenities, worse vibes

Players can compare experiences (couchsurfing at Moderato vs. staying here).
