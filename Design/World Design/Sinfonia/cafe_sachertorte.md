## Satirical Design

### Thematic Focus
- **Primary Theme**: "Commodification of Tradition" - Vienna's café culture reduced to tourist extraction
- **Secondary Theme**: "The Tyranny of Refinement" - atmosphere so pretentious it's fragile
- **Cultural Target**: European café culture that trades on heritage while charging €8 for espresso
- **Vice Element**: Food snobbery, cultural gatekeeping, exploiting tourists' desire for "authentic" experiences

### Satirical Elements
- **Environmental Satire**:
  - Price menu in tiny font (so tourists can't see until billed)
  - "Historic" café that's been renovated 6 times but keeps the old photos
  - Staff trained to make tourists feel uncultured
  - Chess sets no one plays but everyone photographs
- **Character-Based Satire**:
  - Head Waiter who treats coffee orders like moral failings
  - Regulars who've been nursing the same espresso for 3 hours
  - Tourist couples fighting over whether this is "worth it"
  - Intellectual poseurs having loud philosophical debates
- **Dialogue Satire**:
  - "Ah, you would like... the American coffee? Perhaps a diner would suit you better."
  - "This cake recipe has not changed since 1832. The price, however..."
  - "You take PHOTOS of the cake? This is not Instagram. This is Sachertorte."
- **Mechanical Satire**:
  - Prices mysteriously increase for obvious tourists
  - Ordering "correctly" (in German) grants small discount
  - Staff helpfulness scales inversely with how American you seem

### Social Media Moments
- **Instagram Opportunity**: "Perfect" table setup - cake slice, coffee, newspaper, golden afternoon light
- **TikTok Potential**: 
  - Head Waiter's withering assessment of orders
  - Gustav eating €18 cake slices in seconds
  - Property damage counter ticking up in real-time
- **Quotable Content**:
  - "THAT HORSE JUST ATE THREE SACHERTORTES! THAT IS €54!" - Head Waiter
  - "I mean, he has good taste?" - Bradley, unhelpfully
  - "We'll pay you back! Eventually! We're Americans!" - Lord Pilsner, not reassuring anyone

### Seedy Underbelly
- **Surface Presentation**: Elegant café preserving Viennese tradition
- **Hidden Reality**: 
  - "Historic recipe" actually modified in 1987 to reduce costs
  - Staff schnapps breaks are frequent and generous
  - Kitchen operates on near-exploitative wages
  - Regular "intellectuals" are actually real estate agents
  - Wine cellar hasn't been legally inspected since 1993

## Quest Integration: Fiaker Fiasco Phase 2

### Gustav's Café Rampage

**Phase ID**: `fiaker_fiasco_cafe_phase`

**Trigger**: Bros follow Gustav from Opera House Plaza

**Setup**:
- Gustav enters through patio door (knocks over umbrella stand)
- Head Waiter initially doesn't notice (facing other direction)
- Gustav approaches cake display with PURPOSE
- Players arrive seconds later, panting

**Sequence**:

1. **Entry Chaos**
   - Gustav crashes through patio furniture (€85 damage - one table)
   - Umbrella stand topples (€15 damage)
   - A patron's coffee spills (no charge, just screaming)

2. **The Cake Heist**
   - Gustav reaches cake display
   - Contemplates offerings with disturbing intelligence
   - Selects the Sachertorte (of course)
   - Head Waiter: "NEIN! NEIN! DAS PFERD!"
   - Gustav eats entire €85 cake in three bites

3. **Secondary Chaos**
   - Bros attempt to corner Gustav
   - Gustav knocks over two more tables (€80 damage)
   - Chandelier narrowly avoided (€2,000 saved)
   - Gustav grabs opera cake slice on way out (€22 damage)

4. **Exit Strategy**
   - Gustav breaks through service door to kitchen
   - Kitchen chaos sounds (pots crashing, chef screaming)
   - Gustav emerges from back alley door with cream on face
   - Continues toward Opera Backstage

**Damage Tally**:
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Patio table | €85 |
| Umbrella stand | €15 |
| Full Sachertorte | €85 |
| Two marble tables | €80 |
| Opera cake slice | €22 |
| "Emotional damages" | €53 |
| **Total** | **€340** |

**NPC Reactions**:
- Head Waiter: Full meltdown, demands immediate payment
- Patrons: Evacuating, some filming on phones
- Kitchen Staff: Screaming in German, brandishing ladles
- One Regular: Hasn't looked up from newspaper

**Dialogue Options**:
1. "We'll catch the horse and pay you back!" → Quest continues, debt logged
2. "Here's €340 right now." → Quest continues, no café debt (requires funds)

## Environmental Systems

### Status Effects

**Acquired at Location**:
- "Caffeinated" (coffee purchase): +1 Speed, -1 Luck (jitters) for 2 hours
- "Sugar Rush" (cake purchase): +2 Bravado for 30 minutes, then crash
- "Cultured Palate" (order in German correctly): +1 Charm for 1 hour
- "Humiliated by Waiter" (failed order): -1 Charm for 30 minutes

**Recovery Available**:
- Tired: Any coffee removes
- Hungry: Any food removes
- Thirsty: Any beverage removes (even expensive ones)

### Time-of-Day Variations

| Time | Atmosphere | Special Features |
|------|------------|------------------|
| 08:00-11:00 | Morning service, quieter | Breakfast menu available |
| 11:00-14:00 | Lunch crowd, busier | Longer wait times |
| 14:00-17:00 | Kaffeeklatsch peak | Most pretentious, best gossip |
| 17:00-20:00 | Pre-opera crowd | Formal dress observed |
| 20:00-22:00 | Post-opera elegance | Classical music louder |

### Weather Impact
- **Rain**: Indoor seating only, more crowded
- **Sunshine**: Patio opens, premium tables outside
- **Cold**: Warm drinks sell faster, comfort atmosphere
