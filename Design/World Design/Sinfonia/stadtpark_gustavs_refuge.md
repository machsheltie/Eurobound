## Location Overview

The Stadtpark is Sinfonia's grand public park—a sprawling green oasis amid baroque architecture. By day, it's filled with joggers, dog walkers, and tourists photographing the famous Johann Strauss statue. But tonight, it's the final battlefield in the Fiaker Fiasco, where Gustav the runaway fiaker horse has made his last stand.

### Fiaker Fiasco Context
- **Phase 4 (Final)**: The resolution of the chase
- **Running Damage Total**: €2,340 (or €1,940 via Papageno path)
- **Stakes**: Hans-Jürgen's livelihood, Gustav's freedom, bros' conscience (if they have one)
- **Resolution Options**: Multiple endings based on player choices

### Why Gustav Chose the Park
Gustav isn't just running—he's *escaping*. After years of tourist service, he's finally snapped. The Stadtpark represents freedom: open grass, no cobblestones, no drunk tourists asking for selfies. Here, Gustav makes his stand.

## The Skittles Negotiation

### Setup
Gustav won't let anyone approach. He's done with humans. But the Pigeon Lady mentioned something about "rainbow candies." And coincidentally, Chadwick has been carrying a bag of American Skittles since Publandia.

### The Skittles
**Item**: Bag of Skittles (American Import)
- Obtained: Chadwick's inventory (always there, unexplained)
- Purpose: Gustav's favorite treat from past American tourists
- Effect: Grants ability to approach Gustav peacefully

### The Negotiation Mechanic

**Phase 1: Initial Approach**
- Gustav bolts if Bros approach directly
- Hans-Jürgen's approach causes Gustav to back away
- Only Skittles or Papageno enable close approach

**Phase 2: Earning Trust**
- Player must use Skittles (hold out, wait)
- Gustav approaches cautiously
- Single Skittle: Gets attention
- Handful: Gustav approaches fully
- Full bag: Gustav eats from hand

**Phase 3: Resolution Dialogue**
Once Gustav is eating Skittles, player enters dialogue tree with Hans-Jürgen about Gustav's future.

## Resolution Paths

### Path A: Gustav Returns to Hans-Jürgen (Best Ending)

**Requirements**:
- Earn Gustav's trust via Skittles
- Choose dialogue options that address Gustav's needs
- Hans-Jürgen agrees to reforms

**Outcome**:
- Gustav returns to fiaker service
- Hans-Jürgen promises: No more bachelor parties, shorter hours, more Skittles
- Bros' damage debt is reduced by €500 (gratitude discount)
- Unlock: "Horse Whisperer" achievement
- Unlock: Fiaker fast-travel in Sinfonia

**Key Dialogue**:
- Lord Pilsner: "Maybe Gustav doesn't want to quit. He just wants... respect."
- Hans-Jürgen: "You are right. Gustav is not just horse. Gustav is... partner."
- Gustav: *Nuzzles Hans-Jürgen*

---

### Path B: Gustav Retires to Stadtpark

**Requirements**:
- Earn Gustav's trust via Skittles
- Choose dialogue options supporting Gustav's freedom
- Hans-Jürgen accepts loss

**Outcome**:
- Gustav becomes "park horse," lives in Stadtpark
- Hans-Jürgen accepts, gets insurance payout
- Bros' damage debt remains full (no discount)
- Unlock: "Liberation" achievement
- Gustav appears in park as permanent NPC (gives buffs when visited)

**Key Dialogue**:
- Bradley: "Look at him. He's happy here. You can't cage freedom, dude."
- Hans-Jürgen: "Sixteen years... perhaps he has earned this."
- Gustav: *Trots into moonlight, majestically*

---

### Path C: Gustav Escapes Entirely

**Requirements**:
- Fail to use Skittles
- Or aggressive approach
- Gustav bolts

**Outcome**:
- Gustav runs off into the night, never seen again
- Hans-Jürgen devastated, quits fiaker business
- Bros' damage debt increases by €1,000 (horse value added)
- No achievement
- Permanent guilt marker in Bros' journal

**Key Dialogue**:
- Hans-Jürgen: "He is gone. Gustav is gone forever."
- Chadwick: "Dude... we lost a horse."
- Lord Pilsner: "We lost more than that, Chad."

---

### Path D: Bros Bail (Coward's Exit)

**Requirements**:
- Leave park without resolving
- Avoid Hans-Jürgen

**Outcome**:
- Quest "fails," but technically completes
- Damage debt remains plus €500 abandonment fee
- Hans-Jürgen becomes hostile NPC
- Gustav fate unknown
- "Coward" status effect (temporary)
