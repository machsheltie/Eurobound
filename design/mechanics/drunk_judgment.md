5. Embarrassment events

Author, f3tq-q13: "One per night at Shitfaced, one more at Catastrophe".

* A bro who reaches Shitfaced gets his embarrassment that night. It is guaranteed (f3tq-q1).
* At Catastrophe there is one more.
* The author named two events in the f3tq direction: the cab moon (section 6) and the Blarney Trap wet-T-shirt contest (Eurobound-n7w5).
* If an event leaves a status, it is the existing Embarrassed. It is never redefined here. Its definitions agree since Desk Eurobound-jsd0-q1 (ledger l.961).

Who starts the event, where it may fire, how long it runs and how soon it may repeat are the proposal's sections 2.1 to 2.3. **PENDING: Desk Eurobound-f3tq-q15** for their numbers.

The rest of the event pool. **PENDING: Eurobound-3iwg** (Writers' Room with the author).
