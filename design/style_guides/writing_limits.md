# Writing Limits

Two hard limits for NPC rosters and dialogue. Both are revived from the retired World Naming Consistency Guide (`design/legacy/retired_templates/style_guides__Eurobound World Naming Consistency Guide.md`). Nothing else from that guide is revived.

## 1. No theme over 30% of a city's NPCs

(author, 2026-11-11; revived from the retired World Naming Consistency Guide)

No single theme (pub culture, crypto, coffeehouse and so on) may cover more than 30% of a city's NPCs. Count the city's NPCs by theme before adding a new one:

| Band | Theme share of the city's NPCs | Action |
|------|-------------------------------|--------|
| 🟢 Green | Under 25% | Proceed |
| 🟡 Review | 25–35% | Review before adding; consider a different theme |
| 🔴 Stop | Over 35% | Stop creating NPCs of this theme in this city |

## 2. Dialogue lines are 280 characters max

(author, 2026-11-11; revived from the retired World Naming Consistency Guide)

No single dialogue line may exceed 280 characters.
