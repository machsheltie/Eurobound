## 0. How This File Is Used

The **Master Pool** is 28 shitcoins. At any moment the back-alley kiosks (**Broke-ATM**, **QuickRug24**)
render an **active roster of 7–8** drawn from this pool. The roster reshuffles on major zone
transition, hostel/hotel rest, and major story/side-quest beat completion. Which coin pays and which
coin rugs is decided by the per-save seed plus the 35/65 roll at the moment of payment — **nothing in
this file assigns a fixed outcome to a coin.** A coin that made a player rich on one save is a
tombstone on the next.

Every coin entry carries four elements:

1. **Ticker / name**
2. **Pitch** — one line, scam-marketing voice, played straight. This is copy the coin's own people
   wrote. It appears on the kiosk detail pane and on the certificate.
3. **Vault Certificate** — the high-gloss laminated cardstock artifact the kiosk prints on a rug
   pull, per the Certificate Physical Standard below, plus that coin's specific art, seal, serial
   format, and lamination defect.
4. **QR page** — the real-world joke page a player's actual phone opens when it scans the dead QR
   printed on the item asset. Concept plus the load-bearing on-page copy.

**Scope note (ledgered):** this round *designs* the QR pages. Building and hosting the actual web
pages is production work and is explicitly out of scope (spec §5).

**Ban-all note:** QR pages are framed as the real internet and are exempt from the world-name rule
(spec §5). In-game text — kiosk copy, certificates, ticker rows, pitch lines — uses world names only
(Debaucheryville, Sinfonia, Publandia, Shamsterdam). Real brands are exempt and closed (§7a.1).

**Terminal font note (in-world):** kiosk hardware renders a 7-bit bitmap face. No emoji glyphs
resolve. Everything the coins want to scream, they scream in capitals, asterisks, and exclamation
points, because that is all the machine can print.

## 1. Certificate Physical Standard

Every rug pull ejects one certificate, still warm, from a slot that was designed for banknotes.

- **Stock:** 350gsm cardstock, A6 (105 × 148 mm), 125-micron gloss lamination both faces. It has the
  hand-feel of a chain-restaurant dessert menu.
- **Print:** kiosk-internal thermal transfer, four-colour, always a half-millimetre out of
  registration. Metallic and foil elements are cold-stamped by a unit that has never once been
  serviced.
- **Mandatory front furniture, every coin:**
  - Coin wordmark and ticker, oversized.
  - **PROVENANCE VERIFIED** foil seal — the identical gold seal used on Legacy Vault cards.
  - `BEARER OF RECORD: [PLAYER NAME]`
  - `TOTAL HOLDINGS: [enormous number] [TICKER]` — quantity only. No price appears anywhere on a
    certificate. The valuation line always reads `VALUATION PENDING NETWORK RESYNC.`
  - A dead QR, 30 mm, printed in metallic ink over gloss so it throws a highlight at most angles.
  - Serial number in the coin's own format.
- **Mandatory back copy, every coin (identical, microprinted, 4pt):**
  > HOW TO CLAIM: 1. SCAN. 2. CONNECT WALLET. 3. WELCOME TO THE FUTURE.
  > This certificate confers ownership of the tokens described and of no other thing, right, remedy,
  > expectation, or afternoon. Not a security. Not an offer. Not returnable. Not laminated by a
  > professional. The network is the network.
- **Per-coin variation:** art, seal treatment, serial format, and one signature **lamination
  defect**. The defects are the running physical gag of the whole collection and are never remarked
  on by any character.

## 4. Kiosk Terminals — Broke-ATM & QuickRug24

The kiosks are the only way to acquire back-alley purchasing power. They stand adjacent to the
vendors who refuse SOVS, usually within sight of them, usually bolted to something structural.

Both terminals render the same **7–8 coin** board from the same feed; only the chrome differs.

 GIRTH    E 19.66   +64.0%    HIGH     __/‾‾\__/‾‾‾

--------------------------------------------------------

Presentation rules:
- **$SPANK is always on the board and always flat at E 0.00.** It is never removed. It does not count
  toward the 7–8 rotating slots and is not purchasable — the row is inert. It is there because it has
  always been there.
- Sparklines are decorative eight-character ASCII strips and are **not** generated from the
  underlying roll. They are re-randomised on every roster reshuffle.
- 24H figures are rendered green above zero and green below zero. The terminal has one colour.
- `VOL` cycles through `LOW / MED / HIGH / EXTREME / ???` and has no mechanical meaning.
- Roster reshuffles on major zone transition, hostel/hotel rest, and major beat completion, per
  `economy_system.md`. A coin leaving the board leaves without comment; a coin arriving arrives with
  a `NEW` tag that persists for exactly one reshuffle.
- The board is drawn from the per-save seed. Two saves that see the same eight tickers will not see
  the same eight prices.

### 4.4 Machine Voice Rules (for writers)

- The kiosks **never** acknowledge an outcome as bad. There is no loss state in the UI; there is a
  printing state.
- The kiosks are the only entities in the economy that are permitted to sound competent. Sellers know
  exactly what they have.
- No terminal string ever explains a mechanic, a probability, or a joke. If a line could be read as
  the game explaining itself, cut the line.
- Machines may be rude. Machines may not be knowing.

## 7. Engine Requirements (coin-pool scope)

- **Pool data:** 28 coin records — `id`, `ticker`, `display_name`, `pitch`, `cert_art_id`,
  `qr_page_id`, `city_affinity[]` (flavour tag; must not gate availability).
- **`$SPANK` is a special row:** always rendered, never purchasable, excluded from the rotating 7–8.
- **Roster selection** is seeded per save and reshuffled on the three triggers in
  `economy_system.md`. Selection draws from the 27 rotating coins; affinity tags may weight but must
  never exclude.
- **Certificate item generation:** one item instance per rug pull, stamped with `coin_id`,
  `serial` (generated in that coin's format), `quantity`, and `bearer_name`. Serial formats are
  per-coin strings and belong in the coin record.
- **QR assets:** each certificate item art carries a real, scannable code resolving to that coin's
  page. **Building and hosting the 28 pages is production work and is out of scope this round
  (ledgered).** Item art must reserve a 30 mm-equivalent quiet zone at the certificate's specified
  position so codes can be dropped in later without re-authoring the art.
- Certificates land in **Bagholder Assets** (hidden tab). The first `Inspect` on a certificate sets
  `has_inspected_first_crypto_trash = true` and reveals the tab — see `design/economy/wallet_ui.md`.
