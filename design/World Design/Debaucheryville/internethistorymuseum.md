## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Nostalgia weaponized and left to rot. Less an archive, more a hallucination of what Gen X and elder millennials think the internet used to be. Equal parts fever dream and malware cache, humming with ghost pings and unresolved trauma from LimeWire downloads gone wrong. Visitors feel like they've stepped into a cursed yearbook for every regrettable digital moment.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Weaponized nostalgia culture, early internet fetishization, Gen X/millennial digital trauma
- **Secondary Targets**: Museum culture pretension, preservation of worthless ephemera, LimeWire piracy guilt
- **Cultural Specificity**: Debaucheryville's tech culture meets forgotten internet history worship
- **Humor Approach**: Deadpan museum curation of cursed content, treating malware as artifacts
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets nostalgia exploitation and digital hoarding, not legitimate internet history preservation

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling psychological damage disguised as nostalgic education
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Fake museum legitimizing digital trauma as cultural heritage
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Visitors paying emotional toll to revisit cringe-worthy internet history
- **Comedy of Errors**: Museum exhibits actively harm viewers (status effects from interaction)
- **Consequence System**: Random status effects from exhibits create gameplay risk/reward

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Visiting legitimate cultural institution preserving important internet history

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Walking through psychological damage simulator disguised as museum with actively harmful exhibits

**How impaired are they**: Nostalgic enough to think early internet deserves museum preservation, drunk enough to interact with everything

**Obvious signs they ignore**: Chuck E. Cheese animatronic in Furbies exhibit, crime scene tape around smiley face, incense burning at iMac altar, exhibits causing visible status effects

**How locals play along**: Museum staff treat obvious garbage with academic reverence, provide plaques with serious historical context for worthless digital ephemera
