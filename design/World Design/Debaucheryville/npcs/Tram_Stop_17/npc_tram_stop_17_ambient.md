# Shrine Tender

*"He has waited longer than my mother has been alive. The least we can do is keep the candles lit."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_shrine_tender_01`
- **Display Name**: Shrine Tender
- **Internal Name**: Božena Králová
- **Role**: Ambient NPC / World-Building / Shrine Maintainer
- **Primary Location**: Tram Stop #17, Debaucheryville
- **Spawn Condition**: Visit count ≥ 5

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Doing
Quietly maintaining the growing shrine around Jiří's bench. She arranges fresh flowers, lights new candles, and occasionally crosses herself. She treats Jiří with the reverence typically reserved for saints or the dead.

### Function in Scene
Shows how the community has responded to Jiří over the years—not with intervention, but with veneration. The city has absorbed his madness as something sacred.

---

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 60-70 |
| Build | Small, hunched slightly |
| Clothing | Black dress, headscarf (traditional) |
| Accessories | Basket with candles and flowers |

### Distinguishing Features
- Moves slowly, deliberately
- Treats the bench area like holy ground
- Never directly addresses Jiří (respects his focus)
- Crosses herself when leaving

---

## 💬 Dialogue

### Overheard (Not Direct Interaction)
> *quietly arranges fresh flowers*

> *lights a new candle, crosses herself*

> "He has waited longer than my mother has been alive."

> *whispers prayer in Czech*

> "The 17 will come. Or it won't. But he will be ready."

### If Player Approaches
> "Shh. Do not disturb him. He is... concentrating."

> "You want to help? Here. Light this candle. For his journey."

> "My grandmother started this shrine. I continue her work."

- **Role**: Ambient NPC / Community Representation

# Metro Worker (Callback NPC)

*"The 17 hasn't run since 1999. Don't tell Jiří. It would kill him."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_metro_worker_01`
- **Display Name**: Metro Worker
- **Internal Name**: Tomáš Procházka
- **Role**: Callback NPC / Dark Secret Holder
- **Primary Location**: Metro Station (2 blocks from Stop #17)
- **Spawn Condition**: Visit metro station after meeting Jiří

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He Knows
The truth. Tram #17 was discontinued in 1999—four years before Jiří started waiting. The entire transit authority knows. No one has told him.

### Function in Scene
Provides the devastating reveal for players who seek it out. Confirms the tragedy is even worse than it appears.

---

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 50s |
| Build | Working class sturdy |
| Clothing | Metro authority uniform |
| Expression | Uncomfortable when discussing Jiří |

---

## 💬 Dialogue

### If Player Mentions Jiří
> "You've seen Jiří? At Stop 17?"
> *long pause*
> "The 17 hasn't run since 1999. Don't tell Jiří. It would kill him. Maybe literally."

### Follow-up Questions

**"Why doesn't anyone tell him?"**
> "We tried. Once. In 2005. He didn't believe us. Called us liars. Said he'd seen the 17 in 1997. We... stopped trying."

**"Can't you help him?"**
> "Help him how? Take him away? He'd come back. We've accepted it. The city has accepted it. He's... part of us now."

**"Does his family know?"**
> "His wife stopped coming by in 2008. His son... I don't know. No one talks about it."

---

## 📝 Development Notes
- NOT at Tram Stop #17 (at metro station)
- Optional discovery for players who explore
- Delivers the darkest piece of information
- Should feel like learning a terrible secret
- Player cannot tell Jiří (no dialogue option exists)
