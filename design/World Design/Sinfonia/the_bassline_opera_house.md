*Where Mozart meets Molly — the city's cultural crown jewel by day, bass-dropping chaos by night, and cultural gatekeeping around the clock*

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Bassline Opera House satirizes the collision between high culture and party culture — the same venue that hosts refined opera by day becomes a temple of EDM excess by night. It mocks both the pretension of classical music gatekeeping AND the absurdity of club culture appropriating "sophisticated" spaces.

The real joke is that both crowds think they're the "real" audience and the other group is ruining the space. Day visitors complain about bass damage to the ceiling; night visitors complain about stuffy opera types who "don't know how to have fun."

### The Dual Identity
**Day Mode**: "Cultural institution" that weaponizes sophistication. Staff correct your pronunciation, judge your outfit, and quiz you on opera trivia. Every interaction is a test of whether you "belong." The opera itself is intentionally incomprehensible — sung in German with no subtitles, lasting 4+ hours.

**Night Mode**: The same pretension, different flavor. Now it's about knowing the right DJ, having the right connections, and demonstrating you're not a "tourist." The bass drops are timed to classical motifs. The dress code flips from "too casual" to "too formal." Different gatekeepers, same energy.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Cultural gatekeeping, high/low culture divide
- **Secondary Targets**: Opera tourism, EDM club pretension, dress code enforcement
- **Cultural Specificity**: Viennese opera house culture, classical music snobbery
- **Humor Approach**: Both modes equally absurd, both audiences equally mockable
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes gatekeeping behavior, not classical music or EDM itself

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Cultural elitism masking actual vice (The Black-Tie Backroom gambling)
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: "Suggested donations" that become required, hidden fees, overpriced refreshments
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think they can fake either culture (spoiler: they can't)
- **Comedy of Errors**: Rhythm failures during performances, etiquette disasters, wardrobe malfunctions
- **Consequence System**: Embarrassed status from social failures, ejection for major violations

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios

**Day Mode Delusions**:
- **What bros THINK is happening**: "We're experiencing CULTURE. This is sophisticated."
- **What is ACTUALLY happening**: They're falling asleep, mispronouncing everything, and being quietly mocked
- **Lord Pilsner**: Falls asleep during Act 1, snores audibly, claims he "felt the emotion"
- **Chadwick**: Tries to livestream the opera, gets phone confiscated, causes scene
- **Bradley**: "Gregory has researched the libretto extensively." *gets composer wrong*

**Night Mode Delusions**:
- **What bros THINK is happening**: "We're VIPs at the hottest spot in Sinfonia."
- **What is ACTUALLY happening**: They barely got in, they're near the speakers, their ears are bleeding
- **Lord Pilsner**: "This DJ is playing MY request!" *DJ is playing pre-programmed set*
- **Chadwick**: "I know the promoter!" *promoter doesn't know him*
- **Bradley**: "Gregory does not understand why classical music requires such volume."

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Day Mode — Cultural Challenge System

**Opera Attendance**:
- Survive 4 "acts" (mini-challenges testing patience/attention)
- Random quiz from snooty patrons ("What's your favorite Strauss piece?")
- Applause timing mini-game (clap at wrong time = Embarrassed)
- Intermission social navigation (find buffet without looking lost)

**Rehearsal Eavesdropping**:
- Sneak into rehearsal studios
- Overhear gossip that becomes quest clues
- Don't get caught by Maestro's Assistant
- Rewards: Intel on boss weaknesses, Black-Tie Backroom access hints

**Prop Room Puzzles**:
- Navigate backstage labyrinth using theatrical logic
- Use props to solve environmental puzzles (fake sword as lever, costume as disguise)
- Find hidden passages to The Black-Tie Backroom

### Night Mode — Rhythm Combat System

**Dance Floor Survival**:
- Rhythm mini-game with increasing difficulty
- Match button presses to bass drops
- Miss too many = "Embarrassed" status from crowd
- Perfect runs = "Groove Master" buff

**Rival Trio Showdown — "Waltz of the Bros"**:
- Trio crashes the dance floor during transition
- Forced dance-off that becomes rhythmic battle
- Combat synced to music (attacks on beat deal bonus damage)
- Reward: NFT #3 "Mask Selfie GIF"

**Boss Arena Transformation**:
- Stage becomes Maestro Vice von Strauss's domain
- Musical tempo shifts affect combat speed
- Crowd meter determines boss buffs

### City Boss: Maestro Vice von Strauss

**Introduction**: "Welcome to MY house. You've heard the overture. Now comes the FINALE."

**Boss Profile**:
- **Title**: Cultural Gatekeeper of Sinfonia
- **Appearance**: Conductor's tuxedo with tails, wild silver hair, baton crackling with musical energy
- **Personality**: Theatrical, pompous, genuinely believes he's protecting culture
- **Weakness**: His ego — applause mechanics can disrupt attacks

**Boss Phases**:

*Phase 1 — The Overture (100-66% HP)*
- **Tempo**: Moderate, manageable
- **Attacks**:
  - "Crescendo Crush": Ramping damage, hits harder each beat
  - "Downbeat Strike": Heavy single hit on the 1
- **Mechanic**: Learn his tempo patterns
- **Dialogue**: "You lack RHYTHM! You lack CULTURE! You lack EVERYTHING!"

*Phase 2 — The Development (66-33% HP)*
- **Tempo**: Shifts between fast and slow unpredictably
- **Attacks**:
  - "Waltz of Weakness": Party-wide debuff (reduced accuracy)
  - "Staccato Strikes": Rapid multi-hit combo
  - "Encore of Excess": Summons Harried Assistants
- **Mechanic**: Adapt to tempo changes, crowd meter starts filling
- **Dialogue**: "The music CHANGES! Can you KEEP UP?!"

*Phase 3 — The Finale (33-0% HP)*
- **Tempo**: Frantic, bass-drop heavy
- **Attacks**:
  - "Standing Ovation": Self-buff when crowd meter is full
  - "Grand Finale": Room-wide attack requiring perfect dodge timing
  - "Fortissimo Fury": Enraged state if crowd boos
- **Mechanic**: Manipulate crowd (cheer/boo) to control his buffs
- **Dialogue**: "THIS! IS! CULTURE! *bass drop* AND YOU! WILL! RESPECT IT!"

**Victory**:
- Maestro collapses dramatically, muttering about "philistines"
- Crowd cheers regardless of who they were supporting
- **Rewards**:
  - **Conductor's Baton** (Weapon: +5 Charm, chance to Embarrass on hit)
  - **Baroness' Vault Keys** (unlocks late-game gear)
  - **Backstage All-Access Pass** (permanent)
