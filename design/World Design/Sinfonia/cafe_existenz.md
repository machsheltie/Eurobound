## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Café Existenz satirizes coffeehouse intellectual culture — the performative depth, the competitive obscurity, and the way "philosophical discussion" often masks social gatekeeping. Everyone here is performing intelligence for an audience that's too busy performing to watch.

The deeper joke: The actual philosophical content is shallow. The debates are about proving you've READ Nietzsche, not about understanding him. The chess games never end because finishing would mean someone lost.

### The Coffeehouse Hierarchy
**The Pecking Order** (visible through seating):
1. **The Philosopher Kings**: Back room, decades of patronage, speak in aphorisms
2. **The Academics**: Good tables, quote sources, correct others' pronunciation
3. **The Artists**: Window-adjacent, make everything about their work
4. **The Students**: Standing room, desperate to be noticed, over-caffeinated
5. **The Tourists**: Window seats, confused, wondering why no one takes their order

Everyone judges everyone below them. Everyone fears judgment from above.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Intellectual pretension, coffeehouse gatekeeping
- **Secondary Targets**: Philosophy tourism, performative depth, competitive obscurity
- **Cultural Specificity**: Viennese coffeehouse tradition, Central European intellectual culture
- **Humor Approach**: The pretension is the point; sincerity is suspicious
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes pretension, not actual philosophy or learning

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Intellectual elitism masking insecurity and loneliness
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Overcharging tourists, "forgetting" orders, judgment as service
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think ordering confidently makes up for not understanding the menu
- **Comedy of Errors**: Mispronunciation disasters, accidentally agreeing with the wrong philosopher
- **Consequence System**: Embarrassed status from debate losses, Bravado drain from extended conversations

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Dialogue Combat System — "The Debate"

**Core Mechanic**:
Café Existenz features "Dialogue Combat" — conversations that function like turn-based battles but use INT instead of STR and words instead of weapons.

**Debate Structure**:
1. **Opening Statement**: NPC poses philosophical question
2. **Response Phase**: Choose from dialogue options (some require INT checks)
3. **Counter-Argument**: NPC responds based on your choice
4. **Rebuttal**: Your chance to recover or press advantage
5. **Resolution**: Win, lose, or draw (stalemate)

**Dialogue Options Tiers**:
- **Confident Bluff**: High risk, doesn't require knowledge, easily countered
- **Surface Response**: Safe, shows basic familiarity, won't impress
- **Informed Position**: Requires INT check, gains respect
- **Devastating Counter**: Rare, requires previous knowledge items, ends debate in victory

**Win/Lose Consequences**:
- **Win Debate**: +15 Bravado, NPC respect, potential item/info reward
- **Lose Debate**: -10 Bravado, Embarrassed status (30 min)
- **Stalemate**: No Bravado change, NPC slightly annoyed, can retry later

### Philosophical Debate Topics (Random Selection)

| Topic | Easy Response | Hard Counter | Trap Option |
|-------|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| "What is the meaning of life?" | "To find happiness" | "Meaning is constructed, not found" | "42" (nerd reference = judgment) |
| "Is free will an illusion?" | "We feel free, so we are" | "Compatibilism resolves the paradox" | "I choose to believe" (circular) |
| "Can art be objectively good?" | "Beauty is subjective" | "Technical mastery transcends taste" | "I know it when I see it" (dismissed) |
| "Does God exist?" | "It's a personal question" | "The question itself is malformed" | Picking a side (creates enemies) |

### The Bravado Drain System

Extended time in Café Existenz costs Bravado:
- **Every 10 minutes**: -1 Bravado (existential weight)
- **Every debate loss**: -10 Bravado
- **Every mispronunciation**: -5 Bravado
- **Being corrected publicly**: -8 Bravado
- **Server ignoring you**: -3 Bravado

**Bravado Recovery**:
- **Winning debate**: +15 Bravado
- **Finding equally confused tourist**: +5 Bravado
- **Ordering successfully in German**: +10 Bravado
- **Leaving**: Immediate relief (+5 Bravado)

### NPC Philosopher Encounters

**The Nihilist** (Easy)
- **Opening**: "Nothing matters. Why are you here? Why am I here? Why is anything?"
- **Weakness**: Point out he's still drinking coffee (implies caring about something)
- **Victory Reward**: "Void Sugar" (consumable, removes debuffs by removing the concept of debuffs)

**The Kantian** (Medium)
- **Opening**: "What is your duty? Not your desire — your DUTY."
- **Weakness**: His categorical imperative conflicts with his treatment of waitstaff
- **Victory Reward**: "Categorical Cappuccino" recipe (powerful but must be shared)

**The Postmodernist** (Hard)
- **Opening**: "Your question assumes a framework I reject. Explain your assumptions."
- **Weakness**: Gets frustrated if you agree with everything (no conflict to deconstruct)
- **Victory Reward**: "Deconstructed Pastry" (confusing but satisfying)

**The Retired Professor** (Boss-Level)
- **Name**: Herr Doktor Professor Maximilian von Grübelstein
- **Opening**: "I have taught philosophy for 47 years. What could you POSSIBLY teach me?"
- **Weakness**: Genuinely lonely; responding with kindness breaks his defense
- **Victory Reward**: "Honorary Doctorate" (fake, but +5 INT for showing it)
- **Alternative**: Befriend him for coffee mentor relationship (quest unlock)
