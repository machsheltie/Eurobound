## Satirical Design

### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: A darkly absurd meditation on futile persistence, sunk cost fallacy, and the strange dignity of commitment to a lost cause. Jiří has been waiting so long he's become a local landmark, yet the bros only see him as a convenient information kiosk.

**The Irony**: The bros repeatedly ask Jiří for directions, completely missing that he's been trapped in place by his own stubbornness for over two decades. They think he's a "local transit expert" because of his 47,000-entry documentation. They never realize the documentation is evidence of obsession, not expertise.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Absurdist Eastern European humor, bureaucratic futility
- **Secondary Targets**: 
  - Tourists treating locals as information dispensers
  - Blind optimism in the face of obvious failure
  - Sunk cost fallacy taken to absurd extremes
  - The way cities absorb eccentric fixtures into their fabric
- **Cultural Specificity**: Czech public transit culture, the particular melancholy of waiting for something that won't come
- **Humor Approach**: Darkly comedic but ultimately poignant; Jiří is pitiable AND admirable
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Mental health portrayed with dignity; Jiří's condition is absurd but treated respectfully

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Obsession, the inability to accept defeat
- **Hidden Tragedy**: Jiří was going to propose; never made it home; wife waited, then didn't
- **City's Response**: Rather than help him, the city has absorbed him as a tourist attraction
- **Metro Worker's Secret**: "The 17 hasn't run since 1999. Don't tell Jiří. It would kill him."

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: 
> A friendly local with extensive transit knowledge who can give them directions. He clearly knows the system inside and out.

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: 
> A man frozen in time by grief and stubbornness, who has documented 47,000 passing trams while waiting for one that was discontinued in 1999.

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- His beard grows visibly between visits
- Pigeons have nested ON him
- Locals have built a shrine around his bench
- The schedule he references is from 1994
- He hasn't moved from this spot in over twenty years

**How the bros interpret this**:
> "That guy REALLY knows the transit system."
> "He's clearly a transportation expert. Did you see his documentation?"
> "I respect his commitment to local transit. That's REAL European culture."

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Primary Functions
- **Core Purpose**: Information source, recurring NPC encounter, optional emotional questline
- **Player Actions**: 
  - Ask for directions (always helpful, always references the 17)
  - Suggest alternatives (always refused)
  - Offer company (sit with Jiří briefly)
  - Night Vigil (extended emotional sequence)
- **Rewards Available**: 
  - Accurate directions to anywhere in city
  - "Jiří's Blessing" buff (+1 Charm with all locals)
  - Post-credits scene unlock
- **Risk/Reward Balance**: Low risk, high emotional reward

### Interaction Options

**[ASK FOR DIRECTIONS]**
- Jiří knows EVERYTHING about the city layout
- Gives directions in terms of tram stops even for walking
- Always mentions that "the 17 is an option, when it arrives"

**[SUGGEST ALTERNATIVES]**
- Taxi: "A TAXI? Jiří does not SURRENDER."
- Walking: "Walk? And miss the 17? After all this time?"
- Math/Logic: "Your math means nothing here. The 17 operates outside TIME."

**[OFFER COMPANY]**
- Brief sitting sequence
- Jiří warns about the pigeon on the adjacent seat
- Small Charm boost with Jiří for future interactions

**[NIGHT VIGIL]** (After 10+ visits)
- Extended sequence from evening to dawn
- Jiří's full backstory revealed
- Emotional climax with false alarm (the #22)
- "Jiří's Blessing" reward

### Economic Integration
- **No Shop Functionality**: This is a free location
- **Indirect Value**: Directions save time/money by avoiding getting lost
- **Blessing Economy**: +1 Charm with locals = better prices elsewhere
