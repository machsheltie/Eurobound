## Intoxication Law Review (design/mechanics/intoxication_law.md §8)
Status: drafted 2026-11-12 (Eurobound-r1c). The points left pending in 3, 6 and 8 were ruled by the author on 2026-11-12 (Eurobound Desk eb8-q1, "Approve as proposed"); values from `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-grist-genever-review-numbers-proposal.md` §3-§4 [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8]. Units: 1 step = 1 metre walked by the party leader; 40 steps = 1 battle turn. Turns tick only in battle, steps only on the overworld; the status ends when either counter reaches 0, or at rest.

1. **Source**: Oude Genever, 35%, served by Kees at Bottoms Down; one item with the menu's Oude Jenever (round v34, "same_menu"). The Shakes is also inflicted by Fossil Jenever (x2), Korenwijn, gauntlet failure and the Couchsurf morning [source: design/worlds/shamsterdam/bottoms_down.md L218-220, L185, L239, L367]; those sources are outside this review. Alcoholic: yes (round v37 drink_tag; engine flag `"alcohol": true`, 10 clock minutes when bought or drunk).
2. **Immediate effect**: +25 Bravado (round v34, menu numbers); The Shakes, -15% Accuracy for 3 turns (author ruling 2026-11-12).
3. **Escalation**: Further jenever servings stack the duration, not the magnitude [source: bottoms_down.md L241]; Fossil Jenever applies The Shakes x2 [L219]; gauntlet failures accumulate Shakes stacks [source: design/worlds/shamsterdam/PNG_Assets/bottoms_down_png_assets.md L482]. Each extra serving adds +3 turns / +120 steps and the magnitude stays -15%; Fossil Jenever adds +6 turns / +240 steps; each failed gauntlet round adds +3 turns / +120 steps; cap 12 turns / 480 steps (4 servings' worth). Each serving counts as 1 drink toward Buzzed → Beer Goggles → Shitfaced → Catastrophe, with no ABV weighting; the tier thresholds stay with Eurobound-p0j [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
4. **Player-facing cues**: Sprite vibration by a 2px offset-frame swap (no screen shake), an unstable combat reticle, a status bar with stack indicator and an explicit "-15% Accuracy" text readout, the trembling-hand icon, and a tremolo audio bed [source: bottoms_down_png_assets.md L272-278, L351-353, L381, L404-405, L424; bottoms_down.md L242, L386]. Bros' line: "I'm fine. Everything's fine. Why is my hand doing that." [bottoms_down.md L244] On the overworld: the same status bar with stack indicator (showing `ceil(turns left / 3)`) and the "-15% Accuracy" readout on the bro's Status card, and the 2px offset-frame tremor on the walk sprite too; in reduced-motion mode the sprite holds still. Existing assets only, no new text [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
5. **Player control**: Attacks miss more often for 3 turns. Oude Genever cannot be drunk in combat; authored, not a bug [source: bottoms_down.md L234; design/worlds/shamsterdam/npcs/bottoms_down/npc_kees.md L466]. No dialogue or movement takeover is specified.
6. **Duration and recovery**: 3 turns; cured by time, food or sleep [bottoms_down.md L240, L243]. Fritomat and the Stroopwafel Cart reduce the duration and The Salty Swallow gives "mild improvement" [source: design/worlds/shamsterdam/frietomat.md L271; stroopwafel_cart.md L254; de_zilte_waarheid.md L298]. Reduced-motion mode swaps the tremor for the bar and the text readout [bottoms_down_png_assets.md L533]. One serving lasts 3 turns / 120 steps. On the overworld The Shakes has no mechanical effect: walking spends the step counter, and if a fight starts first the remaining turns carry into it. The Couchsurf morning applies one serving after the rest. Fritomat cuts -2 turns / -80 steps per meal; the Stroopwafel Cart -2 turns / -80 steps; The Salty Swallow herring -1 turn / -40 steps; sleep ends it [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
7. **Comedic consequence**: The bro thinks he is proving his constitution and earning the bartender's respect; he gets +25 Bravado and hands that will not hold a weapon still. Kees: "Your hands will shake. This is the drink. Not you." [npc_kees.md L191]
8. **Status interactions**: Stacks with Listing (The Hoisted Pint) [source: design/worlds/shamsterdam/the_hoisted_pint.md L246]; Postponed Hangover does not block it [the_mad_monarchs_wit_tea_party.md L239]; Canal Courage cuts Bravado loss while intoxicated [npc_kees.md L356]. In the hit roll The Shakes is -15 percentage points (95% → 80%), not rounded to the 9-point accuracy steps. It adds with Beer Goggles' −Accuracy (design/characters/LordPilsner/lordpilsner.md L33; Tier 2 keeps it, intoxication_law.md Appendix A) the way Sloppy and Grease Fingers already add; Beer Goggles' own size stays unruled (Eurobound-p0j). No hit floor is added [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
9. **Technical dependencies**: Proposed engineering work: the engine's status list is empty (`game/assets/data/skills/status_effects.json`), so it needs a status with duration stacking, an accuracy modifier in the battle hit roll, a combat-use lock on the item, the offset-frame sprite swap and reticle wobble, and the reduced-motion presentation. The consumable entry needs `"alcohol": true`. From the eb8 ruling: the steps unit and the turn carry (Eurobound-d6yl), `extend` with a cap, and a flat-point accuracy modifier in `BattleCore._hit` [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
10. **Further author approval**: Magnitude ruled (2026-11-12). The points in 3, 6 and 8 ruled and this review signed off [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8]; engine sync may proceed.
