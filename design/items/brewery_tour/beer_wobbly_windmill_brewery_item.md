## Intoxication Law Review (design/mechanics/intoxication_law.md §8)
Status: drafted 2026-11-12 (Eurobound-r1c). The points left pending in 3, 4, 6 and 8 were ruled by the author on 2026-11-12 (Eurobound Desk eb8-q1, "Approve as proposed"); values from `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-grist-genever-review-numbers-proposal.md` §3 and §5 [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8]. Units: 1 step = 1 metre walked by the party leader; 40 steps = 1 battle turn. Turns tick only in battle, steps only on the overworld; a status ends when either counter reaches 0, or at rest. A status with no battle effect (Navigation) has no battle counter.

1. **Source**: Windmill Grist, 11%, the finale of the tasting flight at The Wobbly Windmill Brewery; x3 from the Yeast Golem, +2 from the Couchsurf host [source: design/worlds/shamsterdam/wobbly_windmill_brewery.md L192, L211, L359]. Alcoholic: yes (round v37 drink_tag; engine flag `"alcohol": true`, 10 clock minutes when bought or drunk). Not sold; the opener gates only the stamp (author round v34, Set A; confirmed on bead Eurobound-8gg, 2026-11-12, and the purchase lines swept).
2. **Immediate effect**: +40 HP, +20 Bravado, +15% Speed for 3 turns; -20% Navigation for 5 turns (round v34, Set A).
3. **Escalation**: In the brewery, each flight beer fills the Spin Meter and the visuals ramp to full spin at Windmill Grist; a maxed flight plus Spin Meter can make a bro throw up (Bravado loss) [source: wobbly_windmill_brewery.md L214-217, L257; design/worlds/shamsterdam/PNG_Assets/wobbly_windmill_brewery_png_assets.md L369-376]. A second Grist adds +200 steps to the Navigation penalty, still -20%; the Speed buff refreshes to 3 turns / 120 steps (no extension, no growth); HP and Bravado restore again as normal. Cap 800 steps (4 servings' worth), and rest ends it sooner. Each Grist counts as 1 drink toward Buzzed → Beer Goggles → Shitfaced → Catastrophe, with no ABV weighting; the tier thresholds stay with Eurobound-p0j. Navigation is the party's direction roll: the penalty applies while any bro has it, two bros' Grists don't add (the most is -20%), and each bro keeps his own counter, so it lasts until the last one ends [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
4. **Player-facing cues**: Inside the brewery, the Spin Meter's vignette and rotation (vignette-only on Low LOD, off in reduced-motion mode) [wobbly_windmill_brewery_png_assets.md L372, L376]. "The room keeps spinning even after you leave" [wobbly_windmill_brewery.md L251]. Outside the brewery: (a) a Grist status row on the bro's Status card with an explicit "-20% Navigation" readout, like The Shakes' readout; (b) at each Navigation intersection, the Spin Meter's corner vignette at its first-beer level for that decision only (vignette-only on Low LOD, off in reduced-motion mode, where (a) stands alone); (c) one new small status icon, a spin motif, for the readout row. No new dialogue or narration [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
5. **Player control**: Navigation is Shamsterdam's overworld direction check: the player picks a direction and a probability roll decides where the party actually ends up [source: design/worlds/shamsterdam/voetgangerstunnel.md L120-136]. -20% means more wrong turns: bounded, readable loss of movement control, with the Speed buff getting the party there faster.
6. **Duration and recovery**: Speed buff 3 turns, Navigation penalty 5 turns, so the benefit runs out first. The Navigation penalty lasts 200 steps (5 turns × 40) and is overworld-only: it has no battle counter, so it waits out any fight. The Speed buff runs 3 turns when drunk in battle; drunk on the overworld it lasts 120 steps and carries into a fight that starts first. Its size is 15% of Speed rounded to the nearest point, at least +1, so +1 for all three bros (5/7/8; High Bravado moves Speed +2). On the overworld the Speed buff (120 steps) ends before the penalty (200 steps); drunk in battle, the buff is spent in the fight and the whole penalty is still waiting outside. Cure besides waiting: rest only; food gives no cure [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
7. **Comedic consequence**: The bro thinks he has drunk fifteen years of Hendrik's life and earned the speed; he gets lost faster. Hendrik: "this is 15 years of my LIFE in a glass. I hope you are worthy." [wobbly_windmill_brewery.md L272]; flavor: "You call it 'why is the floor moving.'" [L253]
8. **Status interactions**: Navigation odds already move with Sober (+20%), Contact High (-15%), Space Cake Gamble (-30%), Canal Confusion (-25%) and a map item (+15%) [voetgangerstunnel.md L131-135]; Tunnel Confusion is -15% Navigation across Shamsterdam [L424-426]. Listing (The Hoisted Pint) also drifts overworld walking [the_hoisted_pint.md L242-243]. Canal Courage cuts Bravado loss while intoxicated [npc_brewmaster_hendrik.md L322]. Grist's -20 adds as percentage points with every factor in that table and with Tunnel Confusion (-15). The correct-exit chance is clamped to 10%-90%, so a heavy stack never makes the right exit impossible. Drinking cancels Sober: Sober ends with the party's first `alcohol: true` drink and returns at the next rest, so the first Grist costs 40 points (Sober +20 lost, Grist -20 taken). Listing is independent: it drifts walking, Grist moves the intersection roll, and both apply [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
9. **Technical dependencies**: Proposed engineering work: Navigation exists only in design data (`design/worlds/shamsterdam/items/voetgangerstunnel_items.json`), and the engine's status list is empty (`game/assets/data/skills/status_effects.json`). It needs a Navigation stat feeding the overworld direction roll, a status timer that works outside battle, a turn-limited Speed buff, the Spin Meter shader with Low-LOD and reduced-motion fallbacks, and `"alcohol": true` on the consumable entry. From the eb8 ruling: the steps unit (Eurobound-d6yl), the "no battle counter" case for overworld-only statuses, `extend` with a cap, the Navigation roll with the 10%-90% clamp (base odds unruled, Eurobound-76gz), Sober as a flag cleared by `alcohol: true` and restored at rest, and one Grist status icon [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8].
10. **Further author approval**: Numbers ruled (round v34, Set A). The points in 3, 4, 6 and 8 ruled and this review signed off [approved 2026-11-12, Eurobound-eb8]; engine sync may proceed.
