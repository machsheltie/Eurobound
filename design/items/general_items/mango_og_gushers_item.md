## 🎭 The Deception

### What It Actually Is
Author concept (canon): "A suspiciously believable darkweb vape from Tony420's expanding catalog of childhood nostalgia that has gone catastrophically wrong." It mashes the 90s/early-2000s lunchbox association of Fruit Gushers into cannabis branding so naturally that, unlike Speedcakes, it almost sounds like a legitimate dispensary product. That is part of the joke.

What it does to him (APPROVED, author 2026-11-12): "Gush-Locked", a heavy, paralyzing couch-lock stone where his brain floats away, his mouth turns into bone-dry sand, and he fixates on trivial nonsense while completely unable to execute basic physical momentum.

### What The Bros Believe It Is
In their own words, from the Tropical Drop exchange (canon, verbatim):
> **Lord Pilsner:** "Bro. Gushers make vapes now?"
> **Chadwick:** "Brand diversification. Smart."

They think the lunchbox people put out a vape. What they think it does (APPROVED, author 2026-11-12): a "calibrating, terpene-enhanced creative flow-state" that unlocks effortless European chill, deep philosophical gravitas, and smooth social swagger without any calories.

### The Comedy Gap
APPROVED (author, 2026-11-12): instead of sophisticated European cool, the bro gets profound inertia. In the overworld he is glued to arbitrary scenery pondering deep absurdities. In combat his attacks become toothless, enormous, unfocused swings that pacify enemies instead of damaging them, and he wakes up ravenous and broke. The player reads "Questionably Licensed." on the box; the bros read "Brand diversification. Smart."

### Battle Pacification (APPROVED, author 2026-11-12)
- While Gush-Locked, his regular physical attacks turn into sluggish, uncoordinated attempts to vibe with, or deeply appreciate, the target.
- Each one deals drastically reduced physical damage. **Reduction: TBD (balancing pass).**
- Each one has a chance to inflict **"Pacified" / "Mellowed"** on the target: the enemy skips its next turn or deals reduced damage, because the bro's overwhelming lethargy and unblinking stare are socially disarming. **Chance, which outcome applies, and the final name of the enemy status: TBD.** Neither name exists in `design/mechanics/Battle/status_effects.md`; the draft offered both.
- **No free Bravado:** unlike Speedcakes, he takes nothing from the enemy. He bleeds his own.
- Applies to physical attacks only; skills and items are unchanged unless the balancing pass says otherwise.

**Attack animation (author addition, 2026-11-12), verbatim:** "when he attempts to hit an enemy it should be some overly big movement that looks ridiculous because he's not focused at all."

- Every physical attack while Gush-Locked plays as a huge, exaggerated, completely unfocused wind-up and swing: the arm goes back far too far for far too long, the whole body commits to it, and the swing mostly misses the point. The pacification hit resolves off the back of that swing.
- **Animation note:** one Gush-Locked attack animation per bro, on the 128x128 battle sprites (`design/style_guides/character_sprite_pipeline.md`). Oversized wind-up, slow-motion follow-through, the swing trailing off or stalling in midair, half-lidded eyes throughout. It should read as ridiculous at a glance, with no impact frame worth the name. Frame counts and timing: TBD with the sprite work.

## 🍺 Intoxication Review

**APPROVED (author, 2026-11-12); numbers TBD for the balancing pass.**

### The Three Questions (`design/narrative/bros_choose_debauchery.md`)
| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **What does the bro THINK he's getting?** | A "calibrating, terpene-enhanced creative flow-state" that unlocks effortless European chill, deep philosophical gravitas, and smooth social swagger without any calories |
| **What does he ACTUALLY get?** | "Gush-Locked": a paralyzing couch-lock stone, a mouth of bone-dry sand, and a fixation on trivial nonsense, with no physical momentum at all |
| **The gap: how does it become a gameplay consequence?** | He freezes in the overworld to contemplate scenery, his battle swings turn into giant, pointless pacification flails, he drains his own Bravado, and the hostel morning reveals he ate the party's food or spent its SOVS on delivery |

### No Free Advantage
"Intoxicants do not provide uncomplicated, consequence-free advantages." The cost: the +Defense comes with −Speed and −Accuracy, physical attacks that barely hurt (the Pacified/Mellowed chance is the only upside), a Bravado drain for the whole duration, overworld stops that take control away, the Munchies Tax on any hostel rest, and Cottonmouth after every use.

### 10-Point Design Review (`design/mechanics/intoxication_law.md` §8)
| # | Point | Answer |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Source item or drink | MANGO OG GUSHERS™, bought from Tony420 over ICQ, paid in VapeSwap ($HUFF), delivered by dead drop |
| 2 | Immediate effect | "Gush-Locked": +Defense, −Speed, −Accuracy, Bravado drain; physical attacks become enormous unfocused swings that resolve as pacification hits (reduced damage, chance of Pacified/Mellowed) |
