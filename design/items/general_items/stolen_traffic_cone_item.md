A small traffic cone, stolen (author, f3tq-q2). The bros went to bed at Catastrophe and woke up with it. There is no flashback, no witness and no explanation, ever. The joke is the silence: the one ordinary pickup toast, then nothing (proposal section 4, "Exact silent notification behaviour"). Everything else about the cone (what it looks like, what it is worth) is PENDING (author).

### What The Bros Believe It Is
"PENDING (author)." Under the approved shape no bro comments on the cone, ever, and Pilsner "doesn't know either" (proposal section 4, "Bros" row). If the author wants a bro line anywhere, it waits on a ruling and may never say how the cone was acquired.

## 🎯 Gameplay Integration

### Permanent Cosmetic Change
None. It sits in the inventory and is not affixed to a bro. Any cosmetic or mechanical use is PENDING (author).

**How it arrives (ruled, f3tq-q2 and q12; behaviour from the approved proposal section 4):**
- The roll happens once per wake-up, only if a bro reached Catastrophe since the last wake. The chance is PROPOSED 30-50% (Desk `Eurobound-f3tq-q15`).
- The pickup uses the same toast any pickup uses, with the text "You have added Stolen Traffic Cone to your inventory". No special colour, icon, sound, shake or highlight.
- The toast shows once, on the first control frame after the morning fade-in.
- No bro comments on it. Nothing ever explains it.
- Whether it can be drawn more than once per save is PROPOSED in the proposal ("each item is drawn once per save") and not ruled.
- Every Shitfaced or Catastrophe night also ends with Hungover the next morning (f3tq-q10). That is the night's cost, not the cone's.

**Engine dependencies (PROPOSED in the proposal, not built):** per-bro intoxication tier, the wake-up hook, the evidence pool and the standard pickup toast (proposal section 6, point 9). How a drink raises the tier and how it wears off waits on bead Eurobound-p0j.
