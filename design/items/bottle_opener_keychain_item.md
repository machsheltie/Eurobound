## The Sixteen Slots

Sixteen empty clips wait for the Continental Beer Trail's per-brewery openers — 4 cities × 4 breweries [source: design/quests/general_quests/brewerytour.md, Overview]. Each opener is EARNED on challenge completion at its brewery and added to the keychain (openers are earned, never sold; author ruling 2026-08-04). Each opener has its own item file in `design/items/brewery_tour/`.
