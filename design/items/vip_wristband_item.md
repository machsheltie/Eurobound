# Item Definition: VIP All-Access Wristband

*"Je mi 7!" - The most exclusive children's birthday party wristband in all of Debaucheryville*

## 📋 Item Identity
- **Item ID**: `item_vip_wristband_im_seven`
- **Display Name**: VIP All-Access Wristband
- **Internal Name**: Children's Birthday Party Wristband
- **Item Type**: Permanent Cosmetic / Quest Item
- **Rarity**: Common (bros think it's Legendary)
- **Source**: Shady Wristband Guy, Astronomical Cock-Up Square

---

## 🎭 The Deception

### What The Item Actually Is
A cheap plastic children's birthday party wristband bulk-purchased from a party supply store for approximately 0.003 Sovs each. The text "Je mi 7!" is printed in cheerful Czech, which translates to "I'm 7!" - as in "I am seven years old." These are typically given to children at birthday parties to identify which kids belong to the party group.

### What The Bros Believe It Is
"LEVEL SEVEN ACCESS - the highest tier VIP wristband in all of Europe. This grants us access to every exclusive club, VIP section, and secret venue in Debaucheryville. Level Seven doesn't even EXIST in America. We're basically royalty now."

## 🎯 Gameplay Integration

### Permanent Cosmetic Change
Once acquired, the wristbands become **permanently visible** on all three bros' character sprites for the rest of the game. They cannot be removed, hidden, or replaced.

### Sprite Implementation
- **Visibility**: Always visible on right wrist
- **Layer**: Accessory overlay on arm
- **Animation**: Moves with arm in all animation frames
- **Degradation**: Visual state updates based on in-game days elapsed

### NOT The Same As Equipment Wristband
| Attribute | Scam Wristband (This Item) | Equipment Wristband |
|-----------|---------------------------|---------------------|
| **Name** | VIP All-Access Wristband | Velvet Wristband (VIP Access) |
| **Type** | Permanent Cosmetic | Equippable Accessory |
| **Slot** | None (always visible) | Watch/Bracelet |
| **Stats** | Cosmetic only | +3 Club Entry, +2 Bottle Service |
| **Removable** | Never | Yes |
| **Source** | Shady Wristband Guy scam | Actual club souvenir |

**Note**: Bros CAN equip both - the equipment wristband on one arm while still displaying the scam wristbands permanently.
