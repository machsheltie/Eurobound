## 3. The Artwork Itself

- **Description of the asset:** **[APPROVED — author, Desk Eurobound-rpac-q4, 2026-11-15: "selfie A"; proposed by the Writers' Room as "Flash Blind"]** A 320×240 webcam-era frame. The camera is held at arm's length overhead and the flash fires on the way up, so the face is a white oval with no features in it at all — just a blowout. The bedroom ceiling light sits directly behind and above the head as a bright bulb with a bloom ring, exactly the angle the canon describes. Straightened dark hair fans out across the bedspread below. A corner of a beige CRT is lit blue at the frame edge.
- **Canon anchor:** "clearly a stolen MySpace photo"; "Shot from above, on a bedroom ceiling light" (`legacy_vault_cards.md` Card 4).
