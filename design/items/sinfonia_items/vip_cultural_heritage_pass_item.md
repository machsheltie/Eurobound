## 🎭 The Deception

### What The Item Actually Is
The canon on record, and nothing more:

- **The promise:** Free Unlimited Beer & Traditional Feasting **[LEDGER]**.
- **The catch:** the fine print obligates the bros to complete the Heritage Trial in the basement
  vaults "before a single drop is poured", and the Schnitzel King enforces it when they try to back
  out and demand their drinks **[LEDGER]**.
- **The fine print on record** (the only clauses approved so far; no others may be added without the
  author):
  - Clause nine, as the King cites it: no beer before the Trial is complete ("The Pass is binding in
    this century and in the six before it. You will feast. You will be tried. You will receive no beer
    before the Trial is complete. Clause nine.", `the_schnitzel_king.md:114-115`) **[ROSTER]**
  - "'Unlimited' refers to the duration of your obligation, not to the beer." (The Living Asterisk,
    `living_asterisk.md:75`) **[ROSTER]**
  - "Traditional Feasting is portioned in accordance with tradition. The tradition was a famine."
    (`living_asterisk.md:76`) **[ROSTER]**
  - "By breathing in the Heritage Vaults you consent to be photographed, sung at, and invoiced."
    (`living_asterisk.md:77`) **[ROSTER]**
  - Clause 14(c), on Page Two (the page Chadwick didn't turn): "The Bearer consents to be fed, poured
    upon, photographed and, where tradition requires, buried on the premises."
    (`page_two_of_the_pass_item.md`) **[DESK 2kty-q1]**
- **The payoff:** once the King is beaten, the Pass is honoured. The Free Unlimited Beer is one
  thimble-sized "heritage measure", refilled without limit **[DESK p1y-q3]**, served with the
  On-The-House Wenches' slate for every "complimentary" goblet **[DESK p1y-q4]**.
- **The QR:** its fine print carries a QR, Route B to Holographic Charizard **[DESK 2w7l-q1]** (see
  Gates/Unlocks).

Anything further (its form, who sells it, what it really cost): PENDING (author, Desk `Eurobound-9x55-q1`).
