# NFT icon slab (shared by every Legacy Vault NFT icon)

Author ruling (Desk asset-team-nft-icons-look): "Each icon is a cheap trading-card slab showing its pop-culture image; the Writers' Room proposes the images and I approve". Slab approved on Desk Eurobound-rpac-q4 (2026-11-15). Every NFT icon uses this slab unchanged: same rim, label strip, red "AUTHENTIC" sticker, barcode sticker and scratch pattern. Only the image inside changes. Written by Mistral in the Writers' Room (`WritingRoom/tasks/20261114_2023_nft_icon_images_four_slabs/drafts/mistral_nft.md`).

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **silhouette** | Vertical rectangle, roughly 5:7 width:height, corners rounded, with a thick beveled rim that is visibly deeper than the window it frames; a label strip occupies the top ~1/6 of the height, so the image window below is a wide short rectangle. |
| **scale_cue** | At 128 px with nothing else in frame: a thick clear plastic case, a white strip across the top, a bright image window below it, and one hard white highlight line along the top rim. That is the whole read. |
| **materials_and_cheap_cues** | Injection-molded clear polystyrene with a bluish regrind tint and cloudy plasticizer bloom in two patches; a mold seam down both side edges and a sprue nub on the bottom corner; the inner sleeve is a size too big, so the card inside sits slightly crooked and rattles. The label strip is a paper sticker stuck on crooked with one air bubble and a toner streak across it. Wrong sheen everywhere: dull where it should be glossy, glossy where it should be dull. |
| **colours** | Case plastic: clear grey-blue **#DDE3E6**; rim highlight: white **#FFFFFF**; inner shadow lip: grey **#9AA3A8**; cloudy bloom patches: milky **#C9D2D6**; label strip paper: off-white **#F5F5F0**; label text: near-black **#1A1A1A**; round red sticker: cheap red **#D02A2A**; barcode sticker: white **#FFFFFF** with black bars **#111111**; dust specks: dark grey **#6B6B6B**. |
| **contents** | none (the artwork card inside is specified per-NFT below). |
| **decoration** | Printed paper label strip across the top (words in **lettering**); one round red sticker overlapping the strip's right end; one small white barcode sticker at the bottom-right corner of the case, also crooked. No foil, no hologram, no embossing. |
| **condition** | Fine parallel hairline scratches across the front face, denser near the bottom; one deeper scuff at the top-left corner; two or three dust specks sealed under the plastic; one greasy fingerprint smudge across the lower-right of the window. The rim has a stress whitening crack at one corner. |
| **garnish_and_addons** | none beyond the two stickers named above. |
| **palette_anchors** | Slab itself is location-neutral: **#DDE3E6, #F5F5F0, #D02A2A, #9AA3A8** are new to the item. Each NFT's image window pulls its own anchors from its owning location (see per-NFT fields). |
| **reads_at_icon_size** | 1. A thick clear case with a beveled rim and top highlight. 2. A white label strip across the top with a red dot at its end. 3. A bright pop-culture image in the window below. |
| **must_not_look_like** | Must NOT read as a real grading company's slab: no black-and-gold label layout, no serif logo, no holographic seal, no bottom-heavy barcode block, no "GEM MT"-style grade banner. Must not read as a smartphone, a jewel case, a amiibo box, or a loose card — the thick rim and top strip are mandatory. |
| **lettering** | Label strip, three lines, spelled out exactly: line 1 **"BROCHAIN CERTIFIED"**, line 2 **"GRADE BRO 10"**, line 3 **"SERIAL 00-BRO-99"**. Round red sticker: **"AUTHENTIC"**. Barcode sticker: bars only, no words. Icons are rendered without lettering; no read above depends on the text being legible — the white strip and red dot carry it. |
