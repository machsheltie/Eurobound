## Legendary Hangover — "Imperial Concerto"

(Name per the master spec's Rewards Ladder — "Imperial Concerto"; the legacy source's "The
Imperial Concerto" article is dropped, master wins.)

[source: design/items/brewery_passport_book_sinfonia.md L210-221]
- **Unlock**: All 4 Sinfonia stamps collected
- **Effect**: +18 to ALL stats for entire battle, +Embarrassment immunity during battle
- **Consequence**: "Imperial Hangover" status after battle
- **Imperial Hangover Effects**:
  - -12 all stats for 40 minutes
  - All dialogue options become "overly formal" (comedic)
  - Character randomly bows to NPCs
  - Classical music plays faintly (only you can hear it)
- **Cure**: Strong coffee (Grounds For Concern), sleep, or 40 min wait
- **Price**: 60 Sovs (repurchasable from any stamped Sinfonia location)
- **Flavor Text**: "Four stamps. Four performances. Four ceremonies. You drank like royalty. Now you suffer like royalty. With dignity. And regret."

**Strategic use** [source: design/items/brewery_passport_book_sinfonia.md L223-228]:
- Balanced between Publandia (+15) and Debaucheryville (+20)
- Unique "Embarrassment immunity" useful for social combat bosses
- Shorter recovery than Debaucheryville (40 min vs 45 min)
- Post-battle effects are comedic but manageable
- Best saved for Maestro Vice von Strauss fight or Baroness encounters
