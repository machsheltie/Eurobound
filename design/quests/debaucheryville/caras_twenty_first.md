## 🎭 Core Premise

### The Setup
**What the bros THINK is happening:**
> Lord Pilsner, **newly single** **[CANON — author]**, has found the table he crossed an ocean for: a group of
> just-turned-21s in party clothes, drinking glowing test tubes. "Okay" is an opening. "Seriously?!" means he
> still has it.

**What is ACTUALLY happening:**
> The Blonde Girl thinks a sweet old man is buying her friend's birthday round. She says so at full volume.
> Five girls cheer for "GRANDPA GUY." The bartender, who Pilsner just tipped with a plastic ring, starts a tab
> in Pilsner's name and spends the rest of the night collecting it.

**Why it's funny:**
> The bros got exactly what they came for: a table of 21-year-olds who are thrilled with Lord Pilsner. Just
> not the way he wanted. Then the bill shows up, and three men who flew to Europe to be legends end up in a
> fistfight at an arcade door over five neon shots for a birthday they weren't invited to. They win, and then
> they run from a bartender, who adds a service charge.
