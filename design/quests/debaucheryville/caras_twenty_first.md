## 🎭 Core Premise

### The Setup
**What the bros THINK is happening:**
> Lord Pilsner, **newly single** **[CANON — author; game-wide, round v26]**, has found the table he crossed an ocean for: a group of
> just-turned-21s in party clothes, drinking glowing test tubes. "Okay" is an opening. "Seriously?!" means he
> still has it.

**What is ACTUALLY happening:**
> The Blonde Girl thinks a sweet old man is buying her friend's birthday round. She says so at full volume.
> Five girls cheer for "GRANDPA GUY." The bartender, who Pilsner just tipped with a plastic ring, starts a tab
> in Pilsner's name and spends the rest of the night collecting it.

**Why it's funny:**
> The bros got exactly what they came for: a table of 21-year-olds who are thrilled with Lord Pilsner. Just
> not the way he wanted. Then the bill shows up, and three men who flew to Europe to be legends end up in a
> fistfight at an arcade door over five neon shots for a birthday they weren't invited to. They win, and then
> they run from a bartender, who adds a service charge.

## 🎯 Satirical Targets
- **Primary Target:** The aging bro's belief that he is still in the 21-year-olds' dating pool. To them he's in the grandpa pool.
- **Secondary Targets:** Forced-fun barcade hospitality (TGI Fridays flair, a bar demoted to a ticket booth); tourist-trap bar tabs and "loss prevention" muscle; the bros' sense of generosity (a five-ticket plastic ring as a tip).
- **Cultural Specificity:** the Absinthe Arcade's neon barcade nightlife; tourist venues that run a tab on whoever looks most like a wallet.
- **Y2K/Era References:** *Miss Congeniality* test-tube shots **[CANON — author]**; Pilsner's Sidekick; Chadwick streaming like it's 2003 and the chat is a webcam forum (PROPOSED).
