# Sidequest: The Tram That Never Comes

## 📋 Quest Identity
- **Quest ID:** `debaucheryville_sidequest_eternal_commuter_01`
- **Display Name:** "The Tram That Never Comes"
- **Satirical Subtitle:** "Jiří Has Been Waiting Since Before You Were Born"
- **Quest Giver:** Jiří (The Eternal Commuter) @ Tram Stop #17
- **City:** Debaucheryville
- **Recommended Level:** 2
- **Estimated Duration:** 10 minutes per visit (multiple visits encouraged)

---

## 🎭 Core Premise

### The Setup
**What the bros THINK is happening:**
> A friendly local who can give them directions and insider tips about the city. This guy clearly knows the transit system.

**What is ACTUALLY happening:**
> Jiří has been waiting for Tram #17 for an indeterminate amount of time. He's become a local landmark. Pigeons have nested on him. His beard grows visibly between visits. He will NEVER leave.

**Why it's funny:**
> A darkly absurd commentary on futile persistence, delivered through an NPC who the bros keep trying to get travel advice from while completely missing that he's been frozen in place for possibly decades.

---

## 🎯 Satirical Targets
- **Primary Target:** Absurdist Eastern European humor, bureaucratic futility
- **Secondary Targets:** Tourists treating locals as information kiosks, blind optimism, sunk cost fallacy
- **Cultural Specificity:** Czech public transit culture, Prague's occasionally unreliable trams
- **Y2K/Era References:** Jiří's references to events he's missed suggest he's been waiting since the late 90s

---

## 📖 Quest Structure

### Initial Encounter
**Trigger:** Bros pass Tram Stop #17 for the first time
**Location:** Central Debaucheryville, between Absinthe Arcade and Astronomical Cock-Up Square

*Bros notice a man sitting at a tram stop. He has a reasonable beard, a weathered coat, and pigeons on his shoulders. A small shrine of offerings surrounds him.*

**Lord Pilsner:** "Hey, excuse me? Do you know how to get to the Velvet Curtain Club from here?"

**Jiří:** *without looking away from the tracks*
> "The 17 should be here any moment. The schedule says... ah, the schedule is from 1994. But still."

**Chadwick:** "The... 17?"

**Jiří:** "Yes. The 17. I have a good feeling about today. Today feels like a tram day."

**Bradley:** "Sir, do you know how long you've been waiting?"

**Jiří:** *finally looks at them*
> "My wife asked me to pick up milk. That was in 2003. The milk will be VERY fresh when I return."

*Long pause*

**Lord Pilsner:** "...So you don't know where the club is?"

**Jiří:** *returns gaze to tracks*
> "I know where EVERYTHING is. I have had time to think about the layout of this city. The club is three blocks north. But you could also take the 17. When it arrives."

### Dialogue Pool System

**Every time bros pass Tram Stop #17, Jiří has new dialogue. Categories rotate based on his "mood phase."**

---

#### OPTIMISTIC PHASE (Visits 1-3)

**Line 1:**
> "The 17 should be here any moment. The schedule says... ah, the schedule is from 1994. But still."

**Line 2:**
> "I have a good feeling about today. Today feels like a tram day."

**Line 3:**
> "My wife asked me to pick up milk. That was in 2003. The milk will be VERY fresh when I return."

**Line 4:**
> "Have you ever noticed how the tracks hum slightly before a tram arrives? I have noticed. I notice every hum. Most are false alarms."

**Line 5:**
> "A man told me in 2007 that the 17 was discontinued. I told him that was impossible. I'm still right. He's dead now. Unrelated, I assume."

---

#### PHILOSOPHICAL PHASE (Visits 4-6)

**Line 1:**
> "Is the tram late, or am I early to the rest of my life?"

**Line 2:**
> "Waiting is just traveling through time at normal speed."

**Line 3:**
> "Perhaps the tram was inside me all along. ...No. No, I need the actual tram."

**Line 4:**
> "I have become one with the schedule. The schedule is a lie, but it is MY lie."

**Line 5:**
> "The Buddha said desire is the root of suffering. He never waited for the 17."

**Line 6:**
> "I have read every advertisement on this shelter four thousand times. The phone number for the lawyer has been disconnected since 2011. I called to check."

---

#### BITTER PHASE (Visits 7-9)

**Line 1:**
> "The 22 just passed. The 9 just passed. Even the GHOST TRAM passed. But the 17? The 17 MOCKS me."

**Line 2:**
> "My son graduated university, got married, had children. I missed it. But when that tram comes? Oh, it will be WORTH it."

**Line 3:**
> "They built a metro stop two blocks away. I will NOT abandon my post."

**Line 4:**
> "Someone offered me a ride in a car. A CAR. As if I would betray the 17 with a CAR."

**Line 5:**
> "I have seen empires rise and fall from this bench. Still no tram."

**Line 6:**
> "The pigeons have accepted me as their leader. The pigeons understand commitment."

---

#### CONSPIRACY PHASE (Visits 10-12)

**Line 1:**
> "The 17 exists. I have SEEN it. Once. In 1997. It was beautiful."

**Line 2:**
> "Some say the 17 only appears to the pure of heart. My heart is pure. MY HEART IS PURE."

**Line 3:**
> "The transit authority knows. They KNOW. This is a test. I will pass this test."

**Line 4:**
> "Other trams slow down when they pass. Out of respect? Or mockery? I cannot tell."

**Line 5:**
> "I have documented every tram that has passed this stop. 47,000 entries. None of them were the 17."

**Line 6:**
> "A tourist once took a photo of me for 'local color.' I hope she captured my dignity."

---

#### EXISTENTIAL CRISIS PHASE (Visits 13+)

**Line 1:**
> "What if I AM the tram? What if I'm waiting for myself?"

**Line 2:**
> "A tram passed yesterday going the other direction. The passengers looked so FREE."

**Line 3:**
> "I forgot where I was even going. But I will GO there. Whenever the 17 decides."

**Line 4:**
> "My bench has been replaced twice. I was here for both installations."

**Line 5:**
> "The city council discussed naming this stop after me. I declined. I am not ready to be commemorated. The 17 has not arrived."

**Line 6:**
> "Sometimes I wave at the other trams. To let them know I am not angry at them. It is not their fault they are not the 17."

**Line 7:**
> "Do you know what 'sunk cost fallacy' means? I do. It does not apply here."

**Line 8:**
> "In my mind, I have ridden the 17 to my destination one million times. In reality, I have not moved."

### Bros' Interaction Options

**Each visit, bros can choose to:**

**[ASK FOR DIRECTIONS]**
- Jiří always knows exactly where everything is
- He explains routes in terms of tram stops even for walking directions
- "Three blocks north, or you could wait for the 17 and take it two stops. When it arrives."

**[SUGGEST ALTERNATIVES]**

*Lord Pilsner suggests a taxi:*
> **Jiří:** "A TAXI? Jiří does not SURRENDER. The 17 will come. The taxi is an admission of defeat."

*Chadwick suggests walking:*
> **Jiří:** "Walk? And miss the 17? After all this time? You mock me."

*Bradley offers statistical analysis:*
> **Jiří:** "Your math means nothing here. The 17 operates outside mathematics. The 17 operates outside TIME."

**[OFFER COMPANY]**
- Bros can sit with Jiří for a while
- Jiří appreciates it but warns: "The 17 rewards patience, not companionship. Also, that seat has a pigeon."

**[LEAVE]**
> **Jiří:** "Go. Live your life. Take the trams that come easily. The 17 and I have an understanding."

---

### Progressive Visual Changes

**Visit 1:** Jiří has a neat beard, coat is worn but clean, 2 pigeons on bench

**Visit 3:** Beard slightly longer, coat more weathered, 4 pigeons, small pile of food offerings from locals

**Visit 5:** Beard noticeably longer, pigeons nesting on his shoulders, small shrine of candles around bench

**Visit 7:** Full wizard beard, coat has become part of bench, pigeons have built nest in his hat, tourists taking photos

**Visit 10:** Beard has flowers growing in it, bench has been absorbed into the Jiří-organism, local children leave drawings

**Visit 13+:** Jiří is now a local landmark with a small plaque, pigeons have formed a protective perimeter, city tour buses slow down to point at him

---

### Quest Completion Trigger

**Optional Resolution Path:**

After 10+ visits, bros can choose to:

**[WAIT WITH JIŘÍ FOR ONE FULL NIGHT]**

*Bros sit with Jiří from evening to dawn*

**During the night:**
- Jiří tells his life story (surprisingly beautiful and sad)
- Reveals he was going to propose to his wife the night the milk ran out
- Never made it home to propose
- Wife waited, then didn't
- He's been waiting for the 17 ever since
- The 17 is less about the tram now and more about proving he can finish something

**At dawn:**
> **Jiří:** "You stayed. No one has ever stayed. The pigeons stay, but pigeons do not understand conversation."

> **Lord Pilsner:** "Bro... that was heavy."

> **Jiří:** "Heavy is the head that waits for the 17. But lighter now. Thank you."

*A tram approaches*

**Jiří:** *stands slowly* "That sound... I know that sound..."

*It's the 22*

**Jiří:** *sits back down* "False alarm. But for a moment... for a moment I believed."

**Reward:** Bros receive **"Jiří's Blessing"** - +1 to all Charm checks with locals, who recognize you as "the Americans who sat with Jiří"

## 🎬 POST-CREDITS SCENE REWARD

### Scene Title: "The 17 Arrives"
### Unlock Condition: Visit Jiří 10+ times OR complete night vigil
### Duration: 2 minutes

**SETTING:**
> Tram Stop #17. Debaucheryville. Morning. Five years after the game's events.

**SCENE:**

*The bench is now a proper shrine. Flowers, candles, photos. Tourists pose next to it. A plaque reads: "JIŘÍ'S BENCH - HE WAITED"*

*The bench is empty.*

*A local woman arranges fresh flowers*

**TOURIST:** "Excuse me, is this where the waiting man sat?"

**LOCAL WOMAN:** "Jiří. Yes. He sat here for over twenty-five years."

**TOURIST:** "Did he... did he die?"

**LOCAL WOMAN:** *small smile* "No."

---

*Cut to: Interior of Tram #17*

*Jiří sits in a seat, clean-shaven, coat mended, looking out the window with tears in his eyes.*

*The tram is nearly empty. It's clearly a commemorative revival, "Tram 17 Heritage Line" banner visible.*

**JIŘÍ:** *to no one in particular*
> "It came. After all this time. It came."

**CONDUCTOR:** "Sir, this is the end of the line. You have to get off."

**JIŘÍ:** "I know. I know."

*He doesn't move*

**JIŘÍ:** "But... can I ride it one more time? Just one more?"

**CONDUCTOR:** *softens* "...okay. One more."

*The tram begins moving again. Jiří watches the city pass by.*

---

*Cut to: His old apartment. A woman his age opens the door. This is MARIE, the wife.*

**MARIE:** *seeing Jiří at door* "...Jiří?"

**JIŘÍ:** *holding ancient carton of milk, now a block of mold*
> "I got the milk."

*Long pause*

**MARIE:** "It's been twenty-six years."

**JIŘÍ:** "The 17 was delayed."

*Longer pause*

**MARIE:** *starts laughing, then crying, then both*
> "You idiot. You absolute idiot."

**JIŘÍ:** "I know. I know. But I finished it. I finished something."

*She hugs him. He's home.*

---

*Cut to: Tram Stop #17. A new person sits on the bench. Young man, checking his phone.*

*Tram #22 passes.*

**YOUNG MAN:** *muttering* "Where the hell is the 17?"

*Camera pans to the plaque: "JIŘÍ'S BENCH - HE WAITED"*

*Under it, smaller text: "The 17 runs every three hours. Be patient."*

**NARRATOR:** (voice over)
> "The 17 Heritage Line now runs twice daily. It's never on time. Jiří says that's part of its charm."

**[END SCENE]**

**THE PUNCHLINE:**
> *Text on screen:* "Jiří and Marie renewed their vows on Tram #17. The tram was six minutes late to the ceremony."
