## SHAMSTERDAM DÖNER LOCATIONS

### 📍 5. Leeman's Döner Palace
**Location Type**: Consumable Vendor  
**Location**: Shamsterdam — three streets off the canal ring, near the open-air market

**Visuals**:
A modest storefront with THREE rotating meat towers visible through the window, each one a different shade of golden-brown perfection. The smell of fresh-baked bread mingles with garlic and grilled meat. A small standing counter only — no seats, no luxury, just döner. Locals queue alongside confused tourists. Grease-stained menus in four languages, none spelled correctly. A hand-written sign: "32 YEARS SAME LOCATION. SAME QUALITY. SAME JUDGMENT."

**Vendor**: Mehmet "The Meat Architect"  
Turkish immigrant who's been slinging döner since 1992. Silver hair, massive forearms from decades of meat-slicing. Speaks Dutch, Turkish, English, and Disappointment fluently. Will absolutely judge your pronunciation. Respects appetite above all else.

**Catchphrases**:
- "You want chicken? Chicken is for children. You have the lamb."
- "Thirty-two years I make döner. You think Debaucheryville knows döner? Debaucheryville knows NOTHING."
- "The secret is the bread. Always the bread. Also the meat. And the sauce. Okay, everything is secret."
- "Ah, Americans. You eat like you're afraid the food will fight back. EAT."
- "My grandfather made döner in the old country. His grandfather made döner in the old country. You think some *clown* knows döner?"

**Menu Items**:
| Item | Price | Effects |
|------|-------|---------|
| The Triple Tower | 8 SOVS | All three meats (veal, chicken, lamb), extra garlic sauce, fresh-baked bread. 🥙 +25 HP, +10 Bravado, "Garlic Aura" buff (NPCs keep slight distance for 1 hour) 🇳🇱 Counts toward expansion sidequest |
| Mehmet's Choice | 6 SOVS | "I choose. You eat. This is how it works." Random meat selection, always perfect. 🎲 +20 HP, +5 to random stat, "Blessed by the Architect" buff |
| The Interrogation | 4 SOVS | Mehmet asks where else you've eaten döner before serving you. Answer wrong and he lectures you for 3 minutes. 📚 +15 HP, +2 Wisdom (you learn things) |

### 📍 6. The Kapsalon King
**Location Type**: Consumable Vendor  
**Location**: Shamsterdam — The Flesh Market, 3 AM crowd favorite

**Visuals**:
A bright-lit counter wedged between a coffeeshop and a souvenir store. Aluminum trays stack to the ceiling. The smell of melting cheese and frying potatoes is AGGRESSIVE. A massive hand-painted sign shows a cross-section of the kapsalon with labels: "FRIES (foundation), MEAT (structure), CHEESE (soul), SALAD (lies we tell ourselves), SAUCE (truth)." Drunk tourists and locals alike crowd the counter. Plastic forks scattered on the floor — casualties of structural failure.

**Vendor**: "Big Wim"  
6'5" Dutch-Turkish man, shaved head, gold chain, football jersey stretched over an impressive frame. Third-generation Shamsterdammer whose grandfather was one of the first Turkish immigrants. Speaks in a thunderous mix of Dutch slang, Turkish endearments, and food philosophy. Has strong opinions about EVERYTHING, especially the city where kapsalon was invented (he respects this grudgingly).

**Catchphrases**:
- "Kapsalon means 'hair salon.' You know why? Because the barber who invented it had VISION."
- "In the city where it was invented, they put cheese on everything. This is the way. Accept it."
- "You want small? There is no small. There is only 'you are not ready' and 'regular.'"
- "The fork will break. This is part of the experience. Use your hands like a WARRIOR."
- "Debaucheryville döner? *spits* Publandia döner? *double spits* They wrap their shame in bread. WE MELT CHEESE ON OURS."

## THE TASTE-OFF SHOWDOWN

### Scene: The Ultimate Test

**Setup**: Regardless of which confrontation occurred, the scene converges on the same challenge.

```

The way Shamsterdam has settled disputes for centuries."

Mehmet: *appears from nowhere, arms crossed* 
"The Meat Architect agrees. A formal tasting."

Lord Pilsner: "A... taste-off?"

Big Wim: "Three bites. Three döners. 
Your previous champion versus Shamsterdam's finest."

Mehmet: "No talking. No thinking. Just EATING. 
Your stomach will speak the truth."

Bradley: "This feels like a lot of pressure for 3 AM..."

Chadwick: *already filming* "This is the SUPER BOWL of MEAT."
```

### THE TASTE-OFF MINIGAME

**Mechanic**: A three-round reaction-based minigame

**Round 1 — The First Bite**
- Player takes a bite of their previous champion döner
- Quick-time event: Match the button prompt to "savor properly"
- Success: Accurate memory of original döner
- Failure: "Your taste buds are compromised by cheese"

**Round 2 — The Challenger**
- Player takes a bite of Shamsterdam döner (Leeman's or Kapsalon)
- Quick-time event: More complex button sequence (the Dutch take this seriously)
- Success: Full flavor appreciation
- Failure: "You bit your tongue. Shamsterdam judges you."

**Round 3 — The Moment of Truth**
- Both döners presented simultaneously
- Player must choose by pressing Left or Right within 5 seconds
- Choosing takes LONGER than 5 seconds: Special outcome

## NEW REWARDS: SHAMSTERDAM

### The Aluminum Throne
**Type**: Trophy Item (Display in Hostel Room)
**Description**: "A throne constructed entirely from kapsalon aluminum trays. It's uncomfortable, greasy, and magnificent. Big Wim helped you build it."
**Effect**: +1 HP regen while resting at any hostel
**Hidden Feature**: Sitting in it triggers random döner vendor dialogue

### Continental Gut of Steel
**Type**: Title (Visible in Party Menu)
**Description**: "You have eaten döner across three nations. Your stomach has seen things. Your stomach has DONE things. Nothing can hurt it now."
**Effect**: Immunity to "Food Poisoning" status effect from all vendor food
**Hidden Feature**: Unlocks "food critic" dialogue options with NPCs

### Kapsalon Battle Tray (Upgraded)
**Type**: Weapon
**Description**: "Big Wim signed this one. It's basically a collector's item that you can also throw at people."
**Effect**: +3 Defense when thrown, can be retrieved after battle
