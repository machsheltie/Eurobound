# Enhanced Couchsurfing Chronicles — Publandia Edition

**Premise:** Meet the five most ridiculous women in Publandia, none of whom work anywhere the bros went, all of whom are running something insane, and each of whom offers a "crash spot."
**Optional interiors** where the bros "crash" with people met at a function they weren't invited to, a meeting they weren't qualified for, an initiation into nothing, a siege that isn't happening, and an interview they failed.
**Player must navigate awkward morning-after conversations** to get stat boosts (Charisma, Bravado) or hilariously bad debuffs (Hangover, The Fear).
**1-2 opportunities to crash with NPCs per city** — Publandia runs five, because Publandia has more rooms above more bars than anywhere else on the tour, and it is raining.

> ## ⚠️ BINDING — WHAT A CHRONICLES HOST IS
> **Author ruling.** Couchsurf hosts are **purpose-built characters invented for this system**. They are personas, epithets and self-appointed ranks — *Miss Morphine*, *QUEEN Riviéra Renata*, *Vape Witch Vanessa*, *Backpack Girl Who Never Left*, *CryptoBro69's "Ex"*, *The Eternal Student*. They are not ordinary people with ordinary names and ordinary flats, and they are **never** repurposed city staff.
>
> **A bartender, a café owner, a lecturer, a tour guide and a scorekeeper previously occupied these five slots. All five were struck by author ruling.** They remain perfectly good NPCs in their own location files and **must not be re-promoted into this file by anybody, ever.** Chronicles hosts are written to make the bros look stupid; that is the entire reason each one exists.
>
> ### 🎮 TONE — THE EARTHBOUND REGISTER *(binding on every host in this file)*
> Author, verbatim: *"Our game is based off of EarthBound... We are operating within that type of spectrum where it's really out there. None of their characters were just average characters."*
> 1. **Mundane-modern setting, total absurdity played completely straight.** Nobody in Publandia finds any of these five women strange. That is what makes them funny.
> 2. **One weird premise per character, committed to absolutely.** The premise IS the person — never a quirk bolted onto a normal person.
> 3. **Nothing explains itself.** No lore justification, no origin story, no wink. The bell is a bicycle bell on a stick and nobody says why.
> 4. **Deadpan delivery.** The stranger the character, the more matter-of-fact the presentation.
> 5. **Each one has one inexplicable rule she will not bend** — no umbrellas, don't ask where the groom is, don't laugh, don't mention the book, don't ask her about Ireland — and **none of the five rules is ever explained to anybody.**
> 6. **Memorable on sight.** If a host can be described as "a plausible person with an interesting job," she is wrong and must be reworked.

**House rule for this city:** in Debaucheryville the bros are let into places. In Sinfonia they are *received*. **In Publandia they are told the truth to their faces, plainly, in English, by a woman who is not raising her voice — and they hear a compliment.** Every host in this file says exactly what she means the first time. Not one of them is being cryptic. Not one of them is being cruel. The bros will misquote all five of them for the rest of the game.

> **No stage-Irish.** No lilt, no cadence, no "top o' the mornin'" register anywhere in this file. These women talk like people with jobs and obsessions. The joke is never the accent and never the culture — it is three Americans hearing local plain speech as exotic mysticism, and five lunatics running institutions of one. ## **Enhanced Gameplay Mechanics**

### **Trigger:** Accept an invitation to "crash" from NPCs encountered at a private function, a Thursday meeting, a 1 a.m. recruitment drive, a besieged snug, or the wrong end of a clipboard.

### **Mechanic:** Triggers a morning-after micro-scene with enhanced bro delusion dialogue and choice/random outcomes.

### **Enhanced Payoff Types:**
- **Positive:** +Charm, +Bravado, +Craic, or minor items
- **Negative:** Status effects (Hungover, The Fear, Embarrassed, Absolutely Scuttered), minor Sovs loss, or gag items
- **Mixed:** Flavor collectibles, achievement progress, or FlagMan reference items

### **Publandia-Specific Rule — Everything Gets Said Out Loud and Nobody Hears It**
Debaucheryville hosts take things. Sinfonia hosts invoice things. **Publandia hosts simply tell you.** Every host in this file, at some point in her State 1 scene, states the actual situation in one flat sentence with no ornament on it. It is never a riddle, never a hint, never withheld. It goes straight past all three bros and into the Passport as five stars.

### **Publandia-Specific Rule — Everyone Here Is An Institution Of One**
Every host in this file is the sole officer, founder, chair, commander or fellow of something she made up and has run with total procedural rigour for between nine and twenty-seven years. **The paperwork is always real. The institution never is.** The bros join four of them and are expelled from one, and put all five on their CVs.

### **Publandia-Specific Rule — It Is Raining**
Default weather is `light_rain` at 0.7 probability. Every couchsurf in this city begins with somebody being let in out of the wet, and **the invitation is nearly always about the rain and nothing else.** The bros never once consider this. Cobblestones apply -Speed on every walk of shame in this file.
