# Sidequest: The Eternal Roundabout

## 📋 Quest Identity
- **Quest ID:** `publandia_sidequest_eternal_roundabout_01`
- **Display Name:** "The Cloverleaf of Confusion"
- **Satirical Subtitle:** "Look Kids, Big Ben! Parliament!"
- **Quest Giver:** Auto-triggers when bros attempt to rent a car
- **City:** Publandia (Dublin parody)
- **Recommended Level:** 3
- **Estimated Duration:** 20-30 minutes (plus ongoing background gag)

---

## 🎭 Core Premise

### The Setup
**What the bros THINK is happening:**
> A simple rental car experience to explore the authentic Irish countryside. They're experienced American drivers - how hard can driving on the "wrong" side of the road be?

**What is ACTUALLY happening:**
> They enter a roundabout and literally cannot figure out how to exit. They join a growing collection of vehicles that have been circling for indeterminate periods. It's become a self-sustaining ecosystem of lost tourists.

**Why it's funny:**
> Direct homage to National Lampoon's European Vacation, escalated to absurdist extremes. The roundabout has developed its own culture, economy, and social hierarchy among the perpetually circling drivers.

---

## 🎯 Satirical Targets
- **Primary Target:** Americans completely unprepared for European driving (left-side, roundabouts)
- **Secondary Targets:** Rental car scams, GPS overreliance, tourist overconfidence
- **Cultural Specificity:** Ireland's roundabout-heavy road system, rural driving challenges
- **Y2K/Era References:** Bros reference Mapquest printouts, complain GPS "wasn't like this in the 90s"

---

## 📖 Quest Structure

### Quest Initiation
**Trigger:** Bros visit "Paddy's Premium Motors" car rental
**Location:** Publandia outskirts

*SEAMUS (60s, seen too much, dead behind the eyes) works the rental counter*

**SEAMUS:** "Ah, Americans. Wanting to see the countryside, are ye?"

**Lord Pilsner:** "Yeah, we want the full Irish experience. Open roads. Green hills. Sheep."

**SEAMUS:** "Have ye driven on the left before?"

**Lord Pilsner:** "How hard can it be? It's just... mirror image."

**SEAMUS:** *thousand-yard stare* "That's what they all say."

**Bradley:** "I've studied the road system extensively. I printed directions from Mapquest."

**SEAMUS:** "...Mapquest."

**Chadwick:** "And we have GPS on our phones."

**SEAMUS:** *sliding keys across counter* "God help ye. Especially at the Cloverleaf."

**Lord Pilsner:** "The what?"

**SEAMUS:** "Ye'll see. Everyone sees. Eventually."

---

### The Drive Begins

*Bros in rental car. Lord Pilsner driving. On the wrong side.*

**Lord Pilsner:** "See? This is easy. Just stay left. Stay left. STAY LEFT."

*Honking from oncoming traffic*

**Bradley:** "You're on the right."

**Lord Pilsner:** "I KNOW WHICH SIDE I'M ON, BRAD."

**Chadwick:** *filming from backseat* "Chat, this is authentic European driving content. Lord Pilsner is CRUSHING it."

*They are not crushing it. A sheep looks at them with concern.*

---

### Entering the Roundabout

*They approach The Cloverleaf - a massive, confusing roundabout with multiple exits*

**GPS:** "In 500 meters, enter the roundabout and take the third exit."

**Lord Pilsner:** "Third exit. Got it. Easy."

*They enter*

**Lord Pilsner:** "First exit... second exit... wait, was that the second or first?"

**Bradley:** "I think that was a merge lane."

**Chadwick:** "There's a lot of cars in here."

**Lord Pilsner:** "Just let me focus. Third exit. Third exit."

*They pass an exit*

**Lord Pilsner:** "Was that one?"

**Bradley:** "I don't know. Maybe?"

**GPS:** "Recalculating."

*They continue circling*

**GPS:** "In 200 meters, take the second exit."

**Lord Pilsner:** "SECOND? I thought it was third!"

**GPS:** "Recalculating."

*They pass another exit*

**GPS:** "Make a U-turn."

**Bradley:** "We're in a roundabout. It's ALL a U-turn."

---

### The Roundabout Ecosystem

*After several circles, bros notice they're not alone*

**LORD PILSNER:** "Is that... is that the same van?"

*A family van with "JUST MARRIED 1987" written in faded shoe polish circles past them*

**CHADWICK:** "That sign says 1987."

**BRADLEY:** "That was thirty-seven years ago."

*A skeleton is visible in the passenger seat. The driver waves cheerfully.*

**DRIVER:** *window down* "First time?"

**LORD PILSNER:** "We're trying to find the exit to—"

**DRIVER:** "Doesn't matter. They all lead back here eventually."

*The van continues circling*

---

### The Roundabout Society

*Bros realize the roundabout has developed its own culture*

**Vehicles observed:**
- **The 1987 Wedding Van:** Skeleton bride, still-living groom, eternal honeymoon
- **The Driving Instructor:** Has given up teaching, now just circles with dead eyes
- **The Tour Bus:** Passengers have formed a small community, someone is giving lectures
- **The Learner:** Red "L" plates, has been practicing for 12 years
- **The Local Who Knows:** Claims to know the exit, never takes it
- **The Family of Five (Now Eight):** Children have grown up in the roundabout, had their own children

**TOUR BUS PASSENGER:** *waving* "Welcome! Are you here for the orientation?"

**LORD PILSNER:** "The what?"

**TOUR BUS PASSENGER:** "We do orientations for new arrivals. Tuesdays and Thursdays. You'll want to know about the food truck schedule and the waste management system."

**CHADWICK:** "There's a FOOD TRUCK?"

**TOUR BUS PASSENGER:** "It circles the outer lane. You have to time your merge carefully."

---

### Binary Path Choice

#### PATH A: "The Exit Strategy"
*Bros attempt to escape through logic and determination*

**What bros think they're doing:**
> Using superior American problem-solving to crack the code of European road design.

**What they're actually doing:**
> Making increasingly desperate attempts while missing obvious exits.

**Execution:**

**BRADLEY:** "Okay. I'm going to count the exits. There are... one, two, three... wait, we passed one."

**LORD PILSNER:** "JUST TELL ME WHEN TO TURN."

**BRADLEY:** "NOW!"

*Lord Pilsner turns. It's an entrance lane. More cars join behind them.*

**NEW DRIVER:** *honking* "WHAT ARE YOU DOING?"

**LORD PILSNER:** "WHAT ARE YOU DOING?"

*Both cars now circling together*

---

**Attempt 2: Following Someone**

**CHADWICK:** "That guy looks like he knows where he's going."

*They follow a confident-looking driver*

*The driver is The Local Who Knows. He's been circling for 8 years.*

**THE LOCAL:** *pulling alongside* "You're following me, aren't ye?"

**LORD PILSNER:** "You look like you know the exit."

**THE LOCAL:** "Oh, I know it. I know exactly where it is."

**LORD PILSNER:** "So take it!"

**THE LOCAL:** "I could. But where would I go? Out there? Into the uncertainty? No. In here, I know what comes next. Another circle. Then another. It's... peaceful."

*He accelerates away, still circling*

---

**Attempt 3: The Aggressive Merge**

**LORD PILSNER:** "Screw it. I'm just going to GO."

*He cuts across two lanes*

*Arrives at an exit*

**GPS:** "You have arrived at your destination."

**BRADLEY:** "...this is where we started. The rental place."

**LORD PILSNER:** "WHAT?"

**SEAMUS:** *outside, smoking* "Told ye. They all come back."

---

**Resolution - Path A:**

*After 2 hours of circling, they finally exit by accident*

**LORD PILSNER:** "I DID IT! I GOT OUT!"

*They're on a country road*

**BRADLEY:** "Where are we?"

**GPS:** "In 500 meters, enter the roundabout."

**CHADWICK:** "Is that... a different roundabout?"

**LORD PILSNER:** "NO. NO NO NO."

*It's not the same roundabout. It's a BIGGER roundabout.*

**Rewards - Path A:**
- **"Roundabout Survivor" Title** - +2 Bravado, permanent
- **"Fergus's Respect"** - Rental car discount (still overpriced)
- **Circling PTSD** - Bros flinch whenever they see a circular road sign
- **GPS Trust Issues** - Bradley now insists on paper maps only

---

#### PATH B: "Roundabout Residence"
*Bros give up and adapt to roundabout life*

**What bros think they're doing:**
> Embracing the European experience by becoming part of the local culture.

**What they're actually doing:**
> Living in a car circling a roundabout for three days.

**Execution:**

**LORD PILSNER:** *after hour 4* "You know what? Maybe this isn't so bad."

**CHADWICK:** "We have snacks. We have phone chargers."

**BRADLEY:** "I've actually gotten a lot of reading done."

*They integrate into roundabout society*

---

**Day 1:**
- Meet the other drivers
- Establish position in the circling hierarchy
- Trade snacks with the tour bus community

**Day 2:**
- Lord Pilsner becomes friends with the 1987 Wedding couple
- Bradley gives a lecture on "The Physics of Perpetual Motion" to the tour bus
- Chadwick starts a "Roundabout Life" vlog (gets 3 subscribers, all from the roundabout)

**Day 3:**
- Lord Pilsner is elected "Lane Captain" for the middle lane
- A romance blooms between Bradley and a woman who's been circling since 1994
- Chadwick has existential crisis about whether his content was ever meaningful

---

**The Escape:**

*On Day 3, a local farmer drives through, completely unbothered*

**FARMER:** *window down* "What are ye all doing?"

**LORD PILSNER:** "We live here now."

**FARMER:** "Just... take the second exit. It's signposted."

**BRADLEY:** "We've tried! It doesn't work!"

**FARMER:** "Did ye try... slowing down?"

*Long silence*

**LORD PILSNER:** "...slowing down?"

**FARMER:** "Americans always go too fast. Ye miss the exits. Just... slow down."

*Lord Pilsner slows to 10 mph*

*The exit becomes obvious*

*They exit easily*

**CHADWICK:** "WE WERE GOING TOO FAST?"

**FARMER:** *already gone*

---

**Rewards - Path B:**
- **"Roundabout Elder" Title** - Respected by the circling community
- **"Lane Captain Badge"** item - +1 Charm with confused drivers
- **Bradley's roundabout romance** - She escaped a week later, they text sometimes
- **Chadwick's "Roundabout Life" channel** - 3 subscribers, extremely loyal

---

### Bradley's Existential Observation

**BRADLEY:** 
> "You know, the roundabout is actually a perfect metaphor for life. We're all just circling, looking for our exit, but we don't know which one to take. And the GPS of society keeps recalculating, but it never really knows either. We're all just... circling. Forever. Until we're not."

**LORD PILSNER:** "Brad, you good?"

**BRADLEY:** "I think the roundabout is changing me."

**CHADWICK:** "Should I film this breakdown?"

**BRADLEY:** "Yes. This is content."

**SCENE:**

*Title card: "CLOVERLEAF: A Documentary"*

*The roundabout is now a TOURIST ATTRACTION. There's a gift shop in the center island. Tour buses stop to let people photograph the circling cars.*

**DOCUMENTARY NARRATOR:** (V.O.)
> "The Cloverleaf Roundabout has become one of Publandia's most unusual attractions, drawing visitors from around the world to witness the unique community that has formed within its lanes."

---

*Interview: The 1987 Husband, still circling*

**1987 HUSBAND:** "We could leave now. They finally put in proper signage. But Margaret and I talked about it, and... this is home now. The kids visit on holidays."

*He gestures at the skeleton*

**1987 HUSBAND:** "She agrees. Don't you, Margaret?"

*Skeleton does not respond*

**1987 HUSBAND:** "She agrees."

---

*Interview: A NEW American family, clearly lost*

**NEW HUSBAND:** "We've only been here six hours. Someone will help us, right? RIGHT?"

**NEW CHILD:** "I like it here. That old man gave me candy."

**DOCUMENTARY NARRATOR:** (V.O.)
> "Approximately forty percent of new roundabout entrants exit within the first day. The remaining sixty percent typically require a minimum of seventy-two hours."

---

*Interview: SEAMUS at the rental counter*

**SEAMUS:** "I've been renting cars to tourists for forty years. The Cloverleaf has claimed about four hundred of them. Permanently. They live there now. It's not my fault. I tell them. I always tell them."

**SEAMUS:** "...I forget. I'm old."

---

*Interview: LORD PILSNER via video call*

**LORD PILSNER:** "Do I think about the roundabout? Every day. Every single day. When I close my eyes, I still see the lanes. I still hear the GPS. 'Recalculating.' I hear it in my dreams."

**INTERVIEWER:** "Do you have any advice for people planning to visit?"

**LORD PILSNER:** "Slow down. Just... slow down. That's all it takes. But no one listens. We didn't listen. And now I'm thirty-nine— TWENTY-NINE. I'm twenty-nine. The roundabout ages you."
