## 🎭 Core Premise

### The Setup
**What the bros THINK is happening (Pilsner):**
> He put on a hell of a show. 1.3 million people watched Lord Pilsner shimmy, and it's because of his charisma, his dancing and his irresistible international appeal.

**What is ACTUALLY happening:**
> A 39-year-old American in cargo shorts entered a contest he was neither invited to nor eligible for. The internet cropped his head off. His abdomen went viral independently of him: "His belly has achieved greater cultural penetration than he has." (author)

**Why it's funny (the author's beat):**
> "Not 'Pilsner secretly becomes cool.' It's that reality has inexplicably given this ridiculous man one highly specific physical gift that will only make him more insufferable." And the internet "accidentally preserved the one fact most dangerous to his ego: the son of a bitch really was on beat. So everyone else sees humiliating meme. Pilsner sees independent verification of technique."
