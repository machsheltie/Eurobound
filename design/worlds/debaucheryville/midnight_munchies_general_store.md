# Midnight Munchies General Store

*Late-night survival essentials for those who should have planned ahead — open when everything else is closed, priced accordingly*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: debaucheryville_shop_midnight_munchies_01
- **Display Name**: Midnight Munchies General Store
- **Czech Translation**: "Půlnoční Mlsání"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Everything You Need at 3 AM (For 3 AM Prices)"
- **World Location**: Debaucheryville
- **Area Zone**: Hostel Row, Budget Accommodation District (strategic late-night positioning)
- **Location Type**: Convenience Store / Late-Night Survival / Emergency Supplies / Desperation Depot

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 10 PM - 8 AM (the hours of need)
- **Stat Requirements**: None (desperation is the only currency that matters)
- **Item Requirements**: Gold for purchases (2 Sovs-35), acceptance of markup
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (Midnight Munchies finds the lost)
- **Special Conditions**: Prices increase by 20% after 2 AM ("Desperation Tax")

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through door, clutching whatever you needed badly enough to pay these prices
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (survival is ongoing)
- **Failure States**: Cannot fail (though your wallet might feel like it did)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Regular" status after 5 purchases, Věra stops asking if you're okay

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Midnight Munchies is the convenience store that exists for one reason: you didn't plan ahead, and now you need things at 3 AM. The markup is real, the products are essential, and Věra has been watching this exact scenario play out for 15 years. She doesn't judge — judgment costs extra and she already charges enough.

The satire is the universal experience of the late-night convenience store trip: knowing you're paying too much, knowing you should have prepared, and buying it anyway because what choice do you have?

### The Věra Philosophy
"Every night, same faces with different names. They need energy drinks. They need aspirin. They need things they should have bought before. I'm not their mother. I'm their 3 AM solution. The price includes the lesson: plan ahead next time. They won't. I'll see them tomorrow."

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Late-night desperation pricing, the "I have no choice" purchase
- **Secondary Targets**: Convenience store as lifeline, the 3 AM economy
- **Cultural Specificity**: Eastern European late-night convenience, Prague's tourist survival needs
- **Humor Approach**: Matter-of-fact acceptance of the desperate transaction
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Everyone has been here, no shame in the purchase

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Exploiting the desperate with markup ("Desperation Tax")
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Positioned to catch hostel-to-bar-to-hostel traffic
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who desperately need energy drinks/kebabs at peak pricing hours
- **Comedy of Errors**: The "I should have bought this earlier" realization, the 3 AM kebab decision
- **Consequence System**: Items work, prices hurt, Věra remembers

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Quick convenience store run, no big deal

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Paying premium for poor planning at the hour of maximum need

**How impaired are they**: Usually very (it's 3 AM, they've been out)

**The transaction reality**:
- Energy drink that's 2 Sovs in daytime: 4 Sovs at 3 AM
- Aspirin that's 3 Sovs at pharmacy: 8 Sovs here
- "Worth it" because what's the alternative?
- Alternative: should have planned ahead (too late now)

**The Věra assessment**:
- She knows why you're here
- She's not surprised
- She's also not going to comment (unless you're a Regular)
- The transaction is professional, the markup is understood

**How locals interact**: Only here when truly necessary, accept the prices, respect Věra

### The Desperation Tax
- **Trigger**: 2 AM - 6 AM
- **Effect**: All prices +20-30%
- **Notification**: Visible price change when entering during these hours
- **Věra's Comment**: "Prices went up at 2. You know this. Everyone knows this."
- **Exception**: None (Věra runs a business)

### The Heat Lamp Lottery
- **Trigger**: Purchase from heat lamp station
- **Items**: Kebab, sausage, "mystery item"
- **Quality Roll**: 
  - 60% Fresh enough
  - 30% Questionable but edible
  - 10% Why did you do this
- **Effect**: HP restoration varies with quality
- **Věra's Take**: "I rotate them. When was that one made? ...recently. Probably."

### The "Regular" Status
- **Trigger**: 5 purchases across separate visits
- **Effect**: Věra acknowledges you, offers occasional wisdom
- **Perk**: First warning about Desperation Tax timing
- **Dialogue Unlock**: Věra's actual opinions available
- **Identity**: "You're here enough that I know your face. This isn't a compliment or criticism. It's fact."

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Purchase without flinching at prices, accept the markup with dignity, buy the Extreme energy drink
- **Lose Bravado**: Complain about prices (Věra is unmoved), try to negotiate (Věra is unmoved), ask "is this fresh?" (Věra is unmoved)
- **Neutral**: Standard transaction, standard markup, standard desperation

### The "Could Have Planned Ahead" Notification
- **Trigger**: Purchasing item also available cheaper elsewhere
- **Content**: Brief notification showing where cheaper option exists
- **Effect**: Player knows, player buys anyway
- **Věra's Wisdom**: "You could have gotten this cheaper. But you didn't. And now you're here. The extra cost is the lesson."

## NPCs & Vendors
### The Proprietor

**Věra Novotná**
- **Role**: Owner/operator, overnight shift permanent, witness to human need
- **Appearance**: 60s Czech woman, reading glasses, cardigan, seen everything twice
- **Background**: Former factory worker, opened shop in '09, found her calling serving the desperate
- **Demeanor**: Professional detachment, occasional maternal moments, no judgment, maximum observation
- **The Věra Presence**: You feel watched, assessed, and accepted simultaneously
- **Family**: One of the Debaucheryville Novotnýs. Her brother Jiří has been waiting for Tram #17 since 2003; her nephew Milan watches the astronomical clock all day. "We are a family of people who stay where we are put."
- **Philosophy**: "I sell necessities at necessary hours. The prices are fair for the service. You're paying for availability, not just product."

**Věra's Dialogue Categories**:

*The Transaction:*
- "That's 12 Sovs. ...16 Sovs after 2 AM. You knew this."
- "Energy drink, aspirin, kebab. The classic combination. 15 Sovs."
- "Cash or card. Either works. I've seen worse purchases."
- "Need a bag? ...1 Sov. Everything costs something."

*The Observations (Not Judgment):*
- "Third night this week. You're becoming a regular. This is neither good nor bad. It just is."
- "You look like you need the hangover kit. I'm not telling you what to buy. I'm observing."
- "That kebab has been there since 11. It's fine. Probably. Your choice."
- "You're buying the fake wristband. I sell it. I don't promise it works. You understand."

*The Wisdom (Regulars Only):*
- "You come here every night instead of buying in bulk during the day. You know this costs more. And yet."
- "The energy drinks don't actually give you energy. They borrow from tomorrow. You're always in debt."
- "I've worked this shift for fifteen years. The faces change. The purchases don't. Human needs are consistent."
- "The best customers are the ones who buy the hangover kit BEFORE they need it. Optimists, or realists. Hard to tell."

*The Rare Care:*
- "You don't look good. Not my business, but... drink water. The energy drinks aren't water."
- "It's cold out. Here." *slides hand warmers across counter* "Those are free. Don't tell anyone."
- "You've been here a lot. Are you okay? ...you don't have to answer. I've just noticed."
