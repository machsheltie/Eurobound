## 🎭 Core Concept

### The Racket
Shish Kahn is the kebab Kebab Kombat has been building. Every press of the REGRET button stacks another layer, another sauce, another decision, until the cone is too unstable to stay inside the cabinet and climbs out with four arms. He's the four-armed sub-boss of 90s gore fighters, rebuilt as the thing every night out in Debaucheryville ends with: the 3 a.m. döner. You don't order it. You report to it.

He was digitized, like the rest of the roster, from a man in a foil-and-flatbread costume, photographed under a single sodium lamp in a parking lot. Two of the arms are real. The other two are held on with skewers. Nobody has ever been told which.

The racket is the loop. Beat him and the cabinet prints the **One Regret-Free Kebab Coupon**: 20% off at a participating kebab vendor. That sends the bros out to buy another kebab, which builds another cone, which is why they come back with another four tokens. The cabinet is a kebab-shop affiliate deal with fists.

### What Bros Believe
- **Lord Pilsner** has found a worthy opponent and announces they will eat him afterward, "as is the warrior's way." He always orders the local specialty. Tonight the local specialty has four arms and is punching him.
- **Bradley** believes Shish Kahn is "ninety percent protein, bro" and wants to bench-press him between rounds. He is roughly ninety percent sauce.
- **Chadwick** is setting up a mukbang stream of the fight and keeps telling chat the boss "agreed to it." Shish Kahn agreed to nothing.

### The Comedy Gap
The bros think they're fighting the final boss of a legendary underground cabinet. They're fighting dinner, and dinner is winning. This is the body engine as a fight: nearly-40 stomachs against a 3 a.m. kebab that hits back, and three men who will try to eat it anyway. Satire targets: Debaucheryville's late-night kebab culture as the inevitable last stop of every bender; the arcade's coupon-loop economics; the bros' belief that an appetite is a personality.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | The cone is from tonight. The meat is not. The costume is from 1994 |
| Build | A towering vertical döner cone, glistening and slowly rotating, with four arms bolted on at uneven heights and a flatbread draped over the top like a cape. Moves in a heavy, rotating shuffle; he can't turn his head, so he turns his whole body |
| Height | Looming. The tallest sprite in the Arcade |
| Energy | The last decision of the night |

### Costume & Distinguishing Features
- **The cone**: Stacked layers of shaved meat, each a slightly different colour, with dripping sauce seams between them. A rotisserie spike runs straight through the top and out the crown like a spire.
- **The four arms**: Two meaty and real, two foil-wrapped and skewered on. The foil arms hold carving knives the size of tram signs; the real ones hold a pita and a squeeze bottle.
- **Accessories**: A heat-lamp halo glowing orange overhead; a laminated "MEAT'S FRESH" sign hanging off one knife by a zip tie.
- **Tell**: He rotates a quarter-turn between every attack, like the spit is still running. He doesn't stop rotating in dialogue either.
- **Digitized tell**: The chroma-key green fringe is worst on the two foil arms, which were clearly photographed separately.

### Sprite Notes
- **Battle sprite**: Front-view static (EarthBound style), centred, overlapping the stage floor. Slow 4-frame rotation idle. Background: the Grease Pit (see `condiment_clan_ninja.md`).
- **Phase states**: Phase 2 drops one foil arm (it lands on the stage floor and stays there as a prop for the rest of the fight); sauce seams start dripping; heat-lamp halo flickers. Phase 3: cone visibly leaning, a second sauce colour running.
- **Telegraph (KEBALITY)**: One full turn of wind-up. He raises a pita the size of a bedsheet over his head with all remaining arms, the heat-lamp halo flares white, and either Count Continue or (if the Count is down) Kahn himself, in the same pitched-down announcer voice, says FINISH... HIM. The screen dims to just Kahn and the target bro, the classic fatality darkening.
- **Kebality animation**: The target bro is wrapped, rolled, foiled and set on the spit for one rotation, then dropped back to his battle position, still wrapped (the Stun turn). Played straight, full ceremony.
- **Overworld sprite**: None. He appears on the cabinet attract screen as a silhouette behind the title.
- **Palette-swap budget**: None.

## 🧠 Personality & Dialogue

### Archetype
**The Inevitable Last Order**: Shish Kahn speaks like an ancient warlord and thinks like a heat lamp. He believes, correctly, that every night in this city ends with him, and he treats the bros arriving at 9 p.m. instead of 3 a.m. as an administrative error he'll correct by force. He's courtly, enormous, unhurried and dripping. He never threatens. He *announces terms*, and the terms are always garlic.

### Pre-Battle Dialogue
*Trigger: KK-3 rung begins. The REGRET button flashes on its own five times; the screen fills with stacking meat layers; the cone climbs out.*
> **Count Continue**: "FINAL ROUND! Please welcome... the LAST ORDER OF THE NIGHT!"
> **Shish Kahn**: "Every night ends with me. You have simply arrived early."
> **Lord Pilsner**: "Gentlemen, we have found the local specialty. After this, we eat him. Warrior's way."
> **Bradley**: "Bro's ninety percent protein. I'm benching him between rounds."
> **Shish Kahn**: "I am ninety percent sauce. The other ten percent is not your business."
> **Chadwick**: "Okay chat, the boss agreed to a mukbang. He agreed. Look at him. That's consent energy."
> **Shish Kahn**: "Garlic or chili, little men."
> **All three**: "BOTH."
> **Shish Kahn**: "Correct. And it is extra."

### Defeat Dialogue
> **Shish Kahn** (cone toppling slowly, still rotating): "You have beaten the kebab. Tonight. The kebab is not beaten. It is *postponed*."
> **Shish Kahn**: "Take my coupon. Spend it at three in the morning. I will be there."

*Stage direction: the cone collapses into the screen. The cabinet prints a coupon. Lord Pilsner presses his face to the warm glass to "taste victory."*
> **Lord Pilsner**: "Notes of cumin. And static."
> **Bradley**: "We're getting kebab after this, right?"
> **Chadwick**: "Obviously. Content."

### Post-Defeat Presence (If Any)
Back on the attract screen as a silhouette the moment the fight ends. He returns every time the ladder reaches KK-3. No grudge, no arc. He's the last order of every night, and the bros have plenty of nights left.

### Phases
| Phase | Trigger | Behavior Change |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | Fight start | Four-Arm Carve, Close Shave, Extra Layer. Kebality available once |
| 2 | HP < 50%: **Sauce Integrity Failing** | One foil arm falls off and lies on the stage. Four-Arm Carve drops to 3 hits; Speed rises 3 → 6. Heat Lamp Glare unlocks. Kebality available once more |
| 3 | HP < 20%: **The Lean** | Cone leans; Extra Layer disabled and any stacks drop off. Heat Lamp Glare takes over Extra Layer's weight. Kebality can trigger on its own cooldown (every 3rd round) even with Count Continue alive, still fully telegraphed |

"Sauce Integrity" here is flavor on standard HP-threshold phases, a callback to the cabinet's old `sauce_integrity_reaches_zero` failure condition. No new meter is proposed.

### Buffs & Debuffs They Use
- **Self/ally buffs**: Extra Layer (+Defense); can receive Hardcore Gamer from Count Continue's "FINISH HIM!" when the call isn't being spent on a Kebality cue.
- **Debuffs on the party**: Grease Fingers, Sloppy, Food Poisoning (master list); Stun (1 turn) as used in the Combat Bible.

### Immunities / Resistances / Weaknesses
| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Immune to** | Embarrassed (he's a man in a foil suit in a parking lot; there's nothing left to embarrass) and Food Poisoning (he's the source) |
| **Resistant to** | Stun, 50% resist. A cone on a spike does not fall over easily |
| **Weak against** | Blunt Physical: Lord Pilsner's **Ceremonial Beer Paddle** and Bradley's **Broken Pool Cue Staff** deal +25%, because the whole structure is held together by one 2004 flatbread. Pilsner calls it "tenderising" |
| **Vulnerability window** | The turn after KEBALITY resolves, he has to rebuild the cone: no action, Defense halved, and his Extra Layer stacks drop off |

### Arena & Gimmick
The Grease Pit (shared Kebab Kombat stage; see `condiment_clan_ninja.md` for the description and the fixed resetting ladder, ruled 2026-11-09). In this fight the heat lamp and the stage floor are the main readable elements; the dropped foil arm stays on the floor as a prop from Phase 2. No arena hazard proposed.
