## 🎭 Core Concept

### The Racket
The Arcade doesn't chase unpaid tabs with lawyers. It chases them with two enormous men in matching black polos and tiny employee badges, who treat beating up a customer as an accounts-receivable workflow. **[CANON — approved draft: "built like somebody taught a pair of refrigerators to collect debts"; neckless, matching black polos, tiny employee badges, absurd shoulders.]** They talk in billing language because to them this *is* billing. The violence is itemized.

### What Bros Believe
- **Lord Pilsner** thinks the venue respects him enough to send its best. Only a legend gets two.
- **Bradley** thinks this is the bar fight his personal quest has been waiting for. He's right, and it's over five test tubes.
- **Chadwick** thinks it's a collab and starts streaming.

### The Comedy Gap
Three men who flew to Europe to be legends are brawling at an arcade's front door over **five neon shots for a birthday party they weren't invited to.** The enforcers bring no rage and no menace-speech, just the calm of a debt that has been entered into the system. The bros win, and then they **run away from a bartender** **[CANON — approved draft: "After winning the bros flee"]**, and from the doorway he adds a service charge. The bros aren't scared, they're dodging a bill (author framing); in their account the escape is a victory lap.
