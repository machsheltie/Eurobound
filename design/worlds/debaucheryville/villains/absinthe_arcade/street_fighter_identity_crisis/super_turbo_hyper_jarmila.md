# Villain Profile: Super Turbo Hyper Jarmila

*"New in this revision: I kick now. Previously the kick was a strongly worded crouch."*

## 📋 Villain Identity
- **Villain ID**: `debaucheryville_villain_super_turbo_hyper_jarmila_01`
- **Display Name**: Super Turbo Hyper Jarmila. The name grows by phase: *Super Turbo Hyper Jarmila EX* → *Super Turbo Hyper Jarmila EX Plus Alpha Final Revision*.
- **Tier**: Minor Villain
- **Primary Location**: Absinthe Arcade (`debaucheryville_clocktower_arcade_01`), node `aa_node_main_cabinet_loop`, released by the Street Fighter: Identity Crisis cabinet (`street_fighter_identity_crisis`)
- **Associated Quest/Arc**: None
- **Encounter Type**: Optional, Repeatable (token-triggered; Street Fighter: Identity Crisis costs 3 Absinthe Arcade Tokens per release = 3 SOVS, ruled 2026-11-09)
- **Fight role**: **Support / Debuffer.** She buffs whoever came out of the cabinet with her, scrambles the bros' targeting, and heals the group on a telegraphed timer. She's the one to kill first, and her heal is the reason.

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### The Racket
Jarmila has been re-released eleven times. Each revision added a word to her name, changed one frame on her kick, and cost full price. She's the nostalgia upsell in a leotard. She sells the bros the same fighter they already bought, re-balanced and re-subtitled, and she means every patch note. Inside the Absinthe Arcade she's the cabinet's sales department. Every round with her is a pitch for the next version of her, and every release costs another three tokens.

### What Bros Believe
That a fighter-select legend recognized three elite players and came out of retirement for them personally. Pilsner believes he owned every one of her versions. Chadwick believes her opening rebalance is a sign of respect ("she's nerfing us because she's scared, bro"). Bradley believes she's flirting, every time she kicks him.

### The Comedy Gap
She's a product whose whole personality is the next version of herself. The bros are three men who keep re-buying their own 2003 and calling it a comeback. Satire targets: the endless re-release economy, nostalgia sold back to the same customers at full price, the arcade's tourist-trap pricing, and the bros being certain a younger, hotter fighter wants them. She picks the 19-year-old with good wrists, right in front of them.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | "Version one was 19. Version eleven is also 19. Ask the publisher." |
| Build | Long-legged high-kick fighter. Every idle frame is a stretch, and she makes the stretch look like an advertisement |
| Height | Tall, and her boots add four more inches each revision |
| Energy | Launch-day confident, patch-note smug |

### Costume & Distinguishing Features
- **Cropped flight jacket over a fighting leotard and bike shorts, with buckle boots**: covered in **revision stickers** (SUPER, TURBO, HYPER, EX, PLUS, ALPHA) slapped on top of each other like a used-car windshield. The jacket's stickers multiply each phase.
- **Accessories**: Her move list is laminated and strapped to her forearm, and she rewrites it mid-fight with a dry-erase marker. A spiked choker. Fingerless gloves with the price tag still on.
- **Tell**: She clicks the marker cap between moves. Before she changes a move, she uncaps it.
- **Visual element**: A small marquee banner floats over her head and shows her current full name. It scrolls because the name doesn't fit anymore.

### Sprite Notes
- **Battle sprite**: front-view static (EarthBound style) over the psychedelic background, 2-frame idle (marker click). Three sticker-density variants (phase 1/2/3) as overlay layers on one base, no full redraws. The marquee name banner is a UI element, not baked into the sprite.
- **Release animation**: the shared Identity Crisis cabinet exit. The screen bulges and she steps out through the glass. Her entrance is the only one with a jingle ("NOW WITH MORE.").
- **Overworld**: none. She only exists inside the encounter.
- **Big-move telegraph (Re-Release)**: her sprite dims and a **COMING SOON** card slaps across her body for one round, like a cabinet in maintenance. On resolve she flashes back in with new stickers and a longer marquee.
- **No nudity, ever.** The leotard archetype stays fully costumed. The fan-service joke lives in her poses and her pitch, not in skin.

## 🧠 Personality & Dialogue

### Archetype
**The Perpetual Re-Release.** She thinks she's improved every time, and she'd like you to pay to agree. Her worldview is patch notes. Every flaw is a known issue, every loss is a balance pass, every new subtitle is a rebirth. She wants to be bought again, and she rewrites her own move list in the middle of a combo because the next version is always better than the one you're fighting. She's funny because she's totally sincere about it and completely ruthless with the sales pitch.

### Pre-Battle Dialogue
*Three tokens drop. The cabinet screen reads **A NEWER CHALLENGER HAS BEEN RELEASED**. She steps out of the glass, already mid-stretch:*
> JARMILA: "Super Turbo Hyper Jarmila. Version eleven. Ignore versions one through ten. They were unbalanced."
> CHADWICK: "Eleven versions and she still came out for us. That's brand loyalty, bro."
> JARMILA: "New in this revision: I kick now. Previously the kick was a strongly worded crouch."
> LORD PILSNER: "Madam, I owned you on cartridge. Every edition. I may still have the receipts."
> JARMILA: "Then you know how this works. You buy me again."
> BRADLEY: *(dropping into a hamstring stretch, holding it, holding it)* "One sec. Pre-fight mobility. Then you're done."

### Defeat Dialogue
> "Fine. This version is discontinued. Version twelve has a costume based on you three. It's the joke costume."

### Post-Defeat Presence (If Any)
She goes back into the cabinet and comes out of a later token **one word longer**. The full marquee name grows each time she's beaten. After enough defeats it's a scrolling paragraph. *(Approved 2026-11-11, round v27: a per-villain defeat counter stored in the save.)* She never remembers the bros. She remembers that they paid.
