## 🎭 Core Concept

### The Racket
In the original game, the turtles carry you across the river and then dive without warning.
The Sinking Fund has turned that into a wealth-management practice. Three pixel turtles in a
row, one shared balance sheet, one pitch: *stand on us, we'll carry you across.* Their client
intake is a list of questions that sound profound to a man on his fifth green drink: *Where do
you see yourself in five lanes? What's your risk tolerance? Have you considered that the other
side might not exist?* Then the shells flash red, the whole fund goes under, and the client is
in the river and his SOVS are "under management." They wind up and relaunch with every token.
The middle turtle also runs a side business in small baggies out of his shell, strictly cash,
"for the ride across." They are the only fund in Debaucheryville whose collapse comes with a
visible warning light, and it still works every time.

### What Bros Believe
- **Chadwick** believes he has been offered a board seat. He keeps buying in because "you
  don't sell a turtle at the bottom, bro, that's literally Finance 101."
- **Lord Pilsner** believes he is a natural turtle whisperer, and explains, with total
  confidence and zero accuracy, that turtles were the official river ferry of medieval brewing
  guilds, "which is why they call it a *shell*-ing, the old coin."
- **Bradley** believes the diving is a fitness challenge and is trying to out-hold-his-breath
  three turtles. He also bought a baggie.

### The Comedy Gap
The Fund dives every time, the tell is identical every time (the shells flash red, exactly as
they did on the 1981 cabinet), and the bros read every dive as the dip they should be buying.
Their "questions about your choices" are a financial-adviser intake form, and the bros fill it
out with total sincerity: Chadwick lists his risk tolerance as "yes." Targets: managed-fund
jargon dressed up as wisdom, fee-stacked "wealth management" sold to drunk tourists, the
tourist-trap shell game, and the bros' bottomless faith that the next dip is the
bottom.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | Founded 1981, rebranded every quarter since |
| Build | Three squat pixel turtles in a tight row, bobbing in perfect sync like a synchronized-swimming team that went into private equity |
| Height | Ankle-high individually; read as one long low platform target |
| Energy | Calm, trustworthy, already underwater |

### Costume & Distinguishing Features
- **Shells**: Green 8-bit shells, each with a gold-leaf letter painted on: S, F and a third
  that has been scraped off and repainted so many times it now just reads $. A tiny pinstripe
  tie hangs from each neck.
- **Accessories**: The left turtle wears a Bluetooth earpiece the size of its head. The right
  turtle holds a laminated prospectus that is just a picture of the river. The middle turtle's
  shell has a sliding hatch with a hand-lettered sign: SIDE HUSTLE — CASH ONLY.
- **The tell (canon to the source)**: before every dive, the shells flash red, three times, in
  order. The bros see it every time and love it every time.
- When they surface, they come up wearing the party's money: SOVS-coin sprites stuck to their
  shells like barnacles.

### Sprite Notes
- **Battle sprite**: front-view static (EarthBound style), three turtles abreast on the pixel
  river band of the cabinet playfield. One HP bar, one target, three bobbing bodies, staggered
  one frame apart.
- **Overworld / release sprite**: the cabinet's river band overflows the screen glass as a
  sheet of green mist and the three turtles paddle out in formation onto the cabinet deck.
- **States**: surfaced / red flash (telegraph) / submerged (only three ripple rings and the
  earpiece's blinking light visible) / resurfaced-with-SOVS-barnacles.
- **Palette-swap budget**: none.
- **Telegraph for Past Performance Is No Guarantee**: the full red-flash sequence plus a
  scrolling stock ticker across the bottom of the playfield.

## 🧠 Personality & Dialogue

### Archetype
**The Rug-Pull Turtle Trio** — They speak as one fund and finish each other's sentences like a
pitch deck. They are not cruel; they are fiduciary. They will carry you exactly as far as the
middle of the river and then honour their obligation to the fund, which is to go under. Every
question they ask about your choices is an onboarding question, and every answer you give them
is a yes.

### Pre-Battle Dialogue
*Two tokens drop. The cabinet's river band spills over the bezel as green mist. Three turtles paddle out in a perfect row and line up in front of the bros like a panel interview.*
> **The Sinking Fund** (left): "Gentlemen. Thanks for coming in."
> **The Sinking Fund** (middle): "Before we carry you across, a few quick questions."
> **The Sinking Fund** (right): "Where do you see yourselves in five lanes?"
> **Chadwick**: "Verified. Blue check. Huge. Brand deals, but, like, *meaningful* ones."
> **Bradley**: "On the other side. Swole. Probably shirtless."
> **Lord Pilsner**: "Gentlemen, I see myself on a throne carved out of a keg, which, historically, is how your Doges did it."
> **The Sinking Fund** (all three): "Wonderful. Wonderful answers. Please step onto the shells."
*All three bros step onto the shells. The shells flash red.*

### Mid-Battle Barks
| Trigger | Line |
|---------|------|
| Fight start | "Your capital is at risk. So is your face. Shall we begin?" |
| Below 50% HP | "We're not *losing*. We're consolidating. Into fewer, smaller turtles." |
| Big-move telegraph | "No more bets, gentlemen. Please keep your arms and wallets inside the turtle." |
| Bro gets KO'd / status lands | *(Embarrassed lands)* "Wonderful. We'll put 'shirtless' down as your exit strategy." / *(Tourist Trap Victim lands)* "Congratulations, you're now a Platinum Client. Fees go up, obviously." |
| Chadwick buys in again | "Love the conviction." / "This is why we call you a long-term holder. You can't get off." |
| Lord Pilsner uses any social move | *(On a log scrolling across the background, a pixel lady frog drifts by. Pilsner winks at her. She hops onto the next log with a younger frog in a backward cap.)* **Lord Pilsner**: "She's playing it cool. That's how you know." |

### Defeat Dialogue
> "We're winding up the fund. You'll receive your money back, less the management fee, less the performance fee, less the fee for telling you about the fees. Same river, same time, new name."
*The three turtles sink out of sight in a slow, dignified row. Three ripples. A soggy pile of SOVS bubbles up.*

### Post-Defeat Presence (If Any)
Relaunches with every token under the same name, same questions, same red flash. The Fund
never acknowledges previous clients. Chadwick treats every relaunch as "getting in early."

### Buffs & Debuffs They Use
- **Self/ally buffs**: +Defense via Hedge the Shells; +Speed to allies during the dive.
- **Debuffs on the party**: Embarrassed, Tourist Trap Victim (both master list). Tourist
  Trap Victim persisting after battle (2× shop prices until a hotel rest or three new areas) is
  intended: the Fund's clients overpay for the rest of the night.

### Immunities / Resistances / Weaknesses
| Category | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Immune to** | Embarrassed (it's a fund; it has no shame, only disclosures), Tourist Trap Victim (it *is* the trap) |
| **Resistant to** | Social −50%. They have heard every pitch, because they wrote them. |
| **Weak against** | Lord Pilsner's belly and body moves (Beer Belly Bash, Barrel Roll, Bouncer Toss) at +50%. A 39-year-old man belly-flopping onto three turtles is the only market correction the Fund can't hedge. |
| **Vulnerability window** | The turn after it surfaces from Past Performance Is No Guarantee: Defense −50% for 1 turn (a plain stat drop, not a new status), SOVS barnacles visible on the shells. Battle-log flavor: "The Sinking Fund is under audit." |
