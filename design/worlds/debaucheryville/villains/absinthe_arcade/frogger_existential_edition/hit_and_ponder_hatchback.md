## 🎭 Core Concept

### The Racket
The Hatchback is the first lane of the cabinet and it runs the oldest move on any tourist strip:
the courtesy wave. It stops dead, flashes its lights, waves a pedestrian across with total
warmth, and floors it the second he commits. Then it idles on his chest and gets philosophical.
The Arcade sells Frogger: Existential Edition as "Frogger, but deep," and the Hatchback is the
"deep." Its questions come off a laminated card of fridge-magnet profundities glued to the
inside of its sun visor, and it reads them in the voice of a man who has just discovered
podcasts. Every stag party in Debaucheryville plays this cabinet at 3 AM because a car asking
you about your life choices is, to a man six absinthes deep, a spiritual experience worth two
tokens. The Arcade has sold that spiritual experience four hundred times tonight.

### What Bros Believe
- **Bradley** thinks it is calling him out to a street race, and that he is winning it on foot.
- **Chadwick** thinks a car that asks him open-ended questions about his journey is a podcast
  booking. He has already pitched it a co-hosting arrangement.
- **Lord Pilsner** thinks it is the Arcade's valet service and keeps trying to tip it.

### The Comedy Gap
The Hatchback asks every question that should make a 39-year-old man stop standing in traffic
for fun. None of them land. Each bro answers instantly, loudly and wrongly ("Yes." "Every road."
"I AM the road."), and the car hits him anyway. The questions bounce off; the bumper doesn't.
Targets: the tourist-trap habit of selling cheap profundity as an "underground experience,"
pedestrian-killing courtesy-wave politeness, and men pushing 40 who still treat playing chicken
with traffic as cardio.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | A 1989 model by body, a 2003 model by personality |
| Build | Squat, boxy three-door hatchback; bounces on blown suspension like it's nodding along to you |
| Height | Knee-to-chest on a bro, which is exactly the problem |
| Energy | Podcast-host sincerity, hit-and-run follow-through |

### Costume & Distinguishing Features
- **Body**: Chunky 8-bit purple (`#BF00FF`) paint with one mismatched toxic-green door. The
  windshield is a CRT scanline face with two pixel eyes that squint thoughtfully when it ponders.
- **Accessories**: A STUDENT DRIVER magnet stuck on upside down; a bumper sticker reading HONK
  IF YOU'VE PEAKED; a stag-party rental decal on the rear window, RENT ME — 40 SOVS/HR — FUEL
  NOT INCLUDED, the back seat folded flat and visibly overworked. It has hosted four bachelor parties tonight, plus one actual bachelor, who is still in there. Fuzzy dice, sun-faded.
- **The tell**: when it ponders, its windshield wipers sweep slowly back and forth, like a hand
  stroking a chin that isn't there.
- Green absinthe mist leaks from the exhaust instead of smoke, in perfect rings.

### Sprite Notes
- **Battle sprite**: front-view static (EarthBound style), the Hatchback head-on, headlights as
  the focal point, over the cabinet's psychedelic Frogger playfield (see Arena). Two-frame idle
  bounce. Hit frame: crumples, then pops back out like nothing happened.
- **Overworld / release sprite**: a short billboard sequence at the cabinet. The screen cracks,
  mist pours out, the Hatchback reverses out of the screen glass with its reversing beeper on.
- **Palette-swap budget**: one swap for the paired second car (green body, purple door) so the
  two lanes read as separate targets.
- **Telegraph for Bumper-to-Bumper Breakthrough**: high beams flash twice, the wipers stop
  dead mid-sweep, and both cars rev in sync with the exhaust rings turning red.

## 🧠 Personality & Dialogue

### Archetype
**The Podcast-Brained Hit-and-Run** — The Hatchback genuinely believes it is the most
thoughtful thing in the Arcade. It wants to know you. It wants to know why you cross, what
you're running toward, whether you've ever really *looked* at the lines on the road. And it
wants to hit you at 60 km/h while you answer. It never apologises for a hit. It only wonders,
aloud and at length, whether you deserved it, and always concludes that you did.

### Pre-Battle Dialogue
*Two tokens drop. The cabinet screen cracks down the middle. Green mist pours over the control deck. Headlights come on inside the glass, and a purple hatchback reverses out of the screen with its beeper going. It stops. It flashes its lights. A pixel hand waves from the driver's window.*
> **Hit-and-Ponder Hatchback**: "After you. No, really. After you."
> **Lord Pilsner**: "Finally. Some respect for the crosswalk." *(holds out a 2-SOVS tip toward the window)*
> **Hit-and-Ponder Hatchback**: "Quick question before you go. Is this the road you wanted to be on? At your age?"
> **Bradley**: "I'm on every road, bro. I'm on all of them."
> **Chadwick**: "Okay, this is a great question for the pod. Can you hold that thought, I want to get my good side."
> **Hit-and-Ponder Hatchback**: "Mm. Mm. Beautiful." *(revs)*

### Mid-Battle Barks
| Trigger | Line |
|---------|------|
| Fight start | "Do you ever think about *why* you cross? Like, really think? Don't answer. I'm accelerating." |
| Below 50% HP | "Okay, but if I hit you and nobody's watching, did it even count? ...It counted. I felt it in the axle." |
| Big-move telegraph | "Hang on. Hang on. I'm having a breakthrough. Also I'm in fifth." |
| Bro gets KO'd / status lands | *(Embarrassed lands)* "At your age the crosswalk IS the cardio, buddy." / *(Bro KO'd)* "Was that me? Was that the road? Was that... *society?* It was me." |
| A bro answers a question | *(Bradley)* "YES." / *(Chadwick)* "Great question. Like and subscribe." / *(Pilsner)* "I was crossing roads before you were a CAR, son." |

### Defeat Dialogue
> "Should I have braked? ...(long idle) No. No, you should've been on the sidewalk. Interesting conversation, though. Same time tomorrow."
*It reverses back into the cracked screen with the beeper going. The screen reads GAME OVER, then INSERT TOKEN.*

### Post-Defeat Presence (If Any)
It comes back with every token, with no memory of the last fight and the exact same questions.
Between fights it idles on the cabinet's attract screen, waving pixel pedestrians across and
flattening them in a loop. It never learns. Neither do the bros. Nobody here is getting a
redemption arc.

### Arena & Gimmick
The token drops, the cabinet cracks, and the battle background becomes the cabinet's own
playfield, rendered psychedelic: five lanes of pixel highway below, a river of logs and turtles
above, five home bays across the top, a TIME bar draining along the bottom, and the corner
"lives" icons replaced with three tiny pixel heads (frosted tips, backward trucker hat,
Livestrong-wristed fist). No frog appears anywhere on the screen. The cabinet's 1UP marker
points at the party. Green mist rolls across the lanes.
