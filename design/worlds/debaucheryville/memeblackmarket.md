### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Weaponized nostalgia culture, over-monetization of identity, meme economy absurdity
- **Secondary Targets**: Early internet culture worship, crypto-era meme trading, digital hoarding mentality
- **Cultural Specificity**: Debaucheryville's tech startup underbelly meets nostalgic internet culture
- **Humor Approach**: Deadpan reverence for cursed content, treating memes as sacred artifacts
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets internet culture commodification and nostalgia exploitation, not individual internet users

**What bros THINK is happening**: Accessing exclusive underground meme economy with rare digital artifacts

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Meme Summon System (Original)
- **Shiba Hype (10 Sovs)**: Summons pixelated Shiba barking motivational quotes, +Morale 3 turns, may distract enemies
- **Dat Boi On Unicycle (5 Sovs)**: Appears, says "o shit waddup," leaves, does nothing, pure dopamine hit
- **Dancing Baby.exe (7 Sovs)**: Disables enemy buffs via confusion, 33% chance backfires applying Cringe to player
- **Rage Comic Panel (4 Sovs)**: Reduces enemy attack by 1 for 1 turn, only works on NPCs who "respect classic memes"
- **Trollface Bomb (13 Sovs)**: 60% chance screen-filling LULs deal psychic damage, 20% chance party argues over meme legality

### Meme Trinket System (Original)
- **XP Ring from eBaum's World (50 Sovs)**: Permanent passive "Elite Internet User" - +1 INT, +1 Speed, -1 Charm with NPCs born after 2003
- **Meatspin NFT (Free)**: Doesn't buy, just appears, won't stop spinning, applies "Mentally Unwell" status 3 battles unless purged by glitter priest
- **Funcoland Futures NFT #1999 (Unlock Only)**: Predicts resale value incorrectly, +1 Charisma shady vendors, tooltip: "Do not redeem IRL unless masochist"

### MemeMastery Meter System (Original)
**Fill Meter By:**
- Using 3 Meme Summons in battle
- Reading 2 meme-linked graffiti (Velvet Curtain VIP stall, Kebab Alley wall)
- Consuming CryptoDoner Box during ICQ event

**Full Charge Unlocks:**
- Title: "Shitposter Supreme"
- ICQ Contact: 404_Bratnado.exe (sends cursed chain mail in combat draining enemy MP)
- NFT Unlock: Funcoland Futures #1999
- Enemy dialogue changes: "yo is that trollface?? bruhhh!"
