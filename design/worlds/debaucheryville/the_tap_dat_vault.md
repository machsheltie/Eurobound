# The Tap Dat Vault

*Where nightclub meets GPU breakdown - experimental vapor-brewed lagers in a cyberpunk basement where everything pulses to glitchy techno*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: debaucheryville_clocktower_tapdatvault_brewery_02

- **Location Type**: Cyberpunk Brew Basement Dungeon / Brewery Tour Location #2

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: Midnight only - hidden switch activates at exactly 00:00 game time
- **Stat Requirements**: None for entry (rhythm-based challenge inside)
- **Item Requirements**: Debaucheryville Bottle Opener Keychain (required for stamp/signature brew)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: Must discover hidden switch at Clocktower Plaza
- **Special Conditions**: Large metal vault door opens only at midnight, countdown glyphs indicate timing

### Bottle Opener Requirement
- **Prerequisite**: The Blarney Trap Bottle Opener (Publandia) — required to access ANY brewery tour location
- **Location-Specific Opener**: The Tap Dat Vault Bottle Opener
- **Design**: Vault door shape with soundwave pattern cutout, bottle opener integrated into the door handle
- **Material**: Brushed chrome with cyan and hot pink neon accents (actually glows)
- **Inscription**: "Inhaled & Survived"
- **Acquired**: Complete the rhythm puzzle sequence OR purchase from the Glitching Bouncer for 20 Sovs
- **Special Feature**: Contains tiny LED that pulses in rhythm when squeezed (battery included)
- **Check Point**: Bouncer checks for Blarney Trap opener (quest access) AND scans for this opener (brew section access)
- **Without Blarney Trap Opener**: Cannot participate in brewery tour at all
- **Without Tap Dat Vault Opener**: Can enter vault and explore, but brewing section remains locked

**Bouncer's Opener Check**:

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Back through vault door to Clocktower Plaza
- **Completion Requirements**: Defeat Strobe Brewmistress boss, obtain Vape Stout brew
- **Failure States**: Can be ejected for failed rhythm puzzle attempts (3 strikes)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: Hologram selfie prop access, Brewery Tour quest progression (2 of 4 complete)

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Rhythm-Based Vapor Pressure Puzzle (Original)
- **Core Mechanic**: Press buttons synced to glitchy techno track to maintain beer vapor pressure
- **Timing Challenge**: Match button presses to rhythm indicators scrolling across fermentation chambers
- **Failure Consequences**: 3 failed attempts ejects player from brewery (can return at next midnight)
- **Success Rewards**: Access to brewing area and boss encounter
- **Skill Scaling**: Difficulty adjusts based on player's previous rhythm game performance

### Glitched Brew Ad Bot Mini-Mob (Original)
- **Enemy Type**: Sentient billboard spam with CRT monitor head
- **Dialogue**: "✨UNBOX A LIMITED-EDITION FOAM BOOSTER✨"
- **Combat Mechanic**: Random item removed from inventory if not stunned quickly (3-turn timer)
- **Visual Design**: CRT monitor head with floating ad banner drones orbiting
- **Satirical Beat**: Enemy is literal advertising becoming hostile obstacle

### Strobe Brewmistress Boss Battle (Original)
- **Boss Type**: Sound-wave attack boss using rhythm and audio as weapons
- **Combat Strategy**: Dodge sound-wave attacks timed to music beat, counter during vulnerable phases
- **Status Effects**: Applies Embarrassed status with spotlight attacks that freeze players
- **Sound Wave Patterns**: Bass drops create AOE damage, high-frequency attacks target individuals
- **Victory Reward**: Vape Stout brew, hologram selfie prop access, quest progression, stamp eligibility
