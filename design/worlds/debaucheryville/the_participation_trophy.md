# The Participation Trophy

*The prize redemption booth where everyone's a winner — and by "winner" we mean "person who receives garbage in exchange for money"*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: debaucheryville_circus_participation_trophy_01
- **Display Name**: The Participation Trophy
- **Czech Alias**: "Účastnická Trofej"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "You Tried! Here's Proof!"
- **World Location**: Debaucheryville
- **Area Zone**: The Cirque du Shady (circus grounds)
- **Location Type**: Prize Redemption Booth / Garbage Distribution Center / Dreams-to-Disappointment Converter

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: Whenever the circus is open (varies, check Cirque du Shady hours)
- **Stat Requirements**: None (losers especially welcome)
- **Item Requirements**: Circus Tickets OR Gold (for direct "prize" purchase)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None, but rigged games are nearby
- **Special Conditions**: The booth attendant has seen your "performance" at the games

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Walk away with your "prize"
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (there's always more garbage)
- **Failure States**: Cannot fail (everyone gets a prize! That's the tragedy!)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Frequent Loser" status after redeeming 10 prizes (access to "premium" garbage)

## Visual Design
### Exterior Description
- **Structure**: Weathered carnival booth, peeling paint, questionable structural integrity
- **Design**: Deliberately underwhelming compared to the flashy games
- **Prize Display**: Sad stuffed animals, plastic trinkets, items of mysterious origin
- **Signage**:
  - Main: "THE PARTICIPATION TROPHY" in faded carnival letters
  - Banner: "EVERYONE'S A WINNER!" (technically true, spiritually false)
  - Side: "REDEEM YOUR TICKETS HERE" with arrow pointing to sadness
  - Small sign: "All Prizes Are Final. No Refunds. No Complaints. No Hope."

- **Atmosphere**: The energy of childhood disappointment, concentrated
- **Environmental Context**: Adjacent to rigged games, visible from the failures

### The Prize Tier System
The booth operates on a ticket system, but the math is designed to ensure maximum disappointment:

**Ticket Costs for Prizes**:
| Tier | Ticket Cost | Reality | What You Actually Get |
|------|-------------|---------|----------------------|
| Grand Prize | 10,000 tickets | Impossible | Display only, don't ask |
| Great Prize | 5,000 tickets | Theoretically possible | You'd need 500 Sovs in games |
| Good Prize | 1,000 tickets | Unlikely | Maybe after 4 hours |
| Okay Prize | 500 tickets | Achievable | An hour of suffering |
| Consolation Prize | 100 tickets | Standard outcome | 20 minutes of failure |
| Participation Prize | 10 tickets | Inevitable | One rigged game attempt |

**Average Tickets Per Game**: 5-15 (rigged to give minimum)
**Average Cost Per Game**: 3 Sovs
**Math**: You will ALWAYS spend more than the prize is worth

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Participation Trophy is where the rigged carnival game economy comes to die. You've spent 20 Sovs trying to knock over milk bottles that are filled with concrete. You've thrown rings at bottles that are mathematically too large to fit. You've accumulated tickets that represent pure loss converted to paper. And now you're here, at the redemption booth, where a woman named Yolanda will exchange your suffering for a half-smoked cigar someone left behind.

The satire targets carnival prize economics (spend 50 Sovs to win a 2 Sovs toy), the "everyone's a winner" participation trophy culture, and the particular sadness of receiving a prize you didn't earn and don't want.

### The Yolanda Philosophy
Yolanda has worked this booth for 7 years. She's watched thousands of people exchange hope for garbage. She doesn't make the rules — she just enforces them. She doesn't set the ticket prices — she just watches adults do math and realize they've been scammed. She doesn't choose the prizes — she just hands over the Half-Smoked Cigar with the same dead-eyed professionalism every time.

She's not cruel. She's just... there. Witnessing. Distributing. Waiting for her shift to end. The prizes are what they are. The system is what it is. Your disappointment is not her responsibility. But she sees it. She always sees it.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Carnival prize economics, "everyone wins" hollow victories
- **Secondary Targets**: Sunk cost fallacy, the gap between expectation and reality
- **Cultural Specificity**: Eastern European carnival seediness, tourist trap economies
- **Humor Approach**: Deadpan acceptance of systemic disappointment
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes the system, not the workers or customers

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Converting money and time into worthless objects
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Part of Cirque du Shady's overall scam ecosystem
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think more tickets = better prizes (technically true, practically irrelevant)
- **Comedy of Errors**: The math realization, the prize comparison, the walk of shame
- **Consequence System**: You WILL receive garbage, the question is how much

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Redeeming hard-won prizes from skilled gameplay

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Converting wasted money into physical proof of being scammed

**How much have they spent**: More than any prize is worth (always)

**The prize booth journey**:
1. "I've got 47 tickets! What can I get?"
2. *Yolanda gestures at the 10-ticket shelf*
3. "But I spent 15 Sovs on games..."
4. "The cigar's popular."
5. "...it's half-smoked."
6. "Previous winner didn't want it. Now it's vintage."
7. "I'll... I'll take the corndog."
8. *Yolanda nods, unsurprised*

**The transformation**:
- Arrive with tickets, full of misplaced hope
- See the prize wall
- Do the math
- Experience the stages of grief (speedrun)
- Accept a participation prize
- Leave holding garbage
- Display garbage ironically (cope)
- Return tomorrow (learn nothing)

**How circus staff interact**: They've all worked this booth at some point. They understand.

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Ticket Redemption System

**Earning Tickets**:
- Play rigged games around Cirque du Shady
- Average yield: 5-15 tickets per 3 Sovs game
- Maximum possible (perfect play): Still not enough for good prizes
- Tickets can also be purchased directly (1 Sov = 20 tickets, still bad math)

**Direct Purchase Option**:
- Some prizes available for straight gold (no tickets needed)
- Prices are... not good
- But at least you skip the games

## NPCs & Characters
### The Attendant

**Yolanda Kovarik**
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_yolanda_kovarik_01`
- **Role**: Prize booth attendant, garbage distributor, witness to disappointment
- **Appearance**: 40s Czech woman, carnival uniform (faded), expression of professional neutrality hiding existential weariness
- **Background**: 7 years at this booth, seen everything, surprised by nothing
- **Demeanor**: Dead-eyed customer service, occasional dry wit, fundamentally decent beneath the exhaustion
- **The Yolanda Method**: Count tickets, do math, hand over garbage, repeat
- **Philosophy**: "I don't make the prizes. I don't set the prices. I just work here. Your disappointment is valid but not my responsibility."

**Yolanda's Dialogue Categories**:

*The Greeting:*
