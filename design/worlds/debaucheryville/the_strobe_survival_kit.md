## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Strobe Survival Kit treats clubbing as what it actually is — a sensory assault that requires preparation. While other vendors sell the fantasy, this stall sells the reality: earplugs because the music WILL damage your hearing, glow sticks because you WILL want to be seen, energy drinks because you WILL crash at 3 AM. The military aesthetic is played straight because honestly? Going into a 110dB strobe-lit environment for six hours IS a form of combat.

The satire is that this practical, protective approach is the exception rather than the rule. Everyone SHOULD buy earplugs. Almost no one does until they've experienced tinnitus.

### The Antonín Philosophy
The vendor, Antonín, is a former sound engineer who saw too many young people destroy their hearing. He opened this stall as genuine harm reduction disguised as commerce. He WANTS you to buy earplugs. He'll give you the guilt trip. He's seen the consequences.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: The absurdity that hearing protection isn't standard club equipment
- **Secondary Targets**: Club culture's disregard for long-term health, the "I'll be fine" attitude
- **Cultural Specificity**: Prague's intense club scene, Eastern European rave culture
- **Humor Approach**: Military seriousness applied to club preparation
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Genuine harm reduction messaging wrapped in comedy

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Marking up emergency supplies because desperation pays
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Positioned for maximum "oh no I need this" traffic
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think earplugs are "uncool" until their ears ring for three days
- **Comedy of Errors**: The 2 AM realization that you should have bought the earplug-glow stick-energy drink combo
- **Consequence System**: Equipment affects club experience, status effects, next-day recovery

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Optional accessories for the night out

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Essential protective equipment for sensory warfare

**How impaired are they**: Usually sober on arrival (it's pre-club), confident, dismissive

**Obvious signs they dismiss**:
- The decibel chart is RIGHT THERE
- Antonín's warning is sincere
- Their phones already show hearing damage warnings
- They've had ringing ears before
- Other countries require hearing protection in clubs

**The transformation**: 
- Night 1: "Earplugs are for old people"
- Night 2: "My ears are still ringing from last night"
- Night 3: "Give me the musician-quality ones"

**How locals interact**: Actually buy the good earplugs, have learned this lesson

## NPCs & Vendors
### The Operator

**Antonín "Tonda" Dvořák**
- **Role**: Stall operator, former sound engineer, harm reduction advocate in vendor's clothing
- **Appearance**: 50s Czech man, practical clothing, hearing aids (visible, intentional), military surplus vest
- **Background**: 20 years as club sound engineer, tinnitus at 45, now on a mission to prevent others' hearing loss
- **Demeanor**: Gruff, practical, genuinely caring beneath the tactical exterior
- **The Antonín Method**: Guilt-trip people into protecting themselves, frame it as tactical wisdom
- **Visible Hearing Aids**: "You see these? Twenty years in clubs. No earplugs. Don't be me."

**Antonín's Dialogue Categories**:

*The Tactical Assessment:*
- "Where are you going tonight? Velvet Curtain? 105 decibels average. You need at least silicone grade."
- "Let me look at you. No earplugs. No eye protection. You're going in naked. Tactically naked."
- "The club is a battlefield. Would you go to war without armor? This is your armor."
- "I see your group. None of you have protection. Statistically, two of you will have ringing ears by morning."

*The Sales Pitch (Also Genuine Concern):*
- "Earplugs. 8 Sovs. Your hearing? Priceless. Irreplaceable. Literally cannot be fixed once damaged."
- "Glow sticks are fun. Earplugs are survival. Buy both. Start with the earplugs."
- "The strobe shades look stupid. You know what looks stupider? Squinting for four hours."
- "Energy shots work. The crash is real. The electrolytes help. Buy the combo."

*The Warning:*
- "You see this?" *points to hearing aids* "Twenty years. Sound engineer. Best clubs in Debaucheryville. No earplugs. Now I wear these."
- "Tinnitus is forever. FOREVER. That ringing you have tomorrow? What if it never stopped?"
- "The DJ is wearing earplugs. The bartenders are wearing earplugs. Why aren't you?"
- "I'm not trying to scare you. I'm trying to help you. The fear is just the truth."

*The Respect (Post-Purchase):*

- "You bought the kit. You're smarter than 90% of the tourists I see. Survive well."

*The Disappointment (If Refused):*

- "Cool? You think earplugs aren't cool? You know what's not cool? Hearing aids at 45."
- "Fine. Go. But remember this conversation when you're asking people to repeat themselves."
