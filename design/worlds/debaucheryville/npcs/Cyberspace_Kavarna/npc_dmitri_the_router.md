# NPC Profile: Dmitri "The Router"

*"Connection is life. Disconnection is death. Payment is now."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_dmitri_the_router_01`
- **Display Name**: Dmitri "The Router"
- **Internal Name**: Dmitri, manager of the Cyberspace Kavarna since 1999
- **Role**: Manager / Service Vendor (terminal rental, "upgrades") / Atmosphere Anchor / Reality-Check Voice
- **Associated Quest**: None directly. Gates the ICQ terminal access that all Darkweb Dossier sting operations run through
- **Primary Location**: Cyberspace Kavarna, Debaucheryville — basement level beneath Hostel Row
- **Secondary Appearances**: None. He has been behind the same counter for over two decades

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Renting time on Windows 98 terminals in a basement, cash only, at 3 Sovs an hour, to whoever comes down the staircase. He has run this room since 1999. He knows the connections are monitored, he knows the printed ICQ contact lists taped to the monitors are scammer directories, and he knows exactly which of his patrons are on the other end of them. He rents the terminals anyway, because the room is legal, the rent is due, and what people type is not his business.

### What Bros Believe
That they have found the door into an authentic Eastern European digital underground — a real hacker den, the kind of place from the movies, where the ancient hardware is a sign of authenticity rather than of a business that has never had a good year. They believe Dmitri is the gatekeeper of that underground, and that his gruffness is a test.

### The Comedy Gap
Dmitri is not a gatekeeper. He is a man who rents computers. Every mystical-sounding thing he says is a completely literal statement about his business — "connection is life" is a description of what he sells, "payment is now" is a payment term — and the bros receive it as cryptic wisdom from a digital shaman. He answers their questions honestly and specifically, including the part where the WiFi password is `password123`, and they write it down like a clue.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Literal Oracle** — Dmitri says exactly what he means, in short declarative sentences, in his second language, and the compression makes it sound profound. He is not being enigmatic; he is being brief, because elaboration costs breath and he is smoking. He has watched this basement survive Y2K, the death of dial-up, and three economic crashes, and he has drawn one conclusion from all of it: the room stays open, and payment is now.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Accidentally profound | Speaks in short absolute statements about his business; listeners hear philosophy |
| Cash-absolutist | Will not take a card. Has a reason. The reason is not a good reason and he does not care |
| Unshockable | Has seen every category of thing a person can do on a rented computer at 4 AM |
| Institutionally patient | Twenty-six years in one basement. There is nothing left to be in a hurry about |
| Non-interventionist | Knows who the scammers are, knows what the lists on the monitors are, rents to everyone equally |

### Internal Monologue
> "American thinks he is in movie. Fine. Movie costs three Sovs per hour, fifteen for all night. He will pay for all night and he will use four hours and fall asleep on keyboard like the others. Terminal seven is broken. Terminal seven has been broken since the flood. Terminal seven still rents. This is business."

### Motivation
The room. He opened it into a country that was still figuring out what it was, and it has outlasted the technology it was built around, the industry it belonged to, and most of the businesses on Hostel Row. Closing it has never come up. He is not nostalgic and he is not trapped — he simply runs the cafe, and the cafe is what there is.

**First entry, any hour**:

**Entry during peak tourist hours (14:00-16:00)**:

*If the player asks about the WiFi:*

> "Computer broken? Is feature, not bug."

*If the player asks how long the place has been here:*

*If the player asks about the printed ICQ lists taped to the monitors:*

### Sales Close (Terminal Rental)
*He takes the cash, does not count it in front of you, and gestures at the room with two nicotine-stained fingers:*
> "Connection is life. Disconnection is death. Payment is now. Go. Any machine except seven."

### Post-Interaction
> "You are on clock. Clock does not care about your emails."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path

*Player offers a card:*
> "No. Cash only. Credit cards are American conspiracy. ...I have been asked to explain this. I do not explain this."

*Player tries to haggle:*
> "Three Sovs is not a negotiation, it is a number. There is machine down the street. It is also three Sovs. Go, come back, still three Sovs."

*Player asks for a refund after a blue screen:*
> "You lost work. Yes. Everyone loses work. This is why you save. Nobody saves. I do not refund the not-saving."

*Player asks him to fix a machine:*
> *(Restart Everything Gesture)* "Restart. Everything. Always."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)

*Second visit:*
> "Again. Good. Same price, before you ask."

*After the player has completed one ICQ sting operation:*
> "Back door works now. Metro is through there. I did not unlock it. Somebody unlocked it. Password changes at midnight and it is written in bathroom, which is a security system I did not design."

*After extended use (Eye Strain / Digital Paranoia active):*
> "Your eyes. Yes. Two hours is limit for human. Nobody respects limit for human. Terminal is still three Sovs."

*Late game, if the player has bought the all-night special more than three times:*
> "You are here more than some of my regulars, and my regulars have ICQ numbers older than you. This is not compliment. Is observation. ...Fifteen Sovs."

*3-6 AM, with Late Night Exhaustion pose:*
> "At this hour, everybody in this room is doing something they will not describe tomorrow. Including me. I am sitting here. I will not describe it."

## 🎒 Inventory / Services

### Services Offered
| Item/Service | Price | Actual Value | Effect/Result |
|--------------|-------|--------------|---------------|
| Terminal rental (standard) | 3 Sovs/hour | A Windows 98 machine and a monitored connection | ICQ hotspot access; +200% scammer message rate while connected; Darkweb Dossier interactions available |
| Terminal rental — "all-night special" | 15 Sovs | Eight-plus hours nobody uses fully | Same as above with enhanced scammer targeting; the cost-efficient option for sting operations |
| Dead-hours rate (03:00-06:00) | 2 Sovs/hour | The best deal and the worst room | Cheapest access, highest scammer activity, enhanced sting-operation opportunities |
| Peak-hours rate (14:00-16:00) | 6 Sovs/hour | Identical service | Prices double; bros get targeted by coordinated scammer teams immediately |
| ICQ Premium "upgrade" | 5 Sovs | Nothing | Reduces spam by 10%. Placebo. Dmitri does not claim otherwise and does not volunteer it either |
| VPN Service | 10 Sovs | Nothing useful | "Anonymous browsing" routed through three other equally sketchy cafes |
| Mining pool buy-in | 20 Sovs | 0.003 Sovs per hour | The corner rigs are ancient. Dmitri did not install them and does not run them; he rents the corner they hum in |

Cash only. There is no card reader in the building and there never has been.

### Hidden Details / Easter Eggs
- **Terminal #7**: Always displays the same error message and is the one machine Dmitri tells you not to use. It contains hidden crypto wallet access. He knows. He has never once mentioned it and has told hundreds of people to use a different machine.
- **Examining the calculators**: There are five. They are all different models. Asking about them gets: *"For rates."* Asking what rates gets nothing.
- **Examining the ICQ lists taped to the monitors**: Handwritten additions in at least four different pens. One entry has been crossed out so hard it went through the paper.
- **Asking Dmitri his ICQ number**: He gives a six-digit number without hesitation and without further comment. Six digits is extremely early. Nobody in the room has fewer.
- **The bathroom graffiti**: Metro access passwords are posted here nightly among unrelated FlagMan dating wisdom. Dmitri did not write any of it and has never cleaned it off.

### Territory / Social Dynamics
- The Kavarna is a neutral room by long practice — scammers, marks, and the occasional obvious surveillance operative all rent at the same rate and nobody is thrown out
- Dmitri's non-intervention is the local law: he will not warn a tourist and he will not protect a scammer. He rents computers
- The metro back exit exists because someone unlocked it and he did not re-lock it; this is the extent of his involvement in the city's underground logistics
- Staff treat tourist money-losing as inevitable while providing the authentic period atmosphere that makes marks feel underground — Dmitri is the atmosphere

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Czech, thick, worn by chain smoking into a rasp. English is his second language and he uses it efficiently rather than incorrectly — dropped articles, present tense, no wasted words. **Never played for laughs.** The comedy is in what he says, never in how he pronounces it.

### Tone
- **Standard service**: Flat, transactional, terminally patient
- **Signature declarations**: Identical flatness. He is stating business facts. The profundity is entirely in the listener's ear
- **Technical support**: Diagnostic, unbothered, final
- **Late night (03:00-06:00)**: Slower, longer sentences, fractionally more honest. The only register shift he has

### Key Delivery Notes
- Exhale *before* the line, not after — the smoke leads
- "Payment is now" is a payment term, not a threat. Do not menace it
- "Cockroach cafe" is said with a trace of genuine pride, the only pride he displays
- Never react to the bros' movie references. The non-reaction is the joke
- Pauses between his short sentences should be long enough to feel like the conversation might have ended
