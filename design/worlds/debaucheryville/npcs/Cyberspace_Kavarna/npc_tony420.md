## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
He runs a competent, round-the-clock scam-marketing operation over ICQ. He sells absolutely anything: pills, vapes, suppositories, kidney coupons, opera glasses, dignity. He takes payment **in crypto only**, across every shitcoin in the game, and each coin trades at its own SOVS rate (author, 2026-11-11). "Tony420 and company know exactly what a $SPANK token is worth… They are not confused; they are selling" (`design/economy/icq_airdrops.md:15`). He airdrops worthless coins as free samples that obligate you (`icq_airdrops.md:80`). Then he invoices you in the same coins.

**Nobody ever sees him.** When business requires him in the room, an unnamed goon walks in and holds up a tablet. On it plays a video of Tony with his face heavily blurred and his voice run through a changer, like a crime-show victim being protected. Tony does the whole deal from the tablet (author, 2026-11-11).

### What Bros Believe
That they have a guy. When an unsolicited pop-up hijacks Lord Pilsner's phone, the bros take it as a personal supplier relationship. An airdrop means institutional interest: *"Chadwick, we just got airdropped 100k SpankTokens! We're institutional investors now, bro!"* (`icq_airdrops.md`, AD-01 reaction, author-locked). A free sample means credit: **PILSNER:** *"He fronted us, Chadwick. We're in business."* (`final.md:984`). When a hired thug brings in a tablet showing a blurred man with a demon voice, they read it as a celebrity making a personal appearance.

### The Comedy Gap
The bros think they have a dealer. In fact a broadcast list has them filed under "wallet". He sends the same pitch to every hotspot in four cities. He has gone to crime-documentary lengths to make sure no customer could pick him out of a lineup. And it never occurs to the bros to wonder why their supplier hides his identity like a protected witness. They think the blur is a VIP thing.

He accepts the full range of in-game shitcoins listed in `design/economy/shitcoin_master_pool.md` (§2 Coin Index), and each coin carries its own SOVS exchange rate. **The rate numbers are pending the author's economy pass and are not set here.** MoonCum696969 ($MCUM, #29) can be spent with Tony420 and nobody else (`shitcoin_master_pool.md` #29 spendability note; `icq_airdrops.md` §1.4). **Rug-pulled coins can't be spent** (author, 2026-11-11): once a coin has rugged, Tony won't take it either. **Airdropped coins (author, 2026-11-11):** Tony420 accepts any airdropped coin as payment, including his own airdrops ($SPANK etc.); everywhere else airdropped coins are unspendable (`icq_airdrops.md` §1.4).
