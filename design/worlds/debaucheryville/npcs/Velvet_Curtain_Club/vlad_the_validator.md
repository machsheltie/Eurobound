### Core Personality
- **Archetype:** Crypto Bro Gatekeeper who treats door policy like blockchain verification
- **Satirical Target:** Cryptocurrency obsession + nightclub bouncer power trips
- **Humor Style:** Deadpan contradictions with crypto jargon
- **Primary Motivation:** Protecting the "decentralized" VIP list while being completely centralized
- **Fatal Flaw:** Will abandon his post to explain cryptocurrency to literally anyone

### Character Backstory

#### Personal History
- **Origin Story:** Former IT support guy who bought Bitcoin at 60,000 SOVS and now works doors to pay rent
- **Career Path:** Computer Science → Corporate IT → Crypto "Influencer" → Club Security
- **Personal Relationships:** Owes money to half the other bouncers from failed "investment opportunities"
- **Hidden Depths:** Actually quite knowledgeable about cybersecurity, just terrible at financial decisions

#### Current Situation
- **Daily Routine:** Checks crypto prices, guards door, evangelizes blockchain to anyone who'll listen
- **Current Goals:** Recoup crypto losses while maintaining "diamond hands" image
- **Personal Struggles:** Rent due in fiat currency while portfolio is 95% down
- **Secret Shame:** Still using his mom's Netflix password despite preaching "decentralization"
