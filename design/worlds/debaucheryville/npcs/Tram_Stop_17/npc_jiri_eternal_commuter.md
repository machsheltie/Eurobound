# NPC Profile: Jiří the Eternal Commuter

*"Is the tram late, or am I early to the rest of my life?"*

- **Title**: The Eternal Commuter
- **Role**: Quest Giver / Information Source / Local Landmark / Emotional Core
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_eternal_commuter_01`
- **Primary Location**: Tram Stop #17, Debaucheryville
- **Secondary Appearances**: Final boss background, Post-credits scene

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Waiting for Tram #17. He has been waiting since 2003. The tram was discontinued in 1999. He doesn't know this. He will never stop waiting.

### What Bros Believe
A friendly local transit expert with encyclopedic knowledge of the city's layout. Clearly passionate about public transportation. Great source for directions.

### The Comedy Gap
The bros repeatedly use Jiří as a human GPS, completely missing that he's been trapped in place by grief and stubbornness for over two decades. They admire his "commitment to local transit" while he slowly merges with the bench. His 47,000-entry documentation of passing trams isn't expertise—it's obsession.

### The Hidden Tragedy
In 2003, Jiří's wife asked him to get milk. He had a ring in his pocket. He was going to propose when he returned. The #17 never came. He waited. And waited. She waited too. Then she stopped waiting. He never made it home.

The deepest cut: The #17 was discontinued in 1999. Four years before he started waiting. The metro worker knows. The locals know. No one tells him.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Absurdist Hero** - Jiří embodies the Czech tradition of dark, existential humor. He's pitiable AND admirable. His commitment is ridiculous yet strangely dignified. He's a monument to stubbornness, grief, and the human capacity for self-deception.

### Mood Phases
Jiří cycles through five emotional phases. Each visit may find him in a different mood:

| Phase | Energy | Tone | Trigger |
|-------|--------|------|---------|
| Optimistic | Hopeful | "Today feels like a tram day" | Random / Morning |
| Philosophical | Contemplative | "Waiting is traveling through time" | Afternoon |
| Bitter | Sharp | "The 22 just passed. The 17 MOCKS me." | After tram passes |
| Conspiracy | Paranoid | "The transit authority KNOWS" | Evening |
| Existential | Hollow | "What if I AM the tram?" | Night / Late visits |

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Infinitely patient | Has waited 20+ years without moving |
| Encyclopedic knowledge | Knows every street, stop, and shortcut |
| Committed beyond reason | Will not accept alternatives |
| Philosophical | Has developed complex theories about waiting |
| Surprisingly helpful | Genuinely wants to help with directions |
| Tragically unaware | Doesn't know the #17 was discontinued |

### Internal Monologue
> "47,000 trams. None of them the 17. But the 47,001st... that could be the one. The schedule says it should come. The schedule is from 1994, but schedules don't lie. Schedules are sacred. The 17 will come. It has to come. If it doesn't come, then what was all this for?"

### Motivation
Originally: Get milk, propose to wife.
Now: Prove he can finish something. If he leaves, all the waiting was for nothing. The 17 isn't about the tram anymore—it's about not giving up.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Greeting (First Meeting)
> "The 17 should be here any moment. The schedule says... ah, the schedule is from 1994. But still."

---

### Mood Phase: Optimistic

> "I have a good feeling about today. Today feels like a tram day."

> "My wife asked me to pick up milk. That was in 2003. The milk will be VERY fresh when I return."

> "Have you ever noticed how the tracks hum slightly before a tram arrives? I have noticed. I notice every hum. Most are false alarms."

> "A man told me in 2007 that the 17 was discontinued. I told him that was impossible. I'm still right. He's dead now. Unrelated, I assume."

> "The weather is perfect for tram arrival. Not too hot, not too cold. The 17 prefers mild conditions."

---

### Mood Phase: Philosophical

> "Is the tram late, or am I early to the rest of my life?"

> "Waiting is just traveling through time at normal speed."

> "Perhaps the tram was inside me all along. ...No. No, I need the actual tram."

> "I have become one with the schedule. The schedule is a lie, but it is MY lie."

> "The Buddha said desire is the root of suffering. He never waited for the 17."

> "I have read every advertisement on this shelter four thousand times. The phone number for the lawyer has been disconnected since 2011. I called to check."

---

### Mood Phase: Bitter

> "The 22 just passed. The 9 just passed. Even the GHOST TRAM passed. But the 17? The 17 MOCKS me."

> "My son graduated university, got married, had children. I missed it. But when that tram comes? Oh, it will be WORTH it."

> "They built a metro stop two blocks away. I will NOT abandon my post."

> "Someone offered me a ride in a car. A CAR. As if I would betray the 17 with a CAR."

> "I have seen empires rise and fall from this bench. Still no tram."

> "The pigeons have accepted me as their leader. The pigeons understand commitment."

---

### Mood Phase: Conspiracy

> "The 17 exists. I have SEEN it. Once. In 1997. It was beautiful."

> "Some say the 17 only appears to the pure of heart. My heart is pure. MY HEART IS PURE."

> "The transit authority knows. They KNOW. This is a test. I will pass this test."

> "Other trams slow down when they pass. Out of respect? Or mockery? I cannot tell."

> "I have documented every tram that has passed this stop. 47,000 entries. None of them were the 17."

> "A tourist once took a photo of me for 'local color.' I hope she captured my dignity."

---

### Mood Phase: Existential

> "What if I AM the tram? What if I'm waiting for myself?"

> "A tram passed yesterday going the other direction. The passengers looked so FREE."

> "I forgot where I was even going. But I will GO there. Whenever the 17 decides."

> "My bench has been replaced twice. I was here for both installations."

> "The city council discussed naming this stop after me. I declined. I am not ready to be commemorated. The 17 has not arrived."

> "Sometimes I wave at the other trams. To let them know I am not angry at them. It is not their fault they are not the 17."

> "Do you know what 'sunk cost fallacy' means? I do. It does not apply here."

> "In my mind, I have ridden the 17 to my destination one million times. In reality, I have not moved."

**Walking suggestion:**
> "Walk? And miss the 17? After all this time? You mock me."

**Math/logic suggestion:**
> "Your math means nothing here. The 17 operates outside mathematics. The 17 operates outside TIME."

**Uber suggestion:**
> "I do not know what that word means. Is it a type of tram?"

**Metro suggestion:**
> "The metro is for those who have given up. I have not given up."
