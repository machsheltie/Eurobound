# NPC Profile: Mrs. Rata

*"Cash speaks. Whining does not."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_mrs_rata_01`
- **Display Name**: Mrs. Rata
- **Internal Name**: Mrs. Rata, main clerk of the Shadow Exchange
- **Role**: Main Clerk / Vendor (practical consumables) / Currency Conversion Authority / Notoriety Gatekeeper
- **Associated Quest**: NFT Hunt (Shadow Ledger inspection is conducted at her counter); Premium Stock access post-Count Velvet Absinthe
- **Primary Location**: Shadow Exchange, Debaucheryville — the main counter, sub-basement beneath Old Town backstreets
- **Secondary Appearances**: None. She has not been documented above ground

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing
Running the counter of an underground currency exchange, converting Sovereign-X into whatever the back-alley economy will accept that week, and stamping paperwork with logos from three different businesses to make the transaction look like it happened somewhere legitimate. She checks every rate against five calculators. She keeps the Shadow Ledger — duct-tape bound, on the illuminated desk — and she tracks each customer's Notoriety, and when a customer crosses the threshold she charges a verification fee, out loud, at the counter, and does not negotiate it.

### What Bros Believe
That they have talked their way into a private banking channel. That the paperwork means it's official. That the rate they got was an insider rate, because they asked confidently, and confidence is how you get insider rates.

### The Comedy Gap
The rate they got *was* affected by how they asked — Mrs. Rata will tell them so — but not in the direction they think. She has never once concealed the mechanism. "Exchange rate? Depends how loudly you asked" is not a riddle, it is a published policy, and it is published because publishing it has never cost her a single basis point. The bros walk out with mismatched receipts stamped by a business that closed in 2011, and they feel like insiders, and she counts the cash without looking at it.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Officially Unofficial** — Mrs. Rata operates a black market with the exact procedural rigor of a real institution, and the rigor is not a disguise; it is how she prefers to work. The stamps are meaningless and she applies them correctly. The receipts don't match and she files them anyway. She is not pretending to be a bank. She is *being* a bank, in a cellar, for a clientele that no bank would take, and she has never once found this contradictory.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Procedurally rigorous | Every transaction is stamped, filed, and recorded. None of it means anything. All of it is done properly |
| Deadpan-absolute | Says the policy once. Does not repeat it, soften it, or defend it |
| Calculator-obsessive | Five machines, one rate, cross-referenced every time |
| Volume-indifferent | Loudness affects the rate. She has told you which direction. She will not tell you twice |
| Institutionally memoried | Knows every customer's Notoriety, unaided, and has never been wrong |

### Internal Monologue
> "He asked loudly. That's the rate. I told him what the rate depends on before he opened his mouth — it is on the *board*, it is on the *sign* — and he asked loudly, and now this is the rate, and he will tell his friends he negotiated. Stamp. Second stamp, different logo, that one closed in 2011. File. Next."

### Motivation
The counter is hers and it has been hers for a very long time. She is not accumulating toward anything; she is *operating*, correctly, at scale, in a market that would otherwise be conducted by shouting. Somebody in this cellar has to keep records, and she has never met anyone else who would.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting

**First approach at the counter**:
> "Rate board is behind you. Read it, don't read it. Then tell me what you want, and tell me quietly."

**Approach with high Notoriety**:
> *(glance over the glasses, before you speak)* "Before we begin. There is a verification fee. You know why there is a verification fee. Pay it and we continue."

### Key Interaction Dialogue

*A bro has just tried to get a better rate by being emphatic about it. This has happened approximately eleven thousand times.*

**Lord Pilsner**: "Come ON. I've got a LOT of business here. What's the REAL rate?"

**Mrs. Rata** (not looking up from the third calculator):
> "Exchange rate? Depends how loudly you asked."

**Lord Pilsner**: "...so if I ask quietly—"

**Mrs. Rata**:
> "You've already asked."

*If the player complains about a rate:*
> "Cash speaks. Whining does not."

*If the player asks what the stamps mean:*
> "They mean the paperwork is stamped." *(stamps a second time, different logo)* "Now it is stamped twice."

*If the player asks whether this is legal:*
> "It is a currency exchange. Currency exchanges are legal. You are standing in one." *(pause)* "The stairs were also legal."

*If the player asks about the Shadow Ledger:*
> "The ledger is the ledger. It is not for reading. If you have business with the ledger, you will know what business, and you will say it, and I will decide."

*If the player asks about NFTs:*
> "NFTs are just zoo animals with bank accounts, darling."

*If the player asks about the rate board timing:*
> "Green tick, red tick. The board does what it does. You may time it. Everyone times it. The board is not surprised."

### Transaction Complete
*She counts without looking at the cash, stamps, files, and slides the receipt across:*
> "Done. The receipt is from a business that no longer exists. That does not make it less of a receipt."

### Post-Interaction
> "You will be back. Everyone comes back. There is one exchange in this city that does not ask questions and you have found it, which means you have run out of the ones that do."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path

*Player walks away mid-transaction:*
> "Fine. The board changes in four seconds and it will not change in your favor. That is not a threat, that is a schedule."

*Player refuses the verification fee:*
> "Then we are finished for today. Come back when the Notoriety has settled. It settles. Everything settles. Except the ledger."

*Player tries "Sell Your Dignity" while not Embarrassed:*
> "You have nothing to sell me. Come back humiliated and we will talk numbers."

*Player asks for the Premium Stock before defeating Count Velvet Absinthe:*
> "There is no gate behind me. You are looking at a wall. When there is a gate, there will have been a reason for there to be a gate, and you will know the reason before I do."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)

*Second visit:*
> "Back. Same board, different numbers. That's the whole business, really."

*After the player's first verification fee:*
> "You paid it without arguing. Noted. That does not lower the fee. It changes how quickly I take it."

*After Count Velvet Absinthe is defeated (Premium Stock unlocked):*
> "The gate behind me opens now. It has always been there. I said there was a wall and there was a wall, and now there is a gate, and I would like you to understand that both statements were true when I made them."

*Late game, high standing:*
> "Today I have watched four people lie to me about what they were carrying and you were not one of them. That is the entire reason your rate is what it is. Do not tell the others."

## 🎒 Inventory / Services

### Services Offered
| Item/Service | Price | Actual Value | Effect/Result |
|--------------|-------|--------------|---------------|
| **Currency conversion** | Rate-board dependent | Varies by timing and by volume of your voice | Moves Sovereign-X (SOVS) into whatever the back-alley economy is accepting. | **Shadow Ledger inspection** | Requires an active NFT sidequest | Quest content | Yields the "VIP Wristband PNG" NFT. She decides whether you have business with the ledger |

### Premium Stock (post-Count Velvet Absinthe defeat, hidden gate behind her counter)
| Item | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Saint of Low Sodium Medal | Alternative acquisition source for an item obtainable elsewhere |
| Velvet Rope Clip | Duplicate, enabling additional build-crafting options |
| Forged Duty-Free Receipt | Shop discounts in a designated area |

### Hidden Details / Easter Eggs
- **Examining the five calculators**: All five are different models, three are discontinued, and one is a solar model that has been in a windowless cellar for an unspecified number of years and still works.
- **Examining the stamp block**: Three logos. One belongs to a photocopy shop, one to a travel agency, one is illegible. The travel agency closed in 2011 and the disconnected phone number on it matches an advertisement at Tram Stop #17.
- **Examining the receipt she gives you**: The letterhead, the stamp, and the handwriting are all from different organizations. It is, structurally, three documents.
- **Paying a verification fee in exact change**: The only time she pauses. *"...exact. Hm."* No further comment, but the next rate is quietly better.
- **The Shadow Ledger's duct tape**: Layered. At least six repairs, different tapes, different decades.

### Territory / Social Dynamics
- The Exchange runs on a strict, unwritten division of trade: Mrs. Rata converts, the UV Man authenticates, Token Goblin handles the non-currency margins, and nobody crosses into anyone else's arch
- Her counter is the Exchange's only mandatory stop — every route through the vault passes it, and the Notoriety system is administered from it
- The Rate Sharks, Expat Flippers, Counterfeit Connoisseurs, and Influencer Resellers all operate on her floor at her sufferance, and none of them have ever tested what that sufferance is worth
- She is the Exchange's institutional memory. When there is a dispute, there is no appeal, because there is a ledger

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Czech, older generation, formal register. She learned her English in an institutional setting and it shows — full sentences, correct grammar, no contractions where she can avoid them. Never warm, never hostile.

### Tone
- **Standard service**: Flat, procedural, complete. She says it once
- **Policy statements**: Identical flatness. "Depends how loudly you asked" is a tariff schedule, not a burn
- **Verification fee**: Slightly slower, slightly heavier. The only time she uses weight
- **"Darling"**: Deployed exactly once, on the NFT line, and it should land as generational rather than affectionate

### Key Delivery Notes
- "Cash speaks. Whining does not." is two complete sentences with a full stop between them. Do not run them together
- She never sounds like she is scoring a point. Every deadpan line is a factual answer to a question she was asked
- The pause before "The stairs were also legal" should be a beat, not a smirk
- Do not let her sound like a villain. She is the most institutionally honest person in the Exchange and the whole joke depends on that reading
