*"Is done. Very beautiful."*

- **Role**: Service Provider (Questionable Quality) / Recurring NPC
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_astronomical_cockup_01`
- **Primary Location**: Euro Ink 4 Less, Debaucheryville (Side Alley off Clock Square)

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Running a chain of cheap tattoo shops across EuroMemeia, producing questionable-quality work at discount prices. He learned tattooing from a 3-week online course and genuinely believes he's an artist. Every skeleton he draws comes out looking like a duck.

### What Bros Believe
A skilled European tattoo artist is creating an authentic piece of body art commemorating their clock experience. Lord Pilsner specifically believes he's getting a death skeleton from the ancient clock.

### The Comedy Gap
Jakub can't draw skeletons. He's never been able to draw skeletons. Every attempt comes out round, with what looks like a bill and wings. He thinks this is "artistic interpretation." Lord Pilsner gets a tramp stamp of a duck holding a scythe and believes it's a majestic European death figure.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Confident Incompetent** - Jakub genuinely believes he's a great artist. When customers point out that their skeleton looks like a duck, he explains it's "artistic interpretation" or "European style." He's not malicious; he's just bad at his job and doesn't know it.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Confident | Absolute belief in his artistic abilities |
| Delusional | Genuinely doesn't see the duck |
| Defensive | "European style" explanation for any criticism |
| Entrepreneurial | Has expanded to multiple cities |
| Proud | Considers Lord Pilsner his signature work |

### Internal Monologue
> "This is definitely a skeleton. Skeletons have... bills? Sure. Artistic license. The wings are very skeletal. European skeletons are rounder. This is authentic. He will love it. They always love it eventually."

### Motivation
Started with genuine passion for tattoo art. Never got good but got fast and cheap. Discovered tourists don't come back to complain, so quality doesn't matter. Now runs a small empire of Euro Ink 4 Less shops across EuroMemeia, all producing the same duck-quality work.
