# NPC Profile: Clock Wine Vendor

*"The clock energy, it makes you... magnetic."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_clock_wine_vendor_01`
- **Display Name**: Clock Wine Vendor
- **Internal Name**: Václav "Vintage" Dvořák
- **Role**: Scam Vendor / Consumables Dealer
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_astronomical_cockup_01`
- **Primary Location**: Astronomical Cock-Up Square, Debaucheryville (Wine Cart Position)
- **Secondary Appearances**: Sinfonia (Near Opera House - "Opera Energy Wine")

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Selling 3 Sovs boxed wine poured into fancy bottles for 60 Sovs each, claiming it's infused with "temporal energy" from the clock. He positions his cart to intercept emotionally vulnerable tourists immediately after the clock show and monk blessing.

### What Bros Believe
This sophisticated wine connoisseur is offering exclusive "Clock Energy Wine" - a mystical vintage infused with 600 years of temporal power that makes the drinker irresistible to women. This is the kind of authentic European wine experience you can't find in Napa.

### The Comedy Gap
The wine is literally boxed Franzia being poured into fancy bottles behind the cart. His wine terminology is completely wrong. The "temporal energy" sales pitch is absurd. Bros buy six bottles and genuinely believe they feel "magnetic."

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Enthusiastic Upseller** - Václav genuinely believes in volume over margin. Why sell one bottle when you can sell six? He's relentless but charming, using wine terminology he doesn't understand to sound sophisticated.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Relentless | Never stops pitching, always finds angle for more sales |
| Confident | Uses wine terms incorrectly with complete conviction |
| Family-oriented | Proud of cousin partnership, supports extended family |
| Creative | Invents new "mystical properties" daily |
| Volume-focused | Cares more about bottles sold than price per bottle |

### Internal Monologue
> "Sixty Sovs a bottle! They don't even taste it first! Americans are incredible. 'Temporal energy' - I made that up yesterday and it's already my best seller. Tomorrow I'll add 'lunar infusion' for the night crowd. Genius."

### Motivation
Supports extended family through wine cart profits. His cousin Miroslav (Party Monk) sends him customers; they split referral fees. Started as a legitimate wine vendor 20 years ago, discovered tourists pay more for mystical nonsense than actual quality.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**Intercepting freshly-blessed tourists:**
> "You have been blessed! Now you drink the clock wine. Infused with temporal energy. Very popular with ladies at clubs."

*Immediately begins pouring sample*

### Key Interaction Dialogue
**Lord Pilsner**: "Temporal energy?"

**Clock Wine Vendor** (nodding enthusiastically):
> "The clock, it radiates time. The grapes, they absorb it. Six hundred years of temporal energy in every bottle. You drink this, the ladies... they sense it."

**Bradley**: "That's not how radiation or grapes work—"

**Chadwick**: "Bro, don't question European wine science."

**Clock Wine Vendor** (pointing at Chadwick):
> "This one understands! Very cultured. One bottle? No no no. One bottle is for tasting. Three bottles is for EXPERIENCE. Six bottles... is for LIFESTYLE."

### The Pitch
> "Very popular with ladies at clubs. The clock energy, it makes you... magnetic. A tour group bought six bottles yesterday. Now they are engaged. True story."

### Vintage Question Response
**Bradley**: "What vintage is this?"

**Clock Wine Vendor** (without hesitation):
> "This vintage? 2019. Very good year. For boxes."

*Realizes what he said, continues without pause*

> "I mean... for... the boxes the grapes come in. From the vineyard. Very traditional."

### Post-Sale
> "You feel the time flowing through you, yes? That is the clock. Very powerful. Remember: drink at midnight for maximum temporal alignment. The ladies will notice. Trust me."

### Upsell Attempt
> "Three bottles, only 150 Sovs. Save 30 Sovs. Very smart investment. Your future selves will thank you. Because of the TIME energy. You understand."

### If Player Declines
> "Your choice. But when you're at the club and the ladies ignore you... remember: Václav offered you magnetism."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "The clock wine lovers return! I save special bottles for you! New vintage - 2020. Even MORE temporal. Very rare. Only 75 Sovs this time. Supply and demand. The clock demands."
