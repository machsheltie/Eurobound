# NPC Profile: Tattoo Shop Barker

*"You! Crying man! You need tattoo to remember this feeling!"*

## 📋 NPC Identity

- **Display Name**: Tattoo Shop Barker
- **Internal Name**: Viktor "Ink Man" Horáček
- **Role**: Service NPC / Tattoo Shop Funnel
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_astronomical_cockup_01`
- **Primary Location**: Alley entrance near Astronomical Cock-Up Square, Debaucheryville
- **Secondary Appearances**: Same location only (guards his turf)

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Standing at the alley entrance to Euro Ink 4 Less, spotting emotionally compromised tourists (particularly those crying after the clock show) and encouraging them to make permanent tattoo decisions while vulnerable. He's the hook; the shop is the catch.

### What Bros Believe
A friendly, heavily-tattooed local is recognizing their profound emotional state and validating their desire to commemorate this life-changing experience with permanent body art. He's a kindred spirit who understands.

### The Comedy Gap
Viktor specifically targets crying tourists because they make the worst decisions. His shop is called "Euro Ink 4 LESS" - the name itself is a warning. He promises "no regrets" while his boss inside specializes in giving people exactly that. Lord Pilsner will get a duck instead of a skeleton.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Enabler** - Viktor doesn't scam anyone directly. He simply... encourages bad decisions. He validates emotional tattoo choices with genuine enthusiasm. He believes everyone should express themselves through ink. The fact that the shop he works for is terrible is... someone else's problem.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Encouraging | Validates EVERY tattoo idea enthusiastically |
| Observant | Spots emotional vulnerability instantly |
| Friendly | Genuinely warm, even while enabling mistakes |
| Proud | Loves showing off his own questionable tattoos |
| Philosophical | Has theories about ink and memory |

### Internal Monologue
> "Crying man! Perfect. The crying ones always get the biggest pieces. He wants a skeleton? Jakub will... do his best. What matters is the FEELING. The memory. The permanence. Whether it looks like a skeleton or a duck is... details."

### Motivation
Gets commission for every customer he brings in. Genuinely believes tattoos help people process emotions. Doesn't see himself as part of any scam - he's providing a SERVICE. The quality of the actual tattoos is "artistic interpretation."

### Approach/Greeting
**Spotting crying Lord Pilsner:**

*Steps forward from alley entrance*

### Key Interaction Dialogue
**Lord Pilsner** (still emotional): "A tattoo? I don't know..."

**Viktor** (arm around shoulder):
> "Listen, friend. This feeling? It goes away. Tomorrow you wake up, you forget. But tattoo? Tattoo KEEPS the feeling. Forever."

**Bradley**: "That's not how memory or emotions work—"

**Viktor** (ignoring Bradley):
> "Skeleton from clock? Very popular. Very meaningful. Very fast. You walk in crying man, you walk out... DECORATED man."

**Chadwick**: "Bro, that's actually really deep."

**Viktor** (pointing at Chadwick):
> "This one understands! Ink is memory. Memory is identity. You want to remember who you were in this moment? Tattoo."

### The Pitch
> "We do walk-ins. No appointment. No waiting. No regrets. Mostly no regrets. My colleague Jakub inside - artist. Real artist. European trained."
*Jakub learned from YouTube*

**On the skeleton design:**
> "Skeleton from clock? Very popular. Very meaningful. Very fast. Lord Pilsner... you look like lower back guy. Very classic placement. The ladies, they see it when you dance."

### Directing to Shop
> "Euro Ink 4 Less, just down alley. Tell them Viktor sent you. They know what this means."
*It means commission for Viktor*

### Post-Referral
> "Go. Make memory permanent. I wait here for when you come out - we celebrate with cigarette. Decorated men."

### If Player Hesitates
> "I understand. Big decision. But consider: you came to Europe to FEEL things. You felt them. Now you make them permanent. Otherwise... what was the point?"

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "Back for more ink? We have clock rooster now. Very popular. Also the twelve apostles - though we can only fit six on most people. Back for you. The duck man returns!"
*If Lord Pilsner got the tattoo*
