---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Selling "prime viewing spots" for 10 Sovs per person to watch the hourly clock show. He leads tourists to a spot behind a large stone pillar that technically has a view of the clock but is practically useless for actually seeing the show.

### What Bros Believe
A savvy local insider is giving them access to the exclusive viewing area where REAL clock enthusiasts stand. This is the authentic European experience - knowing where the locals watch from.

### The Comedy Gap
The pillar completely blocks the view. Other tourists can see just fine for free. Pavel's "exclusive" spot is actually the worst possible location, but he frames it as elite. Bros spend the entire show craning around the pillar and convince themselves this IS the authentic way to experience it.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Technically-Honest Hustler** - Pavel never actually lies. He says "best view" and it IS technically a view. He says "exclusive" and he IS the only one selling that particular spot. His genius is in implication and urgency, not deception.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Confident | Absolutely believes in his product (the experience, not the view) |
| Urgent | Creates artificial scarcity and time pressure |
| Technically honest | Never says anything provably false |
| Observant | Spots American tourists from 50 meters by their shoes |
| Persistent | Doesn't take first "no" as final answer |

### Internal Monologue
> "Americans are the best. They don't want value - they want to FEEL like insiders. I'm not selling a view. I'm selling exclusivity. The pillar is part of the experience. It's... curated."

### Motivation
Pure economics. He makes 300 Sovs-500 per day during peak season with zero overhead costs. He's been doing this for 15 years. The pillar spot is "his" - other scammers respect the territory. This isn't a scam to him; it's a legitimate service industry job.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**On spotting Americans entering square**:
> "Americans! Best view in square! Only 10 Sovs each! Very limited!"

**If ignored, follows briefly**:
> "The clock show is in 15 minutes! These spots go FAST!"

### Key Interaction Dialogue
*Bros stop to listen:*

**Lord Pilsner**: "Wait, there's special spots?"

**Viewing Spot Scammer** (nodding enthusiastically):
> "This spot? Very exclusive. The REAL viewers stand here. Tourists, they crowd the front like sheep. But the connoisseurs... they know."

**Bradley**: "That makes sense. Optimal viewing angle would account for crowd interference..."

**Viewing Spot Scammer** (pointing at Bradley):
> "This one understands! Very smart. 10 Sovs each. Three of you, 30 Sovs. I give you the local experience."

### Sales Close
*Leading them to the pillar:*
> "Here. Best spot. See? Very authentic viewing experience. The pillar, it provides... atmosphere."

**Chadwick**: "There's a pillar in the way."

**Viewing Spot Scammer** (unfazed):
> "The pillar is part of it. Locals view from here. Very traditional."

### Post-Sale
> "You can see everything from here. Eventually. Enjoy the show!"
*Already walking away to find next marks*

### If Player Declines
> "Your choice. But when you're fighting the crowd, remember - Pavel offered you the exclusive experience."
*Shrugs, immediately targets next tourist group*

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "The crying Americans! You want second viewing? Different pillar this time! Very exclusive, different angle!"
