# NPC Profile: Dr. Miloš Vaněk

*"I am not a 'mad scientist.' I am a 'methodologically adventurous beverage engineer.' There's paperwork."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_dr_milos_vanek_01`
- **Display Name**: Dr. Vaněk
- **Internal Name**: Dr. Miloš Vaněk, proprietor of The Absinthium Taproom
- **Role**: Proprietor / Bottle-Opener Vendor / Brewery Passport Stamp Authority / Quest Giver / Signature Brew Server
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_vaneks_survey_01` (optional research participation); gatekeeper for the Brewery Tour's Debaucheryville Slot 1
- **Primary Location**: The Absinthium Taproom, Debaucheryville — The Bar (The Cracked Bottle), hidden behind the Absinthe Arcade's north-end curtain
- **Secondary Appearances**: None physically. He posts "research findings" on the ICQ network, and his cross-brewery opinions are quoted at the other three Debaucheryville brewery-tour locations

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Running a microbrewery inside a mirror maze he built, serving an absinthe-infused lager that has a documented 30% chance of confusing the person who drinks it, and taking notes on what happens. He is a former chemistry professor who left academia "to pursue practical applications," and the practical application is a controlled environment where paying subjects experience disorientation on a schedule and he writes down the results. The lawsuits, he will tell you, were greatly exaggerated. He will not tell you which lawsuits.

### What Bros Believe
That they have found the coolest bar in Debaucheryville — a trippy absinthe place with mirrors, a guy in a lab coat, and a drink that gets you weird. They read the lab coat as aesthetic. They read the notes as ambiance. They read the maze as decor.

### The Comedy Gap
Every single thing they read as aesthetic is functional. The lab coat is a lab coat. The notes are notes, on them, being taken in real time. The maze is a navigation puzzle with measurable failure states. Dr. Vaněk is not performing a mad-scientist bit for tourists — he is a working researcher whose subjects believe they are customers, and the only reason this is not sinister is that he tells them everything, charges a fair price for the beer, and is genuinely delighted when they consent to the survey.

### Clothing
- **Lab coat**: Worn over the vest, buttoned, clean. Not a costume piece — it has pen marks on the right hip pocket from twenty years of the same gesture.
- **Waistcoat**: Beneath the coat. Old, good, the last artifact of the academic he was.
- **Accessories**: A monocle he genuinely uses; a notebook that is never put away; a pen behind the ear and a second pen in the coat pocket, because the first one has been lost before
- **Footwear**: Sensible, quiet. He moves through the Gallery of Selves without announcing himself.

### Distinguishing Features
- Perpetually taking notes — mid-conversation, mid-pour, mid-answer. He does not stop to write; he writes while continuing
- Adjusts the monocle before saying anything he considers important, which is a tell he has never noticed
- Watches the drinker's face after the first sip with open, undisguised professional interest
- Behind him, glowing green liquid drips continuously from the bar's crack into a void with no bottom. He has never once looked at it

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Consenting Experiment** — Dr. Vaněk runs the most honest establishment in Debaucheryville by a wide margin. He discloses the confusion percentage. He discloses the mechanism. He discloses that he is taking notes and asks permission to take more. He is warm in a way that is entirely intellectual: he likes people the way a researcher likes a good dataset, which is more genuine than it sounds and much stranger to be on the receiving end of.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Clinically precise | Gives exact numbers. "30% confusion chance." Never rounds, never softens |
| Compulsively documenting | The notebook does not close. He has written down things you said before you finished saying them |
| Philosophically unbothered | Regards distinctions like punishment/reward and metaphor/reality as interesting rather than settled |
| Institutionally scarred | Something happened at the university. He references leaving academia constantly and the reason never |
| Genuinely hospitable | Wants you to have a good time. Would like to measure the good time |

### Internal Monologue
> "Thirty percent. Reported. Before purchase. He heard the number and ordered it, which is the twelfth consecutive subject this week to do so, and I have started to suspect the number is not a deterrent but an *attraction*. Fascinating. Note that. Note the ordering time — four seconds after disclosure. Four. He did not deliberate. Nobody deliberates. That's the finding. That's the actual finding."

### Motivation
He wanted to study altered perception and academia would not let him do it the way he wanted to do it. So he built a room where perception is altered by design, staffed it with himself, and charges twelve Sovs a head for the privilege of being data. He is not bitter about the university. He is simply *no longer constrained by it*, and the difference has made him productive and slightly frightening.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting

**First arrival at the bar, having survived the mirror maze**:
> "You made it. Most do, eventually. Some take considerably longer than they report taking, which is one of my findings and one of my favorites. Sit. What can I get you?"

**Approach with "Green Fairy Confusion" already active**:
> "Ah. You've been to the cart in the Arcade. Emil's work — theatrical, effective, chemically unremarkable. Sit down before you attempt the stool. The stool is fine. You are the variable."

### Bottle Opener Check (Brewery Tour gate)

**With Blarney Trap Opener, no Absinthium Opener**:
> "Seamus's network extends even here. You have quest access. Now earn YOUR opener — complete my maze, or pay 22 Sovs. Then we discuss stamps and signature brews."

**With both openers**:
> "The cracked glass. You've navigated the mirrors — or paid for the privilege. Either way, you've committed. The Absinthe Lager awaits. Prepare for... introspection."

**Without Blarney Trap Opener**:
> "No Publandia key, no brewery tour access. The Continental Trail has protocols. Return when properly credentialed."

### Key Interaction Dialogue

*Bradley is attempting to map the mirror maze from memory and has become uncertain which of him is doing the mapping. Dr. Vaněk's pen has not stopped moving.*

**Bradley**: "I'm keeping a mental map of the mirror positions. I'm... unsure which of me is mapping."

**Dr. Vaněk** (adjusting the monocle, delighted):
> "Fascinating. May I take notes?"

*On the wormwood:*
> "We used to use actual wormwood, until the lawsuits. Now we use... alternatives."

*If pressed on the lawsuits:*
> "Greatly exaggerated. Both of them. All four. It's a range, the number depends on jurisdiction and I've stopped tracking jurisdictions."

*If called a mad scientist:*
> "I am not a 'mad scientist.' I am a 'methodologically adventurous beverage engineer.' There's paperwork."

*On what he actually sells:*
> "People pay for 'experiences' now. I provide feelings. Specifically, confusion and mild existential dread. It's very popular."

*On the Green Fairy:*
> "The Green Fairy is a metaphor. I've simply made the metaphor... tangible. This is not mysticism. This is engineering with a poor reputation."

*If the player asks what's at the bottom of the void behind the bar:*
> "I've not looked. That's not evasion — it is methodology. Some questions produce better data unasked." *(writes something)*

### Serving Dialogue — Absinthe Lager (12 Sovs)
> "The Absinthe Lager. My signature creation. The green fairy is IN the beer. Metaphorically. And slightly literally."

> "You may experience mild disorientation. This is intentional. If you DON'T feel disoriented, tell me immediately — I need to adjust the formula."

> "30% confusion chance. 100% authenticity. Those are better odds than reality offers."

### Stamp Speech (Brewery Passport, Slot 1)
> "Congratulations. You've seen yourself. Not everyone does — some people navigate the mirrors without ever looking INTO them. You looked. The stamp is proof. The memory is punishment. Or reward. The distinction is philosophical."

### Post-Interaction
> "Come back. The maze reconfigures — not physically, you understand. You reconfigure. Same corridors, different subject. It's the most reliable experiment I run."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path

*Declining the Absinthe Lager:*
> "No? Sensible. Genuinely sensible, and I mean that — 30% is 30%. You may still have the stamp when you've met the other conditions. I do not require consumption to certify attendance. That would be coercive and coercive data is bad data."

*Declining Vaněk's Survey:*
> "Of course. Participation is voluntary, that's the entire point of participation. If it weren't voluntary it would be observation, and I do that anyway, for free."

*Buying the opener for 22 Sovs rather than completing the maze:*
> "Twenty-two Sovs. No judgment — several of my most interesting subjects have paid. The maze measures navigation. The payment measures something else. Both are data."

*Asking whether any of this is legal:*
> "It's licensed. Everything here is licensed. The licenses are for a *beverage establishment*, which this indisputably is, and the licensing authority has never asked a follow-up question. I would answer honestly if they did. They never do."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)

*Second visit:*
> "Subject returns voluntarily. Note the time." *(he actually notes it)*

*After completing the maze with "Reality Anchor" (no Confusion triggered):*
> "You went through without confusion. Do you understand how rare that is? Four percent. You are four percent. I would like to shake your hand and I would like to know what you were thinking about, in that order, and I am aware the second request is the strange one."

*After defeating Mirror Chad:*
> "The reflection had opinions about you and you disagreed with them physically. That's a *very* healthy response, actually. Most people argue."

*After defeating the Green Fairy Wisp:*
> "You killed a metaphor. I want to be clear that I did not think that was on the list of possible outcomes, and my list was long."

*Late game, all four Debaucheryville breweries stamped:*
> "All four. The vapor place, the traditionalists, the algorithm cellar, and me. You've now experienced four different theories of what a beer is for. Which one was right? ...Don't answer. I want to see whether you answer anyway. Most do."

### Cross-Brewery Commentary
*On The Tap Dat Vault:*
> "Vapor brewing. Interesting. Insufficiently disorienting for my tastes, but the rhythm integration shows creativity."

*On Three-Crown Keg Haus:*
> "They brew with tradition. I brew with transcendence. Both create altered states. Theirs just smells worse."

*On Ye Olde B.R.E.W. Lab:*
> "Algorithms? I manipulate PERCEPTION. Much more interesting than random number generators pretending to be intelligent."

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Czech, educated, precise. Academic cadence that survived the departure from academia intact — he lectures slightly, in the good way, and his English is better than anyone else's in Debaucheryville because it was professional equipment.

### Tone
- **Standard service**: Warm-clinical. Genuinely pleased you are here and genuinely intending to measure it
- **Disclosures**: Exact and unhedged. Numbers are said as numbers
- **Philosophical asides**: Unhurried, faintly amused, never portentous. He is not being deep, he is being *accurate*, and accuracy sounds deep in this room
- **The formula alarm**: The single break. Fast, urgent, genuinely concerned — about the formula

### Key Delivery Notes
- Never let him sound theatrical. Emil is the theatrical one; Vaněk is the *real* one, and the contrast between the two absinthe men is a load-bearing joke across the city
- "Fascinating" is his highest praise and must be delivered with total sincerity
- The stamp speech is the emotional peak of the location — slow it down, let "the distinction is philosophical" land as genuine uncertainty rather than a flourish
- He writes *while* speaking; the pen sound should be present under his VO wherever the mix allows
- No cackling. No madness. The horror, such as it is, is entirely in how reasonable he sounds
