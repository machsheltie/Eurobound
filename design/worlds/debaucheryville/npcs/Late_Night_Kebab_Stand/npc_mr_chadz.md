# NPC Profile: Mr. Chadz

*"I call this one 'intestinal roulette.' You want two?"*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `debaucheryville_npc_mr_chadz_01`
- **Display Name**: Mr. Chadz
- **Internal Name**: Mr. Chadz (the stand's sign says "MR. CHADZ KEBABS"; nobody has ever heard a first name and he has never volunteered one)
- **Role**: Vendor / Consumable Merchant / Status-Effect Broker / Great Döner Debate Sample Provider
- **Associated Quest**: `debaucheryville_sidequest_great_doner_debate_01` (provides the Debaucheryville regional sample)
- **Primary Location**: Late-Night Kebab Stand, Debaucheryville (Velvet Curtain Club alley exit)
- **Secondary Appearances**: None — the cart does not move, and neither does he

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Running a rusty open-air food cart wedged between a chain-link fence and an NFT-graffitied wall, one-handed, while a burner phone rings about supplier problems and health-inspector timing and buzzes, in between, with price alerts for coins he half-follows. He is a former bouncer who worked out that the same crowd he used to throw out of clubs will pay him 6 Sovs for a wrap ninety seconds later, and that they will pay it in a state where nobody negotiates. He discloses everything. He does not lower the price.

### What Bros Believe
That they have found the authentic late-night European street food experience — the real thing, the one locals eat, the one that doesn't show up in guidebooks. They interpret the health-code jokes as gruff Eastern European humor. They interpret the rat as local color. They interpret the 6 Sovs wrap as a steal.

### The Comedy Gap
Mr. Chadz never lies to them once. He says the meat is "mostly legal." He says the heat lamp is "all vibes." He tells them the FDA stopped answering his emails in 2019 and he tells them the year specifically, like a man citing a source. The bros hear all of it, laugh, and order two. Full disclosure has never once cost him a sale, which is the only market research he has ever needed.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 35-45 |
| Build | Enormous. Ex-door build gone comfortable — still the widest thing in the alley, moves with the unhurried economy of a man who has never had to hurry |
| Height | 6'4" (towers over the cart; has to stoop to reach the low shelf) |
| Energy | Deadpan, immovable, faintly amused |

### Clothing
- **Glittery crop top**: Sequined, catches the fluorescent cart light and throws it around the alley. Worn without irony, comment, or explanation. It is simply what he wears to work.
- **Bulletproof vest**: Worn *over* the crop top. Genuine, scuffed, clearly not new. Leftover from the door years. He has never said why he still wears it and nobody has ever successfully asked.
- **Accessories**: A burner phone on the cart's ledge, face-up, always visible — half of what crosses that screen is the supplier and the inspector, the other half is price alerts he set up once and has never turned off; a stained apron he ties and never washes; a single heavy signet ring on the hand that holds the tongs
- **Footwear**: Steel-toed boots, standing in a rainbow grease puddle, unbothered

### Distinguishing Features
- Checks the burner phone every ten to fifteen seconds, mid-conversation, without breaking eye contact with the customer
- Operates the entire cart one-handed — the other hand is either holding the phone or free in case it rings
- Never raises his voice. Not once. The alley is loud; he simply is not
- Deadpan delivery of every single health disclosure, as though reading serial numbers

### Sprite Notes
The whole read is the crop-top-over-bulletproof-vest combination: absurd, entirely sincere, never acknowledged. Silhouette must be huge — he should crowd the cart. Deadpan face at all times; his expression does not change between "welcome" and "that one might kill you." Sequins need a subtle sparkle pass under the cart fluorescents. Phone-checking is his signature idle. The burner phone carries **supplier and inspector calls plus crypto price alerts** — the screen may show a ticker row or an alert banner, small and unreadable at sprite scale, and that is the only crypto-legible surface anywhere at this location. Everything else at the cart stays crypto-free (the "NFT 4 LYFE" wall graffiti remains set dressing, not a system). No wallet app UI, no coin logos on the cart, no kiosk.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Fully Disclosed Grift** — Mr. Chadz is a professional who has discovered that honesty is the cheapest possible marketing. He tells every customer exactly what is wrong with the food, in plain language, before they buy it. They buy it. He has concluded, correctly, that disclosure is not a risk when the customer is hungry at 3 AM and the nearest alternative is four blocks uphill. He is not cruel and he is not a con man. He is a man with a price list and a heat lamp and no interest in pretending either is better than it is.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Radically honest | Volunteers every health violation unprompted, as a matter of routine, like a safety briefing |
| Unhurried | Has never once moved faster because a customer was in a hurry |
| Bouncer-brained | Reads the crowd before it arrives; knows who's about to be sick, who's about to fight, who's about to overorder |
| Phone-tethered | The burner comes first. Always. Customers wait through the call and never complain. The price alerts get a glance and nothing else — he has never once acted on one |
| Non-judgmental | Has seen everything at this cart and has opinions about none of it |

### Internal Monologue
> "Told him about the meat. Told him about the lamp. Told him the FDA thing — I always tell them the FDA thing, it's a good line, it lands. He laughed. He ordered two. That's the job. Twelve Sovs. The rat's back. Fine. The rat has been here longer than most of my suppliers."

### Motivation
Six years on the door of the Velvet Curtain Club taught him that the money isn't in keeping people out — it's in what they buy on the way home. The cart is a margin business he can run alone, at hours nobody else will work, serving a customer base that never haggles. He is not trying to grow it. He is not trying to legitimize it. It works, and the phone keeps ringing about the supplier, and that's Tuesday.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting

**First approach, any hour**:
> "Cart's open. Everything's hot. Some of it's meat."

**Approach while player has Drunk, Hungover, or Embarrassed status**:
> "Yeah. I know that face. I worked the door six years, I know that face. Sit. Don't sit, there's nowhere to sit. Order."

### Key Interaction Dialogue

*The customer is reading the menu board. Mr. Chadz waits. He does not fill the silence. When they point at anything, he answers before they finish pointing:*

**Lord Pilsner**: "What's actually IN the Gutbuster?"

**Mr. Chadz** (flat, without looking up from the phone):
> "Lamb. Mostly. Pickled chili slaw. I call this one 'intestinal roulette.' You want two?"

**Lord Pilsner**: "...yeah, actually. Two."

**Mr. Chadz**:
> "Twelve."

*On the EuroMeat Pocket™:*
> "It's grey. It's supposed to be grey. Grey is a color, my friend. Five Sovs."

*On the Yogurt Reboot:*
> "Fixes whatever the other stuff did. Then it does its own thing for about two minutes. Buy it before, not after. Nobody buys it before."

*If the player asks about health inspections:*
> "The FDA stopped returning my emails in 2019. Different country, I know. I emailed them anyway. Felt thorough."

*If the player notices the rat:*
> "That's a rat. It found a chili pepper. Good for him. Six Sovs."

*If the phone buzzes with a price alert mid-order and the player asks what it was — he does not look up from the wrap:*
> "Kebab coin's down three percent. Meat-backed, they said. My meat. Nobody asked me. Six Sovs."

*If the player asks whether he holds any (any visit):*
> "I set the alerts up years ago. Never turned them off. That's the whole relationship."

### Sales Close
*He wraps one-handed, phone pinned between shoulder and ear, and slides it across without ceremony:*
> "Meat's mostly legal. Heat lamp's all vibes. Enjoy."

### Post-Interaction
> "You'll be back in forty minutes. Everyone's back in forty minutes. I don't stock up for the morning crowd, I stock up for the *second* wave of the night crowd."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
> "No? Fine. Kebab place four blocks up is closed. The one past the square is closed. The metro's closed. Everything is closed. I'm not saying that as pressure, I'm saying it as a schedule."

*If the player tries to haggle:*
> "It's six Sovs. It's been six Sovs since before you got here. You want to negotiate, go to the Shadow Exchange, they love that down there."

*If the player asks for extra sauce aggressively (the ban trigger):*
> "Hey. Hey. Hands off the pump. This is how you get told to walk. I've told bigger guys than you to walk, and I was getting paid less to do it."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)

*Second and third visit:*
> "Back. Told you."

*After five or more purchases:*
> "You want the Belly of Chadz. Don't argue, you want the Belly of Chadz. Three sauces. Named it after myself. Nobody made me do that."

*If the player has completed a Great Döner Debate sample elsewhere in Europe:*
> "You've been comparing. I can tell. You've got the comparing face. Look — theirs is cleaner. Mine's *cheaper*, and mine's *open*. That's two out of three and the third one's overrated."

*Late in the game, after the player has become locally known:*
> "People talk. Somebody said you sat with the tram guy all night. That's — yeah. That's a thing people notice around here. Sauce is free tonight. Once."

### Primary Schedule
| Time | Location | Activity |
|------|----------|----------|
| 22:00-02:00 | The cart | Peak service; club overflow begins arriving; phone rings constantly |
| 02:00-04:00 | The cart | Maximum volume; the desperate hours; MegaMix Box sales spike |
| 04:00-06:00 | The cart | Second wave; repeat customers; slower, more conversational |
| 06:00-22:00 | The cart | Technically open. Almost nobody comes. He is there anyway, on the phone |

**Note:** The stand is 24/7 by policy but only alive between 22:00 and 06:00. Menu items with Hungover-facing effects are stocked heaviest during the overnight block.

### Positioning Notes
- Sited at the Velvet Curtain Club alley exit deliberately — he watches the door he used to work, and the crowd that gets turned away or thrown out walks directly into his cart
- Stands behind the rotisserie, which puts the spinning meat between him and every approaching customer; the meat is the first thing they see, he is the second
- Faces the alley mouth so he sees arrivals five seconds before they see him and has already read their status
- The burner phone sits face-up on the ledge where customers can see it ring; this is intentional and it makes people order faster

### Hidden Details / Easter Eggs
- **Examining the burner phone**: The screen shows a contact list where every entry is a single letter followed by a number. One is saved as "DO NOT ANSWER (SUPPLIER)." Above the list, an unread alert banner reads `$KBAB -3.1%`. There are forty-one others behind it, all unread, going back months.
- **Examining the bulletproof vest**: A faded strip of tape on the shoulder reads "V.C.C. — DOOR 2." It is the Velvet Curtain Club's staff marking.
- **Buying all eight menu items in a single night**: Unlocks the achievement track entry "World's Pickiest Drunk" progression and one line — *"You bought the whole board. Nobody buys the whole board. That's not a compliment, that's a medical event."*
- **Buying the Yogurt Reboot first, before anything else**: The only time his expression changes. Slight nod. *"...huh. Alright."* (+1 Bravado)
- **The rat**: Persistent, named by nobody, present at the curb across all visits. Examining it three separate times produces escalating deadpan acknowledgments and nothing else.

### Territory / Social Dynamics
- The alley is understood to be his. Nobody else pitches within sight of the cart, and this was settled long before the game starts, by whatever means, and is never explained
- He does not compete for foot traffic; the Velvet Curtain Club's door policy does his marketing
- Locals buy from him quietly and without commentary; tourists buy from him loudly. He charges both the same, which is his entire ethic
- The grease-cure pipeline is real and other vendors know it: club debuffs go in, kebab statuses come out, and the Absinthe Arcade sends him its casualties too

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Czech, worn down by two decades of shouting at doors and then twenty more of not shouting at anyone. Low, gravel-adjacent, unhurried. Authentic but never comic — the joke is never his accent, the joke is the content of what he calmly discloses.

### Tone
- **Standard service**: Flat, procedural, like a man reading a manifest
- **Health disclosures**: Identical to standard service. No shift whatsoever. This is the whole gag
- **Ban warning / door voice**: Volume unchanged, register drops, sentence length shortens. Genuinely intimidating for exactly two lines, then gone
- **Rare familiarity (visit 6+)**: Marginally warmer. Marginally. Do not push this

### Key Delivery Notes
- Never rushes a line. Pauses land better than punchlines here
- "You want two?" is the money line — deliver it as a genuine logistical question, never as a joke
- The FDA line needs the year said clearly: "twenty-nineteen," like a citation
- He should sound like he is reading the truth off a card he has read four thousand times
- Phone interruptions cut him off mid-word; he resumes exactly where he stopped, no apology

- **Source-file contradiction — RESOLVED (author-approved): the crypto phone STAYS.** `Late-Night Kebab Stand.md` body text always described the burner as a "crypto-alert" device; the same file's artistic notes previously read *"NO crypto elements whatsoever."* The ruling keeps the body text and narrows the art note.
