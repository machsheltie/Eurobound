## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Five friends celebrating Cara's 21st. **[CANON — author: "a group of just turned 21 girls in there celebrating one of their groups birthdays. They're cute. They're young."]** They are slightly overdressed for an arcade, drinking glowing neon test-tube shots (the *Miss Congeniality* kind), and Cara is wearing a cheap sash that reads **CARA — 21**. **[CANON — approved draft]** They are not running anything. They are having a great night, and the night just got a free round.

### What Bros Believe
They are **"the exact demographic the bros thought they'd be scoring in their delusion."** **[CANON — author]** The bros flew to Europe believing a party of twenty-one-year-olds was the natural habitat of three men pushing forty. Here it is, with a sash on.

### The Comedy Gap
The bros came for this table. The table sees a grandpa with a wallet. Nobody is cruel to Pilsner. Nobody even notices him long enough to be cruel. The Blonde Girl's misread is sincere: an old guy offered to buy drinks, so obviously he meant all of them, so obviously it's for Cara. The joke is that the most devastating thing that can happen to Lord Pilsner is a group of strangers being *genuinely happy* he exists.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Birthday Girl** — the centre of the night and entirely entitled to be. Cara is happy, loud, slightly drunk and accepting tribute from the universe. A stranger buying her a round is not suspicious to her. It is what birthdays are for.

### Personality Traits (PROPOSED)

| Generous with joy | Cheers for anyone who buys a round, loudly, by nickname |

### Internal Monologue (PROPOSED)

### Motivation
Have the best 21st anyone has ever had. Mission accomplished, subsidised by Lord Pilsner.
