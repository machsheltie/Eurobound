# Three-Crown Keg Haus

*Where nobility meets negligence - Slavic-style triple-ferment brews in a medieval dive bar reimagined by someone who failed architecture*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: debaucheryville_shadowexchange_threecrownkeghaus_brewery_03
- **Display Name**: Three-Crown Keg Haus
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Noble by Name, Not by Health Code"
- **World Location**: Debaucheryville
- **Area Zone**: Shadowed alley near Shadow Exchange
- **Location Type**: Royal Dive Brewery / Stealth Puzzle Dungeon / Brewery Tour Location #3

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: None (always accessible once discovered)
- **Stat Requirements**: +1 Bravado to enter (confidence threshold)
- **Item Requirements**: Debaucheryville Bottle Opener Keychain (required for stamp/signature brew)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: Must discover shadowed alley near Shadow Exchange
- **Special Conditions**: Sticky floor outside warns of venue quality

### Bottle Opener Requirement
- **Prerequisite**: The Blarney Trap Bottle Opener (Publandia) — required to access ANY brewery tour location
- **Location-Specific Opener**: Three-Crown Keg Haus Bottle Opener
- **Design**: Three rusted crowns stacked vertically with bottle opener as the base/throne, miniature stein charm dangles from ring
- **Material**: Intentionally tarnished brass with rust-orange patina ("authentic aging")
- **Inscription**: "Noble by Liver"
- **Acquired**: Complete the stealth puzzle (navigate without waking patrons) OR purchase from Bartender for 15 Sovs
- **Special Feature**: Stein charm actually holds 1ml of liquid (decorative, not functional... mostly)
- **Check Point**: Bartender checks for Blarney Trap opener (quest access) AND this opener (stamp/beer access)
- **Without Blarney Trap Opener**: Cannot participate in brewery tour at all
- **Without Keg Haus Opener**: Can enter (with Bravado check), but cannot receive stamp or Iron Gut Tripel

**Bartender's Opener Check**:
- *With Blarney Trap Opener, No Keg Haus Opener*: "The shamrock opener! Seamus's network reaches even our sticky floors. But you need the THREE CROWNS to drink the Tripel. Sneak past my sleepers, or 15 Sovs. Your choice."
- *With Both Openers*: "The three crowns! You've proven your stealth — or your wallet. Either way, you're NOBILITY now! The Iron Gut Tripel is yours! May your liver serve the realm!"
- *Without Blarney Trap Opener*: "The Continental Trail has rules. No Publandia opener, no brewery tour access. Come back when you're properly... credentialed. We'll still serve you regular swill."

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Back through alley entrance to Shadow Exchange area
- **Completion Requirements**: Defeat Keg Sentinel boss, obtain Iron Gut Tripel brew
- **Failure States**: Can fail stealth puzzle and wake bouncer mini-boss (recoverable)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: Velvet keg throne selfie prop access, Brewery Tour quest progression (3 of 4 complete)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Gritty medieval Slavic dive bar reimagined by someone who failed architecture - 300-year-old castle turned frat basement where nobility meets negligence.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Eastern European craft brewery pretension meeting dive bar reality
- **Secondary Targets**: Post-Soviet tracksuit nobility culture, medieval castle tourism exploitation, failed gentrification attempts
- **Cultural Specificity**: Slavic triple-ferment brewing tradition weaponized for drunk tourist consumption
- **Humor Approach**: GTA-style dive bar satire with Eurotrip "noble" party tourism absurdity
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets pretentious craft brewery culture and dive bar decay, not Slavic brewing traditions themselves

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling "noble heritage brewing" in health code violation dive bar
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Medieval castle aesthetic destroyed by frat basement conversion
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros believe they're experiencing authentic Slavic nobility when it's a barely-functional dive
- **Comedy of Errors**: Stealth puzzle requires avoiding passed-out "nobles" and creaky floors
- **Consequence System**: Embarrassed status as gameplay mechanic AND satirical commentary on gossip culture

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Drinking like medieval Slavic nobility in authentic castle-turned-brewery

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Getting wasted in health code violation dive bar with rusted crown props

**How impaired are they**: Drunk enough to ignore sticky floors, mustard wall art, and gaffer tape repairs

**Obvious signs they ignore**: Moldy booths, flickering lights, passed-out patrons everywhere, "art" drawn in condiments

**How locals play along**: Tracksuit nobles shout drinking songs, bartender references "ancient brewing heritage" while serving in dive bar

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Stealth Floor Tile Puzzle (Original)
- **Core Mechanic**: Navigate room avoiding creaky floor tiles and unconscious patron bodies
- **Tile Challenge**: Step only on safe tiles indicated by visual cues (worn vs intact floorboards)
- **Patron Obstacles**: Passed-out nobles block paths, must navigate around without contact
- **Failure Consequences**: Stepping on wrong tile or touching patron wakes Bouncer Mini-Boss
- **Success Rewards**: Access to bar area and boss encounter
- **Skill Scaling**: Tile patterns become more complex on repeat visits

### Slurring Bar Flies Mini-Mob (Original)
- **Enemy Type**: Drunken duo spreading embarrassing gossip
- **Dialogue**: "Wait… weren't you the one who ghosted Paulina?"
- **Combat Mechanic**: Apply Embarrassed status if gossip phase isn't interrupted (3-turn timer)
- **Visual Design**: Leaning sprites with synchronized occasional vomiting animation
- **Satirical Beat**: Enemy is literally drunk gossip becoming combat obstacle

### Keg Sentinel Boss Battle (Original)
- **Boss Type**: Arm-wrestling robot constructed from kegs
- **Combat Strategy**: Strength-based challenges mixed with timing mechanics
- **Status Effects**: Boss can apply Inspired (confidence boost) or reduce Accuracy
- **Arm Wrestling Phases**: Multiple rounds requiring button mashing with timing windows
- **Victory Reward**: Iron Gut Tripel brew, velvet keg throne selfie access, quest progression, stamp eligibility
