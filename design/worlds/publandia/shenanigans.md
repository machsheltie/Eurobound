# Shenanigans

*The pub where you're always the mark — every game is rigged, every drink is a trick, and you'll keep coming back anyway*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: publandia_pub_shenanigans_01
- **Display Name**: Shenanigans
- **Irish Alias**: "Cleasaíocht" (Trickery)
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Where the House Always Wins (And You Always Return)"
- **World Location**: Publandia
- **Area Zone**: The Craic Tax (side street, slightly hidden)
- **Location Type**: Scam Pub / Rigged Games Venue / Con Artist Hub / Brewery Tour Stop

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 4 PM - 3 AM (prime scamming hours)
- **Stat Requirements**: None (marks don't need qualifications)
- **Item Requirements**: Gold (you'll need it), optimism (you'll lose it)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (fresh marks always welcome)
- **Special Conditions**: "Frequent Victim" status after 3+ scams

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through door, lighter in wallet, wiser in theory
- **Completion Requirements**: Stamp requires losing 3 scams AND returning
- **Failure States**: Cannot truly "fail" — losing IS the experience
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Wise Fool" status unlocks seeing through ONE scam per visit

## Visual Design
### Exterior Description
- **Structure**: Cramped pub front, intentionally easy to miss (plausible deniability)
- **Design**: Just sketchy enough to intrigue, just welcoming enough to enter
- **Facade**: Chipped green paint, one working light, ancient Guinness sign
- **Signage**:
  - Main: "SHENANIGANS" in hand-painted, slightly crooked letters
  - Subtitle: "Traditional Irish Games & Craic"
  - Window: "TONIGHT: Lucky Spin Wheel! EVERYONE Wins!" (everyone loses)
  - Chalkboard: "Happy Hour: 2-for-1 on selected drinks" (selection is nothing)
  - Warning (very small): "For entertainment purposes only"
- **Atmosphere**: Suspiciously welcoming, too-friendly staff visible through window
- **Environmental Context**: Down a side alley, found by "accident" (guided by touts)

### Interior Layout — The Con Cathedral

**The Entry (The Welcome Mat)**
- Deliberately warm, disarming atmosphere
- Friendly host who "recognizes you" (doesn't)
- Complimentary "welcome drink" (waters down subsequent orders)
- The "lucky penny" jar (rigged, obviously)

**The Main Bar (The Distraction)**
- Comfortable seating, good music, attractive staff
- This is where you're softened up
- Drinks are legitimately good (builds trust)
- Bar games "just for fun" (scouting your skill level)

**The Games Corner (The Extraction)**
- Where the real business happens
- Multiple rigged games, rotating selection
- Crowd of "winners" (shills)
- Prizes displayed prominently (rarely awarded)

**The Back Tables (The Recovery)**
- Where you go after you've lost
- Comfort food, sympathy drinks
- Staff who "feel bad" about your luck
- Where the "one more try" pitch happens

**The Snug (The Long Con)**
- Reserved for high-value marks
- Private games, higher stakes
- The really elaborate scams
- Where they take the whales

### The Games (All Rigged)

**The Lucky Spin Wheel**
- Appearance: Colorful wheel with "JACKPOT," "FREE DRINK," "TRY AGAIN"
- Reality: Weighted to land on "Try Again" 85% of the time
- Cost: 5 Sovs per spin
- Scam Level: Entry-level, builds false hope

**Three Card Monty (Find the Lady)**
- Appearance: Classic street hustle, "just for fun"
- Reality: Staff member is a professional, shill "winners" in crowd
- Cost: 10 Sovs per round
- Scam Level: Classic, obvious to everyone except the mark

**The Ring Toss**
- Appearance: Carnival game, "anyone can win"
- Reality: Rings are slightly too small, bottles slightly too large
- Cost: 5 Sovs for 3 rings
- Scam Level: Physics-based deception

**Darts "Double or Nothing"**
- Appearance: Friendly darts game with bartender
- Reality: Your darts are slightly bent, their board is regulation
- Cost: Escalating bets starting at 5 Sovs
- Scam Level: Plausible deniability

**The Cup Shuffle**
- Appearance: Ball under cups, follow along
- Reality: Ball palmed, never under any cup
- Cost: 10 Sovs per round
- Scam Level: Sleight of hand classic

**Lucky Dice**
- Appearance: Roll doubles, win your bet back 3x
- Reality: Dice are loaded against doubles
- Cost: Minimum 10 Sovs bet
- Scam Level: Statistical manipulation

**The "Broken" Slot Machine**
- Appearance: Old slot machine, "usually broken but try your luck"
- Reality: Programmed to pay out just enough to keep you playing
- Cost: 1 Sov per pull
- Scam Level: Psychological exploitation

### The Shill Network

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Shenanigans is a pub where EVERYTHING is a scam, and the joke is that the bros keep falling for it. The satire operates on the beautiful irony that the pub is called "Shenanigans," the games are OBVIOUSLY rigged, the shills are CLEARLY plants, and yet — the marks keep playing. Because hope is irrational. Because "this time will be different." Because admitting you've been had is harder than losing more money.

The deeper joke is that the bros eventually EARN the pub's respect not by winning, but by losing gracefully and coming back anyway. The stamp isn't awarded for being smart — it's awarded for being a GOOD mark. A reliable customer. A beautiful fool.

### The Finnegan Philosophy
Mickey "The Mick" Finnegan runs Shenanigans with genuine affection for his marks. He doesn't see himself as a thief — he's an entertainer. You're not paying for a fair game; you're paying for the EXPERIENCE of hope and loss. The drinks are real, the craic is real, only the games are fake. That's more honest than most places, when you think about it.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Tourist gullibility, the psychology of gambling, "I can beat the system" mentality
- **Secondary Targets**: Obvious scams that work anyway, the sunk cost fallacy, marks who blame luck not rigging
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dublin's tourist trap economy, carnival game psychology
- **Humor Approach**: Obvious deception that still works, self-aware scamming
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes the marks' willingness, not malicious fraud

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Running rigged games (technically illegal, practically tolerated)
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Side-alley location, tout network, tourist targeting
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think they can "figure out" the rigged games
- **Comedy of Errors**: Losing repeatedly, blaming luck, trying again
- **Consequence System**: Losing money, gaining "Wise Fool" status, earning respect through loss

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Fun pub games, bad luck, "almost winning"

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Systematic extraction of tourist money through rigged entertainment

**How impaired are they**: Varies (alcohol helps the process)

**The mark's journey**:
1. "This looks fun!"
2. "I almost won! One more try."
3. "The odds have to even out eventually."
4. "I've spent too much to quit now."
5. "That guy just won! It's possible!"
6. "...I've been had, haven't I?"
7. "...one more try."

**The transformation**:
- Enter confident
- Lose 20 Sovs thinking you're unlucky
- Lose 50 Sovs thinking the odds will turn
- Lose 100 Sovs realizing you've been scammed
- Return the next night (the true mark's journey)
- Earn respect through persistence in foolishness

**How locals interact**: Locals don't come here; tourists are brought here

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Scam Mini-Games

**The Lucky Spin Wheel**
- **Cost**: 5 Sovs per spin
- **Displayed Odds**: 25% Free Drink, 25% Prize, 50% Try Again
- **Actual Odds**: 5% Free Drink, 0% Prize, 95% Try Again
- **Mini-Game**: Tap to stop wheel (input doesn't actually affect outcome)
- **Outcome**: Predetermined by entry, not by timing
- **Loss Counter**: Tracks consecutive losses

**Three Card Monty**
- **Cost**: 10 Sovs per round
- **Displayed Challenge**: Find the Queen
- **Actual Challenge**: Impossible (sleight of hand)
- **Mini-Game**: Watch cards, select final position
- **Outcome**: Queen is palmed before final selection
- **Shill Behavior**: "Winner" celebrates loudly nearby
- **Loss Counter**: Tracks consecutive losses

**The Ring Toss**
- **Cost**: 5 Sovs for 3 rings
- **Displayed Challenge**: Land ring on bottle
- **Actual Challenge**: Rings are 0.5cm too small to fit
- **Mini-Game**: Aim and throw (physics feels fair but isn't)
- **Outcome**: Mathematical impossibility of clean ring
- **Near Miss System**: Designed to look "so close"
- **Loss Counter**: Tracks total throws

**Darts "Double or Nothing"**
- **Cost**: Starting bet 5 Sovs, doubles each loss
- **Displayed Challenge**: Beat staff member at darts
- **Actual Challenge**: Your darts are subtly bent
- **Mini-Game**: Standard darts mechanic, accuracy reduced by 20%
- **Outcome**: Competitive but tilted against you
- **Double-Down**: Option to escalate after each loss
- **Loss Counter**: Tracks total bet exposure

**The Cup Shuffle**
- **Cost**: 10 Sovs per round
- **Displayed Challenge**: Follow the ball
- **Actual Challenge**: Ball is palmed, under no cup
- **Mini-Game**: Track ball, select cup
- **Outcome**: Always wrong (ball revealed in hand after)
- **Speed Progression**: Gets faster to explain failures
- **Loss Counter**: Tracks consecutive losses

**Lucky Dice**
- **Cost**: 10 Sovs minimum bet
- **Displayed Challenge**: Roll doubles, 3x payout
- **Actual Challenge**: Dice loaded against doubles
- **Mini-Game**: Shake and roll (feels random, isn't)
- **Actual Odds**: Doubles hit 8% instead of 16.7%
- **Near Miss System**: 5-6, 4-5 programmed to hit often
- **Loss Counter**: Tracks total losses

### The "Wise Fool" Path

**Stage 1: Fresh Mark**
- Lose at any game
- Staff sympathetic: "Oh, bad luck!"
- Shill "wins" nearby to encourage retry

**Stage 2: Repeat Mark**
- Lose 3+ times across any games
- Staff recognizes you: "Back for another go?"
- Subtle acknowledgment you're a good sport

**Stage 3: Persistent Mark (Stamp Eligible)**
- Lose 3+ scams AND return to Shenanigans
- Mickey approaches personally
- "Wise Fool" conversation triggered

**The "Wise Fool" Conversation**:
- Mickey: "You know the games are rigged. You've always known."
- Player options:
  - "I figured it out" (lie)
  - "I suspected" (partial truth)
  - "I just kept hoping" (full truth)
- Mickey: "And yet you came back. That takes something. Not intelligence. Something better."
- Mickey: "You're a good mark. The best kind. Let me buy you a drink. A real one."
- Mickey: "And here—" *stamps passport* "—you've earned this. Not for winning. For losing with grace."

### Stamp Acquisition
**Requirement**: Must have Bottle Opener Keychain from The Blarney Trap
**Unlock Condition**: Lose at 3 different scams AND return to Shenanigans after
**The Twist**: The stamp rewards being a GOOD MARK, not a winner

**Stamp Design**:
- Circular with jester cap border
- Three-leaf clover with one leaf wilted (luck that doesn't work)
- Banner text: "Shenanigans"
- Bottom text: "Wise Fool Since [Year]"
- Ink color: Faded gold (like your lost money)

### Signature Beer: "The Fool's Gold"
**Brewed By**: Local microbrewery (actually legitimate)
**Style**: Golden Ale with honey and a bitter finish
**ABV**: 5.0%
**Unlocked**: When stamp is acquired
**The Irony**: The only thing at Shenanigans that ISN'T a scam

**"The Fool's Gold" Stats**:
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP Restoration | +30 HP |
| SP Restoration | +15 SP |
| Special Effect | "Learned Optimism" — +10% resistance to scam detection failure |
| Status Risk | 5% "Sentimental" (donate extra gold to NPCs) |
| Purchase Price | 8 Sovs |

**Flavor Text**: "The only honest thing in this entire establishment. Golden, hopeful, with a bitter finish — just like your evening. Brewed for fools by fools. Cheers."

**Mickey on Fool's Gold**:
- "The beer's real. I'm not a MONSTER. You can't scam someone on the beer. That's sacred."
- "Named it Fool's Gold because that's what you came looking for. You found real gold instead. The beer, I mean. Not actual gold. That would be a scam."
- "Every fool who earns their stamp gets the first one free. House rules. We're not heartless."

### Post-Stamp Benefit: "Wise Fool" Status
- Can see through ONE scam per visit (shown as "rigged" before playing)
- Mickey offers genuine tips on avoiding scams elsewhere
- Staff treat you as respected regular, not mark
- Access to "The Snug" for observation (watch others get scammed)
- Optional: Can become a shill for bonus gold (moral choice)

### Bravado Interactions
- **Lose Bravado**: Lose at scams (initial losses)
- **Stabilize Bravado**: Return after losses (persistence recognized)
- **Gain Bravado**: Earn "Wise Fool" status (respected for self-awareness)
- **Special**: Refuse to become a shill = "Moral Fool" achievement

### The Shill Offer (Post-Stamp)
**Trigger**: After earning "Wise Fool" status
**Mickey's Offer**: "You know the games now. Want to be on the winning side? 50 Sovs per night to play 'winner' for fresh marks."
**Player Options**:
- Accept: Earn 50 Sovs/night, help scam others, morally compromised
- Decline: "Moral Fool" achievement, Mickey respects you more
- Ask for more money: Mickey laughs, still respects you
