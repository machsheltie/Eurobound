## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Confidence Man sells exactly what the name implies — confidence, in bottles. The satire operates on multiple levels: he's selling belief as much as beverage, his products DO work (partly through ingredients, mostly through placebo), and the whole operation walks the line between helpful service and elaborate con. He calls himself "The Confidence Man" — a term literally meaning "con artist" — and dares you to trust him anyway.

The deeper joke is that confidence IS partly performance. If his tonics make you FEEL confident, and that confidence helps you succeed... did the potion work? Or did belief work? The Confidence Man would say there's no difference. He'd also say that'll be 15 Sovs.

### The Cormac Philosophy
Cormac "The Confidence Man" Byrne has been selling courage from his cart for 12 years. He's not entirely a con artist — his products contain legitimate herbal supplements and mild stimulants that DO provide effects. But he's not entirely legitimate either — his theatrical pitch and "guaranteed boldness" claims oversell reality. He exists in the comfortable middle, providing a service people want at prices they'll pay, with results that work often enough to keep him in business. Is that a con? He'd call it "sales."

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Self-improvement snake oil, confidence as performance, placebo effect
- **Secondary Targets**: Street vendor culture, tourist vulnerability, belief as product
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dublin market culture, carnival barker tradition
- **Humor Approach**: Self-aware con artistry, products that sort-of work
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes the seller and buyer's belief, not actual supplements

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling belief at premium prices
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Positioning near pubs, targeting nervous tourists
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who need liquid courage for pub challenges
- **Comedy of Errors**: Over-reliance on tonics, confidence crash timing
- **Consequence System**: Buffs that work, then wear off at worst moments

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Purchasing legitimate confidence enhancement

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Buying theatrical belief reinforcement with mild stimulant effects

**How impaired are they**: Usually not yet (buying courage for later)

**The confidence journey**:
1. "That guy says he can make me more confident. I NEED more confident."
2. "15 Sovs for 'Liquid Boldness'? That's... reasonable for BOLDNESS."
3. *drinks potion*
4. "I DO feel more confident! This WORKS!"
5. *attempts bold action*
6. *sometimes succeeds, sometimes doesn't*
7. "I need another potion."

**The transformation**:
- Enter nervous
- Purchase confidence
- Feel empowered (potion + placebo)
- Attempt something bold
- Results vary (40-60% success rate)
- Attribute success to potion, failure to needing more potion
- Return customer created

**How locals interact**: Some swear by him, some avoid him, all know him

### The Pitch System
When approaching The Confidence Man, Cormac initiates his sales pitch:

**The Opening**:
- "You! Yes, YOU! I can see it in your eyes. You need something. Something... BOLD."
- "Nervous? Uncertain? Feeling like the world's against you? I have the SOLUTION."
- "Step right up! The Confidence Man has what you NEED!"

**The Assessment** (Cormac reads your Bravado):
- Low Bravado: "Oh, you DEFINITELY need help. Let me show you something SPECIAL."
- Medium Bravado: "Good foundation, but you could be GREATER. I can help."
- High Bravado: "Already confident! But even legends need an EDGE sometimes."

**The Recommendation**:
Cormac suggests products based on your apparent needs:
- Pre-pub: "Liquid Boldness — you'll own the room."
- Pre-fight: "Fearless Fighter — no hesitation."
- Pre-flirting: "Irresistible Presence — they won't know what hit them."

**The Close**:
- "So what do you say? Ready to become who you were MEANT to be?"
- "This isn't just a purchase. It's an INVESTMENT in your POTENTIAL."
- "The confident version of you is just 15 Sovs away."

### Effect Mechanics
**How Buffs Work**:
- Effects apply immediately upon consumption
- Duration counts from drinking
- Multiple tonics can stack (with diminishing returns)
- Some effects have "crash" consequences when they end

**The Crash System**:
Premium tonics ("The Transformation," "Emergency Confidence") have crash effects:
- When duration ends, reverse effect applies briefly
- Example: "The Transformation" ends → 15 minutes of -20 Bravado
- Strategic timing important

**Placebo Factor**:
All tonics have a hidden "placebo bonus":
- If player believes in product (prior success), +10% effectiveness
- If player is skeptical, -10% effectiveness
- Belief builds with successful uses

### Negotiation System
Cormac's prices are "negotiable" within limits:

**Haggling Options**:
- Accept Price: Standard transaction
- Haggle: Charisma check for 10-20% discount
- Bulk Deal: Buy 3+ for 15% off
- Sob Story: Sometimes works, sometimes backfires
- Walk Away: Cormac might call you back with "special offer"

**Cormac's Responses**:
- Successful haggle: "Sharp customer! Fine, FINE, but only because I like you."
- Failed haggle: "Do I look like a charity? This is PREMIUM product."
- Bulk deal: "Ah, stocking up! Smart. Confidence fades. Always have backup."
- Walk away: "Wait, WAIT! Did I mention the DISCOUNT? For first-timers only!"

### "Valued Customer" Status
**Unlock**: Purchase 5+ items from Cormac
**Benefits**:
- 15% permanent discount
- Access to Special Reserve without asking
- Cormac remembers your preferences
- "Confidence Emergency" service (will find you if you need him)
- Honest recommendations (drops some of the act)

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Successful actions while buffed (potion "worked"), buying expensive options
- **Lose Bravado**: Crash effects, failed actions despite potion, being seen buying nervously
- **Neutral**: Standard purchases
- **Special**: "Beyond the Potion" (succeed at something difficult without any tonic) = +30 Bravado, Cormac's genuine respect
