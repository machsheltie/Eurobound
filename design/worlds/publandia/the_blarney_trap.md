## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Blarney Trap is the flagship pub that KNOWS it's a tourist trap and has achieved zen acceptance of this identity. The name says it all — "trap" is right there, and they're not hiding it. But here's the twist: the Guinness IS properly poured, the music IS live, the welcome IS genuine (if transactional), and if you stick around long enough to achieve "Pub Regular" status, you might actually find something real.

This is the bros' base of operations because it appeared first in their search results. They think they've discovered a hidden gem. The staff have seen a thousand groups exactly like them. The craic flows anyway.

### The Seamus Philosophy
"Americans want 'authentic Publandia.' I give them what they THINK authentic Publandia looks like. Is it authentic? The Guinness is real. The music is live. My great-grandfather probably DID drink somewhere like this. The prices are Craic Tax prices, but the welcome is genuine. You want authentic? Stay long enough to see the real regulars arrive after the tourists leave. That's authentic. But you won't. You'll be in bed by then."

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Tourist pub culture, manufactured authenticity, the "Irish Experience" package
- **Secondary Targets**: Temple Bar pricing, American expectations of Ireland, the commodified craic
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dublin's tourist district economy, Irish pub global brand
- **Humor Approach**: Self-aware tourism that winks at its own performance while still delivering
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Celebrates Irish hospitality while satirizing its packaging

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Craic Tax pricing (8 Sovs pints that cost 6 Sovs across the river)
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: The Backroom's unlicensed activities
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think they've found "the real Ireland" at the most obviously tourist pub
- **Comedy of Errors**: Infinite refills meeting finite tolerance
- **Consequence System**: "Pub Regular" status, Bravado testing, The Fear (next morning)

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Discovering authentic Irish pub culture, making local friends

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Participating in well-orchestrated tourist hospitality, paying Craic Tax prices

**How impaired are they**: Increasingly (infinite refills, remember?)

**The transformation arc**:
- Arrival: "This is SO authentic!"
- Hour 2: "Seamus is basically our FRIEND now"
- Hour 4: "I feel like I UNDERSTAND PUBLANDIA"
- Hour 6: "Absolutely Scuttered" status achieved
- Morning: "The Fear" status, room charges, vague memories of toasts

**What they're missing**:
- The locals are across the river at cheaper pubs
- Seamus calls everyone "friend" (professional skill)
- The "authentic" decor was installed in 2003
- They're paying 40% more than necessary
- The real craic happens after they've gone to bed

**How locals interact**: Staff are professionally friendly; actual locals drink in The Snug or elsewhere

### Drinking Contest Mechanics (Pint-Offs)
- **Trigger**: Challenge or be challenged by NPCs
- **Mechanic**: Rhythm-based mini-game, match pint-raising beats
- **Difficulty**: Increases with each round
- **Miss beats**: Gain "Lightweight" debuff, lose Bravado
- **Perfect rhythm**: Gain "Seasoned Drinker" buff, gain Bravado
- **Consequences**: NPCs remember performance, affects "Pub Regular" progress

### Toast Speech Challenges
- **Trigger**: Social situations, Bravado tests
- **Mechanic**: Choose toast lines, build speech
- **Success**: +Bravado, +respect, possible free round
- **Failure**: -Bravado, mild embarrassment, still drink anyway
- **Options**: Sincere, funny, exaggerated, inappropriate

### The Round System
- **Expectation**: When someone buys you a drink, you buy the next round
- **Refusal**: Damages relationships, -Bravado, staff remember
- **Strategic Buying**: Buy rounds for key NPCs to unlock information/quests
- **The Trap**: "Infinite refills" means infinite round obligations

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Win pint-offs, deliver great toasts, impress staff, survive storytelling
- **Lose Bravado**: Lose drinking contests, refuse rounds, get called out for weak toast, visibly struggle
- **"Pub Regular" Progress**: Consistent presence, staff recognition, Bravado maintenance

### Status Effects (Location-Specific)
- **"Craic'd Up"**: +Charisma, +Comedy timing (from good pub banter)
- **"Pub Regular"**: Unlocks Snug, discounts, staff trust
- **"Absolutely Scuttered"**: -All stats, hilarious dialogue unlocks
- **"The Fear"**: -Bravado regeneration next day (from questionable decisions)
- **"Fair Play"**: +Respect from NPCs (earned through being "sound")
