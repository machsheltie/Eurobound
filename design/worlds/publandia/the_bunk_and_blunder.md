## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Bunk & Blunder is a budget hostel that delivers exactly what you pay for — which is to say, very little. The satire targets hostel culture, budget travel delusions, and the particular horror of sharing sleeping space with strangers whose life choices become ambient audio. 

The bros stay here because they're "saving money for experiences" (drinking). The hostel IS an experience, just not the one they wanted. Every night is a gamble: Will your roommates be quiet? Will the shower have hot water? Will you wake up with your dignity intact? The answers are usually no, no, and definitely no.

### The Colm Philosophy
Colm runs the Bunk & Blunder with the exhausted acceptance of someone who has seen humanity at its worst and lowest and continues showing up for work. He doesn't judge — judgment requires energy he doesn't have. He's witnessed every possible hostel scenario. Nothing surprises him anymore. He just hands you the key card and wishes you luck. You'll need it.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Budget travel, hostel horror stories, the "it builds character" delusion
- **Secondary Targets**: Backpacker culture, "authentic experiences," false economies
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dublin hostel scene, international party hostels
- **Humor Approach**: Shared misery, random roommate chaos, morning-after awkwardness
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Punches at the hostel experience, not poverty

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: False economy (cheap stay, expensive consequences)
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Craic Tax proximity means Craic Tax aftermath
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think they're "roughing it" while complaining
- **Comedy of Errors**: Roommate roulette, bathroom disasters, lost items
- **Consequence System**: "Embarrassed" status, poor sleep debuffs, morning witnesses

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Smart budget travel, authentic backpacker experience

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Paying to not sleep while strangers judge their choices

**How impaired are they**: Usually very (arriving after The Craic Tax)

**The hostel journey**:
1. "15 Sovs a night! We're being SO smart with money!"
2. "Wait, how many people are in this room?"
3. "Why is that guy's alarm going off at 4 AM?"
4. "I can't shower because there's a QUEUE"
5. "Did everyone hear me come in last night?"
6. "...everyone heard me come in last night"
7. *"Embarrassed" status acquired*

**The transformation**:
- Arrive confident in budget decision
- Witness roommate doing something memorable
- DO something memorable (unintentionally)
- Wake up in audience of strangers
- Leave avoiding eye contact
- Book again next night (learned nothing)

**How locals interact**: Locals don't stay here; staff have seen everything

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Accommodation System

**Booking Process**:
1. Approach reception, choose room type
2. Pay upfront (no refunds policy)
3. Receive key card (may or may not work)
4. Room assignment is RANDOM (cannot choose roommates)
5. Rest until morning (quality varies wildly)

### Rest Quality System
Sleep quality depends on random roommate generation:

**Roommate Types** (Randomly Assigned):
| Roommate | Behavior | Effect on Rest |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| The Silent Type | Sleeps quietly, respects space | +20% rest quality |
| The Snorer | Industrial-grade nasal sounds | -30% rest quality |
| The Phone Addict | Screen glowing, typing sounds | -15% rest quality |
| The Early Riser | 5 AM alarm, packing sounds | -25% rest quality (interrupted) |
| The Party Returnee | Arrives 3 AM, loud, apologetic | -40% rest quality |
| The Crier | Quiet sobbing, existential crisis | -10% rest quality, +sympathy |
| The Talker | Wants to share their life story | -20% rest quality OR +info |
| The Witness | Saw what you did last night | "Embarrassed" risk |
| The Sleepwalker | Wanders, possibly into your space | -20% rest, +story |
| The Couple | Thinks they're being quiet (they're not) | -35% rest quality |

**Rest Quality Calculation**:
- Base Rest: 50% HP/SP restoration
- Modified by room type (+/- based on bed count)
- Modified by roommates (cumulative effects)
- Minimum: 20% restoration (you survived)
- Maximum: 90% restoration (miracle night)

### The "Embarrassed" Status System
**Trigger**: Witnessed by roommates doing something regrettable

**Embarrassing Actions** (Player or Triggered):
- Arriving extremely drunk (balance check on entry)
- Loud phone call at inappropriate hour
- Bathroom incident (random event)
- Sleep-talking (random, reveals secrets)
- Wrong bed incident (confusion in dark)
- Snoring (if player has "Full" status from food)

**"Embarrassed" Effects**:
- -10 Bravado for 4 hours
- Awkward dialogue with witnesses if encountered
- Story spreads to other hostels (reputation)
- Can become "Legend" if embarrassment is epic enough

### The Common Room Events
**Random Events When Using Common Room**:

| Event | Trigger | Outcome |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Kitchen Disaster | Using kitchen | Small fire, evacuation, story |
| Power Strip Failure | Too many devices | Darkness, chaos, bonding |
| Notice Board Quest | Checking board | Side quest opportunity |
| Cultural Exchange | Talking to NPC | Learn about other city, hint |
| Lost Item Claim | Random | Someone claims your stuff (yours?) |
| Impromptu Party | Night hours | +Bravado, -rest quality |
| Serious Conversation | 3 AM | Unexpected depth, +wisdom |

### The Shower Gauntlet
**Mechanic**: Queue-based mini-challenge

**Queue Position**: Random each morning (1-8)
**Hot Water Timer**: Depletes with each user
**Success**: Clean, +small buff
**Failure**: Cold shower, "Shocked Awake" status (+alertness, -mood)
**Skip Option**: Stay dirty, -Charisma until bath found elsewhere

### Wi-Fi Hotspot: "BunkWiFiGuest"
- Connection: Unreliable
- Speed: Theoretical
- Password: Changes daily (ask reception)
- Function: ICQ access, selfie uploads, rumor pings
- The Catch: Logs everything (not really, but the paranoia is real)

### "Seasoned Traveler" Status
**Unlock**: Stay 5 nights at The Bunk & Blunder
**Benefits**:
- Can request specific bunk (top/bottom preference)
- Know which shower has hot water
- Recognize "trouble" roommates on sight
- +10% rest quality (adaptation)
- Staff actually remember your name

### Bravado Interactions
- **Lose Bravado**: "Embarrassed" status, poor sleep complaints
- **Gain Bravado**: Epic embarrassment becomes legend, help fellow traveler
- **Neutral**: Standard stay, nothing memorable
- **Special**: "Hostel Hero" (help during crisis) = +30 Bravado, free night

### Status Effects (Location-Specific)
- **"Embarrassed"**: Witnessed doing something regrettable (-10 Bravado, 4 hrs)
- **"Sleep Deprived"**: Bad roommate luck (-5 all stats, until proper rest)
- **"Hostel Bonded"**: Made genuine friend (+contact, quest opportunity)
- **"Shocked Awake"**: Cold shower (+Alertness, -Mood, 1 hr)
- **"Seasoned Traveler"**: 5+ nights (+rest quality, permanent)

- **Demeanor**: Beyond judgment, past surprise, approaching transcendence
- **The Colm Method**: Hand over key, wish luck, ask no questions

## System Integration
