## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: Tours 10 AM - 6 PM; Dungeon accessible 24/7 (the yeast never sleeps)
- **Stat Requirements**: None for tour; combat-ready recommended for dungeon
- **Item Requirements**: Tour ticket (25 Sovs) or quest trigger; equipment for dungeon

- **Failure States**: Party wipe returns to entrance, retry available
- **Progressive Unlocks**: Post-dungeon unlocks "Brewmaster's Pass" (special access)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Stoutworks Brewery is a loving parody of the "brewery tour as religious experience" phenomenon — those massive industrial cathedrals where beer production is treated with the reverence of ancient ritual. The dungeon twist is that the yeast has become TOO alive, the fermentation has gone TOO far, and the brewery's "living beer culture" has taken the concept literally.

It's also a commentary on brewery tourism — paying 25 Sovs to watch beer being made and then buying it at retail price in the gift shop. The dungeon mode is what happens when that controlled environment goes wrong.

### The Yeast Situation (Lore)
- The Stoutworks has been brewing since 1869
- Their "heritage yeast strain" is 150+ years old
- Something in the water (or the Publandia magic) made it sentient
- The Yeast Golems are not evil — just confused about their purpose
- The Vat Master is a yeast colony that achieved consciousness and now believes it IS the brewery
- The solution isn't destruction but negotiation (or destruction, player's choice)

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Brewery tourism, the "beer experience" industry
- **Secondary Targets**: Dublin's brewing heritage commodification, tour gift shops
- **Cultural Specificity**: Guinness Storehouse parody, Dublin brewing legacy
- **Humor Approach**: Taking brewing reverence to absurd literal extremes
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Celebrates brewing craft while satirizing its tourism packaging

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: 25 Sovs tours that end in a gift shop (standard industry practice)
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: The "yeast situation" cover story vs. reality
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who just wanted the free tasting pint
- **Comedy of Errors**: Tour going horribly wrong, foam physics disasters
- **Consequence System**: Dungeon rewards, "Brewmaster's Pass" unlocks

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Standard brewery tour, free pint at the end

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Dungeon crawl through sentient yeast territory

**How impaired are they**: Usually sober on entry (for once), not sober by end

**The tour derailment**:
- "And here we see the fermentation vats— wait, that vat is MOVING"
- "The yeast is supposed to be IN the beer, not FORMING A HUMANOID SHAPE"
- "This isn't part of the tour. This is DEFINITELY not part of the tour."
- "Does the 25 Sovs cover hazard pay?"

**The transformation**:
- Entered as tourists
- Exit as dungeon survivors
- Somehow still bought something at the gift shop

**How locals interact**: Staff are either hiding, infected, or the secret resistance

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Tour Mode (Non-Combat)
**Standard Tour Path:**
1. Welcome Center — History, ticket validation
2. Malt House — Grain education, samples
3. Mash Tuns — Process explanation
4. Fermentation Halls — "The magic happens here"
5. Tasting Room — Sample pints
6. Gift Shop — Commerce conclusion

**Tour Mechanics:**
- Guided rails, no combat
- Information gathering (useful for dungeon)
- NPC conversations
- Hints about "maintenance issues"
- Free pint at tasting room
- Gift shop purchases available

### Dungeon Mode (Combat/Exploration)
**Activation:** Quest trigger or choosing to investigate "staff only" areas

**Floor-by-Floor Breakdown:**

**Floor 2 — Malt House**
- **Enemies**: Grain Golems (basic, swarm-type)
- **Puzzle**: Conveyor belt navigation
- **Mini-Boss**: The Miller (animated milling equipment)
- **Reward**: Grain-based consumables, equipment

**Floor 3 — Mash Tuns**
- **Enemies**: Mash Monsters (emerge from vats)
- **Hazard**: Steam vents (timing puzzle)
- **Puzzle**: Temperature control (too hot = damage, too cold = spawns)
- **Reward**: Temperature-resistant gear

**Floor 4 — Fermentation Halls**
- **Enemies**: Yeast Golems (main enemy type)
- **Hazard**: Foam floods, CO2 pockets
- **Puzzle**: Vat drainage sequence
- **Secret**: Optional "friendly" yeast colony (diplomacy route)
- **Reward**: Foam-proof equipment, yeast samples

**Floor 5 — Barrel Aging Rooms**
- **Enemies**: Barrel Wraiths (veteran, high damage)
- **Hazard**: Rolling barrels, fire pockets
- **Puzzle**: "Angel's Share" evaporation navigation
- **Reward**: Aged whiskey consumables, premium equipment

**Floor 6 — Boss Arena**
- **Boss**: The Vat Master
- **Phase 1**: Yeast swarm management
- **Phase 2**: Direct confrontation
- **Phase 3**: Environmental hazard + boss combo
- **Victory Options**: Defeat OR negotiate (different rewards)

### The Vat Master Boss Fight
**Lore**: A 150-year-old yeast colony that achieved consciousness and believes it IS the brewery, responsible for all beer ever made here.

**Phase 1 — The Swarm**
- Vat Master summons Yeast Golem waves
- Must thin the horde to trigger Phase 2
- Vat Master is invulnerable during this phase
- Special Attack: "Fermentation Wave" (party-wide DoT)

**Phase 2 — The Confrontation**
- Vat Master manifests physical form
- Standard boss combat with special moves
- "Foam Tsunami" — arena-wide dodge check
- "CO2 Burst" — targeted suffocation
- "Living Culture" — healing via yeast absorption

**Phase 3 — The Meltdown**
- Brewery systems go haywire
- Environmental hazards + boss attacks
- Steam vents, conveyor hazards, falling barrels
- Vat Master becomes desperate, attack increase

**Victory Options:**
- **Defeat**: Destroy the Vat Master, standard dungeon completion
- **Negotiate**: Talk down the Vat Master, unlock "Brewing Partner" ending
  - Requires finding the "friendly" yeast colony on Floor 4
  - Different dialogue options
  - Vat Master becomes ally, different rewards
