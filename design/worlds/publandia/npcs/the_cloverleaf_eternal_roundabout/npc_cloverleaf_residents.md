# Chef Maeve Flanagan - The Food Truck

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `publandia_npc_maeve_flanagan_01`
- **Display Name**: Maeve
- **Full Name**: Chef Maeve Flanagan
- **Title**: Operator, The Cloverleaf Kitchen
- **Role**: Food Vendor / Life Support / Practical Survivor
- **Duration Circling**: 8 years
- **Primary Location**: The Cloverleaf (Outer Lane)
- **Vehicle**: Food truck - "The Cloverleaf Kitchen"

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Doing
Running the only food service in The Cloverleaf. She keeps everyone alive. Before she arrived, three residents died of malnutrition. Since she started operating, zero deaths from hunger. "The roundabout provides" is literal—she IS the provision.

### The Comedy
She runs her food truck like a normal business despite circling a roundabout eternally. She has a schedule. She has suppliers (the lorry that circles Wednesdays). She has a MENU.

# Seán "The Exit" Murphy

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `publandia_npc_sean_murphy_exit_01`
- **Display Name**: Seán
- **Full Name**: Seán Murphy
- **Title**: The One Who Could Leave (But Doesn't)
- **Role**: Existential Horror / Philosopher / Escape Guide (Ironic)
- **Duration Circling**: 25 years (by choice)
- **Primary Location**: The Cloverleaf (Middle Lane)
- **Vehicle**: 1999 Nissan Micra (green, rusted)

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Doing
Circling. Forever. By choice. Seán KNOWS which exit leads where. He could leave at any time. He never does.

### The Horror
Seán represents the terror of comfort zones. The roundabout is predictable. Safe. Eternal. The world outside? Chaos. Uncertainty. Endings. Seán has the knowledge and the ability to leave. He stays anyway. This is the game's most genuinely unsettling character.

---

## 👤 Appearance

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age Apparent | 60s |
| Build | Slight, comfortable |
| Clothing | Flat cap, wool jumper (traditional) |
| Expression | Serene, knowing, unsettling contentment |
| Eyes | Have seen every exit. Chose none. |

---

## 💬 Dialogue

### Greeting
> "New, are ye? Ye have the look. The desperate look. Ye'll lose that eventually."

### The Knowledge
> "Exit 6. That's the one you want. Leads straight to the Cliffs of Moher. Lovely, I hear."

> "I know every exit. Where they go. What they lead to. I've studied them for 25 years."

### The Choice
> "Why don't I take it? *long pause* Out there... anything could happen. In here, I know what comes next. Another circle. Then another."

> "Freedom's overrated, lad. The world out there? Chaos. In here? Predictable. Safe. Eternal."

> "I could leave today. Tomorrow. Any time I want."
> *drives past Exit 6 without slowing*
> "...Just not today."

### Philosophy
> "The roundabout teaches you something. That destination is illusion. The journey is eternal. Why fight it?"

> "People think I'm trapped. I think I'm free. Free from decisions. Free from change. Free from... endings."

> "Every circle is the same. And that's beautiful. No surprises. No disappointments. Just... circles."

### If Players Challenge Him
> "You want to know why? Really want to know?"
> *pause*
> "Because out there, things END. Relationships end. Jobs end. LIVES end. In here? Nothing ends. Not ever."

> "Call me a coward if ye like. I call myself... liberated."

---

## 📅 Schedule

| Time | Lane | Activity |
|------|------|----------|
| Always | Middle | Circling, contemplating, avoiding exits |

### Pattern Note
Seán drives past Exit 6 exactly once every 4 minutes. He looks at it every time. He never takes it.

---

## 🎯 Narrative Function

### Existential Horror Element
Seán is the scariest character in The Cloverleaf—not because of what he does, but because of what he represents. The bros can BECOME Seán if they stay too long. The comfort of routine. The fear of change. The eternal loop.

### Player Reaction Design
Players should feel:
1. Initial curiosity (he knows the exit!)
2. Growing discomfort (why doesn't he take it?)
3. Genuine unease (is he right? Is routine better than chaos?)
4. Relief upon escaping (we almost became him)

# The Murphy Family

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `publandia_npc_murphy_family_01`
- **Role**: Recently Trapped Tourists / New Arrival Reference Point
- **Duration Circling**: 3 days
- **Lane**: Outer (confused)
- **Vehicle**: Rental people carrier, stuffed with luggage

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

The Murphy family represents what the bros COULD become if they stayed. Three days in, they're already adapting. The children faster than the adults. Da still refuses to admit they're lost.

---

## 👥 Family Members

### Da (Father)
- Refuses to acknowledge being lost
- Keeps trying random exits
- "We're NOT lost, we're just... exploring"

### Ma (Mother)
- Has accepted their new life
- Already joined tour bus committees
- "This is fine. Everything is fine."

### The Kids (×3)
- Ages 8-14
- Adapted in 24 hours
- Find it "actually kind of cool"
- Already friends with bus children

### Gran
- Fell asleep when they entered
- Woke up on Day 2
- Still confused about location
- "Are we in Kerry?"

---

## 💬 Dialogue

### Da
> "We're NOT lost. I just need to find the right— there! No, wait. That wasn't it."

> "I'm sure we passed that... no, that's a different Celtic cross."

> "The GPS says recalculating. It's BEEN recalculating for three days."

### Ma
> "This is fine. This is all fine. The children are learning valuable life lessons about... perseverance."

> "The nice lady on the bus is very organized. They have committees! It's quite impressive, really."

> "I've always wanted to simplify our lives. This is... extreme. But simplified."

### Kids
> "Are we there yet?"

> "This is actually kind of cool."

> "Can we live here forever?"

> "The bus kids say there's movie night on Fridays!"

### Gran
> "...Are we in Kerry?"

> "I had a lovely nap. Where are we now?"

> "Is this the scenic route?"

> "I remember roundabouts being smaller."

# Old Padraig - The Farmer

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `publandia_npc_padraig_farmer_01`
- **Display Name**: Old Padraig
- **Role**: Accidental Resident / Rural Ireland Parody
- **Duration Circling**: 2 years
- **Lane**: Creates his own (cuts across grass)
- **Vehicle**: Ancient tractor, sheep occasionally visible in trailer

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

Padraig was moving sheep across the roundabout. He took a wrong turn. He's been waiting for "a gap" in traffic for two years. There is no gap. He seems fine with this.

---

## 👤 Appearance

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|

| Build | Farmer sturdy |
| Clothing | Flat cap, wellington boots, work clothes |
| Expression | Infinite patience |
| Vehicle | Tractor older than the roundabout itself |

---

## 💬 Dialogue

### Greeting
> "Grand day for it. Well, grand enough."

### About His Situation
> "Was moving the sheep across. Took a wrong turn. Here we are."

> "I'll leave eventually. Just waiting for a gap."
> *has been waiting for a gap for 2 years*

### Philosophy
> "There's always a gap coming. Just be patient. Very patient."

> "Two years isn't long. My father waited three years for a good harvest once."

> "The grass here is decent. The sheep have no complaints."
