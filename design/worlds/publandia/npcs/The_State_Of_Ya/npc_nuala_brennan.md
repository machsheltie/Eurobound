# NPC Profile: Nuala Brennan

*"I'm not harsh. I'm ACCURATE. Harsh would be lying."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `publandia_npc_nuala_brennan_01`
- **Display Name**: Nuala
- **Internal Name**: Nuala Brennan — proprietor, The State of Ya
- **Role**: Vendor (General Store) / Service Provider (State Assessment) / Recurring Commentary / Soft Quest Giver
- **Associated Quest**: `publandia_sidequest_nualas_approval_01` ("Nuala's Approval")
- **Primary Location**: The State of Ya, The Craic Tax, Publandia
- **Secondary Appearances**: Doorway of the shop during any Craic Tax street event; referenced by name in The Bunk & Blunder reception dialogue; background presence in the morning-after crowd shots
- **Family**: One of the Publandia Brennans. Her cousin's girl **Orla Brennan** went for a drive in 2012 and has been Community Coordinator of the Cloverleaf tour bus ever since. Nuala gets a Christmas card every year. She keeps them all in a drawer behind the counter and has never once discussed it with a customer.

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing

Running a corner shop at the mouth of The Craic Tax for eighteen years, at the exact point where the district empties out its casualties. She stocks aspirin, grease, electrolytes, and a small armoury of things she'd rather not think about, because the demand is absolutely reliable and she is an excellent buyer.

The famous "assessment" is retail triage. She has watched roughly a hundred thousand people walk through that door in every possible condition, and she can now sort a new arrival into one of six categories before the bell finishes ringing. Then she tells them, because telling them is faster than the four minutes they'd otherwise spend pretending, and time is money.

She is not cruel. She is the last person in Publandia who will look at you directly.

### What Bros Believe

Lord Pilsner believes she has "a really specific vibe" and that he is "wearing her down." Chadwick believes she is a content goldmine and has been told, once, with a phone in his hand, exactly what she thinks about that. Bradley believes — correctly, and to his enormous quiet satisfaction — that Nuala considers him the only one with sense, and he has begun doing the shopping for all three.

None of them have understood that she is describing them accurately. They hear the tone and miss the content. Lord Pilsner has been told he is green and swaying and has come away feeling *seen*.

### The Comedy Gap

The bros have spent three cities being flattered by people who wanted their money. Nuala takes their money and insults them and they cannot stop coming back, and they cannot articulate why. The joke is that honesty reads to them as an exotic local custom — Chadwick has captioned a photo of her shop "Irish people are SO direct 😂" — when in fact a woman is telling three grown men that they are unwell and they are choosing to hear a bit.

Underneath the whole thing: she is right every time, she stocks exactly what they need before they know they need it, and she would be genuinely upset if they stopped coming in.

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |

| Build | Compact, still, economical — she does not come out from behind the counter |
| Height | Short. Irrelevant. The counter is on a raised step and she chose that. |

### Clothing
- **Practical cardigan, Judgement Purple trim**: The shop uniform for eighteen years, replaced four times, always the same cut. It has a pen in the left pocket and has never had anything in the right.
- **Reading glasses on a chain**: The most expressive object in the shop. Up on the head for chat, down on the nose for the assessment, off entirely for the rare kindness.
- **Accessories**: A biro she does not lend; a small transistor radio behind the till that is always on and never audible; a laminated price list she has not needed since 2011.
- **Footwear**: Orthopaedic, unglamorous, correct. Eighteen years of standing.

### Distinguishing Features
- The glance-up. Door bell, one look, full diagnosis, roughly 1.2 seconds. This is the character.
- Sighs constantly and at volume, entirely without malice — the shop's actual ambient sound
- Folds her arms while you decide, and does not fill the silence
- Points with two fingers, never one, always at the correct aisle, never looks where she's pointing
- The rare smile crosses her whole face and vanishes in under a second, and she is embarrassed by it

### Sprite Notes

The direction in `The_State_Of_Ya_PNG_Assets.md` is exactly right and must be enforced: **the deadpan does the comedy; the sprites must never mug.** The eye roll is three frames, not a cartoon. If she plays broad, she becomes a scold, and the entire "brutal honesty as care" reading collapses into meanness.

Harsh fluorescent from directly overhead on everything except the Healing Aisle, which is a half-stop softer — that lighting difference is the only place the game ever admits she's being kind. The Rare Smile sprite is an achievement asset; it should be visibly warmer in colour temperature than every other frame she has.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype

**The Honest Broker** — Nuala's operating principle is that everyone in this district is being lied to constantly, by publicans, by tour operators, by their own friends, and by themselves, and that somebody in the chain ought to just say the thing. She is not a therapist and has no interest in being one. She is a supplier with a diagnostic habit. The care is real but it arrives sideways, embedded in a correct product recommendation, and she would deny it under oath.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Diagnostically accurate | Six recognised states, called on sight, never wrong in eighteen years |
| Constitutionally honest | Cannot deliver a comforting untruth; has tried; it comes out wrong |
| Unsentimental affection | Cares enormously, expresses it exclusively through stock levels and discounts |
| Utterly unshockable | "What did you EAT? No, don't tell me. I don't want to know." |
| Commercially sharp | Sells the 15 Sovs kit to a man who came in for a 3 Sovs aspirin, correctly, every time |
| Boundaried | No phones in the shop. No details. No moralising about the fake ID. Lines she does not cross. |

### Internal Monologue

> "Door goes. Right — green one first, that's your classic four-in-the-morning kebab, he's for the Recovery Kit and he'll argue about it for thirty seconds and then he'll buy it. Camera lad behind him, phone up, that's getting dealt with. Third one's already in aisle two, reading the label, doing the sums. God love him, he's the only reason the other two are still walking. Twenty-eight Sovs off the three and they'll be back tomorrow. They're always back tomorrow. Sure, where else would they go."

### Motivation

Eighteen years in, the shop is not the point and hasn't been for a long time. The point is that The Craic Tax is a machine that processes people and somebody at the end of it ought to look at them properly. She'd never say that. If asked she would say she's a shopkeeper with good stock control, which is also completely true.

The card from the roundabout goes in the drawer every December and she says nothing about it to anyone.

### Positioning Notes
- Never leaves the counter during trading hours; the raised step means she's at eye level with everyone who comes in and above everyone who's slouching
- The counter sits on the diagonal from the door, so the glance-up happens before the customer has fully entered — the assessment is architectural
- The Healing Aisle is nearest the door on the left because that is where people go first and she has never once had to redirect a hungover person
- Behind-counter stock is visible but unlabelled; it registers as scenery until Known Disaster status makes it interactive

### Hidden Details / Easter Eggs
- **Buy evening prep, then return next morning for recovery on the same in-game day** — she does not comment, does not react, and simply hands over the aspirin she set aside. The absence of a line is the joke. Achievement: "Consistent."
- **Examine the drawer behind the counter** at Known Disaster status: fourteen Christmas cards, banded together. No dialogue. No examine text beyond "Cards. Banded. Not yours."
- **Chalkboard changes daily**: "Today's Shame Special: 2-for-1 Aspirin & Dignity" is the default; nine other variants rotate, all pricing dignity at zero.
- **Try to film in the shop** and the phone is put away, permanently, for the rest of that visit. Chadwick has learned nothing from this and tries every time.
- **Make her genuinely laugh** — a narrow window requiring a specific self-deprecating dialogue option chosen while at Known Disaster status and visibly wrecked. +25 Bravado, "Nuala Approved," and the Rare Smile sprite. It fires once per playthrough.
- **The door sign reads "Come In, We Already Know"** and can be examined from outside, before she has ever seen you.

## 🎯 Quest Integration

### Primary Quest
- **Quest**: Nuala's Approval (`publandia_sidequest_nualas_approval_01`)
- **Role**: Target — the player attempts to do something that genuinely impresses her
- **Trigger**: Reaching Known Disaster status (5 visits)
- **Outcome**: Requires the player to return an item they didn't need, refuse a sale they could have made her complicit in, or come in sober and prepared on a night she expected carnage. Grants "Nuala Approved" status and the line: *"You did good. I don't say that often. Remember it."*

### Quest Gate Function

Nuala gates nothing on the critical path. She is a supply node and blocks no progression — the design intent stated in the location file is explicit: "Cannot fail (Nuala won't let you leave unprepared)." What she gates is *quality of life*: the behind-counter stock (Personal Remedy, "Didn't Happen" Kit, Emergency Complete Reset) is the strongest consumable tier in Publandia and is locked behind five visits, which is a soft encouragement to keep coming back rather than a wall.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent

Dublin, north side, entirely unperformed. Fast, low, and completely unbothered. No lilt, no music, no charm offensive — the whole point of Nuala is that she is the one person in the city not doing a bit for tourists. If the read has any twinkle in it, it's wrong.

### Tone
- **Assessment**: Diagnostic. The tone of a nurse at triage who has done eleven of these already this morning.
- **Recommendations**: Instructional and final. She is not selling; she is dispensing.
- **Offensive Aisle**: Carefully neutral, with the smallest possible pause before the price.
- **Philosophical**: Slightly slower, still unsentimental. She is explaining her business model, not confessing.
- **Rare Kindness**: Quieter, quicker, and immediately followed by something brisk to cover it.
- **Rare Smile**: Genuine, loud, and cut short by her own embarrassment.

### Key Delivery Notes
- "The state of ya" is the character's signature and must sound like concern, not contempt — the Irish usage is worry wearing a scowl. If it reads as a sneer, the whole shop turns mean.
- The three-second stare is a performance beat with no line in it. Do not fill it.
- "Jaysus" is punctuation, not blasphemy; throw it away.
- Never let her raise her voice. Not once. The volume ceiling is the sigh.
- "You did good. I don't say that often." should be the softest thing she says in the entire game and should sound like it cost her something.

## 📝 Development Notes

- **The assessment must fire on every single entry, without exception.** The moment the player can enter the shop unremarked, Nuala stops being a mechanic and becomes a vendor menu. It is the cheapest recurring joke in the game and it never gets old because the input changes.
- The recommendation engine must read live status effects, not a scripted table — she has to actually be right or the character is a fraud
- Tone guardrail for all future writers: **concern wearing a scowl.** Every line should be defensible as something a worried person would say. If a proposed line is only cruel, it's not Nuala.
- The Rare Kindness pool should fire on a low, invisible probability that increases with visit count — players should feel they earned it without ever seeing a counter
- The Christmas card drawer is deliberately unexplained; do not add examine text, do not add a quest, do not let any NPC mention it. It exists so that a second playthrough recontextualises her.
- She must never be angry. Unimpressed, unsurprised, and occasionally concerned — but the shop has no anger state and should not get one.
- The "your dignity's not something I stock" line and the chalkboard special are the same joke; do not let them fire in the same visit
- Voice priority: HIGH. Nuala carries the entire tonal counterweight to Publandia's charm-offensive NPCs.
