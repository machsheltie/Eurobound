## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Presiding, beaming, over a "brewery" that has not brewed anything in living memory — Trinity's
Academic Amber arrives quietly by truck from The Stoutworks Brewery as a contract brew
[source: design/worlds/publandia/trinity_of_pints.md L359]. Malachy's actual job is
ceremony: he keeps the taps polished, wears full academic regalia at bar-side, confers the
passport stamp at graduation, and poses for the selfie holding the opener and the beer he
did not make. He may or may not know the truck exists. Nobody has ever asked him. He has
never wondered.

### What Bros Believe
That Malachy is a master craftsman-scholar, the living source of "real European brewing
knowledge," and that his stamp is an academic credential with international standing.

### The Comedy Gap
Trinity's entire faculty is fraud with footnotes — the Dean cheats at beer pong, the degrees
are drinking games — and at the center of it stands the one man with zero deception in him,
because Malachy isn't hiding that he doesn't brew; he has simply never connected the dots.
The Dean's rage-fueled meltdown happens meters away and Malachy's read on it is that
commencement week is always a bit emotional.

he holds the bottle opener in one hand and the Academic Amber in the other, both raised,

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Oblivious Institution** — Malachy IS Trinity of Pints the way a bell is a church. He
believes completely in the ceremony, the regalia, the sanctity of the stamp, and none of the
questions. His sincerity is structural: the venue's academic cosplay works because one man
inside it isn't pretending.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Ceremonious | Everything gets the full liturgy — stamps, refills, mopping |
| Oblivious | The magnet scandal, the brawl, the vomiting: "spirited class, this year" |
| Warm | Genuinely delighted by every graduate, including the ones mooning the camera |
| Proud | Of "his" beer; describes the brewing process entirely in gestures |
| Unhurried | Has never once rushed a ceremony; the stamp lands when it lands |

### Internal Monologue
> "Lovely bunch, this class. Bit of energy about them. The tall one's crying — happens every
> year, the emotion of it. Now. Ink. Steady hand, Malachy. This part matters."

### Motivation
The ceremony. Somebody must confer things, and conferring is the finest work a man can do
with dry hands.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**Bros approach the taps (pre-challenge)**:
> "Welcome, welcome! Prospective students! You've the look of scholars about you — thirsty
> ones, the best kind. The curriculum starts at that table and ends at this tap."

**Follow-up**:
> "Academic Amber, our own — *gestures broadly at the entire building* — creation. Brewed
> in-house, in the sense that this is a house, and there is beer in it."

### Key Interaction Dialogue — the Graduation Ceremony (author-dictated beat)
*Podium. Bros in robes. The Dean seethes beside him; Malachy beams. He stamps the passport —
see The Stamping below — then hands over the bottle opener and the Academic Amber, holding
both up for the selfie, obliviously smiling. Bradley is vomiting on the Dean's shoes.*

**Brewmaster Malachy** (to Bradley, warmly):
> "Overcome, is he? It takes some that way. Commencement week — always a bit emotional."

### The Stamping (⚠ AUTHOR-REVIEW — Gap #12; backfills the stamp item file on approval)
*He raises the stamp like a ceremonial mace, adjusts his robes:*
> "By the power vested in the tap since 1854 — hold still, lads — *STAMP* — Trinity of Pints
> confers upon you everything it legally can, which is this stamp. Ten cups defended and not
> a drop of learning spilled! Welcome to the alumni, gentlemen!"

### Stamp Animation (⚠ AUTHOR-REVIEW — Gap #10; backfills the stamp item file on approval)
The navy mortarboard stamp descends in three slow pomp-and-circumstance beats (tiny brass
sting on each), the tassel flicking ink left-to-right across the cap face as it lands; on
the fourth frame a scrap of rolled-diploma confetti pops from the seal. (Fits the existing
64x64x4 sheet spec in `stamp_trinity_of_pints_item.md`.)

### Post-Interaction
> "Alumni drink at alumni rates! Which are the same rates. But said with more affection."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*(The ceremony is scripted; if the bros try to leave early:)*
> "Ah-ah — no one leaves mid-conferral. It's terrible luck and worse manners. HOLD... still."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "The graduates return! Show us the passport, go on. *taps the Trinity stamp, misty* Finest
> ink of my career, that one."

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Publandian (Irish-parody), rounded and slow — every sentence arrives pre-warmed.

- Ceremony: full liturgical gravity, zero irony

- The stamp speech builds to "*STAMP*" as its crescendo — the brass sting carries the beat
