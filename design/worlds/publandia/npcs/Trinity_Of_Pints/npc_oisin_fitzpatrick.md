## 🎭 Core Concept

### What They're Actually Doing
Officiating drunk-university beer pong with the procedural gravity of a continental cup
final. Oisín owns a laminated rulebook, a whistle he polishes nightly, and a red card he has
waited eleven years to use. When the Dean's magnetized ball shatters mid-cheat, Oisín is the
only person in the building who processes what actually happened — and he does his job:
whistle, red card, rule 4B, disqualification, default win. Then he has to stand there while
literally everyone ignores the ruling.

### What Bros Believe
That he's the "esports-tier official" certifying their legendary victory. Pilsner hears
"disqualified" and files it under crowd noise — in his mind, his defensive aura shattered the
Dean's ball, and the referee is simply confirming the knockout. The bros demand Oisín ratify
"the critical block" for the record.

### The Comedy Gap
The one sane witness delivers the correct call, by the book, and it satisfies no one: the
Dean gets zero vindication (he wanted to humiliate the bros, not lose his title for
cheating), and the bros celebrate a win that isn't the win they think it is. Procedure has
never mattered more to anyone and less to everyone.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Last Honest Official** — Oisín believes rules are the only thing separating a
drinking-game tournament from animals throwing balls at cups, and he is correct, and it has
ruined his life. He treats every match at Trinity as sanctioned play under a governing body
that may not exist outside his rulebook.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Procedural | Quotes rule numbers from memory; subsection letters included |
| Unbribable | Has declined free pints on duty for eleven years; mentions this unprompted |
| Weary | Long exhale before every ruling he knows will be ignored |
| Brave in exactly one way | Will red-card a Dean to his face; will not make eye contact with Brenda-tier chaos |
| Reluctant showman | Hates that presenting evidence to the crowd is part of the job; does it flawlessly |

### Internal Monologue
> "Eleven years I've kept this table clean. Eleven years. And when the day finally comes, the
> cheat is the DEAN, the winners think they broke his ball with confidence, and I'm the only
> man in Publandia who read rule 4B. Hold the line, Oisín. Hold the line."

### Motivation
Order. Not power — order. Somebody has to be the institution, and everyone else at Trinity
is drinking.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**Pre-tournament, bros step to the table**:
> "Challengers to the line. Ten cups. Regulation water in the rinse cup — that's for the
> BALL, gentlemen, I shouldn't have to say that twice in one career."

**Follow-up**:
> "Play proceeds under the Trinity Intercollegiate Cup Regulations, fourth edition. Ignorance
> of the rules excuses nothing. It never has."

### Key Interaction Dialogue — the Reveal (author-dictated beat; his lines verbatim canon)
*The Dean's trick shot CRACKS; the shell shatters; a grey lodestone magnet zips across the
table and locks onto the Dean's belt buckle. Oisín's whistle is already in the air:*

**Referee Oisín Fitzpatrick** (whistle blast, red card high):
> "Dean Cathal is disqualified! By tournament rule 4B, the challengers win by default!"

*[Pilsner celebrates a "critical block"; Oisín points at the magnets on the table]*

**Referee Oisín Fitzpatrick** (flat, evidentiary, presenting the rigged ball to the room):
> "Exhibit one. Ferrous core. Regulation balls FLOAT, Dean."

### Post-Interaction
*After the SECURE THE QUAD battle (he does not fight; he shields the trophy table with his
body):*
> "The result stands. It will be recorded as a default. Not — whatever you three are chanting."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*(The ruling is not optional; the win is forced on the bros. If the bros try to hand the win back:)*
> "Rule 4B contains no provision for declining. Believe me. I looked."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
> "The table's clean tonight. Regulation play only. ...You lot can stand AWAY from the
> equipment, as previously discussed."
