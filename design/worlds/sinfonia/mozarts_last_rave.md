# Mozart's Last Rave

*Where the dead literally rise to drop beats — an underground crypt-club beneath the historic cemetery where classical music and EDM collide in eternal torment*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: sinfonia_cemetery_mozarts_last_rave_01
- **Display Name**: Mozart's Last Rave
- **German Alias**: "Mozarts Letzte Raserei"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "He Died at 35 for THIS"
- **World Location**: Sinfonia
- **Area Zone**: Beneath Komponistenfriedhof (Composer's Cemetery)
- **Location Type**: Underground Crypt-Club / Dungeon / Rhythm Combat Zone / Secret Venue

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 11 PM - 5 AM (the dead don't party before midnight)
- **Stat Requirements**: +1 Bravado to enter crypt entrance
- **Item Requirements**: 
  - "Composer's Rest" Cemetery Visitor Pass (5 Sovs, acquired aboveground)
  - OR knowledge of secret entrance from NPC
- **Previous Location Requirements**: Must have heard rumors at The Moderato Pub OR Café Existenz
- **Special Conditions**: Must find the correct grave to enter (hint: it's NOT Mozart's actual grave)

### Finding the Entrance
The club entrance is a satirical puzzle itself:
1. Tourist NPCs crowd around "Mozart's Grave" (wrong grave)
2. Locals smirk and say nothing helpful
3. The ACTUAL entrance: A grave marked "Johann Bassmeister, 1756-1791" (fictional composer who "invented the bass drop")
4. Knock three times on the headstone in rhythm (any rhythm works, but wrong rhythm = comedy dialogue)

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Back through the crypts (if you can navigate them)
- **Completion Requirements**: Survive the full rave, defeat or appease the Undead DJ
- **Failure States**: "Danced to Death" sends you back to cemetery surface with Exhausted status
- **Progressive Unlocks**: VIP Crypt access after first completion, Bone Throne selfie spot

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Mozart's Last Rave satirizes the commercialization of classical music legacy AND the absurdity of themed nightclub concepts. The club literally desecrates musical heritage by turning a cemetery into a rave — and everyone involved thinks it's perfectly normal.

The deeper joke: Mozart would probably have LOVED this. He was known for crude humor and partying. The "desecration" is that they're actually honoring his spirit more than the stuffy opera tourists ever could.

### The Undead DJ Mythology
According to club lore (told by slightly-too-serious bartender):
- Johann Bassmeister was a contemporary of Mozart
- He "invented" the bass drop but was never credited
- He died of "excessive rhythm" at age 35 (same as Mozart)
- His ghost returns nightly to "finish what he started"
- The other undead DJs are his "orchestra"

Everyone plays along. Whether the undead are real, hallucinations, or very good performers is deliberately ambiguous.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Classical music commercialization, death tourism
- **Secondary Targets**: Themed nightclub absurdity, "underground" venues that are actually tourist traps
- **Cultural Specificity**: Vienna's composer graves tourism, Mozart merchandise culture
- **Humor Approach**: Deadpan commitment to absurd premise
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Treats death with irreverence, not disrespect; no real composer graves mocked

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling "authentic underground experience" that's actually well-organized tourist attraction
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: 5 Sovs "cemetery pass" is basically club cover; "tips for the dead" go to owners
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros think they've discovered a SECRET; it's on TripAdvisor with 4.2 stars
- **Comedy of Errors**: Taking the undead premise too seriously OR not seriously enough both cause problems
- **Consequence System**: Rhythm failures summon additional "undead" enemies; social failures = ghostly mockery

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios

**What bros THINK is happening**:

**What is ACTUALLY happening**:

**The Morning After**:

- **Reality**: They danced with theater students in costume earning 15 Sovs/hour

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Rhythm Combat System — "Dance with the Dead"

**Core Mechanic**:
- All combat in Mozart's Last Rave is rhythm-based
- Attacks land on beat = bonus damage
- Defenses timed to bass drops = bonus mitigation
- Missing the beat = "Off-Key" debuff (reduced accuracy)

**Rhythm Difficulty Tiers**:
- **Bone Bar**: Slow tempo, forgiving timing (tutorial zone)
- **Dancefloor**: Medium tempo, moderate windows
- **Requiem Room**: Variable tempo, tight windows
- **Conductor's Crypt**: Boss-controlled tempo, adaptive difficulty

**Combo System**:
- Consecutive on-beat actions build "Harmony Meter"
- Full meter = "Crescendo Mode" (all attacks critical)
- Breaking rhythm resets meter and summons hecklers

### The Elemental Undead DJs (Mini-Bosses)

Four DJ booths, four undead DJs, each controlling a music element:

**DJ Grave-o (Bass)**
- **Location**: North booth
- **Attack Pattern**: Heavy bass drops create floor shockwaves
- **Weakness**: High-frequency attacks
- **Dialogue**: "Feel the BASS... feel it in your BONES... because I don't have any FLESH."

**DJ Soprano Specter (Treble)**
- **Location**: East booth
- **Attack Pattern**: Ear-piercing high notes that cause Dazed
- **Weakness**: Bass attacks interrupt her
- **Dialogue**: "My voice could shatter GLASS. Your skull is... slightly thicker."

**DJ Tempo Mortis (Rhythm)**
- **Location**: South booth
- **Attack Pattern**: Time-distortion effects, slows party movement
- **Weakness**: Attacks during tempo changes
- **Dialogue**: "Time is RELATIVE. Death is ABSOLUTE. And my beats are ETERNAL."

**DJ Harmony Haunt (Melody)**
- **Location**: West booth
- **Attack Pattern**: Charming melodies that confuse party targeting
- **Weakness**: Dissonant attacks
- **Dialogue**: "Such BEAUTIFUL chaos... let me add some STRUCTURE... to your SUFFERING."

### The Undead DJ (Main Boss)

**Boss Profile — Johann Bassmeister**:
- **Title**: The Undead DJ, Ghost of Groove
- **Appearance**: Skeleton in 18th-century formal wear with modern DJ headphones
- **Personality**: Theatrical, bitter about Mozart getting all the credit
- **Domain**: Pipe organ converted to massive synthesizer

**Boss Introduction**:
"Ah, more TOURISTS! Come to dance on the graves of GENIUS? Mozart got the FAME. I got the BASS. Let me show you what REAL music sounds like... from BEYOND THE GRAVE!"

**Boss Phases**:

*Phase 1 — The Warmup (100-66% HP)*
- **Tempo**: 120 BPM, steady
- **Attacks**:
  - "Ghostly Groove": Party must match rhythm or take damage
  - "Bass from the Grave": Ground-pound AOE on bass drops
- **Mechanic**: Learn his patterns, find the rhythm
- **Dialogue**: "Not bad for MORTALS! But can you keep up when I CHANGE THE TEMPO?!"

*Phase 2 — The Breakdown (66-33% HP)*
- **Tempo**: Fluctuates 80-160 BPM
- **Attacks**:
  - "Tempo Terror": Sudden tempo shifts that scramble timing
  - "Skeleton Shuffle": Summons dancing skeleton adds
  - "Drop of Death": Massive bass drop with delayed damage
- **Mechanic**: Adapt to tempo changes, deal with adds
- **Dialogue**: "Mozart WISHED he could drop bass like THIS! History ROBBED me!"

*Phase 3 — The Finale (33-0% HP)*
- **Tempo**: Boss-controlled, reactive to player performance
- **Attacks**:
  - "Eternal Encore": Loops damage if party doesn't break cycle
  - "Symphony of Suffering": Uses all four elemental DJ attacks
  - "Last Rave": Desperation attack, massive damage but long wind-up
- **Mechanic**: Turn his music against him (use pipe organ during wind-up)
- **Dialogue**: "If I can't be REMEMBERED... I'll make sure you NEVER FORGET!"

**Alternative Victory — Appeasement**:
Instead of defeating Bassmeister, players can:
1. Find the "Lost Composition" hidden in the crypt
2. Present it during Phase 3
3. Bassmeister realizes his music WAS preserved
4. He grants rewards peacefully and becomes a vendor

**Dialogue (Appeased)**:
"You... found it? My symphony? It SURVIVED? ...Perhaps death has been too loud. Too ANGRY. Thank you. Dance here anytime. The dead... approve of you."

**Rewards (Either Victory)**:
- **Bone Conductor's Gloves** (Accessory: +10% rhythm game timing, +3 Charm)
- **Eternal Setlist** (Key item: unlocks Requiem Room VIP access)
- **"RIP Your Sobriety"** (Consumable recipe: powerful but risky drink)
- **Undead DJ's Blessing** (Passive: +5% damage when attacking on-beat, permanent)
