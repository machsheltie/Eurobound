# Author Direction: The Opera Divas — Drag Show & Couchsurf Chronicle (Sinfonia)

**Status:** Canon — author ruling (2026-07-31). Governs the Madame Fortissima and Prima Donna Adelheid NPC profiles, the Bassline Opera House show content, and one Sinfonia Couchsurfing Chronicle entry.

## Ruling

Madame Fortissima and Prima Donna Adelheid are **both kept as separate characters**. They are NOT duplicates.

## The Concept

- The opera house show they headline is an **opera drag show** — the divas are drag performers. This is completely obvious to everyone in Sinfonia and completely invisible to the bros.
- The bros score **backstage passes**, party hard with the ladies late into the night, and get invited back to the divas' **apartment above the opera house**.
- Both divas serve the bros **roofie-laced Jägerbombs** (Jägerbombs alone are already a bro red flag; the lacing makes the night an orchestrated, cartoonishly predatory blur). The bros are so obliterated they clock nothing.
- **Whatever happens in the divas' apartment overnight, happens.** Fade to black.
- Morning-after write-up carries the comedy: waking in an unfamiliar apartment above the opera house, mysterious soreness, a full face of makeup on the pillow next to them, zero memory, total bro-brand misinterpretation of all evidence.

## Integration Requirements

1. **Couchsurfing Chronicles:** this IS one of Sinfonia's couchsurf hookup entries — trigger: backstage passes → afterparty → apartment. The planned cross-dressing/drag hookup slot is fulfilled here.
2. **Buffoon Delusion framing** (per validation template):
   - What bros THINK: two glamorous opera divas are obsessed with them; wildest groupie night of their lives.
   - What's ACTUALLY happening: two seasoned drag queens are running their standard tourist-eating evening, and the bros are the entertainment.
   - Obvious signs ignored: the marquee says the show name (drag pun visible to players), adam's apples, wig lace, the entire audience being in on it, makeup station in the apartment.
3. **Tone:** "The Hangover" meets EarthBound — outrageous, cartoonish, MA-rated. The divas are savvy performers in control, never villains; the bros' obliviousness is the joke.
4. **Both divas need full NPC profiles** (NPC_Profile_Template.md) under `npcs/Bassline_Opera_House/` (or the show venue's folder): stage personas, off-stage personas, the Jägerbomb ritual as a scripted event, morning-after callback dialogue on repeat visits.
5. Substance-comedy props (Jägerbomb trays, and any ketamine gag if used) follow the game's established MA vice-comedy style — referenced in dialogue/aftermath, consequences cartoonish.
