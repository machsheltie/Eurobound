## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Crown & Cask satirizes inherited privilege and aristocratic pretension — specifically how imperial aesthetics get applied to mundane activities. The brewery treats beer with the seriousness of royal succession, complete with lineage, ceremony, and the assumption that breeding (of yeast) matters.

The deeper joke: The "royal family" running the brewery is just one guy and his cousins who decided their great-grandfather's recipes deserved more respect. They've committed so fully to the bit that it's become indistinguishable from genuine belief.

### The Coronation Ceremony
To earn your stamp, you must be "knighted":
1. Approach the throne with purchased beer
2. Kneel (optional but "appreciated")
3. Receive the Royal Brewmaster's blessing
4. Drink from the ceremonial stein
5. Be dubbed "Knight/Dame of the Cask"
6. Receive stamp with appropriate pomp

It's ridiculous. Everyone knows it's ridiculous. Everyone does it anyway.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Habsburg nostalgia, imperial cosplay, inherited authority
- **Secondary Targets**: "Family recipe" mythology, craft beer lineage claims
- **Cultural Specificity**: Austrian imperial history as drinking theme
- **Humor Approach**: Total commitment to absurd formality

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Brewery Tour Integration

**Stamp Unlock Challenge: "The Coronation Ceremony"**

**Challenge Type**: Formal Ritual (QTE + Dialogue)

**Mechanics**:
1. Purchase any Crown & Cask beer
2. Approach the throne
3. Complete approach ritual (simple QTE — don't trip)
4. Respond to Royal Brewmaster's questions appropriately
5. Drink from ceremonial stein without spilling
6. Receive "knighting" and stamp

**Coronation Questions**:
| Question | Best Answer | Acceptable | Failure |
|----------|-------------|------------|---------|
| "Do you swear to honor the brew?" | "With my liver and heart" | "Yes" | "It's just beer" |
| "Will you spread word of our excellence?" | "To all corners of the realm" | "Sure" | "I have a blog" |
| "What sacrifice do you offer?" | "My sobriety" | "Gold" (tip) | "Nothing" |

**Ceremonial Drink**: Must complete without spilling (timing mini-game, easy)

**Success**: Stamp + "Knighted" title + signature beer unlocked + "Noble Blood" buff
**Failure**: "Royal Disappointment" — must buy another beer to retry

## NPCs & Characters

### König Klaus — The Royal Brewmaster
- **Role**: Stamp giver, ceremony conductor, self-appointed royalty
- **Appearance**: Ermine-trimmed robe (fake), crown (plastic but dignified), scepter (actually a tap handle)
- **Personality**: Believes completely in the royal bit, offended by any suggestion it's theater
- **Function**: Brewery tour host, ceremony conductor, stamp authority
- **Dialogue**: 
  - "Welcome, subject! You stand in the presence of the SEVENTH Royal Brewmaster!"
  - "My great-grandfather FOUNDED this dynasty! His yeast cultures flow in these tanks!"
  - *during ceremony* "Do you swear to uphold the honor of CROWN AND CASK?"
  - *stamping* "Rise! You are FOREVER a Knight of our realm! This stamp is your proof!"

### The Royal Cousins (Staff)
- **Role**: Servers, ceremony assistants, legitimacy by proximity
- **Appearance**: Matching livery, family resemblance, varying commitment to the bit
- **Function**: Service, atmosphere, occasional eye-rolls when König Klaus isn't looking
- **Dialogue**: 
  - "His Majesty will see you now."
  - *whispered* "Just go with it. It makes him happy."
  - "The Usurper is actually pretty good. Don't tell him I said that."

### The Knighted Regulars
- **Role**: Atmosphere, social proof, ceremony veterans

  - "I've been knighted twelve times. Different beer each time. It's a collection."
