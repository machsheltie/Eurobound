*Where dusty manuscripts hold forgotten power, the vendor knows every opus ever written, and humming the wrong tune gets you banned for life*

## Visual Design
### Cart Description
- **Structure**: Antique wooden cart overflowing with paper, scrolls, and bound folios
- **Display**: Sheet music pinned to every surface, fluttering in the breeze like paper wings

- **Décor**: Inkwells, quill pens, a metronome that ticks disapprovingly, faded composer portraits

- **Expression**: Ranges from "mildly disappointed" to "you have potential" (rare)
- **Quirk**: Can identify any piece of music from three notes — and will tell you what's wrong with your interpretation

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Composer's Cart satirizes classical music gatekeeping — the idea that "real" music appreciation requires expertise, that some interpretations are "correct," and that musical knowledge is a measure of human worth.

The deeper joke: The vendor genuinely loves music and wants to share it, but decades of tourists humming "Für Elise" badly have broken something inside him. His gatekeeping is a defense mechanism against cultural trauma.

### The Humming Crime
The cart's signature hazard:
- Humming near the cart triggers vendor attention
- He WILL identify what you're humming
- He WILL tell you what's wrong with it
- Humming something "common" = mild disdain
- Humming something obscure correctly = genuine respect
- Humming something wrong = physical pain visible in his face

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Classical music gatekeeping, "correct" interpretation culture
- **Secondary Targets**: Sheet music collectors, musical one-upmanship
- **Cultural Specificity**: Viennese musical heritage obsession
- **Humor Approach**: The vendor's pain is real but absurd
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes snobbery, not music education

### Buffoon Exploitation
The cart exists to test musical knowledge:
- Bros will inevitably hum something
- Bros will inevitably get it wrong
- The vendor's reaction is the punishment/entertainment
- Prices remain fair (he's not a scammer, just disappointed)

### The Humming Mini-Game

**Triggered**: When player hums, whistles, or selects "musical" dialogue options near cart

**Mechanic**:
1. Player chooses/accidentally triggers a tune
2. Vendor identifies it instantly
3. Vendor judges the interpretation
4. Consequences based on performance

**Humming Outcomes**:
| What You Hum | Vendor Reaction | Consequence |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------|
| "Für Elise" (basic) | Heavy sigh | Nothing (he's used to it) |
| "Für Elise" (wrong) | Visible pain | "Please stop. Please." |
| Mozart (correct) | Nod | 5% discount |
| Mozart (wrong) | "He's ROLLING in his grave" | Price increase 10% |
| Obscure piece (correct) | Genuine surprise | 15% discount, rare stock shown |
| Obscure piece (wrong) | "You ALMOST had it" | Respect for trying |
| Made-up tune | Confusion, then intrigue | "What IS that?" (quest trigger?) |
| Copyrighted modern song | RAGE | 10-minute ban |

### Patron of the Arts System

**Unlocked After**: 100 Sovs total spent

**Benefits**:
- Access to premium manuscript inventory
- 10% permanent discount
- Vendor actually remembers your name
- Can request specific compositions
- Allowed to hum (within reason)

### Special Interactions

**The Request**:
- Ask for specific piece = vendor finds it instantly in chaos
- Ask for "something good" = vendor's choice (always excellent)
- Ask for "something popular" = vendor dies a little inside, complies

**The Quiz**:
- Vendor occasionally tests customers
- Correct answer = discount
- Wrong answer = lecture (educational but long)
- Admitting ignorance = respect (he appreciates honesty)

**The Recommendation**:
- Based on what you've bought before
- Surprisingly accurate to playstyle
- "You seem like a Beethoven player. Aggressive. Dramatic. Here."
