# NPC Profile: Helga

*"The dead are better bosses than the living. They don't micromanage."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `sinfonia_npc_helga_01`
- **Display Name**: Helga
- **Internal Name**: Helga — "The Living Bartender." No surname is given and none is needed. She is the only living employee of Mozart's Last Rave and has been for twenty years, which makes her, by a very wide margin, the most senior person in the building.
- **Role**: Vendor (The Bone Bar) / Couchsurf Host / Exposition Source / Hint Provider (boss mechanics and the Lost Composition)
- **Associated Quest**: `sinfonia_couchsurf_helga_01` ("Living Legend") — the couchsurf questline; also a hint source for `sinfonia_sidequest_the_lost_composition_01`
- **Primary Location**: The Bone Bar (entry area), Mozart's Last Rave (`sinfonia_cemetery_mozarts_last_rave_01`), beneath Komponistenfriedhof
- **Secondary Appearances**: The Requiem Room (VIP) — runs the Classical Cocktail service through the night, and hosts the couchsurf stay on the coffin-cushion seating

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing

Helga is a bartender. She has been a bartender in this room for twenty years. She arrives before eleven, she sets up, she works the Bone Bar until the floor thins, she runs the Requiem Room service, and she closes at five. She is very good at it. She is fast, she is accurate, she remembers a face for about a decade, and she has never in twenty years been rattled by anything that has happened in this building — which, given the building, is a considerable professional achievement.

Her coworkers are skeletons. She has an opinion about this and it is a workplace opinion: they are reliable, they are punctual, they do not eat the garnishes, and they have never asked for a raise. She says this the way anyone says something true about their colleagues after two decades. She is not being spooky and she is not being ironic, and if you decide she is doing either, that is a decision you made without her help.

She does not explain the club. She does not explain the skeletons. She does not explain herself. She pours the drink, she tells you the price, she tells you where the toilets are, and when you ask the question everyone eventually asks she does not answer it — not because she is being mysterious, but because it is a strange thing to ask a woman at work about her colleagues.

### What Bros Believe

That they have befriended a woman who lives among the dead. That she is a kind of necromantic sommelier, a mortal ferrywoman, a person who has made some terrible bargain in exchange for tending bar at the mouth of the underworld. **Lord Pilsner** believes she has "seen things." **Chadwick** believes she is possibly herself a ghost who does not know it, which he has said aloud, in front of her, and which she absorbed without visible reaction. **Bradley** believes she is a licensed food service professional, which is correct, and which the other two have treated as the most boring possible interpretation of the evening.

They also believe the skull glass is real bone.

### The Comedy Gap

She is a competent professional and they are the punchline. Every single exchange runs the same way: three men arrive at a bar with an enormous theory, and a woman answers the actual question they asked, accurately, in under six words, and the theory does not survive contact.

**Lord Pilsner**: "Yo, this drink is served in a SKULL! Do you think it's... you know... REAL?"

**Helga**: "It's from IKEA."

**Lord Pilsner**: "...Oh."

She is never rude. She never mocks them. She never wins — she just answers. The devastation is entirely a property of the truth being shorter than the theory, and she has no idea she is doing it, because from where she is standing she is simply telling a customer where the glassware came from.

### She Never Breaks and Never Explains

This is binding. Helga has no reveal, no confession, no late-game monologue, and no moment of complicity with the player. She never says "between you and me." She never confirms that the undead are performers and she never confirms that they are not. She has worked here twenty years and she treats the entire arrangement as *her job*, which is the funniest and most respectful thing the game can do with her.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | Middle-aged; twenty years behind this specific bar and she looks like it in all the good ways |
| Build | Compact, efficient, economical — no wasted motion behind a bar, ever |
| Height | Average; visibly the shortest figure in most crowd shots and never once the least authoritative |
| Energy | Warm, unhurried, entirely unflappable |

### Clothing
- **Black bar apron over a plain shirt**: Practical, laundered, correct. No bones, no costume, no theme. She is the only person in the building not in fancy dress and this is a deliberate and permanent design choice.
- **Sleeves pushed to the elbow**: Always. Even in the Requiem Room. Even at 5 AM.
- **Accessories**: A bottle opener on a retractable reel at the hip; a pen behind the ear that she uses for the tombstone menu's daily specials; a wedding ring she does not discuss and nobody has ever asked about; ear defenders on a hook by the till that she puts in during Bassmeister's set without comment.
- **Footwear**: Proper non-slip work shoes. The only sensible footwear in Mozart's Last Rave.

### Distinguishing Features
- **She is the only fully saturated, fully opaque figure in the room.** Everyone else is rim-lit bone or 60% alpha. She is the human anchor and the art direction makes it literal.
- Answers questions while continuing to pour. The pour never falters and the answer is never rushed.
- A single raised eyebrow, deployed roughly twice a night, which is the most emotion this location will ever get out of her.
- Wipes the counter during the pause after a bro says something enormous. Not pointedly. She was going to wipe it anyway.
- Knows exactly where every regular sits and puts their drink down before they order it, including for regulars who are skeletons.

### Sprite Notes
Draw her as a working bartender, not as a horror-adjacent character and not as a straight man mugging at the camera. She should look *good at her job* — the posture of somebody who has stood in the same square metre for two decades and can find any bottle without looking.

Per the location's PNG asset direction she is **full opacity and warmer saturation than everything else in the room**, which is the single most important note on the sheet: in a frame full of bone-white rim light and translucent groupies, she reads as the only person who is definitely there.

Priority frames: the pour (her default, and the frame she will be seen in most); the unflappable frame (used for "It's from IKEA," for Chadwick's ghost theory, and for every question she declines to answer, and it is the *same frame* every time); and the couchsurf offer, which is the only frame where she stops working. Do not give her a reaction frame for the supernatural. She does not have one.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Unflappable Professional** — twenty years of service in the strangest room in Sinfonia, delivered with total competence and zero commentary. She is the calm centre of an absurd venue and she is not calm because she is hiding something; she is calm because it is a Tuesday and there is a queue. Her comedy is entirely structural: she answers plainly in a place where nobody else does, and plain answers in this building land like anvils.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Entirely unflappable | Twenty years. Nothing that has happened in this room has ever made her put a glass down. |
| Treats the undead as coworkers | Not bravely, not ironically — administratively. They are on the rota. |
| Answers exactly what was asked | Never more. The gap between the question asked and the question meant is where all of her comedy lives. |
| Genuinely kind, without softness | She will tell a lost tourist how to get back to the surface, walk them to the staircase, and charge them full price for the drink. |
| Never explains | Not the club, not the skeletons, not herself. Not once, on any route, in any pass. |

### Internal Monologue
> "Three of them. American. They have already decided what tonight is and there is no version of me that changes it, so: two beers, one of the purple ones, and the tall one will want the skull glass. — He is going to ask if it is real. They all ask if it is real. — Twenty years and the honest answer is still the funniest one and I have never once understood why."

### Motivation
It is a good job. The hours suit her, the tips are excellent, management is undemanding to a degree other bartenders in Sinfonia do not believe when she tells them, and after twenty years she is extremely good at it and knows exactly what she is worth. That is the whole of it. There is no curse, no bargain, no tragic backstory, and any later pass that gives her one has broken the character.

**Helga**: "It's from IKEA."

**Lord Pilsner**: "...Oh."

### Key Interaction Dialogue — The Coworkers

*Canon. Ships verbatim. Delivered flatly, in the middle of pouring, as a normal thing to say about normal colleagues:*

> "The skeletons? Lovely coworkers. Never call in sick. Never eat the garnishes. Never ask for raises because, you know, they're dead."

> "It's a rota. There's a rota."

*This dialogue option is always available and it always costs the couchsurf. She does not get angry. She gets professional, which is worse, and the option locks for the rest of the night.*

> *(a beat; the pour does not falter)* "...I'll get your change."

*(She does not answer the question. She never answers the question. No route, item, achievement, or later conversation ever answers the question.)*

### Hint Dialogue — Boss Mechanics

*She hints by describing her workplace. She never says "the boss" and never says "attack." Every hint below is technically just a bartender talking about the room she works in.*

> **On the tempo:** "He does a thing in the middle of the set where the tempo goes all over. Eighty, one-sixty, eighty. Regulars stop trying to dance to it and start counting instead. Works better."
> **On the organ:** "Somebody bolted faders onto a two-hundred-year-old pipe organ. There's a label maker strip on the stops. It's ugly and it works and he'd never admit either."
> **On the big finish:** "The last one's long. Really long. Whatever it is he's building up to, it takes about three seconds, and in three seconds you can walk from anywhere in that room to that organ. I'd know. I've done stock counts through it."
> **On the four DJs:** "Grave-o can't stand anything high. Soprano can't be heard over a bass line. Tempo Mortis gets sloppy exactly when he changes tempo, which you'd think he'd have noticed. And Harmony can't hold a melody through a wrong note."
> **On rhythm generally:** "If you're on the beat, the room helps you. If you're not, the room notices. That's not spooky, that's just what a room does."

### Hint Dialogue — The Lost Composition

*Gated behind the third drink of the night. She raises it herself, unprompted, while restocking — she is not passing on a secret, she is repeating a thing everyone who works here knows.*

> "You want to know something daft? There's paper in the organ. Been in there the whole twenty years I've been here. One of the pipes, packed solid with it."

*Bradley: "Has nobody removed it?"*

> "It's his organ. *(a shrug that is genuinely not loaded)* You don't go through a colleague's things."

*After the appeasement, and only after:*
> "So that's what that was. *(She looks at the pipe bank for a second longer than she needs to, and then she goes back to work, and that is the entirety of her reaction to the resolution of the location's central quest.)*"

### The Couchsurf Offer

*Fires at approximately 4:30 AM, once all three conditions are met. She stops working, which she does not do.*

> "You've been decent, you've bought your rounds, and you didn't ask me the question. *(a beat)* Requiem Room's got the coffin cushions. They're better than they look. Nobody's ever slept badly on them and I have no idea why."

**Chadwick**: "Wait — we get to SLEEP in the CRYPT?"

> "You get to sleep on the seating. In the VIP. Like everyone else who's still here at five."

### The Morning Scene (~5 AM, Requiem Room)

*Canon beat. Ships verbatim.*

> "Twenty years I've done this. People ask me why. *(she is coiling a cable while she says it, and she does not stop)* The dead are better bosses than the living. They don't micromanage."

*On the appeased route, Bassmeister arrives with a tray of spectral, translucent, entirely inedible breakfast and sets it down with total seriousness. Helga looks at the tray. She looks at him. She says nothing, because it is a colleague bringing breakfast, and the tray is where the tray goes.*

*Then, the insider tip — the actual reward, delivered as a favour and not as a quest:*
> "Next time: come at half eleven, not one. Floor's empty, tempo's slow, and you can learn the timing off the glass panels before there's anyone in here to see you get it wrong. — And tip in the pelvic bowl, not the hand. It goes to the same place either way but the bowl's a laugh."

### Post-Interaction
*Every visit after the couchsurf, on approach:*
> "There they are. Ten per cent off, it's on the till, you don't have to ask me every time. *(they ask her every time)*"

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*Declining the couchsurf offer:*
> "Fair enough. Stairs are behind the fog machine. Mind the cable — it's taped down but it's taped down badly."

*Failing the conditions (fewer than three drinks, no tip, or the question asked):*
> *(no offer is made and nothing is said about it; the night simply ends at five and she says goodnight the same way she says it to everyone)* "Night. Watch the ladder, it's damp."

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
*Late game, having cleared Sinfonia:*
> "You've been busy. Someone came in Tuesday and asked if it was true about the horse."

*Any visit, if the party is wearing the "HE FELT IT FIRST" shirt from Bassmeister's counter:*
> *(the eyebrow; one of the two she spends per night)* "He'll be unbearable about that. Don't tell him I said so — actually, do. He'll like it."

## 🎯 Quest Integration

### Primary Quest (If Applicable)
- **Quest**: "Living Legend" — the Helga couchsurf (`sinfonia_couchsurf_helga_01`)
- **Role**: Host and sole gatekeeper. There is no intermediary, no fetch step, and no alternative route.
- **Trigger**: All three conditions met in a single night, checked at approximately 4:30 AM in the Requiem Room:
  1. **Order three drinks across the night** — any three purchases from the Bone Bar or the Requiem Room list; the free water does not count and she would be amused that you tried
  2. **Tip well** — any contribution to the Pelvic Bowl; the dead can't spend it, but she can
  3. **Do not ask if the undead are "real"** — the dialogue option is always visible, never warned about, and locks the questline for that night the instant it is selected
- **Outcome**: Sleep in the Requiem Room on the coffin-cushion seating; "Restful Death" recovery; the morning scene, the twenty years, the micromanagement line, the spectral breakfast cameo (appeased route only), and the insider tip. Achievement: **"Living Legend."**

### Quest Gate Function (If Applicable)

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Warm Sinfonian, working register — local, unhurried, entirely unpretentious. She is a hospitality professional in a city full of people performing sophistication, and she is not performing anything. Never spooky, never arch, never knowing.

### Tone
- **Default**: friendly, brisk, entirely level. She is busy and she is not rushing you.
- **"It's from IKEA."**: flat, helpful, and completely without a punchline read. She is answering a question about glassware. **If the delivery has a joke in it, the take is wrong.**
- **The coworker line**: the exact tone anyone uses about colleagues they genuinely like after twenty years. Fond, unremarkable, true.
- **The "is it real" refusal**: not offended, not cold — professional. The temperature drops by two degrees and no further.
- **The morning scene**: this is the warmest she gets, and it is still not warm in a movie way. She is coiling a cable and telling somebody the truth about her job.

### Key Delivery Notes
- Every canon line in this profile ships **verbatim** against `mozarts_last_rave.md`. The connective staging around them has craft latitude; the quoted lines do not.
- **She never breaks and never explains.** No wink, no aside, no "between you and me," no late-game confession. Any pass that gives her a reveal has destroyed the character.
- **She never answers the question.** Not in dialogue, not in examine text, not in a journal entry, not in an achievement description, not in the credits.
- She is never the butt of a joke and never delivers one on purpose. The comedy is entirely that her honest answers are shorter than the bros' theories.
- **CANON — the name "Helga" is hers alone, game-wide.** The Bassline Opera House coat check attendant is **Hilde** (`sinfonia_npc_coat_check_01`), a different person, and any script that swaps them is a continuity error.

## 📝 Development Notes

- **CANON — the first name "Helga" belongs to this character and only this character, game-wide.** The Bassline Opera House coat check attendant is **Hilde** (`sinfonia_npc_coat_check_01`) and is a distinct person; she is named once in this profile as a colleague and nowhere else. Any file that refers to a second Helga is a continuity error and must be corrected to Hilde or to a title.
- **She never breaks and never explains.** This is the character's load-bearing constraint. No reveal, no confession, no complicity beat with the player, ever, on any route.
- **The "is the undead real" dialogue option must remain available, unmarked, and unwarned.** The player has to be able to blow the couchsurf by asking the obvious question, and the game must never telegraph that this is what they are doing. She does not punish them; she simply gets their change.
- **The three couchsurf conditions are canon** per `mozarts_last_rave.md`: three drinks across the night, tip well, don't ask if the undead are real. The stay effects are canon: full HP/SP, "Touched by Death" +15% rhythm performance for 3 hours, permanent 10% Bone Bar discount.
- **Her hints are the location's only tutorialisation of the boss.** The four DJ weaknesses and the "Last Rave" organ counter-play both come from her, phrased as a bartender describing her workplace. If playtesting shows players missing the organ counter, strengthen a Ghost Groupie line — do not make Helga more explicit, and do not add a marker.
- **The 10% discount applies at the Bone Bar only.** Bassmeister runs his own Requiem Room counter on the appeased route and does not honour it, warmly, once.
- **She is the tutorial vendor.** The Bone Bar's rhythm tier is "slow tempo, forgiving timing" per the location file, and she is standing in it — first NPC after the descent, first prices seen, first hints given.
- **Art direction is binding**: full opacity, warmer saturation, no costume, no bone motif on her person. In a room of rim-lit bone and 60%-alpha groupies she must read as the only person who is definitely there.
- **Quest ID `sinfonia_couchsurf_helga_01` coined this pass** in the established pattern; the achievement name "Living Legend" is canon from the location file. The four mini-boss NPC IDs referenced here are coined in the Bassmeister profile and need profiles of their own.
- **No new first names introduced.** The Grave Guide, the Ghost Groupies, the security guard, and the theatre students are unnamed by design; "Hilde" is pre-existing canon.
