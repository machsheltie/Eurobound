# NPC Profile: The Baroness of Masks

*"Your mask is... adequate. But masks are for faces. What mask does your SOUL wear?"*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `sinfonia_npc_baroness_of_masks_01`
- **Display Name**: The Baroness of Masks
- **Internal Name**: The Baroness of Masks, Keeper of Secrets. **She has no first name and must never be given one.** Not in dialogue, not in examine text, not in a JSON field, not in a late-game reveal. The absence is the character. Any file that names her is wrong.
- **Role**: Social Boss / Power Broker / Rumour Economy Authority / Mystery
- **Associated Quest**: `sinfonia_sidequest_the_baroness_favor_01` ("The Baroness' Favor") — multi-stage social questline. Also gates content in "Mask Etiquette Exam" and "Rumor Network".
- **Primary Location**: The Baroness' Dais, Baroness' Ballroom (`sinfonia_hidden_baroness_ballroom_01`) — masquerade nights only, Friday & Saturday, 22:00–04:00
- **Secondary Appearances**: The Baroness' personal salon (Inner Circle access only); the Whisper Gallery (she is never *seen* there, but two people always report she just left)

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What She's Actually Doing
Running the most efficient private information market in Sinfonia, twice a week, from a raised platform, in a ballroom where every single person present has agreed to pretend they cannot recognise each other.

She is not a mystic. She is a clearing house. Rumours enter her ballroom, get graded, get priced, and leave with someone else. She takes no cut in money — she takes a cut in *knowing*, which is the only currency in this city that compounds. Every favour granted, every invitation issued, every reputation quietly ended at the Ballroom traces back to information that crossed her dais first.

She is also, and this is not a contradiction, having an extremely good time. She has been doing this for a very long while and she still finds people funny.

### What Bros Believe
That they are at a secret masquerade, that they are mysterious, that nobody knows who they are, and that the Baroness may be an actual supernatural entity. Lord Pilsner has entered under the identity "The Count of Cool." Chadwick believes a mask is a personality. Bradley has correctly deduced that they are being discussed unfavourably and has classified this as *valuable intelligence*.

They believe her interest in them is romantic, or occult, or both.

### The Comedy Gap
The bros were identified within thirty seconds — by their accents, their posture, and their complete inability to waltz. The Baroness knew who they were before they reached the bottom of the staircase because the Herald announced "The Count of Cool" and the entire room heard it.

Her questions land as cosmic riddles and are, in fact, ordinary due diligence. "What mask does your soul wear?" is a woman who trades in leverage asking a stranger what he is hiding, and getting a real answer, because he thinks it is a spooky party question. The bros supply her with more usable material in four minutes than most guests give her in a season, entirely for free, while believing they are being enigmatic.

### The Deeper Joke (Canon — Never Resolved)
Is she one person? A title passed between hosts? A collective fiction the city maintains because a city needs one? **The game never confirms.** Witnesses to her appearance disagree with each other, consistently and in detail, and every account is presented as sincere. Lady Geheimnis held the title for twelve years and lost it, which suggests it is a position — and Lady Geheimnis also says, flatly, that she never met the woman who took it from her.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | Unresolvable. Every guest gives a different decade and every guest is certain. |
| Build | Tall in most accounts. Not in all of them. |
| Height | See above. The Herald has announced her four hundred times and has never described her. |
| Energy | Omniscient amusement; dangerous kindness |

### Clothing
- **The gown**: Elaborate, immaculate, and never twice the same. Guests who compare notes after a masquerade discover they have described four different dresses. Nobody finds this alarming, which is the alarming part.
- **The mask**: Shifts colour with the angle of the chandelier — deep violet to masquerade gold to something closer to black. It covers from hairline to upper lip. It has never been removed in the presence of a guest.
- **Accessories**: A single closed fan she does not fan herself with. Gloves past the elbow. No jewellery whatsoever, in a room where everyone else is wearing everything they own — this is a deliberate, and deliberately noticed, choice.
- **Footwear**: Never visible. The dais and the gown see to that.

### Distinguishing Features
- **She arrives at conversations already in progress.** No approach animation. She is not there, then she is, and the transition is never on screen.
- Tilts her head very slightly before answering, as if consulting something.
- Uses the closed fan as a pointer — one tap on the dais rail ends an audience, and everyone in the room hears it over the orchestra.
- Laughs exactly once per masquerade, always at something that was not a joke.
- Remembers the name of every mask she has ever been introduced to, including the stupid ones, especially the stupid ones.

### Sprite Notes
The single hardest asset in Sinfonia and it should be treated as a set-piece, not a character sprite. Priority: presence over detail. She should read as *composed and completely still* in a room where every other sprite is in motion — the ballroom crowd dances, fans flutter, masks turn, and she does not. The colour-shift on the mask is the one animated element she needs and it should be tied to the chandelier's ambient cycle, not to her.

**Do not draw her face.** Not in any frame, not in any state, not partially. If the mask ever comes off in an asset, the character is dead.

For the "witnesses disagree" bit: if budget allows, ship two or three subtly different gown palettes and select randomly per masquerade night, with no in-game acknowledgement whatsoever. Nobody should ever be told this is happening.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Amused Custodian** — the person who ended up holding the city's social machinery and discovered she was good at it. She is not cruel, not seductive, and not a villain. She is enormously powerful, entirely uninterested in using that power for anything dramatic, and quietly delighted by the fact that people keep handing her things she did not have to ask for.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Knows everything, reveals nothing | Her value is not the information; it is the reputation for having it, which she has never once had to prove |
| Genuinely curious | Not a pose. She asks because she wants to know, and people can tell, which is precisely why they answer |
| Dangerously kind | The worst thing she does to anybody is be lovely to them in front of a room that is keeping score |
| Never raises her voice | Not once. The fan tap does the work. |
| Rewards honesty above skill | A guest who says "I have no idea what I'm doing here" outperforms a guest with a perfect cover story, every time |

### Internal Monologue
> "Seventeen counts tonight. Fourteen of them are exactly what they say. Two are lying carefully, which is a service to me. And one of them has just announced himself as the Count of *Cool*, which means he is either the most sophisticated liar in this building or he has told me his entire life in three syllables. — Oh. Oh, he means it. — Bring me that one. I want to hear him talk."

### Motivation
Continuity. Somebody has to hold the middle of this city or it goes back to being a hundred families not speaking to each other, and she has been holding it long enough to be attached to it. Beyond that: she is not bored, and at her tenure that is not nothing.

## 💬 Dialogue

### Approach/Greeting
**The audience, on the dais — canon, ships verbatim:**
> "Everyone comes to me eventually. The wise come curious. The foolish come demanding. Which are you, I wonder?"

**Opening the interrogation:**
> "Your mask is... adequate. But masks are for faces. What mask does your SOUL wear?"

**If the party is announced with a self-invented identity:**
> "The Count of Cool, was it? *(amused)* I've met seventeen counts tonight. Only three were lying. Which are you?"

### Key Interaction Dialogue — The Audience
*Not combat. Pure social encounter. She asks questions that seem innocent and are not, and the party's standing with her moves on every answer.*

**The credential question:**
> "I've hosted a thousand masquerades. Everyone believes they're unique. Almost none are. Tell me why YOU might be."

**Lord Pilsner**: "Your Baroness-ness, I am the Count of—"

> "I know what you are. The question is whether you know."

**Lord Pilsner**: "...I'm... cool?"

> *(amused)* "Perhaps. We shall see."

**If the party admits they have no idea what they are doing — the highest-scoring answer in the encounter, and one the bros can only reach by accident:**
> "*(the fan does not move; something in her attention does)* ...Say that again."

**Bradley**: "We are not from here. We were told there was a party."

> "Do you know how long it has been since anyone in this room told me something *true*? — Sit down. Not there. Here."

**Pressing her for information she has not offered:**
> "You may have that. It will cost you a thing you have not thought about yet, and I will not tell you which thing until after."

**Trying to flatter her:**
> "Mm. That was well constructed. You have used it before, and it has worked before, and it is not working now. Try again, and try worse."

**Asking her real name:**
> "*(the head tilt)* Which one?"

### Rumour Grading (Whisper Gallery Adjacency)
*She never trades personally on the floor — Der Flüsterer runs the exchange. But an audience is where a rumour gets its price.*

| Grade | Value | Her line |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Common | 50 Sovs | "Everyone knows that. Including the person it is about." |
| Uncommon | 150 Sovs | "Better. That has legs." |
| Rare | 400 Sovs | "*(a pause)* Where did you get that." |
| Exclusive | 1000 Sovs+ | "You will not repeat it in this room. You will repeat it to me, once, and then you will forget you carried it." |

### Interaction Type — Granting Favour
**Slight Favour:**
> "You may hear things now. Nothing that matters. But you will hear the shape of the things that do."

**Moderate Favour — the Inner Circle invitation:**
> "The salon behind me. Friday. You need not knock and you need not dress better, though you might consider it."

**High Favour — the Vault:**
> "There is a room under this building with my name on the door and none of my things in it. Take what fits. Leave what doesn't. Do not come back for the rest."

**Maximum Favour — the One Truth:**
> "Ask. One question. Any question in this city, and I will answer it correctly and completely, and then we will never speak of it again." *(the fan taps once)* "Choose carefully. I have watched people spend this on the wrong thing and I have not enjoyed it."

### Post-Interaction
*Ending an audience, always the same, always with the fan tap:*
> "Go and be watched. That is what the room is for."

### If Player Declines / Alternative Path
*If the party never pursues her favour at all — this is Lady Geheimnis' couchsurf requirement, and the Baroness notices:*
> "You have been in my ballroom four times and you have not once come up these stairs. *(genuine, unguarded interest)* That is either the cleverest thing anyone has done here this season, or you have simply not noticed there are stairs. I would like to know which. I suspect I never will."

*If the party is Unmasked and ejected:*
> *(she does not intervene, does not comment, and does not look away — and that is the entire punishment)*

*If the party offends her — "Social Death":*
> "No. *(the fan taps once)* No, I don't think so."
> *That is the whole of it. She says nothing else, ever again, and neither does anyone else in Sinfonia's high society.*

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
*Every subsequent masquerade, post-favour:*
> "Ah. The Count of Cool. *(she uses it every time, and she never once uses it unkindly, which is somehow worse)* You brought your friends. You always bring your friends. I find I like that about you."

*If the party has stayed with Lady Geheimnis:*
> "You slept in a cold house on the other side of the park. *(no accusation in it at all)* How is she? — Do not answer. I would rather want to know."

*Late game, if the party has cleared the Maestro:*
> "The Maestro is no longer conducting. *(a pause exactly one beat too long)* I heard within the hour. I hear most things within the hour. It is not a gift. It is a room."

## 📅 Schedule & Location

### Primary Schedule
| Time | Location | Activity |
|------|----------|----------|
| Fri & Sat, 22:00–23:00 | The Baroness' Dais | Present from before the first guest arrives; nobody has seen her enter the building |
| Fri & Sat, 23:00–02:00 | The Baroness' Dais | Holding court; audiences by approach through the social gauntlet only |
| Fri & Sat, 02:00–03:30 | The Baroness' personal salon | Inner Circle only; the real conversations |
| Fri & Sat, 03:30–04:00 | The Dais | Closing the room. She is the last thing guests see and the first thing they misremember. |
| All other times | — | Not present, not findable, not referenced by any NPC as being anywhere. The Ballroom itself is inaccessible outside masquerade nights. |

### Positioning Notes
- The dais is at the head of the ballroom, elevated, with the chandelier directly between it and the grand staircase — so every arriving guest is lit and she is not.
- **Approaching uninvited is social death.** The gauntlet is the mechanic: Der Schatten gatekeeps the dais, and the Dance Duelists gatekeep Der Schatten.
- She is in the sightline of the Whisper Gallery's entire curve. The gallery's acoustics were designed to carry whispers across the room. Nobody has ever suggested these two facts are related.
- Her sprite must never be shown walking. Present, or not present.

### Hidden Details / Easter Eggs
- **Examine the fan**: *"Closed. Ivory, worn smooth along one edge by a thumb, over a length of time you would rather not calculate."*
- **Examine the dais**: *"No chair. She has never sat down in this room."*
- **Examine her gown, twice, on two different masquerade nights**: the examine text is different both times, and neither text acknowledges the other.
- Ask three separate ballroom NPCs to describe her and receive three descriptions that share not one physical detail. All three are delivered with total confidence. None of them is flagged as unreliable.
- The Herald has a bribe table for every guest in the building **except** her. Attempting to bribe him regarding the Baroness returns: *"I do not announce the Baroness. The Baroness is already here."*
- **The One Truth** has a hidden response if the player spends it asking who she is: she answers completely and correctly, the dialogue box closes, and the answer is not shown to the player. The favour is consumed. No achievement fires. This is intentional and must ship.

### Territory / Social Dynamics
- Three entrances, three social registers: the Opera House route (prestige, via the Maestro's private box), the Cemetery route from Mozart's Last Rave (casual, less judgement), and the Café Existenz back room (intellectual, extra dialogue checks). She treats all three arrivals identically, which the opera-route guests have never noticed and the cemetery-route guests always do.
- Everyone in the room knows everyone else. The masks are a social contract permitting the pretence. She is the only person present whose anonymity is load-bearing rather than decorative.
- Her authority is entirely uncontested and has never been tested on screen. Do not test it.

| City | Location | Role/Context | Callback Dialogue |
|------|----------|--------------|-------------------|

### Evolution Through Game
Her dialogue changes only through the favour tiers — Slight, Moderate, High, Maximum — and the change is entirely in what she is willing to say, never in how she says it. - **Quest**: "The Baroness' Favor" (`sinfonia_sidequest_the_baroness_favor_01`) — multi-stage social questline across multiple masquerade nights

She gates the **Baroness' Vault** (late-game gear tier), **Inner Circle** status, **Rare/Exclusive rumour stock** with Der Flüsterer, and the **One Truth**. Both paths are complete; neither is the "correct" one.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
Refined Central European, placeless on purpose — she should not be locatable to a country, let alone a district. Per the location's audio spec: a mysterious echo and an ageless quality, applied as a light processing pass, never heavy enough to obscure a word.

### Tone
- **Court**: warm, curious, genuinely entertained. **Never sultry, never sinister, never a fortune-teller.** If she sounds like a villain the character is broken.
- **The interrogation**: conversational. She is not testing anybody; she is asking, and getting more than she asked for.
- **Granting favour**: matter-of-fact, almost brisk. The gift is not ceremonial to her.
- **Social Death**: exactly as warm as everything else. No coldness, no edge, no volume. Then silence.

### Key Delivery Notes
- She never raises her voice at any point in the game.
- The pauses are real held beats and the other party always fills them first.
- "Perhaps. We shall see." must be light and amused — the bros must leave that exchange believing they won it.
- The One Truth offer is the only moment she is fully serious, and it should be delivered slower than anything else she says.
- **She must never sound as though she is enjoying anyone's humiliation.** She is enjoying the *people*. The room does the humiliating.

## 📝 Development Notes

- **She has no first name. This is absolute.** No dialogue, examine text, JSON field, or later pass may name her. The One Truth easter egg answers the question and withholds the answer from the player — that is the only permitted treatment.
- **Her face is never drawn.** Not partially, not in shadow, not in a cutscene.
- **The one-person / title / collective-fiction question is never resolved.** Lady Geheimnis' twelve years imply a title; her claim never to have met her successor implies otherwise. Ship both. Resolve neither.
- The randomised gown palette (if implemented) must never be acknowledged by any system, achievement, or NPC.
- She is not a romance, not a threat, and not a mystic. Every draft that drifts toward "seductive enigma" should be pulled back to "extremely good at her job and quietly delighted."
