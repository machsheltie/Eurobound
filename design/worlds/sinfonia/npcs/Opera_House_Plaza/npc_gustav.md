# NPC Profile: Gustav

*(a long look, a snort, and a deliberate turn of the head that ends the conversation)*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `sinfonia_npc_gustav_horse_01`
- **Display Name**: Gustav
- **Internal Name**: Gustav — no surname, no title. He is a horse. He is also, functionally, the most senior employee at the Fiaker stand.
- **Role**: Quest Target / Non-Speaking Character / The Employee Who Finally Quit
- **Associated Quest**: `sinfonia_sidequest_fiaker_fiasco_01` ("The Fiaker Fiasco")
- **Primary Location**: Opera House Plaza, Sinfonia (`sinfonia_imperial_opera_house_plaza_01`) — Fiaker queue, post 3, harnessed
- **Secondary Appearances**: Café Sachertorte (Phase 2, the cake heist); The Bassline Opera House backstage and main stage (Phase 3); Stadtpark (Gustav's Refuge) (Phase 4, resolution); Stadtpark permanently (Path B only)

---

## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
Gustav is a sixteen-year-old white-grey Lipizzan — a Spanish Riding School reject, sold on to the carriage trade as a colt because he would not perform the airs above the ground and would not be told twice. Sixteen years later he pulls tourists in circles around Imperial Square six days a week for a man who has been drunk on the box since roughly 2015.

Today he walked away. Not bolted — walked. He waited until the harness clip was loose, which it was because Hans-Jürgen's hands were unsteady, and he left. Everything that follows — the café, the opera house, the park — is a horse taking the afternoon.

He is not lost. He knows exactly where he is. He knows this city better than anyone chasing him, because he has driven it for sixteen years while the man holding the reins drank.

### What Bros Believe
A noble steed in peril. A magnificent animal, spooked and frightened, who must be gently recovered and returned to his rightful master. Lord Pilsner has cast him as the horse from a movie he cannot name. Chadwick believes Gustav is "vibing." Bradley, alone, notes that the horse is maintaining a consistent lead and never actually running, and does not press the point.

### The Comedy Gap
The bros are conducting a rescue. Gustav is conducting a resignation. Every gap he opens up is deliberate — he speeds up exactly enough to stay ahead and no more, he stops to eat an 85 Sovs cake, he pauses on a lit stage in a stolen opera cape while a soprano holds a high F, and he leaves when he is finished. The chase has a director and it is not any of the humans in it.

The one moment the bros come genuinely close to understanding him is the Skittles negotiation — and they get there by accident, on advice from a woman who feeds pigeons, using American candy that has been in Chadwick's pocket since Publandia for reasons nobody has ever explained.

### The Comedy of the Cape
At some point in Phase 2 Gustav acquires an opera cape. The game never shows him taking it. It is simply on him afterward, and he wears it for the rest of the quest, and he wears it well.

## 👤 Appearance

### Physical Description
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 16 — elderly for a working carriage horse |
| Build | Muscular, deep-chested Lipizzan frame gone slightly heavy; visibly tired at rest |
| Height | ~15.2hh; large in a plaza, enormous inside a café |
| Energy | Unimpressed, deliberate, entirely self-possessed |

### Coat & Markings
- **Colour**: White-grey Lipizzan, dappled through the shoulder and quarters. Born dark, greyed out by his eighth year, as the breed does.
- **Provenance**: Spanish Riding School reject. There is a small, faded brand on the near hindquarter that Sinfonians recognise on sight and tourists photograph without understanding.
- **Condition**: Well-fed, well-shod, badly rested. Harness rub marks on the coat behind the shoulder — visible, and the only piece of evidence in the entire quest that anything is actually wrong.

### Accessories (Progressive, During the Chase)
- **Phase 1**: Full Fiaker harness, blinkers, plume. Immaculate — Hans-Jürgen's one remaining professional standard.
- **Phase 2 onward**: Broken harness pieces still attached and trailing; **stolen opera cape**, red, clasped at the throat; Sachertorte crumbs and apricot glaze on the muzzle.
- **Phase 3 onward**: Silver feathers from the Queen of the Night's torn gown caught in mane and cape.
- **Phase 4**: All of the above, plus mud to the fetlock and visible steam off his back.

### Distinguishing Features
- **The stare.** Long, level, unblinking, held two full seconds past comfortable, then broken by him and not by you.
- Ears track the speaker with complete precision. He is listening. He is choosing not to respond.
- Deliberate, unhurried turn-away — the whole body, not just the head. It reads as a door closing.
- Considered head tilt when offered something worth considering. Reserved almost exclusively for Skittles.

### Sprite Notes
He must never read as comic-cute or as a cartoon horse. The comedy comes entirely from a completely realistic, dignified, slightly weary animal doing extremely undignified things with total composure. Priority frames: the judgemental stare (hold it — this is his signature and it needs real frames), the cake-eating cycle (satisfied, unhurried, eyes half-closed), the deliberate turn-away, the head tilt, and the Phase 4 defiant stand in moonlight with the cape moving. The cape must be a separate layer so it can be added from Phase 2 onward without redrawing the base. Do not give him human facial expressions — everything is posture, ears, and the eye.

## 🧠 Personality

### Archetype
**The Silent Resignation** — an intelligent, patient working animal with sixteen years of accumulated evidence and no way to file a complaint, who has arrived at the only form of protest available to him: leaving. He is not vengeful, not feral, not "wild at heart." He is done. There is a difference, and the whole quest turns on it.

### Personality Traits
| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Done with tourists | Sixteen years of selfies, shouting, and men in matching shirts. The Americans are not the cause but they are the last of it. |
| Smarter than assumed | Knows every route in Sinfonia; has been navigating unaided for years; picks his stops for effect |
| Excellent taste | Selected the Sachertorte from twelve cakes. Not the nearest one. The best one. |
| Not cruel | Never charges, never kicks at a person, never actually hurts anyone. Property, yes. People, never. |
| Conditionally forgiving | He will consider returning. He will not be caught. Those are different transactions and only one of them is on offer. |

### Internal Monologue
*Gustav does not have interior monologue in dialogue form and must never be given one. For writers' reference only, never to be rendered on screen:*
> The clip was loose. The clip has been loose before. This time I did not wait for him to notice.

### Motivation
Rest. Not freedom in the abstract — rest. Grass instead of cobblestones. Quiet instead of a bachelor party. Six hours instead of twelve. Path A exists because those are things a person could actually give him, and Hans-Jürgen finally says so out loud.

## 💬 Dialogue

**Gustav does not speak, is never subtitled, and never gets a thought bubble.** All of his "dialogue" is physical and is rendered as stage direction. Two NPCs "translate" him, and both translations are unverifiable and played completely straight by the speaker.

### Approach/Greeting
**First sighting, Opera House Plaza:**
> *He is already looking at you. He was looking before you arrived. Long stare. Snort. He turns — the whole body — and walks away at a pace that is not a run.*

### Key Interaction Dialogue — During the Chase
| Situation | Gustav |
|-----------|--------|
| Party approaches at speed | *Annoyed whinny. Increases pace by exactly the amount required and no more.* |
| Party approaches slowly | *Allows it. To four metres. Then not.* |
| Cornered | *Plants. Squares up. Does not move and will not be moved.* |
| Someone shouts | *Ears flat, one step back, and the distance he gives up is never recovered* |
| Someone offers food that is not Skittles | *Looks at the food. Looks at the person. Looks away.* |
| Camera raised | *Turns his head away from the lens. Every time. Sixteen years of practice.* |
| Hans-Jürgen calls his name | *Angry snort. Stamps a forefoot once. Looks deliberately at something else.* |

### Café Sachertorte — The Heist
> *He walks the length of the display case. He considers all twelve cakes. He is not hurried and he is not random. He selects the Sachertorte — the centre pedestal, the crown jewel, the 85 Sovs one — and eats it in three bites.*

*Herr Ober: "NEIN! NEIN! DAS PFERD!"*

> *Gustav does not look up.*

### Bassline Opera House — The Stage
> *He walks onto a lit stage during a rehearsal of "Der Hölle Rache." He stops centre. The cape settles. The soprano holds her high F. He stands in the light for four full seconds — long enough for the orchestra to falter — and only then does he leave.*

### Stadtpark — The Skittles Negotiation
**Phase 1 — Initial Approach:**
> *Direct approach: he bolts. Hans-Jürgen's approach: he backs away, head high, and puts a lamppost between them. Papageno's approach (if the ally path was taken): he permits it, because Papageno has been feeding him birdseed through the stage door for years and Gustav has always known exactly who that was.*

**Phase 2 — Earning Trust:**
> *One Skittle, held flat on an open palm: the ears come forward. The head tilts. He does not move.*
> *A handful: he comes. Eleven metres, then four, then close. He is not hurrying and he is not being caught — he is choosing to arrive.*
> *The full bag: he eats from the hand. He is still watching the humans. He eats anyway.*

**Phase 3 — Resolution:**
> *He listens to the entire conversation about his future. He does not react to any of it until Hans-Jürgen says the word "partner."*

### Resolution — Path A (Returns)
> *Heavy sigh. He walks to Hans-Jürgen, stops, and lets himself be hugged. He does not return the hug. He does not leave, either. He nuzzles once — quickly, as if hoping it will not be recorded — and stands.*

### Resolution — Path B (Retires to the Park)
> *He turns from all of them, walks unhurried into the moonlight across the lawn, and does not look back. At the treeline, once, briefly, he does.*

### Resolution — Path C (Escapes)
> *He is gone before anyone reaches him. There is a gap in the hedge and there is nothing on the other side of it.*

### Post-Interaction
*Path A, back at the Fiaker queue, on every visit:*
> *He is in harness at post 3. He looks at the party. He remembers. He would prefer that not be a whole thing.*

*Path B, Stadtpark, on every visit:*
> *He is on the lawn. He raises his head when the party enters and lowers it again. Standing within three metres of him grants the buff. He does not require anything else from anyone ever again.*

### Callback Dialogue (Return Visits)
*Path A, if the party attempts a selfie with him post-quest:*
> *He turns his head away from the lens. Then, after a pause, he turns it back. The photograph is terrible and Chadwick posts it anyway.*

*Path B, late game, if the party arrives with Skittles:*
> *He takes one. He takes the rest. He stands with them for a while, which is the closest thing to gratitude a horse can issue.*

### Translated "Dialogue" (Delivered by Others, Never by Gustav)
**Pigeon Lady** — the only person who claims to speak for him and is never contradicted:
> "He tells me he is tired. Tired of the cobblestones. Tired of the tourists. Tired of the smell of beer."
> "Gustav says... you are the first Americans who did not ask for a selfie."
> "Give him the rainbow candies. He likes the rainbow candies."

**Papageno / Franz** — in character as a bird-man, entirely sincerely:
> "*translating* Gustav says he will consider returning. But he wants guarantees."

## 🎯 Quest Integration

### Primary Quest
- **Quest**: The Fiaker Fiasco (`sinfonia_sidequest_fiaker_fiasco_01`)
- **Role**: Quest Target across all four phases; resolution subject
- **Trigger**: Hans-Jürgen's plaza encounter (day hours). Gustav is visible fleeing before the party is given the objective.
- **Outcome**: Four resolutions. A — returns on reformed terms (best; 500 Sovs gratitude discount, "Horse Whisperer" achievement, Fiaker fast-travel). B — retires to Stadtpark ("Liberation" achievement, permanent park buff NPC, no discount). C — escapes entirely (debt +1,000 Sovs, "Horse Guilt" status, permanent journal marker). D — bros bail (debt +500 Sovs abandonment fee, "Coward" status, Hans-Jürgen hostile).

### Quest Gate Function
Gustav gates nothing directly, but he is the sole gate on the quest's economics: the difference between Path A and Path C is a 1,500 Sovs swing in the party's accumulated damage debt, and that debt is tracked by every vendor in Sinfonia until it is cleared.

## 🗣️ Voice Direction

### Accent
None. **Gustav must never be voiced, never be subtitled, and never be given a thought bubble.** Any implementation that gives him words breaks the character completely.

### Tone
- **Sound design**: realistic horse vocalisations only — snorts, a single annoyed whinny during the chase, heavy breathing at Stadtpark. No comic cartoon horse sounds, ever.
- **The stare**: scored with a single low cello note, per the plaza audio spec. Nothing else in the mix.
- **Cake consumption**: absurdly, disproportionately loud and satisfied, plus a cash-register ching. This is the one place the sound design is allowed to be funny.
- **Stadtpark**: one tired, emotional whinny, and otherwise only breathing.

### Key Delivery Notes
- Everything he "says" is animation, ears, and timing. Give the animator the beats; do not give the writer the lines.
- The Pigeon Lady's translations are always delivered as plain fact, never as whimsy, and are never confirmed or denied by the game.
- He is never scored as a villain during the chase and never as pitiable at the resolution. He is scored as *decided*.
