# Baroness' Ballroom

*Where everyone wears a mask, nobody uses their real name, and the only thing more elaborate than the lies is the chandelier*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: sinfonia_hidden_baroness_ballroom_01
- **Display Name**: Baroness' Ballroom
- **German Alias**: "Der Ballsaal der Baronin"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Anonymous Chaos in Formal Attire"
- **World Location**: Sinfonia
- **Area Zone**: Hidden location (accessed via multiple routes)
- **Location Type**: Secret Masquerade Venue / Social Combat Zone / Rumor Trading Hub / Dance Challenge Arena

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: Masquerade nights only (Friday & Saturday, 10 PM - 4 AM)
- **Stat Requirements**: +2 Charm AND +2 Bravado (must project confidence AND sophistication)
- **Item Requirements**: 
  - Masquerade Mask (purchased or crafted)
  - Formal Attire (Black-Tie or equivalent)
  - Invitation (acquired through quest, purchase, or social engineering)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: Must learn of existence via:
  - Café Existenz gossip chain
  - The Black-Tie Backroom rumor
  - Defeating Maestro Vice von Strauss (grants Baroness' Vault Keys)
  - "Mask Etiquette Exam" completion at The Bassline Opera House
- **Special Conditions**: Must choose a "masquerade identity" at entry (affects all interactions)

### The Invitation System
Invitations are the currency of exclusivity:
- **Purchase**: 200 Sovs from The Black-Tie Backroom (if High Roller status)
- **Quest Reward**: "Mask Etiquette Exam" completion
- **Social Engineering**: Convince an NPC to give theirs (high Charm check)
- **Theft**: Pickpocket from Kaffeehaus Row intellectuals (high risk)
- **Forgery**: Craft fake invitation (requires specific items, 30% detection chance)

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through any of three secret exits (varies by entry point)
- **Completion Requirements**: Successfully navigate one social encounter without being "unmasked"
- **Failure States**: 
  - "Unmasked" — identity revealed, ejected with Humiliated status (worse than Embarrassed)
  - "Social Death" — offend the Baroness, permanent reputation damage in Sinfonia
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Inner Circle" status after three successful visits (Baroness' private salon)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Baroness' Ballroom satirizes high-society anonymity — the way masks supposedly free people to be "themselves" when actually everyone just performs a DIFFERENT lie. The masquerade isn't about removing pretense; it's about layering more elaborate pretense on top.

The deeper joke: Everyone knows everyone else. The masks are a social contract that lets them pretend they don't. The "secrets" shared are open knowledge. The "scandals" revealed are already public. The entire event is performance art about performance.

### The Baroness Herself
**The Baroness of Masks** is Sinfonia's true power broker:
- Nobody knows her real identity (including, possibly, herself)
- She's been hosting masquerades for "decades" (but never ages?)
- All social currency in Sinfonia flows through her
- Offending her is worse than any other failure
- Befriending her unlocks the city's deepest secrets

Is she one person? A title passed between hosts? A collective fiction everyone maintains? The game never confirms.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: High-society anonymity theater, masquerade culture
- **Secondary Targets**: Social climbing, rumor economy, identity performance
- **Cultural Specificity**: Viennese ball culture, Habsburg masquerade traditions
- **Humor Approach**: The masks reveal more than faces ever could
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Satirizes social performance, not actual identity exploration

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Reputation as currency, social destruction as weapon
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Selling rumors, trading secrets, blackmail disguised as gossip
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros who think a mask makes them suave (it does not)
- **Comedy of Errors**: Wrong identity assumptions, accidental reveals, dance disasters
- **Consequence System**: Humiliated status, social death, reputation damage across Sinfonia

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Masquerade Identity System

**Identity Creation** (Entry):
Choose a masquerade persona from templates:
- **The Count/Countess**: Nobility claim (+Charm checks, -if exposed)
- **The Artist**: Creative type (+Wit checks, -practical knowledge)
- **The Scholar**: Intellectual (+Knowledge checks, -social grace)
- **The Mystery**: No claims (+flexibility, -no bonuses)
- **Custom**: Name your own (all checks neutral)

**Identity Consequences**:
- All NPC interactions reference your chosen identity
- Wrong identity claims can be challenged
- Successful identity performance builds "Credibility Meter"
- Exposure destroys credibility and triggers Humiliated status

### Social Combat System — "The Mask Dance"

**Core Mechanic**:
Social encounters function as combat with reputation as HP:
- **Social HP**: Starts at 100 (your reputation for the evening)
- **Attack**: Cutting remarks, pointed questions, dance challenges
- **Defense**: Wit, deflection, changing subjects
- **Critical Hit**: Perfect comeback or dance move
- **Death**: Social HP reaches 0 = Humiliated and ejected

**Encounter Types**:

**The Interrogation** (Verbal Combat)
- NPC asks probing questions about your identity
- Must maintain cover through responses
- Wrong answers damage Social HP
- Perfect lies restore Social HP

**The Dance Duel** (Rhythm Combat)
- Challenged to dance by Dance Duelists
- Rhythm mini-game determines success
- Public failure = massive Social HP loss
- Victory = Social HP boost + opponent embarrassed

**The Rumor Exchange** (Trade Mechanic)
- Trade information for information
- Value determined by exclusivity and accuracy
- False rumors detected = Social HP damage
- Hot tips boost reputation

**The Approach** (Boss Approach)
- Attempting to reach the Baroness
- Requires passing through social gauntlet
- Each failure reduces approach progress
- Success = Baroness audience

### The Whisper Gallery — Rumor Trading System

**Rumor Types**:
| Rumor Grade | Value | Risk | Source |
|-------------|-------|------|--------|
| Common | 50 Sovs | Low | Anyone |
| Uncommon | 150 Sovs | Medium | Specific NPCs |
| Rare | 400 Sovs | High | Inner Circle |
| Exclusive | 1000 Sovs+ | Very High | Baroness only |

**Trading Mechanics**:
- Rumors have "freshness" — old news worth less
- Can combine rumors for higher value
- False rumors detected by high-INT NPCs
- Quest hints often buried in rumor trades

**Valuable Rumors Available**:
- "The Maestro's real relationship with the Baroness"
- "Which painting in the Opera House is forged"
- "The Card Shark's tell at The Black-Tie Backroom"
- "Where Mozart's actual grave is located"

### Dance Duelist Encounters

**The Dance Duelists**: Elite social enforcers who challenge outsiders

**Dance Challenge Format**:
1. Duelist approaches with dance invitation (refusing = cowardice, Social HP loss)
2. Rhythm mini-game begins (waltz timing)
3. Complexity increases each phase
4. Winner determined by cumulative score
5. Loser suffers public humiliation

**Duelist Roster**:

**The Debutante** (Easy)
- First challenge, forgiving timing
- Dialogue: "Your first masquerade? How... charming. Let's see if you can follow."

**The Veteran** (Medium)
- Tighter windows, occasional tempo changes
- Dialogue: "I've danced here for twenty years. You have twenty seconds to impress me."

**The Champion** (Hard)
- Near-perfect required, surprise tempo shifts
- Dialogue: "They call me 'The Waltz.' I have ended careers on this floor."

**The Baroness' Shadow** (Optional Boss)
- The Baroness' personal dance partner
- Defeating them = direct Baroness audience
- Dialogue: "The Baroness sees potential in you. Prove her right. Or become another forgotten mask."

### The Baroness of Masks (Social Boss)

**Boss Profile**:
- **Title**: The Baroness of Masks, Keeper of Secrets
- **Appearance**: Elaborate gown, mask that shifts colors, ageless presence
- **Personality**: Knows everything, reveals nothing, finds mortals amusing
- **Domain**: Controls all social currency in Sinfonia

**Audience Mechanics** (Not traditional combat):
The Baroness encounter is pure social combat:
- She asks questions that seem innocent but probe deeply
- Wrong answers reduce standing with her
- Perfect answers intrigue her
- Goal: Earn her "favor" (quest unlock, Inner Circle access)

**Baroness Dialogue Samples**:
- "Your mask is... adequate. But masks are for faces. What mask does your SOUL wear?"
- "I've hosted a thousand masquerades. Everyone believes they're unique. Almost none are. Tell me why YOU might be."
- "The Count of Cool, was it? *amused* I've met seventeen counts tonight. Only three were lying. Which are you?"

**Baroness Favor Rewards**:
- **Slight Favor**: Access to rare rumors
- **Moderate Favor**: Inner Circle invitation
- **High Favor**: Baroness' Vault access (late-game gear)
- **Maximum Favor**: The Baroness reveals ONE truth (player's choice of mystery)
