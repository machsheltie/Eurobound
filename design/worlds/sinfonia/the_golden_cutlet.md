## Satirical Design

### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept** **[LEDGER]**: a hyper-commercialized Medieval-Times-meets-beer-hall: fake medieval
armor, aggressive tourist exploitation, forced audience participation, trap doors. The bros paid for a
VIP package that promises free beer and feasting; the fine print turns it into a binding Heritage
Trial, enforced by a man who takes "living history" far too seriously.

**The Irony** **[LEDGER] [DESK]**: the bros paid for the Pass, and the Pass is the trap
(the_schnitzel_king.md:72-76). Everything in the building is fake except the King's costume, which is
absurdly expensive and genuinely authentic. Beating the Trial wins the Free Unlimited Beer: a thimble,
refilled without limit, served with the slate for every "complimentary" goblet.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus** **[LEDGER]**: authenticity tourism and the "living history" upsell.
- **Secondary Targets** **[ROSTER]**: the fine-print VIP package and "unlimited" offers (The Living
  Asterisk); dinner-theater fake chivalry and the "hand-made" upcharge on deep-fried plastic (The
  Triple-Dredged Knight); the "tableside" surcharge and food as theater (Tableside Thumper); forced
  audience participation (Voluntold Wrangler); the souvenir photo upsell (Flash Pillory); the
  "complimentary" drink that turns up on the tab (On-The-House Wenches); method acting and
  authenticity cosplay as a service job (Method Peasant); the hubcap-sized "traditional" portion made
  for photos (Total Plate Eclipse).
- **Cultural Specificity**: the schnitzel-house heritage tavern is the Sinfonia target **[LEDGER]**.
  Further city-specific detail: PENDING (author).
- **Humor Approach**: per `eurobound-satire-voice`; the bros never notice the con.
- **Satire Strength**: full strength, no narrator, no moral framing.

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: aggressive tourist exploitation **[LEDGER]**: the binding fine print, the
  "complimentary" mead chalked on the slate, the mandatory frame on the free photo **[ROSTER]**.
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: PENDING (author) beyond the roster gimmicks.
- **Buffoon Exploitation** **[ROSTER]**: the bros pay for everything the Pass promised free, love the
  food that is being thrown at them, and take every humiliation as VIP treatment.
- **Comedy of Errors**: PENDING (author) beyond the roster.
- **Consequence System** **[ROSTER] [DESK]**: Flash Pillory's post-battle print bill (the only
  post-battle bill in the dungeon); the Wenches' slate, due at the King's defeat with the thimble.

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening** **[LEDGER] [ROSTER]**: they bought Free Unlimited Beer & Traditional
Feasting. The Living Asterisk is "the guy who brings the free beer"; the Wenches' goblets mean "the
Pass promised" free drinks and "it's working"; the Voluntold Wrangler has finally discovered them.

**What is ACTUALLY happening** **[LEDGER] [ROSTER]**: they signed up for the Heritage Trial. No beer is
poured until it is done, every goblet is chalked on the slate, every photo goes on a bill, and the
unlimited beer is a thimble.

**Obvious signs they ignore** **[ROSTER]**: the clauses The Living Asterisk reads aloud ("'Unlimited'
refers to the duration of your obligation, not to the beer."); Chadwick's reading of the Pass ("I read
the whole thing, bro. The top part. Where it says free.").
