## 🎭 Core Concept

### The Racket
**[CANON — author, 2026-08-03]** The Golden Cutlet is a massive, tacky, multi-level "authenticity"
tourist-trap tavern: hyper-commercialized Medieval-Times-meets-beer-hall. Fake medieval armor,
aggressive tourist exploitation, forced audience participation, trap doors.

The bros arrive holding a group-purchased online **"VIP Cultural Heritage Pass"** (Chadwick's idea)
that promises **Free Unlimited Beer & Traditional Feasting**. The fine print obligates them to
complete a **"Traditional Austrian Heritage Trial"** in the basement vaults before a single drop is
poured. When they try to back out and just demand their drinks, the King enforces the contract.

The basement holds a full combat roster per vault. Author direction: real battles, not just
minigames.

> **Naming flag (player-facing text).** "Austrian" is the author's dictated wording and is quoted
> here as dictation. Player-facing text uses no real place names, and the setting bible treats real
> nationality words as real-world inspiration. The approved draft uses "the Heritage Trial" in all
> player-facing lines **[CANON — author approved, 2026-11-12, Eurobound-p1y]**. Whether that is the
> trial's final in-game name: **OPEN QUESTION (author).**

### What Bros Believe
**OPEN QUESTION (author).** Canon fixes only that buying the Pass was Chadwick's idea and that the
bros want the free beer, not the Trial.

### The Comedy Gap
**[CANON — author]** The satire targets are authenticity tourism, the "living history" upsell, and
the fine-print VIP package: a free-beer promise that turns into a binding obligation, enforced by a
man who takes his heritage costume more seriously than the bros take anything. The bros paid for
the Pass, and the Pass is the trap.

### Phases
**[CANON — author approved, 2026-11-12, Eurobound-p1y]**. Triggers are in words; HP thresholds
are OPEN QUESTION (balancing pass).

| Phase | Trigger | Behavior Change |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| 1: "Traditional Feasting, As Promised" | Fight start | The Pass promised Traditional Feasting, so he delivers it by force. His *Course* attacks do damage and inflict **Grease Fingers**. The bros love the food. By the end of the phase the whole party is greasy, which looks like pure penalty (no tech items) and isn't (see Phase 3). Add: the Voluntold Wrangler joins and hooks a bro onstage "for the toast". |
| 2: "The Trial Clause" | Once the King is hurt enough to stop serving | He invokes the Heritage Trial proper and starts pulling levers (canon: trap doors). *Trap Door Ruling* drops one bro into the vault below until he climbs back up on his own, holding a junk item. Add: On-The-House Wenches pour for whoever is still standing. |
| 3: "The Costume Is Real" | Once he's badly hurt | He stops protecting himself and starts protecting the costume. He would rather die than let a tourist stain the brocade. Every hit from a bro with **Grease Fingers** leaves a stain on the costume, and each stain makes the King spend his next turn dabbing it with a napkin and club soda, open to everything. Phase 1's grease is now the party's best weapon. The bros don't know why it's working; the player does. Grease-free bros can still fight him, just the long way. |

### Arena & Gimmick
**[CANON — author]** The Golden Cutlet's basement vaults; the dungeon has trap doors and forced
audience participation. **[CANON — author approved, 2026-11-12, Eurobound-p1y]** Both are in the

party, and in Phase 3 that grease stains the costume and opens the King up.

author ruling): a VIP lanyard from a bachelor party that never came back up; half a pair of
