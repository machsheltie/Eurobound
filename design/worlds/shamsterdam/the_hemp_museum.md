# The Hemp Museum

*Where "educational" meets "we all know why you're here" — a 500-year history you'll forget in 5 minutes*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: shamsterdam_roodelicht_hemp_museum_01
- **Display Name**: The Hemp Museum
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "It's About The PLANT, Actually"
- **Alternative Name**: Hash, Marihuana & Hemp Museum (official, longer, same energy)
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: Roodelicht Row, attempting respectability in the Red Light District
- **Location Type**: Educational-Adjacent Attraction / Gift Shop with Context / Alibi Generator

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 10 AM - 10 PM (museum hours, aggressively reasonable)
- **Stat Requirements**: None (patience stat helps)
- **Item Requirements**: 10 Sovs entry fee (includes "free" hemp tea sample)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (often visited as "cultural" excuse)
- **Special Conditions**: "Combo ticket" with neighboring attraction available (15 Sovs, separate gift shops)

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through gift shop (unavoidable, obviously)
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (wander until you've "learned enough")
- **Failure States**: Cannot be banned (you paid 10 Sovs, they're committed now)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: None (experience is consistent: come, pretend to learn, buy something, leave)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: A museum desperately trying to be educational while knowing exactly why tourists actually visit. The cognitive dissonance between "500 Years of Hemp History" signage and tourists speed-walking to the growing room is the entire joke. It's cultural alibi generation: "We went to a MUSEUM today" (it had live plants and a really well-stocked gift shop).

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: "Educational" tourism as cover for actual interests, the performance of learning
- **Secondary Targets**: Museum gift shop economics, the "hemp vs. marijuana" semantic dance, cultural legitimization of vice
- **Cultural Specificity**: Amsterdam's unique position of having museums about things that are quasi-legal there
- **Humor Approach**: The gap between what the museum presents and what visitors actually want
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets tourist behavior and commercial museum culture, not legitimate hemp history or cannabis policy

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Monetizing the gap between "education" and "interest"
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Selling seeds that are legal to buy, illegal to grow (everywhere else)
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros pay 10 Sovs to look at plants they could see at De Groene Gids for free
- **Comedy of Errors**: Trying to appear cultured while clearly only interested in one exhibit
- **Consequence System**: Purchases here mark you as tourist (staff at coffeeshops recognize the bags)

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Cultural education, legitimate museum experience, intellectual enrichment

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Speed-walking past rope exhibits to look at plants, then buying a grinder

**How impaired are they**: Usually sober coming in (it's a museum!), varies for subsequent locations

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- Spending 45 seconds on "Industrial Hemp," 15 minutes on "Growing Room"
- The gift shop is 40% of the floor space
- "Educational" seeds come with very detailed growing instructions
- The "free hemp tea" is a loss leader for gift shop traffic
- Audio guide exists specifically so they can say it's educational
- Staff answers "where's the growing room?" before you ask

**How locals play along**: Staff maintain educational theater while efficiently directing you toward the gift shop

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Museum Navigation
- **Self-Guided Tour**: Wander freely, no required path, everyone makes same path anyway
- **Audio Guide Option**: 3 Sovs extra, provides actual information, almost never selected
- **Exhibit Interaction**: Each display can be examined for flavor text, most get skipped
- **Speed Run Timer**: Hidden tracker of how fast bros move through industrial section vs. growing room

### Exhibit Engagement System
| Section | Average Visitor Time | Bros' Time | Actual Content Value |
|---------|---------------------|------------|---------------------|
| Entry/Timeline | 2 minutes | 30 seconds | Historical context |
| Industrial Hemp | 10 minutes | 45 seconds | Legitimate fascinating history |
| Historical Usage | 8 minutes | 3 minutes (vintage ads) | Actually interesting if read |
| Growing Room | 5 minutes (intended) | 15 minutes | Plants. Living plants. |
| Gift Shop | "As needed" | 20 minutes | Purchasing validation |

### "Feel the Hemp!" Interactive
- **Location**: Industrial section, fabric sample station
- **Mechanic**: Touch prompt, receive texture description
- **Result**: Brief educational popup, quickly dismissed
- **Bros' Engagement**: "Huh. Okay." *continues toward stairs*

### "Identify the Terpenes!" Mini-Game
- **Location**: Growing section, smell jars
- **Mechanic**: Smell jar, guess the terpene profile from multiple choice
- **Difficulty**: Impossible without actual knowledge
- **Success Rate**: ~10% (lucky guesses)
- **Reward**: +Trivial Knowledge status, good for one coffeeshop conversation starter
- **Failure**: Staff politely corrects you, explains anyway, you forget immediately

### The Growing Room Experience
- **Location**: Second floor, heart of the museum, where everyone actually goes
- **Features**:
  - Observation window to live growing cannabis plants
  - Informational panels about cultivation (for education!)
  - Grow light ambiance (purple glow)
  - Staff positioned to prevent "extended photography"
  - Smell jars (legal way to experience different strains)
  - "No Touching" sign (tested regularly)
- **Gameplay**: Longer interaction time here than rest of museum combined
- **Photography**: Allowed (of the room), discouraged (of the plants close-up, for reasons)
- **Bros' Behavior**: "This is actually really interesting" *takes 40 photos*

### The "Free" Hemp Tea
- **Trigger**: Entry ticket includes "complimentary hemp tea sample"
- **Location**: Small station near gift shop (funneling traffic)
- **Effect**: +Calm status (minimal), -Sovs from gift shop proximity
- **Taste**: "Earthy" (translation: disappointing)
- **Function**: Loss leader, makes you feel you "got something" from the 10 Sovs, puts you in buying mood
- **Dialogue**: "The tea is complimentary. Gift shop is right there. Take your time."

### The Audio Guide (Almost Never Selected)
- **Cost**: 3 Sovs extra
- **Content**: Genuinely informative 45-minute tour
- **Selection Rate**: ~5% of visitors
- **Effect**: If selected, actually provides interesting information
- **Bros' Probability**: 0% (they're not here for that)
- **Staff Reaction**: Visible surprise when requested

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Use audio guide and quote facts later, correctly identify terpene, engage staff in actual historical discussion
- **Lose Bravado**: Ask "where's the weed?" immediately, get corrected on hemp vs. marijuana, buy the obvious tourist shirt

## System Integration
### Cultural Alibi System
- **Effect**: Visiting the Hemp Museum provides "Cultural Activity" flag
- **Usage**: Can be referenced in future dialogue: "We went to a MUSEUM"
- **Cross-Reference**: Kitchen confessionals at The Tolerance Hostel may challenge this alibi
- **Bravado Context**: Claiming museum visit as primary Shamsterdam activity: +Bravado if believed, -Bravado if called out

### Gift Shop Bag Marker
- **Effect**: Purchasing anything produces branded bag
- **Consequence**: Carrying bag marks you as "museum tourist" at coffeeshops
- **NPC Reactions**: Budtenders may comment, prices may reflect
- **Optional**: Can discard bag to remove marker (lose style points)

### Cross-Location Dependencies
- **De Groene Gids**: Staff recognizes museum bags, may comment on "doing things backwards"
- **Café de Schaamte**: Bros can discuss "cultural experience" over espresso (Havok unimpressed)
- **The Tolerance Hostel**: Kitchen conversations may include museum "recommendations"
- **Coffeeshop Quarter**: Museum visit can unlock dialogue options about strains/terpenes (usually wrong)

### Terpene Knowledge Application
- **If Mini-Game Passed**: Can attempt to identify strains at coffeeshops
- **Success Rate**: Low (knowledge degrades rapidly)
- **Effect**: Correct identification: +Bravado, staff respect
- **Effect**: Incorrect identification: Staff correction, mild embarrassment
