## Visual Design
### Exterior Description
- **PROPOSED**: a glossy pastel frontage with the gift shop's window as the biggest thing on it, ring lights glowing in the display. It must look nothing like the Museum of Questionable Art, the Hemp Museum or The Gallery of Generational Wealth.

### Interior Visuals (author: two wings plus the gift shop)
- **Wing A, the Selfie Factory**: pastel photo sets (ball pit, giant donut, infinity mirror room) and zero art. One real painting hangs in a corridor nobody walks down. That corridor is canon's "nobody enters a single gallery". The painting (author, 2026-11-13, Eurobound Desk wdti-q4): "A genuine 1600s self-portrait, the oldest selfie in the building; nobody has ever looked at it". No real artist is named.
- **Wing B, the Museum of You**: every exhibit is a mirror or a screen showing the visitor; every plaque credits "the artist" (you).
- **The selfie gift shop** (author): self-checkout only (wdti-q3). Stupid overpriced selfie sticks, ring lights, "Have you hugged a narcissist today?" t-shirts. It also prints your face on mugs (concept B).

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
A museum whose only subject is the visitor. The art is the backdrop; the visitor is the exhibit.

### Cultural Targets
- Selfie museums and "Instagrammable" experiences.
- Narcissism sold back to the narcissist at gift-shop prices.
