# Shamsterdam Condom Vending Machines — City-Wide Prop System

*Thirty-four machines. Nobody looks at them. Three Americans photograph every single one.*

## System Identity
- **System ID**: `shamsterdam_system_veiligomaat_01`
- **Display Name**: The Veilig-o-Maat Network
- **English Translation**: "Safe-o-Mat" (the brand name is stamped on every unit; nobody in Shamsterdam says it out loud)
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Available Since Before You Were Interesting"
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam (all districts)
- **System Type**: Recurring Environmental Prop / Micro-Vendor / Collectible Circuit / Running Gag
- **Not a Location**: This is a prop class, not a node. Machines are placed *inside* existing location scenes. No machine gets its own map.

---

## Core Concept

Condom vending machines are bolted to walls all over Shamsterdam. They are as unremarkable as post boxes. Locals have walked past the same machine four thousand times and could not tell you what colour it is.

To the bros, each one is a discovery.

The gag is entirely one-directional: the city is not doing anything. The machines are not a joke, an attraction, a provocation, or a statement. They are infrastructure — the municipal equivalent of a fire extinguisher — and three grown men are treating a wall-mounted public health fixture like they have found a Renaissance fresco. Chadwick photographs each one. Lord Pilsner delivers an unsolicited lecture about "European maturity" while standing in a bike lane. Bradley buys one item at machine #3 on the first afternoon and never mentions it again for the rest of the game.

**Design rule**: no NPC ever explains the machines, defends them, is embarrassed by them, or acknowledges that the bros are excited. The complete absence of local reaction is the punchline.

## Placement Across Districts

**Total city-wide: 34 machines.** The count is fixed, authored, and used as the denominator for the collectible layer.

| District | Count | Placement Character |
|----------|-------|---------------------|
| Roodelicht Row | 11 | Dense, conspicuous, immaculate, restocked twice weekly. The district treats them as a utility. |
| Coffeeshop Quarter | 6 | Beside ATMs and cigarette machines, always in a row of three vending units so the joke is that it's the *boring* one |
| Canal Ring Promenade | 5 | Bolted to bridge abutments and houseboat gangway posts, half of them lichen-covered |
| Cheese & Clog Corner | 4 | Wedged between clog displays, which locals find neither funny nor notable |
| Museum Mile | 3 | Including the plinth unit (see Variants) |
| Voetgangerstunnel | 2 | Zone 2 and Zone 4. The Zone 4 unit is not on any map and Henk maintains it does not exist. |
| Unclassified | 3 | The three that make no sense. See Variants 4–6. |

### Conspicuous vs. Absurd Placement

**Conspicuous (24 machines)** — exactly where you'd expect. Beside a pharmacy. Beside a cash machine. At the mouth of a nightlife street. Backlit, signposted, multilingual, entirely reasonable. These are the ones the bros photograph *first* and are the least interesting.

**Absurd (10 machines)** — placed with total municipal sincerity in locations that read as surreal to a visitor and as "well, there was a wall there" to a local:
- Bolted to the empty plinth where the **I SHAMSTERDAM** sign used to be
- Inside the Hemp Museum's industrial-fibre exhibit
- At the bottom of a canal (still illuminated, still functional)
- Inside a 1-Sov pay toilet in Roodelicht Row, so accessing a 2-Sov machine costs 3 Sovs
- Facing directly into a brick wall at 30cm clearance, on the Canal Ring — you can operate it but you cannot read it
- Voetgangerstunnel Zone 4, in a corridor with no other features, no lighting fixture of its own, and no signage
- Two metres from the Condomerie's front door, which Femke has never once commented on
- On the Klompen Konfessional's exterior wall, under a hand-painted sign reading "AUTHENTIC DUTCH EXPERIENCE" that predates the machine and refers to the clogs
- Behind the FEBO Automat's heating unit, permanently warm to the touch
- In the Café de Schaamte courtyard, where Agent Havok has used it as a dead drop exactly once

## Machine Variants

### Variant 1 — Mk. IV "Blauwe Bok" (Pristine)
**Count**: 14 | **Districts**: Roodelicht Row, Museum Mile, Coffeeshop Quarter

Backlit blue-white panel, contactless payment, six-language interface, tamper-proof housing, spotless glass. Restocked twice weekly by Ilse (see NPCs). A small municipal decal in the corner reads **GGD GOEDGEKEURD** (health-service approved) with a certificate number and an inspection date that is genuinely recent.

- Dispenses Tier 1 and Tier 2 stock
- Card reader works, first time, every time — which the bros find suspicious
- Interaction is instant and undramatic; there is no animation flourish, no jingle, no "ka-ching." A small clunk. That is all.

### Variant 2 — Mk. II "De Guldenvreter" (Ancient, Jammed)
**Count**: 4 | **Districts**: Canal Ring Promenade (2), Cheese & Clog Corner (1), Voetgangerstunnel Zone 4 (1)

*"The Guilder-Eater."* Enamel-painted steel, sun-bleached from red to salmon. Pull-knob mechanism, no electronics, no card slot. The coin slot is sized for **guilders** — a defunct pre-Sovs coinage nobody has been able to spend in living memory. No exchange exists and nobody offers one; a guilder is a novelty lump of metal that happens to be the exact size this machine wants.

- Cannot be operated with current coinage at all. The bros will try. Repeatedly.
- Requires a **Guilder Coin** item (see Quest Hooks: The Guilder Problem)
- Even with a correct coin: **40% jam chance** per pull. Jamming produces a flat mechanical clack and nothing else.
- Stock inside is visible through a scratched window and is very obviously from 2011
- Percussive maintenance (hit it) has a 15% success rate and a 10% chance of dispensing *two*, which the bros will describe as "beating the system"
- The Zone 4 unit's stock is not from 2011. Nobody has established what it is from.

### Variant 3 — "De Bakfietsmaat" (Repurposed)
**Count**: 1 | **District**: Canal Ring Promenade, outside a bike repair shop

A Mk. IV housing that a bike shop acquired, rehoused, and refilled with what they actually sell. The VEILIG-O-MAAT branding is intact because removing it would have required a ladder.

Now dispenses:
- Inner tube patch kits (3 Sovs)
- Rear bike light, red, one AA not included (6 Sovs)
- Cable lock, comically inadequate (9 Sovs)
- **Slot 7 only**: still contains the original 2011 stock. Nobody has emptied it. Nobody will.

The shop owner has never mentioned the machine to anyone. If asked about it, an NPC line fires: *"It was there. It holds things. It is a good machine."*

### Variant 4 — "De Plinth-o-Maat" (Nonsensical Placement, Museum Mile)
**Count**: 1

Bolted to the concrete plinth where the **I SHAMSTERDAM** sign used to stand before it was removed. Tourists still gather at the empty plinth and photograph each other beside nothing. Now they photograph each other beside nothing *and a condom machine*, and the machine is the only object in the frame.

- Interacting triggers the game's single best unlabelled screenshot
- Adds a photo to the **Shamsterdam Memories** album captioned automatically as `I SHAMSTERDAM (2 of 2)`
- Cross-reference: `shamsterdam.md` → Museum Mile → the empty plinth gag

### Variant 5 — "De Verdronken Automaat" (The Drowned Machine)
**Count**: 1 | **District**: Canal bottom, accessible via the Canal Bike Recovery diving mini-game

At the bottom of the canal, among the 12,000 bicycles, sitting upright on the silt. **Still illuminated.** Still functional. The card reader accepts payment underwater on the first attempt.

- Always machine **#34** in the collectible circuit. The last one requires a dive.
- Dispenses one item: **"?"** — see Tier 5
- No NPC will discuss it. The Canal Bike Recovery quest-giver, if asked, changes the subject to bicycles.
- Bradley refuses to discuss having seen it. This is the only subject on which Bradley is completely silent.

### Variant 6 — "De Hennepautomaat" (Hemp Museum)
**Count**: 1

Inside the Hemp Museum's industrial-fibre exhibit, which spends four display boards insisting that hemp is a versatile agricultural material and that visitors are thinking about the wrong thing. The machine dispenses hemp-fibre packaging, includes a genuinely informative leaflet about crop rotation, and is the single most educational object in the building.

- The world's most bored tour guide walks past it eleven times per tour and has never gestured at it
- Buying from it awards the **"Actually Learned Something"** micro-achievement, the only one in the Hemp Museum

## Product Range & Escalating Absurdity

The stock tier is gated by machine rarity, not by player level. Common machines carry boring stock; the rarer the machine, the stranger the slots — because the rare machines are the ones nobody audits.

### Tier 1 — Standaard (18 machines)
Deliberately unfunny. This is the baseline against which everything else is measured.

| Item | Price | Effect |
|------|-------|--------|
| Standaard Drie (3-pack) | 2 Sovs | Inventory item. Sets `prepared` flag. No stat effect. No dialogue. |
| Nachtpakket (6-pack) | 3 Sovs | Same, bulk. Weighs nothing. Means nothing. |

### Tier 2 — Toeristenlijn (9 machines)
Where the city starts monetising your fascination.

| Item | Price | Effect |
|------|-------|--------|
| Molenzegel ("Windmill Seal") | 4 Sovs | Souvenir. +Comedy. Chadwick auto-photographs the packaging. |
| Tulpenreeks (4 colours) | 4 Sovs | Cosmetic set. Cross-ref: De Botanische Leugen sells the bulbs that also won't survive customs. |
| Klompje (clog-shaped tin) | 5 Sovs | +0 stats, +100% Tourist Identification. The tin is the product. |
| Oranje Editie | 5 Sovs | It is always orange. There is no explanation and none is offered. |

### Tier 3 — Regionale Editie (4 machines)
The regional variants. These are real products, sincerely manufactured, and that is the entire joke.

| Item | Price | Effect |
|------|-------|--------|
| Haring-editie | 6 Sovs | Bravado check to purchase. Cross-ref: De Zilte Waarheid. Nobody can establish whether it is a joke. It is not a joke. |
| Stroopwafel-geur | 6 Sovs | +Comfort, +Munchies risk. Smells correct. Deeply unsettling. |
| "Gezellig" Variety Pack | 6 Sovs | The packaging uses the word wrong. Increments the game-wide Gezellig Misuse Counter. |
| Hennep-vezel | 6 Sovs | Hemp Museum only. Includes an actual crop-rotation leaflet. |

### Tier 4 — De Rariteiten (2 machines)
The machines nobody restocks correctly. Slots 7 and 8 contain items that are not, by any definition, the product on the front of the machine.

| Item | Price | Effect |
|------|-------|--------|
| Slot 7: An Emergency Rain Poncho | 8 Sovs | Genuinely useful. Actually waterproof. The single most practical item purchasable in Roodelicht Row. |
| Slot 8: One (1) Battery, Size AA | 8 Sovs | No explanation. Fits the Bakfietsmaat bike light. Nobody connects these facts. |

### Tier 5 — De Vraagteken (1 machine)
A single slot, labelled only **"?"**. 10 Sovs. Located in **De Verdronken Automaat** at the canal bottom.

Randomised on purchase from the entire table above, plus:
- **4% chance**: a small plastic hippo (Fabergé Egg Hunt callback — the hippo is identical to the quest item and is *not* the quest item)
- **1% chance**: an empty package containing a folded receipt reading `BEDANKT` (thank you) and nothing else

## Interaction Model

### Base Interaction
1. Approach prompt: **"Examine machine"** / **"Use machine"**
2. Examine → flavour text, one line, deadpan, plus the machine's circuit number (`Veilig-o-Maat #17 of 34`)
3. Use → slot grid UI, six to eight slots, prices in Sovs, no confirmation dialogue for Tier 1–2
4. Dispense → small mechanical clunk. No fanfare. **Never** a jingle.
5. Chadwick photographs it automatically if the party is together. This cannot be disabled. It is a feature.

### Touch & Mobile Notes
- Interaction hitbox 44px minimum; machines are wall-mounted so the hitbox is a vertical rectangle
- Slot grid UI reuses the FEBO Automat panel layout with a different skin — deliberate visual rhyme between "wall of hot croquettes" and "wall of prophylactics," both presented by the city with identical bureaucratic neutrality

### The Sticker Layer (Micro-Collectible)
Every fourth machine has a sticker slapped over part of the branding. Nine stickers city-wide, each a single line of readable micro-text. Examples for the writing pass:
- A band flyer for a gig that happened in 2019
- `KAPOT` (broken) — on a machine that works perfectly
- A tram network diagram, obsolete
- A hand-written `WERKT WEL` (it does work) over the `KAPOT` sticker, in different ink
- A "Stay Away Campaign" discouragement pamphlet corner, torn off and stuck down
- `WILLEM WEET` — *Willem knows*. Same hand as the Voetgangerstunnel graffiti. This is on the Zone 4 machine.

Photographing all nine stickers is a hidden achievement: **"Read the Small Print."**

## The Collectible Layer — The Circuit

The bros treat 34 pieces of public health infrastructure as a scavenger hunt. Nobody asked them to.

| Milestone | Reward |
|-----------|--------|
| 5 machines | Chadwick starts numbering them out loud. "That's five. FIVE." |
| 10 machines | Title: **"Amateur Cartographer"** |
| 20 machines | Title: **"Route Planner"**. Ilse begins to recognise the party. She does not greet them. |
| 30 machines | Lord Pilsner's "European maturity" monologue unlocks its final, longest variant (47 seconds; skippable; nobody skips it) |
| 34 machines | Title: **"Fully Stocked"** + Ilse hands over a spare Veilig-o-Maat key fob |

**Veilig-o-Maat Key Fob** (cosmetic): opens nothing. Cannot be used. NPCs in other Shamsterdam locations occasionally glance at it and say nothing — same treatment as Willem's Accordion. Deliberate mechanical rhyme.

**Album integration**: each machine auto-logs to the Shamsterdam Memories photo album as `Machine #n of 34`. The album page for machines is, by the end of the city, the single largest section — larger than Casa Rosé, larger than the canals, larger than any of the actual sights. Neither the game nor any NPC comments on this.

| Effect | Trigger | Mechanics |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Prepared** | Hold any Tier 1–2 stock | Passive flag. No combat effect. Occasionally unlocks a dialogue option in which a bro says something marginally less stupid. Never acknowledged by anyone, including the bro. |

## Quest Hooks

### 1. "De Ronde" (The Round) — Micro-Quest
Ride along on Ilse's restocking route.
- **Trigger**: interact with Ilse while she is servicing a machine, three separate times
- **Content**: forty minutes of the flattest possible conversation about logistics, stock rotation, and which machines get vandalised (none — vandalism is a tourist assumption)
- **Reward**: the full 34-machine map, which trivialises the circuit and which the bros will still manage to misread

### 2. The Guilder Problem
De Guldenvreter needs a coin from a dead currency. Three sources:
- **Canal Bike Recovery** dive: one guilder in the silt (free, requires the dive)
- **FEBO Automat** coin return: one guilder, discoverable by examining the coin slot after any purchase (free, requires one examine)
- **Het Rode Winkeltje** (Roodelicht Row gift shop): Trijntje sells one for **12 Sovs**. It is not money and cannot be spent anywhere; she tells you so, unprompted, before selling it. She is not scamming anyone — she is pricing a novelty coin, and the bros buy it anyway, having been informed.

### 3. The Question Mark
Find the Tier 5 machine. Requires the Canal Bike Recovery dive. Feeds "Fully Stocked."

### 4. Dead Drop (Fabergé Egg Hunt tie-in, optional)
Agent Havok uses the Café de Schaamte courtyard machine as a dead drop exactly once, in a scene where the dead drop is entirely unnecessary because he is sitting four metres away. He insists on the tradecraft. Slot 6. The item is a folded napkin.

## Relationship to the Condomerie (Non-Competition)

**This is a hard design constraint. The machines must not undercut Condomerie Het Gulden Vlies.**

| | Condomerie Het Gulden Vlies | The Veilig-o-Maat Network |
|---|---|---|
| Register | Artisanal, curated, boutique, staffed | Industrial, anonymous, unstaffed, everywhere |
| Comedy engine | Human awkwardness in front of a professional | Total absence of human reaction |
| Price band | 10–100 Sovs | 2–10 Sovs |
| Hours | 11:00–22:00 | 24 hours |
| Product overlap | Sections 1 and 4 only | Tiers 1–3 only |
| Gift wrapping | Yes, three tiers | The machine does not know what a gift is |
| Staff judgement | None, professionally maintained | None, structurally impossible |

**Enforced separation**: the machines may never carry anything from the Condomerie's **Section 3 (Sizes)**, **Section 5 (Art Pieces)**, the **Mystery Box**, the **Designer Limited Edition**, or the **Gift Card**. Those exist because a human being is standing there to sell them and a human being is required to survive buying them. The machine cannot generate that comedy and must not try.

**Femke's position** (add to Condomerie dialogue pool):
> "The machine? The machine is fine. The machine is for two in the morning."
>
> "We are for a Tuesday afternoon and a question. The machine has never answered a question in its life."
>
> *(if the party has bought from 5+ machines before entering)* "Ah. You have been meeting the automats." *(beat)* "They are not competition. They are colleagues."

**Joost's position**:
> "There's one two metres from our door. Yes, I know. No, it doesn't bother us. It's a wall. Walls have things on them."

**Mechanical link**: purchasing at the Condomerie after 10+ machine purchases fires Femke's colleagues line and awards nothing. It is a line, not a reward. That is correct.

## NPCs

### Ilse — Route Technician
- **NPC ID**: `shamsterdam_npc_ilse_route_tech_01`
- **Role**: Restocking technician, city-wide roaming ambient NPC, quest-giver for "De Ronde"
- **Appearance**: 30s, high-vis vest over a fleece, cargo trousers, a keyring the size of a fist, a small unbranded white van she cannot legally park anywhere she parks it
- **Behaviour**: Appears at a random machine somewhere in the city on a rotating schedule. Services it. Leaves. Two machines per in-game day.
- **Demeanour**: Completely unbothered. Working. Has a route to finish.

**The core gag beat** (scripted, fires once, high priority):
> The party is photographing a machine. Ilse arrives to restock it. She does not ask them to move. She works around them — reaching between Chadwick and his shot, opening the panel past Lord Pilsner's elbow, restocking, closing, locking, leaving. The bros thank her.
>
> **Ilse**: "Ja."

**Dialogue**:
- "Thirty-four. Twice a week. It's a route." *(This is the most she has ever said about her job.)*
- "The one in the tunnel, Zone 4 — that one's not mine. It's on the list. It's not mine." *(She does not elaborate. Ever.)*
- "People think they get smashed up. They don't. Nobody touches them." *(pause)* "Why would you."
- *(asked about the canal machine)* "It works. I've seen the numbers." *(She has not been down there. She checks the till remotely. The till reports sales.)*
- *(after 20 machines photographed)* *(She recognises them. She does not greet them. She adjusts her route slightly to avoid them and does not consider this a decision.)*
- *(on completing "De Ronde")* "That's it. That's the round." *(hands over the fob)* "Don't lose it. Doesn't open anything."

**Voice direction**: flat, occupied, not rude. The tone of someone answering a question while counting. Never exasperated — exasperation would imply the bros have registered.

## The Running Gag — Authoring Rules

1. **No local ever reacts.** NPC pathing actively routes *around* bros who are photographing a machine. No pointing, no staring, no comment, no eye-roll. The eye-roll would be an acknowledgement, and an acknowledgement kills the joke.
2. **The machines are never funny on their own.** Every laugh comes from the bros' response to something entirely mundane. If a machine has a joke printed on it, the joke is wrong.
3. **Bradley's silence is canon.** He buys one thing at machine #3 on day one. It never appears in his inventory display. It is never mentioned. If a writer is tempted to pay this off, they must not.
4. **Lord Pilsner's monologue escalates.** Four variants, unlocked at 5 / 15 / 25 / 30 machines, on the theme of "European maturity about the human body," each delivered while doing something that proves he has understood nothing — standing in a bike lane, blocking a doorway, holding a clog-shaped tin.
5. **Chadwick's photo count is the real counter.** The album section is the largest in the city by the end. Nobody says anything about this.
6. **The clunk.** Every dispense is one small mechanical clunk. Never a jingle, never a chime, never a fanfare. The audio team must resist. The absence of ceremony is the entire sound design brief.
