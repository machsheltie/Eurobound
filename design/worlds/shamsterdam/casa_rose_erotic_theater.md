## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 8 PM - 3 AM (shows run hourly on the hour)
- **Stat Requirements**: None for entry (Bravado check to actually watch shows)

- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (usually reached via bachelor party peer pressure)
- **Special Conditions**: Mandatory drink purchase inside (15 Sovs minimum), group discounts that aren't really discounts

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through front door (walk of contemplation past the pink elephant)
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion requirement (survive one show, technically "complete")
- **Failure States**: Cannot be banned (they've seen worse than you)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Veteran" status after 2+ visits (staff recognizes you, unclear if this is good)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The tourist-trap strip club that exists purely because bachelor parties expect it to exist. The "famous banana show" is a rite of passage nobody actually enjoys — it's performed, witnessed, Instagrammed (illegally), and then exaggerated in retelling for decades. The entire business model runs on FOMO, peer pressure, and the sunk cost of that 65 Sovs entry fee. Everything is overpriced, understaffed, and exactly what you came for even though you'll claim otherwise.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Bachelor party ritual tourism, paying for "experiences" you'll lie about
- **Secondary Targets**: Red light district commodification, the gap between expectation and reality, mandatory group activities
- **Cultural Specificity**: Amsterdam's infamous live show scene reduced to tourist obligation
- **Humor Approach**: The discomfort of describing what you saw vs. what actually happened
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets tourist behavior and commercial exploitation, not performers or sex workers

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Extracting maximum money from tourists who feel obligated to be here
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Every element designed to maximize spend: entry fee, drink minimums, "premium" upsells
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros peer-pressure each other into spending 100 Sovs+ on something none of them wanted
- **Comedy of Errors**: The gap between what the show is and how they'll describe it to people back home
- **Consequence System**: "Witnessed Something" status effect, +Discomfort, stories that don't match

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Legendary bachelor party experience, once-in-a-lifetime cultural immersion

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Tourist trap extracting 100 Sovs+ per person for fog machine and awkward eye contact avoidance

**How impaired are they**: Increasingly impaired (drink minimum), impairment is the business model

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- 15 Sovs drinks (watered down)
- "Premium" seats are just closer to things you don't want to be close to
- Staff has seen a thousand bachelor parties and is bored by all of them
- The "world famous" show is performed for tourists who don't know any other shows exist
- Group "discount" costs more per person than individual tickets
- Everyone's taking photos despite "no cameras" (photos are the real product)

**How locals play along**: They don't come here — this is 100% tourist extraction, locals haven't been since 1997

### Entry & Seating System
- **Promoter Encounter**: Sidewalk NPC tries to guide you in, offers "special deal" (not special, not a deal)
- **Ticket Purchase**: Counter service, upsell to "premium" (85 Sovs), group "discount" calculation that always costs more
- **Drink Minimum**: 15 Sovs minimum first drink purchase, menu intentionally confusing
- **Seating Selection**: Choose section (front/middle/back), each has different mechanics

### The Show Experience (Abstracted)
**Note**: Actual show content is abstracted for obvious reasons — focus is on bros' reactions and discomfort comedy

- **Pre-Show**: Announcement, lights dim, fog machine activates, bros realize where they are
- **During Show**: Reaction mini-game — maintain composure while processing what's happening
- **Post-Show**: Bros attempt to describe what they saw, descriptions never match
- **Bravado Check**: Pass = maintain cool, describe experience as "interesting cultural moment"
- **Bravado Fail**: Visible discomfort, descriptions become increasingly vague and inaccurate

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Order confidently, tip generously (even though you shouldn't), maintain composure during show, describe experience as "educational"

## System Integration
### Status Effect Application
- **Witnessed Something**: Applied after show, lasts until next city
  - Effect: Random dialogue options reference "that thing in Shamsterdam"
  - Bros' descriptions never quite match what players "saw"
- **Drink Minimum Achieved**: Required status to remain in venue, tracked
- **Premium Experience**: Front row modifier, increased Participation Risk
- **Pink Elephant Photo**: Added to album automatically, Bravado effect varies by context shown

### The Story Retelling System
After visiting Casa Rosé, bros will randomly reference the experience:
- **Lord Pilsner's Version**: Sophisticated, worldly, "really quite artistic"
- **Chadwick's Version**: "Unfortunately I didn't get footage but trust me it was CONTENT"
- **Bradley's Version**: Description is metaphors only
- **Player Note**: All three versions contradict each other

### Cross-Location Dependencies
- **Café de Schaamte**: Next-morning espresso location, bros process experience over coffee
- **The Tolerance Hostel**: Fellow guests ask "did you go to Casa Rosé?" (everyone has an opinion)
- **De Groene Gids**: Bros may have been here first, impairment level affects show experience
- **The Pink Elephant Photo**: Appears in other tourists' photos as background (you're everyone's backdrop)

### Time-Based Mechanics
- **Show Schedule**: Hourly starting at 9 PM, "special late show" at 1 AM (same show)
- **Peak Bachelor Party Hours**: 10 PM - midnight (maximum matching-shirt density)
