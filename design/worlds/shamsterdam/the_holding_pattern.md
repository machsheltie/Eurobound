# The Holding Pattern

*One SOV. No change. Four cubicles. The longest queue in The Flesh Market, and every demographic in it.*

> **Source:** canon is `roodelicht_row_district.md` §12 (lines 603-616), Cascade stage 13. Name: Eurobound Desk `ucpf-q34` (was De Bijna-Toiletten). Building look: Desk `aogw-q1`, verbatim: "A narrow canal-house shopfront, turnstile in the doorway, four cubicle doors through the window". ## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: `shamsterdam_roodelicht_holding_pattern_01`
- **Display Name**: The Holding Pattern
- **Satirical Subtitle**: none ruled
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: The Flesh Market, Zone 1/2 boundary (between the window corridor, Pane & Pleasure, and the square, De Pauze)
- **Location Type**: Pay toilets / Cascade set piece (stage 13)

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Cost**: 1 SOV at the turnstile. No change given. The attendant cannot change a note or lend a SOV.
- **Time Restrictions**: open whenever the district is (canon: "the longest queue in the district at all hours")
- **Special Conditions**: the queue. The crate does not fit through the turnstile (Cascade stage 13).

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: back out through the turnstile into the queue's tail
- **Failure States**: arriving without exactly one SOV

## Visual Design
### Exterior Description (author, aogw-q1)
- **Structure**: a narrow canal-house shopfront.
- **Doorway**: the 1-SOV turnstile sits in the doorway itself.
- **Window**: the four cubicle doors are visible through the shop window.
- **The queue**: "containing every demographic simultaneously" (canon). It is the longest queue in the district.
- **Signage (canon, verbatim)**:
  > `1 SOV`
  > `NO CHANGE GIVEN. NO CHANGE HELD. NO CHANGE EXISTS.`
  > `CHANGE MACHINE OUT OF ORDER — SORRY` *(dated and signed. The date is four years ago. The signature is illegible and possibly two people.)*
  > `THE ATTENDANT CANNOT CHANGE A NOTE, CANNOT LEND YOU A SOV, AND CANNOT BE MOVED BY YOUR STORY. THIRTY THOUSAND PEOPLE HAVE TOLD HER A STORY.`
- **Shopfront name lettering**: PROPOSED: "THE HOLDING PATTERN" painted on the fascia in the same plain style as the notices.

### Interior Visuals
- Four cubicles, seen through the window; the turnstile; the attendant's post.
- **A Wrap-O-Matic is mounted inside**, so reaching a 2-SOV machine costs 3 SOVS. "Nobody has ever raised this." (canon; cross-ref `shamsterdam_condom_machines.md`)

### Technical Specifications
- **Color Palette (PROPOSED)**: Canal Brick #6E3B2E (house front), Municipal Tile #E9E6DF (interior wall tile seen through the window), Turnstile Chrome #B8BCC2, Notice Laminate #F4F1E8 (the four notices), Corridor Spill #C8324A (red light leaking in from the Zone 1 side).
- Must not look like the other canal houses on the plates (feedback: no look-alike buildings): vary storeys and roofline in the plan.

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**The great leveller** (canon). Everybody needs it, nobody has change, and the rules are absolute.

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- The 3-SOV condom (1 SOV to get in, 2 SOVS at the machine).
- A change-machine apology four years old, signed by possibly two people.

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios (canon, Cascade stage 13)
"The Fuse has detonated, the chips have detonated, the schedule is a ruin, the crate does not fit through a turnstile, and a man needs one SOV. **Peak physical farce.** Nobody helps and nobody is cruel; everybody is simply already in the queue and having a better night."
