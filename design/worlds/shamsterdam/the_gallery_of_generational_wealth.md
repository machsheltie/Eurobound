## Visual Design
### Exterior Description
- **PROPOSED**: the grandest frontage on Museum Mile, so it looms over The Dong Palace next door: columns, gilt lettering, a velvet rope at the door, and a brass donor plaque bigger than the door itself. It must look nothing like the Museum of Questionable Art or the Hemp Museum.

### Interior Visuals (concept A, author-approved)
- **The portrait galleries**: portraits of men who never worked.
- **The yacht wing**: inherited yachts, in miniature.
- **The trust-fund document hall**: trust-fund paperwork, lit like holy relics.
- **The side room, the Nepo Baby Hall of Fame** (author): portraits of the founders' descendants, each with a plaque listing their achievements. Every plaque reads **"Achievements: Was Born."**

### Technical Specifications
- **Color Palette (PROPOSED)**: Vault Gilt #C9A227 (lettering, frames, donor plaque), Old Money Green #1F3A2D (gallery walls), Marble Hush #E7E2D6 (floors, columns), Velvet Rope Red #7A1E2B, Relic Spotlight #FFF3C4 (the document hall). Neighbour: The Dong Palace's Civic Stone #9C9184 frontage; the two must read as different buildings side by side.

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
A museum of other people's inheritances. The pun on the name is one the bros never land on (canon).

### Cultural Targets
- Old money and inherited wealth presented as culture.
- Art as a tax instrument: the docents brag that the paintings were bought as tax write-offs (author).
- Nepotism: the Hall of Fame.

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
- **What bros THINK**: they're among their own kind; generational wealth is a club they're basically in.
- **What's ACTUALLY happening**: they paid more than their flight to look at proof they will never be rich.
