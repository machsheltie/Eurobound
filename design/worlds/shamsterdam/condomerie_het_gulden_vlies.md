*The world's first specialty condom shop, where protection becomes performance art and your purchase history haunts you*

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 11 AM - 10 PM (civilized hours for uncivilized purchases)
- **Stat Requirements**: Bravado check to enter solo, no check with group (safety in numbers)
- **Item Requirements**: Gold for purchases (3 Sovs-50), willingness to make eye contact with staff
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (the shop draws the curious)
- **Special Conditions**: Bachelor parties receive automatic "group discount" (and group judgment)

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through front door, past other browsing tourists, avoiding eye contact
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (though staff remember repeat customers)
- **Failure States**: Cannot fail (purchases are judgment-free, theoretically)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Condomerie Regular" status after 3 visits (staff nod, other customers notice)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Condomerie Het Gulden Vlies is a REAL Amsterdam institution (opened 1987), and the satire is the beautiful awkwardness of a shop that treats condoms as luxury goods. It's simultaneously progressive (sexual health! normalization! education!) and hilarious (grown adults nervously giggling at novelty shapes). The shop is completely professional; the customers are completely not.

The "Golden Fleece" name is a mythological reference treated with absolute seriousness by staff and absolute immaturity by tourists. The satire isn't the shop — it's the human inability to buy protection without becoming twelve years old inside.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Adult human inability to handle sexual health products maturely
- **Secondary Targets**: Bachelor party culture, souvenir purchasing logic, "I'm buying this as a JOKE" denial
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dutch sexual openness vs. tourist sexual awkwardness
- **Humor Approach**: The gap between the shop's professionalism and customer behavior
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Respects sexual health normalization, satirizes tourist awkwardness

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Marking up novelty items because tourists will pay for the "story"
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Amsterdam's openness creates tourist discomfort goldmine
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros buying "gag gifts" they definitely won't use (definitely)
- **Comedy of Errors**: The facial expressions, the nervous laughter, the group dynamics
- **Consequence System**: "Novelty Purchase" inventory items with unclear future purpose

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Hilarious bachelor party shopping, "gag gifts" for the groom

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Sexual health retail therapy disguised as comedy

**How impaired are they**: Usually somewhat (Red Light District adjacent, drinking likely)

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- They're spending significant money on "jokes"
- The staff are completely serious
- The products are real, functional items
- Their nervous laughter reveals everything
- Someone has to CARRY these back to the hostel

**The truth**: 60% of "gag gift" purchases are quietly kept by the purchaser

**How locals interact**: Staff maintain complete professionalism regardless of customer behavior

- **Exit**: The walk of pride/shame depending on bag visibility

### The "Gag Gift" Purchase System
- **Declaration**: Player can declare purchase as "gag gift" or "personal"
- **Effect**: No gameplay difference (the shop doesn't judge)
- **Internal Tracking**: Game secretly tracks the ratio (for epilogue humor)
- **The Reality**: Bros' "gag gifts" outnumber "personal" 5:1 (sure, bros)

### Staff Interaction System
**Staff Approach Levels**:
1. **Hands-Off**: Staff remain at counter, customer browses freely
2. **Available**: Staff offer assistance if customer lingers, no pressure
3. **Educational**: Staff provide information when asked, comprehensive knowledge
4. **Bachelor Party Protocol**: Staff switch to efficient, non-judgmental bulk processing

### The Bachelor Party Experience
- **Trigger**: Enter with 3+ party members
- **Effect**: "Bachelor Party" flag activated
- **Staff Response**: Subtle shift to group-handling mode
- **Discount**: 10% off bulk purchases (they know what you're here for)
- **The Groom Spotlight**: Staff may ask "Who's the groom?" (groom becomes red)
- **Photography Policy**: "Exterior only, please" (they've had incidents)

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Purchase without visible embarrassment, ask staff for recommendations, buy gift card with straight face
- **Lose Bravado**: Excessive nervous laughter, drop product and fumble, ask if items are "real"
- **Group Dynamics**: Bravado easier to maintain in group, harder solo
- **The Counter Moment**: Making eye contact during transaction affects Bravado

### The "Did You Really Need That?" Inventory Effect
- **Novelty Purchases**: Occupy inventory space
- **Visibility**: Other NPCs may notice shopping bag
- **Hostel Return**: Kitchen NPCs may comment on visible bags
- **The Question That Follows**: "What's in the bag?" (from other party members)
