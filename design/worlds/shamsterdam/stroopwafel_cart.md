# De Stroopwafel Kar (The Stroopwafel Cart)

*Where fresh-pressed caramel meets tourist markup — 8 Sovs for what locals pay 2 Sovs, but the warmth is real*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: shamsterdam_market_stroopwafel_cart_01
- **Display Name**: De Stroopwafel Kar
- **English Translation**: "The Stroopwafel Cart"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Fresh, Hot, and Priced For Your Wallet's Destruction"
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: De Kuipmarkt / Various tourist hotspots (cart is mobile)
- **Location Type**: Street Food Vendor / Comfort Item Source / Tourist Pricing Education

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 9 AM - 6 PM (market hours), cart may relocate during tourist peaks
- **Stat Requirements**: None (sugar is universal)
- **Item Requirements**: Gold for purchases (cash preferred, card with sigh)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (cart finds the tourists)
- **Special Conditions**: Cart moves between preset locations based on time of day

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Walk away with your stroopwafel, immediately
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (vendor interaction is brief and efficient)
- **Failure States**: Cannot fail at buying a stroopwafel (this is the one thing that goes right)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: None (but the taste memory is permanent)

### The Cart Layout
- **Front Counter**: Display of finished stroopwafels, size comparison visible
- **The Irons**: Two large circular waffle irons, synchronized operation
- **Caramel Station**: Pot of warm syrup (stroop), spreading implements
- **Batter Area**: Fresh dough balls ready for pressing
- **Warming Rack**: Fresh-made stroopwafels staying warm for immediate service
- **Cash Box**: Weathered, well-used, efficient

### The Stroopwafel Creation Process (Visible Theater)
1. **Batter Ball**: Dough portioned from container
2. **Press**: Placed in iron, pressed flat
3. **Cook**: Both sides golden (timing is crucial)
4. **Split**: Hot waffle split horizontally with wire
5. **Fill**: Stroop (caramel syrup) spread on bottom half
6. **Close**: Top half placed, gentle press
7. **Serve**: Handed over while still warm
8. **Timing**: 90 seconds per stroopwafel, worth the wait

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The stroopwafel cart is simultaneously a tourist trap (8 Sovs for what costs 2 Sovs at a supermarket) and a genuinely excellent experience (fresh stroopwafels are legitimately life-changing). The satire isn't that the cart is bad — it's that tourists happily pay 5x the local price for something they could get cheaper, and THEY'RE STILL MAKING THE RIGHT CHOICE because a fresh, warm stroopwafel from a cart is genuinely incomparable to a packaged one.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Tourist pricing acceptance, the value of "authentic experience" vs actual value
- **Secondary Targets**: Dutch stereotype merchandising, "traditional" performance for tourists
- **Cultural Specificity**: Stroopwafels are genuinely beloved Dutch tradition; the markup is the target, not the food
- **Humor Approach**: The bros overpaying and STILL being correct that it was worth it
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Stroopwafels are cultural treasure; satire targets pricing, not the product or vendors

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Charging tourist prices with a smile
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Cart strategically positions at tourist peaks
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros pay 8 Sovs for what locals get for 2 Sovs
- **Comedy of Errors**: No errors — this is the one thing that goes completely right
- **Consequence System**: None negative — stroopwafels are pure good in this world

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Authentic Dutch culinary experience, fair market price

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Paying 5x markup for something available cheaper everywhere, BUT getting fresh instead of packaged

**How impaired are they**: Usually sober (this is daytime, market hours)

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- "Local price: 2 Sovs" crossed out on the sign
- Locals walking past to the market interior
- Supermarket visible across the street
- Same product in grocery for 2 Sovs/pack of 8

**The twist**: Bros are STILL right to buy it because fresh stroopwafels are THAT much better

**How locals react**: Understanding nod — tourists overpay, but fresh IS better, judgment is suspended

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Cart Interaction System
- **Approach**: Walk to cart, join line if present
- **Queue System**: 1-3 customers ahead at tourist hours, 0 off-peak
- **Ordering**: Simple choice — small (4 Sovs) or large (8 Sovs)
- **Wait**: Watch the making process (90 seconds)
- **Receive**: Stroopwafel handed over on wax paper
- **Consume**: Immediate gratification

### Menu & Consumables
| Item | Price | HP Effect | SP Effect | Status Effect | Notes |
|------|-------|-----------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Stroopwafel (Small) | 4 Sovs | +25 HP | +15 SP | Comfort (3 turns) | Still delicious |
| Stroopwafel (Large) | 8 Sovs | +50 HP | +30 SP | Comfort (5 turns), Sugar Rush | The correct choice |
| Stroopwafel (Supermarket) | 2 Sovs/8 | +5 HP | +5 SP | None | Available elsewhere, not here |

### The "Fresh vs Packaged" Revelation
- **Trigger**: First purchase from cart after having supermarket stroopwafel
- **Dialogue**: 
  - Lord Pilsner: "Wait. WAIT. This is what they were SUPPOSED to taste like?"
  - Chadwick: "All those packaged ones were LIES. BEAUTIFUL LIES."
  - Bradley: "I'm experiencing new emotions. I didn't know I could feel this way."
- **Effect**: "Enlightened" status — future packaged stroopwafels provide half comfort

### The Wait Mini-Experience
During the 90-second wait, random observations:
- Watch the iron press (satisfying)
- Smell the caramel (compelling)
- Notice the price disparity (educational)
- See locals walking past (informational)
- Anticipation builds (valuable)

### Comfort Status Effect
- **Source**: Fresh stroopwafel consumption
- **Duration**: 3 turns (small) / 5 turns (large)
- **Effect**: +10% resistance to negative status effects
- **Visual**: Slight warm glow around character
- **Narrative**: "The warmth stays with you, inside and out"

### Sugar Rush Status Effect (Large Only)
- **Source**: Large stroopwafel consumption
- **Duration**: 2 turns
- **Effect**: +15% Speed, -5% decision-making (optional purchases more likely)
- **Stacks With**: Comfort status
- **Warning**: May lead to additional market purchases

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Order "groot" (large) with correct pronunciation, receive stroopwafel without visible price shock, share piece with stranger (optional generous action)
- **Lose Bravado**: Ask "how much is that in dollars," ask for "a cheaper one," visibly wince at price

### Cart Finding System
- **Morning**: Guaranteed at De Kuipmarkt entrance
- **Midday**: Guaranteed at Verdoemplein adjacent
- **Afternoon**: Guaranteed at Wandelpark entrance
- **Random**: Small chance of cart appearing at other tourist locations
- **Quest**: "Track the Cart" — visit all three locations, receive discount token (one-time 2 Sovs off)

## NPCs & Vendors
### Cart Staff

**Vendor Tante Bep**
- **Role**: Cart operator, stroopwafel master, tourist price enforcer
- **Appearance**: Older Dutch woman, apron, practical clothing, efficient hands
- **Title**: "Tante" (Aunt) — affectionate term, she's been doing this for 30 years
- **Background**: Third-generation stroopwafel maker, inherited the cart, knows exactly what she's doing
- **Demeanor**: Warm but efficient, no time for haggling, pride in her product
- **Dialogue**:
  - "Klein of groot? Small or large?"
  - "Groot is better. Fresh, more caramel. Same love, bigger joy."
  - "Yes, it's eight Sovs. Yes, I know the supermarket. No, it's not the same."
  - "Watch. See how I split it? That's sixty years of family practice."
  - "The stroop is my grandmother's recipe. The price is my landlord's recipe."
  - *on pronunciation attempt* "Close enough. Here's your stroopwafel."
  - *to regular* "The usual? Groot? Of course groot. You've learned."

**Assistant Mila**
- **Role**: Batter prep, line management, card payments (with sigh)
- **Appearance**: Young Dutch woman, family resemblance, learning the trade
- **Background**: Tante Bep's niece, summer job that's becoming lifetime career
- **Demeanor**: Friendly, slightly apologetic about prices, genuinely loves stroopwafels
- **Dialogue**:
  - "It'll be about ninety seconds. Worth the wait, promise."
  - "Card? I can do card. There's a... small fee. Cash is easier."
  - "My aunt's been making these since before I was born. The recipe never changes."
  - "Local price? That's... you saw that sign, huh? It's a joke. Sort of."
  - "First time? You're about to understand why people line up."
