# FEBO Automat

*Where 3 AM decisions meet wall-mounted mystery meat — pull the handle, accept the consequences*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: shamsterdam_central_febo_automat_01
- **Display Name**: FEBO
- **Full Name**: FEBO Automatiek
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "The Wall Provides. The Wall Judges Not."
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: Central Shamsterdam, multiple locations (this is the flagship 24-hour)
- **Location Type**: Automat Fast Food / 3 AM Salvation / Regret Dispensary

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 24 hours (the wall never sleeps)
- **Stat Requirements**: None (desperation is the only requirement)
- **Item Requirements**: Coins (2 Sovs-4 per item) — the machines don't take card, the counter does
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (FEBO finds you when you need it)
- **Special Conditions**: Peak hours 2-4 AM, line forms, judgment suspended

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through front door, into the night, with your choices
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (FEBO is a state of being, not a destination)
- **Failure States**: Cannot fail at FEBO (the wall accepts all)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "FEBO Regular" status after 3+ visits, staff nod of recognition

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: A Dutch institution where hot fried snacks live in a wall of tiny compartments, available 24/7 by coin insertion. No judgment, no conversation, no pretense — just you, your coins, and your 3 AM decisions. FEBO is the great equalizer: club kids, shift workers, tourists, and locals all standing at the same wall, making the same face at the same kroket. It's been serving the Netherlands since 1941, and it will outlive us all.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Late-night food desperation, the democratization of questionable choices
- **Secondary Targets**: Fast food efficiency taken to logical extreme, the beauty of low expectations
- **Cultural Specificity**: FEBO is REAL and BELOVED — this is an affectionate portrait, not mockery
- **Humor Approach**: The universal experience of 3 AM hunger meeting a wall of mystery meat
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: FEBO is Dutch cultural heritage; satire targets the tourist experience, not the institution

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Enabling poor decisions by making them available 24 hours, coin-operated
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: The munchies pipeline from De Groene Gids to FEBO is REAL
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros discover that 3 Sovs FEBO is better value than 18 Sovs pancakes
- **Comedy of Errors**: First-timer confusion at the wall, wrong item selection, kroket temperature surprise
- **Consequence System**: FEBO doesn't judge, but your stomach might (food effects vary)

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Desperation eating at weird Dutch vending machine thing

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Participating in 80+ year Dutch tradition, joining the community of 3 AM survivors

**How impaired are they**: Usually very (this IS 3 AM Shamsterdam), which is precisely when FEBO shines

**Obvious signs they understand**:
- This is exactly what it looks like
- The food is exactly what it looks like
- The price is exactly what it looks like
- FEBO makes no promises it can't keep
- Everyone at the wall is in the same situation

**How locals interact**: No "playing along" needed — locals love FEBO unironically, tourists are welcome

## Gameplay Mechanics
### The Wall Interaction System
- **Approach**: Walk up to the wall, examine options
- **Selection**: Choose compartment (each shows contents through glass)
- **Payment**: Insert coins (2 Sovs-4 depending on item)
- **Acquisition**: Turn handle, door opens, item is yours
- **No Returns**: What you take is what you get, the wall has spoken

- **Trigger**: Visiting FEBO after De Groene Gids within 2 hours
- **Effect**: All food HP restoration +50%
- **Dialogue**: "This is the best thing I've ever eaten" (it's a 2 Sovs kroket)
- **Explanation**: The Space Cake Effect makes everything taste better
- **Mechanical Reality**: Bros gain more HP from FEBO when properly... prepared

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Order confidently, finish your kroket without flinching at temperature, successfully navigate the coin machine on first try
- **Lose Bravado**: Ask "what's in it" (rookie move), burn mouth on first bite (everyone's first time), struggle with coin mechanism while line forms

### FEBO Regular Status
- **Requirement**: Visit 3+ times
- **Effect**: Counter staff nod of recognition
- **Benefit**: Occasional extra napkin, the rarest of gifts
