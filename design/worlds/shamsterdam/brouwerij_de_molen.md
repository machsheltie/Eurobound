# Brouwerij de Molen (The Windmill Brewery)

*Where Dutch tradition meets tourist disorientation — craft beer in a spinning building that may or may not be causing your dizziness*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: shamsterdam_countryside_brouwerij_molen_01
- **Display Name**: Brouwerij de Molen
- **English Translation**: "The Windmill Brewery"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Respect The Grain. Fear The Spin."
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: Shamsterdam Countryside (short train ride from central)
- **Location Type**: Brewery Tour Location #1 / Working Windmill / Mini-Dungeon

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 11 AM - 8 PM (brewery tour hours, last tour at 6 PM)
- **Stat Requirements**: Mild Constitution recommended (spinning room + alcohol)
- **Prerequisite**: The Blarney Trap Bottle Opener (Publandia) — required to access ANY brewery tour location
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (accessible once expansion installed)
- **Special Conditions**: Must take short "scenic" train ride from central Shamsterdam (8 Sovs round trip, views are... fields)

### Bottle Opener Requirement
- **Prerequisite**: The Blarney Trap Bottle Opener (Publandia) — required for brewery tour access
- **Location-Specific Opener**: Brouwerij de Molen Bottle Opener
- **Design**: Miniature windmill with rotating blade arms, bottle opener integrated into the windmill base
- **Material**: Brushed copper with grain-gold accents
- **Inscription**: "Respect The Grain"
- **Acquired**: Defeat the Yeast Golem OR purchase from Hendrik for 22 Sovs
- **Special Feature**: Blade arms actually rotate when you spin the opener
- **Check Point**: Hendrik checks for Blarney Trap opener (quest access) AND this opener (stamp/beer access)
- **Without Blarney Trap Opener**: Cannot participate in brewery tour at all
- **Without Windmill Opener**: Can explore and tour, but cannot receive stamp or purchase Windmill Wit

**Hendrik's Opener Check**:
- *With Blarney Trap Opener, No Windmill Opener*: "The shamrock opener. Seamus's network. You have access to the tour. But the windmill opener — you must EARN it. Defeat my golem. Or 22 Sovs. The grain demands respect."
- *With Both Openers*: "You carry the windmill. This means you understand — or you paid. Either way, you've committed to the spin. The Windmill Wit awaits. May the grain guide your steps."
- *Without Blarney Trap Opener*: "The Continental Trail has rules. Even windmills bow to tradition. No Publandia opener, no brewery tour access. Return when properly credentialed."

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Back through gift shop, return train to Shamsterdam central
- **Completion Requirements**: Complete tour, defeat Yeast Golem, acquire Windmill Wit
- **Failure States**: Yeast Golem defeat = 30-minute brewery lockout, "fermentation recovery"
- **Progressive Unlocks**: Boss defeat unlocks premium tasting room access, brewmaster selfie opportunity

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: A 350-year-old windmill converted into a craft brewery, trading on authenticity while being profoundly disorienting. The brewmaster lectures about "respecting the grain" while tourists try not to fall over from the combination of spinning building and strong Belgian-style beers. It's genuine Dutch heritage weaponized for tourism, and the line between "traditional craft" and "calculated Instagram bait" has been ground finer than the grain.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: "Authentic" experience tourism, paying premium for discomfort disguised as tradition
- **Secondary Targets**: Craft beer pretension, brewmaster as lifestyle guru, Dutch windmill fetishization
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dutch windmill tourism meets craft beer culture explosion
- **Humor Approach**: The gap between brewmaster's reverence and tourist's survival instinct
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets tourist behavior and brewery pretension, not Dutch heritage or legitimate craft brewing

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Charging 40 Sovs for a tasting flight in a building designed to maximize disorientation
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: The windmill genuinely works, the dizziness genuinely accumulates, the liability waivers are comprehensive
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros think they're having artisanal experience, they're being spun while drinking
- **Comedy of Errors**: Each beer makes the spinning more noticeable, each floor makes it worse
- **Consequence System**: "Windmill Wit" consumable has navigation penalty — the spinning follows you

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Exclusive craft brewery experience in historic Dutch windmill

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Getting dizzy in a spinning building while someone lectures about barley

**How impaired are they**: Increasingly impaired as tasting flight progresses, spinning building doesn't help

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- The building is SPINNING
- Tasting flight goes from 5% to 11% ABV
- Other tourists holding onto tables
- Brewmaster's "Respect The Grain" speech is 40 minutes
- Gift shop prices suggest they need to recoup something
- The Yeast Golem is MADE OF DUST from this very mill

**How locals play along**: Brewmaster treats everything with deadly seriousness, staff have seen a thousand dizzy tourists

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Brewery Tour Flow
1. **Ground Floor**: Purchase tour (25 Sovs), receive tasting token, browse gift shop (trap)
2. **First Floor**: View brewing operations, learn history, floor vibrates
3. **Boss Encounter**: Yeast Golem on grinding floor
4. **Second Floor (post-boss)**: Tasting room, flight consumption, brewmaster lecture
5. **Blade Level (post-boss)**: Premium selfie opportunity
6. **Exit**: Through gift shop (again), return train

### The Yeast Golem Boss Battle
**Boss ID**: shamsterdam_boss_yeast_golem

**Encounter Setup**:
> The grinding mechanism shudders. Grain dust swirls in an unnatural pattern. From the accumulated flour and forgotten yeast cultures, something RISES.

**Boss Stats**:
- **HP**: Medium-high (brewery dungeon mid-boss)
- **Weakness**: Fire attacks, Alcohol-based items (dissolves yeast)
- **Resistance**: Physical attacks (body is semi-solid)
- **Status Immunity**: Confused (it's a golem, already confused)

**Boss Phases**:

**Phase 1 — "Rising Dough"**
- Golem forms from grain dust, tests party with basic attacks
- Attack: "Flour Cloud" — AoE that applies "Bleary" status (-Accuracy)
- Attack: "Yeast Punch" — Single target, moderate damage
- Weakness Window: After Flour Cloud, shaking off excess

**Phase 2 — "Fermentation Frenzy"**
- Golem begins expanding (yeast rising), attacks become stronger
- Attack: "Over-Proofed Slam" — Heavy single target
- Attack: "Wild Fermentation" — Summons 2 "Sourdough Sprites" (weak adds)
- Special: Every 3 turns, golem EXPANDS, increasing damage but lowering speed
- Weakness Window: During expansion, before attack lands

**Phase 3 — "The Collapse"**
- Golem at maximum size, unstable, desperate attacks
- Attack: "Dough Hook" — Pulls party member into melee range
- Attack: "Final Rise" — Charges for 2 turns, massive AoE
- Vulnerability: If hit with fire OR alcohol item during charge, instant vulnerability phase
- Defeat: Collapses into harmless bread (drops Windmill Wit recipe)

**Victory Rewards**:
- Windmill Wit x3 (consumable)
- "Yeast Slayer" title
- Access to Tasting Room (second floor)
- Brewmaster Selfie Spot unlocked (blade level)

**Bros' Combat Dialogue**:
- **Lord Pilsner**: "It's made of BREAD. How is BREAD this AGGRESSIVE?"
- **Chadwick**: "I'm getting this. This is CONTENT. The golem is CONTENT."
- **Bradley**: "We should use fire."
