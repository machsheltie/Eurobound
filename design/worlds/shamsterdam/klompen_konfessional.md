*Where tourists confess with their wallets that they're buying wall decorations, not footwear*

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Klompen (wooden clogs) are genuinely part of Dutch heritage — farmers wore them, craftspeople made them, they're a real tradition. The satire is the modern tourist clog economy, where millions of wooden shoes are sold annually to people who will NEVER wear them. The shop is a "Konfessional" because every purchase is an implicit admission: "I am buying a wooden shoe I will put on a shelf and never think about again."

The staff know. The tourists know. The transaction happens anyway. This is the confession.

### The Mathematics of Clog Commerce
- **Annual tourist clog purchases in Amsterdam**: ~2 million pairs
- **Clogs actually worn after purchase**: ~0.5%
- **Clogs that become planters**: ~15%
- **Clogs that become doorstops**: ~10%
- **Clogs that sit in closets until house move**: ~70%
- **Clogs thrown away during house move**: ~65% of those
- **The shop's awareness of this**: Complete

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Souvenir purchasing as ritual, buying symbols not objects
- **Secondary Targets**: "Authentic" manufacturing claims, try-before-you-buy theater
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dutch clog tradition vs. tourist clog economy
- **Humor Approach**: The elaborate pretense that these are "footwear"
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Respects genuine clog craftsmanship, satirizes tourist purchasing logic

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Selling 40 Sovs of wood as "wearable footwear" when everyone knows the truth
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: The try-on station exists to create the illusion of function
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros convinced they'll "definitely wear these at home"
- **Comedy of Errors**: The 10-second walk attempt, the immediate blister formation, the quiet shelf placement
- **Consequence System**: "Clog Burden" inventory item, never equipped, silently judging

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Purchasing authentic Dutch footwear for actual wearing

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Buying wooden decorations shaped like shoes

**How impaired are they**: Usually sober (this is daytime shopping), which makes the denial purer

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- Wood is not a comfortable footwear material
- Their regular shoes exist for reasons
- The "wearable" section is suspiciously small
- Nobody in Shamsterdam is actually wearing clogs
- The shop sells more miniatures than full-size (telling)

**The test**: Try walking 10 steps. Just 10 steps.
- Step 1-3: "This is different but fine"
- Step 4-6: "Okay there's some... adjustment needed"
- Step 7-9: "I think I'm bleeding"
- Step 10: "I'll take two. For the WALL."

**How locals interact**: Staff maintain the footwear illusion with complete professionalism

### The Try-On Experience
**The Station**:
- Bench, sizing chart, selection of try-on clogs
- Mirror for full effect viewing
- Subtle first aid supplies nearby

**The Process**:
1. **Size Selection**: Staff ask shoe size, provide "equivalent" clog
2. **The Insertion**: Foot enters clog, immediate wooden reality
3. **The Standing**: Upright in clogs, wobble begins
4. **The Walk Attempt**: 3-10 steps recommended maximum
5. **The Decision**: Continue delusion or embrace decoration truth

**Possible Outcomes**:
- **"They're great!"** (lying, purchases anyway)
- **"Maybe I need a different size"** (denial, the size isn't the problem)
- **"I'll take them for decoration"** (confession achieved)
- **"Do you have the miniatures?"** (wisdom gained)

### The Confession Mechanic
At checkout, staff ask: "Will you be wearing these, or are they for display?"

**Response Options**:
1. **"Wearing, definitely"** — Staff nod, internal tracking notes delusion
2. **"Display, probably"** — Staff nod warmly, confession acknowledged
3. **"Both!"** — Staff's poker face intensifies
4. **"...decoration"** — "Honest Customer" status unlocked, staff respect +10

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Walk 10 steps without visible pain, purchase "wearable" pair with confidence, claim Dutch heritage
- **Lose Bravado**: Remove clogs within 5 steps, ask "do these come in comfortable," purchase only miniatures
- **Neutral**: Standard tourist transaction, standard delusion maintenance

### The "Clog Burden" Inventory Item
- **Type**: Equipment (technically) / Decoration (actually)
- **Weight**: Heavy (wooden shoes are DENSE)
- **Equip Option**: Available, provides -50% movement speed, +Blister status
- **Actual Use**: None during gameplay, occupies inventory
- **Epilogue Reference**: "The clogs are on a shelf. They've always been on a shelf."

### The Craftsman Demonstration
- **Trigger**: Approach demonstration area during active carving
- **Content**: Traditional clog-making techniques, genuinely impressive
- **Duration**: 5-minute cycle, can watch multiple
- **Effect**: +Appreciation for craft, no change in wearability assessment
- **The Question That Emerges**: "This craftsmanship is amazing. I'm still not wearing these."

- **Appearance**: 50s Dutch woman, traditional dress (for tourists), practical shoes (for reality)
- **Background**: Family shop since 1972, seen the full evolution from footwear to souvenir
- **Demeanor**: Warm, professional, diplomatically honest when asked directly
- **The Hendrika Balance**: Sells the tradition, knows the reality, respects both
- **Dialogue**:
  - "Welcome to Klompen Konfessional! Traditional Dutch clogs, made with love and history."
  - "These? Hand-painted, canal-blue pattern. Beautiful on a wall. Also technically wearable."
  - "Try them on? Of course! The station is there. Take your time. Not too many steps."
  - "Comfortable? They're... traditional. Comfort is modern. These are heritage."

  - "The miniatures are our best sellers. People are honest with miniatures."
  - "My grandmother wore clogs daily. In fields. On farms. You probably have different floors."
  - *after confession* "For display? Wonderful. They display beautifully. That's also a tradition now."

**Craftsman Pieter**
- **Role**: Clog carver, demonstration performer, legitimacy provider
- **Appearance**: 60s Dutch man, wood shavings in beard, genuine craftsman energy
- **Background**: Actually trained in traditional clog-making, rare and real
- **Demeanor**: Proud of craft, aware of modern context, philosophical about it
- **Dialogue**:

  - "My father made clogs for farmers. I make clogs for tourists. The craft is the same."
  - "You want to try making one? No. This takes years. But you can watch."
  - "The shape hasn't changed. The purpose has. That's not the clog's fault."
  - *after tourist try-on failure* "The clogs work perfectly. The feet are the problem."

**Associate Jeroen**
- **Role**: Floor sales, try-on assistance, witness to suffering

- **Background**: Summer job that became career, has developed clog philosophy
- **Demeanor**: Helpful, realistic, has seen the 10-step walk many times

  - "The modified ones have padding. It helps. A little. Some."
