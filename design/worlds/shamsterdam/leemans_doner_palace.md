# Leeman's Döner Palace

*Where 32 years of Turkish mastery meets 3 AM tourist desperation — the döner that locals swear by*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: shamsterdam_latenight_leemans_doner_01
- **Display Name**: Leeman's Döner Palace
- **Dutch-Turkish Hybrid**: "Leeman's Döner Paleis"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "You Want Chicken? Chicken Is For Children."
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: Late-night district, near club exits and tram stops
- **Location Type**: Late-Night Food Vendor / Döner Shrine / Standing-Room Warrior Station

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 11 AM - 5 AM (extended hours for the lost and hungry)
- **Peak Hours**: 1 AM - 4 AM (when the real customers arrive)
- **Stat Requirements**: None (hunger is the only requirement)
- **Item Requirements**: Gold for purchases (3 Sovs-8), willingness to be judged
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None (döner finds all who need it)
- **Special Conditions**: Standing counter only — no seats, no weakness

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Into the night, fed, with garlic aura activated
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion (döner is sustenance, not achievement)
- **Failure States**: Cannot fail at eating (though Mehmet may judge your order)
- **Progressive Unlocks**: "Regular" status after 3 visits, Mehmet remembers your order

- **Napkin Station**: Industrial dispenser, you'll need many

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: Leeman's Döner Palace is the REAL döner experience — Turkish immigrant family running a no-frills operation since 1992, serving the exact same quality whether it's noon or 4 AM. The satire isn't the food (the food is genuinely excellent), it's the culture clash between Turkish döner mastery and tourist ordering incompetence. Mehmet has spent 32 years perfecting his craft; tourists spend 32 seconds butchering their pronunciation.

This is döner as it should be: standing up, eaten fast, judged slightly, enjoyed completely.

### The Mehmet Philosophy
- **On Meat**: "Thirty-two years I make döner. The meat knows when you respect it."
- **On Speed**: "Fast food means food that is fast. Not food that is slow. You eat. You go. Life continues."
- **On Tourists**: "They try. Sometimes they succeed. Always they eat. This is enough."
- **On Competition**: "Debaucheryville? Debaucheryville knows NOTHING. The port city knows döner. Shamsterdam knows döner. Debaucheryville knows... beer."

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: The late-night döner ritual, tourist mispronunciation, meat hierarchy gatekeeping
- **Secondary Targets**: Standing-room-only food culture, garlic sauce commitment
- **Cultural Specificity**: Turkish-Dutch immigrant food culture, döner as institution
- **Humor Approach**: Mehmet's loving judgment, the gap between respect and tourist behavior
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Celebrates Turkish immigrant contribution, satirizes tourist incompetence

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Enabling 3 AM food decisions that seem brilliant at the time
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Positioned perfectly to catch post-club traffic
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros ordering "the chicken one" and facing silent judgment
- **Comedy of Errors**: Pronunciation attempts, sauce regret, garlic consequences
- **Consequence System**: "Garlic Aura" status, "Meat Sweats" possibility

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Grabbing late-night food, simple transaction

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Entering a temple of döner culture, being evaluated and found wanting but fed anyway

**How impaired are they**: Usually very (this IS 3 AM)

**Obvious signs they notice**:
- The meat towers are HYPNOTIC
- Mehmet is watching their order decision
- Other customers seem to know exactly what to say
- The bread smell is overwhelming rational thought
- They're about to make garlic choices they'll smell for days

**The revelation**: This is genuinely the best döner they've ever had
**The consequence**: They will compare all future döner to this and be disappointed

**How locals interact**: Order efficiently, eat efficiently, nod to Mehmet, leave

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Ordering System
- **Approach**: Join queue (or step up if empty)
- **The Evaluation**: Mehmet assesses you before you speak
- **Order**: State meat, bread type, sauce preferences
- **The Judgment**: Mehmet's reaction to your choices
- **Preparation**: Watch the master work
- **Payment**: At the end, as is tradition
- **Eating**: At the standing counter, like a warrior

### The "Triple Tower" Experience
**Leeman's Signature Item**
- **Content**: All three meats, extra everything, fresh bread
- **Size**: Substantial (warrior portion)
- **Effects**: +40 HP, +15 SP, Garlic Aura (1 hour), possible Meat Sweats
- **Mehmet's Commentary**: "You want The Triple Tower? You have big appetite. Or big problems. Either way, I help."

### The Garlic Aura Status
- **Source**: Döner consumption (standard), Enhanced by extra garlic
- **Duration**: 1-3 hours depending on garlic level
- **Effect**: NPCs maintain 20% larger conversation distance
- **Cure**: Time, strong mints, or embracing it
- **Stacking**: Multiple döners = extended duration
- **Bros' Reaction**: "We ALL have it so it doesn't count."

### The Meat Sweats Status
- **Source**: Triple Tower or excessive consumption
- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Effect**: +10% HP regen, -10% Speed
- **Visualization**: Slight shimmer on character sprite
- **Cure**: Time, water, or more food (doesn't help, feels right)

### Pronunciation Challenge (Mini-Game)
- **Trigger**: Ordering for the first time
- **Challenge**: Pronounce "döner" correctly (or acceptably)
- **Outcomes**:
  - Perfect: Mehmet nod, slight respect
  - Acceptable: Standard service, no comment
  - Tourist: "...doner. Okay. I understand."
  - Catastrophic: "You want... what? Show me on menu."

### The Great Döner Debate Integration
- **Quest**: "The Great Döner Debate: International Expansion"
- **Progress**: Eating here counts as Shamsterdam sample
- **Requirement**: Try specific item, remember opinion
- **Mehmet's Stake**: "You tell them Debaucheryville is better? You don't come back. I remember faces."

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Order veal confidently, get extra hot sauce and survive, pronounce "döner" correctly
- **Lose Bravado**: Order chicken with visible hesitation, ask "what's in it," request no garlic
- **Neutral**: Standard order, standard consumption, standard garlic aura
