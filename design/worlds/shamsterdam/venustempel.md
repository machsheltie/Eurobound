# Venustempel (Venus Temple)

*Where Victorian curiosity meets artisan chocolate genitalia — the world's first sex museum, relocated to Museum Mile, now with a working fountain out front that everybody shares*

## Location Identity
- **Location ID**: shamsterdam_museumrow_venustempel_01
- **Display Name**: Venustempel
- **English Translation**: "Venus Temple"
- **Satirical Subtitle**: "Giggling Since 1985. Dipping Since 2011."
- **World Location**: Shamsterdam
- **Area Zone**: Museum Mile (the Cultural Alibi District), plaza frontage, opposite the empty "I SHAMSTERDAM" plinth
- **Location Type**: Chocolate Phallus Museum / Historical Sex Museum / Public Fountain Landmark / Group Photo Disaster Zone / Cultural Alibi
- **Relocated From**: `shamsterdam_roodelicht_venustempel_01` (Roodelicht Row, 1985–2011). The old ID is retired. All references route to the Museum Mile ID.

### Why It Moved
In 2011 the museum bought the chocolate collection off a retiring confectioner, discovered it needed four times the floor space, and found four times the floor space on Museum Mile at a rent it could afford because Museum Mile at the time was two museums and a plinth. The Roodelicht building was sold to a waffle franchise. The Giggling Figure was unbolted, driven six hundred metres in a van, and re-bolted inside the new lobby within the week. It has not been switched off since.

The fountain was installed the same year, outside, as a "temporary promotional feature."

## Access & Requirements
### Entry Conditions
- **Time Restrictions**: 10 AM – 11 PM. The fountain plaza is public space and never closes.
- **Stat Requirements**: None
- **Item Requirements**: 9 Sovs entry (raised from 5 Sovs when the chocolate wing opened; the sign still says 5 Sovs under a sticker that says 9 Sovs)
- **Previous Location Requirements**: None. Most players arrive at the fountain first and the museum second, which is exactly how the business is designed.
- **Special Conditions**: "18+" sign at door remains more suggestion than enforcement. The fountain has no age restriction at all, because it is a fountain.

### Exit Conditions
- **Normal Exit**: Through the gift shop (unavoidable, naturally), then out onto the plaza, past the fountain, one more time
- **Completion Requirements**: No completion. Surviving the Giggling Figure counts as "doing the museum." Dipping once counts as "doing the fountain."
- **Failure States**: Cannot be banned. Can be asked to stop leaning into the cascade. Will be asked to stop leaning into the cascade.
- **Progressive Unlocks**: **Cultural Alibi** flag (see System Integration) — permanent, city-wide, and the entire reason the bros come here

## Visual Design

### Exterior — The Plaza
- **Approach**: A wide paved forecourt on Museum Mile, the only museum on the strip with a queue outside it, and the queue is not for the museum
- **The Building**: Nineteenth-century civic frontage, four storeys, considerably grander than the Roodelicht canal house and slightly too grand for what is inside it. Art deco "VENUSTEMPEL" lettering salvaged from the old site and mounted on a building that predates art deco by sixty years. Nobody has commented on this.
- **Signage**:
 - Main: "VENUSTEMPEL" (salvaged, art deco, mounted crooked by two degrees, never corrected)
 - Sub-sign: "SEXMUSEUM — World's First Since 1985"
 - Banner: "DE CACAOGALERIJ — Nu Geopend" ("The Cocoa Gallery — Now Open"). It has said "Now Open" since 2011.
 - Window: "ADULTS ONLY" (font suggests this was added later)
 - Door plaque: "Educational Exhibits" (sure)
 - Neon: Pink outline of Venus figure, salvaged, still flickering, still not fixed
 - Plinth plaque: "FONTEIN VAN DE GULLE GEVER — 2011" ("Fountain of the Generous Giver"). Nobody uses this name.
 - Laminated municipal card, bolted to the plinth: **HYGIËNESCORE** with a letter in a window that changes over the course of the game
- **Atmosphere**: Warm chocolate smell reaching two hundred metres down the Mile. Constant crowd noise. Camera shutters. A working fountain making the specific wet slapping sound of moving couverture.
- **Environmental Context**: Between Het Rijke Museum and a bureau de change. Directly across from the empty "I SHAMSTERDAM" plinth, so the two crowds interleave and photograph past each other.

### De Grote Fontein (The Great Fountain) — Signature Landmark
- **What It Is**: A four-metre working chocolate fountain, sculpted as a giant phallus, cast in food-grade stainless with a bronze-effect finish, pumping 340 litres of tempered dark couverture in a continuous cascade from the tip down over four tiers of collection bowls.
- **It Is Real**: This is not a decorative sculpture. It is a functioning commercial chocolate fountain of a type you would rent for a wedding, scaled up by a Rotterdam engineering firm that had never been asked to do this before and has not been asked since. The chocolate is hot. The chocolate is edible. People eat it. All day.
- **It Is Free**: Dipping costs nothing. This is the whole business model. The fountain is free, the sticks cost money, the museum sells neither the sticks nor the fountain — it sells the tickets that the queue generates. Free chocolate makes a crowd; a crowd makes a landmark; a landmark makes ticket sales. The museum understands this completely and says so in its own annual report.
- **Continuous Public Use**: Between forty and four hundred people are dipping at any given hour. It is a shared communal food surface in a public square with no supervision, no queue barrier, and no rule beyond a sign.
- **The Sign**: A stainless plate at chest height reading **"EEN KEER DOPEN A.U.B. / ONE DIP PLEASE."** It has been replaced four times, each time larger. It is currently the size of a road sign. It has never worked once.
- **Servicing**: Filtered continuously. Topped up hourly from a service hatch in the plinth. "Fully replaced weekly," per the museum. Drained, stripped and hosed 11 PM – 6 AM by a two-person night crew who wear full PPE and do not make eye contact with each other.
- **What Goes In It**: Strawberries. Marshmallows. Bananas. Pineapple. Rice krispy treats. Bare hands. Sleeves. A phone case. Somebody's hat, once, on a dare, and the museum did not stop the fountain.

### Gunter's Deep Dips — The Cart
- **Location**: Four metres from the fountain, angled so the queue for the cart feeds directly into the queue for the fountain
- **Operator**: **Gunter** (see NPCs), twelve years on this pitch — he followed the fountain when the museum moved
- **Appearance**: Green-and-white cart, awning, spotless, a small radio, a card reader on a cord, a laminated hygiene card taped to the side showing a straight A
- **Function**: Sells the dipping items at 5 SOVS. Sells a bare skewer with nothing on it for 1 SOV to people who brought their own food or brought nothing at all. Sells a hand wipe for 1 SOV that almost nobody buys.
- **The Contrast**: His cart is immaculate. The fountain four metres away is a shared public trough. He knows. He has no stake in it. His hygiene card is an A and he will point at it.

Hand-lettered, laminated, mounted at customer eye height. It is the filthiest text in Shamsterdam and Gunter has never once acknowledged it. He wrote it because it sells. If a customer reads it aloud and laughs, he says the price.

>
> **Organic Strawberries**
> *(Plump, firm, and begging to be absolutely smothered.)*
>
> **Pineapple Chunks**
> *(For a sweeter finish. You know why. We don't need to explain it to you.)*
>

> *(Just the tips. Yes, it's redundant. No, we don't judge.)*
>
> **Jumbo Marshmallows**
> *(Soft, sticky, and expands nicely in the mouth.)*
>
> **Rice Krispie Squares**

#### The Warning Sign to Patrons
Bolted beside the board. Municipal in tone, entirely sincere, and universally ignored.

> **"Please maintain a firm, two-handed grip on your skewer at all times. Do NOT double-dip, do NOT stroke the fountain base, and absolutely NO open-mouth catching directly from the tip of the statue. We've had incidents."**

**"We've had incidents" is never explained.** No NPC in the game elaborates. Gunter has two deflections and neither is an answer. Ruud does not know. Anke will not say. Gerda's written findings reference an incident log by number and never by content. The player will never find out and must never be able to.

**The Contradiction That Is The Whole Mechanic**: The sign forbids double-dipping. Everybody double-dips. Every single person who reads that sign then does the thing the sign forbids, within thirty seconds, and nobody — including them — notices the sequence. Gunter never comments on it.

### Interior — Entrance Hall
- **Theme**: Grand civic lobby, marble floor, ticket desk, and a Victorian mannequin bolted to the ground four steps inside the door
- **The Giggling Figure**: Relocated, re-bolted, unchanged. Still the first thing you meet. Still on a motion sensor. Still lifts its skirts every thirty seconds. Still makes an entire lobby of strangers jump in unison. The move gave it a bigger audience and better acoustics.
- **The Bench**: Came from the old site. Same bench. Visibly well-used. Positioned in direct sight of the Giggling Figure, because Anke moved it there herself on day one.
- **The Framed Fine**: On the lobby wall, next to the 1985 opening photograph, hangs a framed municipal hygiene violation notice. The museum is not hiding it. The museum thinks it is funny. The museum is correct that nobody reads it.
- **Ticket Desk**: **Anke**, fifteen years, cross-stitch between customers, 9 Sovs cash or card, coins for the machines upstairs.

### Interior — Ground Floor: De Cacaogalerij (The Cocoa Gallery)
- **Theme**: A serious museum of chocolate phalluses, curated with total sincerity, placarded in three languages
- **Layout**: Broad gallery, climate-controlled to 17°C, vitrines on plinths, spot-lit, hushed. It looks like a jewellery exhibition. It is not.
- **Exhibits**:
 - **De Oervorm** ("The Original Form"): the oldest surviving piece in the collection, 1974, hand-moulded, cracked down one side, in a nitrogen-flushed case with its own humidity readout. Genuinely fragile. Genuinely irreplaceable. Insured for more than the van that moved it.
 - **The National Styles Wall**: Belgian, Swiss, French, Dutch and Italian traditions side by side, with a placard explaining tempering differences at a level of detail that assumes you care
 - **The Scale Progression**: Eleven pieces ascending in size along a twelve-metre plinth, ending in one the museum has never given a name and refers to internally as "the big one"
 - **The Tempering Room**: A glass-walled working kitchen where two chocolatiers produce the gift shop stock in front of you. Marble slabs, seeding, a thermometer, absolute concentration. It is the most technically impressive thing on Museum Mile and it draws a smaller crowd than the mannequin downstairs.
 - **Hollow vs. Solid**: A cutaway display explaining structural failure modes. In summer this display is dated and updated weekly with actual casualties.
 - **The Failures Case**: Bloomed, seized, split and collapsed pieces, labelled with what went wrong. The curator's favourite case. Nobody stops at it.
 - **De Fontein: Een Technisch Verhaal**: A small display about building the fountain, including the original Rotterdam engineering drawings and a laminated letter from the firm politely declining to build a second one.
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Cocoa smell (pumped, deliberately, from the tempering room extraction), 17°C chill, low murmur, the specific quiet of people reading placards about something they cannot photograph without consequences

### Interior — First Floor: De Mechanische Vleugel (The Mechanical Wing)
- **Theme**: "Historical Erotica Through The Ages" — the entire original Venustempel collection, intact, relocated wholesale
- **Exhibits**:
 - **Peep Show Devices**: Coin-operated Victorian mutoscopes showing "scandalous" flip-card animations. 1 Sov, thirty seconds, original machinery.
 - **The Marilyn Monroe Statue**: Standing over its subway grate, dress on a thirty-second timer, everyone takes the same photograph
 - **John Lennon Erotic Sketches**: Yes, really. He drew these. Under-lit, off the main sightline, still the most genuinely interesting object in the building.
 - **Historical "Art"**: Framed prints that would have been contraband 150 years ago
 - **Medical Diagrams**: "Educational" anatomy charts from the 1800s, more terrifying than titillating
 - **Chastity Belt Display**: Real artefacts, questionable provenance, universally photographed
 - **Vintage Pin-Up Collection**: From tasteful to "what decade was this acceptable"
 - **Early Film Room**: Looping clips from the earliest adult cinema. Surprisingly boring.
 - **1970s Section**: An aesthetic that has not aged well and is fascinating for exactly that reason
- **The Adjacency Nobody Questions**: You walk out of a nitrogen-flushed vitrine of 1974 chocolate and directly into a room of Victorian mutoscopes, and the transition is marked by a single placard reading "VERVOLG →" ("continued"). Not one visitor in ten thousand has asked why these two collections are in the same building.
- **Atmosphere**: Warmer than the gallery below, red and pink wash, mechanical clicking, that specific museum hum

### Interior — Second Floor: Ervaar de Tempel (Photo Ops)
- **Theme**: "Experience The Temple" (liability waivers implied)
- **Exhibits**:
 - **The Throne**: Oversized chair shaped like... something... mandatory group photo spot
 - **Mirror Maze**: Short but disorienting, reflects things you would rather not see reflected
 - **Costume Corner**: Rental accessories for "artistic" photos (5 Sovs)
 - **The Swing**: Exactly what it sounds like, "for display only" (sign ignored)
 - **Wax Figures**: Celebrities in "compromising" poses (legally distinct, allegedly)
 - **Green Screen Station**: Composite yourself into the fountain cascade (10 Sovs printed). The single best-selling photo product in the building.
- **Atmosphere**: Flash photography constant, groups rotating through, gift shop visible and pulling you forward

### Interior — Exit: The Gift Shop
- **Attendant**: **Lotte Beekman**, pink apron, ribbon in her hair, and twice the floor space she had on Roodelicht Row, about which she is genuinely delighted
- **Stock**: The legacy Venustempel merch (Giggling Figure postcards, "I JUMPED" shirts, the licensed John Lennon print), the full Cacaogalerij chocolate line produced in the tempering room downstairs, fountain keyrings, fountain magnets, and a 4 Sovs hand sanitiser in a bottle moulded in the shape of the fountain
- **Deliberately Restrained**: The shop is spacious, sparsely faced, and expensive. Pieces sit on lit shelves with their cocoa percentage printed on the label. There are no sprinkles. There are no fondant bow ties. There is no pick-and-mix bin. A boxed set is 45 Sovs and the boxes are good. This is a policy, it comes from the curator, and it is the single clearest way a player who has already visited the Museum of Questionable Art can tell the two places apart.
- **The Toilet**: Lotte lets anyone use it, ticket or not. It is the most-used facility on Museum Mile. She has never analysed why.

### The Other Chocolate Museum
The Shamsterdam Museum of Questionable Art, across town on Roodelicht Row, also sells chocolate anatomy: white, milk and dark, sprinkles, fondant bow ties, 3.50 Sovs "Fabergé-style collector's eggs," and a teenager on the till who cannot be bothered. Venustempel is aware of this.

Venustempel's position, delivered unprompted, once, in one dry institutional sentence and never repeated:

- **Anke**, if asked whether the two museums are related: "No." *beat, without heat* "They sell eggs."
- **The wall text beside De Oervorm**, in three languages, final line: *"The Venustempel does not lend to, borrow from, or authenticate the collections of other institutions in this city."* It names nobody. Everybody on Museum Mile knows who it means.
- **Lotte**, if a customer compares prices: "They're a different sort of shop. Ours are made downstairs. Would you like the box?"
- **The curator** (never seen, referenced only): reportedly used the phrase "debases the form" in a trade interview in 2016 and has not commented since.

**The distinction must be legible to a player who visits both**, on four axes:
| Axis | Museum of Questionable Art | Venustempel |
|------|---------------------------|-------------|
| Price point | 3.50 Sovs eggs, novelty pricing, pile-it-high | 12 Sovs single pieces, 45 Sovs boxed set, cocoa percentage on the label |
| Signage | Neon "MUSEUM" in letters unsure of themselves, TripAdvisor stickers | Salvaged art deco, three-language gallery placards, municipal hygiene card |
| Staff | One bored teenager | Five trained staff on rotation, one of whom is doing nothing but saying "one dip" |
| Queue | None | Down the Mile, all day, and it is not even for the museum |

The rivalry is entirely one-directional. The Museum of Questionable Art has never mentioned Venustempel and almost certainly does not think about it.

## Satirical Design

> **TONE RAIL — read before writing a single line here.** Per `Design/Character Profiles/bros_core_principle.md` (author ruling, 2026-08-01): **this location is not safe, not boring, and not corporate. It is subversive.** The bros came to Europe for exactly this and they are having the time of their lives. Nothing at Venustempel is an anticlimax, nothing is administrative, and nobody is disappointed. The comedy is **escalation into absurd physical consequence**, not deflation. Paperwork, permits and hygiene cards are *garnish on genuine mayhem* — never the main event. If a beat here lands as "it turned out to be tedious," it is wrong and must be rewritten toward excess.

### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: A four-metre chocolate phallus fountain, free, hot, always on, in a public square, and you may dip whatever you like into it. It is glorious. It is the single best thing that has ever happened to Lord Pilsner. He will talk about it in three cities.

The museum behind it is a genuine institution devoted, with total sincerity and gallery-grade lighting, to chocolate genitalia — and that straight face is exactly why the whole thing is funnier, not tamer. Nobody at Venustempel is embarrassed about anything. Gunter's menu board says *"Firm, rigid, and heavily textured for maximum friction against the flow"* and Gunter says the price. The staff are unshockable, the collection is magnificent, the fountain is enormous, and the museum has been printing money on this since 2011.

**The bros get exactly what they came for.** Immediately. Fully. More than they asked for. Then it compounds. Then the consequences arrive, physically, absurdly, on them, in public. They learn nothing. They post everything. They will describe this fountain to strangers for the rest of the game and each description will be less accurate than the last.

And the payoff underneath all of it: it is a **shared** fountain. Everybody double-dips. **Nothing is better than spreading the love for phalluses through sharing germs**, and not one person in that plaza has had the thought — least of all the three men who dipped forty-one times between them before lunch.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: The bros' delusion **about themselves** — that they are worldly transgressive rebels who discovered this, rather than line item #4,000 this month with a predictable average spend. They are right about the fountain. They are wrong about being interesting.
- **Secondary Targets**: The tourism machine as **hyper-competent at excess** — a business that is gleefully, professionally, absurdly good at delivering more than anyone asked for and charging for all of it. Venustempel is not a dull corporation. It built a four-metre chocolate phallus fountain and then scaled it.
- **Tertiary Target**: The cultural alibi. The bros can now truthfully say they went to a museum. They will say it for the rest of the game. They will say it in Sinfonia. They are not lying and they are not joking.
- **Cultural Specificity**: Shamsterdam's total lack of squeamishness — everything is signposted, nothing is hidden, nobody is coy, and the city simply carries on trading. Museum Mile as a district built around the appearance of culture, hosting the one place on it with an actual curator.
- **Humor Approach**: **Gleeful, unapologetic, MA-rated excess.** Filthy signage played dead straight. A magnificent absurd landmark treated as civic infrastructure. Escalating physical consequences that land on the bros in public. The institution's own straight face is the setup; the fountain and the germs are the punchline.
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Crude, yes — explicit, no. Nothing is depicted. Every asset is confectionery, machinery, mannequins, signage, paperwork, or visitor reactions. No minors, no real people or businesses. The satire punches at the tourists and never at the staff, who are competent, unbothered and entirely unashamed.

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: A free public trough of hot chocolate as a customer-acquisition funnel. 90,000 Sovs a year to run, returned eleven times over. The museum is not embarrassed by this. The museum is *delighted* by this and says so in its annual report.
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: The 1 SOV bare skewer — Gunter's highest-margin product is a piece of wood, sold to men who intend to put it in a public fountain and then in their mouths
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: 10 Sovs to be green-screened into a cascade you can stand in front of for free. It outsells everything. Chadwick buys three.
- **Comedy of Errors**: The bros spend 31 SOVS on skewers and 0 on the wipe, dip forty-one times, and blame the döner
- **Consequence System**: **Sharing the Love** — see Gameplay Mechanics. Escalating, tracked, cumulative, physical, public, and never once attributed correctly.

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: They have discovered the greatest landmark in Europe, they are the first men clever enough to fully exploit it, and their conduct at it is legendary.

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: They are having a genuinely great time at a magnificent public fountain — exactly as designed, exactly as budgeted for, on schedule, at an average spend the museum could have predicted to the SOV. They are not transgressing. They are the core demographic, behaving precisely as the business model assumes, being processed by five professionals who have seen ten thousand of them and are already looking past them at the next group.

**And it is going to catch up with them.** Physically. Publicly. Within about six hours.

**How impaired are they**: Sober. Daytime. It's cultural. This is somehow worse.

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- The HYGIËNESCORE card bolted to the plinth at chest height
- The framed municipal violation notice in the lobby, next to the 1985 photograph
- The metre-wide "EEN KEER DOPEN A.U.B. / ONE DIP PLEASE" plate
- Gunter's cart hygiene card, which is an A, displayed next to a fountain that is not
- Gunter's 1 SOV hand wipes, offered at the point of sale, every time
- Lotte's 4 Sovs Handreiniger, recommended cheerfully to every single departing customer
- Gerda Vonk roping off the fountain for forty minutes with a sample kit while a crowd waits for her to finish
- The fact that the fountain is free and the sticks are not
- The fact that Gunter, who has worked here twelve years, has never eaten from it

**How locals play along**: They don't play along and they don't need to. They built the thing, they disclosed all of it, they are entirely unashamed of any of it, and they sell the skewers. Total transparency changes nothing. The fountain is glorious and it is also a biohazard, and both of those are just true at the same time, and the city has decided it can live with that.

## Gameplay Mechanics

### The Fountain (Primary Interactive)
- **Access**: Free, public, no ticket required, no age gate, available from first arrival in Shamsterdam
- **Interaction Prompt**: `DIP` — available whenever the party holds any dippable item
- **Dippable Items**: Anything on a stick from Gunter's cart; the 1 SOV bare skewer alone; any food item in inventory; a bare hand (always available, always a bad idea)
- **Effect Per Dip**: Restores HP based on the item, grants a small Bravado bump for the first dip of a session, and applies **Sharing the Love** stacks
- **No Cooldown**: The fountain never says no. The fountain has never said no to anything.

### The Dipping Cart — Gunter's Deep Dips
Board items are 5 SOVS each, exactly as the board states. The two off-board items are Gunter's own additions and are the only things on the cart he is quiet about.

|------|-------|-----------|------------------|-------|
| Organic Strawberries | 5 SOVS | +18 | +1 per dip | Three berries. Structurally sound. |
| Pineapple Chunks | 5 SOVS | +16 | +1 per dip | The only item that survives four dips. Bradley's choice, eaten dry. |
| Banana Slices | 5 SOVS | +14 | +1 per dip | Falls off the skewer. Always. Retrieved by hand 100% of the time (+3). |

**Achievements**

- **"Eén Keer Dopen"** ("One Dip") — leave Shamsterdam having dipped exactly once. The rarest achievement in the city.
- **"Ik Heb Niet Eens Gedoopt"** ("I Didn't Even Dip") — reach Tier 3 as Bradley without a single voluntary dip

### The Health Inspection Running Gag
- **Inspector**: **Gerda Vonk**, municipal hygiene inspector, clipboard, probe thermometer, shoe covers, no small talk
- **Schedule**: Quarterly, on a fixed in-game calendar. Four visits over a standard playthrough.
- **Procedure**: Ropes the fountain. Forty minutes. Takes a sample. Photographs the sign. Writes the violation. Leaves. Never shuts the fountain down.
- **The Card**: The HYGIËNESCORE plate on the plinth downgrades across the game as a visible world-state tell.

| Visit | Score | Museum's Response |
|-------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | **A** | None required |
| 2 | **B** | Replaced the ONE DIP sign with a larger ONE DIP sign |
| 3 | **C** | Added a rope. Added a second attendant whose entire job is saying "one dip." |
| 4 | **IN BEHANDELING** ("Under Review") | Replaced the score card with a card showing only the inspection date |

- **The Economics, Stated Plainly In-Game**: The fine is 1,200 Sovs. The fountain clears that before noon on a Saturday. Switching it off for a day costs more than the fine. Nobody is hiding this calculation; it is in the annual report, which is on a shelf in the gift shop, for 8 Sovs, and has sold four copies.
- **Gerda Is Not An Antagonist**: She is correct, professional, thorough and entirely unbothered. She has told them. They have listened. Nothing has changed. This is normal and she has forty other premises this week.
- **The Bros' Reading**: A woman in a hi-vis vest roping off a chocolate fountain reads to them as an exclusive event. They queue harder. Chadwick films her. She does not object; she is in a public square and she has read the same regulations he has not.

### The Giggling Figure Encounter (Retained)
- **Trigger**: Motion sensor, automatic on entry, four steps inside the door
- **First Encounter**: The whole lobby jumps at once and then the whole lobby *laughs*. The bros are startled for exactly half a second and delighted for the rest of the day. **+1 Bravado, not −Composure** — they are not scared of a mannequin, they think it rules. Composure is a temporary state that applies only to ambient visitor NPCs and reads as a gag, never as a penalty on the party.
- **Second Encounter**: Photo opportunity. Chadwick INSISTS on filming. Then re-filming. Four takes.
- **Anticipation Tell**: Mechanical clicking ~1.2 seconds before activation. Once learned, the bros start *counting down out loud* for the benefit of strangers who have not learned it.
- **The Bench**: Ambient visitors use it. The bros never sit on the bench. Lord Pilsner has offered his seat to someone who needed it and considers this the most gentlemanly act of his life.
- **Staff Reaction**: Completely deadpan. Seen it ten thousand times, and now in better acoustics.

### The Cacaogalerij (Museum Wing)
- **Examine**: Every vitrine has full placard text in three languages. Reading three placards grants **+Cultural Alibi** progress.
- **Photography**: Permitted everywhere except De Oervorm (nitrogen case, no flash). The bros will photograph De Oervorm with flash. Ruud will appear.
- **The Tempering Room**: A three-minute idle observation window. Watching the full cycle grants **+Trivial Knowledge** and a permanent dialogue option about seeding temperatures that no NPC in the game responds well to.
- **The Failures Case**: Examining it grants nothing mechanically and is the only exhibit in the building with a genuinely sad placard. It is worth reading.

### The Photo Consequences System (Retained & Reframed)
- **Photos Taken**: Collected in the **Venus Temple Collection** sub-album
- **The Split**: Fountain exteriors are unflagged and shared everywhere. Interior photos are flagged — not because the bros are ashamed, but because certain NPCs will simply stop the conversation.
- **Usage Restrictions**: Interior photos cannot be shown to Mom (ICQ), Ingrid (hostel), or Agent Havok. The bros try anyway, every time, with total confidence.
- **The Payoff**: The fountain photo they send to their mothers is a four-metre chocolate phallus, posed with, at arm's length, grinning. They have not noticed. Mom has. Mom replies **"Looks fun!"** and nothing else, and that reply is the single most devastating line in Shamsterdam.

### Bravado Interactions
This location is a **Bravado engine**. The default trajectory is upward, loudly, and the bros earn most of it legitimately.

- **Gain Bravado**: Dip. Dip again. Dip something already bitten. Lean into the cascade. Ride out the Giggling Figure without flinching. Read three Cacaogalerij placards and repeat one incorrectly to a stranger. Correctly identify the John Lennon piece. Say "couverture" out loud. Buy the 45 Sovs boxed Collectie. Read Gunter's board aloud, in full, at volume, to the queue.
- **Lose Bravado**: Only four things do it, and none of them is enjoying yourself. Asking Gunter whether the fountain is "clean." Asking Gunter whether the chocolate is "real." Buying the 1 SOV bare skewer and being seen doing it. Reaching Tier 5 in front of an NPC who then quietly ends the conversation.

## System Integration

### The Cultural Alibi Flag
- **Type**: Permanent world flag, granted on paying the 9 Sovs and entering the Cacaogalerij
- **Effect**: Unlocks the dialogue option "We went to a museum" in every city, with every NPC, for the rest of the game
- **The Joke**: It is completely true. They did go to a museum. It is a real museum with a curator, a climate-controlled vitrine, a tempering room, and placards in three languages. They cannot describe a single exhibit.
- **NPC Reactions**: 60% polite acknowledgement, 30% "which one," 10% "the chocolate one?" — and the bros always say yes to "the chocolate one" with visible pride
- **Escalation**: Combined with The Hemp Museum, unlocks "We've been to TWO museums," delivered with decreasing conviction each time it is used

### Photo Album: "Venus Temple Collection"

## Audio Design

### Environmental Audio (Original Atmosphere)
- **Plaza Soundscape**:
 - The cascade — a continuous wet, heavy, slightly slapping flow. The location's signature sound. Recognisable from two streets away.
 - The pump — a low mechanical hum under the cascade
 - Skewer snap and cart transaction rhythm from Gunter's Deep Dips
 - Crowd murmur, camera shutters, and one specific laugh that recurs every forty seconds from somewhere in the queue
 - Ruud, distant, saying "one dip" on an endless loop
- **Interior Soundscape**:
 - Gallery hush and climate-control whisper (ground floor)
 - Mechanical clicking, pneumatic hissing, Victorian music box (first floor)
 - Camera flash bursts and group photo chatter (second floor)
 - Till, wrapping paper, tape dispenser (gift shop)
- **Music Style**: Plaza — bright, brassy, faintly municipal, like a city that has decided to be fun about this. Ground floor — hushed, tasteful, a gallery cue played completely straight. First floor — subdued vintage cabaret with a music-box quality.
- **Audio Transitions**: The cascade is audible, attenuated, from the entrance hall and the ground floor. It never fully goes away. That is deliberate.
- **Interactive Audio**: The dip (a specific, wet, deeply satisfying sound), the lift-and-drip, the drop into the collection bowl, coin clunk, peep show flip, Marilyn's whoosh

### Comedy Audio Integration
- **The Dip**: Engineered to be genuinely, unreasonably satisfying. The player should want to do it again. That is the mechanic.
- **The Drip**: A three-second trailing drip after every lift. Bare-hand dips have a distinct, worse version.
- **The Giggle**: Signature sound effect, unmistakable, unchanged since 1985
- **Jump Scare Stinger**: Musical hit on the bros' first Giggling Figure encounter
- **The Gurgle**: At Tier 2, a subtle ambient stomach gurgle enters the party's audio bed and never fully leaves. It gets louder by tier. No character ever mentions it.
- **Character Voice Direction**: Gunter's clipped efficiency, Anke's deadpan, Ruud's fraying neutrality, Gerda's procedural flatness, Lotte's genuine warmth
