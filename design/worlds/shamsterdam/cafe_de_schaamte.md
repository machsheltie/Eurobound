# Café de Schaamte

*Where European sophistication meets American obliviousness — outdoor espresso served with a side of judgment*

### Exterior Description
- **Approach**: Cobblestone canal-side walkway with bicycle traffic constantly threatening pedestrians
- **Entrance**: No formal entrance — outdoor terrace with mismatched chairs spills onto the sidewalk
- **Signage**: Hand-painted wooden sign reading "Café de Schaamte" with small text: "Est. 1847 — Judging Tourists Since 1967"
- **Atmosphere**: Cigarette smoke (from Europeans), weed smoke (drifting from nearby coffeeshop), and espresso steam mingle in the canal breeze
- **Environmental Context**: Perfect view of tourists falling into the canal while trying to take selfies

### Interior Visuals
- **Layout**: No true interior — outdoor terrace with 12 small tables, cramped spacing forcing uncomfortable proximity to strangers' conversations
- **Lighting**: Natural daylight filtered through ancient awning, string lights at night, canal water reflections dancing on everything
- **Furniture & Props**:
  - Wobbly wrought-iron tables with mismatched chairs (none comfortable)
  - Espresso machine older than most American cities, operated by barista with aggressive competence
  - Chalkboard menu with prices that make Americans gasp
  - Single communal ashtray passed between tables (technically illegal, universally ignored)
  - Stack of international newspapers, all weeks old, most read incorrectly
- **Decorative Elements**:
  - Faded photographs of the café through decades (tourists looking confused in all of them)
  - Potted geraniums in traditional Dutch style, desperately trying to brighten the judgment
  - Canal-facing railing where locals lean and smoke while making eye contact with disappointing tourists
  - Hand-written sign: "FREE WIFI — Password: 'OrderSomethingFirst'"
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Steam from espresso machine, occasional canal boat wake splashing terrace edge, bicycle bells constant
- **Hidden Areas**: None (everything happens in public, that's part of the shame)

## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The quintessential European outdoor café where locals performatively ignore tourists while internally cataloging every faux pas. Espresso costs 5 Sovs, comes in a cup the size of a thimble, and the waiter won't check on you for 45 minutes because that's "the European way." A place where Americans learn that "café culture" means sitting, being judged, and paying for the privilege.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: American tourists expecting American service in European establishments
- **Secondary Targets**: Performative European sophistication, overpriced canal-side pricing, outdoor smoking culture collision
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dutch directness meets tourist obliviousness, Amsterdam's exhaustion with "that kind" of visitor
- **Humor Approach**: Dry European judgment humor meets loud American confusion comedy
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Targets service culture clash and tourist behavior, not legitimate café workers or Dutch people

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Charging 8 Sovs for a croissant because you're sitting near water
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: Staff have perfected the art of serving tourists while making them feel unwelcome
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros think they're experiencing "authentic European café culture" while being systematically overcharged
- **Comedy of Errors**: Agent Havok's attempt at "blending in as a local" is painfully obvious to everyone except the bros
- **Consequence System**: Ordering incorrectly results in barista sighing loud enough to damage Bravado

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Having a sophisticated European café experience, receiving exclusive intel from mysterious local contact

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Being overcharged for tiny espresso while a poorly-disguised Sheltie hands them crayon drawings and calls it a "mission briefing"

**How impaired are they**: Sober enough to feel cultured, oblivious enough to miss every red flag

**Obvious signs they ignore**: Agent's newspaper is upside down, "mission dossier" is clearly a brunch recipe, his "Dutch accent" slips constantly, he flinches at pigeons

**How locals play along**: Café staff serve them without comment because tourist money is tourist money, plus this is the most entertaining thing they've seen all week

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Café Service System (Original)
- **Ordering Process**: Approach counter, attempt to order, receive judgment
- **Service Timing**: Waiter arrives on their schedule, not yours (random 30-90 second delay after ordering)
- **Tipping Confusion**: Game prompts "Tip?" — any amount triggers different NPC response (Europeans don't tip like Americans)
- **Language Barrier Mini-Game**: Attempt to pronounce Dutch menu items, fail gracefully or catastrophically

### Menu & Consumables
| Item | Price | Effects | Barista Judgment Level |
|------|-------|---------|----------------------|
| Espresso | 5 Sovs | +15 SP, removes Drowsy status | Low (acceptable order) |
| "American Coffee" | 5 Sovs | +10 SP, +Shame buff (locals notice) | Medium (tourist detected) |
| Stroopwafel | 4 Sovs | +20 HP, +Comfort status | None (everyone loves stroopwafel) |
| "Croissant" (day-old) | 8 Sovs | +10 HP, -Sovs significantly | High (you paid 8 Sovs for THIS?) |
| Heineken (bottle) | 6 Sovs | +Tipsy status, -Bravado (locals judge) | Maximum (tourists drink Heineken) |
| "The Giselle Special" | ??? Sovs | Item doesn't exist, barista confusion | Extreme (Havok-exclusive order) |

## NPCs & Vendors
### Café Staff
- **Barista Wouter**: *"I have made coffee since before your country had coffee. Do not tell me you want it 'iced.'"*
  - Operates ancient espresso machine with mechanical precision
  - Sighs are measurable on the Richter scale
  - Will serve you perfectly while making you feel inadequate
  
- **Server Margot**: *"You want the check? You will get the check when I decide you are finished."*
  - Appears and disappears on unknowable schedule
  - Treats all questions as minor inconveniences
  - Knows exactly what you want, serves what you need

### Café Regulars (Atmospheric NPCs)
- **The Cyclist Who Stopped**: Dutch local on phone call, espresso untouched, radiates judgment
- **The Backpacker Who's "Basically a Local Now"**: American who's been here three weeks, corrects other tourists' pronunciation incorrectly
- **The Instagram Table**: Four people, eight phones, no conversation, photographing drinks from every angle
- **Old Man Reading**: Same newspaper every day, same table since 1983, claims he "doesn't speak English" but clearly does

### Agent Havok (Quest NPC)
- **Initial Appearance**: "Mysterious Local Contact" at corner table
- **Visual Tells**: SHAMSTERDAM hoodie with tags, upside-down newspaper, tail occasionally wagging under table
- **Cover Identity**: "Joop" (allegedly), "Very Shamsterdam local person who lives here and is from here"
- **Mission Role**: Quest giver for Fabergé Egg Hunt, potential recurring mission source
- **Dialogue Style**: Military briefing meets desperate improv ("As we say here in my homeland... *checks hand* ...Shamsterdam...")

### NPC Dialogue (Original)
**Barista Wouter:**
- "Espresso. Single or double. These are your choices. No, I will not make it 'fun-sized.'"
- "The wifi password is 'OrderSomethingFirst.' Yes, with capitals. Yes, all one word. No, I will not repeat it."
- "Your friend ordered a 'venti.' We do not have 'venti.' We have 'espresso' and 'slightly more espresso.'"

**Server Margot:**
- "You have been sitting for one hour. You have ordered one espresso. In America this is perhaps acceptable. Here, we notice."
- "The check will arrive when the check decides to arrive. You may wait, or you may leave money and hope it is correct."
- "No, you cannot 'just get the wifi' without ordering. This is a café, not a library. The library is three blocks east."

**Agent Havok (Cover Broken):**
- "Ja, ja, very authentic, this establishment. I come here every day. To my favorite table. Which is... *looks around* ...this one. Now."
- "The 'Giselle Special' is, ah, a local delicacy. You would not understand. Very... *waves hands* ...Shamsterdam."
- "We must speak quietly. The pigeons. They could be working for *anyone*."

- **Late Night (10 PM - 6 AM)**: Café technically "closed" but Havok's table mysteriously accessible
