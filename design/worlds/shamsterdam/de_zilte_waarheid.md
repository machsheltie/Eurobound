## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The herring stand is a cultural truth test disguised as street food. Eating raw herring the Dutch way — holding the fish by its tail, tilting your head back, and lowering it into your mouth in public — is a moment of absolute vulnerability. Every tourist wants the "authentic experience" until they're standing at the stand, fish in hand, realizing they have to DO this in front of PEOPLE. De Zilte Waarheid (The Salty Truth) is that you will learn something about yourself here, and the fish is just the delivery mechanism.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: The gap between "wanting authentic experiences" and being willing to look foolish for them
- **Secondary Targets**: Tourist photo-op culture, performative travel, the male pride of "I can eat anything"
- **Cultural Specificity**: Dutch haring tradition is genuine national culture, the comedy is tourist reaction
- **Humor Approach**: The fish isn't the joke — your face while eating it is
- **Sensitivity Considerations**: Respects Dutch tradition, satirizes tourist hesitation and bravado

### Seedy Underbelly Elements
- **Primary Vice**: Charging 4 Sovs for a moment of existential revelation
- **City-Specific Comedy Operations**: The stand is positioned for maximum visibility of tourist attempts
- **Buffoon Exploitation**: Bros talk big about eating anything, then meet the herring
- **Comedy of Errors**: The technique is simple, the execution is always photographed
- **Consequence System**: "Fish Breath" status, but also genuine respect earned

### Buffoon Delusion Scenarios
**What bros THINK is happening**: Casual street food experience, quick cultural checkbox

**What is ACTUALLY happening**: Public vulnerability test, character revelation, potentially life-changing fish experience

**How impaired are they**: Usually sober (this is daytime, the herring demands clarity)

**Obvious signs they ignore**:
- The illustrated instructions suggesting this isn't "normal" eating
- Other tourists' expressions mid-attempt
- The vendor's patient, seen-everything expression
- Local customers doing it effortlessly, instantly
- The seagulls waiting for dropped fish

**The twist**: The herring is actually delicious IF you commit — hesitation is the enemy

**How locals interact**: Brief nod if you succeed, complete non-acknowledgment if you struggle (mercy)

### The Traditional Method Tutorial
**The Shamsterdam Way — Illustrated and Inescapable**

**Step 1: The Grip**
- Hold fish by tail between thumb and forefinger
- Fish should hang freely, showing commitment
- Do not look at the fish with fear

**Step 2: The Tilt**
- Head back, face to sky
- Mouth open, dignity suspended
- Accept that photos are being taken

**Step 3: The Lower**
- Lower fish toward mouth
- First bite should be significant
- Chewing is required (swallowing whole is not the goal)

**Step 4: The Repeat**
- Continue until only tail remains
- Tail may be discarded or eaten (advanced)
- Fish breath is now your companion

**The Hesitation Penalty**:
- Holding fish too long without committing = visible struggle
- Attempting modified technique = "tourist grip" noted
- Putting fish down = the seagulls win

### The "First Bite" Moment
- **Trigger**: Initial herring consumption
- **Effect**: Brief pause, game checks for reaction
- **Possible Outcomes**:
  - **Commitment**: Full bite, proper technique → +Bravado, +Respect
  - **Hesitation**: Small bite, modified method → Neutral
  - **Retreat**: Put fish down, request bread → -Bravado, Fish Wasted status

### Status Effects
**Fish Breath**
- **Duration**: 2 hours (in-game)
- **Effect**: NPCs maintain slightly larger conversation distance
- **Cure**: Strong mints (available at stand, 1 Sov), time, or not caring
- **Social Impact**: Other herring survivors recognize you as one of their own

**Onion Aura** (if ordered "met uitjes")
- **Duration**: 3 hours (in-game)
- **Effect**: Stacks with Fish Breath, romance options limited
- **Benefit**: +Authentic status, certain Dutch NPCs respond more warmly
- **Bros' Response**: "We ALL have it so it doesn't count"

**The Courage Buff** (Traditional method only)
- **Duration**: Rest of day
- **Effect**: +10% Bravado retention, faced a fear
- **Narrative**: "You ate raw fish in public. Other challenges seem smaller now."

### Bravado Interactions
- **Gain Bravado**: Complete traditional method, order seconds, help another tourist with technique
- **Lose Bravado**: Retreat to bread option, drop fish (seagull victory), visible struggle exceeding 30 seconds
- **Neutral**: Ordering broodje haring (acceptable gateway)

### The Seagull Threat
- **Presence**: Constant overhead surveillance
- **Behavior**: Waiting for dropped fish, tourist hesitation, unguarded moments
- **Mechanical Effect**: Dropped fish = instant seagull acquisition, -Fish, -Dignity
- **The Circle of Life**: Seagulls have been doing this for generations

## NPCs & Vendors

**Visser Maarten (Fisherman Maarten)**
- **Role**: Stand owner, herring preparer, silent judge
- **Appearance**: 60s, weathered face, fisherman's apron, hands that have processed 100,000 herrings
- **Background**: Third-generation herring vendor, worked the boats before the stand, knows fish
- **Demeanor**: Patient, efficient, has seen every possible tourist reaction, nothing surprises him
- **Expression**: Neutral but with eyes that have witnessed humanity's relationship with raw fish
- **Dialogue**:
  - "Haring? Traditional or on bread?"
