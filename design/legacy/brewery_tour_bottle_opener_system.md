# Brewery Tour — Complete Bottle Opener System

*Every location, every opener, every prerequisite*

---

## System Overview

### The Golden Rule
**The Blarney Trap Bottle Opener is THE KEY to the entire Brewery Tour quest.** Without acquiring it first, you cannot:
- Participate in the Brewery Tour at any location
- Acquire any other bottle openers
- Get any stamps
- Purchase any signature beers

### How It Works
1. **FIRST**: Buy The Blarney Trap Bottle Opener (15 Sovs) OR win a Pint-Off at The Blarney Trap
2. This unlocks the Brewery Tour quest AND gets you The Blarney Trap's stamp + signature beer
3. **THEN**: At each subsequent location, you must acquire THAT location's specific bottle opener
4. Each location's opener = access to that location's stamp + signature beer
5. Collect all openers in a city = city collector bonus
6. Collect all openers everywhere = Continental Beer Master

### The Prerequisite Chain
```
THE BLARNEY TRAP OPENER (15 SOVS or Pint-Off)
         ↓
    [UNLOCKS BREWERY TOUR QUEST]
         ↓
    ├── PUBLANDIA (3 more openers to acquire)
    ├── DEBAUCHERYVILLE (4 openers to acquire)
    ├── SINFONIA (4 openers to acquire)
    └── SHAMSTERDAM (2 openers to acquire)
```
