# EUROBOUND CANON — THE BROS CHOOSE DEBAUCHERY

**Status:** standing Eurobound canon, author-approved 2026-11-09. Binding on all game content, systems and agents. Verbatim author text.
**Related:** `design/mechanics/intoxication_law.md`.

AUTHOR DIRECTIVE

Pilsner, Chadwick, and Bradley are enthusiastic, self-deluding adult antiheroes.

They actively pursue partying, drinking, drugs, hookups, strip clubs, sex workers, outrageous performances, and other questionable experiences.

They are not reluctant participants who need to be taught how to misbehave.

The comedy comes from the collision between their enormous confidence and their increasingly unreliable judgment, aging bodies, and terrible decisions.

Intoxicants do not provide uncomplicated, consequence-free advantages. Any apparent benefit should be temporary, deceptive, compromised, or accompanied by an entertaining drawback.

Do not append moral lectures to drug or alcohol scenes. The characters' choices and their consequences communicate the satire.

Sexual encounters may be enjoyable, embarrassing, disappointing, unexpectedly successful, or catastrophically awkward. The protagonists do not require a wholesome lesson afterward.

When designing an item, status, or encounter, ask:

What does the bro THINK he's getting? What does he ACTUALLY get? And how can the difference become a memorable gameplay consequence?

The joke should land through dialogue, animation, player choice, and consequences—not explanatory narration.

Keep the bros willing. Keep them delusional. Keep the consequences funny.
