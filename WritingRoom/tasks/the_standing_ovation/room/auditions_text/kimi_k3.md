## 1. Quest Header

**Working quest title:** **After the Ovation: Beer Lord Rising**  

**City / venue:** Sinfonia — The Standing Ovation  

**Location ID:** `sinfonia_operadistrict_standing_ovation_01`  

**Optional trigger:** Enter during a scheduled late-night performance of **MAX VEX PRESENTS: THE FIRST DAUGHTERS**. No prior visit, inventory item, cover charge, or quest completion required.  

**Estimated playtime:** 20–25 minutes  

**Core comic premise:** The bros attend a genuinely brilliant revue and mistake being monetized, erased, and sexually discombobulated for a three-man career renaissance.

**Working billing:**

> **AFTER THE OVATION**  

> **MAX VEX PRESENTS: THE FIRST DAUGHTERS**  

> *A Turn-of-the-Millennium American Political Drag Revue*  

> **CHELSEA MORNING · JENNA TALIA · BARBARA BUSHED**

## 2. Opening / Entry

The Standing Ovation has changed its posture.

Orchestra Pit tables angle toward the stage. The organ-pipe taps remain in full operation, but the bartenders now pour under sharpened theatrical spots. Above the curtain:

> **APPLAUSE IS ENCOURAGED.  

> RHYTHM IS OPTIONAL.**

Chadwick raises his camcorder and pans across the room.

**CHADWICK:** Chat, we have found Sinfonia’s best-kept cultural secret.

A server passes beneath the twenty-foot illuminated show poster.

**SERVER:** It’s on the building.

**CHADWICK:** Hiding in plain sight.

Herr Dramatisch sweeps up with three playbills beneath his cape.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** You may take a standard table. Or—for seventy-two SOVS—join my **Producer’s Circle**: a reserved front table, three house pours, and public acknowledgment of your patronage.

**PILSNER:** Boys. They’re recruiting us.

**BRADLEY:** It’s on the menu.

**PILSNER:** Printed in advance. Saves time.

### CHOICE 1 — Seating

**[Take a standard table.]**

**PILSNER:** We don’t purchase prestige. We attract it.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** Then it may find you at the bar.

They receive an ordinary table with a perfectly good view. Pilsner calls it “the wing position” and claims the sightlines are more tactical.

**[Join the Producer’s Circle — 72 SOVS.]**

**BRADLEY:** Beers are eight each. That’s forty-eight for somebody saying your name.

**PILSNER:** A sentence about me.

Bradley considers this.

**BRADLEY:** Front table?

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** Magnificent front table.

**BRADLEY:** Yeah, okay.

Before the show, Herr Dramatisch raises one hand.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** Tonight’s Producer’s Circle: Lord Pilsner.

Pilsner stands and acknowledges the room with a small, presidential wave. Chadwick half-rises beside him.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** Only one name was supplied.

Chadwick sits.

**PILSNER:** Consolidated billing.

**CHADWICK:** I get that.

The house lights fall.

## 3. The Actual Drag Performance

A spotlight finds **Max Vex** at the curtain.

Their black tailcoat is cut close at the waist, satin running through the torso like a corset before disappearing into immaculate tuxedo trousers. One earring catches the light. They wait until the room stops applauding its own ability to applaud.

**MAX:** Good evening. No flash. Keep your drinks vertical and your personalities to a manageable length.

Max’s gaze settles briefly on Chadwick’s camera.

**MAX:** You may film. You may not make it my problem.

**CHADWICK:** Open-set access.

**PILSNER:** He’s press.

**MAX:** Of course he is.

The band strikes a clipped, glamorous introduction.

**MAX:** Tonight: three young women, one voracious American press corps, and absolutely no dignity left for anybody holding a camera.

Chadwick lowers his camcorder. Then raises it again.

### CHELSEA MORNING — “No Comment”

Chelsea Morning enters in a powder-blue skirt suit, pearls, and a luxurious chestnut wig, carrying three textbooks and a velvet folder marked **PRIVATE**.

Pizzicato strings. A polite harpsichord. Chelsea walks with the crushing composure of somebody informed that the cameras are not present while looking directly into one.

A photographer’s flash pops from the wings.

She freezes into a perfect public smile.

Another flash.

The smile improves by exactly one percent.

Then the harpsichord drops into a hard electronic beat.

Chelsea tosses the textbooks into the wings, where a stagehand catches all three in one stack. Her live vocal begins as crisp, smiling patter. Each verse is punctuated by another flash and another increasingly impossible pose: chin down, shoulder back, one foot on a chair, pearls swung once around her neck and caught without looking.

Two silhouettes appear behind a scrim, copying her poses with deliberate inaccuracy. Chelsea turns. They become perfectly still.

She opens the **PRIVATE** folder. It contains a mirror.

Chelsea checks her reflection, snaps it shut, and tears away the skirt to reveal crystal-trimmed trousers. She launches into a tap break along the stage’s narrow apron, finishing inches from the front row without disturbing a glass.

**PILSNER:** Dude. That’s Chelsea Clinton.

**CHADWICK:** That’s not Chelsea Clinton.

**PILSNER:** I know what Chelsea Clinton looks like.

**CHADWICK:** Apparently you don’t.

**PILSNER:** I watched MTV.

Chelsea holds the final note while removing one pearl earring, inspecting it for an imaginary wire, and dropping it into the velvet folder.

The audience roars.

Chadwick stands for a better angle. A server presses him firmly back into his chair.

**CHADWICK:** Fixed camera position. Good.

### JENNA TALIA & BARBARA BUSHED — “Which One Are You?”

Two nearly identical figures burst from opposite wings in rhinestone-trimmed going-out tops, asymmetrical satin skirts, tiny shoulder bags, and heels built with structural confidence.

**Jenna Talia**, honey blond, attacks the choreography: fast turns, high kicks, smiles thrown directly at individual patrons.

**Barbara Bushed**, dark-haired, sings the first verse with cool precision while performing the same routine with half the visible effort. Whenever Jenna adds a spin, Barbara removes one. They remain synchronized.

Their duet depends on switching places.

Jenna crosses left; Barbara crosses right. Feather fans rise. When they drop, Jenna is singing Barbara’s low harmony and Barbara is delivering Jenna’s breathless spoken interjections.

They move into the audience on parallel routes, sitting on table edges without putting weight on a single drink.

Jenna plucks Chadwick’s camcorder from his hands and turns it on him. He grins into the lens while, behind him, Barbara performs a flawless sequence of turns and finishes balanced on the arm of an empty chair.

Jenna returns the camera.

**CHADWICK:** I’m in the number.

**PILSNER:** You’re in the way.

**PILSNER:** Oh shit. Bush twins.

**BRADLEY:** Both of them?

**PILSNER:** Yeah.

**BRADLEY:** Which one’s which?

**PILSNER:** Dude, nobody knew.

Onstage, the twins open their shoulder bags simultaneously and produce two bottles of spring water. They toast with exaggerated innocence, trade bottles without looking, and resume identical poses.

The younger patron at the next table leans toward Pilsner.

**YOUNGER PATRON:** Who is Bush?

**PILSNER:** You’re kidding.

**YOUNGER PATRON:** No, but keep going.

**PILSNER:** The president.

**YOUNGER PATRON:** Which one?

Pilsner looks at the stage, then at his beer.

**PILSNER:** I watched this happen live.

**YOUNGER PATRON:** On TV?

**PILSNER:** On TRL.

The younger patron waits for clarification that will not help either of them.

**PILSNER:** Bradley, get another round.

**BRADLEY:** We just got these.

**PILSNER:** This one’s become archival.

### FINALE — “First Daughters, Last Call”

Chelsea returns in a white crystal tailcoat. The twins flank her in black versions, now wearing each other’s wigs.

The three sing a close-harmony finale through deliberately impossible chair choreography. Chelsea remains exact; Jenna keeps trying to escape the formation; Barbara keeps hauling her back by one sparkling coat tail.

At the bar, Herr Dramatisch conducts the taps with his cape. Three bartenders deliver simultaneous pours on the final chord. Chelsea takes one glass without breaking the harmony. The twins discover they have somehow ended up with the same glass and solve the problem by sharing a straw with frosty twin dignity.

The final formation collapses into a perfectly controlled can-can.

The **APPLAUSE** sign illuminates after the room is already standing.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** Magnificent.

The balcony continues.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** **MAGNIFICENT.**

The queens bow separately, then drag Max between them for a fourth bow. Max permits this for exactly two seconds before reclaiming center stage.

**MAX:** You may now return to your drinks. Several have developed a richer inner life than their owners.

## 4. Post-Show Max Encounter

The house lights warm. At the bar, the queens accept congratulations, correct one another’s stories, and steal garnish from Herr Dramatisch’s latest pour.

Pilsner intercepts Max with his playbill.

**PILSNER:** Lord Pilsner.

*If he purchased the Producer’s Circle:*

**PILSNER:** I produced tonight.

**MAX:** You heard your name and remained seated. The theater is grateful.

*Otherwise:*

**PILSNER:** I recognized every reference.

**MAX:** The playbill will be thrilled.

Max takes in the frosted spikes, puka shells, and cargo shorts.

**MAX:** Lord Pilsner is a lot of administration for a man dressed like a spring-break prize pack. Beer Lord.

Pilsner receives this like a knighthood.

**PILSNER:** Beer Lord. That has a touring future.

Max laughs despite themself.

**MAX:** God, you’re serious.

**PILSNER:** And yet you’re laughing.

**MAX:** I am. That’s on me.

Chadwick pushes between them, camera up.

**CHADWICK:** Chadwick. I got the whole show. I’m thinking three uploads, behind-the-scenes footage, director’s commentary.

**MAX:** You’re thinking loudly.

**CHADWICK:** It’s a process. I can get you a MySpace presence.

**MAX:** I already have a presence.

**CHADWICK:** Different demo.

Max looks at his two popped collars.

**MAX:** One collar per personality?

**CHADWICK:** Exactly.

**MAX:** Ambitious.

Chadwick nods, encouraged.

Max turns to Bradley, who has been silently gripping his playbill.

**MAX:** And Basketball Jersey. You’re cute.

*Bradley freezes.*

**MAX:** Don’t ruin it by speaking.

**BRADLEY:** I wasn’t—

**MAX:** There it is.

Bradley straightens. Then, with terrible purpose, he lowers the playbill to belt level and holds it there with both hands.

Max notices.

**MAX:** Oh.

**BRADLEY:** What?

**MAX:** Nothing.

**BRADLEY:** What?

**MAX:** Apparently somebody here is more progressive than he thought.

**BRADLEY:** It’s the denim. It bunches.

Max looks at the relaxed-fit jeans.

**MAX:** Does it?

**BRADLEY:** Leg day.

**CHADWICK:** You haven’t done legs since we landed.

**BRADLEY:** That’s why it’s backed up.

Max gives the playbill one affectionate tap.

**MAX:** Trouser Snake.

**BRADLEY:** That’s not better.

**MAX:** It isn’t for you.

They are already turning toward the bar.

**MAX:** Right. Beer Lord, Trouser Snake and…

*Max looks at Chadwick.*

**MAX:** …what did you say your name was again?

**CHADWICK:** Chadwick.

**MAX:** Right.

**CHADWICK:** You know my name.

**MAX:** Do I?

**CHADWICK:** Could you just say it into the lens? Once?

Max leans toward the camera. Chadwick brightens.

**MAX:** Tip your server.

From the bar:

**CHELSEA MORNING:** Max. Jenna’s teaching him the chair routine.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** THE CAPE HAS FOUND ITS DANCE.

**MAX:** Not the cape.

Max leaves immediately. By the time they reach the bar, they are laughing at something Barbara has said and stealing the cherry from her drink. Barbara takes it back.

Bradley has not moved.

Chadwick reviews his footage: his own face, Bradley’s playbill, and Max’s public-service announcement.

**CHADWICK:** They’re saving my name for the reveal.

**PILSNER:** Obviously.

## 7. Quest Objectives, Completion, Rewards and Consequences

### Objectives

1. Attend **After the Ovation**.

2. Watch **The First Daughters** through the finale.

3. Meet Max Vex after the show.

4. Choose how the party handles the next round.

Leaving before the finale does not fail the quest; it remains available at the next scheduled performance.

### Completion

Control resumes after the chosen aftermath. The party may drink, leave, or watch Chadwick try to improve his footage by adding a title card.

At the bar, Max and the queens are discussing food.

**MAX:** I am not splitting another bottle with people who call that dinner.

## 8. Implementation / Continuity Notes

### Essential flags

```text

so_after_ovation_seen

max_vex_met

pilsner_nickname_beer_lord

bradley_nickname_trouser_snake

chadwick_vex_no_nickname

so_after_ovation_complete

aftermath_water | aftermath_drinks | aftermath_toast

```

Only one `aftermath_*` flag is set.

### Status duration

**TROUSER SNAKE** uses the supplied values directly and persists through the current evening unless Bradley drinks substantial alcohol. The alcohol branch suppresses its immediate physical/social presentation and applies ordinary intoxication; it does not clear attraction, revoke the nickname, or record any identity resolution.

If required by the engine, `TROUSER_SNAKE_SUPPRESSED` is a proposed container rather than a new permanent trait. Evening rollover clears the active or suppressed status, not the social flags.

### Scheduling dependency

**Proposed schedule:** **11:30 PM–12:10 AM**, with the encounter and aftermath available before the existing **1 AM** closing. This requires no extension to the venue’s listed **4 PM–1 AM** hours. Any later residency would require a separately approved exception.

### Awaiting approval

- Proposed prices and collectible playbill/photograph

### Optional and repeat visits

The quest requires only choosing this performance. It works on a first visit without brewery or main-story prerequisites. Before completion, later performances retain the opportunity. After completion, the revue may remain replayable without repeating first-meeting dialogue, purchases, rewards, or the automatic status award.
