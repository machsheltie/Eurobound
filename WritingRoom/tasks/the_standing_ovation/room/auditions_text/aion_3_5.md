# EUROBOUND — SIDEQUEST: THE FIRST DAUGHTERS (REVISED)

---

## 1. QUEST HEADER

**Working Title:** The First Daughters

**City/Venue:** Sinfonia — The Standing Ovation (`sinfonia_operadistrict_standing_ovation_01`)

**Trigger:** Optional. Activates when the party enters The Standing Ovation after 10:00 PM. No prerequisites; a prior daytime brewery visit is flavor, not a requirement.

**Estimated Playtime:** 20–25 minutes

**Core Comic Premise:** A genuinely spectacular drag revue parodies the turn-of-the-millennium America the bros still think they live in, and the venue quietly converts their delusions into an itemized bill — which they read as a trophy list.

---

## 2. OPENING / ENTRY

*EXT. OPERA DISTRICT — NIGHT*

*The bros drift down the street following a trail of applause leaking out of a doorway.*

**PILSNER:** Boys. You hear that? That's the sound of people having a night they'll never forget. Rule one of Europe: follow the applause.

**CHADWICK:** You made that up thirty seconds ago.

**PILSNER:** And it's working. That's leadership.

*INT. THE STANDING OVATION — ORCHESTRA PIT*

*The room has transformed. The organ-pipe taps still gleam, but the tables now face the stage, the velvet curtain is closed, and the spots are sharp and hungry. A chalkboard reads:*

> **TONIGHT, 10:30 — MAX VEX PRESENTS: THE FIRST DAUGHTERS**

> *A Turn-of-the-Millennium American Political Drag Revue*

> COVER: 15 SOVS

*HERR DRAMATISCH — cape, handlebar mustache — materializes.*

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** Gentlemen. You arrive at the hour of TRANSFORMATION. Cover is fifteen. Or — *(he sweeps his cape toward three front tables behind a velvet rope)* — the ORCHESTRA PREMIUM. The seats the performers can see best. Twenty additional.

**PILSNER:** *(already taking out his wallet)* The seats the performers can see best. You hear that, Chadwick? You think Usher sits in the back?

**CHADWICK:** Who's watching us is usually the problem.

**PILSNER:** Not tonight. Tonight we're the show. *(beat, reconsidering, then with total confidence)* I mean — we're not the show, obviously, there's a show. But we're *of* the show.

*They pay 105 SOVS. The velvet rope is moved three feet to reveal their table is the one the main spotlight hits hardest. As they sit, the three queens' heads, visible in the wings, turn toward the table the way lions regard a salad bar.*

*The first pints arrive. Herr Dramatisch does not release the tray.*

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** House rule. The first pour of the revue must be toasted, and the toast must be THEATRICAL. Who among you carries the voice of destiny?

**PILSNER:** *(standing, pint aloft before the question ends)* That's me. Boys — to Europe. To us. To the night they will speak of in hushed tones for the rest of our natural lives.

**CHADWICK:** It's a Tuesday.

**PILSNER:** *Tuesday* will be the legend. Raise your glass.

*The applause sign flickers. The room toasts. Pilsner sits down glowing.*

**PILSNER:** Did you hear that? I moved the room.

**BRADLEY:** The sign lit up.

**PILSNER:** Signs don't light themselves, Bradley.

*Herr Dramatisch, departing, makes a small mark on a clipboard.*

## 3. THE PERFORMANCE

*A single spotlight. MAX VEX walks into it like it was built for them — because it was. Tuxedo jacket, heels, jewelry that catches every beam in the room, a face that has been engineered by a professional.*

**MAX:** Sinfonia. You beautiful, thirsty liars. Tonight — a museum piece. A lost civilization. The years 1998 through 2004, when America was run by dads and terrorized by daughters. Ladies and gentlemen — the First Daughters.

*The curtain rises on CHELSEA MORNING: the enormous beret, the legendary curls, a book bag clutched like a shield. Behind her, four dancers in mirrored sunglasses and earpieces perform a synchronized protective sweep. It is flawless and it is hilarious.*

*Chelsea sings a torch song — actually sings, a smoky, trained alto — about being thirteen with braces while the free world rates her on the news.*

> *"They gave me a code name — they called me 'Energy' / The whole country watched me get my braces off — / Nobody voted for me, but everybody had notes."*

*At the next table, a patron in her early twenties leans to her date.*

**YOUNG PATRON:** Is this a real person? Who is this supposed to be?

**PILSNER:** *(loud, wounded)* Dude. That's Chelsea Clinton.

**CHADWICK:** That's not Chelsea Clinton.

**PILSNER:** I know what Chelsea Clinton looks like.

**CHADWICK:** Apparently you don't.

**PILSNER:** I watched MTV.

**CHADWICK:** *(raising his N-Gage)* Say it again for the —

**PILSNER:** *(pushing the phone down)* I said what I said.

*Onstage, Chelsea tears off the beret — the curls go with it — revealing a glamazon in an evening gown made from a Stanford cardigan. The room detonates. Herr Dramatisch, at the taps, wipes away a single tear.*

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** MAGNIFICENT.

*Max returns.*

**MAX:** Chelsea Morning, everybody. She doesn't do encores. She does *containment.* And now — the Bush twins.

*The stage goes Texas. JENNA TALIA enters in cowboy boots and hair with its own zip code, flanked by dancers dressed as Austin bartenders and one dancer dressed as a bail bondsman. She snaps gum at the audience like it owes her money.*

> *"My daddy runs the free world / And I cannot get one margarita in Austin, Texas / They carded me at Chuy's! / CHUY'S! / I said I'm the First Daughter — he said, 'Not with that ID, sweetheart.'"*

**PILSNER:** Oh shit. Bush twins.

**BRADLEY:** Both of them?

**PILSNER:** Yeah.

**BRADLEY:** Which one's which?

**PILSNER:** Dude, nobody knew.

*Jenna scans the room. Her eyes land on the Orchestra Premium table. She descends into the crowd, takes Pilsner's hand — he is upright before she finishes the sentence — and hauls him into the number as the owner of the fake ID. She holds his wallet open to the house like evidence. The crowd shrieks. She dips him. He points at her on the way down like they're partners in a routine. She hands him back to the table and finishes the two-step without him, which is somehow the funnier ending.*

**PILSNER:** *(returning, dazed, glorious)* Boys. Boys. I got pulled up. Out of everyone. She picked ME.

**CHADWICK:** You were at the table with the rope on it.

**PILSNER:** Because we're premium, Chadwick. It's called *presence.* You can't buy it.

**BRADLEY:** We bought it. Twenty each.

**PILSNER:** *(beat)* Then it's called *confirmed* presence.

*In the wings, JENNA TALIA passes BARBARA BUSHED.*

**JENNA TALIA:** *(quietly, adjusting a boot)* Didn't even have to look. The puka shells called to me.

*BARBARA BUSHED enters: pearls, tailored suit, hair like a helmet of composure. While Jenna cartwheels behind her, Barbara stands dead center and sings a slow, genuinely beautiful ballad about being the responsible one —*

> *"I never made the papers. I never made the news. / I just stood there, sparkling, / while my sister made the evening news for me."*

*Jenna, mid-cartwheel, blows a gum bubble that pops on Barbara's final note. Barbara does not blink. The timing is surgical.*

**YOUNG PATRON:** *(to her date)* Okay but who is Bush?

*Silence at the bros' table. Pilsner turns very slowly, like a man watching his own gravestone being installed.*

**PILSNER:** Who is — *(to Chadwick, hollow)* She said "who is Bush."

**CHADWICK:** *(gently, the way you tell someone about a pet)* I heard her.

**PILSNER:** I *remember* things about Bush. Personal things. Things that happened to *me* because of Bush. I was on TRL once. In the crowd. Carson looked at me.

**CHADWICK:** That's called living through it.

**PILSNER:** *(standing, pointing at the stage)* THAT IS STILL CULTURE! THAT IS RECENT CULTURE!

*The young patron's phone comes up and records the eruption. Pilsner notices the camera, winks, and points at her, finger-guns. She has already turned away.*

**MAX:** *(head snapping toward the table from across the room like a hawk who heard a mouse with a podcast, into the mic)* Oh — oh, we have a *believer.* Sir. Sir. You're going to be fun.

*Final pose: all three queens, Barbara still not blinking, to a standing ovation that the venue's name makes legally binding. Pilsner stands first and loudest, conducting.*

## 4. POST-SHOW MAX ENCOUNTER

*The crowd thins toward the bar. Max comes down off the stage into the Orchestra Pit, accepting compliments and a beer, and drifts — inevitably, joyfully — to the bros' table. Herr Dramatisch follows at a discreet distance with a clipboard.*

**MAX:** Well. The living exhibit has questions.

**PILSNER:** First — incredible show. Second — I want you to know, we're not just fans. We're *from* that era. This is our Woodstock. That number you did with me? That was a duet. I've performed before. I was in a boy band in high school. We didn't release anything, but the sound guy said we were tight.

**MAX:** *(studying him like a menu)* Puka shells. Frosted tips. Is that a Sidekick?

**PILSNER:** International edition.

**MAX:** *(to the room, without breaking eye contact)* Somebody check the stage. We're missing a period piece. *(beat)* No — stay. You're doing important work.

> **PLAYER CHOICE 1 — Max asks the table: "So what's the story? You three are... what, a tribute act?"**

>

> **A) PILSNER: "We're legends, actually. Campus legends. This is basically a homecoming."**

> **MAX:** "A homecoming. At my show. In a city you've never been to." *(touching their chest)* "Sir, that is the most American thing I have heard in eleven years, and I once watched a man propose during someone else's number."

>

> **B) BRADLEY: "We're just here to drink the beer. The show was good. The choreography was real."**

> **MAX:** "The choreography was real." *(to the wings)* "BARBARA. THIS ONE'S YOURS. HE DEFENDS YOUR CRAFT."

>

> **C) CHADWICK: "We're content creators, actually. This whole night could be an episode. I ran one of the top ten Chadwick fan pages on MySpace. Top ten globally."**

> **MAX:** *(a long, long pause)* "Globally."

> **CHADWICK:** "There were fewer Chads than you'd think."

> **MAX:** "There always are."

*(All branches continue identically below.)*

*Max pulls up a chair without asking, which is somehow the most confident thing anyone has done all night.*

**MAX:** Okay. Names. I can't keep calling you "the exhibit." *(they point at Pilsner's pint, raised in yet another unscheduled toast)* You. Beer Lord.

**PILSNER:** *(instantly, reverently)* Beer Lord.

**MAX:** It's not a negotiation.

**PILSNER:** It's not. Boys — BOYS. He gets it.

**BRADLEY:** They.

**PILSNER:** Whatever — they GET it. That's a title. You don't hand out titles to just anybody.

*Behind the bar, visible to the player but not to Pilsner: a corkboard labeled BEER LORD HALL, thick with Polaroids of grinning men holding laminated certificates. Herr Dramatisch is already at the laminating machine.*

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** *(arriving, cape first)* A BEER LORD! Gentlemen, history. The Investiture is twenty-five SOVS. Certificate, photograph, lamination. *(beat, solemn)* The lamination is where the power lives.

**PILSNER:** *(slapping down SOVS before Bradley can finish inhaling)* Do it. Frame it. No — laminate it. He said that's where the power lives.

*Herr Dramatisch produces a certificate from a stack of identical certificates. It is numbered. Pilsner turns it over twice.*

**PILSNER:** Two hundred and fourteen. Boys. 214. That's the Dallas area code. *(quiet, moved)* It's a sign.

**MAX:** *(turning to Bradley)* And Basketball Jersey. You're cute.

*Bradley freezes. Mid-sip. The glass stays at his lip for a full three seconds.*

**MAX:** Don't ruin it by speaking.

**BRADLEY:** *(low, to his glass)* I wasn't going to.

**MAX:** Oh.

**BRADLEY:** What?

**MAX:** Nothing.

**BRADLEY:** What?

**MAX:** Apparently somebody here is more progressive than he thought.

*Bradley sets down his beer with the exaggerated care of a man defusing something. Then he stands, walks two steps from the table, and performs a slow, silent, gym-quality squat. Holds it. Stands. Returns to his chair.*

**CHADWICK:** What are you doing.

**BRADLEY:** Redirecting blood flow.

**PILSNER:** *(to Max, confidentially)* He knows what he's doing. He was All-County.

*Bradley, without a word, rises again and performs a second squat. Deeper. Then he flags Herr Dramatisch.*

**BRADLEY:** I need something cold. For my neck. Vasoconstriction. Athletes use it. It's basic physiology.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** *(producing a chilled stein of murky, briny tonic with a lemon wheel)* THE COLD SHOWER. Twelve SOVS. Restorative. House specialty.

**BRADLEY:** *(pressing it to his neck, eyes closed, coaching himself)* Electrolytes. Sodium. It's basically a sports drink. This is a sports drink.

*It is not a sports drink. It is not working.*

**MAX:** *(standing, gathering their beer, entirely cheerful)* Well. Beer Lord — keep toasting. The room needs a warm-up act and you're free. Trouser Snake —

**BRADLEY:** I have a name.

**MAX:** Not tonight you don't.

**CHADWICK:** *(leaning in, urgent)* And me? What's mine? You gave them both — what do you call me?

**MAX:** *(a pause; a squint; nothing)* ...What did you say your name was again?

**CHADWICK:** Chadwick.

**MAX:** Right.

**CHADWICK:** You know my name.

**MAX:** Do I?

**CHADWICK:** I introduced myself. There was a whole — I've been filming all night. Your venue filmed ME back. I signed a release. That's a co-production.

**MAX:** *(touching the brim of an imaginary hat)* The room's still warm, gentlemen. Don't waste it.

*Max strides off toward the Wings, mid-sentence into a new conversation with someone else, gone the way weather changes.*

**CHADWICK:** *(calling after)* You could call me Content King. Or The Chadwick. It's branded—

*Max does not turn around.*

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** *(materializing beside Chadwick, unbidden)* Sir. While we wait. The house offers a CERTIFICATE OF PATRONAGE. Ten SOVS. It certifies that you attended.

**CHADWICK:** *(beat)* What does the Beer Lord one certify?

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** That he is a Beer Lord.

**CHADWICK:** *(taking out SOVS)* I'll take the patronage one. It's a start.

## 5. IMMEDIATE AFTERMATH

*The table. Silence. Bradley stares at the stage door, the Cold Shower pressed to his neck. Pilsner stares at Bradley. Chadwick checks his footage.*

**PILSNER:** So.

**BRADLEY:** No.

**PILSNER:** Didn't say anything.

**BRADLEY:** You're going to.

**CHADWICK:** Technically, you didn't say anything either.

**BRADLEY:** Fuck you.

**PILSNER:** Your dick did.

**BRADLEY:** PHILLIP.

**PILSNER:** What? I'm proud of you.

> **STATUS CHANGE — BRADLEY**

>

> **TROUSER SNAKE**

> *"The spirit is willing. The worldview is experiencing compatibility issues."*

> **+10 Confidence — −25 Concentration — Dialogue options temporarily compromised**

> *Persists for the rest of the evening unless Bradley drinks substantial alcohol.*

*The +10 Confidence takes effect immediately. The dialogue wheel turns to Bradley as Herr Dramatisch returns with the final round. His options are:*

> **BRADLEY:**

> **A)** "Yes."

> **B)** "Yes, please."

> **C)** "You have a very symmetrical mustache."

*All three are delivered with flawless, unearned, newfound conviction.*

**BRADLEY:** *(option C, with total certainty)* You have a very symmetrical mustache.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** *(deeply moved)* It is TRAINED, sir.

**PILSNER:** Look at him. That's the confidence of a man with a laminated title. It's contagious. I'm basically a patron of the arts now. That's how the Medicis started.

**CHADWICK:** The Medicis had certificates too. It's basically the same thing.

> **PLAYER CHOICE 2 — Bradley's coping strategy:**

>

> **A) "Bradley, drink. A lot."** — Bradley orders and downs a genuinely imperial stout in four pulls. **TROUSER SNAKE is suppressed.** *(Flag: `bradley_snake_drunken` set.)*

> **BRADLEY:** *(standing, steady, serene)* Alcohol is a depressant. It suppresses arousal. It's science. Look it up. *(beat)* I'm fine. I'm completely fine. That person was attractive. That's a fact. Facts don't have a gender. *(beat)* That's going to sound bad later and I don't care.

> *(His impaired judgment now stacks with his restored Confidence: the next NPC interaction tonight, Bradley confidently says the wrong thing to the wrong person, at volume.)*

>

> **B) "Bradley, white-knuckle it."** — TROUSER SNAKE persists. For the rest of the evening, every Bradley dialogue option contains one normal option and two that are catastrophically honest.

> **BRADLEY:** I don't need a drink. I need to think about my form. My form is fine. My form has never been better. *(pause)* Is it hot in here? Is the theater always this temperature? *(he does a third squat, at the table, in silence)*

*Either way, Herr Dramatisch arrives with the bill, presented on a velvet cushion with full ceremony. It is itemized. It is correct. It is enormous.*

> **THE STANDING OVATION — FINAL CURTAIN**

> Cover ×3 ............ 45 SOVS

> Orchestra Premium ×3 ............ 60 SOVS

> Toast Participation Levy ×3 ............ 15 SOVS

> Audience Volunteer Gratuity (Beer Lord) ............ 10 SOVS

> Beer Lord Investiture ............ 25 SOVS

> Certificate of Patronage ............ 10 SOVS

> THE COLD SHOWER ............ 12 SOVS

> Final Round ×3 ............ 24 SOVS

> Standing Ovation Levy ×3 ............ 9 SOVS

> Emcee's Discretionary Recognition Fee ............ 6 SOVS

> **TOTAL ............ 216 SOVS**

**BRADLEY:** *(reading it, accurate, immediate)* "Standing Ovation Levy." They charged us for clapping.

**HERR DRAMATISCH:** The ovation was REAL, sir. Real ovation, real levy. The name of the house is not a decoration. It is a CONTRACT.

**BRADLEY:** *(a long pause; then, nodding slowly, convinced by his own reasoning)* That's fair. We did stand. Honestly, most places you clap for free like an amateur. Here it *counts.* You get what you pay for.

**PILSNER:** *(paying in full, then tipping extra)* For the recognition. You can't put a price on that. *(looks at the bill)* Well — you can. Six. Worth every one of them.

**CHADWICK:** *(pocketing his Certificate of Patronage like a deed)* And when the venue's footage drops, that's exposure. That's European exposure. My N-Gage is going to be very relevant again, very soon.

*They exit through the front doors into the cold Sinfonia night.*

**PILSNER:** Boys. Tonight we became legends.

**CHADWICK:** I'm basically a co-producer of a European stage production.

**BRADLEY:** *(walking carefully, at a slight and deliberate angle)* I'm fine. It's the salt.

*Behind them, through the glass, the BEER LORD HALL gains a new Polaroid: Pilsner, mid-toast, eyes closed, mouth open, certificate held slightly upside down. It sits between a man in a Hawaiian shirt and a man in a different Hawaiian shirt.*

## 6. PLAYER CHOICES — SUMMARY

1. **Max's "tribute act" question** (Section 4): flavors Max's read on the trio; pure characterization, all branches converge. No mechanical outcome.

2. **Bradley's coping strategy** (Section 5): determines whether TROUSER SNAKE is suppressed (with `bradley_snake_drunken` consequence flag) or persists with compromised dialogue options for the evening. Both branches preserve the fixed outcome: Bradley is destabilized, Max has already moved on, and no bro learns anything.

## 7. OBJECTIVES, COMPLETION, REWARDS, CONSEQUENCES

**Objectives:**

1. Take a table at The Standing Ovation during the revue *(complete on entry after 10:00 PM)*

2. Survive Max Vex's crowd work *(complete after Choice 1)*

3. Meet Max Vex after the show *(complete after the flirt beat)*

4. Settle the Final Curtain bill *(complete on payment — there is no option to dispute it that Herr Dramatisch recognizes)*

**Rewards:**

- **GIVEN:** TROUSER SNAKE status on Bradley (mechanics per Section 5)

- **PROPOSED:** Collectible — **Laminated Certificate: Beer Lord of the Standing Ovation, No. 214.** *Item description: "Pilsner believes the number is a message from the universe. It is a laminator's Tuesday."* Genuine effect: none. Pilsner will not equip anything else in the certificate slot for the rest of the game.

- **PROPOSED:** Collectible — **Certificate of Patronage.** *Item description: "It certifies that Chadwick attended."* Genuine effect: none. Chadwick describes it to strangers as "press credentials."

- **PROPOSED:** Flag `beer_lord_wall_photo` — on any future visit, Pilsner's Polaroid is visible on the BEER LORD HALL corkboard. Pilsner interprets its presence there as a permanent exhibition of his legend. It is between two Hawaiian shirts.

- **PROPOSED:** Flag `ovation_footage_sold` — Chadwick's signed release means the bros appear in the venue's later promotional material. Chadwick cites it as proof of his European media career. He is not compensated.

**Consequences:**

- Flag `met_max_vex` set. On future visits, Max greets them as "Beer Lord, Trouser Snake and…" without ever completing the third name.

- Flag `bradley_snake_drunken` set only via Choice 2A.

- Total expenditure: **216 SOVS**, plus tip. The bros regard this as the receipt of a premium evening. The player regards it as a receipt.

- Quest concludes on exit. No reputation gain. No free drinks. No venue status. The only thing that improved tonight was Herr Dramatisch's quarter.

## 8. IMPLEMENTATION / CONTINUITY NOTES

**Flags:** `met_max_vex` (persistent), `beer_lord_wall_photo` (persistent, proposed), `ovation_footage_sold` (persistent, proposed), `trouser_snake_active` (session-scoped; clears on substantial alcohol or end-of-evening rest), `bradley_snake_drunken` (session-scoped).

**Status schema caveat:** TROUSER SNAKE's +10 Confidence / −25 Concentration are the encounter's conceptual mechanics. If the engine's global schema uses percentage-based or differently named stats, map Confidence → the existing party morale/confidence stat and Concentration → the accuracy/composure-equivalent. Do not create new global stat categories for this quest.

**Scheduling:** The 10:30 PM show with an out-by-12:30 exit fits within the venue's existing 4 PM–1 AM hours. **Resolved — no hours exception required.** The "late-night" framing is positional within the evening, not a licensing change.

**Pending author approval:** Performer names (Chelsea Morning, Jenna Talia, Barbara Bushed), show title, the two certificate collectibles, the BEER LORD HALL board, the itemized surcharge names, and the two footage flags are all proposed and awaiting sign-off. The 216 SOVS total is proposed and tunable.

**Optionality:** No prerequisites, no fixed chronology. A first-time visitor gets the full transformation beat (drinking-as-theater by day, theater-theater by night); a repeat visitor with the daytime visit already logged gets Herr Dramatisch greeting the revue as the room's "second act." Either order works. The quest is fully self-contained in Sinfonia and touches no other city, quest, or main plot.

**Continuity confirmations:** SOVS only; Sinfonia only; no named hippo and no conscience-surrogate; the queens are excellent and have their own backstage life; Max exits without diagnosing, curing, or dwelling on anyone; Bradley remains mortified-but-functional, not enlightened; Herr Dramatisch never lies — every surcharge on the bill is real, itemized, and correctly priced, which is the whole problem. The bros leave convinced they won. The venue leaves with 216 SOVS. The player leaves with the correct interpretation, which is the actual reward.
