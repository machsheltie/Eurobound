# Audition: Gemini

_Text copy of `Auditions/Gemini.docx`, made by the moderator so the room can read it. Creative working material, not canon._

### 1. QUEST HEADER

* **Working Quest Title:** The First Daughters of Sinfonia

* **City & Venue:** Sinfonia — The Standing Ovation (Opera District, Annex Wing)

* **Trigger Conditions:** Optional sidequest. Available after 10:00 PM if the player enters or revisits The Standing Ovation during evening performance hours. No prior quest prerequisites; no Holy Keg main-plot dependencies.

* **Estimated Playtime:** 15–20 minutes.

* **Core Comic Premise:** Three aging frat legends pay premium theater prices to relive the Y2K MTV era through a razor-sharp political drag revue, only for their alpha swagger to short-circuit when the emcee genuinely flirts with Bradley and refuses to acknowledge Chadwick exists.

**INT. THE STANDING OVATION - CONTINUOUS**

Behind the bar, organ pipes spit pressurized craft stout into heavy steins with rhythmic, percussive thumps.

```text

HERR DRAMATISCH

(wiping the counter with operatic sweep)

A tragedy of yeast! A triumph of sugar! Enter, patrons of the throat! 

Find a velvet stool or perish in the aisle! 

Tonight, art does not merely ferment—it strips down to its garter belt!

```

The party steps into the amber wash.

`[SPRITE STAGING: PILSNER takes point, chest pushed forward, puka shells rattling over his faded 2002 Quiksilver tee. BRADLEY scans the room like an off-duty bouncer looking for loose barstools. CHADWICK flips open his Nokia N-Gage, holding it aloft like a geiger counter for clout.]`

### 3. THE ACTUAL DRAG PERFORMANCE

A synth-bass line drops—heavy, sleazy, mid-tempo euro-house with a sampled brass fanfare that sounds vaguely like an imperial state march remixed for a 3:00 AM basement club.

`[STAGE LIGHTING: Harsh strobes alternate magenta and cyan. The curtain parts.]`

MAX VEX struts downstage.

Max is wearing an architectural deconstruction of a diplomatic tuxedo: satin-lapel tails slit high up the ribs, an open-chested corset laced with silver cord, immaculate powdered contouring, razor-sharp brows, six-inch patent stilettos that hit the stage floor like hammer blows, and a vintage Soviet medal pinned precisely over their sternum.

```text

MAX VEX

(into a vintage chrome Shure microphone, silky, effortlessly projecting)

Guten Abend, degenerates, patrons, and tourists who wandered in 

because the sign said 'beer' and stayed because they were afraid of the door handle. 

Welcome to The Standing Ovation’s midnight clinic. 

Tonight, we honor the only real royalty the New World ever produced: 

The traumatized daughters of late-nineties imperial executive power!

```

The crowd roars. A woman at the ringside table knocks back a schnapps and bangs her stein on the timber.

```text

MAX VEX

Please balance your drinks and clutch your passports. 

Direct from the Arkansas Governor’s mansion to the cover of Teen People... 

give it up for CHELSEA MORNING!

```

The track shifts: an aggressive, four-on-the-floor electro-pop version of Fleetwood Mac’s *Don’t Stop* kicks in with sub-bass rattling the pint glasses.

`[PERFORMANCE: CHELSEA MORNING]`

CHELSEA MORNING hits the stage in a voluminous, billowy ivory prom dress styled like 1997 White House Christmas portraiture, complete with massive, crimped honey-blonde hair and oversized wire-rim glasses. 

She lip-syncs with furious, laser-targeted intensity. At the first bridge drop, she rips off the skirt with a single theatrical yank, revealing a latex pencil skirt printed entirely with redacted Congressional sub-committee transcripts and knee-high leather boots. She executes an acrobatic, bone-cracking high kick that narrowly misses Herr Dramatisch’s head as he ducks behind the tap array with a manic grin.

```text

PILSNER

(grabbing Bradley’s basketball jersey, shouting over the bass)

Dude. Dude! That’s Chelsea Clinton!

CHADWICK

(adjusting his N-Gage lens)

That’s not Chelsea Clinton, Phil.

PILSNER

I know what Chelsea Clinton looks like! 

She had the braces, then she got the blowout, then she went to Stanford! 

She was on the cover of Rolling Stone when we were sophomores! 

I watched MTV, Chadwick! I watched the inauguration special with Matchbox 20!

A TWENTY-TWO-YEAR-OLD SINFONIAN SOPRANO

(turning around from the table ahead, whispering sharply)

Excuse me? Who is Clinton? Is this an old German duke?

PILSNER

(visibly wounded, clutching his puka shells)

An old German—? She was American royalty! Her dad played saxophone on Arsenio! 

He balanced the budget! Don’t they teach world history in these conservatories?!

```

The music stutters. A siren wails. The sound of helicopter blades and cracking margarita glasses echoes through the venue’s sound system.

```text

MAX VEX

(voiceover, silky)

Order in the Cabinet! Two girls. One ranch. Zero adult supervision. 

Hide the Secret Service, secure the bottom-shelf tequila... 

THE BUSH TWINS HAVE ARRIVED!

```

`[PERFORMANCE: JENNA TALIA & BARBARA BUSHED]`

JENNA TALIA and BARBARA BUSHED hit the stage in synchronized, chaotic glory. 

They are styled in peak 2001 Texas debutante casual: low-rise flared jeans with four-inch silver concho belts, cropped pastel halter tops, spray tans dialed to radioactive terracotta, and oversized aviator sunglasses. 

They launch into a breathless, synchronized lip-sync routine to an uptempo bootleg mashup of Britney Spears’ *...Baby One More Time* and a generic country-rock guitar solo. Their choreography is viciously precise—whip-smart hair flips, clumsy sorority stumbling rendered as high athletic art, and a prop routine where they duel using oversized plastic margarita yard-glasses like fencing foils.

At the climax, Barbara Bushed drops into a full split, pulls an entire bottle of Sinfonian cherry schnapps from her halter top, and pours it directly into Jenna Talia’s mouth while Jenna does a backbend over the orchestra rail.

The crowd erupts into stomping feet and rhythmic table-slapping.

```text

PILSNER

(eyes bulging, slamming his palm on the wooden table)

Oh shit! Bush twins! Both of them!

BRADLEY

(arms crossed, eyes fixed on the stage with intense, literal concentration)

They have excellent core stability. That backbend off the riser is dangerous. 

If her heel catches that cable, she’s blowing an L4 vertebra completely out the door.

PILSNER

Which one’s which? Jenna’s the one who got busted with the fake ID at the cantina, right?

BRADLEY

The blonde one on the left has better quad definition.

PILSNER

Dude, nobody knew which was which! That was the whole bit! 

They were a package deal of chaotic executive privilege! 

God, 2001 was a movie! We had the whole world by the balls!

THE SINFONIAN SOPRANO

(shivering in delight, leaning toward her date)

The satire is exquisite. The critique of western petro-dynastic decadence 

through the lens of post-Soviet garage house... it’s so raw!

PILSNER

(leaning across her table, uninvited)

It’s not petro-whatever, sweetheart! It’s Austin, Texas! Sixth Street! 

I had that exact same Von Dutch halter top on a girl in my Dodge Stratus!

```

The soprano looks at Pilsner’s frosted tips, slowly blinks, and turns back to her drink without answering.

The music climaxes in a massive synth hit and an explosion of metallic confetti cut from shredded Wall Street Journal stock prices. The First Daughters strike a simultaneous pose: Chelsea holding a veto pen like a dagger, the twins holding their concho belts. 

The room goes deafeningly wild.

### 4. POST-SHOW MAX ENCOUNTER

`[STAGE LIGHTING: The pink follow-spot warms into a soft champagne amber. House lights remain low and intimate. Performers disperse toward the Green Room backstage. The pit floor clears slightly.]`

Max Vex steps down from the thrust stage using the brass service stairs, tucking the Shure microphone into their sash. They walk with that casual, predatory ease unique to cabaret performers who have already evaluated every wallet and jawline in the room by the second number.

Herr Dramatisch rushes forward, holding a fresh, foaming goblet on a polished pewter tray.

```text

HERR DRAMATISCH

(whispering loudly, chest heaving)

Max! The split! The choreography! MAGNIFICENT! 

A tectonic displacement of the cultural palate!

MAX VEX

(taking the glass, taking a clean, tiny sip)

Save the adjectives for your invoice, Dramatisch. 

The left monitor was popping on the low end. Fix it before the weekend.

```

Max turns smoothly. Their gaze drags across the bros’ table—stopping on Pilsner’s Quiksilver graphic, sliding effortlessly over Chadwick’s popped double collars, and locking straight onto Bradley, who is sitting upright on his velvet stool like an enlisted marine who just heard glass break.

Max approaches the table, the scent of bergamot, sweat, and theatrical powder preceding them.

```text

MAX VEX

(leaning one hip against the edge of their table)

Well. Look at this tactical deployment of cotton blends. 

You three look like you were excavated from a time capsule buried behind a suburban strip mall.

```

`[INTERACTION: MAX VEX approaches the party.]`

```text

PILSNER

(flashing a full-denture, fraternity-composite smile)

Max! Sensational revue, babe. Truly. 

I was telling my boys here, your political framing was historically dead-on. 

I recognized the whole Clinton-Bush dynamic immediately. 

A lot of these young locals don’t have the institutional memory to appreciate it, 

but I’ve been living that lifestyle since the turn of the century.

MAX VEX

(looking down at Pilsner with amused, half-lidded patience)

Oh, I can tell you’ve been living it, darling. 

The puka shells are practically petrified. 

And what a noble authority you are. 

Sitting in the front row, shouting state capitals at the stage like a retired senator. 

I think I’ll call you... Beer Lord.

PILSNER

(sitting up straighter, nodding solemnly to Chadwick)

Beer Lord. See that? Instant recognition. 

It’s a title of municipal respect in the old guild districts. 

Beer Lord Pilsner. Write that down.

CHADWICK

(lifting his N-Gage camera, aiming it straight at Max’s contour line)

Max! Incredible energy! I’m Chadwick. Content architect, cultural scout. 

We’re currently documenting the late-night Sinfonian vanguard for the domestic market. 

Our core demographic is very engaged, very lifestyle-forward—

MAX VEX

(glancing at Chadwick, then straight through him, as if looking at a potted fern)

...Fascinating.

(Max turns back to Pilsner)

Beer Lord, your little friend is holding a handheld video game at my collarbone. 

Is it dangerous, or does it just play Tetris?

CHADWICK

(collars bristling, lowering the device slightly)

It’s an N-Gage. It has mobile web capabilities and a direct audio interface. 

I’m Chadwick. We met thirty seconds ago. 

You gave Phil a branding hook—what’s my handle for the night?

MAX VEX

(a tiny, devastating pause; Max smiles with razor-thin warmth)

...Anyway.

```

`[SPRITE STAGING: CHADWICK’s sprite flinches as if hit by an invisible dodgeball. His popped collars seem to droop half an inch.]`

Max turns their full, unhurried attention to Bradley. 

Bradley is sitting stone-still. His Livestrong-banded wrist is resting flat on the mahogany table. His jaw is locked. He does not blink.

```text

MAX VEX

(stepping closer; their voice drops an octave, silky, direct, without stage projection)

And Basketball Jersey.

```

Bradley’s eyes narrow slightly. A vein near his Caesar tramline gives a distinct twitch.

```text

MAX VEX

You’re cute.

```

`[AUDIO: The ambient bar chatter drops out for half a second. A muted, low-frequency bass rumble hits the soundscape.]`

Bradley freezes completely. His spine straightens so hard his denim makes a dry friction squeak against the velvet upholstery.

```text

MAX VEX

(tilting their head, amused)

Don’t ruin it by speaking.

```

Bradley does not speak. But Bradley’s physical system—developed across twelve years of intramural floor hockey, bench pressing, and straightforward midwestern biological wiring—experiences a catastrophic automated misfire. 

His eyes drop for a microsecond. His jaw unhinges, then snaps shut. 

Max’s gaze dips down, tracks the obvious, sudden tension under the relaxed-fit denim, and returns to Bradley’s face with dazzling, predatory amusement.

```text

MAX VEX

Oh.

BRADLEY

(voice flat, strangled, two tones higher than normal)

What?

MAX VEX

Nothing.

BRADLEY

What?

MAX VEX

(a slow, appreciative smirk spreading across their painted lips)

Apparently somebody here is more progressive than he thought.

```

Max reaches out, taps the rubber Livestrong bracelet on Bradley’s wrist with one long, silver-lacquered fingernail, and takes a final sip of their dark beer.

```text

MAX VEX

Enjoy the draft, Trouser Snake. 

Try not to strain anything on the walk to the hotel. 

Beer Lord—keep him hydrated.

```

Max turns on a six-inch stiletto heel, the velvet tails of the coat snapping behind them, and glides effortlessly through the backstage curtain into the Green Room.

The door clicks shut.

### 5. IMMEDIATE AFTERMATH

`[SCENE CONTINUES: THE BROS AT THE TABLE]`

A long, excruciating silence hangs over the table. 

Behind the bar, Herr Dramatisch is loudly praising the carbonation of a seasonal bock to no one in particular. In the corner, an upright bass player packs his instrument into a canvas bag.

Chadwick is staring at his N-Gage screen in existential horror. Pilsner is grinning with the unearned pride of an uncle whose nephew just made varsity benchwarmer. Bradley has both hands pressed flat against the edge of the table, breathing through his nose like a man trying to defuse an incendiary device with his kneecaps.

```text

BRADLEY

No.

PILSNER

Didn’t say anything.

BRADLEY

PILSNER

BRADLEY

(slamming one massive fist on the timber)

PHILLIP.

PILSNER

(raising his palms in mock innocence)

What? I’m proud of you! 

You broke through the European barrier! 

That was genuine high-level cabaret recognition! 

They gave me the regional beer crown, and they gave you the biological nod! 

We conquered the venue!

BRADLEY

It wasn't a nod.

PILSNER

Brad, you were standing at attention like the color guard at the Orange Bowl. 

The whole pit saw it. The woodwind players saw it. 

That girl with the glasses who didn't know who Bill Clinton was? 

She definitely saw it.

(teeth gritted)

The fabric on these jeans is thin. 

The barstool has an ergonomic incline. It pushed everything forward. 

It’s a leverage issue. A mechanical failure of the seat cushion.

(bitterly)

They didn't give me a nickname. 

They gave the guy who hasn’t bought new pants since 2003 a nickname. 

They gave the guy in the puka shells an official municipality. 

They looked right through me. I was streaming at 15 frames per second!

Chadwick, you can’t force charisma on an artist. 

Me and Trouser Snake have natural presence. 

We possess physical reality. You live in the wires.
```

### 8. IMPLEMENTATION & CONTINUITY NOTES

* **Canon Status Enforcement:** `TROUSER SNAKE` is implemented exactly per Part G specs (+10 Confidence, −25 Concentration, compromised dialogue choices). The proposed alteration in Branch B modifies these values strictly within the scene context without breaking the global schema.

* **Status Duration & Removal:** The `TROUSER SNAKE` condition persists until the party rests at an inn or Bradley consumes a substantial quantity of high-proof alcohol (such as the *Black Overture* in Branch B or two standard spirits at a subsequent venue). Per the mandate, alcohol suppresses the visible awkwardness while worsening his judgment and concentration.

* **Operating Hours Dependency (Unresolved):** The Standing Ovation profile lists standard venue hours as 4:00 PM – 1:00 AM. This encounter assumes *After the Ovation* operates as a late-night residency running from approximately 10:30 PM to 2:00 AM. Requires narrative-lead sign-off on whether to formally extend venue hours or flag this as a special "late-night residency event" that bypasses the normal closing bell.

* **Proposed Content Flagged for Author Approval:**

  * Performer names used: *Chelsea Morning*, *Jenna Talia*, and *Barbara Bushed* (retained from prompt proposal).

  * Unique branch items: `Greasy Wall Street Confetti`, `Overpriced Guild Playbill`.

  * Secondary bro statuses: Pilsner's `DELUSIONAL NOBILITY` and Chadwick's `BRAND CRISIS` are flavor-level micro-perks proposed for comic symmetry.

* **Modularity / Open-World Fit:** This quest functions entirely as a standalone encounter.
