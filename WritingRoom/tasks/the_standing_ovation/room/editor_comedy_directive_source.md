## From the reply body

1. Tony420's Xanax Flintstone Vitamin

Sketchy ICQ dealer · obsolete internet culture · terrible judgment

A dubious cartoon-shaped pill sold through an ancient ICQ contact, complete with an unsolicited pickup message and wildly implausible marketing claims.

The joke is that Bradley trusts an anonymous dealer because the username ends in 420 and the product supposedly resembles a children's vitamin.

Design distinction: This should be a risky, questionable item with potentially serious consequences, not an actual guaranteed cure. Benzodiazepines such as Xanax mixed with alcohol can cause dangerous sedation and respiratory depression, and an unknown pill may contain anything. The game can satirize Bradley's disastrous decision without presenting the combination as safe or medically reliable.

2. Sleep with one of the First Daughters performers

Adult encounter · beer-goggled celebrity delusion · morning-after chaos

Bradley actually gets somewhere romantically, which is an even bigger problem for his self-image.

He can wake up pleased with himself, convinced he hooked up with political royalty, while his friends insist they've participated in an international scandal.

This option resolves his immediate sexual frustration, not his attraction or personality. The performer must be an enthusiastic, consenting participant, and both characters must be capable of consenting.

I would avoid depicting the pill as a magical remedy that reliably cancels arousal. It's much funnier if Tony420 has an absurdly confident sales pitch and the item is clearly not trustworthy. And given the planned drinking, I'd keep the pill away from the drunk branch entirely rather than casually combining the two.

The hook-up route, meanwhile, has enormous comic potential precisely because it represents a genuine success that the bros interpret in the stupidest possible way.

They don't need to discover a moral lesson. They can have a terrific, consensual night, then wake up boasting about having slept with Chelsea Clinton and the Bush twins.
