## Satirical Design
### Theme & Vibe
**Core Concept**: The Standing Ovation satirizes performance culture — the way everything becomes a show, even drinking. The brewery takes the theatrical seriously while serving beer, creating absurd juxtaposition between high art pretension and pub reality.

The deeper joke: The beer is actually excellent. The drama is completely unnecessary. But everyone commits to it anyway because performative appreciation has become indistinguishable from genuine enjoyment.

### The Bravo Challenge
To earn your stamp, you must perform:
1. Stand when your drink is delivered
2. Deliver a toast speech (judged by crowd)
3. Drink with appropriate gravitas
4. Receive (or not receive) applause
5. Bow regardless of outcome

The crowd is supportive but has STANDARDS.

### Cultural Targets
- **Primary Satirical Focus**: Performance culture, theatrical pretension in everyday life
- **Secondary Targets**: Craft beer ceremony, applause addiction, critic culture
- **Cultural Specificity**: Viennese opera traditions applied to drinking
- **Humor Approach**: Complete commitment to unnecessary drama

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Brewery Tour Integration

**Stamp Unlock Challenge: "The Bravo Challenge"**

**Challenge Type**: Toast Performance (Social Mini-Game)

**Mechanics**:
1. Order any signature beer
2. When delivered, spotlight finds you
3. Deliver toast speech (dialogue choices)
4. Crowd judges (Charm + Speech quality)
5. Drink ceremonially
6. Results announced

**Toast Speech Options**:
| Choice | Difficulty | Crowd Reaction |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| Humble Thanks | Easy | Polite applause, stamp earned |
| Dramatic Monologue | Medium | Enthusiastic response if delivered well |
| Operatic Attempt | Hard | Standing ovation OR complete silence |
| Wing It | Variable | Depends entirely on Charm stat |

**Success**: Stamp + signature beer unlocked + "Encore!" buff (+5 Bravado, 1 hour)
**Failure**: "Booed Off" status, must buy round for nearest table to retry
