## 🎭 Core Concept

### What He's Actually Doing
A greasy cyber-dealer running a competent scam-marketing operation from rented terminals and ICQ. He sells absolutely anything and takes actual ICQ purchase payment in cryptocurrency. He knows exactly what his products and coins are worth. He is not confused; he is selling.

### What the Bros Believe
Pilsner reads an unsolicited pop-up as a personal supplier relationship. Chadwick believes paying in MoonCum696969 makes him an early adopter and the transaction content. Bradley wants the purple one. None of them considers closing the window.

### The Comedy Gap
Tony420 broadcasts the same pitch to every suitable wallet; the bros experience concierge service. The product does exactly what he said, which is the one thing about him they never find remarkable.

## 🧠 Personality

**Archetype: The 3 AM Concierge.** A salesman with infinite inventory and zero shame who treats every unsolicited message as customer service and every customer as a subscription.

- All lowercase, no punctuation in message bodies; the voice is the brand
- Competent urgency, scarcity and insider copy; never winking
- Airdrops are free samples that obligate
- Sells anything; does not explain products or his business model
- Unbothered by law enforcement: "its a feature. not a bug."

**Motivation (PROPOSED):** moving product and coin, preferably in the same message. He likes the work and has noticed that the opera crowd buys.
