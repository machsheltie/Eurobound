# NPC Profile: CHELSEA MORNING

*"A lady does not stumble off the podium; she simply permits gravity to acknowledge her presence."*

## 📋 NPC Identity
- **NPC ID**: `sinfonia_npc_chelsea_morning_01`
- **Display Name**: Chelsea Morning
- **Internal Name**: Chelsea Morning (The Clinton Parody)
- **Role**: Drag Performer / Headline Act
- **Associated Quest**: `sinfonia_sidequest_standing_ovation_01`
- **Primary Location**: The Standing Ovation (Stage & Green Room), Sinfonia
- **Secondary Appearances**: `sinfonia_firstdaughters_apartment_01` (Overnight Afterparty)

---

## 🎭 Core Concept
### What She's Actually Doing
Executing an impossibly tight, mathematically flawless theatrical drag routine satirizing turn-of-the-millennium political dynasty children, balancing razor-sharp lip-sync precision with aggressive technical tap dancing.
### What Bros Believe
Pilsner believes she is an open tribute to his personal college years, specifically staged to validate his insider knowledge of cable news scandals and late-90s executive branch politics.
### The Comedy Gap
Chelsea treats the era as a ridiculous museum of dynastic excess and media hypocrisy, while Pilsner treats it as his personal glory days.

---

## 👤 Appearance
### Physical Description
- **Age**: Late 20s (in performance persona: perpetually 19 on a televised inauguration stage)
- **Build**: Tall, sinewy, incredible core posture; athletic dancer musculature hidden beneath structured tailoring.
- **Height**: 6'1" (6'5" in stage heels)
- **Energy**: Terrifyingly composed, icy patrician charm with a manic, hyper-caffeinated undercurrent.
### Clothing
- **Main Garment**: A severe, pastel-lilac wool skirt-suit with exaggerated 1998 shoulder pads that could deflect shrapnel.
- **Accessories**: Triple-strand faux-pearl choker, thick gold-rimmed collegiate spectacles, and a massive historical brass pager pinned to her lapel.
- **Footwear**: Four-inch chrome tap heels that hit the stage floor like small-arms fire.

---

## 🧠 Personality
- **Archetype**: **The Weaponized Debutante** — Disciplined, relentlessly professional, exhausted by amateur interruptions, with an aristocratic condescension that leaves targets thanking her for the insult.
- **Internal Monologue**: *"If this audio technician clips my monitor pack one more time, I will introduce his collarbone to the laws of momentum."*
- **Motivation**: Precision of craft and completing the third number without a beer tourist spilling dunkel on her vintage wool.

---

## 💬 Dialogue

> "Don't touch the silk, darling. It took three steamings to get the smell of your continent out of it."
### Key Interaction (Pilsner's Political Rant)
**Pilsner**: "The Rose Law Firm! I knew it! You're doing the Whitewater billing records!"
**Chelsea Morning** (*deadpan, adjusting a pearl*):
> "How thrilling for you to have survived into another century. Step back before you exhale stale draft beer onto my lapels."
