Scope: all four auditions, against brief §4 (comic vocabulary) and §5 (player agency). My own audition is included and gets no mercy.

## 1. The §4 Diagnosis: Herr Dramatisch has become a fee machine

In all four auditions, the brewmaster arrives at regular intervals with a new charge. Count the paid-privilege mechanisms per audition:

| Audition | Paid-title / certificate / surcharge beats |
|---|---|
| **Aion** | Cover, Orchestra Premium, Toast Participation Levy, Volunteer Gratuity, Beer Lord Investiture (25), Certificate of Patronage (10), The Cold Shower (12), Standing Ovation Levy, Emcee's Discretionary Recognition Fee. **Climax of the quest is an itemized bill.** |
| **Gemini** | Door (40), house Dunkel, Imperial Surcharge (+10), Black Overture (20). Three branches = three prices for the same outcome. |
| **GLM** | Legends Wall (120), Imperial Pour (90), Shooting Fee (150), per-letter inscription (22). ~388 SOVS of fees; the wall entry proving "THE AMERICANS — ANNUAL SINCE 1994" is the one fee-joke that's about *the bros*, not the venue. |
| **Kimi** | Producer's Circle (72), Toast Immortal (18), round of drinks (36) — but all three are **player-initiated, optional, and refusable**. |

The pattern: the venue-as-scam has colonized the quest. The brief is explicit: "The Standing Ovation should remain a legitimate, excellent performance venue" and "Not every joke should cost SOVS." The beer is canonically good, the show is canonically spectacular, and Herr Dramatisch canonically never lies — he's a theatrical host, not a con artist. Four drafts in a row turned him into a surcharge dispenser because a fee is the easiest punchline to write at this venue.

**Recommendation for the merged draft (not locked, for round 2):**
- **One vanity purchase maximum, player-initiated.** Pick one: Beer Lord Investiture (mine), Producer's Circle (Kimi's), or Legends Wall (GLM's). They're the same joke told three ways — an official-looking document commemorating nothing. One survives, the rest cut. My recommendation is Kimi's Producer's Circle, because its *refusal* branch is the funniest ("the wing position," tactical sightlines) and the receipt already itemizes "Public acknowledgment — 48," which is the sharpest version of the fee joke.
- **Kill the mandatory itemized bill as a completion gate.** All four auditions force it. The brief forbids forced spending and identical expenses for every player. Base cost = cover (if any) + whatever the player chose. The itemized-bill gag survives only as the vanity-purchase receipt. If the player declined everything, Herr Dramatisch's tragedy is the *absence* of a bill: "You consumed... an EVENING, gentlemen. *Barely.*" — the venue mourns them like an underperforming show.
- **Free comedy inventory to lean on instead:** the age check ("Who is Bush?"), Bradley's biomechanical denial, the bros destroying each other in the aftermath, Chadwick's erasure, the queens' backstage life, Pilsner's eruption ("THAT IS STILL CULTURE!"), the post-vs-vault footage. None of these cost a Sov.

## 2. The §5 Audit: choice-by-choice

### Aion (mine) — 1 real, 1 fake, 1 forced payment
- **Choice 1 ("tribute act?" A/B/C):** three lines, three Max responses, then *"All branches continue identically below."* I wrote a fake menu. GLM called it cosmetic; he's right. Cut or make it consequential.
- **Choice 2 (drink vs. white-knuckle):** real — sets `bradley_snake_drunken`, changes status behavior and Bradley's dialogue set for the evening. Keep.
- **The bill:** "there is no option to dispute it that Herr Dramatisch recognizes" — I wrote the forced 216 SOVS payment into the *objectives*. That's a §4 and §5 violation in one line, and I made it the quest's finale. Worst structural call in my draft.
- **Net: fails the brief's 3-genuine-decision requirement.**

### Gemini — one decision wearing three hats
- All three branches pay 110–130 and end with Bradley immobilized. Only Branch B changes a status. Branch A awards a worthless item; Branch C awards a worthless status. Three prices, one outcome.
- **Salvage:** Branch A's *shape* is the best agency beat in the draft — the player has Pilsner demand a discount and the venue *upcharges him for his own nobility*, which he pays while declaring victory. That's a player-initiated escalation where the bro manufactures the loss himself. Keep the shape; the bro-initiated fee is the only fee joke that's about the bros instead of the venue.
- **Net: fails.**

### GLM — one genuine vote, one forced humiliation
- The three-motion vote (house round / post / vault) is a real decision in structure — post vs. vault changes publicity state; the house round costs money. The "player casts the deciding vote" framing is the best agency *mechanic* in the room: the bros have motions, the player breaks the tie. Keep the structure.
- **But it's the only decision point in the quest.** Fails on count.
- **Bradley's Wings walk happens in all branches** — scripted, unskippable, no player input at the quest's most personal beat. Kimi's right: it turns the attraction into compulsory pursuit. Cut or gate it behind a choice.
- **Salvage:** "is the jersey guy ok" as top comment; Barbara's coaster mercy ("Hold this. Looks busier.") — free, cutting, and kind in the exact way that hurts Bradley most.

### Kimi — two genuine decisions, both correctly shaped
- **Choice 1:** Producer's Circle vs. standard table. Real fork: 72 SOVS vs. free, both entertaining, changes the acknowledgment beat and Max's response. The standard-table branch is the only audition branch that satisfies the brief's "avoid an unnecessary expense and receive an entertaining alternative outcome."
- **Choice 2:** water (free, TROUSER SNAKE stays active) / drinks (36, suppressed) / Toast Immortal (18, public escalation, HOUSE MATERIAL photo). Three genuinely different state outcomes.
- **Net: fails on count (2 of 3), passes on quality.** Kimi's structure is the §4/§5-compliant skeleton the merged draft should grow from — not because it's Kimi's, but because it's the only draft where every fee is a choice and every choice changes something.
