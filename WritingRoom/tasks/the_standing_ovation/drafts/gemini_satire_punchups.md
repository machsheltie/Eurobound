## 1. REVUE STAGECRAFT & MUSICAL NUMBERS
*The First Daughters* is a dazzling, hyper-competent European political drag revue. The performers are virtuosos. The humor comes from their terrifying technical discipline juxtaposed against fossilized American political dynasty scandals—and the bros' complete inability to view it as anything other than a college mixer tribute.

### Number 1: "Subpoena in D Minor" (Chelsea Morning Solo)
*Staging: Severe blue spotlight. Chelsea stands rigid at a mahogany lectern in a razor-shouldered lilac wool suit with shrapnel-grade shoulder pads. A chrome tap shoe keeps metronomic, machine-gun time against the hollow stage floor.*

**CHELSEA MORNING** *(Sings with icy, crystal-clear operatic vibrato)*:
> "Daddy's in the basement burning legal pads,  
> Mother's on the telephone castrating bad lads!  
> The grand jury’s waiting on the second floor,  
> But a debutante never leaves an unlocked door!  
> 
> *[Machine-gun tap breakdown: rat-a-tat-tat-tat-CLACK]*  
> 
> Did the shredder jam? Did the server crash?  
> Did an intern trip over forty grand in cash?  
> Don’t ask for the records from the Little Rock branch,  
> You can find the title deeds at the Whitewater ranch!  
> 
> Stand up straight! Fasten your pearls!  
> God made the Senate, but He fears First Girls!"  

*[Chelsea snaps her collegiate spectacles down her nose, locks dead eyes with Pilsner in the front row, and drops into a razor-sharp freeze on a high B-flat. The audience erupts.]*

**PILSNER** *(Slamming his fist on the table, hoarse)*:
> "The billing records! Castle Grande! She's singing to *me*!"

## 2. BIOMECHANICAL RESET SLAPSTICK POLISH (BRANCH D3C)
*Bradley rationalizes his involuntary physical arousal through terrible gym science. In strict accordance with Ruling #4 (gemini_satire_punchups) and §17: One ridiculous claim, then the physical disaster, then silence or a three-word rationalization maximum. No secondary medical lectures.*

### Option 1: The Table-Press ("Core Decompression")
*Action: Bradley grips the underside of the heavy four-inch oak brewery table with his palms and attempts an isometric leg-press against the floorboards.*

**BRADLEY** *(Veins bulging on his temple, teeth clenched)*:
> "The blood has to choose! It's hydraulics!"

*[Bradley drives upward with his legs. Aged brewery timber groans violently under 230 pounds of leveraged dad-bod muscle. The bolted floor brackets scream and rip out of the masonry. Three stein glasses slide off and shatter. Bradley collapses onto the floor, wheezing.]*

**BRADLEY**:
> "Lactic acid spike."

### Option 3: The Playbill Truss & Coaster Intervention ("Structural Camouflage")
*Action: Bradley takes the heavy, 300gsm laminated revue playbill, creases it down the center, and shoves it vertically into his waistband.*

**BRADLEY**:
> "Cardstock has tensile strength on the bias."

*[CRRRR-UNCH. A horrific sound of stiff cardboard buckling under outward force. Chelsea Morning’s laminated face bows outward like an icebreaker prow.]*

**PILSNER**:
> "You just snapped Chelsea Clinton's nose in half."

*[From the wings, BARBARA BUSHED glides over with her chilled martini. She looks at Bradley's contorted torso, looks down at the creaking playbill, and slides a thick, four-ply cork beer coaster across the polished wood into his white-knuckling hand.]*

**BARBARA BUSHED**:
> "Hold this. Looks busier."

*[She takes a microscopic sip and turns away.]*

**BRADLEY** *(Clamping the cork to his sternum)*:
> "Yes, ma'am."

## 4. ELECTION NIGHT 2000 TRIVIA BEAT POLISH (BRANCH D2C - OPTIONAL ALTERNATIVE)

**BRADLEY** *(Whispering)*:

**MAX VEX** *(Stares at Pilsner for three seconds, head tilted)*:
> "That was the correct amount of useless. You may keep your seat, vintage creature."

*[Polite, amused applause ripples through the Sinfonian patrons. Pilsner sits down, adjusting his frosted tips with divine vindication.]*

> "Told you, boys. You keep your head in the game, the room opens up."
