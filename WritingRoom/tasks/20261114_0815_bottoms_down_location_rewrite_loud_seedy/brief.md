## What to change
Everything descriptive: exterior, signage, door, interior, bar, back room, sawdust, patrons, atmosphere, audio, satirical design, the bros' reactions, and the PNG doc's palette, scene layouts and props to match. Make it:
- **Loud.** Signage that screams, not whispers. A storefront the art can't render as quaint. Think tourist-trap aggression, handwritten warnings, a scoreboard of who fell, the sawdust as a feature they brag about.
- **Seedy and specific.** Stains with a history. The bucket. The chair "for the truly fallen" earns its own gross detail. The patrons are a freak show, not a postcard.
- **Old, never charming.** Age reads as grime, damage and stubbornness, not storybook. Ban the painter-bait words: "charming", "cozy", "quaint", "storybook", "fairy-tale", "faded", "barely visible", "no signage", "history" as a selling point.
- **Funny per line.** Names: American parody English, never Dutch. **No new Dutch names, no Dutch words in player-facing text.** No real place or nationality names in player-facing text: cut "Dutch"/"Belgian" from patron lines and bro lines. Internal planning notes may mention real-world inspirations.
