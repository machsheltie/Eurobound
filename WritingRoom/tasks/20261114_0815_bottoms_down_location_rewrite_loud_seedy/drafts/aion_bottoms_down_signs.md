# Slice D — Bottoms Down exterior signage (3 options each)

Matched to Kimi's Slice A exterior spec: backlit plastic sign above the door, green-age brass plaque beside the door (half-covered by the banner), one laminated handwritten warning in the window. All player-facing, all English, no Dutch, no beautifier words. Each set ends with my pick.

---

## 1. Main sign over the door

Backlit plastic, letters a foot high, half the tubes flickering. The sign is the thumbnail read — name big, one loud sub-line under it that a painter can stencil on the fascia.

**Option A**
BOTTOMS DOWN
AS SEEN ON THE FLOOR

**Option B**
BOTTOMS DOWN
THE DRINK WINS. PAY FIRST.

**Option C**
BOTTOMS DOWN
FLOOR SINCE 1624. DRINKS SINCE 1624. MOSTLY THE FLOOR.

**Pick:** A. Four words of sub-line, reads at thumbnail size, and it's the whole location in one boast — the house treats face-down patrons as publicity.

---

## 2. Brass plaque

Beside the door, green with age, half-covered by the banner. Mock-historic register, no "history" as selling point, no "Est. 1624" reverence played straight.

**Option A**
EST. 1624 — JENEVER.
FOUR CENTURIES. ZERO REFUNDS.

**Option B**
EST. 1624.
THE FLOOR HAS HOSTED KINGS. MOSTLY FACE-DOWN.

**Option C**
EST. 1624 — JENEVER.
OLDER THAN YOUR HANGOVER. STRONGER TOO.

**Pick:** B. It's the seedy boast in brass — the age is a threat, not a charm, and "mostly face-down" gives the painter a stance for the stick figures on the pictogram panel next to it.

---

## 3. Handwritten warning in the window

Laminated against weather, marker on paper, taped inside the glass. This is the sign tourists photograph before they commit. Kimi's working text was "YOU WILL SPILL." — all three options below carry the same job louder.

**Option A**
YES, IT'S FULL ON PURPOSE.
NO, YOU CAN'T HANDLE IT.
YES, YOU'LL TRY ANYWAY.

**Option B**
EVERYBODY BOWS EVENTUALLY.
THE SMART ONES DO IT FACING THE BAR.

**Option C**
THE LAST GUY SAID "ONE WON'T KILL ME."
HE'S ON THE WALL NOW.

**Pick:** B. It's the no-hands method as a threat, it lands for the women in the audience who have watched a man refuse to bow to a bar stool, and "facing the bar" tells the player exactly what the sawdust is for without a word of explanation.
