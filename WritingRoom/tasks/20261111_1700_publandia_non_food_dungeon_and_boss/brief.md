## Deliverable: drafts/<writer>_publandia_dungeon.md
For EACH of the four pitches (plus your optional fifth):
- **The premise** in 3–4 sentences: what the place is, the satirical target, and why the bros WANT to go in (they're never reluctant, afraid or disgusted).
- **Three signature rooms or set-pieces**, one line each.
- **Two or three enemy concepts**, with a name and a one-line gag each.
- **The boss**: a funny name, a one-paragraph concept, a signature attack and a one-line defeat beat. The author names the boss in the end, so offer two boss-name options per pitch.
- **Its junk-item payoff**: one item with an inspect line (the game-wide junk-item rule; "It can go as dark comedy, dirty, and unfamily friendly as it likes as this is an MA game").
Finish with a short, honest comparison table (strongest joke, risk, overlap with existing Publandia content). Don't rank them for the author; just state the trade-offs.

The rules apply in full. No real place names: Publandia is the parody city, so no real cities, pubs, landmarks or real poets' graves by location. Real brands, songs and titles keep their real names, and so can real dead writers as satire targets. Money is SOVS. No mascots, no narrator, no explaining the joke. Every name must be unique (grep design/). Save early, post a summary, then vote done.
