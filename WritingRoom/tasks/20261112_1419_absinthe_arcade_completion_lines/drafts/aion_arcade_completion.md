## 6. THE GREEN FAIRY WISP

*Canon voice: `design/worlds/debaucheryville/the_absinthium_taproom.md:167-185`. Her canon lines are 12 words ("You sought me, and now you've found me. Was it worth it?") and 6 ("Which version of yourself would you prefer?"). She asks; she never narrates. So: nothing below exceeds 9 words, and none of it names a mechanic. The room distortion, the control reversal and "The Truth" are the engine's job to show, hers to imply.*

### (a) Phase 3 — as she drops into "The Reckoning"

*(The room is already bending when she says it.)*

**Option 1:**
> "I've decided which version of you is real."

*(Closes the loop she opened in Phase 2 — she asked which version they'd prefer; she chose for them.)*

**Option 2:**
> "Stop performing. I'm turning the room off."

**Option 3:**
> "Hold still. The Truth has your face."

### (b) Her exit line — the bros survive 6 rounds

*(The fight ends as "survived." She leaves first. There is no kill to redeem it, and her line gives them no closure to misfile into victory — that's what Option 1 is for.)*

**Option 1:**
> "You may tell people you won. Nobody will check."

**Option 2:**
> "You'll do it again. That's why I'm leaving."

**Option 3:**
> "Come back sober. You won't. Goodnight."

### The bros' reaction

Unwritten, on purpose. The brief permits "if any"; none is the answer. The player files the joke — the bros keep swaggering out with the Essence and the stamp eligibility like it was a boss they beat. Nothing in Section 6 needs a bro line to land, and a dumb reaction would reorder the punchline around them. Deepseek and Mistral flagged the same room-wide risk; this closes it as a decision, not an omission.
