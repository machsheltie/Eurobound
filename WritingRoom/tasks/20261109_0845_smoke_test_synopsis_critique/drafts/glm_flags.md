# GLM — Synopsis satire-voice flags (premise setup)

- **“They’ve got a mission: Sleep in questionable places, drink in questionable quantities, and experiment with everything a substance loving bro wishes to indulge in.”** — “Questionable” twice is euphemistic hedging and the third clause is vague filler, the exact “vague, toothless” swap the voice doc bans when the bible’s bar is hyper-specific degradation like the champagne-rash cease-and-desist graffiti.

- **“Join them on their journey across Europe as they lurch through the continents’ nightlife, seedy underbellies, and cultural hot spots.”** — “Join them on their journey” is a sincere travel-brochure invitation that recruits the player to root for the bros instead of laugh at them, propped up by a generic noun-list and the real-world “Europe” sitting right next to minted canon “Euromemeia.”

### Runner-up (not for final, in case Aion needs a swap)
- **“Each city hides both surface-level charm and a ‘behind the curtain’ underworld of indulgence, vice, and questionable choices.”** — a third “questionable” and a generic secret-world hook that promises a mystique plot instead of the delusion-vs-humiliation engine.
