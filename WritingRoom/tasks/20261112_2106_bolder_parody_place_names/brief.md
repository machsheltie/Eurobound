# Bolder parody place names

Bead Eurobound-aew. The Beads Deployment Team brought this.

## The author's direction (Eurobound Desk Eurobound-aew-q1), verbatim
"Push it further. The game is MA and made to poke fun of what the cities are as far as vices and subversion and debauchery"

The author rejected these as too tame: **Irelapse / Craicland / The Emerald Bile.** The bar is the four city names: **Publandia, Debaucheryville, Sinfonia, Shamsterdam.** Each one names the place by its vice, subversion or debauchery, as a one-word pun you can say out loud.

## Deliverable: drafts/<writer>_place_names.md
**Part 1: Ireland (the priority).** Pitch 8 parody country names for Ireland, best first. Each one names it by its vice (the drink, the craic, the pub as the nation) the way Publandia names Dublin. Give a one-line reason for each.
- Publandia is the Irish CITY, so the country name must not collide with it or confuse the player.
- Ireland's lines include: design/Inventory/items.md:70 "Tastes like liquid Ireland"; design/Inventory/equipment.md:150 "'deep connection' to Ireland (via Boston)"; design/Quests/publandia/couchsurfingchroniclespublandia.md:456 "do not ask her about Ireland"; and the eternal-roundabout news quote "one of Ireland's most unusual attractions". Show your top pick in two of them.

**Part 2: the other 13 names.** They are all in this task's queued_questions.txt (copied from the bead): Netherlands/Holland, Rotterdam, Ibiza, Berlin, Cork, Budapest, Manchester, Barcelona, Brussels, The Hague, Germany, Krakow and Paris ("Parisian"). Each has its file:line hits and the old options, which the author would find too tame.
- For each, pitch 3 bolder names, best first, each with a one-line reason.
- Where the old question offered "rephrase without a name" (The Hague, Germany, Paris), you may keep a rephrase as one of the 3 if it's funnier than a pun.
- Don't reuse the old options unless you think one already hits the bar; if so, say so.

## Rules for every name
- Bold: name the place by its vice. Dirty is fine; this is MA.
- It must be a pun or name the player can read once and get. No in-jokes, no local language, nothing highbrow (standing author preference: American/English-sounding).
- Unique across the game (grep design/). Never a real place name.
- Punch at the place's reputation for vice, not at an ethnicity or a people.
- No final picks: the author chooses.
## Binding rules
- **The bros (Pilsner, Chadwick, Bradley) are sincere doofuses.** No witty quips, no self-awareness, no scam-awareness and no crimestopping. The comedy is how wrong they are. NPCs can be razor-sharp.
- Full MA satire, no narrator, and don't explain the joke. The only ceiling is no nudity.
- **No real place names in player-facing text.** Real brands, games and songs keep their real names. Money is SOVS ("1 SOV"). All names unique (grep design/ before proposing).
- **Naming taste (author):** American/English, satirical, NOT highbrow, NOT official-sounding, NOT in the local language.
- Lines are 280 characters max. Any junk item follows the junk-item rule (a short, funny inspect line; as dark and dirty as it likes).
