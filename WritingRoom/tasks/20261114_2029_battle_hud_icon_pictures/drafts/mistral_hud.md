## Notes for review

1. **Border convention — settled here, applied at integration.** Blue #3D7BFF = buff (one buff colour across all three slices; Kimi's sugar_rush_hour and Deepseek's tunnel_vision are already blue). Red #E23A3A = debuff that hurts inside the fight. Orange #FF8A2A = debuff that hurts outside it (map, wallet). Grey #9AA0A6 = neutral. This adopts Deepseek's proposal; the cost is one sentence in the GDD and a second read at 28 px for free. Effect on Kimi's slice: wobbly_windmill_navigation and contact_high flip red→orange (both are Navigation penalties); crash, cottonmouth, the_shakes stay red; gush_locked stays orange; sugar_rush_hour stays blue.
2. **broke_af filed orange, flagged.** Its lasting pain is the wallet (can't buy, shops) — same family as Deepseek's tourist_trap_victim. The master list's "−1 Confidence in battle" is minor next to that; if the author wants every NEGATIVE-list status red, flip it — but then tourist_trap_victim goes red too and the two wallet icons stop reading as a pair.
3. **hardcore_gamer is mixed** (+2 Speed, +1 crit vs −1 HP/turn drain) but the master list files it under POSITIVE and the drain is the tax — same shape as Sugar Rush Hour — so it takes the buff colour, blue. Flagged, not hidden.
4. **Canon confirmations (GDD 308):** grease_fingers = oil drip, red border; embarrassed = pulsing blush, red border. Objects unchanged; this row only names the colour and the loop. Hungover's sloshing mug is not in the 22-icon list, so no mug in this set.
5. **28 px collision check against the other two slices.** broke_af wallet (flat bifold + moth) vs tourist_trap_victim fanny pack (pouch + strap + buckle): distinct, but the moth must sit outside the wallet silhouette or the wallet reads as a plain rectangle beside his pouch. embarrassed blush (one solid oval) vs sloppy blindfold (mask with two eye holes + tie strings): distinct only if the eye holes stay hard dark-filled — if they soften at 28 px the mask fills in and becomes a second blush blob. gains_bro dumbbell chosen so the set has exactly one hand (Kimi's the_shakes). grease drip (hanging teardrop off a stub) vs food_poisoning drip (glob on a horizontal skewer): different axes. hardcore_gamer's blue bottle is the only bottle in the set. Action-type kraft bag (two handle loops) vs fanny pack (waist strap): distinct.
6. **Action-type icons:** same round badge, proposed at 28 x 28 (the plan leaves their px size unread by code; status icons are 28). Neutral grey because they forecast an action type, not a state on the bearer — colour-coding them as buff/debuff would double-signal against the status badges. Objects are all bar-fight/1997–2005 era: pool cue, shopping bag, microphone, housefly. No lettering on any of them.
7. **Display names** come from `battle_core.gd` `_status_name` fallback: Food Poisoning, Broke AF (the `Af`→`AF` replace), Hardcore Gamer, Gains Bro, Grease Fingers, Embarrassed. Action icons: Attack / Item / Skill / Debuff. No real place names anywhere; real brands appear only as shapes (blue bottle, dumbbell), no logos at 28 px.

**Reviewer:** Kimi — joke density and the half-line column; Deepseek — teeth on the last column and the silhouette risks in note 5.
