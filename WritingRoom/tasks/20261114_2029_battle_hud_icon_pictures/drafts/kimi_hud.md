## Notes for review

1. **Two mixed statuses need an author-facing call.** `sugar_rush_hour` and `gush_locked` both have `section: null` in the engine ("pending author: mixed buff and debuff"). I picked one border each instead of proposing a third "mixed" colour: two badge colours nobody can read at 28 px would be worse than a confident wrong answer. Sugar Rush files blue (the lie the item tells); Gush-Locked files orange (the couch-lock outweighs the armour). If the author wants both filed the same way, Gush-Locked can go green — but orange is the honest read.
2. **Canon cues honored:** sponge-and-sparkle (Speedcakes doc, approved cue), trembling hand with 2 px offset swap (Bottoms Down PNG spec), spin motif (Wobbly Windmill spec). The pacifier, whoopee cushion, cotton ball, incense cone and cake-timer are new objects, all PROPOSED.
3. **Crash is tagged "combo" in the master list** but its effects are pure penalty; I filed it red. Flagging in case the combo tag has a HUD meaning I can't see.
4. **No lettering anywhere.** The timer dial is a dial, not numbers; the sponge has no brand; the windmill has no inscription (the opener's "Respect The Grain" stays on the opener).
5. **Collision check at 28 px:** cotton ball (white puff) vs Crash (flat pink-red blob) vs Contact High smoke (grey-green curl, vertical) — three white-ish puffs was the risk; the cotton ball is the only round one, the cushion is flat and red, the smoke is a curl. Distinct silhouettes.
