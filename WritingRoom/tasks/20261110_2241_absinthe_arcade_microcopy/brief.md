## Deliverables (drafts/)
1. drafts/token_attendant_mutters.md: 30 ambient one-liners (max ~12 words each) he mutters while working when the bros walk near him, grouped by what he's doing (unjamming a ticket machine, kicking the token machine, mopping/servicing the bathroom, refilling the claw, generic shift misery). He mutters to himself or the machines, not to the bros. Plus 5 lines for when he's drunker after 01:00.
2. drafts/ddr_expert_lines.md: for each bro, 6 short lines reacting to the difficulty screen refusing to leave EXPERT (selecting EASY bounces back), and 3 lines each after a failed EXPERT song. The bros are frustrated, never scared, never self-aware: they blame the machine, the floor, the song, Europe.
