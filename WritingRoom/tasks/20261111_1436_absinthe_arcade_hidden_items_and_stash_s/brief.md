# Absinthe Arcade hidden items and stash spots

Author's assignment (chat with eurobound-e3, 2026-11-11), verbatim:
"Our writers should decide upon a few hidden items in the absinthe arcade. Like all good rpgs there should be hidden things in the area and maybe chests (or their equivalent for this game)"

The room proposes. The author approves. Two jobs.

## Job 1: what is Eurobound's "chest"?
Every RPG has a searchable container that rewards poking around. Propose what plays that role in Eurobound: the thing the bros open, rummage, shake down or reach into. It has to belong to this world (three aging frat bros on a bender through a parody 90s/2000s Europe) and work in the Arcade first. It may later be reused game-wide, so say whether your idea travels to bars, hostels and streets or is Arcade-only.
Give two options with your favourite marked. For each give the name, what it physically is, how the bros open it, and one line the game shows when it's empty or already looted.
Do not set drop rates, stats or prices. Those are the author's. If your idea needs a mechanic (locked, needs tokens, one-time), describe it and mark it "AUTHOR: mechanic".

## Job 2: 3 to 5 hidden things in the Arcade
For each give:
- the name (unique in the whole game)
- where it is hidden, using the real room layout below
- how it is found: inspect a spot, or inside a Job 1 container
- one inspect line
- what it is for: junk with a payoff line, a key or quest hook, or a tradeable. Effects and numbers are "AUTHOR: numbers"; do not invent stats.
Two options per slot, favourite marked.

## The Arcade as it stands (canon, do not change)
- Interior about 15.4 x 18 real metres: the main hall (about 15 x 12 m) plus the north L-wing (about 8 x 6 m), framed for one fixed-tilt HD-2D screen. The mezzanine is visual only, not walkable, so nothing can be hidden up there. (NEXT_WORK.md, rulings 2026-11-02)
- Already hidden, so don't duplicate them: the Absinthium Taproom entrance behind the Time Crisis machine; the secret restroom in the claw machine maintenance closet, unlocked with 3 tokens in the broken DDR machine, with its graffiti and its own lore items (design/worlds/debaucheryville/absinthearcade.md L94-140; the claw_machine_maintenance_closet_restroom_items.json file); and the Sea-Monkeys NFT in the rigged claw.
- Money is SOVS, 1 SOV = 1 token. The claw costs 5 tokens and the DDR restroom 3. Cabinets are battle encounters, and the rigged claw is the only mini-game.
- Real arcade titles keep their real names (Frogger, Street Fighter, DDR, Time Crisis, PAC-MAN). The Green Fairy mural is inside too. The black window cat is canon set dressing: leave it alone.
- Check names against design/World Design/Debaucheryville/items/absinthe_arcade_prize_catalogue.md and the prize-names task (tasks/20261111_0622_absinthe_arcade_prize_names/final.md).
- People here: Tomáš "Two-Fifty" (bartender), Jakub "Tokens" Dvořák (token attendant), Cara and Backpack Girl. Hidden things may involve them but must not change their canon.

## The junk-item rule (author, 2026-11-11, binding)
"little bit items like that that seem to be junk reward the player for investigating them and that they should come with really funny microcopy." Keep inspect lines under 25 words. On this microcopy the author said: "It can go as dark comedy, dirty, and unfamily friendly as it likes as this is an MA game." The bar: "A coat-check stub for an entire human being. Nobody came back to claim him."

## Rules
House rules apply (bible/00_room_rules.md, 01_comedy_directive.md, 02_intoxication_law.md, 03_bros_choose_debauchery.md). No intoxicant gives a free advantage. No mascots, pets or companions. The bros are never afraid or disgusted, never self-aware. No narrator, no winking, no explaining the joke. No real place names. Era is 90s/2000s.
