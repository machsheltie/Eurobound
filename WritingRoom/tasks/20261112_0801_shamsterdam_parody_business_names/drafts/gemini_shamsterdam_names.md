# Shamsterdam Parody Business Names: Gemini Proposals

## Location 1: Condom Shop (formerly "Condomerie Het Gulden Vlies")
*Design doc context: Specialty retail boutique in Shamsterdam Red Light District selling custom/novelty condoms with complete deadpan professional dignity.*

### Candidate Names:
1. **The Rubber Baron** *(TOP PICK)*
   - *Joke Note:* Gilded Age American monopoly swagger slapped onto a retail shop hawking ribbed latex souvenirs.
2. **The Glove Compartment** *(TOP PICK)*
   - *Joke Note:* Utilitarian dad-car terminology repurposed as sleazy tourist-trap innuendo for buying rubber.
3. **Wrap Attack**
   - *Joke Note:* Aggressively tacky Y2K suburban mall energy (think 'Shark Attack' surfwear or laser tag) sold to bros on a bender.
4. **The Jimmy Depot**
   - *Joke Note:* Big-box retail warehouse framing for something you shove into your cargo shorts in a panic.
5. **Raincoat Express**
   - *Joke Note:* Euphemism delivered with the bleak efficiency of an airport newsstand or oil-change franchise.
6. **Sheath & Barrel**
   - *Joke Note:* Pretentious Crate & Barrel suburban lifestyle mall parody masking novelty dick balloons.

## Location 2: Sex Museum / Chocolate Fountain (formerly "Venustempel")
*Design doc context: Relocated to Museum Mile; Victorian erotica meets a 4-meter working chocolate phallus fountain that tourists continuously double-dip.*

### Candidate Names:
1. **The Meat Museum** *(TOP PICK)*
   - *Joke Note:* Monosyllabic, borderline-butcher-shop vulgarity that lets the bros insist they "spent the afternoon studying culture."
2. **The Dong Palace** *(TOP PICK)*
   - *Joke Note:* Chinese buffet / tacky roadside roadside-diner royal naming convention framing a monumental cocoa biohazard.
3. **The Boner Pavilion**
   - *Joke Note:* Grand civic world's-fair architecture phrasing wrapped around a four-meter fountain of lukewarm dipping fudge.
4. **Phallus Palace**
   - *Joke Note:* Classic neon strip-mall alliteration that screams cheap bachelor party photo-op.
5. **The Nut Hut**
   - *Joke Note:* Roadside bait-and-tackle shack cadence for an exhibition hall featuring Victorian peep shows and hot couverture.
6. **The Dicktionary**
   - *Joke Note:* Pseudo-academic pun pretending to be an educational reference volume so tourists have a shameless cultural alibi.
