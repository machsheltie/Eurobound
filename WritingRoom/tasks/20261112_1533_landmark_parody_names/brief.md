## Binding rules
- **The bros (Pilsner, Chadwick, Bradley) are sincere doofuses.** No witty quips, no self-awareness, no scam-awareness and no crimestopping. The comedy is how wrong they are. NPCs can be razor-sharp.
- Full MA satire, no narrator, and don't explain the joke. The only ceiling is no nudity.
- **No real place names in player-facing text.** Real brands, games and songs keep their real names. Money is SOVS ("1 SOV"). All names unique (grep design/ before proposing).
- **Naming taste (author):** American/English, satirical, NOT highbrow, NOT official-sounding, NOT in the local language.
- Lines are 280 characters max. Any junk item follows the junk-item rule (a short, funny inspect line; as dark and dirty as it likes).
