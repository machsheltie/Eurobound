Canon (`absinthe_arcade_items.json:121`, `key_items.json:315`) says only "unfamiliar musical symbols". The Asset team's ref used a **treble clef** — the single most recognisable music symbol on the planet, which makes "unfamiliar" false on sight, and the unreadable clue is the point of the item (its future use is `music_symbol_clue` in Sinfonia).

- **Option A (recommended): a medieval neume.** One chunky square lozenge with a single hooked tail, one fainter lozenge behind it, then the pulse repeats. Unfamiliar to players and to anyone who reads music; points at Sinfonia's old-music pretension.
- **Option B: an invented glyph.** Three filled note-heads stacked on one diagonal beam, belonging to no notation system that exists. Unfamiliar to every player, impossible to mistake for a brand.
- **Option C: keep the treble clef.** Consistent with what shipped, but it argues with the item's own word.

The table below is written with Option A; a one-line swap in `decoration` covers B or C.
