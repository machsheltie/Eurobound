## 1. `item_shady_loot_crate_voucher` — Shady Loot Crate Voucher

### silhouette
A single rectangular paper voucher, roughly 2.5:1 width to height, lying flat with one corner lifted in a curl. A perforated stub line runs down the left edge; the main body is the larger right panel. No card stock thickness — it bends like a receipt.

### scale_cue
The perforated stub edge plus the one big stamped word across the middle are the two things that read in an empty 128 px frame.

### materials_and_cheap_cues
Thin thermal fax paper, slightly greasy to the look — a faint translucent patch where a thumb held it. Corners browned. The perforation is torn halfway open, not cleanly punched. No laminate, no embossing, no hologram.

### colours
- Paper: yellowed cream #F5F0DC (location anchor, Canvas Cream).
- Main print: faded red #B22222 (location anchor, Faded Big-Top Red), slightly blurred like a worn ribbon.
- Stamp: neon pink #FF6EC7 (location anchor, Neon Marquee).
- Grease thumbprint: brown #7A5C3A. Vape film: gray #C0C0C8 (location anchor, Vape-Cloud Gray) as a faint smear across one corner.

### contents
None — it is paper.

### decoration
One crude starburst stamp in pink #FF6EC7, off-centre, half over the edge of the print. The perforation stub is blank.

### condition
Dog-eared upper-right corner, one coffee ring, the half-torn perforation, a crease where it was folded to pocket size, browned edges, grease thumbprint, vape-film smear.

### garnish_and_addons
None.

### palette_anchors
Inherited from `cirque_du_shady.md:120` Technical Specifications: #B22222, #F5F0DC, #FF6EC7, #C0C0C8. New to this item: #7A5C3A (grease brown).

### reads_at_icon_size (128 px blind checklist)
- [ ] A paper ticket/voucher, not a card — thin, curled corner, perforated stub on the left.
- [ ] One big pink starburst stamp and faded red print; the word across the middle reads as a single block, not a barcode.
- [ ] Greasy, browned, handled — a hawker's tent object, not a bank or VIP pass.

### must_not_look_like
- A credit card or laminated VIP pass — no thickness, no shine, no chip.
- A lottery ticket — no barcode, no numbers grid.
- Clean premium cardstock — yellowed, browned, greasy.
- A gift certificate — no bow, no foil.

### lettering
PROPOSED — canon specifies no printed text on the voucher; spelling it out here for the icon, flagged for the Desk. All caps, thermal-print blur, faded red #B22222, with the pink #FF6EC7 starburst stamp reading over the last line:
- `SHADY LOOT CRATE`
- `VOUCHER — REDEEM AT PRIZE BOOTH`
- `A BIT OF A GAMBLE, HONESTLY`
- Stamp, pink #FF6EC7, rotated: `NO REFUNDS`
Stub (left of perforation): no words.

## 2. `item_used_glitter_hat` — Used Glitter Hat

Author-ruled look (Desk asset-team-desc-used_glitter_hat): a glitter party crown — "the bros' 'basically a crown'" — 80s toy-store princess-crown style, with glitter and streamers. No My Little Pony branding, logo, or characters. Used and grubby, matching its own line "Glitter hides the stains. Mostly."

### silhouette
A zigzag crown band of five rounded plastic points, wider than tall, about 2:1 width to height, with a thin elastic string hanging from one side and two curly streamers dangling from two of the points. Reads as a party favour, not jewellery.

### scale_cue
The five-point zigzag silhouette is the one thing that reads in an empty frame — crown shape, not cap, not headband.

### materials_and_cheap_cues
Thin vacuum-formed plastic, brittle-looking, with a visible mould seam down the centre of one point. Glitter is glued on by hand in uneven clumps — some patches bare where it has flaked off, showing the glue. Flat cloudy plastic gems pressed into three points. Cheap gray elastic, stretched out.

### colours
- Crown base plastic: depressing yellow #F0E68C (prize-booth palette anchor, Depressing Yellow), dulled.
- Glitter: mixed specks of magenta #FF6EC7, purple #9370DB, and silver #C0C0C8 — uneven, clumped.
- Gems: cloudy pink #F4A7C3, one cracked.
- Streamers: one magenta #FF6EC7, one purple #9370DB, both curled.
- Elastic: gray #C0C0C8.
- Stains: brown #7A5C3A sweat marks inside the band; one dark patch where glitter no longer covers.

### contents
None — it is a hat.

### decoration
Glue-on glitter over the whole band, three flat gems, two streamers. No jewels that are real, no enamel, no branding of any kind — no logo, no character art.

### condition
Previous owner sweated in this: brown sweat stains along the inner band, glitter worn bare in patches (glue showing), one point bent slightly, gems smudged with fingerprints, one gem cracked, elastic stretched and gray, a dark stain on one streamer that the glitter only mostly hides.

### garnish_and_addons
Two curly streamers attached at the points; that is all — no pom-pom, no veil, no feather.

### palette_anchors
Inherited from `the_participation_trophy.md:80` Technical Specifications: #F0E68C, #9370DB. Inherited from `cirque_du_shady.md:120`: #FF6EC7, #C0C0C8. New to this item: #F4A7C3 (cloudy gem pink), #7A5C3A (stain brown).

### reads_at_icon_size (128 px blind checklist)
- [ ] A five-point zigzag plastic crown with dangling streamers — a cheap party crown, not a tiara, cap, or real crown.
- [ ] Glitter sparkle against bare worn patches — shiny in clumps, dull where it has flaked.
- [ ] Grubby: brown stain at the band, bent point, stretched elastic — worn by someone else first.

### must_not_look_like
- A real gold crown or a smooth tall tiara — plastic zigzag, five points, elastic string.
- A new clean party hat — stains, flaked glitter, bent point, stretched elastic.
- A baseball cap or trucker hat — no brim, no mesh.
- Any branded character merchandise — no logo, no character, no franchise marks of any kind.

### lettering
None.

## 3. `item_beatdown_casting_flyer` — Shady Productions Casting-Call Flyer

### silhouette
A single rectangular sheet of paper, roughly 1:1.4 width to height, curling at the corners, lying at a slight angle. Thin enough that the curl reads.

### scale_cue
The dense block of all-caps print across the upper half is the one thing that reads in an empty frame — a sheet of text, not a card, book, or screen.

### materials_and_cheap_cues
Thin thermal fax paper, slightly translucent, edges browned from the fax heat. Dot-matrix-style print, slightly blurred, one line double-struck where the print head stuttered. Creased down the middle from being folded. No gloss, no card stock, no staple.

### colours
- Paper: yellowed cream #F5F0DC (location anchor, Canvas Cream) with browned edges #D2B48C (prize-booth anchor, Worn Canvas).
- Print: near-black #1A1A1A.
- One smudge: brown #7A5C3A where a thumb gripped it; one coffee ring #8B6F47.

### contents
None — it is paper.

### decoration
None. No logo, no image, no border — just the text block on blank paper. The blank lower two-thirds is part of the look: the same flyer, every time.

### condition
Corners curled, edges browned/singed from the fax, middle crease, coffee ring, thumb smudge, one double-struck stuttered line, a small tear at the bottom edge.

### garnish_and_addons
None.

### palette_anchors
Inherited from `cirque_du_shady.md:120`: #F5F0DC. Inherited from `the_participation_trophy.md:80`: #D2B48C. New to this item: #1A1A1A (print black), #7A5C3A (thumb smudge), #8B6F47 (coffee ring).

### reads_at_icon_size (128 px blind checklist)
- [ ] A sheet of paper, not a card or screen — curling corners, browned edges, middle crease.
- [ ] A dense block of all-caps text across the upper half, rest blank.
- [ ] Fax-cheap: blurred dot-matrix print, one double-struck line, coffee ring.

### must_not_look_like
- A glossy magazine or poster page — thin, browned, curling.
- A phone screen or email — paper with fax-browned edges.
- A laminated notice or clean printer paper — creased, ringed, smudged.
- A card or envelope — full sheet, single text block.

### lettering
Exact, word for word, all caps, one block, upper half of the sheet, left-aligned, near-black #1A1A1A — no other words anywhere on the flyer:
`WANTED: ATHLETIC-INTELLECTUAL HYBRID. CARGO SHORTS PREFERRED. NO EXPERIENCE NECESSARY.`
