## 1. THE APPROACH, THE OFFER, AND WHO GOES

*Absinthe Arcade. She is on the floor, back against a cabinet, half a kebab in her hand, ukulele across her knees. One string is visibly wrong.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Oh — hey. Hey. You want the rest of this? I'm serious, take it. I've eaten. Sit down. You look like a story."

*They sit. The notebook is out before anyone has said a name.*

**Backpack Girl** *(writing, not looking up)*:
> "Okay. Don't say the thing you say when you meet people. Say the thing you'd say if you were already bored of me."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "I'm divorced."

*A beat. She writes.*

**Lord Pilsner** *(producing it, eager, unfolding it)*:
> "It's laminated."

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Why is it laminated."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "They give you two copies. I wanted one that would survive."

**Chadwick:**
> "Four hundred and six."

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Four hundred and six what."

**Chadwick:**
> "It fluctuates."

**Backpack Girl:**
> "That's not a following. That's a group chat."

**Chadwick:**
> "It's a *community*."

**Bradley:**
> "My resting heart rate's forty-one."

*She stops writing.*

**Bradley:**
> "I timed it myself."

*She writes something long. It takes a while.*

**Backpack Girl** *(finally looking up, warmer than the situation deserves)*:
> "Okay. Nobody said anything true, so I'm going to say the true thing. There's floor at my place. There's always floor at my place, that's my whole practice."

**Backpack Girl:**
> "And there's my room. One bed. One of you. The other two get the floor and the common room and the book."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "The book?"

**Backpack Girl:**
> "My roommates run a book on how long guests stay. You're all in it. It's not personal, it's *data*."

*All three stand up at the same time. It is the most competitive any of them have been all trip.*

### The pick

*She looks at the notebook, then at him.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "You. Divorce. Four times, you told me. Four times in six minutes."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "It's a big thing."

**Backpack Girl:**
> "It's a *chorus*. Bring the paperwork, I want the dates."

**Lord Pilsner** *(to the other two, hoarse with feeling)*:
> "Bros. She wants the dates."

### She takes him and goes

*She is already moving. The pack never comes off. He follows.*

**Backpack Girl** *(over her shoulder, to the two left on the floor)*:
> "Common room's through there. Mats in the cupboard. Don't move the mats, they've got a system."

**Backpack Girl** *(the last thing, from the door, entirely sincere)*:
> "There's a vending machine. It's not a good vending machine, but it's *ours*."

*The door closes.*

**Bradley:**
> "That's the fastest anyone's ever picked one of us."

**Chadwick:**
> "It's because I had the phone."

**Bradley:**
> "It's because he had the phone."

**Chadwick** *(filming the door)*:
> "I'm going to watch this back."

## 3. THE MORNING-AFTER — "THE BOOK TAKES THE RING"

**Which bro:** Lord Pilsner.
**Result used (author's list):** **stolen from** — routed to the roommates' book, not to her. She is asleep in the other room for the loss itself and steals nothing.
**Why it's funniest for him:** he is on this trip to celebrate a divorce he is nowhere near finished with, and he is the only bro who would put the one thing he cannot make a story out of on a table and walk away from it.

**The night, implied:** the door, the wrong string, the tuning. At 05:40 the door opens and he comes out and she comes out behind him with the notebook and sits on the edge of the third mat, because the bed is now covered in pages. Nobody says anything about it. He goes back in at six and he is back on the mat by 06:50.

**The morning — 07:40. Common room.**

*Pilsner is on a third mat, which he did not have at 02:40. His hair is up on one side. He is awake, not moving, and pleased about the mat.*

*She is in the doorway of her room with the notebook, mid-line, not looking up.*

**Backpack Girl** *(not looking up)*:
> "Don't go anywhere. I'm at the bit where you're tall."

**Lord Pilsner** *(to the bros, instantly, quietly, thrilled)*:
> "I'm in the song."

**Chadwick:**
> "The floor's where she puts the important guys. Closer to the music."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "She needed the bed. You can't sleep on pages."

*Roommate 2 comes out of the kitchenette drying a mug. There is a ring on his pinky finger. It is very obviously too small.*

**Roommate 2** *(holding up the hand, to nobody)*:
> "It won't come off."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "That's my ring."

**Roommate 2:**
> "Yeah."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "Take it off."

**Roommate 2:**
> "I've tried."

**Roommate 1** *(opening the book, reading it back flatly)*:
> "'Nobody hears it before me.' Ring on the table, ring's in play. You went in at one. She played it at six. In this room. To us. And the vending machine."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "I was in the room."

**Roommate 1:**
> "You were asleep."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "I was in the room while I was asleep."

**Roommate 2:**
> "I've run this book nine times. It's never been a ring."

**Lord Pilsner** *(to the bros, with total conviction)*:
> "He's keeping it warm for me. That's what he's doing. He's a warmer."

*Nobody hands it back. Roommate 1 closes the book.*

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "He can't get it off. It's not even off."

*She looks up from the notebook for the first time.*

**Backpack Girl** *(delighted, appalled, sincere)*:
> "Oh, you *lost* it. That's not a verse, that's a bridge. Twenty minutes. Don't leave the building."

*She tears a page out, crosses the room, and puts it in his hand. It is the ballad. Verse two is the aura. Verse three is him, and verse three has the divorce story in it four times, because he told it four times.*

**Backpack Girl** *(hand on his shoulder, meaning every word)*:
> "It's called *The Ballad of the Bros*. Nine o'clock, this room, there's a vending machine, and it's going to be beautiful. Bring the other two. They're the chorus."
