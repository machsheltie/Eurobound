### OPTION A — "THE BOOK TAKES THE RING"
**Which bro:** Lord Pilsner.
**Result used:** **stolen from** — routed to the roommates' book. She is asleep in the other room the whole time and steals nothing.
**Why him:** he is on this trip to celebrate a divorce he is nowhere near finished with, and he is the only bro who would put the one thing he cannot make a story out of on a table and walk away from it.

**The night, implied:** the door, the wrong string, the tuning. At 05:40 the door opens and he comes out and she comes out behind him with the notebook and sits on the edge of the third mat, because the bed is now covered in pages. Nobody says anything about it. He goes back in at six and he is back on the mat by 06:50.

**The morning — 07:40. Common room.**

*Pilsner is on a third mat, which he did not have at 02:40. His hair is up on one side. He is awake and not moving, because moving would mean saying where he is.*

*She is in the doorway of her room with the notebook, mid-line, not looking up.*

**Backpack Girl** *(not looking up)*:

**Roommate 2** *(holding up the hand, to nobody)*:

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "That's my ring."

**Roommate 2:**
> "Yeah."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "Take it off."

**Roommate 2:**
> "I've tried."

**Roommate 1** *(opening the book, reading it back flatly)*:

*Nobody hands it back. Roommate 1 closes the book.*

**Lord Pilsner:**

*She looks up from the notebook for the first time.*

**Backpack Girl** *(delighted, appalled, sincere)*:

*She tears a page out, crosses the room, and puts it in his hand. It is the ballad. Verse two is the aura. Verse three is him, and verse three has the divorce story in it four times, because he told it four times.*

**Backpack Girl** *(hand on his shoulder, meaning every word)*:
