## 1. The Approach & Crash Offer (Rewritten)

*Absinthe Arcade. Backpack Girl is seated against a cabinet, half a kebab in hand, ukulele across her knees. She extends the kebab toward the bros as they approach.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Oh — hey. Hey. You want the rest of this? I'm serious, take it. I've eaten. Sit down, you look like a story."

*She pats the floor beside her. The bros sit. She produces the notebook before they've finished introducing themselves.*

**Backpack Girl** *(writing, not looking up)*:
> "So there's floor at my place. There's *always* floor at my place, that's kind of my whole practice. But you—"

*She looks up. She is looking at one bro. The other two do not exist for the next four seconds.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "—you look like you need a bed. A real one. With a door that closes. Come on. You're not going to find a more authentic version of this city than a hostel room at 4am, and I say that as someone who has been extremely thorough."

*She stands, pack still on, and offers him a hand up. The other two bros remain seated against the cabinet.*

**Backpack Girl** *(to the two on the floor, warmly)*:
> "You two are welcome to the common room! The roommates run a book on how long guests stay. You're both definitely a 'one.' No offense."

*She leads the chosen bro away. The other two watch him go. Chadwick is already filming.*

**Chadwick:**
> "Bros. He's going to a *room*. With a *door*. That's basically a honeymoon."

**Bradley:**
> "I'm just saying, if he's not back by breakfast, I'm eating his kebab."

## 2. Morning-After Options

### Option A — "The Roommate's Boyfriend" (Beaten Up)

**Which bro:** **Bradley**

**Why it's funniest for him:** Bradley is the muscle, the protector, the one who "will charge anyone who threatens them." Waking up beaten by a stranger's boyfriend — and having his protein shake stolen — inverts his entire battle role. He will spend the rest of the game insisting he "took a dive for the plot."

**The Night:** Implication only. Cutaway to the common room at 2am: two bros on floor mats, a roommate taking bets, the door to her room closing. Muffled ukulele. Then silence. Then a single, deliberate wrong chord.

**The Morning — 6am. Hostel common room.**

*Bradley is on a floor mat between Chadwick and Pilsner. His face is a ruin. One eye is swollen shut. His basketball jersey is torn at the collar. He is holding an empty protein shaker bottle like it might still contain something.*

*Backpack Girl is already strumming. Two roommates are awake, watching, holding money. A third roommate — large, silent, wearing a towel around his neck like a prizefighter — is eating Bradley's protein powder straight from the tub with a spoon.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Okay so I've been up since four and I think I got it — it's called *The Ballad of the Bros* and the second verse is about the way your aura screamed 'tourist.' Not in a mean way! In a *documentary* way. Sit down, I only need eleven minutes."

*She looks at Bradley. She does not flinch. She is genuinely, warmly concerned, the way one is concerned about weather.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "You look *terrible*. That's so generative. Hold on, that's a bridge."

**Roommate 1** *(not quietly)*: "Two days. I said two days."
**Roommate 2**: "I said one. He's got the face of a one."

*The large roommate with the protein tub says nothing. He has not stopped eating. He has not stopped looking at Bradley.*

**Backpack Girl** *(to the roommates, delighted)*:
> "He stayed the *night*. That's a whole movement. You owe me."

*She turns back to Bradley, hand on his shoulder, meaning it completely.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Thank you for this. Truly. You gave me a *whole second movement*. The floor's a no, obviously. But thank you."

*She hands him a page torn from the notebook. It is the ballad. It is about him. It is not flattering. She means every word.*

**Result:**
- **-10 Bravado** (no sleep, 6am ukulele, concussion)
- **Gain collectible: Ballad of the Bros**
- **Bradley's protein powder is gone.** The large roommate has also taken his Livestrong bracelet. It is on the roommate's wrist now. It fits better.

**Bro Rationalisation Lines:**

**Bradley:**
> "Bros. I took a dive. For the *plot*. That's basically producing."

**Chadwick:**
> "He got *documented*, bros. That's a credit. That's IMDb."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "A man who inspires a ballad AND a beating in one night? FlagMan's system doesn't have a flag for that. That's off the chart."

### Option B — "The Archive Takes Its Cut" (Stolen From / She's Gone)

**Which bro:** **Chadwick**

**Why it's funniest for him:** Chadwick is the influencer, the documentarian, the one who films everything. Waking up to find his Nokia N-Gage, his chain wallet, and his ring light gone — stolen not by her, but by the roommates as "archive tax" — while she has already left to "perform the premiere" is the exact inversion of his need to capture and monetize every moment.

**The Night:** Implication only. Cutaway to the common room: Chadwick is attempting to livestream to his "network" on a Nokia N-Gage. Backpack Girl is politely pretending to watch. The door closes. The stream buffers. It never un-buffers.

**The Morning — 6am. Hostel common room.**

*Chadwick is on the floor mat. His trucker hat is on sideways. His chain wallet is gone. His Nokia N-Gage is gone. His ring light is gone. In their place: a single page from the spiral notebook, and a half-eaten kebab.*

*Backpack Girl is not there. Two roommates are awake, watching, holding money.*

**Roommate 1**: "Two days. I said two days."
**Roommate 2**: "I said one. He's got the face of a one."

**Roommate 1** *(to Chadwick, pleasantly)*:
> "She left at four. Premiere's at the other hostel. You're the subject, not the plus-one."

**Roommate 2:**
> "Archive tax. For the material. She said you'd understand. You're in the liner notes."

*The page is the ballad. It is about him. It is not flattering. The last line is underlined: "He filmed the whole thing on a phone that was already old when he bought it."*

**Result:**
- **-10 Bravado** (no sleep, 6am absence, robbery)
- **Gain collectible: Ballad of the Bros**
- **Chadwick's Nokia N-Gage, chain wallet, and ring light are gone.** The roommates refer to this as "the archive taking its cut." Backpack Girl genuinely does not know they took anything. She would be horrified. She is never told.

**Bro Rationalisation Lines:**

**Chadwick:**
> "Bros. I'm *immortalized*. That's not theft, that's curation. The phone was vintage anyway."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "A woman who leaves before you wake up is playing hard to get. FlagMan covered this. That's yellow at worst."

**Bradley:**
> "She took your stuff and left you a song? That's basically a trade. You got the better deal, bro."

## 3. States 2 and 4 — Smallest Possible Fixes

The infected refusal (State 2) and cured callback (State 4) are approved canon. Only lines that assume a floor-crash for three need adjustment.

### State 2 — Infected Refusal

**Line that no longer fits:**
> "The floor's a no, obviously. But thank you."

**Problem:** Assumes the offer was floor space for all three. The new setup is a room with a door for one.

**Smallest fix:**
> "The room's a no, obviously. But thank you."

*One word. Everything else in State 2 — the shimmer, the notebook, the ninety-second composition, the eleven people and a vending machine, the euphemism ballad — works unchanged.*

### State 4 — Cured Callback

**Lines that no longer fit:**
> "Floor's still a no. That was never about the rash."
> "—no, okay, no, get in. GET IN."

**Problem:** "Floor's still a no" assumes the floor was the offer. "Get in" implies the floor/common room, not the room with the door.

**Smallest fix:**
> "The room's still a no. That was never about the rash."
> "—no, okay, no, come on. COME ON."

*"Come on" replaces "get in" — same energy, same urgency, but it implies leading him to the room, not shuffling sideways to make floor space. The rest of State 4 — the wrong chord, the "you cured the verse, you did not cure the song," the reversal for the material — works unchanged.*
