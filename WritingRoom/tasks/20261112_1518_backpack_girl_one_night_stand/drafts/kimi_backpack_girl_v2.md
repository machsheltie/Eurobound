## 1. The Approach & Crash Offer (Rewritten)

*Absinthe Arcade. Backpack Girl is seated against a cabinet, half a kebab in hand, ukulele across her knees. She extends the kebab toward the bros as they approach.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Oh — hey. Hey. You want the rest of this? I'm serious, take it. I've eaten. Sit down, you look like a story."

*They sit. The notebook is out before the introductions are done.*

**Backpack Girl** *(writing, not looking up)*:
> "Okay, so, house situation, because I like you all already: there's one bed and there's floor. The bed has a door. The door closes. The floor's for two of you and it's genuinely good floor."

**Backpack Girl** *(looking up, warm as a lamp)*:
> "One of you gets the bed. I already know which one. The roommates have a book on how long you'll stay, so no pressure, but you're all a 'one.'"

### The pick — three variants (the author's chosen bro slots in)

His pitch visibly flops. She picks him for her own reason, which has nothing to do with what he said.

**IF PILSNER:**

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "Fun fact about me: I'm recently divorced. Very recently. I keep the paperwork on me. Laminated. In case."

*He produces a folded document from a plastic sleeve. It has been laminated. It has been laminated recently.*

**Backpack Girl** *(hand on his arm, radiant)*:
> "You laminated the end of your marriage. You're the bed. You're so the bed."

**IF CHADWICK:**

**Chadwick:**
> "We're kind of, like, cultural ambassadors? Unofficially."

**Backpack Girl** *(writing, delighted)*:
> "That's SO interesting. Say 'unofficially' again? I want the exact stress pattern."

**Chadwick:**
> "Unofficially."

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Perfect. You're the bed."

**IF BRADLEY:**

**Bradley:**
> "I can carry you. I carried a keg once. It was mostly empty. Still counts."

**Backpack Girl** *(looking at him with enormous, genuine concern)*:
> "You look like you need a bed. A real one. With a door that closes. You're the bed."

### The other two (all variants)

*She stands, pack still on, and offers the chosen one a hand up.*

**Backpack Girl** *(to the two staying, warmly)*:
> "You two get the common room! The mats are by the vending machine. If the roommates wake you up it's because the odds moved. It's not personal, it's the book."

*She leads him away. The two on the floor watch him go.*

**Chadwick** *(if not chosen)*:
> "Bros. He's going to a ROOM. With a DOOR. I've never even seen a door close."

**Bradley** *(if not chosen)*:
> "If he's not back by breakfast his kebab goes bad. That's just food safety."

**Lord Pilsner** *(if not chosen)*:
> "He needed it more. She could see it. I've never needed anything. That's my whole thing."

### The night (cutaway, all variants)

*The door closes. From the common room: tuning. One chord. The wrong string, held a moment too long. A roommate turns a page of the book. Someone says "new line, new line" and coins come out.*

*Morning.*

## 2. Morning-After Options

### Option A — "All the Doors Are Blue" (Beaten Up)

**Which bro:** **Bradley**

**Why it's funniest for him:** Bradley is the muscle, the one who charges anyone who threatens them. He survives the night untouched and gets folded at 6am over a bed he didn't know he was in — beaten up for sleeping with someone's girl, except he was only asleep next to her, and the girl he actually slept with is in the kitchen writing it down. He will cite his own restraint as the reason.

**The Night:** The cutaway above, plus one tail beat: *5am. A hallway. Four identical blue doors. Bradley, half asleep, counts doors under his breath, loses count at two, and goes through the third one.* Nothing else is shown.

**The Morning — 6am. Hostel common room.**

*Bradley is on a mat between Chadwick and Pilsner. One eye is swollen shut. His jersey is torn at the collar. He is holding his protein shaker like it might still contain something.*

*Backpack Girl is already strumming. Two roommates are awake, holding money. At the table, a man in a hi-vis work jacket is eating muesli. He does not look at Bradley. He has not once looked at Bradley.*

**Backpack Girl:**
> "Okay so I've been up since four and I think I got it — it's called *The Ballad of the Bros* and verse three is the part where you sleepwalked into a stranger's relationship. That's not a metaphor! That really happened to you! You're a GOLDMINE."

**Backpack Girl** *(hand on his shoulder, deeply sincere)*:
> "You look terrible. That's so generative. Also you snore in a really interesting time signature. I got a whole verse out of it."

**Roommate 1** *(not quietly)*: "Two days. I said two days."
**Roommate 2**: "I said one. He's got the face of a one."

**Roommate 1** *(to Bradley, helpfully)*:
> "Third door's his girlfriend's. Yours was the second. All the doors are blue. Nobody's mad."

*The man in the hi-vis jacket turns a page of someone else's newspaper. The muesli never stops.*

**Backpack Girl** *(handing him a page torn from the notebook)*:
> "Thank you for this. Truly. You gave me a whole third verse before breakfast. Nobody gives me a third verse before breakfast."

*The page is the ballad. It is about him. It is about last night. It is not flattering. She means every word.*

*Down the hall, a door closes. The girlfriend has gone back to sleep.*

**Result:**
- **-10 Bravado** (no sleep, 6am ukulele, one (1) concussion)
- **Gain collectible: Ballad of the Bros**
- The girlfriend is never named. The boyfriend is never named. Both are new and unnamed per the brief — no flag required.
- Nothing is stolen. The beating is the only result used in this option.

**Bro Rationalisation Lines:**

**Bradley:**
> "Bros. I let him have those. Guy works nights. You don't stiff a working man."

**Chadwick:**
> "Bros, I filmed the whole thing. It's vertical. It's my best work."

**Lord Pilsner:**
> "A man who inspires a ballad AND a beating in one night? FlagMan's system doesn't have a flag for that. That's off the chart."

### Option B — "House Rules" (She's Gone + Stolen From)

**Which bro:** **Chadwick**

**Why it's funniest for him:** Chadwick is the documentarian, and the document is what gets taken. The man who films everything wakes up as the only man in Europe with no phone, no footage, and a song about himself he can't post. She signed the note with a different name than the one she gave him, and he reads it as intimacy.

**The Night:** The cutaway above, plus one tail beat: *4:40am. Backpack Girl passes the common room with the pack on and the notebook in her teeth, and lets herself out. She waves at the vending machine.*

**The Morning — 6am. Hostel common room.**

*Chadwick is on a floor mat. His chain wallet is gone. His Nokia N-Gage is gone. On his chest: a page from the spiral notebook and half a kebab in a napkin.*

*Two roommates are awake, holding money. One of them is wearing his chain wallet. Pilsner and Bradley are asleep on the neighboring mats. Bradley has one arm extended toward the kebab, in his sleep.*

**Roommate 1**: "Two days. I said two days."
**Roommate 2**: "I said one. He's got the face of a one."

**Roommate 1** *(to Chadwick, pleasantly)*:
> "She's gone. Moved out at five. House rules. She left you the page and the kebab."

**Chadwick:**
> "My N-Gage is gone. My wallet's gone."

**Roommate 2** *(hand resting on the chain wallet, mildly)*:
> "Free shelf."

**Chadwick:**
> "It was on my pants."

**Roommate 2:**
> "Shelf's a shelf."

*The page is the ballad. It is about him. It is about last night. It is not flattering. The last line is underlined:*

*At the bottom, in different pen: "Had to go — house rules, not you. You're verse three. Eat the kebab. — D"*

*She told him her name was Sienna.*

**Result:**
- **-10 Bravado** (no sleep, 6am departure, robbery)
- **Gain collectible: Ballad of the Bros**
- **Gone: Chadwick's chain wallet and Nokia N-Gage.** The roommates took both during the 5am move. Backpack Girl does not know. She would be horrified. She is never told.

> "Leaving before you wake up is respect, bros. My ex-wife never once left before I woke up. Twelve years."
