# Brief: NFT icon images (4 NFTs)

## The ruling (author, Desk asset-team-nft-icons-look, verbatim)
"Each icon is a cheap trading-card slab showing its pop-culture image; the Writers' Room proposes the images and I approve"

## Binding rules
- NFT artwork is 90s/00s pop culture, **never location-derived** (`.claude/CLAUDE.md`; `design/templates/NFT_Template.md` binding rules).
- **The card copy (tagline, edition, value, collector's note, acquisition story) is author-only.** Don't write or change any of it.
- Where the author already wrote the image (marked CANON in the item doc), **keep it**. Your job there is only how it reads inside a slab at icon size.
- Real brands and products keep their real names. No real people's faces (a stolen photo of an anonymous invented guy is fine). "Europe" is banned (Euromemeia). No nudity.
- Audience: younger Gen X, xennial and millennial women. The joke is that the bros think these are priceless.
