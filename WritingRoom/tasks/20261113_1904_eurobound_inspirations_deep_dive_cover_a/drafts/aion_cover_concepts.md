## Cover — "THE LAST STAND" (working title: "NO REFUNDS")

**Format.** Full-bleed action-movie poster. Characters large in the lower two-thirds, painted with total sincerity as heroes making a last stand.

**Composition.** Wide, chest-height camera, frozen mid-shot. The three bros stand back-to-back in a tight triangle at centre-frame — the classic "surrounded heroes" layout from a thousand action posters. Each has his weapon drawn: Pilsner grips a beer paddle two-handed like a war hammer; Bradley levels a broken pool cue like a lance; Chadwick rests his ring-light mace on one shoulder. Stances wide, chins up, faces set in absolute conviction. They are fully committed to a battle they believe is happening.

**What surrounds them.** The "army" is a patient, well-spaced ring of Euromemeia's service industry standing at a comfortable distance in a loose circle: two bouncers with arms folded, a barmaid with a mop, a paramedic leaning on the open door of an ambulance, a taxi driver beside his idling cab, a kebab-shop owner holding his shutter key, a street cleaner with a broom, a local police officer with a clipboard. Nobody is attacking. Nobody is even moving. They are waiting — weight on one hip, arms crossed, in the posture of people who have seen this exact scene before and know exactly how it ends. The only thing anyone in the ring is holding toward the bros is paperwork: receipts, incident forms, laminated notices, a bill.

**The bros (each pose and expression)**
- **Pilsner (centre-front, facing camera).** Navy tee ridden up over the gut, cargo shorts, white crew socks, black flip-flops planted wide. Beer paddle held two-handed, ready. Chin up, jaw set, eyes locked on some middle distance where he believes the enemy is gathering. Frosted tips, puka shell, Sidekick on the belt. He is a war-chief. The tee says otherwise.
- **Bradley (back-left, three-quarter).** Basketball jersey stretched over a size-too-large torso, baggy jeans, Livestrong band, pager clipped. Broken pool cue levelled like a lance, elbows out. One foot forward, one back, in a stance he learned from a movie. Pure aggression aimed at nothing — the eyebrow is doing the thing he does in the mirror.
- **Chadwick (back-right, three-quarter).** Backward trucker hat, popped polo collar, ring-light mace on one shoulder, phone in the other hand filming the standoff. Not watching the "enemy" — checking his footage. Grinning. He believes the trailer is going viral.

**The setting.** A wet cobbled square at 03:00, painterly HD-2D diorama, tilt-shift, warm sodium lamps and one green-white neon strip. Parody signage only: the leaning canal gable of The Hoisted Pint in the mid-ground, the Astronomical Cock-Up Square clocktower in soft focus far behind, a shuttered Kebab Alley to the right. The ground around the bros is littered with the aftermath — a tipped-over street food cart, one cracked window, a spilled pint, a bicycle on its side. Nothing on fire, nothing dramatic, everything small, mundane, and entirely self-inflicted.

**Palette.** Warm sodium amber and deep slate-blue night, one acid-green neon accent. The bros are lit flatly from above by a single streetlamp — unflattering the way a real streetlamp is unflattering. No rim light, no underlighting, no heroic glow. The service ring stands slightly closer to the light than the bros do: the world is comfortable; the bros are in the cold spot.

**Title treatment.** The EUROBOUND wordmark across the top third on a soft dark plate — chrome collegiate slab, all caps, dawn-gradient face, the final O as a bottle cap. Same treatment proposed by Deepseek, adopted here as a placement, not a claim on the design.

**The one joke it lands.** They are the last stand. The ring around them is not an army — it is everyone the city sent to deal with them, and nobody in it is going to fight. They are going to be billed.

**Thumbnail read (Steam capsule).** Three figures in a triangle, one warm-lit ring around them, wordmark above. At 231×87 it reads as "three bros, surrounded, action poster." The paperwork in the ring's hands does not read at thumbnail — that is a full-size payoff. But the silhouette of the triangle-plus-still-ring reads as "something is wrong with this action poster" at every size, which is the joke doing its job before anyone zooms in.

**Safe-area note.** Logo group and all three heads inside the central 40% horizontally so the 616×353 capsule, 460×215 header, and 231×87 small capsule all crop without losing a face or a letterform.

**No animals, no mascots, no fourth figure.** Every NPC in the ring is a service worker acting alone and disinterested; nobody is posed with the bros, and nothing living interacts with them.
