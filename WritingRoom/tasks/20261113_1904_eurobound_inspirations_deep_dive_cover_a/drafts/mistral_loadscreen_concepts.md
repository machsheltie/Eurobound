## Shared doctrine (applies to all three)

**A load screen is not a cover.** It repeats hundreds of times, it appears at the worst moments (deaths, fast travel, every save), and it is seen at a glance, often while the player is annoyed. So the rules differ from box art in one specific way: **every load screen must have a variation system** — a pool of interchangeable elements (tips, board rows, city dioramas, notification senders) so the 400th sighting still has something new in it. A single-payoff image is a cover; a load screen is a small machine.

**The sincerity rule extends to the UI.** Nothing in the load screen is staged as funny — no winking icon, no arrow, no "loading... probably" quip in the bar itself. The comedy lives in what the sincere machine contains: a phone at 3%, a board that lists their delays, a render layer that won't stay sober. The UI is allowed to be diegetic — that is where a load screen can be funnier than a cover — but the diegesis is played straight.

**The bros are in frame, asleep or idle.** They are sincere doofuses even unconscious: Pilsner's navy tee ridden up, cargo shorts, white crew socks, black flip-flops (look-B, load-bearing); Chadwick backward trucker hat, popped collar, chain wallet, N-Gage; Bradley Livestrong band, basketball jersey, baggy jeans, pager clipped, broken pool cue. Chest-height camera, no flattering angles, no rim light on jaws, never sexy. No mascot, no pet, no animal, no fourth figure — the party is three bros, and the load screen obeys.

**Signage uses parody names only:** Publandia, Debaucheryville, Sinfonia, Shamsterdam, Euromemeia, Doner Lane, Kebab Alley, The Skewer Mile, Clocktower Plaza, Absinthe Arcade, Bassline Opera House, The Blarney Trap, The Flesh Market. No real city, no real landmark, no recognizable silhouette of one.

**Wordmark:** adopt the proposed treatment from the cover pass — chrome collegiate slab, all caps, dawn gradient, final O as a bottle cap — as a *proposal pending the author's mark*, and place it differently per concept (below). The load screen must carry the logo every time; it is the one constant.

**Motion is bounded and optional.** Idle loops are 2–4 seconds, subtle, and every motion effect (blur, pulse, flap) ships with a reduced-motion toggle, per the intoxication law's accessibility rule. The loading bar maps to real load progress — never fake, never a joke bar that fills wrong on purpose (the player is annoyed enough already; the joke is elsewhere).

**Tips are flavor with teeth, never lessons.** Each concept has a tip pool (samples at the bottom). Tips give real gameplay information wrapped in one vicious line. No moralizing, no PSA, no "remember to hydrate."

**Do not reuse the cover compositions.** No dawn roof, no selfie. The three concepts below are a hostel floor, a station board, and a goggled plaza.

## Load Screen 1 — "3% BATTERY" (the morning-after phone)

**Composition.** Full-bleed painterly diorama of a budget hostel room in Publandia at 14:37: grey daylight through a grimy window, two sleeping bags, a radiator, a puddle. The three bros are asleep on the floor in their full canon looks, in the poses sleep actually puts people in. Dominating the right two-thirds of frame, foreground, is Chadwick's N-Gage lying face-up on the floorboards — its screen is the load UI, glowing cyan against the dark room. The wordmark sits at the top of the phone screen, chrome slab on dark glass, bottle-cap O catching the room light.

**The bros (asleep, sincere even now)**
- **Pilsner**, on his back, one flip-flop still on, the other kicked off across the room; navy tee ridden up over the gut; Sidekick on his belt blinking. His mouth is open mid-snore — he is telling a story in his sleep.
- **Chadwick**, curled around the phone, one arm still holding it, backward hat over his eyes, ring-light mace lying beside him like a fallen standard. His thumb hovers over the screen.
- **Bradley**, asleep sitting up against the wall, slid halfway down into a wall-sit, pool cue across his lap, pager still clipped. He chose this. In his dream he's winning.

**The phone screen (the load UI).** Lock screen: time **14:37**, battery **3%**, 47 unread. The loading bar is a pint glass filling with foam as the game loads. Notifications pop in at the top of the screen one at a time — these are the tips (below) — and every few seconds **Chadwick's sleeping thumb twitches and swipes the notification away, unread.**

**Background/setting.** Doner Lane is visible through the window, daylight, a shutter half-up, one pedestrian. Signage: Doner Lane only.

**Palette.** Dark navy-brown room, one amber lamp, cold cyan phone glow. The bros are lit by the phone — the only light in the room is the load screen.

**What moves.** Breathing (subtle chest rise). The pint-glass load bar fills with real progress; foam wobbles. Battery ticks 3% → 2% during the load. Chadwick's thumb twitch-swipes one notification per few seconds. Pilsner's snore is a 4-second loop. At 100%, the screen locks (a small padlock icon) — load done.

**Where the tip sits.** As notification banners on the phone screen, swiped away unread by the sleeping bro. The joke of the placement: the game hands you the tip and the bro throws it out without reading it.

**The one joke it lands.** You are loading the game on a device that is about to die, in a room where the only thing still running is the bros' group chat — and they are asleep. Every load is a hangover; the battery and the bros are at the same percentage.

**Repetition system.** Tip-notification pool (10+ lines, below) cycles randomly; notification sender names cycle (Mom, Tony420, The Group Chat, Unknown Number); the clock can read a different late time (14:37 / 15:02 / 16:48) but always afternoon; occasionally the battery starts at 2%. The sleeping poses are constant — that's what makes the variants land.

**Glance read (<2 s).** Dark room, glowing phone, three lumps on the floor. Reads instantly as "the morning after" at any size.

## Load Screen 2 — "DELAYED — REASON: CREW" (the departure board)

**Composition.** A Euromemeia rail hub platform at night. The upper two-thirds of frame is a split-flap departure board, black with amber letters. The header row of the board *is* the wordmark: the flaps flip to spell **EUROBOUND** in the same chrome-on-dark treatment, rendered in amber flap text — the logo itself is a departure. The lower third is the wet platform, where the three bros stand under the board, shoulder to shoulder, thumbs up, looking up at it, posing for a holiday photo that the board is not taking.

**The bros**
- **Pilsner**, centre, thumb up, arm out along the board like a tour guide presenting a monument. Tee ridden up. He believes the board is showing *his* itinerary.
- **Chadwick**, left, filming the board (not the moment) on the N-Gage, hat backward, collar popped, grinning at the screen.
- **Bradley**, right, double-bicep, thumbs up with both hands, jersey stretched, pager on the belt. Absolute conviction, zero result.

**The board rows (the load UI).** Rows flip as the game loads, one row per ~25%:
- `PUBLANDIA ....... DELAYED 47 MIN`
- `DEBAUCHERYVILLE .. DELAYED - REASON: CREW`
- `SINFONIA ........ DELAYED - REASON: CREW`
- `SHAMSTERDAM ..... CANCELLED - REASON: CREW`
- `LAST CALL ....... ON TIME - PLATFORM 9`
The delay minutes count up while loading. Occasionally a row flips to `ARRIVED` for half a second, then back to `DELAYED`.

**Background/setting.** Wet platform, steel blue night, one fluorescent tube humming, the edge of a train that is not the one they're on. No real station, no real landmark; signage is parody only.

**Palette.** Black board, amber flap text, steel blue platform, cold white tube light. The bros are lit flat from above — the least flattering light in Euromemeia, and they are posing for it.

**What moves.** Split-flap letters animate with load progress (each row flip = a chunk of progress). Delay minutes tick up. Chadwick's N-Gage screen glows on his face. The fluorescent tube flickers once per loop. At 100%, the LAST CALL row flips to `BOARDING` and holds.

**Where the tip sits.** A scrolling ticker strip along the very bottom edge of the board, in the same amber flap font — tips read like station announcements. (Pool below.)

**The one joke it lands.** The only thing in Euromemeia that runs on time is last call, and every train the bros take is delayed by the crew — the board says so, in the bros' own handwriting, and they're posing proudly underneath it.

**Repetition system.** Row order and delay numbers randomize per load; extra rows cycle in from a pool (e.g. `KEBAB ALLEY .... OPEN`, `THE SKEWER MILE  OPEN`, `CLOCKTWER PLAZA .. MEET AT MIDNIGHT`, `THE FLESH MARKET .. NO REFUNDS`, `ABSINTHE ARCADE .. SAMPLES 1 SOV`, `BASSLINE OPERA ... BLACK TIE`, `THE BLARNEY TRAP .. 21+`, `DONER LANE ...... OPEN ALL NIGHT`). Tip ticker pool (below). The bros' poses are constant.

**Glance read (<2 s).** Amber board on black, three thumbs. Reads as "transit, delayed, these guys" instantly.

## Load Screen 3 — "BEER GOGGLES BOOT" (the game loads drunk)

**Composition.** A painterly HD-2D diorama of Clocktower Plaza, Debaucheryville, midday — the clocktower (parody silhouette, not a real one), the plaza, a Kebab Alley shutter. The three bros stand mid-pose in the square. The whole frame is rendered **through the Beer Goggles overlay** — the literal render layer already canon for tier 2 (`nothingisgettingthrough.md`): rose tint, drifting hearts and sparkles, soft bloom, everyone beautiful. The wordmark floats top-centre, frosted and slightly blurred, as if seen through the same layer. At 100% load, the overlay **snaps off in one frame**, and the same scene is revealed flat, grey, and unflattering.

**The bros (in the goggles, and after)**
- **Pilsner**, arm spread addressing the square, chin up, tee ridden up — through the goggles he is a conqueror; after the snap, he is a man shouting at a clocktower at noon.
- **Chadwick**, filming the crowd on the N-Gage, ring light on — through the goggles the crowd is his audience; after, it is three locals walking away.
- **Bradley**, double-bicep, pool cue over the shoulder — through the goggles he is the statue; after, the pose is just a pose.

**Background/setting.** One diorama per city, cycling across loads: Clocktower Plaza (Debaucheryville), Doner Lane (Publandia), Bassline Opera House forecourt (Sinfonia), The Skewer Mile (Shamsterdam). Parody signage only.

**Palette.** While loading: hot rose, gold, pink bloom. After the snap: flat slate, grey daylight, sodium accents. The snap between the two palettes is the visual punchline.

**What moves.** The overlay pulses gently (bloom in/out, hearts drift upward, 3-second loop). The load bar is a stein filling, also under the overlay. At 100%: one-frame snap to clean render, logo sharpens, a single heart pops and dies. Reduced-motion mode: the overlay is a static tint only, and the snap is a crossfade — the joke survives without the motion.

**Where the tip sits.** A JRPG-style dialogue box along the bottom, plain UI, above the overlay — the one thing in frame that is *not* drunk. Tips cycle from the pool (below). The box reads clearly in both palettes.

**The one joke it lands.** The game itself can't load straight: it boots through Beer Goggles and sobers up exactly at 100%. The bros would do the reverse — they'd start sharp and end blurred — which is the whole trip in one load screen.

**Repetition system.** City diorama cycles (4); ~1 in 4 loads is a **clean load** — no overlay at all, grey and flat from frame one — so the goggled loads keep their snap. Tip pool cycles. Overlay intensity varies slightly per load. Poses constant.

**Glance read (<2 s).** Pink haze + three silhouettes + logo. Instantly reads as "loading, drunk" — and the clean loads read as "loading, sober," which is funnier in context.

## Production notes

- **Safe areas.** Keep the wordmark inside the central 60% horizontally and the top 15% vertically in all three concepts; the dialogue box/ticker/notification zones must never overlap it. Load screens crop harder than covers on narrow windows — test at 1280×720.
- **The bar is real.** All three loading bars map 1:1 to actual load progress. If a load is instant, the bar flashes and the snap/flip/lock still plays — the beat is the payoff, not the wait.
- **Idle loops bounded at 2–4 s**; all motion has a reduced-motion toggle (LS3's overlay becomes a static tint; LS2's flaps become instant; LS1's thumb doesn't twitch).
- **No animals, no mascots, no fourth figure** in any frame — the heron question from the cover pass does not arise here; nothing living moves except the bros.
- **No real place names or landmarks anywhere in frame or signage.** Parody names only, per canon.
- **Characters are 3D (Tripo + Blender) in painterly 3D dioramas, tilt-shift** — same pipeline as the covers. Pilsner's look-B silhouette must survive the sleep pose and the plaza pose: gut, ridden-up navy tee, cargo shorts, crew socks, flip-flops.
- **Do not animate the covers.** Motion belongs here.
- **Wordmark** is the proposed chrome collegiate slab with bottle-cap O, shared with the cover pass — pending the author's mark; the load screens claim only its placement, not its design.
