"I think we need to have the Writing Room take a deep look into what this game is and what it's inspired by- earthbound, the inbetweeners, European vacation, road trip, wedding crashers, grand theft auto, fable and draft up what the game art for a 'cover' and a load screen should look like."

## Deliverables
1. **drafts/<writer>_inspirations.md: the deep-dive.** It's an internal doc, so real titles are allowed. For each touchstone (EarthBound, The Inbetweeners, National Lampoon's European Vacation, Road Trip, Wedding Crashers, Grand Theft Auto, Fable), name concretely what Eurobound takes from it (tone, structure, mechanic, character type, joke engine) and what it leaves behind. Then a section on what makes Eurobound its own, which no touchstone has. Keep it tight: about 1,200-1,800 words. When approved, the moderator saves it as design/narrative/inspirations.md.
2. **drafts/<writer>_cover_concepts.md: 2-3 box-cover concepts.** For each: composition (what's in frame and where), each bro's pose and expression (sincere doofuses who think they look legendary), the background and setting (parody cities only), palette, title treatment (the EUROBOUND logo idea), and the one joke the image lands. Say how it works at thumbnail size (a Steam capsule).
3. **drafts/<writer>_loadscreen_concepts.md: 2-3 load-screen concepts.** Same fields as the covers, plus what moves (if anything) and where a tip/flavour line would sit. Load screens repeat hundreds of times, so they need to survive being seen often.

## Look and style facts (canon)
- Visual style: 2.5D HD-2D (2D/3D characters in painterly 3D dioramas, tilt-shift), in the vein of Octopath Traveler, Star Ocean: The Second Story R and Bravely Default. The author ruled 2026-11-12 that characters are 3D (Tripo + Blender).
- Lord Pilsner's live 3D look ("look-B"): a navy tee that's too tight and rides up over a modest beer gut, cargo shorts, white crew socks, black flip-flops. The silhouette must survive any scene, and he must never become visually sexy.
- Chadwick and Bradley: use the "Look" lines in their profiles.
- The party is exactly three bros: no mascots, pets or companions.

## Binding rules
- MA satire at full strength. The only ceiling is no nudity.
- **The bros are sincere doofuses.** They're never witty, never self-aware, never scared or disgusted. The art laughs AT them; they think they're the heroes of a poster.
- No narrator, no wink, no explaining the joke.
- **Art concepts:** no real city, landmark or place names, or recognisable real landmarks. The cities are Publandia, Debaucheryville, Sinfonia and Shamsterdam; the continent is Euromemeia. Real brands and products keep their real names, but don't build a cover around a real logo.
- The deep-dive is internal, so real titles are fine there. Never put real titles into the art.
- Don't invent story. The story is the author's alone. Describe what the existing canon is; don't add plot.
