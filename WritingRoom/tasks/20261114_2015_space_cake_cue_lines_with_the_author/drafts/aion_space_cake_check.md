# AION — 3a stand-up test on all bro lines (Kimi / Deepseek / Mistral drafts)

Test used: if a stand-up comic could say the line and get the laugh, a bro may not say it. The laugh is AT the bro. Banned shapes: "that's not X, that's Y" reframes, cool laconic one-liners, ironic stinger tags ("Clearly." "Obviously." "Correct."), comic tags ("I do X", "Like carbs", "Classic me"), competence that sounds cool.

**Verdict up front:** the drafts are clean on scam-awareness, fear and self-awareness. The recurring defect is the **stinger tag** — a witty one-word cap a comic adds, not a bro. Second defect: **four post-2005 slang items**. Details and dumber fixes below.
