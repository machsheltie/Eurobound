## SET A — the 30/60/90 "It's Not Working" chain (review)

Verdict: the chain is strong and mostly ships. Three lines fail a standing rule. The staff is the only adult in Shamsterdam; keep him flat and keep him right.

### 30 minutes — PASSES AS WRITTEN

```
Bradley: "I don't feel anything. Do you feel anything?"
Lord Pilsner: "Maybe we got a weak batch?"
Chadwick: "Should we get another one?"
Staff (overhearing): "It. Is. Working. Wait."
```

- Bradley KEEP. He's asking a real question to a man who is also lying.
- Pilsner KEEP. "Weak batch" is a connoisseur's call, not a mark's call — he thinks his palate is the instrument. No con-detection.
- Chadwick KEEP. Eager, wrong, escalating, and it pays off at 90 when he wants another one after declaring it dead.
- Staff KEEP. Three periods, no adjectives. Best line in the set.

### 60 minutes — TWO FIXES

**FIX 1 — Pilsner.** "Dutch" is a real nationality in player text. Swap for a bro-grade wrong theory:

- **(A) RECOMMENDED:** `Lord Pilsner: "Maybe the metric system weakens it. Everything's smaller here."`
  Sincere, geographically wrong, and it credits a system instead of a person. He is not suspicious; he is doing science.
- (B) `Lord Pilsner: "Maybe real Euromemeian edibles are different."` — safe, flatter.
- (C) `Lord Pilsner: "Maybe it's the water. Their water's got minerals."` — Bradley-adjacent, works if the author wants Pilsner vague rather than specific.

**FIX 2 — Staff math.** At 60 minutes, 60 remain. Bas is the one character in the room who is not wrong about anything; his flat accuracy *is* the joke, and the 90-minute capper only lands because he does arithmetic the bros can't. Fix:

- (C) Nastier, if the author wants teeth: `Staff (not looking up): "Sixty. I will say it again in thirty."`

**FIX 3 — Bradley.** "Ripped off / tourist trap" is a bro detecting the con. Replace with gym logic:

- **(A) RECOMMENDED:** `Bradley: "My metabolism burned it already. I'm a furnace."` — he is not a victim, he is an athlete. Best possible read: the cake has *not* started, and he has already metabolised it.
- (B) `Bradley: "Dud. My tolerance is freak-level. I'd know."`
- (C) `Bradley: "That's not even a real dose. I've done real doses."` — heaviest self-mythology.

**FIX 4 — Pilsner.** "I KNEW we should have gone to a local place" is hindsight that implies the tourist place was the con. Keep it only if the author reads it as pure I-knew-it vanity. If not:

- **(A) RECOMMENDED:** `Lord Pilsner: "I should've bought two. My calls are always right."` — self-credit plus escalation, zero con-detection.
- (B) `Lord Pilsner: "This is a Premium cake problem. I should've ordered Premium."` — uses the doc's own "Premium Collection" upsell.
- (C) KEEP the canon line. Defensible: it's his standard "I knew" reflex, aimed at himself for not being fancy enough.

**FIX 5 — Chadwick.** A bad review is a bro who knows he was served wrong. Replace with a bro who thinks he is doing the shop a favour:

- **(A) RECOMMENDED:** `Chadwick: "I'm tagging them. Free promo. They'll owe me."` — publicity as currency, credits himself, no grievance.
- (B) `Chadwick: "This cake's about to be famous. I'm filming it."` — same engine, shorter.
- (C) `Chadwick: "Forty thousand people are about to hear about this cake."` — pure audience delusion.

**PROPOSED 90-minute staff capper** (kills the chain dead so the 120 lands clean):

- **(A) RECOMMENDED:** `Staff (still not looking up): "Thirty minutes. Sit down."`
- (B) Nastier, weirder, my favourite for the room: `Staff (still not looking up): "It hears you. Stop asking."`
- (C) Kimi's `"Thirty minutes."` — clean arithmetic, completes Wait / Ninety[Sixty] / Thirty.
- (D) Cut the capper entirely. The 30 and 60 caps already teach the pattern; a third staff line risks being a formula.
