## The approved mechanics (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-contact-high-space-cake-proposal.md`, author-approved; don't change any of it)
- The bros buy a Space Cake at De Groene Gids. A **Space Cake Timer** runs for **120 game-clock minutes**, and nothing happens.
- At **30, 60 and 90 minutes**, the "It's Not Working" lines are available (`design/worlds/shamsterdam/de_groene_gids.md:219-240`).
- At **120 minutes it lands**: three distortion rings, a warp vignette, and the trio's canon **"OH."** frame. The bro gets +40 Bravado, 15 seconds of reversed movement, and Space Cake Gamble (−30 Navigation, random teleportation) for 90 minutes.
- **Teleport** (tunnel, Voetgangerstunnel): a wrong turn sometimes becomes a hard cut to a different tunnel exit, costing 30 extra clock minutes. The bros come up somewhere they didn't walk to. Exit labels include "Where you started", Roodelicht Row (red-light district), the Coffeeshop Quarter and Centraal.

## Three sets of lines to draft (each writer writes drafts/<name>_space_cake.md)

**Set A: the 30/60/90 "It's Not Working" chain, a review.** The existing lines are the author's canon base. Keep every line that passes. Flag and propose a fix only for lines that break a standing rule:
- Bros are **never scam-aware**. "We got ripped off. Classic tourist trap." breaks this; a bro may not realise he's been had.
- **No Dutch / real-nationality words in player text.** "Maybe Dutch edibles are different?" needs a parody swap (Shamsterdam, or nothing).
- The 3a stand-up test (`WritingRoom/bible/01_comedy_directive.md` §3a).
Keep the staff lines; they're the cutting voice.

**Set B: the landing.** The canon frame is the trio's "OH." Propose **one short follow-up line per bro** (10 words max) said right after "OH.", plus **an option for silence** (just "OH."). Never afraid, never disgusted, never self-aware. He's been proven right that it was a good cake, or he credits himself.

**Set C: the teleport arrival.** A floating bark when the bros come up at an exit they didn't walk to, missing 30 minutes. **3 lines per bro** (10 words max), plus a **one-line variant for arriving at "Where you started"** and for **Roodelicht Row**. The bro is pleased, impressed or filing it as a shortcut. Never confused-scared, never "where are we?" panic.

## Rules
- Floating barks, no replies. 10 words max for B and C.
- Bros: sincere, wrong, pleased with themselves. Never clever, never afraid, never scam-aware, never self-aware.
- 1997–2005 slang only. "Europe" is banned: the continent is Euromemeia.
- MA. Drugs are in-bounds. No moralising, no PSA.
- Write your file within your first 4 tool calls. Read only this brief, `design/worlds/shamsterdam/de_groene_gids.md` lines 210-245, and `WritingRoom/bible/01_comedy_directive.md` §3a.
