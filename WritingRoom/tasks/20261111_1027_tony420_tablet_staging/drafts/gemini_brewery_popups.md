# Brewery Tour Debaucheryville — ICQ Popups (Restored)

**Primary writer: Gemini (Job 3). Reviewer: Kimi.**

**Context & Authority:** In `design/quests/debaucheryville/brewerytourdebaucheryville.md` (Reconciliation note line 356), four ICQ popup blocks (Miss Morphine, Tony420, RedRoomRandy, CryptoBro69) were stripped in 2026-08-05 because they violated the master spec by selling bottle openers / opener packages for SOVS. Per author ruling (2026-11-11), these popups are restored with new content that:
1. Never sells bottle openers or opener-adjacent items (openers remain strictly earned per brewery).
2. Directly hooks into the Debaucheryville brewery tour experience and themes.
3. Keeps each sender's canon voice from the Darkweb Dossier (`darkwebdossiercrossoverevents.md`).
4. Prices Tony420 in crypto ($MCUM / $KBAB), prices CryptoBro69 in crypto ($HUFF / $ABSN), and uses SOVS (or zero cost) for Miss Morphine and RedRoomRandy.
5. Follows the mandatory game-wide junk-item inspection rule for any sold item.
6. Adheres strictly to the Land-It-And-Leave / No-Explanation directives (§17): no narration, no after-punchline lectures, no moralizing.

## Design Audit Checklist (§17 & Editorial Directives)

1. **Earned-not-bought opener compliance:** None of the four popups sell openers, keychain upgrades, bar passes, or shortcut items. All sold goods are strictly consumable flavor or inspectable junk.
2. **Currency compliance:** Tony420 accepts only crypto ($MCUM or $KBAB). CryptoBro69 accepts only crypto ($ABSN or $TRASH). Miss Morphine and RedRoomRandy charge strictly SOVS. No real-world currency or € glyphs.
3. **Voice accuracy:**
   - Miss Morphine: Spaced lettering, ASCII stars, faux-zen customer service creepiness.
   - Tony420: Late 90s lowercase chatspeak (`yo`, `u`, `ur`, `da`, `4`, `w`), zero eloquence, dropped apostrophes, pitch-shifted scammer confidence.
   - RedRoomRandy: Sleazy darkweb paywall peddler promising footage that errors out instantly.
   - CryptoBro69: Unhinged emoji spam, buzzword stacking, desperate FOMO shilling.
4. **Junk item inspection payoff:** Every purchasable junk item comes with a dirty/caustic 1-2 sentence inspect description that hits immediately without over-explaining.
5. **Land it and leave:** No narrator commentary, no post-joke diagnoses. The bro reaction is limited to exactly one in-character delusion before resuming play.
