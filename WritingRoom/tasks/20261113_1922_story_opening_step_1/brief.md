Bradley and Chadwick surprise Pilsner with a scheme to run off to Euromemeia (Europe) for a post-divorce trip to go and enjoy all of the scandalous, seedy things Debaucheryville, Sinfonia, Publandia and Shamsterdam have to offer. But it needs to be written funny af. We will cover steps 2 and on after this."

## The job
Write a scene-by-scene script of exactly these beats, and only these. The author's beats are the story. **You write the comedy inside them. Add no new beat, event, character arc, quest or plot.** Steps 2 and on wait for the author.

Scenes, in order:
1. The divorce papers arrive. Pilsner finally gets them.
2. The surprise: Chadwick and Bradley spring the Euromemeia trip on him (all four cities' seedy offerings).
3. The leavings: Pilsner leaves his girlfriend; Chadwick and Bradley leave their wives. These can be short beats, a montage or cross-cuts; your call.
4. Mommy's house: Pilsner leaves the house he lives in. Mommy's hug and her smothering, ridiculous advice for a nearly-40 man. The author's example line is the bar to beat (meds, socks, call home, "mommy loves you").
5. The Uber to the airport. He meets the two bros there.
6. The plane takes off.
7. They land in **[LANDING CITY]**, leave the airport and head to the hotel. The landing city is pending the author (Desk Eurobound-0z1r-q1: the author wrote Debaucheryville, but a 2026-08-03 ruling has Publandia first). **Write it as the slot `[LANDING CITY]`** and keep the scene's jokes city-agnostic where you can.
8. The nap. They wake up ready to go out. (The author says "you nap": the player is in control here.)

## Rules
- MA satire at full strength. The only ceiling is no nudity. No narrator, no wink, no moral, no lesson.
- **The bros are sincere doofuses.** Never witty, never self-aware, never scam-aware, never scared or disgusted. They don't land roasts on each other. The world laughs AT them: Mommy, the girlfriend, the wives, the Uber driver, airport staff, flight attendants, fellow passengers and the hotel desk carry the knives.
- **Names:** don't name the girlfriend, the wives or Mommy beyond "Mommy". Every NPC stays a role (the Uber driver, the gate agent). The hometown stays unnamed. The airports and the hotel need parody names: give 2-3 options each, marked [PROPOSED], and grep design/ for collisions. No real place names anywhere.
- No new mechanics, stats or items. If a joke needs one, flag it as a question instead.
- Lines are 280 characters max. Money is SOVS once they're in Euromemeia; back home, no currency is named.
- Format: screenplay-style scene headers, stage directions in italics, speaker in caps.
