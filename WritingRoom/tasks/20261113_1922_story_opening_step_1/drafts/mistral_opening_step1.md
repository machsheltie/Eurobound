## SCENE 1 — INT. MOMMY'S HOUSE, BASEMENT — DAY

*A basement frozen in 2004: a foosball table doing laundry duty, a framed Limp Bizkit poster, a lava lamp glowing on a shelf like a nightlight. LORD PILSNER — frosted tips, puka shells, navy tee riding up over a modest gut, cargo shorts, white crew socks, black flip-flops — sits on the couch in a towel, watching a TV that is off, holding a remote like a scepter.*

*The basement door opens. MOMMY (60s, cardigan, glasses on a chain) comes down one step at a time carrying a thick envelope held away from her body.*

MOMMY: Sweetie. The lawyer's man left this. It says "final." I'm leaving it on the stair. I'm not reading it and I'm not touching it.

*She sets it down. Steps back. Closes the door behind her.*

*PILSNER does not move for a long time. Then he picks up the remote. Points it at the envelope. Changes the channel. Nothing happens.*

PILSNER: *(to the TV)* ...Open.

*Nothing opens. He picks up the envelope. Turns it over. Puts it down. Picks up the remote again.*

PILSNER: Mom. Is it in there.

MOMMY: *(from upstairs)* Yes, sweetie.

PILSNER: All of it.

MOMMY: *(from upstairs)* All of it.

*He opens the envelope. Reads the first page. Reads it again, out loud, to the empty basement.*

PILSNER: "Irreconcilable differences."

*He turns pages. Stops at one. Reads it twice.*

PILSNER: *(louder, to the stairs)* Mom! "The paddle is awarded to the wife."

MOMMY: *(from upstairs)* I saw that one coming.

*He signs the last page with a pen he has to shake. He draws a tiny paddle next to his signature because he thinks it's funny. It is not funny.*

## SCENE 2 — INT. MOMMY'S HOUSE, BASEMENT — LATER

*PILSNER sits with the papers fanned across the foosball table. The basement door FLIES open. CHADWICK — backward trucker hat, popped-collar polo, chain wallet, ring light clipped to his N-Gage — comes down filming. BRADLEY — basketball jersey, baggy jeans, Livestrong bracelet, pager clipped to a belt loop — follows carrying a pizza box.*

CHADWICK: Don't move. Don't say anything. This is the reaction. This is content.

PILSNER: It's final.

CHADWICK: *(to camera, whisper)* Final. Say it again.

PILSNER: It's final. There's a seal.

BRADLEY: *(setting the pizza box down)* We heard "seal" from the driveway. We parked like it was a graduation.

CHADWICK: *(spinning the box around)* We made you a map.

*Inside the lid: a hand-drawn map of Euromemeia. Four cities in a wobbly line, arrows between them, the last one circled three times.*

CHADWICK: Euromemeia. Four cities. Publandia — pubs that don't close, pubs that close and immediately open again.

BRADLEY: Debaucheryville. Clubs, absinthe, and a whole street where the windows glow red. A district, bro. A whole district.

CHADWICK: Sinfonia. The classy one. Opera. We wear blazers.

BRADLEY: Over the jerseys. Blazers over jerseys.

CHADWICK: Shamsterdam. Everything else. They have a menu, Pils. A MENU.

PILSNER: A menu of what.

BRADLEY: Everything.

CHADWICK: *(tapping the last circle)* And it's booked. Non-refundable. We made it expensive to say no. That's love, bro. That's what love costs.

PILSNER: I have a girlfriend.

BRADLEY: Bring her.

CHADWICK: No.

PILSNER: I live in a basement.

CHADWICK: You live in a basement *for now.*

*PILSNER looks at the map. At the papers. At the map again. He picks up a slice of pizza.*

PILSNER: ...When do we leave.

CHADWICK: *(to the ring light)* HE'S IN. Title of the video: HE'S IN.

## SCENE 3 — THE LEAVINGS — MONTAGE

*Three doorways. No music. Nobody waves.*

**INT. THE GIRLFRIEND'S APARTMENT — DAY.** *THE GIRLFRIEND (30s, athleisure, one earbud in) is on the phone and does not pause it.*

PILSNER: It's not a breakup. It's a boys' trip. Europe. One last ride before I... before the seal fully seals.

GIRLFRIEND: *(to the phone)* He's doing it now. *(to PILSNER)* Mm.

PILSNER: It's already booked. Non-refundable.

GIRLFRIEND: *(to the phone)* He's explaining the ticket policy. *(pause, to PILSNER)* I know. *(handing him a single key on a lava-lamp keychain)* Take the lava lamp. It was always yours.

PILSNER: *(taking it, actually moved)* ...Thanks.

GIRLFRIEND: Don't thank me. I'm not a monster.

*She goes back to her phone. Does not look at him leaving.*

**INT. CHADWICK'S HOUSE — DAY.** *CHADWICK'S WIFE packs his duffel with the efficiency of a deployment. CHADWICK films her doing it.*

CHADWICK: Babe, you're the best. You're literally packing for your own widowhood.

CHADWICK'S WIFE: *(not looking up)* I packed SPF 50 sunscreen. For when you come back looking like a scrotum.

CHADWICK: *(to camera)* She gets it.

**INT. BRADLEY'S SUBURBAN KITCHEN — DAY.** *BRADLEY'S WIFE stands at the counter holding a gym-membership cancellation printout. BRADLEY flexes at the doorway.*

BRADLEY: It's a wellness trip. European cities are extremely walkable. I'll come back shredded.

BRADLEY'S WIFE: *(sliding the printout over)* I cancelled your gym membership. You haven't been since the aughts. The aughts, Bradley.

BRADLEY: *(genuinely wounded)* I go.

BRADLEY'S WIFE: You go to the smoothie place next to the gym.

*BRADLEY nods slowly, like she just proved his point.*

BRADLEY: ...Because of the walkability.

## SCENE 4 — EXT./INT. MOMMY'S HOUSE — DEPARTURE

*The front porch of a modest suburban house. PILSNER comes out with a rolling duffel and a small cooler bag. MOMMY follows, holding a laminated card and a Ziploc of pills.*

MOMMY: Mommy loves you. It's going to be okay, sweetie.

*She hugs him. The hug lasts. While it lasts she checks his scalp with two fingers and does not comment on what she finds.*

MOMMY: Call when you land. Call every night. Your father called his mother every single day, and he was forty-one when he stopped.

PILSNER: Mom.

MOMMY: Blood pressure meds. Side pocket. I put them in the days-of-the-week box AND I labeled the box, because I know you.

PILSNER: Mom.

MOMMY: Clean socks every day. EVERY day. I sewed your name into all of them so if you lose them they can find their way back. It took me a week.

PILSNER: *(looking at the sock in his hand)* ...Mom. This says "LORD."

MOMMY: That's your name.

MOMMY: *(pressing the Ziploc and the laminated card into his hands)* This is my number, on a card, because you lose phones. If something happens you call Mommy first and the ambulance second.

PILSNER: Mom.

MOMMY: *(zipping his duffel herself, then sliding the divorce papers out of his cargo pocket and into the outside pocket, flat, where they're quick)* You'll want to show somebody you're single. Quick is good.

*An Uber pulls up. MOMMY watches it, then him, then pats her own pockets for money she already put in his bag.*

MOMMY: There's something in the front pocket. Don't spend it on a hat.

*PILSNER climbs in with the duffel and the cooler. He rolls the window down. She takes his face in both hands and kisses his forehead, and holds his chin one second too long.*

MOMMY: You're still my little man.

PILSNER: Mom.

MOMMY: I know.

*The car pulls away. In the rear window: MOMMY on the porch, waving with both arms. Then she stops, goes inside, and the deadbolt turns.*

## SCENE 5 — INT./EXT. UBER → DEPARTURE AIRPORT — DAY

*INT. UBER. The DRIVER (40s, dead eyes, a podcast playing through one earbud) watches PILSNER in the rearview eating a crustless sandwich over the cooler bag.*

UBER DRIVER: Airport?

PILSNER: International. We're doing Euromemeia. Post-divorce. The seal came through.

UBER DRIVER: Congratulations.

PILSNER: *(mouth full)* She kept the air fryer. There's a whole page about it.

UBER DRIVER: *(flat)* I drove her to the notary. Small world. Tip's up front.

*PILSNER stops chewing. Beat.*

PILSNER: ...Five stars, bro.

UBER DRIVER: Already submitted.

**EXT. [DEPARTURE AIRPORT] — CURBSIDE.** *[PROPOSED signage: NONSTOP REGRET REGIONAL — "Leaving Was Always an Option."] CHADWICK and BRADLEY wait at the curb with matching rolling duffels and neck pillows worn like medals. CHADWICK is livestreaming.*

CHADWICK: HE'S HERE. THE GROOMSMAN OF HIS OWN FUNERAL.

BRADLEY: *(to PILSNER, dead serious)* Did you stretch. International flight. Deep vein. I've been doing calf raises since Tuesday.

PILSNER: Mommy made sandwiches.

CHADWICK: *(panning the camera to the cooler bag)* SHE MADE SANDWICHES. THIS IS NOT A DRILL.

*They go inside in a V-formation, three rolling duffels, flip-flops clip-clopping.*

## SCENE 6 — INT. PLANE — TAKEOFF

*Economy cabin. PILSNER window, BRADLEY middle, CHADWICK aisle with the ring light still on. A FLIGHT ATTENDANT does the safety demo. The bros watch like it's a halftime show.*

BRADLEY: *(counting rows to the exit)* Two rows back. If we go down over the ocean I'm getting the door.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT: *(flat)* ...exits are here, here, and the one this gentleman keeps making eye contact with.

CHADWICK: *(whispering, filming)* She's looking at me.

PILSNER: *(gripping the armrest as the engines spool)* This is it. This is where it starts. I feel like a young Tony Hawk.

*The plane lifts. All three go silent and grab the armrests in one synchronized flinch they will deny forever.*

BRADLEY: *(quiet)* Turbulence.

CHADWICK: *(quiet)* Content.

*The seatbelt sign dings. PILSNER immediately reclines all the way into the lap of the MAN BEHIND, who was eating.*

MAN BEHIND: *(to no one)* It's a four-hour flight.

PILSNER: *(eyes closed, fully reclined)* Then we're gonna be great friends.

*SMASH CUT: the plane's shadow crossing a cartoon map. A dotted line, a tiny plane icon. The map is labeled EUROMEMEIA in a board-game font.*

## SCENE 8 — INT. [HOTEL] ROOM — THE NAP — PLAYER CONTROL

*A room with two beds and a chair that has seen things. Duffels thrown but not unpacked. Afternoon light going to evening outside.*

*CHADWICK is already horizontal on one bed, ring light still on, asleep mid-snore, filming nothing. BRADLEY is on the other bed doing slow calf raises off the edge.*

BRADLEY: Just a nap. Twenty minutes. Tactical. It's what athletes do.

*He lies down. Asleep in four seconds, mid-sentence about REM cycles.*

*PILSNER stands between the beds in socks and flip-flops, holding his Sidekick, vibrating.*

PILSNER: *(to the room)* We are NOT napping. We are in Euromemeia. There are women out there who have never met an American with a paddle.

*Beat. CHADWICK snores. BRADLEY's pager — still clipped, still "just in case" — beeps once. Neither wakes.*

*PILSNER looks at the bed. At the window. At the bed.*

PILSNER: *(lying down, fully dressed, on top of the covers)* ...Twenty minutes. Then we go out. Twenty minutes.

*[PLAYER CONTROL: the prompt appears — NAP / DON'T NAP. Both options nap. The only difference is how long PILSNER pretends he isn't going to.]*

*The screen dims. The neon sign outside flickers on. Somewhere below, a hen party screams.*

*When the screen comes back up it is full dark. PILSNER sits bolt upright, hair in a vertical shock, Sidekick in hand.*

PILSNER: BOYS. It's NIGHT. We slept through the afternoon. We are THREE HOURS BEHIND SCHEDULE.

*CHADWICK wakes filming. BRADLEY wakes doing a calf raise.*

CHADWICK: *(to camera, voice thick)* We back. Content never sleeps. It just... buffers.

BRADLEY: *(cracking his neck)* Tactical. I feel twenty-five.

*PILSNER is at the mirror, re-frosting his tips with tap water, tightening the puka shells, sliding the divorce papers into the minibar like a safe.*

PILSNER: Gentlemen. Publandia, Debaucheryville, Sinfonia, Shamsterdam — it all starts tonight. We are going OUT.

*He opens the door. Hallway light. Music from somewhere.*

PILSNER: *(over his shoulder, to no one)* ...I should call Mommy.

*He does not call Mommy. The door closes. END OF STEP 1.*
