## SCENE 1 — INT. MOMMY'S HOUSE, BASEMENT — DAY

*A finished basement preserved in 2003 amber: inflatable chair, a Limp Bizkit poster framed like a diploma, a foosball table holding unfolded laundry. LORD PILSNER — frosted tips, puka shells, navy tee too tight over a modest gut, cargo shorts, white crew socks, black flip-flops — sits on the couch in a towel, doing nothing at full volume.*

*The basement door opens. MOMMY (60s, cardigan, reading glasses on a chain) descends one step at a time, a fat manila envelope held at arm's length like a dead bird.*

MOMMY: Sweetie. This came. It says "final." I'm putting it on the stairs. I'm not touching it after this.

*She sets it down, retreats two steps, then — because she can't help it — fluffs the envelope so it lies flat.*

PILSNER: *not getting up* Read it to me.

MOMMY: No.

PILSNER: Then summarize.

*MOMMY stares at him. Then at the envelope. Then she goes back up the stairs.*

MOMMY: *(from the top step)* She kept the air fryer. There's a whole page.

*The door closes. PILSNER looks at the envelope. Looks at the remote. Picks up the remote.*

PILSNER: *(to himself)* ...Page one better be about the paddle.

*He does not open it.*

## SCENE 2 — INT. MOMMY'S HOUSE, BASEMENT — LATER

*The envelope is open now, pages fanned on the foosball table over the laundry. PILSNER is on his Sidekick, thumbing with both hands, pacing in flip-flops.*

PILSNER: *(into phone)* It's final. FINAL final. Stamped. There's a seal, bro. A SEAL. That's how you know it's over. They don't seal things that aren't dead.

*The basement door bangs open. CHADWICK — backward trucker hat, popped collar, ring light already clipped to his N-Gage — descends filming. BRADLEY — basketball jersey, baggy jeans, Livestrong bracelet, pager clipped to a belt loop — follows carrying a pizza box.*

CHADWICK: Say that again. For the stream.

PILSNER: I'm not saying it again. It was a moment.

BRADLEY: *(setting down the pizza)* We heard "divorce" and came straight over. Didn't even finish leg day.

CHADWICK: Bro. We have been WAITING for this.

PILSNER: It's a funeral, Chadwick.

BRADLEY: It's a starter pistol.

*CHADWICK spins the pizza box around. Written on the inside lid in marker: a crude map of Euromemeia — four cities circled, connected by arrows, the last one circled twice.*

CHADWICK: Euromemeia. Four cities. Publandia: pubs that never close. Debaucheryville: absinthe, clubs, a whole red-light district.

BRADLEY: Sinfonia: the fancy one. We wear blazers over the jerseys. Hookers, but classy.

CHADWICK: Shamsterdam: everything else. ALL of it. Legal, bro. They have menus.

PILSNER: I can't. I have a girlfriend. I have a situation. I live in a basement.

BRADLEY: Had. Had a situation. The seal, bro.

CHADWICK: We already booked it. Non-refundable. That's how much we love you. We made it financially stupid to say no.

*PILSNER looks at the pizza-box map. Looks at the divorce pages. Looks back at the map. He picks up a slice.*

PILSNER: ...When do we leave.

CHADWICK: *(to the ring light)* HE'S IN. TITLE OF THE VIDEO: HE'S IN.

## SCENE 3 — THE LEAVINGS — MONTAGE

*Quick cross-cuts. No music sting explains anything.*

**INT. THE GIRLFRIEND'S APARTMENT — DAY.** *THE GIRLFRIEND (30s, athleisure, holding a smoothie) listens with the flat calm of a woman reading a terms-of-service agreement.*

PILSNER: It's not a breakup. It's a victory tour. Europe. The boys. One last ride before the seal fully... seals.

GIRLFRIEND: You're thirty-nine.

PILSNER: Thirty-eight. In Europe.

GIRLFRIEND: *(already walking away, keys in hand)* Take the lava lamp. It was yours. It was always yours.

*PILSNER takes the lava lamp. He looks genuinely moved.*

**INT. CHADWICK'S HOUSE, KITCHEN — DAY.** *CHADWICK'S WIFE packs HIS bag for him — efficiently, rolling his popped-collar polos like a woman deploying troops. CHADWICK films her doing it.*

CHADWICK: Babe, you're the best. You're literally packing for your own widowhood.

CHADWICK'S WIFE: *(not looking up)* I packed the sunscreen. SPF 50. For when you come back looking like a scrotum.

CHADWICK: *(to camera)* She gets it.

**INT. BRADLEY'S SUBURBAN KITCHEN — DAY.** *BRADLEY'S WIFE stands at the counter with a protein shake and a printout. BRADLEY is flexing at nothing.*

BRADLEY: It's a wellness trip. We're going to walk a lot. European cities are very walkable. I'll come back shredded.

BRADLEY'S WIFE: *(sliding the printout across)* I already cancelled your gym membership. You haven't been since the aughts. The aughts, Bradley.

BRADLEY: *(genuinely wounded)* I go.

BRADLEY'S WIFE: You go to the smoothie place next to the gym.

*BRADLEY nods slowly, like she's just proven his point.*

BRADLEY: ...Because of the walkability.

*SMASH CUT to all three wives/girlfriend in three separate doorways, watching three separate SUVs/Ubers pull away, each holding a phone already open to a group chat labeled with a single emoji. None of them waves.*

## SCENE 4 — EXT./INT. MOMMY'S HOUSE — DEPARTURE

*The front door of a modest suburban house. PILSNER emerges with a rolling duffel bag that's mostly polos. MOMMY follows him out onto the porch, a tiny cooler bag in one hand.*

MOMMY: Mommy loves you. It's going to be okay, sweetie.

*She hugs him. It's a real hug. It goes on exactly one second too long. PILSNER pats her shoulder twice — the international bro signal for "release."*

MOMMY: Call when you land. Call every night. Take your blood pressure meds — I put them in the side pocket with the little days-of-the-week box, because I know you won't take them otherwise.

PILSNER: Mom.

MOMMY: Clean socks every day. EVERY day. Your father had foot rot in '97 and he only had the one foot to begin with—

PILSNER: Mom, the Uber's here.

MOMMY: *(pressing the cooler bag into his hands)* Sandwiches. Crusts cut. And I wrote your name on your underwear. All of it. So the European girls know it's taken care of.

*She waves from the porch. PILSNER climbs into the Uber, cooler bag on his lap like a parachute. The car pulls away. MOMMY is still waving. She does not stop waving.*

MOMMY: *(to no one, still waving)* He forgot the lava lamp.

## SCENE 5 — INT./EXT. UBER → DEPARTURE AIRPORT — DAY

*INT. UBER. The DRIVER (40s, dead eyes, phone mount displaying a podcast app) watches PILSNER in the rearview eating a crustless sandwich over the cooler bag.*

UBER DRIVER: Airport?

PILSNER: International terminal. We're doing Europe. Post-divorce. The seal came through.

UBER DRIVER: Congratulations.

PILSNER: *(mouth full)* She's keeping the air fryer. There's a whole page.

UBER DRIVER: *(flat, eyes on the road)* I drove her to the notary. Small world. Tip's up front.

*PILSNER stops chewing. Beat.*

PILSNER: ...Five stars, bro.

UBER DRIVER: It's already submitted.

**EXT. [DEPARTURE AIRPORT] — CURBSIDE.** *[PROPOSED signage: LAYOVER INTERNATIONAL — "You Were Leaving Anyway."] CHADWICK and BRADLEY are already there with matching rolling duffels and neck pillows worn like medals. CHADWICK is livestreaming the curb.*

CHADWICK: HE'S HERE. THE GROOMSMAN OF HIS OWN FUNERAL.

BRADLEY: *(to PILSNER, dead serious)* Did you stretch? International flights. Deep vein. I've been doing calf raises since Tuesday.

PILSNER: Mommy made sandwiches.

CHADWICK: *(into camera, panning to the cooler bag)* SHE MADE SANDWICHES. THIS IS NOT A DRILL.

*They head inside, three rolling duffels in a V-formation, clip-clopping in flip-flops and untied basketball shoes.*

## SCENE 6 — INT. PLANE — TAKEOFF

*Economy cabin. The bros have three seats in a row: PILSNER window, BRADLEY middle, CHADWICK aisle with the ring light still on. A FLIGHT ATTENDANT does the safety demo. The bros watch like it's a halftime show.*

BRADLEY: *(counting rows to the exit)* Two rows behind. I'm counting. If we go down over the ocean I'm getting the door.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT: *(demo, flat)* ...exits are here, here, and the one this gentleman is making eye contact with.

CHADWICK: *(whispering, filming)* She's looking at me.

PILSNER: *(gripping the armrest as the engines spool)* This is it. This is the takeoff. This is where it starts. I feel like a young Tony Hawk.

*The plane lifts. All three of them go completely silent and grab each other's armrests — a single synchronized flinch they will deny forever.*

BRADLEY: *(quietly)* Turbulence.

CHADWICK: *(quietly)* Content.

*The seatbelt sign dings off. PILSNER immediately reclines all the way back into the lap of a man behind him who was already eating.*

MAN BEHIND: *(to no one)* It's a four-hour flight.

PILSNER: *(eyes closed, fully reclined)* Then we're gonna be great friends.

*SMASH CUT: the plane's shadow crossing a cartoon map. A dotted line. A little plane icon. The map is labeled EUROMEMEIA in a font that suggests a board game.*

## SCENE 8 — INT. [HOTEL] ROOM — THE NAP — PLAYER CONTROL

*A room with two beds and a chair that has clearly seen things. Their duffels are thrown but not unpacked. The light outside is afternoon going to evening — prime going-out light.*

*CHADWICK is already horizontal on one bed, ring light still on, snoring with his mouth open, filming nothing. BRADLEY is on the other bed doing slow, deliberate calf raises off the edge of the mattress.*

BRADLEY: Just a nap. Twenty minutes. Tactical. It's what athletes do.

*He lies down. He is asleep in under four seconds, mid-sentence about REM cycles.*

*PILSNER stands between the two beds in his socks and flip-flops, holding his Sidekick, vibrating at a frequency only he can hear.*

PILSNER: *(to the room)* We are NOT napping. We are in EUROPE. There are women out there who have never met an American with a paddle.

*Beat. The room does not answer. CHADWICK snores. BRADLEY's pager — still clipped to his belt loop, still "just in case" — goes off with a single ancient beep. Neither of them wakes up.*

*PILSNER looks at the bed. Looks at the window. Looks at the bed.*

PILSNER: *(already lying down, fully dressed, on top of the covers)* ...Twenty minutes. Then we go out. Twenty minutes.

*[PLAYER CONTROL: the prompt appears — NAP / DON'T NAP. Both options nap. The only difference is how long PILSNER pretends he's not going to.]*

*The screen dims. The neon sign outside the window flickers on. Somewhere below, a hen party screams.*

*When the screen comes back up, it's full dark. PILSNER sits bolt upright, hair in a perfect vertical shock, Sidekick in hand, eyes wild.*

PILSNER: BOYS. It's NIGHT. We slept through the afternoon. We are THREE HOURS BEHIND SCHEDULE.

*CHADWICK wakes up filming. BRADLEY wakes up doing a calf raise.*

CHADWICK: *(to camera, voice thick)* We good. We back. Content never sleeps. It just... buffers.

BRADLEY: *(cracking his neck)* Tactical. I feel incredible. I feel twenty-five.

*PILSNER is already at the mirror, re-frosting his tips with hotel water, tightening the puka shells, sliding the divorce papers into the minibar like he's checking them into a safe.*

PILSNER: Gentlemen. Publandia, Debaucheryville, Sinfonia, Shamsterdam — it all starts tonight. Put on your second-best polo. We are going OUT.

*He opens the door. Hallway light. Music from somewhere. The night is a loaded gun.*

PILSNER: *(over his shoulder, to no one)* ...I should call Mommy.

*He does not call Mommy. The door closes. END OF STEP 1.*
