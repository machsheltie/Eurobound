# Brief: Claw closet sparking wiring, zap barks

## The ruling (author, Eurobound Desk bcnr-q9, 2026-11-14, verbatim)
> "Sparks, and a bro gets zapped (a joke line) if you walk too close; no game"

Bead Eurobound-ms3r. The Absinthe Arcade claw closet (`design/worlds/debaucheryville/clawmachinemaintenanceclosetrestroom.md`: "Exposed wiring snakes across walls, some sparking faintly when player passes too close" at :44; "Sparks when player passes too close" at :99; "Electrical sparks (random 2-5 second intervals)" at :173). The old "hazard mini-game" is gone: **no game**. When the party leader walks too close, sparks fly and the leader gets zapped and says one line. The Orchestrator builds the trigger once the author approves the lines.

## Audience (author, 2026-11-14)
"I have told it several times the game is for younger Gen X/xennial/millenial women". She is laughing AT the bro who just got zapped.

## Format
- **A floating bark**: one short line above the leader's head, no dialogue box, no reply. **10 words max**, fewer is better.
- The line is said by **whichever bro is leading** (Lord Pilsner, Chadwick or Bradley).
- Deliver **4 candidate lines per bro** (12 total), then mark your **pick** per bro. The author may ship one per bro or a small pool.

## Rules for every line (binding: `writer_role.md`, `bible/00_room_rules.md`, `bible/01_comedy_directive.md` §3a)
- **Never afraid, never hurt-sounding, never disgusted.** A bro who just took a shock from a wall is pleased, impressed, grateful, or files it as something good. He came to Europe for this.
- **Never clever.** Run the stand-up test on every line: if a stand-up comic could say it and get the laugh, a bro may not say it. No reframes ("That's not X, that's Y"), no cool one-liners, no self-aware jokes, no puns on "shock" or "current" or "electric" that a writer would be proud of.
- **Sincere, wrong, pleased with itself.** The bro misreads the zap: a free service, a sign, a European feature, a workout, a compliment from the building.
- Each bro sounds like himself (read `design/narrative/Synopsis.md` and the character docs): Pilsner's divorced grandeur and paperwork, Chadwick's 400-odd followers and filming everything, Bradley's gym logic and resting heart rate.
- Period: the bros are frozen in 1997–2005. No post-2005 slang ("green flag", "do numbers", "slay", "no cap" etc.).
- MA is fine. No nudity.
