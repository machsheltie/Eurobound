# THE DONG PALACE
## Scene Script — "One Dip"

**Location:** Shamsterdam, Museum Mile, plaza frontage
**Time:** 13:40, peak. Forty-one people in the queue. None of them for the museum.
**Party:** LORD PILSNER, CHADWICK, BRADLEY. Sober. Daytime. It's cultural.

---

### SCENE 1 — THE APPROACH

*The cascade is audible two streets away. Wet, heavy, rhythmic. The party rounds the corner.*

**LORD PILSNER** *(stopping dead, arms rising like he's being arrested by joy)*
Gentlemen.

**CHADWICK** *(already filming, walking backwards into a bollard)*
Oh, that's — that's the whole trip. That's the whole trip right there. Everything before this was a warm-up.

**LORD PILSNER**
Four metres. It's four metres. Look at the craftsmanship. Look at the *tempering.*

**BRADLEY** *(squinting at the plinth)*
It's a chocolate fountain shaped like a cock.

**LORD PILSNER**
It's a *civic landmark,* Bradley.

**BRADLEY**
It's a chocolate fountain shaped like a cock, Pilsner.

**LORD PILSNER** *(genuinely moved)*
Yes. Yes it is. This is the single greatest thing Europe has ever produced and I will be saying that in three cities.

*He sets off toward it at a pace that suggests a man who has forgotten he is thirty-nine.*

**CHADWICK** *(to camera, hushed, reverent)*
Day four. Museum Mile. We found the heart of this continent and it is beating at seventeen degrees and pumping three hundred and forty litres of couverture.

**BRADLEY**
We haven't even had lunch.

**CHADWICK**
We've had *this.*

### SCENE 2 — GUNTER'S DEEP DIPS

*GUNTER stands behind a spotless green-and-white cart, loading skewers. He does not look up until they are close enough to be a transaction.*

**GUNTER**
Anything on the board, five. Skewer on its own, one.

*He hands over three strawberries without being asked, because that is what happens to people who stand here.*

**LORD PILSNER** *(reading the board aloud, to the entire queue, at volume)*
"Get them while they're hot and heavy." — Yes. — "Plump, firm, and begging to be absolutely smothered." — *Yes.* — "Firm, rigid, and heavily textured for maximum *friction against the flow.*"

*He turns to Gunter, radiant.*

Sir. SIR. Whoever wrote this is a poet.

**GUNTER**
Five SOVS.

**LORD PILSNER**
I want to shake his hand.

**GUNTER**
Five SOVS.

**LORD PILSNER** *(paying)*
Is he here? Is the poet here?

**GUNTER** *(bagging, flat)*
Wipe? One SOV.

**LORD PILSNER**
No.

**GUNTER**
Wipe? One SOV.

**CHADWICK**
No. Wait — actually, do you take photos for the—

**GUNTER**
No. Strawberries?

**BRADLEY** *(reading the warning sign, out loud, to nobody)*
"Please maintain a firm, two-handed grip on your skewer at all times. Do NOT double-dip, do NOT stroke the fountain base, and absolutely NO open-mouth catching directly from the tip of the statue." — ..."We've had incidents."

*Beat.*

What incidents?

**GUNTER**
Before your time.

**BRADLEY**
It opened in 2011.

**GUNTER**
Before your time. Strawberries?

**BRADLEY**
I'll have the pineapple.

**GUNTER**
Five SOVS.

**BRADLEY**
And I'm not dipping it. I'm eating it dry. I have standards.

*GUNTER looks at him for exactly as long as it takes to hand him a bare skewer with three pineapple chunks on it. It is not on the board. He did not charge for the chunks.*

### SCENE 3 — THE FOUNTAIN

*The party approaches the cascade. A FAMILY OF FIVE stands directly in front of it, all dipping marshmallows. The youngest is on her fourth.*

**PARENT** *(mildly, not pursuing it)*
One dip. It says one dip.

*The child dips four more times. Nobody stops anything.*

**LORD PILSNER** *(rolling up a sleeve)*
Alright. Hold my phone.

**CHADWICK**
I'm filming.

**LORD PILSNER**
Hold my phone.

**CHADWICK**
I'm *filming.*

**LORD PILSNER**
Then *hold my phone in the shot.*

*He dips. The sound is unreasonably satisfying. He comes up with a strawberry the approximate size of a fist and holds it aloft.*

**LORD PILSNER**
Once is for tourists.

*He dips again.*

**BRADLEY**

### SCENE 4 — THE SIGN

*The party stands in front of the stainless plate reading **ONE DIP PER CUSTOMER. WE ARE WATCHING.** It is the size of a road sign.*

**LORD PILSNER**
"One dip per customer."

*Beat.*

Per *customer.*

**BRADLEY**
That's per person.

**LORD PILSNER**
That's a legal term, Bradley. "Customer." Singular. It's a loophole. They've left us a loophole.

**BRADLEY**
It means one dip.

**LORD PILSNER** *(already dipping)*
It means one dip *per instance of custom.* I am currently a *new instance.*

**CHADWICK**
That's not how—

**LORD PILSNER**
That's *exactly* how.

*RUUD arrives at a jog, polo tucked, expression of a man on his ninth hour.*

**RUUD**
One dip. One dip, please. Sir. One dip.

**LORD PILSNER** *(to Ruud, warmly, hand extended)*
My friend. My *friend.* Read the sign. Read it carefully. "Per customer."

**RUUD** *(not unkindly, not moving)*
It means one dip.

**LORD PILSNER**
It's ambiguous.

**RUUD**
It isn't.

**LORD PILSNER**
It's a *little* ambiguous.

**RUUD**
Sir. One dip. There's a queue.

*There is a queue. It is forty-one people long. Roughly a third of them are now watching.*

**LORD PILSNER** *(quieter, to the others, delighted)*
He said "sir." Did you hear that? He said "sir." There's a *regime* here.

**CHADWICK** *(filming Ruud)*
Can you say "one dip" again, but, like, looking at the fountain?

**RUUD**
No.

### SCENE 5 — THE GIGGLING FIGURE

*Entrance hall. Marble. Ticket desk. ANKE looks up from a cross-stitch.*

**ANKE**
Nine SOVS. The sign says five. The sign is old. Cash or card.

**LORD PILSNER**
Three adults.

**ANKE**
Nine each.

*The party steps four paces inside. Mechanical clicking. 1.2 seconds.*

*A Victorian mannequin lifts its skirts. A recorded giggle plays. The entire lobby jumps in unison — forty strangers, one motion.*

**LORD PILSNER** *(startled for exactly half a second, then absolutely delighted)*
**WHAT THE—** do it again. Do it again, I want it on the phone this time.

**CHADWICK** *(already set up)*
I got it. I got it. I didn't get it. Do it again.

*Clicking.*

**CHADWICK**
Got it. Do it *again,* the light was —

*Clicking.*

**CHADWICK**
Again.

**ANKE** *(without looking up)*
It's every thirty seconds. It's not going anywhere. The bench is there. Many people use the bench.

**BRADLEY** *(to Anke, indicating the mannequin)*
Is that the famous one?

**ANKE**
That's the famous one.

**BRADLEY**
From 1985?

**ANKE**
From before 1985. The museum's from 1985. The figure's older than the museum. We took it with us. It's had two buildings and one laugh.

*Beat.*

**BRADLEY**
That's actually interesting.

**ANKE**
It isn't. Nine SOVS each.

### SCENE 6 — THE INSPECTION

*Plaza. A hi-vis vest. GERDA VONK unspools a rope around the fountain with the calm of a woman with forty other premises this week. The crowd forms a horseshoe. Nobody leaves.*

**CHADWICK** *(camera up)*
Can you say your name for the camera?

**GERDA** *(not looking up, writing)*
Gerda Vonk. G-E-R-D-A. Move, please, you are in the rope.

**BRADLEY**
Is it dangerous?

**GERDA**
It is a shared food surface in a public square with no supervision. That is what I have written. It is on the plinth. Good afternoon.

**LORD PILSNER** *(to the others, thrilled, sotto)*
She's roped it off. There's an *event.*

**BRADLEY**
She's inspecting it.

**LORD PILSNER**
There's an *event.* We're queueing.

**BRADLEY**
It's the same fountain.

**LORD PILSNER**
It's a *roped* fountain.

*He joins the horseshoe. So do they. Forty minutes pass. Nobody complains. A BACHELOR PARTY in matching "GARY'S LAST STAND" shirts invents a dipping contest with rules they are enforcing on each other.*

**GARY'S FRIEND**
GARY. GARY. Both hands. BOTH HANDS, GARY.

**CHADWICK** *(filming Gary, quietly, to nobody)*
That's us. That's us in eight years.

**BRADLEY**

**CHADWICK**
That's us in eight years.
