**SCENE: The Dong Palace Plaza, Museum Mile, Shamsterdam. Daylight. The Fudge Spire cascades in the background, wet and heavy. Gunter's cart is four metres away. A laminated HYGIENE RATING card bolted to the plinth reads: UNDER REVIEW (SINCE 2011).**

---

**[The bros arrive. Lord Pilsner spreads his arms wide, genuinely moved.]**

**LORD PILSNER**  
Gentlemen. GENTLEMEN. This is the single greatest thing Europe has ever produced.

**CHADWICK** *(already filming)*  
This is CONTENT. The *only* content. Every other city was a warm-up.

**BRADLEY** *(eyeing the fountain)*  
It's... wet.

**LORD PILSNER**  
It's not wet. It's four metres of warm, free, public chocolate shaped exactly like my divorce. And it's legal. In fact, it's cultural.

---

**[They approach Gunter's cart.]**

**GUNTER**  
Anything on the board, five. Skewer on its own, one.

**LORD PILSNER** *(reading the board aloud, delighted)*  
"Firm, rigid, and heavily textured for maximum friction against the flow." Sir. SIR. Whoever wrote this is a poet.

**GUNTER**  
Five SOVS.

**LORD PILSNER**  
I'm not ordering that. Not yet. I'm just saying— that's literature. Give me four strawberry sticks. No, five. I'm not sharing.

**CHADWICK**  
Rice Krispie squares. Two. They have "maximum friction." I need that in my content.

**BRADLEY**  
Pineapple chunks. And I'm not dipping. I'm eating them dry. I have STANDARDS.

**GUNTER**  
Wipe? One SOV.

**LORD PILSNER**  
Does this look like a face that needs a wipe?

**CHADWICK**  
We're about to baptize ourselves in a public trough. Wipes are for cowards.

**GUNTER**  
My cart is clean. What happens at the fountain is between you and the fountain.

**LORD PILSNER**  
See? He gets it. He's seen things. This man has *seen* the fountain.

**GUNTER**  
I have seen the fountain. Next, please.

**[They pay. Gunter hands over the sticks. The bros move toward the fountain.]**

**CHADWICK**  
Wait. Read the sign. "ONE DIP PER CUSTOMER. WE ARE WATCHING." They're watching. This is a performance.

**LORD PILSNER**  
Once is for tourists. *(dips a strawberry into the cascade)* That's one.

**CHADWICK**  
And the rule is for tourists. We're local legends. We haven't even been inside the museum and we're already part of the exhibit.

**[Chadwick dips a Rice Krispie square. It immediately starts dissolving.]**

**BRADLEY**  
Told you. Rice Krispie. Too brittle for a four-metre phallus.

**LORD PILSNER**  
That's what makes it brave. You don't see a Rice Krispie back down from the flow.

**[Chadwick tries to re-dip the same square. It disintegrates into the collection bowl.]**

**CHADWICK**  
Reclaimed by the source. Beautiful.

**[Lord Pilsner double-dips his strawberry.]**

**LORD PILSNER**  
See? Second coat. Luxury.

**[Ruud approaches.]**

**RUUD**  
One dip. One dip, please. Sir. One dip.

**LORD PILSNER**  
We're not double-dipping. We're *layering*.

**CHADWICK**  
It's artisanal.

**RUUD**  
Please do not put your face in the fountain. Please. Sir. Please.

**CHADWICK**  
I would never. *(beat)* Without a tripod.

**[Ruud walks away. Bradley eats his pineapple chunks dry, watching.]**

**BRADLEY**  
You two are going to get the Amster-dam runs. I'll be the only one who can still walk tomorrow.

**LORD PILSNER**  
You're eating pineapple chunks on a stick, Brad. You're not above it. You just haven't committed.

**BRADLEY**  
Discipline. That's what separates me from you animals.

**[Lord Pilsner hands Bradley his half-dipped strawberry skewer.]**

**LORD PILSNER**  
Try this. It's transcendent.

**BRADLEY**  
I'm not putting my mouth on your used stick.

**LORD PILSNER**  
You'll live. It's chocolate. It's sterile.

**CHADWICK**  
Chocolate's antibacterial. *(dips his phone case into the cascade)* It's fine. Content.

**BRADLEY**  
That is *not* a thing.

**CHADWICK**  
It is now. I'm documenting it.

**ANKE**

**LORD PILSNER**

**CHADWICK**

**CHADWICK**
