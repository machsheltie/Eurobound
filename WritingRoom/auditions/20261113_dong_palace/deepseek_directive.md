## SCENE 1 — THE PLAZA

**SOUND:** the cascade arriving before the plaza does. Wet, heavy, slapping.

**STAGE:** The three bros walk up Museum Mile. Chadwick is walking backwards. He has been walking backwards for two hundred metres.

**CHADWICK:** *(walking backwards, phone up)* It's a fountain. It's a fountain. It's af— it's a fountain. Gentlemen. It is a **fountain**.

**LORD PILSNER:** *(stopping. Bravado visibly rising off him like heat)* Bradley.

**BRADLEY:** I see it.

**LORD PILSNER:** Bradley. Look at it.

**BRADLEY:** *(already looking)* I'm looking.

**LORD PILSNER:** I want you to say what you're looking at.

**BRADLEY:** *(beat)* I'm looking at a chocolate fountain.

**LORD PILSNER:** Say the rest of it.

**BRADLEY:** *(beat, quieter, moved despite himself)* ...It's enormous.

**CHADWICK:** *(to phone, breathless)* Chat. Chat. CHAT. Amsterdam has a **giant chocolate cock** and nobody told us and I want to know who dropped that ball and I want them **fired**.

**LORD PILSNER:** *(arms wide, addressing the plaza, the queue, the city)* GENTLEMEN. I have been to Europe. I have seen the pyramids. I have seen the Colosseum in Vegas. And I am telling you **now**, standing here, in the cold light of day, that this is the single greatest thing the continent of Europe has ever produced.

**BRADLEY:** You've been here four seconds.

**LORD PILSNER:** And I'll be honest with you, it's been the best four seconds of my life.
